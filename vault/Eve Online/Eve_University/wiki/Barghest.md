1. # Tactics
Despite being shield tanked, the Barghest is commonly armor tanked to open up additional tackle
and propulsion options. Barghests are commonly fit with rapid heavy missiles to maximize burst DPS.

1. # Notes
In the folklore of the north of England, "barghest" is the name of either a kind of monstrous supernatural black dog, or (more generically) for ghosts, goblins, elves, and other ill-defined supernatural threats.
