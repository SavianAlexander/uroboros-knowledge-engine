# Blood Forsaken Hub

The **Blood Forsaken Hub** is a **combat anomaly** found in **Blood Raiders** infested regions. It is the level 3 variant of the **Blood Hub**.

1. # Walkthrough
Single ungated normal space pocket. Killing the last hostile ship triggers a reinforcement spawn. Be advised, especially if attempting to complete the site inattentively, that in addition to being warp disrupted during the 3rd Reinforcement Spawn, you will often be double-webbed and occassionally triple-webbed, causing damage to spike if you are attempting to tank with angular velocity. Bounties of 24m as at 03-14-2025. They have since been nerfed.
