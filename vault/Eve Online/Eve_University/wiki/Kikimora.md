1. # Summary
Despite technically being a "tech 1" hull, the Kikimora behaves in combat much more like a **Tactical Destroyer** or even an **Assault Frigate**. It is extremely fast, long-ranged, accurate, and hard-hitting for its size, allowing it to destroy both hostile Frigates and hostile *Carriers* (and their Fighters) with surprisingly little difficulty. To further capitalize on its speed and damage, despite being by all rights an armored ship, Kikimoras are very frequently Shield-tanked.

Kikimoras are not commonly used in PVE, as the enhanced durability of Tactical Destroyers makes them commonly preferrable. However, the extreme DPS of ramped-up entropic disintegrators allows it to take down rats quite effectively and safely, if not quickly.

1. # Notes
In Slavic mythology, the Kikimora was an evil house spirit, created by babies who died unbaptised.
Like all Tech 1 Triglavian subcapital ships, the Clade that designed the Kikimora, Svarog, has their name engraved on the sides of the ship in Triglavian font ().
