# Pith's Penal Complex

- Pith's Penal Complex** is a DED rated complex found in null security space. This site can be found by scanning cosmic signatures.

The acceleration gates in this site let all subcapital ships in.

1. # Walkthrough

The First Gate Required CPU Management 5

  1. # 1st pocket

Enemies have special names. "Assistant Warden" battleships have ECM. Rats are simply renamed; they don't drop anything atypical from the usual loot.
