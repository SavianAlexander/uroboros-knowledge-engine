1. # Summary
The **Vulture** is the Caldari fleet command ship, designed as a railgun platform. With its second optimal range bonus replaced with a shield HP bonus, it has worse range and similar damage to its Tech 1 basis, the **Ferox**. In exchange, it is the tankiest shield command ship by far, making it the preferred booster for providing command bursts to large shield fleets. Its missile-using counterpart, the **Nighthawk**, has a higher damage potential.
