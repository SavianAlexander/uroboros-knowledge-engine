# Yochuko Eskaila

- Yochuko Eskaila** is a level 4 **Caldari COSMOS** agent located in Ihakana - Shady Acres.

Missions in the chain are relatively easy, but all of them requires fighting in Low-sec. The final mission will reward you with the biggest Caldari faction standings gain in the whole **Okkelen** part of the **Caldari COSMOS** arc.

1. # Deputy - The Outpost (1 of 4)

Single gated pocket. Your objective is to destroy all enemies. Mission pocket located in Ihakana (Low-sec)

|}

1. # Deputy - The Sting (2 of 4)

  - Blitz:** destroy the **Storage Silo** and return to the agent

Single gated pocket. Your objective is to destroy the **Storage Silo** structure.

Mission pocket will be located in one of the nearby **low** or high sec system.

|}

|}

1. # Deputy - The C-Wing (3 of 4)

Single gated pocket. Your objective is to destroy all frigates and destroyers in the pocket. Despite what the mission briefing says, to complete the mission you need to destroy only frigates and destroyers, mission will be completed without destroying any structures. Battleship also can be ignored. Mission is relatively easy, and can be done in a cruiser.
Location is in the same system as the agent.

|}
It is not verified, do frigates have any kind of warp disruption or not.

1. # Deputy - The Gunship (4 of 4)

  - Blitz:** destroy **Choiji the Vanquisher** and return to the agent

Single gated pocket. Your objective is to destroy **Choiji the Vanquisher**. Choiji has active shield tank with 115 HP/s shield boost rate

Location is in the same system as the agent.
