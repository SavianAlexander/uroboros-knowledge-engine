# Serpentis Logistical Outpost

The **Serpentis Logistical Outpost** is **combat signature** that can be found in low-sec or null-sec, or as an escalation from completing the anomaly **Serpentis Yard** or **Serpentis Rally Point**. Technically the site is an **unrated complex**, but behaves like a **DED 6/10** and thus can be considered the Serpentis DED 6/10.
The site is located behind an acceleration gate that allows T2 Battleships or lower to access and consists of two parts: Part 1 is found by probe scanning or as an escalation. Completing Part 1 will always cause an escalation to Part 2 at another location.

  - Warning:**
The Serpentis Logistical Outpost and Serpentis Logistical Outpost Part 2 are considered to be the hardest Serpentis Escalations.
Use extreme caution when completing the sites due to the Scramming Frigs and T2 Neuting Towers
