# Secrets of the Abyss

Secrets of the Abyss was a in game event that ran from 21st August to 4th September 2018. The event consiststed of doing **Abyssal Deadspace** sites.

1. # Rewards

In addition to the usual Abyssal Deadspace rewards you can also find mutaplasmids for both ancillary shield boosters and armor repairers.

The event also uses the agency based point system for tiered rewards.

Agency tasks:
- Delve into the abyss (chain)
# Complete one T1-T5 site - points
# Complete one T2-T5 site - points + accelerator
# Complete one T3-T5 site - 30 points
# Complete one T4-T5 site - 40 points + accelerator
# Complete one T5 site - 100 points. Resets back to first one.
- Exploration sequencing (chain)
  - Enter abyssal deadspace - 5 points
  - Complete pocket 1 - 5 points
  - Complete pocket 2 - 5 points
  - Complete pocket 3
- Mutation experimentation (chain)
  - 
  - Use gravid mutaplasmid - 2 points
- Abyssal extermination
  - defeat 10 enemies - 2 points

You will get reward at certain milestones:
- 50 points: Extended 'Hercules' Cerebral Accelerator
- 150 points: Damavik Singularity Storm SKIN (Permanent)
- 450 points: Vedmak Singularity Storm SKIN (Permanent)
- 750 points: Extended 'Hercules' Cerebral Accelerator
- 1000 points: Leshak Singularity Storm SKIN (Permanent)
