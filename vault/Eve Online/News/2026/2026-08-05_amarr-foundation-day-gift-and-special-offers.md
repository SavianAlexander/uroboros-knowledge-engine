## Amarr Pilot Expert System


If you truly want to show your devotion, you can choose from a multitude of Amarr ships to fly, and the NES is now offering a new Amarr Pilot Expert System, giving you access to a range of skills to fly T1 Amarr sub-capital ships for 7 days. 

Amarr ships in EVE Online are renowned for powerful energy turrets, exceptional armor resilience, and capacitor-focused warfare. They excel at projecting sustained laser damage with impressive optimal range while fielding durable armor tanks, making them formidable in prolonged engagements across both PvE and PvP. 

This is a fantastic way to try these out before committing to the full training queue. The Expert System will remain available in the future, too.
