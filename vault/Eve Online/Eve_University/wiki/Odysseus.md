1. # Summary
The **Odysseus** is an Expedition Command Ship belonging to the **Servant Sisters of EVE**. It has the usual bonuses that most Sisters of EVE ships feature.

- Ability to fit covert cloak.
- Armor resistances and a large amount of midslots giving it high versatility for an armor tanked ship. Like the , shield tanking is an option despite the armor resistance bonus.
- Bonuses to drone hitpoints and damage.
- Bonuses to probe strength and virus strength of analyzer modules.

Additionally, it has some unique features for a battlecruiser-sized hull which separate it from regular **command ships**:

- It has a Drone Capacity of 600, the largest drone bay of all subcapital ships.
- It has a Ship Maintenance Bay of 75,000, allowing it to hold up to a destroyer or multiple frigates depending on the size.
- It has a small Fleet Hangar of 1,000 allowing easy loot swapping and sharing between fleet members.
- It has a large Expedition Hold of 50,000, allowing it to store loot from Cosmic Signatures. Notably it has been mentioned that it can store Gas, Salvage, Datacores and Cap Booster charges.
- Additionally, it has a unique bonus which allows it to get various buffs based on environmental effects (in addition to the normal modifiers from them). These bonuses are listed in the section below.
- It can also fit the Zero-Point Mass Entangler module, which greatly reduces its mass (while preventing warp and dramatically lowering AB/MWD thrust), allowing it to travel through frigate sized **wormholes** and manipulate the amount of mass being passed through wormholes it uses.
