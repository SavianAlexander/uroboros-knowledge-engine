# Mordunium Deposit

- Mordunium Deposit** are periodically spawned in **sovereign nullsec** systems that contain an active **Ore Prospecting Array**. These are part of the Pyerite Prospecting Array. They will also rarely spawn in **lowsec** non-**faction warfare** systems.
