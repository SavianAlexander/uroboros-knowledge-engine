# Festering Ruins

- Festering Ruins** is a **combat relic** site found in the [Fountain](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Fountain) nullsec region. Up to T2 **Battleships** can enter this site. This site appears to Probes as a combat site.

The site is very rare and hard, and should only be attempted by skilled players. **Rogue Drone**, **Serpentis**, **Angel Cartel** and **Guristas** rats are present in this site. Omni tanks are highly recommended, especially against Kinetic and Thermal damage.

Compared to other combat relic sites, this is noticeably harder due to higher DPS, especially in the fourth and last pocket.
