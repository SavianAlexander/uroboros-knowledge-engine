# Angel Rally Point

The Angel Rally Point is a **combat anomaly** found in **Angel** infested regions. The site is easily done with T2 fit sniper Dominix. The site pays estimately 2,3 million isk in bounties.
Angel Rally Point has 2 variations.

Variation 1 "Workers Quarters"

Variation 2 "Fuel Silos"

1. # Walkthrough
Single ungated normal space pocket. Spawns are triggered by the last ship killed unless otherwise tagged.
