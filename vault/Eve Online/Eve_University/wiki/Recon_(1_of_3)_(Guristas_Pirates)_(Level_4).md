# Recon (1 of 3) (Guristas Pirates) (Level 4)

<noinclude>
Parts: **1 of 3 (Guristas Pirates)**, **2 of 3**, **3 of 3**
</noinclude>

Video: [Kronos Part 1,2 & 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TuC62x_bs9Y)

Recon is a three part mission. **Part 2** and **part 3** are the same for all factions.

  - Blitz:** Use fast frigate to quickly take the acceleration gate in first room. Mission is completed when you land in second room.

  1. # 1st pocket
The gates are not locked, so you can blitz this mission without a single kill. Go to warp gate, activate it, when you reach the next pocket you are done!

If you stay and fight:
- Groups spawn approximately 1 minute apart, starting just after warp-in.
- Rats will spawn approximately 30-45 km away and big ships orbit around 32km.
- All groups aggro on spawn, though they will aggro drones that are out as well.
- Bunker can drop commodities.
