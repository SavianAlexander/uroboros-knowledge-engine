# Blood Annex

- Blood Annex** is an **Unrated Complex** found by scanning in Low Security space with Blood Raider presence.
1. # Walkthrough
This site is similar to the **Blood Vigil**.

Upon warping to the site you will land at an ancient acceleration gate.

  1. # First Pocket
Sentries can be ignored, although blow them up if you cannot tank their damage. Destroy the stasis towers though.

Like its **Blood Vigil** counterpart, the second room of this complex contains 4 structures named "Powerful EM Forcefield".  They should be blown up upon entering this room, as they will provide insane reps to all enemies in this pocket.
