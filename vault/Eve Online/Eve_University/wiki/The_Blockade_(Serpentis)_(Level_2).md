  1. # Mining
Every cycle completion OR deactivation of a mining laser has a 50% chance to cause the warp-in Beacon to fire a 500 EM damage smartbomb with a radius of 250 kilometers.  That is not a typo: the warp-in beacon, itself, fires the smartbomb.  All players and player-owned drones in range take damage.  Damage to NPCs is untested.  Wrecks and the loot structure (the "Starbase Ship Maintenance Array" mentioned above) do NOT take damage.

CAUTION: This testing only applies to this specific variant of "The Blockade".  Other variants may have a beacon-smartbomb that deals a different damage type or amount.

Plagioclase - 23 asteroids with a total of 677,404 units (237,091.4 m^3)
Pyroxeres - 13 asteroids with a total of 404,510 units (121,353 m^3)
Veldspar - 39 asteroids with a total of 1,592,457 units (159,245.7 m^3)
