# Ancient Ruins

- Ancient Ruins** is a **combat relic** site only found in the [Catch](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Catch) nullsec region, especially in the 9HXQ-G constellation. Up to T2 **Battleships** can enter this site. This site appears to probes as a combat site.The site is very rare and hard, and should only be attempted by skilled players. **Sansha's Nation** and **Rogue Drone** rats are present in this site. Omni tanks are highly preferable, especially EM and Thermal damage. Damage in the site can easily become excessive if care is not taken in managing triggers.

  - Extra:**
- Bounties: approx. 60-70M isk + bounty adjustment payout
- Loot: approx. 50M isk
- Salvage: approx. 20M isk
