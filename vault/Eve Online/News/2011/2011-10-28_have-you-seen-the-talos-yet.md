Following the [Ship-troduction](//www.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&nbid=3001) of the Minmatar Tornado, we are happy to announce its Gallente counterpart, the Talos. Check out CCP Guard's latest [dev blog](//www.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&nbid=3014) for more information on this battlecruiser.

Tornado, Talos and the respective Caldari and Amarr designs comprise the next generation of battlecruisers in the game. Based on the player-submitted designs, these ships will be able to fit battleship-sized weapons and spread mayhem on the battlefield in the EVE Online [winter expansion](//www.eveonline.com/en/winter2011). Information on the two remaining battlecruisers will be released in the coming weeks.
