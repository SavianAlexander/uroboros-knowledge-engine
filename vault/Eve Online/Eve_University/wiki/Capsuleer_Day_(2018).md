# Capsuleer Day (2018)

- Capsuleer Day 2018** was an event that ran from May 2nd to May 15th 2018, [celebrating EVE Online 15th anniversary](http://updates.eveonline.com/date/2018-05-06/). It was the annual version of the **Capsuleer Day** recurring event.

During this event, various rewards and smaller events were available for capsuleers.

1. # Operation: Conscious Interruption
Combat sites that were part of the **Operation: Conscious Interruption** could be found as beacons in overview and as scannable combat signatures.   
They gave points that could be used to collect rewards, and the NPCs drop various loot.

1. # Rewards for active clones during the event
On May 7th, these rewards were placed in the redeeming system of every account that was active in the 30 days prior to the fifteenth anniversary.

- All
  - **Apotheosis**, a shuttle.
  - Anniversary Capsule SKIN
  - Anniversary Apparel

- Omega clones
  - **Sunesis**, a destroyer.
  - **Gnosis**, a battlecruiser.
  - **Praxis**, a battleship.
