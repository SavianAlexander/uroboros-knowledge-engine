# Provisional Angel Outpost

- Provisional Angel Outpost** is a low security unrated site found through **exploration**.

1. # Walkthrough

The site will require you to fly long +100 km distances. A fast ship is recommended. Gistum Defeater cruisers can rarely web.

  1. # 1st pocket

The timed wave will appear after few minutes. Approaching the acceleration gate will trigger gate wave.
