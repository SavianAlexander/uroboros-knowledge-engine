# Serpentis Secrets

- Serpentis Secrets** is an **expedition** that escalates from **Serpentis Provincial HQ** and can lead into both low-sec and null-sec regions.

1. # Walkthrough
This escalation consists of four parts.

1. # First Part
The first room is ungated, meaning you can warp around in it, and people can warp directly to you. 
If Shadow Serpentis is there: kill him, possibly get escalation. Else (or If didn't get escalation): Use acceleration gate, possibly get escalation.

  1. ## First room

Entering this room can trigger the escalation. 

In the room you find a lot of Siloes and a structure/ship called "Ours De Soin". 

1. # Second Location

This site consists of one ungated room. 

The enemies are grouped in in four separate groups. You spawn in the middle of them. 
The two statis towers webs you to a halt, making you take **A LOT** of damage, and the damping makes them hard to hit. This can make the site hard to survive.

1. # Third Location

This site is yet another ungated pocket. You spawn 100km from the NPCs and the two web-towers makes the travel to the station very slow, while the damp makes hitting anything hard. 
If you get the Shadow Serpentis Battleship to spawn, kill it for the escalation. If not, destroy the station. 
The Siege Sentries does a lot of damage, but they are only two. Clear the area of rats before you engage the station.  

|}

|}

1. # Fourth and Last Location
Access: Gateless, Open Access 

This site is extremely difficult, and can require 3-4 people in very tough ships. The site contains 4 waves, and if you manage to spawn 2 at once, the site is near impossible. 
The four waves trigger when you attack the Serpentis Privateer Stronghold (wave 1), when it reaches 50% shields (wave 2), at 25% shield (wave 3), and when it goes into armor (wave 4).  
 You spawn 200 km from the station.
Kill the frigates, then the web-towers, then the siege blasters and then the rest.
