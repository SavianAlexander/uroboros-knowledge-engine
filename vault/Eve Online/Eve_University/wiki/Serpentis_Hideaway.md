# Serpentis Hideaway

- Serpentis Hideaway** is an unrated **combat site** belonging to the **Serpentis Corporation** pirate faction. It can be found via **exploration** as a **cosmic anomaly** within systems owned by the **Gallente Federation** or occupied by the Serpentis Corporation. As an ungated complex, the limitations as to which ships can complete this site are tied to the security rating of the system where the anomaly spawns (T2 **Battleship**s for high-security systems, and up to **Titans** in low-security and null-security systems). Completing this complex may award the player with valuable Shadow Serpentis **faction modules** and ammunition.

This combat site consists of a single ungated pocket of space containing a variety of **Frigate**-sized enemy ships. Destroying all hostile ships may escalate into the **DED rated** **Serpentis Narcotic Warehouses** complex. Three variations of this combat anomaly exist: **Serpentis Hidden Hideaway**, **Serpentis Forsaken Hideaway** and **Serpentis Forlorn Hideaway**.

Total cargo loot amount to around 20k ISK.
