# Skurk Tekkurs

- Skurk Tekkurs** is a level 2 **Caldari COSMOS** agent located in Vahunomi.

1. # Designated Driver - The Garage (1 of 4)

Rewards= 100,000 ISK + 25,000 ISK (time bonus)

The **belt rat**s are suppose to drop it, but the drop rate seems low, can also be purchased off contracts.

1. # Designated Driver - The Decoder (2 of 4)

Collateral 20,000 ISK

Rewards=120,000 ISK + 60,000 ISK (time bonus)

1. # Designated Driver - Clean Out (3 of 4)

Kill all the bandits and bring back a mechanical part to finish this mission. 5 Bandit Mechanic frigates, last one drops loot.
