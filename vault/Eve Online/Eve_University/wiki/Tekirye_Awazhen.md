# Tekirye Awazhen

- Tekirye Awazhen** is a level 3 agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located in Ishisomo - NOH Recruitment Center.

1. # Interested in Work? (1 of 5)

Ancient Weapon drops from the Storage Silo ine **Contested Caldari Lai Dai Refinery** - Contested Scordite field deadspace complex in the Airmia system to agent.

1. # Troubles With Outlaws (2 of 5)

Miner drop from Guristas Maniac in **Contested Caldari Lai Dai Refinery** - Contested Plagioclase field and Contested Scordite field, deadspace complex in the Airmia system to agent.

1. # We Caught a Spy… (3 of 5)

Transport 1x Spy (3 m3) from agent to given destination.

1. # Pirates Brought to Justice (4 of 5)

Guristas Outlaw Dogtag (0.5 m3) drop from Guristas rats in **Contested Caldari Lai Dai Refinery** deadspace complex in the Airmia system to agent.

1. # Time for Goodbyes (5 of 5)

Transport 2 Propel Dynamics Reports (3.0 m3) from agent to given destination.
