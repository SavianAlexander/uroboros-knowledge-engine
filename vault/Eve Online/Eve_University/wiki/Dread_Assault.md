# Dread Assault

An empire dreadnought has been dispatched to resolve the conflict in this sector. Your fleet’s mission is to ensure its success by supplying enough capacitor power for the dreadnought to activate its superweapon and obliterate the enemy structure. Hostile ships will attempt to destroy your ships, ECM jam your ships, and drain the dreadnought's capacitor. 

1. # Fleet size & composition
- Optimal fleet size: 5 ships (see **Rewards** below)
- Ship types: T1 cruisers and below

1. # Type of ships allowed
This homefront operation takes place in a **deadspace pocket** with an acceleration gate that has ship restrictions.

- Tech 1 frigates, navy and pirate frigates
- Tech 1 destroyers, navy and pirate destroyers
- Tech 1 cruisers
- Tech 1 haulers, including **Squall**
- **Venture**, **Venture Consortium Issue**
- **Pioneer**, **Pioneer Consortium Issue**, **Perseverance**

1. # Mission parameters
- Time-limit: 20 minutes
- Dreadnought Capacitor Requirements (30,000)
- Enemy Structure Health
- Siege Mode Timer: 90 seconds

1. # Hostiles
A fleet consisting of frigates, destroyers, cruisers, and battlecruisers will be present upon arrival. They will concentrate their fire on one capsuleer ship at a time, beginning with the first vessel to enter the site, and may periodically switch targets as a group.

Hostile EWAR ships (such as ECM, see the table below) may target different ships than the rest of the room, and each may pick different targets resulting in (for example) multiple player ships getting jammed at the same time.

Hostile drone aggro ships (see the table below) will target any drones that are deployed, but will revert to attacking the currently focused capsuleer ship if no drones are present.

1. # Rewards
Rewards for Dread Assault sites consists of raw ISK payout upon completion, following the standard 5-pilot fleet payout curve with in a maximum payout of 15,000,000 ISK payout per pilot with a fleet of 5.
