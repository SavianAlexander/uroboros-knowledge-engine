# Vision of Greatness

- Vision of Greatness** is the Epic Arc of the **Association for Interdisciplinary Research** (AIR).

1. # Notes
Ship restrictions prevent using any ship that isn't a Standard **frigate** (i.e. one of the 6 basic T1, nonfaction frigates) of the respective Empire. Basic ships and modules are granted by the mission givers.

1. # Rewards
+2.5% faction standing for **Amarr**, **Caldari**, **Gallente**, and **Minmatar** with no derived standing loss

1. # Starting agent
Cassandra Ahfrim of AIR Laboratoties offers the first mission of the arc in Manifest V - AIR Laboratories Trade Center in **Exordium**. All of the missions take place in Exordium.
