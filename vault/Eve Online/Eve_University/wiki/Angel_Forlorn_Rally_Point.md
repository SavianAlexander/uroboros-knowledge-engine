# Angel Forlorn Rally Point

The Angel Forlorn Rally Point is a **combat anomaly** found in **Angel** infested regions. It is a level 4 variant of the **Angel Rally Point**. The site is easily done with T2 fit sniper Battleship. This site pays estimately 15,8 million isk in bounties.
