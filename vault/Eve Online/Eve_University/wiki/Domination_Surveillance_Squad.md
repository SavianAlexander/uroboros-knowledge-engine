# Domination Surveillance Squad

- Domination Surveillance Squad** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **Provisional Angel Outpost**.
1. # Walkthrough
The first three locations are usually in low security space and the last in null security space, but this can vary.  For locations #1-3, take the Newly Constructed Acceleration Gate into the first pocket if no escalation is given from destroying the commander or if the commander ship does not spawn. Gistum Breaker/Defeater cruisers rarely web.

  1. # First Location
Kill Domination cruiser, loot wreck, possibly get escalation. Otherwise take the Newly Constructed Acceleration Gate.

  1. ## First Pocket

1. # Loot
The Domination commanders drop faction ammo and tags, and may also contain Domination cruiser modules. Nikmar Eitan may drop Gistum C-Type cruiser modules or a rare **Cynabal** BPC.
