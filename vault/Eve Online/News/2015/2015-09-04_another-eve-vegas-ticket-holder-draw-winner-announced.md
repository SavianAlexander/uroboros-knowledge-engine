On August 18th, week we announced the [second round of EVE Vegas 2015 ticket holder prize draws](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/eve-online-news/eve-vegas-2015-ticket-sales-draw-round-2/). Today, we're ready to annouce the third winner of this draw!

As outlined in the news article linked above, this winner will receive a Resort Vista room upgrade and complimentary airport limousine transfers. The winner has been drawn.

Congratulations to [Paul Maken](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Paul%20Maken) of [The Rising Stars](//gate.eveonline.com/Corporation/The%20Rising%20Stars) on being selected as a winner!

Winners of the prize draw will be contacted via email with full details on how they can claim their prize. 

There are still more prizes available to EVE Vegas ticket holders that will be awarded in August and September, so be sure to keep your eyes peeled for more chances to win room upgrades as an EVE Vegas ticket holder!

Tickets are selling fast, so be sure to pick up yours today in order to avoid disappointment and make sure that you are included in all the prize draws listed above.
