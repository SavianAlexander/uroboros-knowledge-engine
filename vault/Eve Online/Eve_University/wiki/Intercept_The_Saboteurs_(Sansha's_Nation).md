# Intercept The Saboteurs (Sansha's Nation)

- Blitz:**
Mission completed once last Group 4 ship is killed (drops the needed item in a cargo container), you do not have to kill other groups.
See note above for aggressing Group 4 individually without triggering Group 3.

1. # Pocket

  - Notes:** Attacking group 3 or 4 gets aggro from 3 and 4 (**except** when pulling aggro at the range; this way you can easily do it in a much smaller ship).
Drones don't cause any aggro but may aggro further ship themselves when out. Taking them back in drone bay doesn't lead the aggro on you, NPC ships just head back to their initial location even if very close to you, as long as you don't fire.
