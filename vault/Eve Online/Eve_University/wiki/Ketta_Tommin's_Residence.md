}} **Ketta Tommin's Residence** is a static COSMOS deadspace complex found in Hjoramold system. The acceleration gate to this residence is in the Asteroid Belt 2 at planet 10. 

1. # Site details
  1. # Warp-in
Nothing special, just an asteroid belt and an acceleration gate, which requires a Mikado ID slice to use. The ID Slice can be found from the Maru Mikado battleships in the **Bazeri Palen** site.

Do note that **belt rat**s may be present, as well as Blood Raider diamond rats if there is an active FOB in system.

  1. # single pocket
You will face NPCs named Maru in here, they can be treated like Angels, both for damage deal and damage receive. They will only drop ammunition, drones and booster charges. The site is easy to clear, only frigates and cruisers that pop quickly. On warp-in you land directly in a pack with autoaggro. Three more packs wait for you that can be pulled individually, but watch out for drone aggro. The named NPC in here drops an ID Card Generator. Only enter the site if you need this item, there is nothing else worth your time.
