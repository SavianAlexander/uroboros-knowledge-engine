# Minor Conduit

This combat anomaly is found when a system is either leaning towards the Triglavian Collective or the EDENCOM forces. It is a harder and more advanced version of the **Emerging Conduit** site.

They contain 5 waves of Triglavian NPCs containing 12-15 ships in each.

Clearing this site helps the EDENCOM forces.
