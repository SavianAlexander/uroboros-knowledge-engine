1. # Summary
The **Dramiel** is the Angel Faction frigate. It is the fastest frigate available by quite some margin, even faster than interceptors, and is very popular because of that. While it can only fit two small projectile turrets, its role bonus effectively gives it the damage of four. The Dramiel makes an excellent tackler, but is rather expensive for that purpose, so is most often seen as a solo ship.
