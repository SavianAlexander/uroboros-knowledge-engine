# Minerals

- Minerals** are a type of material in EVE Online that is obtained by **reprocessing** ore or, additionally, by reprocessing equipment, and used in **manufacturing** of nearly all items.

There are 8 base minerals: 
 Tritanium, 
 Pyerite, 
 Mexallon, 
 Isogen, 
 Nocxium, 
 Zydrine, 
 Megacyte, 
 Morphite.

For the minerals that are reprocessed from Moon Ore see **Moon mining#Moon Ore Refining**.

Note that actual yield (net, or less than the gross yield) will depend on reprocessing equipment, player skills, level of reprocessing implant (if any), and corporation standings if using an NPC station.
