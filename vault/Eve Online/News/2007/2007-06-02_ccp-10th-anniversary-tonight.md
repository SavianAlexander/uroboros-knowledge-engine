10 years ago, 1997.06.03, CCP was founded and a few good men started what would one day be the greatest game ever, EVE. A lot has happened since, CCP and EVE has grown and we've seen both good and bad times. Even with recent events most of it has been good and we believe that we're on the right path to make sure both CCP and EVE will be even greater in the future. We'd also like to thank everyone for making EVE and CCP what it is today.

We would like all players to join us in celebrating this event, but since not everyone can fly out to Iceland we thought we'd have an online toast all with the EVE players all over the world. Please make sure you have the beverage of your choice, champagne maybe, and join us in a toast at midnight, 00.00 GMT, tonight!

To make it a bit more interesting we'd like to ask everyone to post in [this thread](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=530423) what you will drink for this toast. So remember, 00.00 GMT tonight, no matter where you are or what you're doing, stand up and make a toast for CCP and EVE!
