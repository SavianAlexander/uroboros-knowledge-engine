Roc Wieler of [Roc’s Ramblings](//everamblings.wordpress.com) has a question: “Has anyone thought about how our immortal dealings are going to further upset the natural order of the universe?” With Tyrannis approaching, Roc wants [pod pilots to submit stories](//everamblings.wordpress.com/2010/05/02/tyrannis-the-contest/) from the point of view of one of the little people on the planets who will be experiencing our conquest first hand. Winners of this contest will be awarded ISK as well as EVE Store coupons, and the contest runs through May 18, 2010.
