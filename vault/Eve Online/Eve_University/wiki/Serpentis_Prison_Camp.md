# Serpentis Prison Camp

- Serpentis Prison Camp** is a DED Rated Complex (rated 8/10) type, occupied by the **Serpentis** pirate faction. It can be found via **exploration** by probing in Nullsec systems.

The site consists of a volume of space with an acceleration gate and contains various structures and their defending pirates. Using the gate allows access to other areas within the site. When attacked, the defenders may send multiple waves of reinforcements. Each group's appearance is triggered by the destruction of members of the previous group. The names and amount of ships listed may vary slightly, but will always be of Battleship class or smaller.

This site's acceleration gate allows only Battleship class ships or lower and their Tech 2 equivalents to enter the site.

You may receive this site as an escalation from a **Serpentis Hub** or a **Serpentis Forsaken Rally Point**.

  - Note:** The Serpentis Prisoner Isolation Facility (the structure that contains your loot) has a very strong web and will immediately target you upon warp in. Be prepared to not be able to speed tank or move closer to the structure. Also of note is that it shoots XL Inferno torpedoes that do approx 2000 thermal damage, though their explosion radius is appropriate to the signature radius of capital ships so they apply little damage to battleships or smaller ships. The outpost also repairs itself to the tune of about 1000 DPS and has lots of structure hit points.

Incoming damage can be very high by reason of the Stasis Towers and the structure's web. Suggest refitting for maximum tank. Kill the webbing towers first, then the Elite Frigates, then move on to the rest. Profile is shown here: [Database Entry](http://games.chruker.dk/eve_online/item.php?type_id=3809) 

- There is a glitch  on this structure whereby the structure will stop repairing armor if it switches aggro. The structure's armor repair can be turned off by forcing it to switch its primary target (the target it fires torpedoes at and webs). A drone ship does this by having its drones out and attacking the structure, then flying over 120km away from it. Players in ships without drones will have to have ensure another pilot is in space within application range of the structure.*
