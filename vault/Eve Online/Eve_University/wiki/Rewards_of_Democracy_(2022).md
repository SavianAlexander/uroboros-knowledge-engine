# Rewards of Democracy (2022)

- Rewards of Democracy** is a login campaign that runs from 7 June to 14 June 2022, corresponding with the voting period of the 17th **Council of Stellar Management** (CSM17). Capsuleers must have logged in for 5 out of the 7 days to collect all rewards. The campaign rewards up to 12,500 **Skill Points** for Alpha clones plus 25,000 more Skill Points for Omega clones.
