1. # Summary
With 5 high slots and 5 low slots, the **Wolf** is a brute of a damage-dealing frigate. Its 2 mid slots means it is not well suited to **tackling**, as it has very little space for tackle and utility modules. The same slot limitation hampers any really serious shield tanking, which requires mid slots. Consequently, when compared to its brother the **Jaguar** the Wolf has more firepower but less speed and less utility beyond its core purpose of dealing damage.

1. # Skills

Like other Tech 2 ships, the Wolf has tight fitting requirements, which are eased by **fitting skills**, and also benefits from excellent **support skills** in general. As a turret-focused damage-dealer, the Wolf deserves Tech 2 small projectile turrets, and the best gunnery support skills you can supply.

1. # Tactics

Within the broad activity of damage-dealing, the Wolf has many valid fits, from an armor-tanked autocannon brawler, through a balanced mid-range kiting autocannon fit, to a fast long-range kiting artillery fit. Most fits will mount an Assault Damage Control for brief bursts of near-immunity to damage; this can work especially well when supported by fast, agile frigate-sized armour logistics ships. Artillery fits fighting at long range can sometimes do without the ADC, if their primary defensive plan is to warp out.
