## Totality Day YC127


Every year on October 13 the Kybernaut community celebrates **Totality Day** - the anniversary of the conclusion to the long and bitter struggle during the [Triglavian Invasion](https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Triglavian_Invasion_(Chapter_3)) of [YC122](https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Yoiul_Conference) which resulted in the creation of the [Pochven](https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Pochven) region.

As part of this year's celebrations there are a series of NPSI (**N**ot **P**urple **S**hoot **I**t) activities being hosted by various Kybernaut groups. These are public events meaning that anyone is welcome to come along! To find out how to get involved join the [Triglavian Collective Discord](https://discord.gg/PrZHUDJjSB) and look for announcements!

## Alliance Tournament Art Contest


[Nora Maldoran](https://evewho.com/character/93584216) aka long time EVE blogger [the greybill](https://thegreybill.wordpress.com) is running a community art contest to commemorate Alliance Tournament XXI with 5,000 PLEX up for grabs! To be in the hunt for a piece of that prize you have to create a new piece of media that either celebrates the AT as a whole or just a particular team.

Full details on the contest including deadlines and how to submit your entries are included in their [forum post](https://forums.eveonline.com/t/alliance-tournament-art-contest-5-000-plex-in-prizes) and their [blog post](https://thegreybill.wordpress.com/2025/08/08/alliance-tournament-art-contest-5-000-plex-in-prizes) about the contest. Good luck!


## EVE Down Under


Australia's biggest player gathering **EVE Down Under** took place once again last month in Sydney with CCP Larrikin and CCP Convict in attendance. The event was held at the the impressive [Fortress](https://fortress.games) esports and gaming facility. The program included some large scale set-piece battles on the Thunderdome test server and a PvP tournament as well as a presentation (including an exclusive first look at the 2D map) and some live data science from CCP, and quite a bit of time spent in the bar as you'd expect. 🍻

EVE Down Under 2026 is already in the works so keep an eye out for news by joining the [EVE Down Under Discord](https://discord.gg/7S6uRRuE3D)!
