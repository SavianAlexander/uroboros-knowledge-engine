# Concerned Citizens - Lost Stash (2 of 3)

<noinclude>
  - Gallente COSMOS** mission
Agent: **Pattok Nortul** 
Agent location: Nickel & Dime Store (Augnais)
Standings required: none
Parts: **(1 of 3)**, **(2 of 3)**, **(3 of 3)**</noinclude>

The agent sends you to look for stolen goods without giving you a mission pocket to plunder. You, being one confused mission runner, will get a hint about the location of the stolen goods by reading the (long) mission briefing.

The stolen goods can be found in **Contested Gallente Skeleton Comet** in Deltole. The "Serpentis Junkie" frigates in the first room drop them.<noinclude>
