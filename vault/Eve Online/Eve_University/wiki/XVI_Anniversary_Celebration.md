# XVI Anniversary Celebration

The **XVI Anniversary Celebration** was a limited time event that ran from May 2nd to May 20th, 2019. Capsuleers who logged in during the 18-day period could collect login rewards, with different rewards available up to the 16th log in. The event featured Permaband SKINs, silver monocles, and 1,250,000 total skill points in rewards.

1. # Login Campaign

Omega clones also received alpha clone rewards. Alpha clones that upgraded to Omega during the event period were able to collect all Omega rewards from previous days when they were an Alpha clone.
