# Serpentis Base

- Serpentis Base** is a **combat site** that can be scanned down with probes in null security Serpentis regions. The site is behind an acceleration gates. Warping to the side lands you at 0 on the gate.

An extender fit Drake can run this site. Bring a MWD or long-range weapons, as the enemies all spawn very far away.

1. # Walkthrough
  1. # Room #1
The rats are few, but far away from each other. Acceleration gate is 56 km from warp-in. It unlocks when all enemies are killed.

1. # Rewards
You can get about 4 million isk in bounties from this site.  If the faction battleship spawns, you can get an additional 4 million isk in bounties, plus the value of its faction loot.

1. # Escalation
The site completes and de-spawns upon destruction of the faction battleship or the Serpentis Armory structure in Room #2 (if the faction battleship did not spawn). The site has a chance to escalate to **Contract Killers**.
