# Kidnappers Strike

- Kidnappers Strike** is a 10-part **storyline** **mission chain** which has multiple faction variants. It includes the following missions:

- **Kidnappers Strike Part 1-4**

- **Kidnappers Strike Part 5-10 (Blood Raiders)**
- **Kidnappers Strike Part 5-10 (Guristas Pirates)**
- **Kidnappers Strike Part 5-10 (Sansha's Nation)**
