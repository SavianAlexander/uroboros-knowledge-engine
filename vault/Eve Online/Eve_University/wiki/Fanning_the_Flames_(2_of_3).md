# Fanning the Flames (2 of 3)

<noinclude>
  - Gallente COSMOS** mission
Agent: **Croir Arghe** 
Agent location: Arid Park (Alsottobier)
Standings required: 6.00 (or perhaps 5.00)
Parts: **(1 of 3)**, **(2 of 3)**, **(3 of 3)**</noinclude>

In this courier mission you will need to pick the items from one station and drop in another station. 4 jumps in total to complete the mission and return to agent. An explorer frigate can with enough expanders get enought cargo space and they are quite fast compared to hauling ships. The mission can be completed remotely but accepting next requires you to travel back to the agent.<noinclude>
