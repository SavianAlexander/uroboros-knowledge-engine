### Renegade Capsuleers,


A special Liberation Day pack has been added to the [EVE Store](https://secure.eveonline.com/). Pick one up, and you’ll get a new booster never before seen in New Eden, new Chainbreaker Khumaak SKINs for 8 ship hulls, Skill Points, and PLEX.

The new Heimatar Rise Accelerator, is inspired by the Minmatar uprising’s rallying call; the single word ‘rise!’. It offers a +6 bonus to all learning attributes. The Heimatar Rise Accelerator’s base duration is set at two days (or four days at max skill levels), so make sure to get the most out of it. 

Alongside it are the familiar Agency boosters - 'Pyrolancea' DB3, 'Hardshell' TB7 and 'Overclocker' SB9, providing further improvements. 

The pack also contains 1000 PLEX and 256,000 Skill Points. It’s only available at the [EVE Store](https://secure.eveonline.com/) between 11 and 18 July. Be sure not to miss out!

**Please note; this pack can only be purchased once per account.*


## **CHAINBREAKER KHUMAAK SKIN SALE**


The Liberation Day celebrations have also landed in the NES. Pilots can pick up classic Chainbreaker Khumaak SKINs with a 50% discount on their regular price. 

Made for select Minmatar hulls, the eye-catching SKINs are illuminated with holographic banners and detailing. The SKINs utilize a techonlogy to display banners on their hulls – proudly asserting Matari independence and culture.

Now is the time to head to the NES. The sale runs from 11-to-18 July exclusively. Don’t miss out!****
