# Anomic Base (Blood Raider)

- This is a tough mission, don't allow the unremarkable ship list fool you.**  Fit your ship as if you were doing a PVP fight; at the very least protect your capacitor against neuts.  If you speed tank, stay out of range.  And you will be pointed.

    - Make sure to review the neuting/webbing ranges!***
