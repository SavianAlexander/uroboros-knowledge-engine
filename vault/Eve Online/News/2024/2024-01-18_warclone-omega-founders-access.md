### Budding Vanguard,


The time approaches for Founders to go planetside once again in the next First Strike playtest from **25-29 January**. This Founders’ Access to the First Strike January playtest gives you the chance to try out EVE Vanguard, the in-development FPS module for EVE Online, and contribute to its ongoing development. The only requirement to join the playtest is to have Omega access, and there has never been a better time to upgrade, as both the [**EVE Store**](https://secure.eveonline.com) and the New Eden Store are offering 14 days of Omega access for a limited time, giving anyone interested in jumping into EVE Vanguard an unmissable opportunity. 

From now until **23:59 UTC on 28 January**, you can upgrade with 14 days of Omega time for only $9.99 in the EVE Store, or 250 PLEX in the NES. So, don’t miss the chance to go boots-on-the-ground and try out EVE Vanguard!
