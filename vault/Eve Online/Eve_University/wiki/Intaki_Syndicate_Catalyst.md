1. # Summary
The Intaki Syndicate Catalyst is a special edition of the **Catalyst** given out as a Buddy Program reward, similar to the **Aliastra Catalyst**, **Inner Zone Shipping Catalyst**, **InterBus Catalyst**, and the **Quafe Catalyst**. It is functionally equivalent to the standard Catalyst but with a different skin and additional flavor text.

All models were converted to a regular Catalyst and a corresponding Intaki Syndicate **SKIN** with the Mosaic release in April 2015.
