# Croir Arghe

- Croir Arghe** is an agent in **Gallente COSMOS**. He is a level 4 agent but his standing requirements are not clear, while normally L4 agents require 5.00 standing many sources claim he requires 6.00 standing.

Croir Arghe offers in total three missions. They are all hauling missions with very short routes. The last one requires at minimum 1200 m3 cargo spac so choose your ship with that in mind. **Magnate** with cargo expanding modules and rigs can do this and the normal hauling ships of course too.
