# Wormhole sites

This is a full list of **wormhole space** exploration sites.

  - Cosmic anomalies and ore sites** are warpable (colored green) in the scanner window when you enter a wormhole system, and do not need to be scanned down. **Data, relic, and gas sites**, on the other hand, must be **scanned down**. There are no **DED complexes** or acceleration gated rooms in wormhole space, with the exception of the **Drifter wormholes**, which are very specific in many ways and not the concern of this article.
