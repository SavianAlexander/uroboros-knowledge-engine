## Overview
The Drifters are retaliating. It’s time for the final push!

---

Capsuleer Day XXII: Nexus Reckoning has seen the war between capsuleers and the Drifters reach a boiling point. Pilots have been busy all over New Eden striking back at the scourge and reaping vast riches in the process. But now the Drifters are retaliating! 

In a final pushback under the codename Wrath of Tyrannos, the clash is set to reach its zenith. Starting today, the culmination of this month-long event sees Labyrinth Complex and Nexus Point sites appearing in highsec and lowsec, respectively, with the latter appearing in lowsec as Locus Point sites. This not only ups the ante in the fight against the Drifters, but the spoils of war are going up as well, as even more prizes are added to the seasonal track for Capsuleer Day XXII, making the struggle even more lucrative and worthwhile than before!  

This is your last chance to help your chosen faction gain a permanent foothold in Drifter wormhole systems, so a lot is riding on your actions.  

Don’t forget that both the daily login rewards, and the seasonal reward track, contain amazing Capsuleer Day Tyrantbreaker SKINs, in addition to a host of other items like SKINR components, Skill Points, and more.
