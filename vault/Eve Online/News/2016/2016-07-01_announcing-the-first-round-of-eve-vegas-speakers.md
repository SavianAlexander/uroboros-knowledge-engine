As October 28-30th draws closer, preperations have begun on what is set to be the largest [EVE Vegas](//vegas.eveonline.com/) ever!

With this in mind, we're happy to announce our first round of developer speakers who will be heading out with us to form part of the presentation and roundtable team at [Planet Hollywood](//www.caesars.com/planet-hollywood) over the course of the event. Our first confirmed developer attendees are:


- CCP Seagull - Executive Producer
- CCP Mimic - Development Producer
- CCP Ghost - Development Producer
- CCP Affinity - Game Designer
- CCP Rise - Game Designer
- CCP BlueScreen - Senior 3D Artist


Covering everything from Game Design to Ship Balancing and Scope Network Challenges to New Player Experience improvements, the schedule for this EVE Vegas is swiftly looking to be the most interesting and player focused we've ever had, with announcements, videos and of course the traditional round of Vegas style celebration of all things EVE. Coupled with this, we have our two first player presenters to announce:


- Jayne Fillon - How to be a content creator
- Tyrrax Thorrk - Guiding Hand Social Club History


Both will be joining us on stage at EVE Vegas 2016 to present their take on their favorite topics in EVE. If you're still looking to be part of the EVE Vegas presenter lineup, submissions are still open until July 27th, and you can read more about what we're looking for in presenters in [this news article!](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/eve-online-news/player-speaker-submissions-for-eve-vegas-2016-are-now-open/)

If you want to know more about EVE Vegas, feel free to check out the [EVE Vegas Website](//vegas.eveonline.com/), which will take you to the official EVE Vegas website, and if you haven't gotten hold of your tickets to the event yet, then what are you waiting for?

If you're still looking to pick up tickets for EVE Vegas 2016, you can head on over to the [EVE Vegas Eventbrite page](//www.eventbrite.com/e/eve-vegas-2016-tickets-24686308413). Picking up tickets today also makes sure that you're included in the [EVE Vegas 2016 Ticket Holder's Draw](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/eve-online-news/the-eve-vegas-2016-ticket-holders-draw/), with the chance to win upgrades to your EVE Vegas experience.

Be sure to grab tickets as soon as you can, though, as they're selling fast!

We're looking forward to seeing you all there!
