# Hetras Dakumon

- Hetras Dakumon** is a level 2 agent in **Amarr COSMOS**.

This agent gives two missions to retrieve items from COSMOS sites, two combat missions and one courier mission.

1. # Clean Hands – Blood Trail (1 of 5)

1. # Clean Hands – Rampart Cyst (2 of 5)

1. # Clean Hands – Iron Lady (3 of 5)

1. # Clean Hands – Rust in Peace (4 of 5)

1. # Clean Hands – Corpse Cauldron (5 of 5)
