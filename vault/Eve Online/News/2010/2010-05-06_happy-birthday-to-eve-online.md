**Seven years of EVE Online.** A perfect time to reflect on old victories and losses. A proper time to remember real life friendships forged during fleet ops. A good time to look back at the 12 expansions before Tyrannis to see how far we've come. And yet another reason to raise a glass to you, the pilots of New Eden, who have made EVE what it is today. **We thank you.** Here's to seven-hundred more.
