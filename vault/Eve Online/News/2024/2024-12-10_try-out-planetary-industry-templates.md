## Overview

---

### Industrious capsuleers,


The planets of New Eden have long presented rich opportunities for resourceful industrialists to extract and refine the materials that keep the cluster thriving. Now, there is a new way to streamline your Planetary Industry (PI) operations: Templates, allowing you to set up a planetary colony and save it to a PI browser. You can then share your layouts with your fellow capsuleers and corporation members, or use them to set up a new colony much faster than before.

Starting today, you can opt-in to test out these new work-in-progress templates using the Feature Previews tab in the Settings menu (by pressing 'Esc'). While not a big revamp of Planetary Industry, this is a quality-of-life improvement intended to simplify colony setup and management. The doors are being opened today so you can start kicking the tires and help us work out the kinks.

Your feedback will be crucial in shaping this and future updates, so please share your insights through the dedicated [forum thread](https://forums.eveonline.com/t/planetary-industry-templates/470630).
