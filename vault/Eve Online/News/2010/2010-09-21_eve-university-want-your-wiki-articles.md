Eve University, a name synonymous with helping new players find their feet in EVE Online, have four $50 EVE Store coupons to award to capsuleers. The contest is open to EVE players, and to enter you simply need to update or write new articles on [UniWiki](//wiki.eveuniversity.org/). The four people with the most detailed, comprehensive and professional-looking article edits will each win an [EVE Store](//store.eveonline.com/) coupon code worth $50, provided by CCP. Full details can be found [here](//forum.eveuniversity.org/viewtopic.php?f=28&t=33033).
