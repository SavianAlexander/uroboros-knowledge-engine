# Crimson Gauntlet (2020)

- Crimson Gauntlet** appears during the recurring **Crimson Harvest** event.

If a capsuleer is present at the Crimson Gauntlet it will appear in the Overview of every capsuleer in the same System.

Enemy ships can warp to tactical bookmarks.

|}

|}

  1. # 1st Room

- Three waves of 6-9 random frigate/cruiser/battlecruiser rats.
- The acceleration gate opens after second wave is destroyed. Third wave does not need to be destroyed.
- Cthonic Attar can be mined.

  1. # 2nd Room

- Three waves of 6-9 random frigate/cruiser/battlecruiser rats.
- Fourth wave is one Harvester Overseer. Overseer webs and scrams. The overseer drops the valuable loot. He also sometimes drops the Crimson Gauntlet Shortcut Keypass which allows skipping the second room.
- 18x Cthonic Attar
