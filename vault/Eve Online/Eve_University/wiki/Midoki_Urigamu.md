# Midoki Urigamu

- Midoki Urigamu** is a level 3 agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located in Sakkikainen - Settler's Waystation.

1. # Pit Stop Slash Saloon - Need a Helping Hand (1 of 4)

Transport 100 Construction Workers (300 m3) from Liekuri VII - Moon 22 - Expert Distribution Warehouse to agent.

1. # Pit Stop Slash Saloon - Materials For Construction (2 of 4)

Transport 7 Large Crates of Construction Blocks (4900 m3) from Ishisomo VIII - Propel Dynamics Factory to agent.

1. # Pit Stop Slash Saloon - The Construction Tools (3 of 4)

Retrieve 60 Construction Tools (600 m3) from **Urigamu‘s Warehouse** deadspace complex near Otitoh V - Moon 1 to agent.

1. # Pit Stop Slash Saloon - Eliminate The Competition (4 of 4)

Destroy the **Caraton Pleasure Gardens** deadspace complex in Sakkikainen, then report back to the agent.
