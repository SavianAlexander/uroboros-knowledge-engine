We're happy to present the first 2016 video update from CCP Seagull, Executive Producer for EVE Online.

In this update, CCP Seagull talks about features coming to New Eden over the course of the next few releases as we move toward Fanfest 2016, including player involvement in assisting the Upwell Consortium with [construction of the first ever Astrahus class citadel](//updates.eveonline.com/date/2016-01-25/), news of the new EVE Launcher moving out of beta and into full release, as well as [Skill Trading](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/skill-trading-in-new-eden/) and a short [update on the roadmap](//updates.eveonline.com/) for the start of the year.

Also, remember that for the latest updates, announcements and news on EVE Online, you'll want to be in the heart of the action here in Reykjavik at Fanfest on April 21-23rd 2016!

Tickets are still on sale, and are available along with further information [on the Fanfest website](//fanfest.eveonline.com/).
