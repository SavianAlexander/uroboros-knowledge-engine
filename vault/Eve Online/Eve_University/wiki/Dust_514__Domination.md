# Dust 514: Domination

1. # Domination

A 'King of the Hill' style variant, based on Skirmish but with just one Null Cannon to fight others.

The game can go on for a little while longer as both teams battle to control one point. This is a popular game style as the style of fighting is often frantic and lends itself well to the use of Drop Uplinks, a popular way for a team to gain more War Points.
