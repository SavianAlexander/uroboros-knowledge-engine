We are happy to announce that the [Tiamat](//community.eveonline.com/releases/tiamat) release has been successfully deployed!

Tiamat brings the new Minmatar Tactical Destroyer, [the Svipul](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/the-republic-strikes-back-the-svipul-tactical-destroyer-and-projectile-changes-in-tiamat/), along with balancing to [projectile weapons](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/the-republic-strikes-back-the-svipul-tactical-destroyer-and-projectile-changes-in-tiamat/), many more [corp little things and friendly fire control](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/corp-little-things-friendly-fire-control/), visual updates to structures in missions and deadspace areas, new UI icon additions, more themed UI elements,  quality settings for sun flares, atmospheric sounds for pirate faction structures, improvements to the audio engine, additions to turret audio and of course a new [theme tune](//soundcloud.com/ccpgames/eve-online-tiamat-2015-release) for Tiamat.

In addition to these features, a number of new inhabitants of New Eden will be emerging from their slumber to begin exploring, their motives unknown and their tolerance for capsuleers untested until first contact.

You can read more about what's coming in the "[Coming to EVE in the Tiamat Release](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/coming-to-eve-in-tiamat/)" devblog by CCP Seagull, and check out the [full patch notes here](//community.eveonline.com/news/patch-notes/patch-notes-for-tiamat).
