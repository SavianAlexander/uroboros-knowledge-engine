# Retin Ariato

- Retin Ariato** is a level 2 agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located in Ishisomo at beacon "Rusty Ridge Mine". If you can't see them, ensure that "Beacons" and "Agents in Space" are visible in your overview settings.

1. # Wiyrkomi Freelancing – Missing Weapon Stash (1 of 4)

Bring 3 Wiyrkomi Rifles (15 m3) to the agent. You can find them in the **Contested Caldari Lai Dai Refinery**.

1. # Wiyrkomi Freelancing - Another Task (2 of 4)

Pick up 3x 1MN Afterburner I (15 m³) from a station in the same system and bring them to the agent.

1. # Wiyrkomi Freelancing - An Ancient Monument (3 of 4)

Pick up 3x Excavation Equipment (3 m³) in the neighboring system and bring it to the agent.

1. # Wiyrkomi Freelancing - Unsuccessful Excavation (4 of 4)

Ancient Weapon drop from Storage Silo in **Contested Caldari Lai Dai Refinery** - Contested Scordite field.
