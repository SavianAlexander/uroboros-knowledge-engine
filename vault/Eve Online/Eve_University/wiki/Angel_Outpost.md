# Angel Outpost

The **Angel Outpost** is an **unrated cosmic signature combat site** that can be scanned using Core Probes in **Angel** infested regions of Low Sec space. Estimated payout 8.5 mil ISK, not counting faction drops.

To complete this site you will need quite strong tank to survive the high dps present in the second pocket - a T2 tanked Drake should have no trouble.
