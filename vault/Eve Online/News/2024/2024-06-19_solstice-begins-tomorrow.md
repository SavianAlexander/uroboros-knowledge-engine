## Overview
Brace for FPS combat on a hostile new tropical planet!

---

**Attention Vanguard, **

The Solstice event begins tomorrow at 12:00 UTC and runs until 12:00 UTC 1 July. The build is already available on the EVE launcher so be sure to [**download it now**](https://vanguard.eveonline.com/) to ensure you're locked and loaded. This marks a significant step forward in the development of EVE Vanguard as the ultimate sci-fi sandbox FPS, set in the EVE Universe. 

Among the new additions coming with this event is an immersive yet treacherous new battleground with exotic flora, polluted waters, added verticality, and close-quarter combat that will test Vanguard warclones in unfamiliar terrain. 

The update also debuts the adaptive weapon system, allowing players to modify gun behavior with swappable chipsets and switch between firing modes in combat. 

New industry-focused objectives, tactical gadgets like proximity mines and motion scanners, and weapon SKINs round out the major additions. 

With this combination of features, Solstice represents a leap forward in the evolution of the sandbox FPS experience, and of the Vanguard warclones by the Deathless Circle. Wherever this shrouded organization’s true ambitions may ultimately lie, it is clear that the development of this new breed of immortal warclone – and acquisition of related materials – is one of their top priorities. 

 For an in-depth look at the challenges that await in Solstice, watch the latest In Focus video.  

The Solstice event is a prime opportunity to experience the evolution of EVE Vanguard. Your skill and dedication in the combat zones will not only shape the future of the game, but also be **well-rewarded**. 

Those who rack up the most impressive achievements can earn exclusive rewards. 


## TO THE VICTORS GO THE SPOILS


Those of you who have participated in previous playtests may be eligible for rewards for your valor in combat. Everyone who participated in the First Strike December event will receive the Arkombine gun SKIN, and those who finished at least four out of six event challenges over the course of the monthly playtests will receive the EDENCOM SKIN. Eligible players will receive further information in their inboxes. In addition to these rewards, all warclones who participate in the Solstice event for at least one hour will receive a commemorative Solstice gun SKIN at a later date, so be sure to drop in and enter the fray to claim this bonus.  


## JOIN THE GROWING RANKS OF VANGUARD


The Solstice event is open to all Omega players, and now is the perfect time to upgrade if you haven’t already. Act now to [claim a free 7- day Omega offer](https://support.evevanguard.com/hc/en-us/articles/12864823872284-Accessing-EVE-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Guide), valid until 20 June.  

After that, until 1 July, you can purchase the Warclone Omega Bundle to receive 14 days of Omega along with exclusive Arkombine Arisen SKINs for the Barghest and Moros Navy Issue. 

All you have to do as an Omega clone is to install EVE Vanguard and as soon as the Solstice event begins, you’ll be ready for the combat zone. 


## SEIZE THE MOMENT, SHAPE THE FUTURE


Solstice marks the final public event in EVE Vanguard for the coming months, and we encourage all players who want to contribute to its development to participate. For the next phase of EVE Vanguard’s development, EVE Vanguard Founders’ members will have the chance to continue playing the game in private playtests and help shape the future of ground combat in New Eden. Throughout Solstice and beyond, players can join the EVE Vanguard Founders’ community [**on Discord**](http://discord.gg/evevanguard), where they will have direct access to developers, get a first look at concepts and prototypes, and make their mark in the ongoing development process. We look forward to announcing further public events and playtests in the future.
