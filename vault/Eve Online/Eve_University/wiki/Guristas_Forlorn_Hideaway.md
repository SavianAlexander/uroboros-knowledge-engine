# Guristas Forlorn Hideaway

The **Guristas Forlorn Hideaway** is a **combat anomaly** found in **Guristas** infested regions. It is a level 4 variant of the **Guristas Hideaway**.

1. # Walkthrough
Single ungated normal space pocket. Killing the last hostile ship triggers a reinforcement spawn.
