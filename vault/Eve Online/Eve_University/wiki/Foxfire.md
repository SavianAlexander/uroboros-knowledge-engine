# Foxfire

<noinclude> </noinclude>

Accept the Mission remotely.

Gated deadspace pocket one jump away in AJCJ-1.

  - Blitz:** take the Acceleration Gate, then destroy Guristas Sentry Guns. Destroy the Caldari Point Defense Batteries, then destroy the Caldari Navy Storage Bunker and pick up Lieutenant Kipo “Foxfire” Tekira.

Tips:
- Decloak the Acceleration Gate by destroying all the Guristas Sentry Guns
- You will lose 2.4% Caldari State Faction Standing when you destroy the Guristas Sentry Guns
- You will lose 2.4% Caldari State Faction Standing if you destroy the Caldari Stasis Tower or Caldari Point Defense Battery
- If you don't kill the Caldari Navy Destroyer, reinforcements won't spawn
