# Rogue Drone Outgrowth Colony

Rogue Drone Outgrowth Colony is a **Researching the Rogues** combat site event where you can get valuable stuff. Enemy damage is split 50%/50% between 2 damage types, with the Colony Nest Overseer being somewhat omni with an 18%x3 + 45%x1 spread randomly. The frigates and destroyers have 0% resists in all categories while the Colony Nest Overseer has an omni tank of 33% structure and 40% Armour/Shields.

The Colony Nest Overseer is very powerful and very dangerous. It will randomly warp-in when there is 1-4 enemies left (unconfirmed). It uses Web at 10km, Neut at 5km, and Disrupt at 25km. It will warp-in at 400-500km away. After that it will warp about 20km from you. There it instantly locks you, but does not instantly disrupt (2 second delay until it activates its disruptor).

It will drop 3 Overseer Effects, but Rarely drops Random Kador, Semiotique, Valimor Legacy, and Wiyrkomi SKINs, Random Dose, Drone BPC.
