## Overview
FOR ALL EXPERIENCE LEVELS

---

Two new packs are now available in the EVE Store to help rookies and veterans alike make the most of Capsuleer Day XXIII and the 23rd anniversary of EVE Online. 

The First Flight Pack is built for capsuleers looking for a strong start and includes a learning expert system to help you fly Tech II ships for the first time, in addition to Skill Points, PLEX, a unique SKIN, and more. 

For veteran capsuleers looking to stock up and stand out, the Empire Warpath Pack is stuffed with value. Among other things, it includes a wealth of PLEX, SP, SKINR components, four unique SKINs, and much more. 

All the SKINs in these packs are empire themed, and turn every engagement into a spectacle, with dynamic effects that respond to repairs, deceleration, and raw distance traveled. From blazing brake lights to triumphant fireworks, each SKIN celebrates decisive action, bringing style, momentum, and victory to the front lines of New Eden.
