1. # Otomainen V Mining Operations
When you warp in Jorek Lephny will warn you that his friend Markus will turn your ass into biomass unless you get out. You of course are here to kill Jorek and Markus.

Lephny's Mining Boat is easy to kill. Markus Ikami on the other hand has very strong active shield tank (1500 HP/10 s). you may need to bring a friend to take him down. Best damage to use is kinetic.

Update 27.01.2026.: Markus Ikmani's tank is nothing special. 660 DPS Heavy Missile Tengu destroyed it in 5-6 volleys.
