# Sansha War Supply Complex

- Sansha War Supply Complex** is a **DED Complex** that can be scanned down with probes, and can be found in low security space in some Amarr regions or in null security regions with Sansha presence. It can also be received as an escalation from the **Sansha Rally Point**.

The site is behind an acceleration gate.  Warping to the side lands you at 0 on the gate.

This site is rated DED 6/10.  3 Battlecruisers and a Battleship ran this site easily. A well tanked T3 Cruiser is usually sufficient to clear the site in ~30-40 minutes.

|}

  1. # Room 3

Group 1 auto-aggros on landing. Group 2 will not aggro unless approached or attacked and will shoot drones if not aggressed first by a player.

|}

  1. # Room 4

The two sentries and the group camping the warp-in will auto-aggro on landing. Attacking Skomener Effotber will aggro the entire pocket.

Skomener Effotber has strong armor tank that needs at least 600 DPS of pure EM damage (70% weakest resist) to break. He does ~450 dps EM/Thermal damage at 70km range. Combined with the full room aggro the total incoming damage in this pocket can well exceed 1000 dps. There are no webbing ships so speed tanking will reduce the room damage by a lot.

|}

  - Blitz:** Kill Skomener Effotber, bookmark wreck, warp out, wait 3 minutes, warp back to loot wreck.

1. # Loot

You can get about 24 million ISK in bounties and a similar amount of meta module loot.  There is an opportunity to get Centum Cruiser A-type Module drop, and you are guaranteed a 19th Tier Overseer's Personal Effects (~51 million ISK).
