# Beizan Picholen (2 of 6)

<noinclude>

Gallente **data center** storyline mission
Agent: **Avrue Auz**
Agent location: Lirsautton I - CreoDron Factory 
Standings required: none
Parts: **(prequel)**, **(1 of 6)**, **(2 of 6)**, **(3 of 6)**, **(4 of 6)**, **(5 of 6)**, **(6 of 6)**</noinclude>

The agent tells you to pick up some reports from a station 2 jumps away. The reports are the generic variant, so you can bring your own to avoid traveling. The rewarded "FedMart Reports" allows you to take missions from **Mamo Guerre** whom you find in the next mission.
