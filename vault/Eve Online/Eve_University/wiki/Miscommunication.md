# Miscommunication

<noinclude> </noinclude>

Gated semi-deadspace pocket up to nine jumps away.

  - Blitz:** hack the Venal Regional Comms Tower and warp out.

Complete the Mission remotely.

Tips:
- Remember to equip the Data Analyzer before undocking
- Holoreel GRS-81A is a lore item
- Venal Regional Comms Tower is a Easy difficulty hack (70 HP green core). Since you will most likely not be using a hacking bonused ship if you do not have prior experience with the epic arc, take your time on the hack.
- Three frigates sit on the warp-in in room 1 and auto-aggro on landing. The rest of the fleet is spaced out much further away and aggros relatively slowly as long as you don't shoot at them.
