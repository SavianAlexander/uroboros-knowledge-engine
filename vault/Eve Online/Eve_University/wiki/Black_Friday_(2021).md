# Black Friday (2021)

- Black Friday** was a limited time event, including a PLEX sale and a booster login campaign, lasting from 25 November to 30 November, downtime to downtime.

1. # PLEX Sale
Between 25 and 30 November, selected **PLEX** packages were given a discount.

- 110 PLEX - 25% off
- 240 PLEX - 20% off
- 500 PLEX - 20% off
- 1100 PLEX - 15% off
- 2860 PLEX - 15% off plus free SKIN (**Vedmak** Copper Lightning SKIN)
- 7430 PLEX - 15% off plus 3 free SKINs (**Vedmak**, **Cynabal**, & **Nightmare** Copper Lightning SKIN)
- 15400 PLEX - 15% off plus 5 free SKINs (**Vedmak**, **Cynabal**, **Nightmare**, **Charon**, & **Rorqual** Copper Lightning SKIN)

1. # NES Sale

Between 25 and 30 November, 1 month Omega and MCTs came with a free SKINs.

- 485 PLEX - 1 Multiple Pilot Training Certificate plus free SKIN (**Kikimora** Copper Lightning SKIN)
- 500 PLEX - 1 Month of Omega plus free SKIN (**Drekavac** Copper Lightning SKIN)

1. # Bonus Booster Gifts

Capsuleers had five days to log in for four days worth of rewards. Omega Clones had access to rewards worth ~10k **Skill Points**.
