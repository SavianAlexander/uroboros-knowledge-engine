1. # Summary
Nefantar Thrasher is a special edition of the **Thrasher** given out as a reward for the pre-purchase of EVE: The Second Decade Collector's Edition. It is functionally equivalent to the standard Thrasher but with a different skin and flavor text.

All models were converted to a regular Thrasher and a corresponding Nefantar **SKIN** with the Mosaic release in April 2015.
