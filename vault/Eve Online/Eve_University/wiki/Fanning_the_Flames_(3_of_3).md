# Fanning the Flames (3 of 3)

<noinclude>
  - Gallente COSMOS** mission
Agent: **Croir Arghe** 
Agent location: Arid Park (Alsottobier)
Standings required: 6.00 (or perhaps 5.00)
Parts: **(1 of 3)**, **(2 of 3)**, **(3 of 3)**</noinclude>

In this courier mission you will need to pick the items from one station and drop in another station. 6 jumps in total to complete the mission. A **magnate** with expanders and cargo rigs can get enough cargo space and they are quite fast compared to hauling ships. Alternatively use a real hauling ship. The mission can be completed remotely.<noinclude>
