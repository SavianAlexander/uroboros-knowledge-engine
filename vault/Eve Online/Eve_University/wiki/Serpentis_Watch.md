# Serpentis Watch

- Serpentis Watch** is an **Unrated Complex** which can be found in **Hi-sec** through **exploration**. The biggest ships in this site are two battlecruisers, one in each pocket. Serpentis rats weakest resistance is against **thermal damage** and they on average deal 55% **thermal damage** and 45% **kinetic damage**. If using a drone boat, set drones to passive and focus fire so they won't accidentally shoot the trigger ship and spawn the next wave, overwhelming you.

This site is possible to complete in a T1 Cruiser, however the damage can easily become excessive if care is not taken in managing the triggers. It is recommended to bring a well-tanked T2 Heavy Assault Cruiser or Battlecruiser.

1. # Walkthrough

You will arrive at an ancient acceleration gate. 

  1. # First Pocket
There are three options for unlocking the Acceleration gate to the next room.

- Destroy each wave until the fourth wave, and destroy the Corelum Militant Commander
- Approach the Acceleration gate, which sets off the alarm and spawns the fourth wave, skipping waves 2 and 3, and destroy the Corelum Militant Commander. If you cannot tank the rats, destroy all of them EXCEPT FOR THE CORELUM SCOUT.
- Unlock the Acceleration gate by having a Serpentis Silver Tag in your inventory (the tag is consumed).

  1. # Second Pocket

|}

Tower wave is triggered by attacking the Starbase Control Tower. It will not spawn if the Gate Wave is already destroyed. 

|}

Gate wave is triggered by attacking the Smuggler's Gate. Simply using an EWAR module(preferably Target Painter) works. Shooting at the gate will also trigger tower wave if it has not been triggered already.

The gate wave consists of one large group by the gate and three smaller groups scattered around the site. Once all groups are in range the incoming dps can be too much for t1 cruiser or even a battlecruiser.

|}

The commander wave is triggered by destroying Supervisor's Guardians (in the Gate Wave). The Shadow Serpentis frigate will approach you very quickly, at 1700 m/s. He can be separated from the others, therefore avoiding a lot of DPS. Supervisor's Henchmen tend to keep behind the supervisor and regularly retarget drones.
