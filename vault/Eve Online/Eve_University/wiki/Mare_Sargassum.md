# Mare Sargassum

1. # Overview
  - Mare Sargassum** is an **expedition** found by completing **Desolate Site** combat sites.  It may contain up to four parts.  The first three will be in high-sec space, while the fourth can be in low-sec or high-sec.
The sites do not have gates, and can be easily completed in a cruiser.

1. # Walkthrough

  1. # Part 1
----

The initial defenders can vary wildly.

The initial defenders consists of around 6 frigates and one sentient drone.
- Note: Trash Construction is not vulnerable (despite warp-in message) - just approach it to trigger reinforcement wave. (On 26-Nov-YC124 destroying elite frigates didn't trigger reinforcement wave.)

|}

Reinforcement wave triggers when the elite frigates are destroyed. It is possible to have this wave included in initial defenders, possibly due to warping through the trash construct.

|}

Two additional spawns triggered by killing ships from initial spawn

|}

|}

  1. # Part 2
----
There is at least one reinforcement wave. The escalation can be triggered by killing either the Sentient drone, or all enemies *except* the Sentient drone. 

|}

|}

  1. # Part 3
----
Fewer rogue drones, and killing the sentient drone once again triggered the escalation. As a bonus, you get to see a great big red cube in space. The Borg turned more evil?

|}

|}

  1. # Part 4
----
This part may be in a low-sec system (26 Feb 2017 ended in a .5 system / 26 Apr 2017 ended in .6 system / 26 Aug 2019 ended in a .3 system / 9 Dec 2019 ended in a .5 system / 3 Sep 2020 ended in a .2 system / 18 Mar 2024 ended in Ahbazon / 9 Nov 2024 ended in a .5 system).

5 small rogue drones waiting on warpin, and 5 "Drone Hold" cans. Completion popup happened after blowing up the first wave and starting to open cans. Strain Splinter drones can warp scram and web. Two reinforcement waves later appeared, one with a Rogue Mining Overseer dropping T2 salvage. One of the Drone Hold cans contained some drone goo (Dark Compound, Condensed Compound). All drone hold cans can be empty.

|}

|}

|}

|}

1. # Rewards
Each part has a commander rat which, when killed, will drop T2 salvage and Rogue Drone components.  Lootable cans may contain drone alloys.
