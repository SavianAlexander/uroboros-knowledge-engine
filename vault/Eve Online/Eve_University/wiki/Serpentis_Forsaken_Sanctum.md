# Serpentis Forsaken Sanctum

- Forsaken Serpentis Sanctum** is a **combat anomaly** found in Serpentis infested null security regions.

1. # Site

Consists of one ungated pocket. Warping in from certain directions can cause you to decloak; even at 100km.

Core Fleet Admiral will do Explosive damage in addition to Kin/Therm.
