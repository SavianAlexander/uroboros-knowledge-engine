# Dewy Nebula

- Dewy Nebula*' is a gas exploration site containing gas used in the production of **Medical boosters**.

This is a null sec gas site found through Exploration.

Minable Gas Clouds:

2 Amber Cytoserocin (1,000 and 2,000 units each gas clouds)

Amber Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Pure Standard Blue Pill Booster.

This site is found only in the[E-8CSQ](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Vale_of_the_Silent/E-8CSQ)constellation of the[Vale of the Silent](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Vale_of_the_Silent)region of space.
