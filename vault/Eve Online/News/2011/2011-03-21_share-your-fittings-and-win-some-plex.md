With the [PVP tournament](//fanfest.eveonline.com/en/events/pvptournament) at Fanfest less than a week away, we are inviting players to come up with the best possible ship set ups for players to use during the event. We are working with our friends at Battleclinic who have customized their [online fitting tool](//eve.battleclinic.com/browse_loadouts.php) which will allow players to submit fittings for all Tech I cruisers and Assault Frigates.

The winners will be chosen by the PVP Tournament organizers and your load out could be the one that wins this event. We will choose the top three fittings with winners receiving two PLEX as a reward. To be in with a chance of winning, simply post your fitting in [this thread](//www.eveonline.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=1485350).
