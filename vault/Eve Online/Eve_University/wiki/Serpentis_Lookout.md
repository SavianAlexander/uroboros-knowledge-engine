# Serpentis Lookout

No NPCs at initial warp in.
Initial gate will allow Tech 2 and 3 cruisers to pass.

1. # Walkthrough

While mining after the site is complete, an additional frigate may occasionally spawn.

  1. # First Room
The acceleration gate to next room is locked until all ships have been destroyed. The missile battery does not need to be destroyed to proceed. Shooting at each group once before deploying drones avoids drone aggro.

The gate to the second room is 27km from warp in.

|}

  1. # Second Room
To trigger commander spawn destroy the destroyers by the solar harvesters. These destroyers are on left side when looking at the enemies. Escalation can be triggered by killing the commander or by destroying the Serpentis control center.
