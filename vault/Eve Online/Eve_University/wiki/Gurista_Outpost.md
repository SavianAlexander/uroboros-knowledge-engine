# Gurista Outpost

1. # Overview
This **combat site** is an unrated complex which can be found in **Low-sec** through **exploration**.
  1. # Difficulty & Ship Recommendation
You can do first pocket in **Assault Frigate** with ~200DPS, orbiting at 10km. Second pocket is same except you need to manually control your range due to large asteroid in center of complex.
