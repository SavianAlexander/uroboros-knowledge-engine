1. # Class Overview
Wormhole PVE introduces the basics of PVE in Wormholes. It will give an overview of what PVE in Wormhole space can look like, from Solo PVE to Fleet PVE.

This class covers the following topics:

- Why wormhole PVE?
- Wormhole Classes
- Wormhole Effects
- Planetary Industry
- Gas Huffing
- Exploration (Hacking)
- Ore Anomalies
- Combat Sites
- Q&A
