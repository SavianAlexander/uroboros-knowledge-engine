# Chemical Yard

- Chemical Yard** is an unrated combat site that can be found by **Probe scanning** in high and low security regions. The acceleration gates in site allow only T1 and pirate faction cruisers and below to enter.
1. # Walkthrough
  1. # First Pocket

|}

Reinforcements arrive once all cruisers from initial defenders are destroyed.

|}

  1. # Second Pocket
----
The group nearest to warpin aggresses as you land. Destroying this group triggers commander wave. Destroying the reinforcement wave from group 3 is most likely second escalation trigger.

Each group aggresses when approached or attacked. Groups 3 and 4 are far enough that they will not aggress unless specifically approached and can be ignored. Group 4 spawns differently sometimes, it may have 2 cruisers and 2 battlecruisers or 3 cruisers.
