# Rogue Eradication

<noinclude></noinclude>

Video: [Barghest Walkthrough (Jan 2025)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P8l3_JWeNtY&list=PLr-IftNTIujQFGN_KtZKCcC2CTXTPVnbY&index=3)

  1. # First Pocket
- NPCs await your ship in two clusters: One behind you and one to your left.
[Orientation of ship in first pocket.](http://i609.photobucket.com/albums/tt174/climb22033/RogueEradication5-1.jpg)

|}

  1. # Second Pocket
- Acceleration Gate dangerously lands your ship on top of a Drone Bunker and several Wall structures that can entangle your ship.
- Landing spots vary slightly per gate activation so entanglement is chance based.
[Close ships at warp-in.](http://i609.photobucket.com/albums/tt174/climb22033/RogueEradication5-2.jpg)

|}

|}

  1. # Notes
- Plan your tank's resistances and repair for up to 2010 hp/s of damage from initial agro. Dealt as:
  - Pocket 1 - 1,728 hp/s (Therm: 46%, Expl: 26%, Kin: 22%, EM: 6%)
  - Pocket 2 - 2,010 hp/s (Therm: 36%, Expl: 35%, Kin: 15%, EM: 14%)
- Use a passive tank. Or plan a capacitor that recharges or receives 80 Units/s.
