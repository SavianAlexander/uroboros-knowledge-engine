# Union Day Elite Combat Challenge

The **Union Day Elite Combat Challenge** is a limited-time combat site that spawns during the **Caldari Union Day (2022)** in Caldari Lowsec space. It contains 4 rooms with different faction enemies, being the Gallente Federation, Triglavian Collective, Rogue Drones, and the Guristas Pirates, in order. 

Notes:
- You will NOT get any faction standing losses from killing any opponents in this site.
- You will receive a considerable amount of neut pressure while doing this site, especially on the second room.
- There are no other differences with the highsec variant alongside the room shuffling, new boss, and the increased amount of rats on each pocket.
