# Nature Pictures

<noinclude>
</noinclude>

  - Objective**
Go to Atlangeins V - Moon 13 - The Scope Development Studio, and pick up 1x Encoded Data Transmission (50 m³). Transport it back to Sister Alitura at Arnon IX - Moon 3 - Sisters of EVE Bureau station ().

  - Notes**
This is a simple, safe courier mission. Set your destination for Atlangeins and travel the four high sec jumps there. Pick up the cargo, return to Arnon, and report back to Sister Alitura to complete the mission.
