1. # Summary
Inner Zone Shipping Catalyst is a special edition of the **Catalyst** given out as a Buddy Program reward, similar to the **Aliastra Catalyst**, **Intaki Syndicate Catalyst**, **InterBus Catalyst**, and the **Quafe Catalyst**. It is functionally equivalent to the standard Catalyst but with a different skin and additional flavor text.

All models were converted to a regular Catalyst and a corresponding Inner Zone Shipping **SKIN** with the Mosaic release in April 2015. Despite this, the model was given a unique quote with the release of the Revenant expansion in November 2024, over nine years after all copies of the ship were converted out of existence.
