1. # Summary
The Faction Version of the **Moros**. Compared to its parent hull, the faction version sports an extra bonus to warp scrambler and disruptor range and strength, a 50% higher damage bonus per level of , an extra mid slot, and extra bonuses to capital hybrid **turret** optimal range and capital armor repairer amount. Additionally, as is typical for a faction hull, the Moros Navy Issue has higher base tank, scan resolution, sensor strength, cpu, and powergrid.
