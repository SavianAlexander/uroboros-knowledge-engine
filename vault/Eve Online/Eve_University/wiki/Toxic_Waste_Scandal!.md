# Toxic Waste Scandal!

- Toxic Waste Scandal** is a rare **Expedition** that can escalate from **Angel Base**.

It consists of 2 parts.
1. # Walkthrough

  1. # First Location
You land at an unlocked acceleration gate. Take it if there is no commander or if destroying it does not trigger escalation.

|}
  1. ## First Pocket
No enemies are present except for several storage silos. One of them deals about 1000-2000 damage to all ships in proximity. The damage scales rapidly if the silos are approached.

|}

The escalation fails silently if no escalation is triggered from the storage silos. *Your ship starts taking damage upon entry. Warp off and return and the leakage should be gone. If safe upon return, destroy silos until one is marked and yields a new location for escalation*
