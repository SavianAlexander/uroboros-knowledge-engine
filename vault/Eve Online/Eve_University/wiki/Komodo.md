1. # Summary
Staying true to the **Guristas** philosophy of combining **missiles** and **drones** in combat, the **Komodo** is unique in that it can command one squadron of heavy **fighters** and one squadron of light fighters, with the hull bonuses giving each squadron the damage of two, as well as doubling their durability. The Komodo is the only **Titan** to feature a resist bonus with its 4% shield resist bonus per level of . Thanks to that resist bonus, the Komodo's faction hitpoint increase, Sov upgrades, Implants, and weather, the Komodo is the tankiest ship in the entire game, able to fit a theoretical maximum of up to 352 *million* EHP without overheating hardeners. By overheating the hardeners a maximum of 582 *million* EHP then becomes temporarily possible. Despite this, however as of 10 April 2026, 6 Komodos have died in the game [zKillboard](https://zkillboard.com/ship/45649/losses/), more than any other faction titan except the **Vanquisher**. 4 of those Komodos died due to being caught without adequate support and another, a Fraternity Komodo, was killed during an ambush dread bomb by almost 200 Imperium dreads. 

Blueprint copies for this ship can be acquired from the insurgency LP stores in Zarzakh or the pirate FOB in insurgency systems.
