# Rogue Trial Yard

1. # Overview
This **combat site** is a **Unrated Complex** which can be found in **Low-sec** via **exploration**. Its signal strength is high (20%), making it easy to scan down.
1. # Walkthrough
  1. # First Pocket

Spatial Rift is roughly 7-10km from warp-in. Depending on the angle that you land you may end up <5 km from the rift and immediately trigger the proximity spawn.

  1. # Second Pocket
Frigates will be 25-30km away and will aggro very quickly. Destroyers are 50-60km away and will not aggro unless approached or attacked.

1. # Rewards
Total bounty about 1.8 M ISK. (recent 01/2017 run gave 2.69M ISK, plus ~11M ISK in Rogue Drone Component drops)
May drop Sentient drone components:
- Sentient Damage Control, 2017-08-26, 270 mill +
- Sentient Drone Navigation Computer, 2018-04-29, ~135M
- Rogue Drone 43-X Nexus Chip, 2020-10-19, ~119M ISK
- Red box doesn't work: 4mil bounty and 5.6mil salvage/loot 2023-01-29

1. # Escalation
Killing the Sentient Annihilator Alvum may start the escalation chain **Moving Day**.
