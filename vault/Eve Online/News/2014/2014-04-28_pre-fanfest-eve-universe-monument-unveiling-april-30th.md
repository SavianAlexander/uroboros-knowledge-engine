As part of the kick off to [Fanfest 2014](//fanfest.eveonline.com/en/default), CCP would like to cordially invite all Fanfest attendees to the unveiling and dedication of the EVE Universe Monument on April 30th, 2014.

Please join us on Rastargata in 101 Reykjavik, close to CCP headquarters at 17:00 UTC on Wednesday, April 30th for the celebration ceremony.

For further information on the EVE Universe Monument, please see the [official website](//eve.com/monument), and the [Fanfest 2014 schedule.](//web.ccpgamescdn.com/fanfest/2014/FF2014_Brochure_WEB.pdf)

We hope to see you there!
