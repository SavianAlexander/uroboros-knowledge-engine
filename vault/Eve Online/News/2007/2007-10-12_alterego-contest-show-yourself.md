Everyone is coming to Fanfest, and we want to see who you are. We're running a [competition](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2007/alteregocontest.asp) on which Fanfest attendee can do the best meld of their avatar and real-life face. You'll be put up alongside our devs in the Fanfest advertising, and will of course have the chance to receive rewards during the event itself.

Discussion of this news item may be found in this [forum thread](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=612438).

Please note that the rules for this has changed and the contest is open to all players!
