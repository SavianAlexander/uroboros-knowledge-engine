## Overview
Extended reward track and bigger escalations.

---

The Gallente Election event is reaching fever pitch, and your chance to shape the future of the Federation is running short!

The reward track for supporting your selected candidate has been extended by 50%, from 1,000 to 1,500 points. New rewards include 75,000 SP, more political favors you can use with item traders, a special SKINR component, and a final reward of four Presidential Candidate Favors – enough to trade for a powerful and valuable new officer module!

And of course, more points mean added support for your presidential candidate and more influence on the results.

The Rampant Drone Fabricator sites now see a new escalation, where the presence of any capital ship will dramatically increase the difficulty and reward amount.

Completing the event challenges can give you PLEX, Skill Points, new hybrid drones, a unique Campaign Bus, new and powerful officer modules, and much more. These exclusive hybrid drones both deal damage and disrupt enemies with EWAR, a powerful new, and exclusive combat combination.

Don’t forget to trade in your political favors before the event ends on 16 April!
