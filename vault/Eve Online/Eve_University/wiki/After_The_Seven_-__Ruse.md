# After The Seven - "Ruse"

<noinclude> 
This mission is issued by a Security division agent and is part 4 of the 5 part mission chain **After The Seven**</noinclude>

A **Caldari Shuttle** will be granted when accepting the mission. Use the shuttle to accomplish the mission.

  1. # 1st pocket
When you warp in Anakism is waiting, he won't know who you are so he won't attack you. Make your way to the container and grab the kidnapped citizens.

Once you grab the citizens Anakism realizes who you are and will attack you. Rush to the second gate and warp to second pocket.

|}
When approaching the holding cells at ~20km

When at about 5.5km form the cells

Now Anakism turns hostile

  1. # 2nd pocket
You will land next to a stolen CONCORD ship (a container). Transfer the hostages into the container and the mission is completed.

|}
When delivering the Kidnapped Citizens
