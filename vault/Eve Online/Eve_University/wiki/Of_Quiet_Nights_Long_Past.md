  - Objective**
Fly to the monastery at the designated location in Lisudeh, destroy all the pirates, and acquire 1x Strange Coded Document (0.1 m³) - return it to the [Lisudeh IV - Moon 4 - Theology Council Tribunal station](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/station/Lisudeh_IV_-_Moon_4_-_Theology_Council_Tribunal).

  - Notes**
Warp to the encounter. In about 35-50km distance you will be paired up against 8x Scavengers of various factions. Kill them all. The 1x **Strange Coded Document** can be found in a **Cargo Container** that drops from the last destroyed ship. Mission complete. Return to base pilot!

On turn in, Doctor Canius says: *"Good job cleaning those guys out. I wouldn't worry about the ramifications of slaughtering some godless scavengers. The first rule of any good reclaiming is it's about souls, not bodies. You just happen to have a lot of the latter left over. Now, let's have a look at that document you found."*
