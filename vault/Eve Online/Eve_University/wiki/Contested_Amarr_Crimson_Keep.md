# Contested Amarr Crimson Keep

- Contested Amarr Crimson Keep** is an **Amarr COSMOS** combat site found in Garisas system. The site shows up as a warpable beacon.

The rats in the site are just normal deadspace rats that have been renamed so they won't drop anything unusual.

1. # 1st pocket - Misty Hollow

The rats are spread out across the pocket. The containers are locked until the group by them is destroyed.
