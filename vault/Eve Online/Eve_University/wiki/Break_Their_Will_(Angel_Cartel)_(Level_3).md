# Break Their Will (Angel Cartel) (Level 3)

- Blitz:** Destroy Repair Station, warp out.

Repair Station repairs itself and rats. Destroying the Auxiliary Power Arrays disables the Repair Station's repair function.

Note: 28 May 2023: Destroying the Auxiliary Power Arrays did not stop the station from repairing for me -- **Evon R'al**

  1. # Warp-in
At warp-in there are no rats. Wave 1 spawns when the repair station is hit. Auto-aggro on waves spawning.
