# The Blockade (Angel Cartel) (Level 4)

1. # Single Pocket
- May [Fleet] warp at preferred distance.
- Best to set drones to passive.
- Battleship triggers may vary between different instances of the mission and can differ based on pocket composition.
- Significant amount of target painting in reinforcements 1 and 3 which will balloon your signature radius to over 5x normal size. Strongly advised to kite and snipe target painting elite frigates and cruisers first if you're running a fit that relies on speed tanking, like a Gila or HAC.
