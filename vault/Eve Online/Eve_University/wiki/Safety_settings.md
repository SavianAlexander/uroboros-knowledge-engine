# Safety settings

The **safety setting** controls the possibility to execute actions that would result in **suspect** or **criminal** status.

1. # Settings
There are three safety settings:
- - Enable safety to prevent all actions that would give you suspect or criminal status.
- - Partial safety prevents actions that give criminal status, while allowing those that give suspect status.
- - Disable safety to allow actions which give suspect or criminal status.

In short, this means that if you set the safety to **enable safety** it is impossible for you to take any action that would make you a suspect or a criminal. Similarly, the **partial safety** setting will allow you to go suspect, but neither the full safety nor the partial safety will allow you to take any action that would make you a criminal and get you in trouble with CONCORD.

  - Alpha clones** in **high security systems** cannot set safety to Disable safety. Pilots that are controlling an **Upwell structure** in high security systems cannot set safety to Partial safety or Disable safety.

  1. # Changing
Changing the safety setting is as simple as clicking the little dot next to the HUD (head-up display) and selecting the appropriate safety level. Changes will persist until you decide to change it again.

1. # What safety setting should I use?
If you're not sure how safety works it's probably best to keep it on  and only change it when ordered to by your fleet commander. Other than that, most people tend to set their safety based on where they operate, in what kind of space.

Something to consider when deciding what safety to use, is what it'll stop you from doing and what it'll allow you to do. What actions would you potentially miss if you were forced to change your setting before doing it? How much trouble could a misclick cause if you set your safety too low? Once you've considered that, your safety choice should be fairly obvious.

See the **timers** page and the **remote assistance** section on the timers page for more information.
