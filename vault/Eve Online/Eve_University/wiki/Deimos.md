1. # Summary
A logical progression of the **Thorax**, the **Deimos** is designed as a platform for hybrid turrets (blasters and railguns). It can fit a microwarpdrive and burn towards the enemy to melt them at close range, or it can try to kite enemies.

1. # Tactics
The Deimos can deliver very high on-paper DPS with blasters, or respectable DPS at railgun ranges. Its bonus for armor repairers helps it mount active armor tanks, and lets it burst tank using an ancillary armor repairer especially effectively, which might make it more suited to solo and small-gang fights. The Deimos has some typically tough T2 damage resistances, especially against kinetic damage, but the other hybrid turret HAC, the **Eagle**, can mount a larger buffer tank and fight at longer ranges, and might be more suited to large-scale fleet contexts.

1. # Notes
In ancient Greek mythology Deimos was the son of **Ares** and Aphrodite, and twin brother of **Phobos**. He represented or personified terror and dread. The two moons of the planet Mars are named after Phobos and Deimos; **Deimos** is the smaller of the two.
