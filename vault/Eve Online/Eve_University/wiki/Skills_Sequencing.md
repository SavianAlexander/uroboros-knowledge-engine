# Skills:Sequencing

# Overview
Sequencing skills exclusively influence the creation, storage, and sale of player-made **SKINR** ship designs, introduced with the Equinox expansion in June 2024.

Sequencing skills use Charisma and Perception as their training attributes.

The following are the skills in the Sequencing section (in alphabetical order):
