  1. # Room 2
  - WARNING!** Second room contains 4 siege railguns (80km away) that can hit for 300-400 against a tengu with 62% thermal, 92% kinetic resists! They volley at the same time.

2023.9.17 update

Siege railguns have a damage type of 10% EM, 10% Explosive, 30% Thermal, 50% Kinetic. 

A Proteus with a resistance of 87% Thermal, 95% Kinetic, 50% EM, 10% Explosive, would take 200~350 damage from one hit.

4 siege railguns DO fire at the same time, rate of fire around 15 seconds a shot.

attacking the station will cause a Kinetic smartbomb activation, causing around 6000 Kinetic damage. The smartbomb does not repeat.

When the station is brought down to 20% structure, it would spawn a wave of 20 battleships plus more scram web frigates and a dread guristas conquistador with a 10m isk bounty. It is recommended to use afterburner to avoid damage from battleship torpedos.

If you kill the station and warp out but do not kill the faction spawn, the whole site including the faction commander will despawn.

I recommended killing the scram / web frigates immediately and taking down a couple of the siege rail guns before attacking the station. The DPS from the railguns plus the second wave can quickly overwhelm even a blingy ship.

1. # Escalation
The site can escalate to **No Quarter**.
