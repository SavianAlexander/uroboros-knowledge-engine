# Smuggler Interception (Angel Cartel) (Level 4)

Video: [Maelstrom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G8NHrmj6_68) 2013-04], [Rattlesnake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qXfSW0zE_NM) 2013-10], [Dominix](https://youtu.be/AA1Yj0c6gYU) 2021-01], [Gila](https://youtu.be/7iU651dS1v8) 2021-04-18]

  - Optional Approach:**
I've kited this in an armor tanked **Kronos** without ever getting into armor. All you need in both pockets is an AB to keep out of their range (70km), fit accordingly.
I've sniped from the warp-in beacon in a **Dominix** with Bouncer II, starting with closest Hostile (group 2 was closer) only got to 75% Shield.
In the 2nd pocket on the other hand, the hostiles close in fast, either burn off or Micro Jump when the heat gets too intense.

  - Blitz:**
Pocket 1: Kill everything
Pocket 2: Only the ships in the group with the Transports needs to be destroyed to complete the objective (don't forget to loot the 10 Militants).

  1. # 1st pocket

Acceleration gate is 28km away from warp in. Gate is locked until all ships have been destroyed.

|}

  1. # 2nd pocket
All Groups Aggro Separately. The transporter ships drop the mission objective. Mission is marked completed once the group 2 is dead.

|}

  - Standings gain/loss:**
- Angel Cartel: -0.3% (Angel Cartel Personnel Transport)

1. # Loot, Salvage & Bounty
Loot & Salvage: 6.7 mil
Bounty: 6 mil
