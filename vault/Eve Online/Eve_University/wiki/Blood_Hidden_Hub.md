# Blood Hidden Hub

- Blood Hidden Hub** is a **combat anomaly** found in nullsec regions with Blood Raider presence. This site is a Level 2 variant of the **Blood Hub**.
1. # Walkthrough
Ungated pocket with 9 waves of rats. The site consists of an asteroid field and two shipyards.
