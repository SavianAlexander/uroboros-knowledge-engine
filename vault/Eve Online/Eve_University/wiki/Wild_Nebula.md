# Wild Nebula

- Wild Nebula** is a gas **Exploration** site found in High-sec, Low-sec and Null-sec, containing gas used in the production of **Medical Boosters** and Components.

Gas Harvesting in low/null security space can be perilous. If hostile pilots are in system with you, remember to check your **directional scanner** for probes or other potential threats.

1. # Defenders
No rats spawn in this site.

1. # Harvestable Gas Clouds
2 Malachite Mykoserocin (1000 units each)

  1. # Malachite Mykoserocin
Malachite Mykoserocin is used in the production of the Synth Mindflood Booster and Hypnagogic Neurolink Enhancers.

1. # Location
These sites can be found in the [Aridia Region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Aridia#const), [Immensea Region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Immensea#const), [Khanid Region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Khanid#const), [Tenerifis Region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Tenerifis#const), [Kor-Azor Region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Kor-Azor) and [Great Wildlands Region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Great_Wildlands#const)
