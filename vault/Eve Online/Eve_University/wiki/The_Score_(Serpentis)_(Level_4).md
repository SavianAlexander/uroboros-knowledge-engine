# The Score (Serpentis) (Level 4)

Video: [[Machariel](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C1haMNaD7AU) March 2013], [[Maelstrom](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g3ezMO8iyDg) April 2013], [[Gila](https://youtu.be/MRXlutIQPHc) April 2021], [[Praxis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f7Mt1EkwRQ0) June 2024]

It is recommended to fit AB or MWD as the acceleration gates are 60 and 45 km away and the rats in last room are 80 km away.

No hostiles at warp-in area.

  - Blitz:** Clear the 1st and 2nd rooms, kill the 4 Battleships in the final room.

1. # 1st Pocket

Acceleration gate to next pocket is at 60 km and is locked until all hostiles are cleared. Group 2 and 3 are almost together but can be aggro separately if careful.

|}

1. # 2nd Pocket

Acceleration gate to next pocket is 40km from warp. The gate is locked until all ships are destroyed. Aggroing group 3 will also aggro group 2.

|}

1. # 3rd Pocket

Third and final pocket. Destroy the battleships to complete the mission.

The ships are clustered around an asteroid installation 80 km from warpin.

|}
