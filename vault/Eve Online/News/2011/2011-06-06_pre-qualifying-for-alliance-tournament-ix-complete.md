All Pre Qualifying rounds are now complete as we move toward next weekends's opening of the Group Stage in Alliance Tournament IX.

The Tengu continues to be the popular choice of ship in the tournament so far with 69 fielded to date. The Cyclone has shot up surprisingly in use and threatens the Tengu with 63 fielded. The Scimitar (51), Sabre (40) and Dramiel (30) maintain their popularity in the top 5 as well.

Next week begins the actual Group Stages leading up to the Finals in two weeks. Teams are even now most likely gathering ships and equipment as well attempting to find out what the competition will be using.
