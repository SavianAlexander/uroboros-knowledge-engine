The Imperfects are hosting a tournament which continues this weekend, sponsored by CCP and the community, which pits DUST 514 corps against each other for fantastic prizes. But it's not only mercenaries that can profit from this tournament. In a first for New Eden, capsuleers can bet on the matches and then go orbitally bombombard the participants to influence the outcomes! Plus, there are additional sub prizes for players in both games to take advantage of!

Round 3 of the tournament begins at 21:30 on the 23rd and continues all weekend. More details can be found in this [forum post.](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=206955&find=unread)
