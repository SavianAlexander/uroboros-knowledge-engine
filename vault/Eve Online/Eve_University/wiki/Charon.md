1. # Summary
The **Charon** has the highest cargo capacity of any **freighter** in the game, being able to carry 1,247,762 of cargo at Caldari Freighter V when fit with faction modules to maximize cargo. Caldari freighters with Caldari Freighter IV can hold more than any other freighter, even with their respective freighter skills trained to V, so if you want to carry massive amounts of materials, the Charon is the way to go. However, the Charon have absolutely no power nor CPU capacity, and no slots for modules, allowing it to only equip passive modules such as expanded cargohold or microfiber internal structures.

1. # Skills
Since Freighters cannot fit any high or mid slot modules, if you want to focus primarily on flying freight there are only a handful of support skills that will matter.

  1. # Tanking:
- - Adds Structure (Hull) hit points.

- - Adds Armor hit points.

- - Increases shield capacity.
- - Reduces shield recharge time.
- - Reduces shield damage penetration when shields are less than 25%.

  1. # Navigation:
- - Increases ship agility (time to align).
- - Increase sub-warp velocity.
- - Decrease capacitor use per jump.

  1. # Spaceship Command:
- to V - Maxes out Charon cargo capacity and velocity.

Not absolutely necessary, but helpful:

  1. # Neural Enhancement:
- IV - Allows +4 implants for faster training, allows for Warp and Align implants to improve both.

  1. # Trade:
- - Allows creation of 4 more contracts per level (total of 21 at level V).
  - - Allows creation of 4 more contracts per level (total of 41 at level V).
