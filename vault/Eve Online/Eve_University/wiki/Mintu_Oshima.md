# Mintu Oshima

- Mintu Oshima** is an agent in one of the chains of agents in **Caldari COSMOS**. The chain consists of (ordered):
- **Akira Helkelen**
- **Ryoke Aura**
- **Goru Nikainen**
- **Kochi Utrainen**
- **Hitami Magye**
- **Mintu Oshima**
Each agent in the chain provides items required for starting or completing missions for the next agent in the chain.

1. # Hidden Treasure - The Treasure Map (1 of 4)

Ancient Treasure Map is the reward from **Hitami Magye** final mission.

1. # Hidden Treasure - Retrieve The Keycard (2 of 4)

Transport 1x Oshima's System Scanner (800 m3) from Ishisomo IX - Moon 9 - Chief Executive Panel Bureau to agent.

1. # Hidden Treasure - Retrieve The Keycard (3 of 4)

Kepheur's Keycard (0.1 m3) drops from Tao Pai Motow in the Kazka Asteroid Cluster from **Contested Kazka Headquarters**.

1. # Hidden Treasure - Find The Gallente Fortune (4 of 4)

It is recommended to use a ship with a Microwarpdrive, since the gate in the fourth pocket is 178km away from the landing point and you can not warp to it.

Acceleration gate at warpin. No enemies.
