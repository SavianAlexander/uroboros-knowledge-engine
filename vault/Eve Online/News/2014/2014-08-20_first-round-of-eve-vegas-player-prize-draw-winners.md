## Overview
We are happy to announce the first round of winners from the EVE Vegas Player Prize Draw! Draconis Estemaire of Basgerin Pirate [B-P06] has won a Limousine Trip. Mikel Kashada of Conclav3 of Shadow5 [CO53] has won a Lomousine Trip. Bashfool of Pandemic Legion [-10.0]

---

We are happy to announce the first round of winners from the [EVE Vegas Player Prize Draw!](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/eve-online-news/eve-vegas-competition-2/)


- [Draconis Estemaire](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Draconis%20Estemaire) of [Basgerin Pirate [B-P06]](//gate.eveonline.com/Corporation/Basgerin%20Pirate) has won a Limousine Trip.
- [Mikel Kashada](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Mikel%20Kashada) of [Conclav3 of Shadow5 [CO53]](//gate.eveonline.com/Corporation/Conclav3%20of%20Shadow5) has won a Lomousine Trip.
- [Bashfool](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Bashfool) of [Pandemic Legion [-10.0]](//gate.eveonline.com/Alliance/Pandemic%20Legion) has won a [Resort Vista Room Upgrade](//www.planethollywoodresort.com/content/scaffold_pages/rooms/planet_hollywood/phv/en/resort_vista_room/_jcr_content/cards/card.roomhighlights.-_-etc-_-designs-_-cet-_-properties-_-planet_hollywood.phv.1.html).


Prize winners will be contacted by CCP in the coming days with further details on how to claim their prizes.

The next prize to be drawn will be for a [Panorama Suite Upgrade](//www.planethollywoodresort.com/content/scaffold_pages/rooms/planet_hollywood/phv/en/ph_panorama_suite/_jcr_content/cards/card.roomhighlights.-_-etc-_-designs-_-cet-_-properties-_-planet_hollywood.phv.1.html). This draw will take place and the winner announced on Wednesday, August 27th, 2014.

And now an update on the [EVE Vegas Alliance Prize Draw!](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/eve-online-news/eve-vegas-competition/)

There are currently 77 alliances being represented at EVE Vegas 2014, and there are 5 alliances that have reached the 10 member threshold to be included in the draw. These are as follows:


- Goonswarm Federation (47 attendees)
- Pandemic Legion (27 attendees)
- The Bastion (17 attendees)
- Get Off My Lawn (13 attendees)
- Overload Everything (13 attendees)


The following alliances are currently close to making the attendance threshold required to be included in the draw:


- Black Legion (8 attendees)
- Brave Collective (9 attendees)
- Brothers of Tangra (6 attendees)
- RAZOR Alliance (8 attendees)
- Shadow Cartel (9 attendees)
- Tactical Narcotics Team (8 attendees)


Tickets are still available for EVE Vegas 2014 at the [EVE Vegas Secure Website](//secure.eveonline.com/EveVegas/), if you'd like to join your wingmen for an amazing experience in Las Vegas from October 17-19 2014!
