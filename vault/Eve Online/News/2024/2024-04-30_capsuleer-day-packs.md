### Fearless capsuleers!


Two new value packs have been released in honor of Capsuleer Day celebrations! From 30 April to 30 May, the [EVE Store](https://secure.eveonline.com/) is offering these packs full of brand new Police SKINs, Omega, PLEX, and skill training accelerators at a great price.

**ABYSS EXPLORER PACK contains: **


- 1 month [Omega](https://www.eveonline.com/omega)
- Standard ‘Boost’ Cerebral Accelerator
- Faction Warfare Expert System
- Space Exploration Expert System
- Imperial Navy Slicer Imperial Police SKIN
- Omen Imperial Police SKIN
- Prophecy Navy Issue Imperial Police SKIN


The accelerator and expert system will speed up your training toward your goals, granting you access to new ships and new SKINs to explore New Eden. 

**ABYSS PROTECTOR PACK contains:**


- 1,350 PLEX
- 2x Genius ‘Boost’ Cerebral Accelerators
- Stabber Republic Police SKIN
- Rifter Republic Police SKIN
- Skybreaker Aegis Police SKIN
- Stormbringer Aegis Police SKIN
- Thunderchild Aegis Police SKIN
