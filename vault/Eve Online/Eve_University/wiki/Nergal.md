1. # Summary
An improved version of the **Damavik** released with the Invasion update, the Nergal retains the range bonus that the Damavik lost in this update, while also getting the armor resistance bonus that was given to the Damavik in its place.
The Nergal is the be-all end-all ultimate pinnacle of frigate DPS. A fully ramped Nergal can break the tank of a battleship with ease. Most Nergals are fit with officer or faction weapons due to the cost of the hull.

The Nergal generally sees a niche use with L4 mission runners for handling all frigate **anomic missions** due to the high natural tracking of disintegrators plus its high spooled-up damage which makes quick damage-dealing a non-issue.
