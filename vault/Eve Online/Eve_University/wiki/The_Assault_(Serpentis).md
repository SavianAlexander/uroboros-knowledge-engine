# The Assault (Serpentis)

Videos: [Baghest (blitzed)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EixR9W4zj34), [Gila (blitzed)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9xLMbkPMzGQ), [Vargur](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j3MwzzsTQL4), [Golem](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B6SSnc35zYM), [Apocalypse Imperial Issue (Sniper)](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S574EY2it4c), [Gila](https://youtu.be/ZvDV-6EEUFo), [Manticore (Blitz)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qe-fhz8Ntz4)

The mission consists of three pockets and you only have to clear the NPCs in the third (last) pocket. You might have to clear some warp disrupting frigates in pocket 1 and 2 so you can take the acceleration gate.

  - Blitz:** In the first and second pockets, ignore all hostile targets and proceed through the acceleration gates. Note that the gates cannot be activated while warp disrupted, so it may be necessary to destroy any disrupting frigates before activating the gates. Destroy all the ships in the final pocket.

  - Warning:** It can be helpful to fit a  or  to help counteract sensor dampening.

  1. # 1st Pocket (Optional)

Attacking group 1 has a chance to aggro group 2. Attacking group 3 will aggro group 2. Destroying any of the triggers will spawn reinforcements. Avoid triggering reinforcements too early as it includes more warp disruption and heavy dps.

The acceleration gate is not locked, and can be activated at any time. However, note that the acceleration gate cannot be used while warp disrupted.

|}

|}

  1. # 2nd Pocket (Optional)

Auto-aggro from groups 1 and 2 on warp-in. Destroying the trigger battleships will spawn reinforcements.

The acceleration gate is not locked, and can be activated at any time. However, note that you cannot take the acceleration gate while warp disrupted.

|}

|}

  1. # 3rd Pocket

Group 1 will aggro drones. Group 2 will aggro when approached within 40km. Mission is completed once both groups are destroyed.
