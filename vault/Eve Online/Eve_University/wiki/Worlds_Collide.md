# Worlds Collide

- Worlds Collide** is a mission with multiple level and faction variants.

Level 1:
- **Angel Cartel & Sansha's Nation**
- **Blood Raiders & Angel Cartel**
- **Guristas Pirates & Angel Cartel**
- **Serpentis & Blood Raiders**

Level 4:
- **Angel Cartel & Sansha's Nation**
- **Blood Raiders & Angel Cartel**
- **Gallente Federation & Sansha's Nation**
- **Guristas Pirates & Serpentis**
- **Serpentis & Guristas Pirates**

Identifying the faction variants:
  - thumb|left|If you read the mission briefing it'll list the two factions in order. In this case it says  first and  second, so it's the [[Worlds Collide (Guristas Pirates & Serpentis)|Guristas Pirates & Serpentis** variant.]]
