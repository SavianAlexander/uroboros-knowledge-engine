1. # Summary
The Khanid Assault Frigate **Vengeance** is the 'tanky' assault frigate of the Amarr line-up, and it can be an excellent PvP heavy tackler. Solo, the Vengeance can beat its prey by simply out-tanking other ships in the same class. While it is low on effective DPS, it can sustain a lot of punishment with a passive buffer tank or a dual active tank.

1. # Tactics
The Vengeance gives bonuses to Rockets and armor resist. So with those bonuses, its role within a fleet is usually a heavy tackle and/or close range brawler. It can be fitted with a Warp scram, Stasis Web, and a 1MN Microwarp drive to aid in fleet or it can be fitted with a target painter, instead of a Stasis Webifier, to bloom the target for any heavy missiles that are inbound. The CPU on this particular assault frigate is lacking; however, if your core fitting skills are at optimal then creative fits are very obtainable due to the amount of powergrid that is usually left over.
