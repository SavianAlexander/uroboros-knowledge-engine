The elections for the [9th Council of Stellar Management (CSM9)](//community.eveonline.com/community/csm/) are about to come to a close! Voting closes at midnight tonight, April 22nd, so make sure your voice is heard!

Check out all the candidates [here](//community.eveonline.com/community/csm/candidates/), and cast your ballot [here](//community.eveonline.com/community/csm/vote).

If you'd like more information on how the election works and why the CSM is so important, you can check out some of our Dev Blogs [here](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/csm9-voting-is-now-open/).

Remember to tell all your friends and assosciates to vote as well! A strong CSM is a strong player voice in EVE Online's future developement and time is running out!
