# SCC Secure Key Storage

The **SCC Secure Key Storage** is a **data site** that appears as a cosmic signature of level II scanning difficulty in Low security systems anywhere in empire space. The site provides **ESS** reserve keys.

- First room requires coordinated hacking with a data analyzer. Due to the hacking mechanics the site should only be attempted with a small fleet or multiboxing at least one strongly tanked **marauder** and two hacking bonused ships.
- Tank for omni damage, especially in the second room. Be prepared to be potentially swarmed by multiple reinforcement waves at once and expect upwards of 4-5k omni damage. NPCs will often split aggro which will make the site more doable for burst tanking fleets.
  - Most of the incoming damage will be Kinetic/Explosive. The sentries are the only NPCs which deal EM, the SCC Security frigates deal Thermal/Kinetic, the cruisers deal Kinetic/Explosive with a heavy preference towards Explosive, and the battleships do purely Kinetic damage.
- Most NPCs in this site have uniform resists. The SCC Security Heavy GunStar is weakest to Kinetic damage, while the Stasis GunStar is weakest to Thermal.
- Proximity mines in this site are weaker than their counterparts in some other exploration sites like the **Superior Sleeper Cache** and the **AEGIS Capital Ship Security Facility**, but still do roughly 4000 omni damage. Tank your hacking ships accordingly in case you accidentally trigger them.
- A non-interactable **Invisible Cloud - Disallow Cloaking - ESS Main Room** will decloak any incoming ships warping into the site and prevent them from recloaking.

  1. # Warp-in

You land in the middle of a decently sized minefield 30 km off the acceleration gate to the next room. Clear out the sentries before you attempt to hack the containers needed to unlock the acceleration gate. Note that the Stasis GunStars will also deal damage to you along with their EWAR effect.

Failing a hack on either gate control can will summon a response fleet. They must be successfully hacked **within 15 seconds of each other** to unlock the gate for 2 minutes.

Note that the sentries have high tracking speed (15 and 35 for the Heavy and Stasis GunStar, respectively), so do not expect to speed-tank.

|}

  1. # Pocket 2
The ESS key must be looted within one hour of successfully hacking the cans in the previous room, or the site will despawn.

NPCs will periodically spawn a large swarm of fast-moving Infiltrator IIs shortly after warp-in. The drones deal a LOT of EM damage and will orbit you at 7800m. They move extremely fast and have small signature radius but low EHP so a large smartbomb can deal with them fairly easily.

Battleships will orbit extremely far away (90-100 km) and must be sniped. Other NPCs will generally close in below 20 km. Cruisers make up the majority of the incoming damage and should be primaried on warp-in - each does ~250 DPS Kin/EX.

Last ship destroyed will spawn a container containing an ESS key with a direction corresponding to one of the four "quadrants" of nullsec space, the specific regions in each quadrant being listed below. The direction can be NE, NW, SE or SW. The key itself is configured to authorize either 15 or 45 releases of funds (i.e. payouts of reserve bank loot) after being used on an ESS reserve bank.
