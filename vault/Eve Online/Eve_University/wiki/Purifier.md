1. # Summary
The **Purifier** is the Amarr **Stealth Bomber**. It is best used with EM torpedoes and EM bombs (though when bombing with others it is more important to match bombs than to use the best for yourself).

1. # Skills
While the skills above will allow you to 'fly' the Purifier it is pointless to fly a Stealth Bomber without a covert ops cloaking device ( IV), or torpedo launchers ( I).
