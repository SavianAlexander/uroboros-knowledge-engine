# Greater Good

- Greater Good** is a 5 part **mission chain** issued by **Distribution** division Agents. it consists of 2(3) courier and 3(2) encounter missions.

There are 2 versions of the second mission in the chain, standard an encounter mission but when declined replaced by a courier mission
