# Unauthorized Military Presence (Blood Raiders) (Level 4)

- Unauthorized Military Presence** is a mission involving combat against the **Blood Raiders**.

Video: [Nightmare](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3gTViKR_Z18), [CNR](https://youtu.be/kOEaU8eNzNc).
[Sentry Dominix](https://youtu.be/lPiIYuq69SY), [Gila](https://youtu.be/ynJHDX3bzoU) 2021-04-28]

  - Blitz:**
- Ignore ships at warpin and take the acceleration gate.
- In the pocket destroy the group by the shuttle wreck (the group that has transport ships).
- Remember to take the militants from the transport ship wreck. Though militants from other sources will also work.

  1. # Warpin

Gate is unlocked, you can ignore this group if you want to. The Acceleration Gate is 20 km from the warp-in point.

|}

  1. # 1st pocket
There will be a group at the Ammatar Holy Dome when you warp-in and two others will spawn within a few seconds. They will look like two groups.

- The group at the Ammatar Holy Dome will aggro drones.
- No Aggro on warp in. Group 2 aggroes by proximity 70km.
- Destroy group 2 to complete the mission. Remember to take the militants from the transporter wreck.

|}

No structures drop loot.
