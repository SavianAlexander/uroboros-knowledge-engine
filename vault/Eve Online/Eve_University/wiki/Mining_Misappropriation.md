# Mining Misappropriation

Video: [Sentry Dominix](https://youtu.be/uHFYaq1jBhE), [Gila](https://youtu.be/PS81l2HcCZE) 2021-05-02]

Single ungated pocket. You warp-in directly to beacon. You can´t warp to optimal range and also no bookmarks possible.

Your mission is to set an example and destroy everything. No blitzing, everything must be destroyed. Since this miner has a defense fleet a Catalyst isn't the ship to use.

In the pocket you will find a single Hulk called Illegal Miner. Destroying it triggers a series of spawns. The hulk has impressive shield tank but it will die.

|}

Destroying the hulk spawns three battleship. The ships spawn some distance away but they have short range weapons (20 km orbit).

|}

Second wave spawns even further away. These have longer range weapons and will orbit at 50 km.

|}

Again far spawn that will approach to 50 km orbit.

|}

Rewards:

Bounty: ~13 Mio ISK

Salvage: ~3 Mio ISK

Loot: ~6.5 Mio ISK
