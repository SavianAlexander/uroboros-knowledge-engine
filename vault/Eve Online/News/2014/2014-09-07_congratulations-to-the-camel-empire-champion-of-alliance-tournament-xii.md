After four weeks of intense fighting in the [12th Alliance Tournament](//community.eveonline.com/community/alliance-tournament/), nerve-wracking matches, and awesome fun, we have a winner! Congratulations to *The Camel Empire* for winning Alliance Tournament XII and showing incredible battles!

*The Camel Empire* lost in the whole AT XII only one match (against *The Tuskers Co.* in the [Undefeated Semifinals](//community.eveonline.com/community/alliance-tournament/tournament-brackets/)) while winning all other matches (for example against tournament heavyweights such as *Pandemic Legion* and *Tactical Narcotics Team*).

*The Camel Empire* won the Best-Of-Five final series on September 7 against *Nulli Secunda* convincingly 3:0. The final matches were of incredible quality and could have turned easily the other way if the pilots would have made even the slightest mistake.

With winning the Alliance Tournament comes also a nice prize of 25 brand new Chameleon Recon Cruisers and 25 Whiptail Interceptors. More about the AT XII prizes can be found [here](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/alliance-tournament-xii-prizes-revealed/).

All playbacks and recordings will be available on the [CCP YouTube channel](//www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLQvKSs1k6DLNl0dsPrXOR37KmduoYrtNx). Player created 3D playback of all the matches can be found on the EVE Online Fansite [Null-Sec.com](//null-sec.com/atxii/#/match/125).

We thank all the participants of the AT XII for creating such a fantastic tournament!
