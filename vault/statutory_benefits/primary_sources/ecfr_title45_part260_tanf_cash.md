### § 260.30 - What definitions apply under the TANF regulations?
**(a) Assistance.** The term 'assistance' includes cash, payments, vouchers, and other forms of benefits designed to meet a family's ongoing basic needs (food, clothing, shelter, utilities, household goods, personal care items).
**(b) Disregards.** State TANF agencies must apply an initial standard work disregard ($90.00 standard work expense) followed by an incentive earnings disregard (minimum 30% of remaining earned wages) to encourage self-sufficiency.
