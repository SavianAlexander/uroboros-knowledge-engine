# Blood Raider Chemical Lab

1. # Overview
This is a Cosmic Signature **chemical lab** found in **low-sec**. To access the chemical laboratories, you need a Data Analyzer module. 

1. # Walkthrough
Single ungated pocket. 
The Cruise Missile Batteries are equipped with Mjolnir (EM) ammo and will deal a very large alpha strike.  You should destroy some of these prior to hacking the cans.
The cans can be hacked and looted without killing any of the defenders.

|}

Reinforcements arrive once the ships from initial defenders are destroyed.
