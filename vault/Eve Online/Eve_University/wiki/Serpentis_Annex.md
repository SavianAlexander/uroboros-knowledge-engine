# Serpentis Annex

1. # Walkthrough
This complex is similar to **Serpentis Vigil**.

The complex is accessed by activating the acceleration gate on warp-in.

A battleship or above is recommended for this site.

  1. # First Room
Destroy 2 Serpentis Bunkers and Core Drug Dealer will probably spawn. Kill him, then clear the pocket.
