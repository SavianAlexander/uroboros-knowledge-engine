# Small mining colony

- Small mining colony** is an unnamed and unmarked site in Autama system. The site is located at Autama VII - Moon 1. This site is tied to mission chain **The Celestial Imperative**. The enemies inside respawn periodically.

1. # Warp-in

|}

|}

At warp-in, there are two frigates defending the acceleration gate. Destroy them to unlock the gate. Despite their description, no standing loss with the Caldari State is incurred.
