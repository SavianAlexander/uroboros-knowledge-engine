# Fortification Frontier Stronghold

{{whcobox|
- The site has no warp scrambling. If you are worried about having to escape, this is the site for you.
- Total of 1,782,140 veldspar spread across the rocks.
- Waves 1 and 3 spawn at 0 on the Sleeper Enclave.  Wave 2 spawns about 50-60 km off.
- Killing the Awakened Preserver in the third wave will prematurely despawn the site.
