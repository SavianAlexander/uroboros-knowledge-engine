# Enormous Mercoxit Deposit

- Enormous Mercoxit Deposit** are spawned in **sovereign nullsec** systems as a guaranteed spawn in systems with any tier 3 **Ore Prospecting Array**.

The respawn time on guaranteed sites is 12 hours.

3 types of Mercoxit spawns with this site, regular, Magma (5%), and Vitreous (10%).
