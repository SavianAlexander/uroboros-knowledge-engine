# Traffic Stop

This 3 man Homefront Operation site is found throughout **high-security** Empire space. The only **regional difference** is the types of haulers and what damage types they are weak to.

The objective of Traffic Stop is to prevent haulers carrying contraband from reaching the station. These haulers will warp onto the grid in waves, you must **cargo scan** the haulers and if any of them possess **contraband data**, you need to destroy them. Once any hauler is aggressed, it will deploy drone frigates to attack your ships and begin accelerating rapidly until it reaches the station and docks.

The shuttles can be ignored as they never carry contraband.

Pilots should resist , the damage dealt by the escort frigates in all regions. There is no hostile **EWAR**.

This site takes the full 9 minutes, and you need to ensure both victory criteria are met: (1) don't let 5 ships carrying contraband dock up; and (2) don't shoot or kill more than 2 civilians. The mission will fail if either of those occur prior to the 9-minute timer on the site ending. Partial progress in these failure conditions (such as letting 4 smugglers through) has no effect on site payout.<gallery>

1. # Fleet size & composition

- Optimal fleet size: 3 ships
- Fleet composition: T1 Frigates, T1 Destroyers fit for cargo scanning, tackling and destruction

1. # Type of ships allowed
The operation takes place in a **deadspace pocket** with an acceleration gate. The gate allows these ships:

- T1 Frigate (including Navy and Faction)
- T1 Destroyer (excluding Navy and Faction)
- Venture, Venture Consortium Issue

1. # Regional differences
The Haulers to be scanned match the empire space where the site is located, and their resists vary (see the table below)

The Phaseless Cutter frigates do not vary.

1. # Hostiles
Friendly shuttles and haulers warp onto the grid periodically. Shuttles can be ignored. Some haulers will hold contraband in their cargo hold.

When a hauler is aggressed, they will become hostile and begin accelerating toward the station. Hostile Phaseless Cutters will spawn near the hauler.Haulers differ in resistance based on the site's location:

1. # Bugs, Quirks, and Notes
After the Homefront enemy behavior update in May 2026, Phaseless Cutters shoot more than just player ships. They will shoot the other NPC haulers, both guilty and innocent. This doesn't trigger the haulers (i.e. they stay blue and do not speed up), nor does Phaseless Cutters destroying innocent haulers count as strikes towards failing the site.

1. # Rewards
Rewards consists of raw ISK payout upon completion, following the standard 3-pilot fleet payout curve with in a maximum payout of 12,000,000 ISK payout per pilot with a fleet of 3. To be eligible for payout, each pilot must be actively involved during the mission. 

Each smuggler carries 6 Contraband Data (0.01 each) that can be sold to NPC buy orders for 350,000 ISK each. As well as some other commodities that can be sold on the market.
