# Desolate Site

- Desolate Site** is an Unrated Complex found in high sec and low sec via **exploration**.

You must be flying a T3 cruiser or smaller to enter the acceleration gate.

1. # Walkthrough

Initially there are only two groups on grid. Third group consisting of a frigate and a destroyer appears in few seconds. You can ignore the groups that have destroyers or drones. Destroying the group with frigates only will trigger additional waves.

The first and second spawns appear by the Warning Sign structure.

|}
Finishing first spawn can trigger escalation.

1. # Loot
- Salvage:  814K-1.42M
- Loot: (sentient drone) Drone Capillary Fluids + Drone Synaptic Relay Wiring (~5m). Possible Rogue Drone 46-X Nexus Chip (~50m). Sentient Modules (~100m) (very rare).
- Bounties: about 50k (defenders) and 80k (reinforcement)
