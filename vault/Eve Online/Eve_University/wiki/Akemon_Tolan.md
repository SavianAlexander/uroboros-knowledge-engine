# Akemon Tolan

- Akemon Tolan** is an Amarr agent in the **Caldari COSMOS** located in the Prison Facility room of the **Contested Guristas Outrider Post** in Otomainen. Over the course of this mission chain you need to provide three items: Jakon's Head, Myrkai's Data Chip, and a Guristas Shuttle. Myrkai's Data Chip drops from a structure at the agent's location but that structure only spawns once a day. People run this right after downtime and then sell Myrkai's Data Chip through contracts for a lot of isk. This is worth it because the final mission rewards the **hardwiring implant** Inherent Implants 'Noble' Hull Upgrades HG-1008 which is worth a lot.

1. # Escape From The Lion's Den - Mission Impossible (1 of 3)

You can obtain Jakon's Head at Otitoh VII – Moon 19. Best acquire it before you accept the mission.

1. # Escape From The Lion's Den - Key To Survival (2 of 3)

Myrkai's Data Chip drops from the Guristas Officer's Quarters structure which spawns next to the agent who is in the Prison Facility room of the **Contested Guristas Outrider Post** in Otomainen. But this structure only spawn once a day after downtime. The local players usually have a good setup to make sure to get this daily drop. But you can usually buy it from contracts.

1. # Escape From The Lion's Den - The Getaway (3 of 3)

The Guristas Shuttle drops from the Shuttle Storage Silo structure in the Supply Depot room of the **Contested Guristas Outrider Post**. This structure again only spawns once a day after downtime. But as it drops several shuttles you can buy it for a reasonable price on the normal local market.
