1. # Summary
The Reaper is the Minmatar **Corvette**. This ship is given free of charge to Minmatar players who click on the "Board my Corvette" button while docked in a station. It has no blueprint and can not be manufactured or reprocessed.

As with all Corvettes, the Reaper is a cheap and disposable hull, but is outclassed in every regard by almost any other player-piloted ship. Minmatar pilots wishing to advance into different hulls may want to upgrade to one of the following depending on their preferred piloting style:
