The Bracket for [Alliance Tournament XI](//community.eveonline.com/community/alliance-tournament/), which will start on Saturday July 20th, are now available. They can be found [here](//community.eveonline.com/community/alliance-tournament/tournament-brackets/).

These new and improved brackets will be updated as matches progress through the day to keep you informed of the status of your favorite teams as they progress towards victory (or defeat). You can also keep track of the match schedule [here](//community.eveonline.com/community/alliance-tournament/schedule/).

Check out the Alliance Tournament XI live on Twitch.tv at [twitch.tv/ccp](//www.twitch.tv/ccp) and see some of the best alliances in the game duke it out for fame, glory, and profit.
