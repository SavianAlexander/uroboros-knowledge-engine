# Diablo Nebula

This is a high/low sec gas site found through **Exploration**. It may also spawn in some nullsec systems.

Gas Harvesting in low and null sec requires extra caution against other players

Defenders:
None

Minable Gas Clouds: 

3 Amber Mykoserocin (2,000 units each) 6,000 units total.

Amber Mykoserocin is used in the production of the Synth Blue Pill Booster and Axosomatic Neurolink Enhancer.

This site is found in Black Rise, Cache, Perrigen Falls The Forge, and regions of space.
