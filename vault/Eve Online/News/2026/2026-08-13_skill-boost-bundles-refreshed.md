## Overview
Skyrocket your training with boost bundles

---

Capsuleers, 

Skill Points are one of the most important currencies in EVE, shaping pilots’ progression and careers in New Eden, with Skill Injectors being a cornerstone of the player-driven market. 

Last year, [Skill Boost Bundles received an overhaul](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/new-skill-boost-bundles2025), based on feedback from the community. 

Now, the Skill Boost Bundles purchase history has been reset, giving every capsuleer another opportunity to accelerate their training and unlock new possibilities in New Eden. This means pilots who picked one up after its redesign last year can purchase it again. We are doing it this way now instead of reshuffling the bundles, like we did before, to see if this works better for all concerned.  

Skill Boost Bundles provide instant Skill Points along with Genius ‘Boost’ Cerebral Accelerators, combining an immediate injection of SP with accelerated training over time. Whether you’re working toward your next ship, expanding into a new career, or adding another specialization to a seasoned character, they offer a straightforward way to get there faster.  


## Choose Your Boost


The current Skill Boost Bundles were redesigned in 2025 to make their value clearer and give capsuleers more flexibility over their Skill Points. Each bundle’s instant SP corresponds to the additional amount a character would train as Omega over one, three, six, or 12 months, with larger bundles dividing that SP into equal portions that can be distributed between characters on the same account. 

There are four bundles to choose from:

For more on the thinking behind the redesigned bundles, including how their SP values relate to Omega training and the Skill Injector market, [learn more in the original announcement](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/new-skill-boost-bundles2025). 


## Boost Your Training Again


The  purchase historyhas now been reset. If you purchased a Skill Boost Bundle before, you’re eligible to buy it again. If you haven’t picked one up before, now is the perfect time to put an immediate surge of Skill Points behind your next ambition. 

Choose your boost, accelerate your training, and get closer to whatever comes next.
