The [new EVE Online website](//www.eveonline.com), focused on showcasing the EVE universe, has been deployed. A [new community website](//community.eveonline.com/) has replaced the old EVE Online website and will play host to the various resources and support tools existing EVE players use on a regular basis. Please take your time to browse and bookmark both sites and give us your feedback in [the official feedback thread.](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=767304&#post767304)

A dev blog with more information on this change can be found [here](//www.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&nbid=3390).

The deployment of the new sites is staged, which means that while some will see them immediately, for others it will take a little while longer.

We hope that you enjoy the new EVE Online website, and that you find the new community website to be an improvement to your daily EVE routine.
