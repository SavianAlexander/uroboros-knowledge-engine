# Crimson Harvest (2023)

- Crimson Harvest** is a recurring event lasting from October 5 to November 6, 2023, **downtime** to downtime.

It involves the **Blood Raiders** pirate faction and the **Order of Tetrimon** mercenaries.

1. # Agency campaign
Each character can choose between two options for the Agency challenges for this event. You will receive points for completing tasks for the one you choose in the agency. Be aware that although you will only receive points for completing task for the side you chose, there is no penalty for shooting both sides and taking the loot from both their sites.

1. # Activities
  1. # Combat
Combat sites for both sides appear as anomalies in the probe scanner, when warped to they create a beacon that can be seen from the overview. Both sides are very similar to each each other, structured as three rooms with the second room being bypassed by a locked gate in the first room and the battleship being in the third room. The second and third rooms contain some Cthonic Attar that can be mined.

There are 2 different types of level III data sites for each faction: Crimson Harvest Network Node/Hub and Tetrimon Network Node/Hub. Network Nodes spawn in high sec, while Hubs spawn in low sec. The site consists of one room with three cans approximately 20 to 40km from the warp in point surrounded by some appropriately themed objects. You will receive agency points only from the sites opposing your faction of choice but both sites can be completed for loot without penalty.

Sites will complete 10 minutes after a player has entered the room. If a player is there for more than 10 minutes the site will complete when they leave the room.

Non exhaustive list of possible loot:
- Ironblood SKINs
- Deathglow Remnant SKINs
- Cold Iron SKINs
- Crimson Scythes, Blood Dagger and Scarlet Embrace Fireworks
- 'Outlaw' Boots (Blood Raiders)
- Molecular-forging tools
  - AG-Composite Molecular Condenser
  - LM-Composite Molecular Condenser
- Overseer's Personal Effects (tiers 5-18)
- Tetrimon Base Shortcut Keypass
- Basic 'Radiance' Cerebral Accelerator
- Extended 'Radiance' Cerebral Accelerator
- Potent 'Radiance' Cerebral Accelerator
- Agency and Tetrimon drugs
- Warclone Blanks
- Normal data site loot such as datacores, decyptors and components.

1. # Special Mechanics
During the Crimson Harvest all player ship kills will have an increased loot drop rate, with an average of 90% compared the normal average of 50%.
