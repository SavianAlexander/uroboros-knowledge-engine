PC Gamer UK's [January Issue](//www.pcgamer.com/2010/12/22/pc-gamer-uk-january-issue-eve-online/) has something you may happen to recognize on the cover. Yes, cover. Evil Amarr cover. They sent a reporter out to our Reykjavik office for a whirlwind few days of interviews about CCP's past, present and future that included nearly everyone from CCP Yokai to CCP Solaris. If you are a fan of the game you play, this is a not-to-be-missed, gorgeous EVEsplosiontacular. Grab your copy on shelves, at the above link or [here](//gb.zinio.com/browse/publications/index.jsp?productId=500608931&sch=true) digitally if it's more convenient.

Furthermore [PC Gamer.com](//www.pcgamer.com/2010/12/22/before-and-after-eve-onlines-new-character-creation/) just hosted some great before/after shots of the character creator shipping in Incursion 1.1 and an interview with a couple of the men behind the new faces of EVE. Dec 22 is clearly EVE in PC Gamer Day.
