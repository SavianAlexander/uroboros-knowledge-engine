# Assistance

<noinclude> </noinclude>

Ungated semi-deadspace pocket zero jumps away in the same System.

  - Blitz:** destroy all Mercenary Overlords, then destroy the single newly spawned Mercenary Overlord.

Tips:
- Approach Audalle Roire with a throwaway ship to spawn the enemies, then warp at 100 km with your combat ship.
- Group 1 spawns about 5 seconds after the trigger (a message pops up to let you know), giving you some time to warp out; you can circle around the trigger to align with a celestial first
- The rest of the hostile spawn appears in rapid succession and will be right on top of you.
- Mercenary Overlords deal 70% Thermal damage over 30 km range and approach at 300 m/s, with limited damage projection past 25 km. Because of the fast spawn rate, there will be **12** of them attacking you at once. You should either be fit to face-tank nearly 2000 DPS of (mostly) Kinetic and Thermal damage or quickly kill the Mercenary Wingmen outside of their webbing range and speed-tank.
- Mercenary Wingmen only have a 1% chance of having a warp disruptor, but better safe than sorry
- You do not need to destroy any ship except for the Mercenary Overlords
