# 3-Frigate Abyssals

The 3-frigate level 1 Abyssals event is a template by **Turlough Dominian**. Most of the content is from forum posts by organisers like Turlough and Devola Gestalt.

  1. # Event objectives
- Participants are divided into teams of three, with one amongst them being the designated Abyssal FC.
- Each team activates a set of calm filaments (can be provided by the events organiser) and runs sites together.
- The event organiser can optionally provide prizes to whichever team completes each site first/runs the most sites in a given amount of time. See the **Abyssal speedrun contest template** for more on this.
