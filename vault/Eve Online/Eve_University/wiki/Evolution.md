# Evolution

- Blitz:** Destroy the Infested Station and warp out.

  1. # 1st pocket

When you warp in only some of the frigates are present. Rest of the ships spawn in few seconds.

One of the battleships in each wave triggers the next wave 1.

  1. # Bonus pocket

Warning: very high incoming damage.

When you warp in there is a Caldari carrier wreck, called "State Izanaki Wreck", present. Drones will spawn in few seconds.

The carrier wreck contains 1125 small arms, 370 marines, Caldari Navy Fleet Rear-Admiral Insignia and few meta modules.

(Updated June 30,2026: I got the mission from SOE. Only an Imperial Navy Fleet Rear-Admiral Insignia and a meta module found in Amarr Carrier wreck. Estimated 3 million）

  - Tips:**
- Spawns appear at one minute intervals. Kill the point first.
- A MJD is recommended as warping out may cause a re-spawn.
- Incoming damage may exceed 4,000- 6,000 DPS. Fit accordingly.
