# Emma Tharkin

- Emma Tharkin** is a level 4 **Caldari COSMOS** agent for Caldari Navy located in Otitoh on the Friggi gate.

She gives the mission Improving Relations to capsuleers with 9.20 standings towards the Caldari State.
