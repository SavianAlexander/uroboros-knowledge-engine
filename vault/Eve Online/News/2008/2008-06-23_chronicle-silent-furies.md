The Empyrean Age is upon us, shaking the foundations of galactic empires. Everyone must adapt to survive, including the leaders of the world. Starting today, we'll be publishing five weekly [chronicles](//www.eve-online.com/background/potw/archive.asp) that give you an inside look into the decision-making of each of the four faction heads: [Sanmatar Maleatu Shakor](//www.eve-online.com/background/potw/23-06-08.asp) of the Minmatar Republic, President Souro Foiritan of the Gallente Federation, Head of State Tibus Heth of the Caldari State, and Empress Jamyl Sarum of the Amarr Empire.

Why five chronicles for four leaders, we hear you cry? Because the fifth casts light on the dark pillar of power in EVE, the one that has roared to the fore with blood and fire: The capsuleers.

To discuss the chronicle, please visit [this forum thread](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=804922).
