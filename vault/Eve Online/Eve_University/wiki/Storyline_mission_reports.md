# Storyline mission reports

This is a list of reported **storyline mission**s.  Note that Storyline mission offers ignore the division of the Storyline Agent; an agent listed in the Station Services window as "Storyline - Mining" could give you a combat mission, for example.

Encounter missions only really need the volume column used when bringing something to or from the mission location is part of the objective.
