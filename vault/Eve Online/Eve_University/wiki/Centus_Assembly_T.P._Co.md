# Centus Assembly T.P. Co.

- Centus Assembly T.P. Co.** is a DED rated combat site found in null security space.

1. # Walkthrough

Anybody who want to use the gate and complete the site need  himself, which excludes alpha characters.

  1. # 1st pocket

True Power' Assembly Security HQ has very strong tank and does not need to be killed. Should be within 30 KM in order to effectively kill it.

|}

  1. # 2nd pocket

Again Centus Reprocessing Station has very strong tank and does not need to be killed. Should be within 30 KM in order to effectively kill it.

|}

  1. # 3rd pocket

Again True Power Assembly Management HQ has very strong tank and does not need to be killed. Should be within 30 KM in order to effectively kill it.

|}

  1. # 4th pocket

The Special Products container may contain faction modules. The defenders need to be killed before it can be opened.

|}

|}

  1. # 5th pocket

Station Ultima fires EM citadel torpedoes that hit extremely hard but does not have very significant tank. 60k shield HP with 0 EM resist, 300 HP/s active shield reps. 10k armor and 50k structure. As long as you immediately begin moving quickly upon landing in the pocket its damage is survivable. Theoretically it deals upwards of 500,000 EM damage with each shot but the torpedoes have capital-ship sized damage application (10,000m), and they are fired extremely slowly (every 120 seconds).

|}
