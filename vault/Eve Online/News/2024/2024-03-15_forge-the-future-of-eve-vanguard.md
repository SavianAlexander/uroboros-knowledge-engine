## Overview
Shape the planetside battlefields of New Eden on 21 March.

---

Since December, monthly First Strike events have seen legions of warclones take to the battlefield in EVE Vanguard, shaping the development of this new sci-fi sandbox shooter through their participation and feedback, and forging the future of planetside combat in New Eden. It has become increasingly clear that the Vanguard are here to stay, and their impact will only increase. The question is, what will the rest of New Eden do? 

Soon, the doors will open once more, introducing a mining laser prototype which will add another layer of complexity to gameplay, a sleek new combat suit, and new contracts that give Vanguard mercenaries a choice between corruption and suppression, deepening its connection to EVE Online.  

Not only will you get the chance to try out and impact new features early in development, but you can also earn fantastic rewards in EVE Online by proving your mettle on the battlefield. These include the** Corax and Osprey Navy Issue Arkombine Arisen SKINs**, as well as **PLEX**.   

As in the previous playtests, you will need Omega to get Founders’ Access to EVE Vanguard. The EVE and New Eden Stores are offering a **Warclone Omega Bundle**, giving you 14 days Omega access, along with the Orthrus and Phoenix Navy Issue Arkombine Arisen SKINs for a limited time. Make sure you upgrade to Omega so you don’t miss out.  

More information will be coming next week. We will see you planetside on **21 March**.
