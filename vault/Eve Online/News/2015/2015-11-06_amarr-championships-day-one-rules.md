Hello contenders and capsuleers alike!

The rules for the first day of the Amarr Championships have been released, [and can be found on the forums in the EVE Alliance Tournament Section](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=454275). The rules released only cover the first day of the tournament on Sunday, November 8. If you are competing, make sure you read them so you know what you can fly.

If you've missed any of the news about the Amarr Championships, you can find [the announcement here](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/announcing-amarr-championships/) and [the general rules and schedule here](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/amarr-championships-rules-and-schedule/).

We hope you are ready for the first weekend of action. The Empire certainly is. Please remember that Day One starts on **Sunday, November 8 at 13:00 UTC** on [CCP's Twitch channel](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=454275). There are no matches scheduled for Saturday.
