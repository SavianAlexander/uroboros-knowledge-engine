We have now published the thrilling final installment to [Black Mountain](//www.eve-online.com/background/potw/25-02-08.asp), a series of EVE chronicles that began in September 2007. If you missed all the action, start with the introductory pieces [here](//www.eve-online.com/background/potw/23-10-06.asp),and [here](//www.eve-online.com/background/potw/07-05-07.asp) and then plunge into the dark, swirling vortex of this [grand story](//www.eve-online.com/background/potw/27-08-07.asp). Be warned that like EVE itself, Black Mountain can be dark and brutal at times, though there are a few flickering lights of humanity shining forlornly in the blackness of space.

The EVE chronicles, written by CCP Abraxas, are published on Mondays every two weeks and read by thousands of people. For the full list, check out the [chronicle archive](//www.eve-online.com/background/potw/archive.asp).
