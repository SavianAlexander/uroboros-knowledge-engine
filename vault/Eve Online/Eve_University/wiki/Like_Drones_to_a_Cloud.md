{{MissionBriefing
|Drones scare the daylights out of some people. To be completely honest, the big hives even scare me a little. The smaller drone groups are annoying, but more manageable. Usually. 

Anyway, there's a Chartreuse Cytoserocin cloud over in <System> that seems to be attracting these wild drones. We keep clearing them out, but new drones just end up replacing them. We've figured out that, for whatever reason, they're attracted to the gas. So the best solution is to just remove the cloud: no cloud, no drones. If you can harvest that cloud out, it'd be a big help. 

}}

  - Warning:** You must own and be able to use a Gas Cloud Harvester to complete this mission.

Unlike most mining mission, who have only a chance of **belt rat**s showing up, this mission has its own rats.

Ungated normal space pocket. The rats spawn near a drone structure. The spawn appear at 5 minute intervals.
