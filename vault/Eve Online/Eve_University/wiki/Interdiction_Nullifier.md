# Interdiction Nullifier

An **Interdiction Nullifier** is a type of module that allows a ship to bypass **warp disruption fields**.

1. # Fitting
Interdiction Nullifiers are high slot modules. Only one Interdiction Nullifier may be equipped to a ship, and only certain ships are able to equip them.

Fitting the following incompatible modules to your ship will prevent the Interdiction Nullifier from nullifying disruption field effect (that is, it will not provide any beneficial effect at all):
- **Burst Jammers**
- **Higgs Anchor**

An Interdiction Nullifier passively reduces ship's drone bandwidth, targeting range, and scan resolution by 50% as its downside.

1. # Usage
A nullifier is an activatable module. While it is active, the ship can enter **warp** while in an **Interdiction Bubble**. An active Interdiction Nullifier negates both warp disruption fields that the ship is in and those it could get into after warping (i.e. it negates effect of drag/catch bubbles). If a warp command is given while in a bubble with an active nullifier and does not complete before the interdiction effect ends, the ship will fail to warp.

Similarly to Warp Core Stabilizers, an Interdiction Nullifier can be activated while cloaked. 

1. # Compatible ships
Only specific types of ships can fit an Interdiction Nullifier:
- **Interceptors** - combat interceptors can fit, fleet interceptors additionally get an 80% reduction in reactivation delay and penalties and doubled duration
- Tech I **Haulers**, **Blockade Runners**, and **Deep Space Transports**
- **Covert Ops** Frigates (except **Stealth Bombers**)
- **Strategic Cruisers** - additionally, each has a subsystem that reduces the reactivation delay and penalties by 80%
- **Victorieux Luxury Yacht**

1. # Passive nullification and usage
Prior to May 2021 some ships had passive nullification built-in. In May 2021 it was removed from all ships that previously had it, and added to **shuttles**.
