Set your autopilots for Warsaw!

Come down to the [Hard Rock Café](//www.hardrockcafe.pl/) in downtown Warsaw at 19:00 on July 30. CCP Gargant will be there to make sure no one goes thirsty while sharing war stories with the rest of us, and some of Poland's most prominent pilots are sure to drop in.

For those who haven't attended an EVE player gathering before, we especially encourage you to join us for a relaxed and friendly night out with fellow EVE players. We look forward to seeing you there and if you have any EVE interested friends, you're welcome to bring them along!
