# Guristas Strike - Possible Leads

<noinclude>
  - Guristas Strike - Possible Leads** is the 4th mission of the mission chain **Guristas Strike**. Unlike most storyline missions this mission gives no faction standing, but it does give agent standing. The faction standing is awarded for the final mission in the chain.
</noinclude>

Transport one Encoded Data Chip (0.1 m3) to a station 2-5 jumps away. Recommend a fast, small ship to transport the goods.

Depending on the agent's location, the transport station may be in **lowsec**.
