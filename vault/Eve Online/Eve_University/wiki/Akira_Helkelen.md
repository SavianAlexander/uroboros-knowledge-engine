# Akira Helkelen

- Akira Helkelen** is a level 3 agent in one of the chains of agents in **Caldari COSMOS**. The chain consists of (ordered):
- **Akira Helkelen**
- **Ryoke Aura**
- **Goru Nikainen**
- **Kochi Utrainen**
- **Hitami Magye**
- **Mintu Oshima**
Each agent in the chain provides items required for starting or completing missions for the next agent in the chain.

1. # A Mad Scientist‘s Dream – Payment (1 of 5)

The reward from this mission, the Private Citizen Tsuna's Passcard, is used to unlock the gate of **Tsuna's Private Compounds** complex in Otomainen. The Passcard is not consumed on use. You will need this Passcard in order to obtain mission objective for the next mission in this chain.

1. # A Mad Scientists Dream – Simple Microorganisms (2 of 5)

Provide 400 Potent Viral Agent (200 m3) from the first stage of the **Tsuna's Private Compounds** deadspace complex in the Otomainen system to agent. The Private Citizen Tsuna's Passcard from the previous mission is required to enter.
The items are in the "Science Lab" in that complex. It only spawns once per day(after downtime).

1. # A Mad Scientists Dream – The Drill (4 of 5)

Provide 4 Drill Parts (400 m3) from the **Rush Town Ruins** deadspace complex in the Otomainen system to agent.

1. # A Mad Scientists Dream – Last Hindrance (5 of 5)

Provide 1 Ratei's Insignia (0.1 m3) from the entrance stage of the **Rush Town Ruins** deadspace complex in the Otomainen system to agent. The rat reps pretty hard. You need probably around 250 DPS to break his tank.
Completing this mission awards 1x Neophite, required by **Goru Nikainen**.
