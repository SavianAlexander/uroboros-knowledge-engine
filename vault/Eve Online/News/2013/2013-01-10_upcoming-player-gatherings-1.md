The Community Team would like to draw your attention to a few player gatherings taking place in the coming weeks, and encourage anyone closeby to join for drinks and party time with fellow capsuleers!

**EVE Vancouver  - Monthly Gathering**

Join a whole host of EVE players from Vancouver BC for this monthly player gathering for drinks and food, at this completely open event that everyone who can make it is welcome to attend.For further information about this event, please see [this thread](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=2438761).

**EVE Montreal - Saturday, January 12, 2013**

This weekend, meet up with Canadian EVE players at this reguarly scheduled meet that keeps growing more and more in size and popularity with every gathering. More information is available [here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=151213).

**EVE Austin, TX - Saturday, January 12, 2013**

This month's meetup in Austin, TX will be a combination of a meet and a LAN party, where many a capsuleer will indulge in serious internet spaceships business.Further information on how to fleet up for this is available [here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=167038).

**EVE Reykjavik - Thursday, January 17, 2013**

CCP's second EVE Reykjavik after a highly successful first gathering late last year. Join more devs than can possibly be named in a single news article for beers and internet spaceships talk.You can find out more about this gathering [here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=185323).

**EVE Cleveland, OH - Saturday. January 19th, 2013**

The first gathering for Cleveland, Ohio in downtown Cleveland. Fleet up with fellow internet spaceships pilots for food and drink and EVE related discussions.More information about this gathering is located [here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=2422494).

**EVE Toronto - Friday, January 25, 2013**

Join a whole host of Canadian EVE Players for this awesome player gathering right by Union Station in Toronto. Meet up with fellow pilots for drinks and serious spaceship conversations.More information can be found regarding EVE Toronto [here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=183060).

**EVE London - Saturday, February 9, 2013**

The traditional 6 monthly gathering of Her Majesty's finest Pilots hosted by Veto Corp and [www.spawncamped](http://www.spawncamped) .net in Central London. CCP Falcon, CCP Guard and CCP Navigator will also be in attendance, so feel free to stop by for a beer.You can read more about this gathering [here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=176728).
