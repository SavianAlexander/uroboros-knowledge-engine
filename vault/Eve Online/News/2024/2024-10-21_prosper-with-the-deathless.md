## Overview
Your efforts can lead to even more rewards.

---

**Adventurous capsuleers,**

You valiantly drove back the Drifter forces and brought an end to the siege of Zarzakh, and have since then worked tirelessly to bring the Deathless the key to a mysterious shrouded structure. Many of you have reaped great rewards by trading Atavum for blueprints and ISK in Zarzakh, or by selling them at Society of Conscious Thought stations.

The Deathless has now made it clear that he will have uses for Atavum further down the line. The current collection efforts and rewards are temporary, but it seems that there will be further opportunities to gain riches from Atavum after that window closes. The artifacts will, therefore, still have value and stay relevant, so there are still ample reasons to hunt for them. 

The Society of Conscious Thought has also announced that it is willing to buy journal entries from explorers, as they were written by a former SoCT member. This introduces more avenues for adventurous capsuleers to earn wealth and rewards, so there is no shortage of opportunities out there.

Fly safe.
