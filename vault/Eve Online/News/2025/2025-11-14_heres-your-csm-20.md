Influential capsuleers, 

The votes have been counted, and the results are in. The 20th iteration of the Council of Stellar Management is here: 

We want to give a huge thank you to all who participated in CSM 20. This election season shattered expectations, with a whopping 55 candidates running and an astounding 49,870 voters! That is the second highest voter turnout ever recorded, eclipsed only by CSM 7.  The CSM is supremely important to us here at CCP, and we're so happy to see the support for it continue to grow. 

In a continuation of last years format, CCP has selected two members who finished in the 11 through 20th spots to round out areas of expertise. Those are: Mick Fightmaster and Val Auroris!

If you’d like to see a more detailed breakdown of how the votes were distributed through the STV system, you can check out our [CCPTV CSM 20 coverage](https://www.twitch.tv/videos/2618190463).

If you’d like to see a complete breakdown of the results, you can [check out the full ballots, along with the STV script, here!](https://web.ccpgamescdn.com/aws/eveonline/files/CSM_20_Results.zip)

Don’t forget to claim your reward for voting: the Metamorphosis. Every account who voted can find theirs in the New Eden Store (NES) in game.

o7 and fly safe!
