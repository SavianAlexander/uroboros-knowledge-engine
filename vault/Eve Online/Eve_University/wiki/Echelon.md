1. # Summary
The Echelon was awarded to every account who logged into EVE during the release of the Incursion expansion in late 2010. It is the best ship in the game to run data sites, having a very high virus strength and coherence even with mediocre hacking skills, far exceeding even tech 2 ships with dedicated bonuses for exploration.
