1. # Summary
The **Nereus** is the agile, tanked Gallente **hauler**. Its cargo hold can expand to 11 372 (no rigs), its align time can be reduced to 3.6 s, with a warp speed of 4.5 AU/s.

1. # Tactics
Smart hauling practices:
- **Don't Haul AFK**
- **Use insta-undocks**
- **3,000 ISK per EHP**.
- Overload tank modules when attacked
- Set drones to aggressive to let ECM drones engage without having to target lock first
