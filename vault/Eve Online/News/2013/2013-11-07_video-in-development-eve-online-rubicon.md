CCP Guard and CCP Mimic have been hard at work this week, assisting in the testing of a lot of new features for EVE Online: Rubicon, EVE's 20th free expansion which will be launched on November 19, 2013.

This latest In Development video takes a look at some of the upcoming features that will be arriving with Rubicon, including the Interbus Ship Identification System, deployable structures and the new Sisters of EVE hulls.

You can check out CCP Guard and CCP Mimic's testing session in the new [In Development video here!](//www.youtube.com/watch?v=x70cCW0oEco)
