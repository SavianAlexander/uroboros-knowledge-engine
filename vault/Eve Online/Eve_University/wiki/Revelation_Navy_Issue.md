1. # Summary
The Empire Faction Version of the sturdy **Revelation**. Compared to the **Tech 1** hull, the faction version has a 50% bigger DPS bonus per level of , an additional bonus to **capacitor warfare**, an extra high slot, more CPU and powergrid, more base tank, and slightly higher sensor strength and scan resolution.
Since its introduction November 8, 2022, as part of the Uprising Expansion, the Revelation Navy Issue is one of the powerhouses of both lowsec and nullsec dreadbrawls, due to its extreme tank (standard fits with Amulets can reach up to 10 million effective hit points), instantly swappable ammo and long turret ranges. Usual dreadnought doctrines place the Revelation Navy first, alongside the Zirnitra.
