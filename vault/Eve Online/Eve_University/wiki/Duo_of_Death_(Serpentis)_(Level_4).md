# Duo of Death (Serpentis) (Level 4)

Video: [Raven Navy Issue](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hx1AKeLCrn0), [Gila](https://youtu.be/ZckHABhoQmU), 11-04-2021]

Single gated pocket. No Hostiles at the initial gate.

The pocket has two battleships, some sentries and spider drones. The drones have very long range and are fast, you can't practically avoid being webbed. Battleships lock and attack at 25km. The last Battleship killed drops the objective item.
