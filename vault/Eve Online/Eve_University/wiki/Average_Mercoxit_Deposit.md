# Average Mercoxit Deposit

- Average Mercoxit Deposit** are periodically spawned in **nullsec** systems. These sites also have a chance to spawn in sovereign nullsec systems with a tier 2 mining upgrade.

3 types of Mercoxit spawns with this site, regular, Magma (5%), and Vitreous (10%).
