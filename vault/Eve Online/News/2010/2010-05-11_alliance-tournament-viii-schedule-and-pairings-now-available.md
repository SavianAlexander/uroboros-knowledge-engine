Alliance Tournament VIII is fast approaching, where the top pilots in EVE Online fight for the glory and honor of their alliance. The teams are now entered and the tournament pairings for the first qualifying round which takes place on June 5th & 6th have now been [announced](//www.eveonline.com/events/alliances/tournament/t8/schedule1.asp).  For more information on pairings, matches and schedules, check out the [Alliance Tournament](//www.eveonline.com/events/alliances/tournament/t8) section of the website, or head straight to the [Alliance Tournament Discussion](//www.eveonline.com/ingameboard.asp?a=channel&channelID=422285) forum and find out who people are rooting for!
