# Dread Guristas Fleet Staging Point 3

- Dread Guristas Fleet Staging Point** is an **Expedition** that almost certainly escalates from **Dread Guristas Fleet Staging Point 2**.

1. # Walkthrough
This site is the hardest of the three Fleet Staging Point parts, as heavy webbing, neuting and high incoming damage from spawns makes it fairly difficult to solo. Preferably use a well-tanked T3 Cruiser or fleet of battleships to handle the site, but it may also be blitzed with a well-fit Ishtar, killing only the frigates and the structure. 

If using a T3 Cruiser, make sure to keep moving as fast as possible to try and avoid as much damage as possible.

The second reinforcement wave contains 3x Guristas Energy Neutralizer Sentry II so watch your cap.

The Guristas Fleet Outpost (structure that contains your loot) has a strong -80% web that reaches out to 50 km. It fires Citadel Kinetic torpedoes that can instantly destroy an unprepared ship. High resists to Kinetic damage are strongly recommended.
