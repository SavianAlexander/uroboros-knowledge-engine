# Cardinal Nebula

- Cardinal Nebula** is a gas site found by scanning in the null security region of [Feythabolis](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Feythabolis).
1. # Walkthrough
This site has a pair of Vermillion Cytoserocin gas clouds containing approx. 6000 units of gas in total. However, it is guarded by Mercenary and **Rogue Drone** NPCs.

The first enemy wave is present at arrival. The second wave appears halfway through defeating the spawn wave. The third wave arrives 20 minutes after anyone begins harvesting gas.
