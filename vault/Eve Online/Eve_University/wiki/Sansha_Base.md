# Sansha Base

- Sansha Base** is a **combat site** that can be scanned down with probes in null security Sansha regions. The site is behind an acceleration gate. Warping to the side lands you at 0 on the gate. Bring a MWD or long-range weapons, as the enemies all spawn very far away.

Completing the site chages your Sansha standing by -0.3

  1. # Room 1

|}

|}

|}

Acceleration gate is 56 km from warp-in. It unlocks when all enemies are killed.

  1. # Room 2
Any last enemy in a wave can be a trigger for faction spawn. Goal in this room is to destroy Sansha Minor Refinery which spawns 160 km off warp-in. Sansha Minor Refinery drops tech 1 ammo.
