# Event types

- **Trivia events**
- **Scan the can**
- **Holiday exchanges**
- **Fight Club**
- **Musical ships**

1. # Event ideas, from Sally Digital's thread
  1. # Tourism
- **EVE Gate Pilgrimage** (Incl: Black Monolith, Hollow Asteroid and Sansha's Wormhole)
- **Titanomachy: Graveyard of Giants**
- Steve the Titan
- Mine Veld with Chribba
- Shattered Planets
- Tourism Quest - Can you visit all the places on the list?

  1. # PVP-based
- Free for alls - Usually based on ship size, Noobship and up
- Small gang Tourneys (Similar to Alliance Tournament setup)
- AWOXing Games
- Hunter Tracker games - One group hunts another. The winner is who survives longest/kills the most
- Salvage Wars - The winner is who collects the most salvage rather than most kills
- LOL fleets - Noobships, Ventures, Drunken Roams, whatever else devious little minds can think up 8)

  1. # PVE-based
- Low/Null/WH mining
- Gas huffing Fleets
- Epic Arc Fleets - Working together to complete the Epic Arc missions
- Cosmos Mission Fleets
- Mass missioning fleets/L5's
- Drifter complexes/Drifter Hunts
- Wormhole Daytripping

  1. # Objective-based
- Scavenger Hunts - Find specific items/take pics from specific places. The first to complete them all wins.
- Subwarp races - Who can navigate around a laid out track the fastest
- Warp races - Navigate from system to system using BM's left in cans to find your next way point
- Capture the can
- Can Football - Football using cans and tractor beams
- Gauntlet - Make a run from high - Low/Null, Kill a rat and return. Quickest wins
- Blockade Run - Can your team break the blockade and deliver a cargo full of Quafe to the thirsty masses

  1. # Competitions
- Raffles/Lotteries - Requires Director approval
- Picture competitions - Tourism Pics, Propaganda etc
- Playlist - Assemble the best 15-30 minute playlist to be listened to while playing EVE. Can be catagorised by activity
 
  1. # Large Preparation Events
- E-Uni Olympics - A series of Olympic Style events
- Hunger Games - Arena based, where participants start with unfit ships and MTU's and have to scavenge their fitting from cans in the arena. Winner is last man standing. Can even be run with teams of 2 representing each community.
