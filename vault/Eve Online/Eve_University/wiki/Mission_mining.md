# Mission mining

This article is based on Jen Loo's post in the, no longer existing, IVY forums. Also see the [EVE-Survival's Mining in Missions](https://eve-survival.org/wikka.php?wakka=MiningInMissions) page for information that may be missing from here.

Mission mining is defined as harvesting the asteroids that are commonly found in **deadspace** combat/encounter missions.  Different **missions** have different amounts of **ore**, often varying based on the enemy fought (Guristas vs Serpentis vs Angel Cartel) and the level of the combat mission.  Some of these mission ore pockets can be harvested on the first day and will then respawn after the daily downtime.  In addition, you'll be presented with a known volume of ore with no competitors.  

The general strategy to remember is that you should always be careful to avoid completing objectives if you want the ore to respawn after **downtime**.  This means avoiding killing key structures that will trigger completion of the mission.  In some missions, completion can be triggered by using the acceleration gate to move to the next mission pocket.  So your goal should be to do as little as possible inside the mission area if you want to mine the mission ore all week.

  1. # Blockade, The
- ~625K Veldspar
- ~240K Plagioclase
- ~150K Pyroxeres in a bunch of smallish asteroids.

  - Warning:** All asteroids are booby trapped and causes a grid-wide smart-bomb effect to take place when the asteroids are mined.  This damage is more then mining frigates, mining cruisers, mining barges and mining exhumers can deal with and may likely result in ship loss.  (Estimated damage is 500 every few seconds.)

  - Resisting the smart-bomb with a venture:** It is possible to resist the smart-bomb and mine anyway, I did mine Pyroxeres semi-AFK with a Venture that never loose more than half its shield (Citadel patch, fitted with tech I shield modules and rigs to 3249 EHP 13 HP/s with 1 medium and 1 small shield extender, mid and low passive shield recharger/relay, shield rigs). Note that the smart-bomb only make damages when mining, therefore it stops when the asteroid is depleted or the mining hold is full. This with a < 1 month account, no advanced skills, no tech II, a few millions ISK including rigs.
