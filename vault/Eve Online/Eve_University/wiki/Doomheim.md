# Doomheim

The internal corporation used for characters in the graveyard. Whenever a character is biomassed or deleted that character is assigned to Doomheim until it is purged. Characters in Doomheim do not show up in normal searches. Characters in Doomheim will occasionally be purged and new characters can be created with the same name.
Under certain circumstances, customer support and GM can recover biomassed characters. Due to game mechanics, biomassed characters in Doomheim will still show up on killmails.

1. # Doomheim Station
Doomheim Station is owned by and is the headquarters of the Doomheim Corporation. It has no location in-game and can not be remotely set as a home station. Numerous NPC corporations 
also have Doomheim set as their HQ.

1. # Agents
If one has **Agent standings** with an NPC who is removed from the game by CCP, those standings and some information about that NPC will still show up in the standings tab until the character is truly removed from the database.

1. # Duplicate names
Since mid 2023 Biomassed characters that reside in Doomheim can have counterparts that are **active with the same name**.
