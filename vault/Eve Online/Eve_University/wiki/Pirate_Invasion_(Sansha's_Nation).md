# Pirate Invasion (Sansha's Nation)

Videos:[Navy Raven](https://youtu.be/H4HgNzfuRvE)[Vargur](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Ciltzi1nnI)[Rattlesnake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=17nx6AYUnvg)[CNR](https://youtu.be/78iZsjHSlxA)[Sentry Dominix](https://youtu.be/TUl4yicrJ2I)

Single gated pocket. At warp-in, you will see Group 4. Four other groups will spawn several seconds later.
The spawn order is Group 2, Group 1, Group 3 and finally Group 5.

It is recommended that you do not move your ship. The proximity aggro bubble for Group 4 is about 80km and you may get aggroed EITHER during warp-in or if you move towards it.

Group 1 will auto-aggro immediately.
Groups 2, 3, 4 & 5 will remain neutral until attacked or aggroed via proximity.

  - Warning**
- The two Loyal elite frigates in Group 1 will frequently warp scramble you in under 20 secs.
- Proximity aggro is a major factor in this mission. Please be aware that you can aggro a neutral group by just moving into its aggro bubble.

Aggro Notes:
- Attacking Group 1 does not aggro any other groups. You can safely deploy drones to attack this group.
- Attacking Group 2 aggros Group 3 (and vice versa). You can safely deploy drones after the pair have been aggroed.
- Attacking Group 4 aggros Group 5 (attacking Group 5 didn't aggro group 4). You can safely deploy drones after the pair have been aggroed.
- Group 4 may aggro drones if they get too close to that group.

  - Tips:**
- Be absolutely careful not to move towards the neutral groups. Move away from them to minimize setting off the proximity aggro
- Recommended order of elimination: Group 1, Group 5 & Group 4, finally Group 2 & Group 3.
- Mission is flagged completed when ALL ships in Group 5 are killed.

  - Blitz:**Destroy all ships from Group 5 and warp out. Kill warp disruptor if needed.

Ships ordered by distance. Not by spawn order. Ships spawn one group at a time over several seconds after you warp in.
