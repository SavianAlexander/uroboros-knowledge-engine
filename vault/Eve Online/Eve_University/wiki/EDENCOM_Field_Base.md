# EDENCOM Field Base

- EDENCOM Field Base** is a combat anomaly that can be found in **EDENCOM Fortress** systems. It is the hardest of the three EDENCOM combat anomalies.

The site consists of single ungated normal space pocket. **Triglavian Collective** rats spawn in waves at the center of the site.
