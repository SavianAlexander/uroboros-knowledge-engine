Janus Bravour believes, as does the author of this tale, that stagnation is tantamount to death. Janus Bravour is one of the people leading the Caldari State into a new and glorious time. Janus Bravour is [in trouble](//www.eve-online.com/background/potw/15-09-08.asp).

This is a new EVE Chronicle. They are short-stories written by CCP Abraxas, published every other Monday, and intended to examine the various aspects of life in New Eden. The entire list is contained [here](//www.eve-online.com/background/potw/archive.asp).
