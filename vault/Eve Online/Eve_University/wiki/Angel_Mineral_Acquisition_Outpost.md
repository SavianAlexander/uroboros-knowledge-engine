# Angel Mineral Acquisition Outpost

The **Angel Mineral Acquisition Outpost** is a **DED complex** that can be scanned using Core Probes in **Angel** infested regions of Low and Null Sec space. It may also be received as an **escalation** from an **Angel Rally Point** anomaly. It is considered as a 6/10 DED rated complex but this is not displayed in the warp in popup text.
