---

The EVE is Real [image](//www.eveisreal.net/#!/image-contest/) and [video](//www.eveisreal.net/#!/video-contest/) contests have begun, and you can now vote for your favorite player creations at [EVE is Real](//www.eveisreal.net). There are over 4,000 images and videos to choose from. Good prizes await the winners; you can vote once each per day for your favorite image and video. Vote early, vote often! Voting ends September 1, 2011.

While you're at it, be sure to share your favorites using the EVE is Real sharing feature. The more you share, the more Aurum the community will win!
