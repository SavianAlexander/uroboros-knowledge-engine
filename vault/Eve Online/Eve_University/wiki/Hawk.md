1. # Summary

The **Hawk** is an assault frigate specialised in missiles and rockets. It can be fitted with light missiles, but is more often mounted with rockets: its velocity bonus to missiles allows it to fire rockets out to overheated web range.

One of the Hawk's distinguishing features is its layout of 5 mid slots, a feature shared by very few other combat frigates, the **Jaguar**, **Griffin Navy Issue**, and **Hookbill**; this gives it a lot of flexibility for fitting shield tank modules and/or tackling modules.
