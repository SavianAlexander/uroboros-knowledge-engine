1. # Summary
The Skiff is the toughest of the Exhumers without any modules, and becomes as tough if not tougher than many battleships when fully fitted and skilled. However, the skiff sacrifices yield and some ore hold capacity to do this. Therefore, the skiff should be used when under the threat of being ganked or when having to operate under heavy NPC fire. When fitted out correctly, it is impossible to make a profit from suicide ganking a skiff, which should deter all but the most determined people from attacking you in high sec.

For more information on this and other ORE ships, please visit the **ORE Basic Ship and Skill Guide** page.

1. # Tactics

- Due to how vulnerable and cumbersome the mining barges are, it is ill-advised to travel between stargates in lowsec in one, as they make for very juicy targets. Instead, a good idea is to transport your Skiff packaged in a hauler, which raises less (albeit still some) suspicion.
  - A **Badger**, even at base skills, has a capacity of 3,900, allowing it to carry a packaged mining frigate, barge, and even an exhumer, and still have enough cargo space for its modules, can perform the cloak trick, and when designed well, can align in under 6 seconds. Beware however, that when packaging a ship, all its **rigs** are destroyed, so either use cheap Tech I rigs or design your ship around not using them.
