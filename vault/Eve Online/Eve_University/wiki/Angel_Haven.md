# Angel Haven

The Angel Haven is a **combat anomaly** found in **Angel** infested regions. The site is moderately difficult, but may be done with a sniper Battleship or an advanced Cruiser (Faction, T2 or T3).

1. # Walkthrough
Ungated normal space pocket. There are 2 variants of this anomaly, commonly referred to as "gas" or "rock" haven by players. The rock haven variant is readily identified by the warp-in popup received. Each also feature different structures within the site.

Note: In Case of a Dreadnought spawn, note that if the only Ship left is the Dreadnought you must not leave the Site or the Dreadnought will despawn!

1. # Rewards
Bounty of the site is around 30 Million ISK (2020).

1. # Escalation
This site can escalate to **Angel Cartel Naval Shipyard** or alternatively escalate to **Angel Capital Staging**, **Angel Shielded Starbase** and **Angel Occupied Mine**.
