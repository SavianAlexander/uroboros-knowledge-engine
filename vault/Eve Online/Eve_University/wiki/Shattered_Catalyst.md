# Shattered Catalyst

- Shattered Catalyst** is the third and final  part of the ORE epic arc **Fractured Legacy**.

Video: [[Fractured Legacy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t_ECwuKHIVY) 18 November 2025]

The actual mission is within 4-6 jumps away.

Hack either the Interstellar Gravimetric Transmitter or the Emergency Purge Initiator.

On accepting the mission, when not in the correct fitting:

1. # Warp-in

|}

1. # Single room
Approach the Upwell Research Structure to make the containers able to be hacked. The Rubble asteroids will be blocking your path and you will need to mine them in order to get close to the structure without triggering the proximity mines. The mines themselves do 1400 omni damage each.

|}

When approaching the Stellar:

Warp-in triggered by approaching within a certain distance of the Upwell Research Structure.

|}

You will be prompted to either hack the Interstellar Gravimetric Transmitter or the Emergency Purge Initiator to transmit the data or destroy the evidence. Hacking one will make the other invulnerable and non-interactable, and vice versa. This does not seem to affect your final reward.

No penalty received for failing the hack.

Both Proof of Transmission and Proof of Destruction can be sold to NPC buy orders for 1,500,000 ISK.

1. # Notes and references

Previous mission: **Pioneering Spirit**
