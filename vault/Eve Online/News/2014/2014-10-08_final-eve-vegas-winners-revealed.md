We are happy to announce the final two  winners of the [EVE Vegas Player Prize Draw!](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/eve-online-news/eve-vegas-competition-2/)

[AJ Ross](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/AJ%20Ross) of [Wraithguard.](//gate.eveonline.com/Corporation/Wraithguard.) and [Metistia](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Metistia) of [Precision Strike Brigade](//gate.eveonline.com/Corporation/Precision%20Strike%20Brigade) have each won a [Panorama Suite Upgrade](//www.planethollywoodresort.com/content/scaffold_pages/rooms/planet_hollywood/phv/en/ph_panorama_suite/_jcr_content/cards/card.roomhighlights.-_-etc-_-designs-_-cet-_-properties-_-planet_hollywood.phv.1.html)! These rooms are valid for guests arriving Thursday, October 16, 2014 and departing Monday, October 20, 2014).

These final two prize winners will be contacted by CCP in the days after their announcement with further details on how to claim their prizes. 

If you haven't secured your ticket for EVE Vegas 2014, feel free to head on over to the [EVE Vegas Website](//vegas.eveonline.com/)! You can also book your Planet Hollywood Resort and Casino room for a good deal using the EVE Vegas 2014 room code. For more information, [see here!](//vegas.eveonline.com/#location)

Be sure to snap up tickets as soon as you can as we're almost sold out! You can also [follow us on facebook here](//www.facebook.com/pages/EveVegas/206914836005616)!

There is of course still the chance to be part of the winning Alliance in the [EVE Vegas Alliance Prize Draw](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/eve-online-news/eve-vegas-competition/)! Be sure to bring as many of your Alliance wingmen to EVE Vegas as possible to be in with a chance of winning! The results of this draw will be announced this Friday after a draw at 12:00 UTC!
