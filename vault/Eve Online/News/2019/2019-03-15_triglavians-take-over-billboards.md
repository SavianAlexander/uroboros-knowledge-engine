## Overview
Alton Haveri reports on the Triglavian Collective's takeover of New Eden's billboards. The cryptic, looping message which is delivered by "Zorya Triglav" has raised many questions for analysts, with CONCORD now being urged to reveal all of their findings about the Triglavian Collective and their intentions.
