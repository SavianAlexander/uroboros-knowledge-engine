# Blood Refuge

- Blood Refuge** is an unrated **combat site** belonging to the **Blood Raiders** pirate faction. It can be found via **exploration** as a **cosmic anomaly** within systems owned by the **Amarr Empire** and **Ammatar Mandate**. As an ungated complex, the limitations as to which ships can enter this site are tied to the security rating of the system where the anomaly spawns (up to T2 **battleship**s for high-security systems, and **titans** in low-security and null-security systems). Completing this complex may award the player with valuable Dark Blood **faction modules** and ammunition.

This combat site consists of a single ungated pocket of space containing a variety of **frigate**- and **destroyer**-sized enemy ships. Destroying all hostile ships may escalate into the **DED rated**, **Mul-Zatah Monastery** complex.
