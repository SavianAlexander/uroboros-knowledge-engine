# Pirate Invasion (Serpentis)

1. # Warp-In

|}

  1. # 1st Pocket
Each group will aggro independently when attacked.
Acceleration gate to 2nd pocket is at 65-70km.

|}

|}

|}

  1. # 2nd Pocket
Attacking the 3rd Group will aggro the entire pocket.

|}

|}

|}

  1. # Mining

All asteroids are in Pocket 2.  Exact amount is unknown, but a partial survey showed 60,000 to 90,000 Veldspar per asteroid for a rough estimate of 2 million to 3 million Veldspar total.

|}

  1. # Notes
- Video: [Cerberus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d0SkvzNiF1o)
- The War Installation in Pocket 1 may take over 1,000 Heavy Missiles to destroy, making it not worthwhile.
- Structures not listed don't drop loot.
- Structures in the 2nd Pocket are invulnerable.
