He beat us. He beat us all and spared no one!

It had gone unsettled for too long. Who was the best CCP poker player? After grueling qualifiers at each office, CCP held a global poker tournament final table on our company trip to Mexico. Emerging victorious was CCP Tallest, level designer for EVE Online and transplant to the Atlanta office. As his prize, CCP will be sending him to Las Vegas next week to compete in the [World Series of Poke](//www.wsop.com/)r. Follow along with his journey at the "[Stebbi Knows Poker](//www.facebook.com/group.php?gid=135809363101575)" Facebook group, watch his mysterious [interview](//www.youtube.com/watch?v=K-nebE6qp7M) and discuss his chances in [this thread](//www.eveonline.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=1344233). He considers it an honor and a duty to represent internet spaceships. Please wish him luck.
