## Overview
Only available until 10 Sep!

---

### Capsuleers,


If you haven’t yet ordered EVE’s 20th anniversary Collector’s Edition – produced in partnership with Limited Run Games – then you need to act fast! The Collector’s Edition is [ONLY available by pre-order](https://limitedrungames.com/products/eve-20-th-anniversary-collectors-edition) **until 23:59 ET on 10 September** and will not be on general sale anywhere. 

Be sure to get your order in now so you don’t miss out on the [exclusive contents](https://www.eveonline.com/now/collectors-edition), such as the exquisitely produced 8” Megathron ship model, adorable 6” Fedo plushie, an exclusive New Eden Soundtrack CD, EVE art print by Lloyd George, a blueprint poster, a lapel pin set, multiple DLC items, a signed certificate of authenticity, and more.

Limited Run Games are renowned for the craftsmanship and quality of their releases and the exclusive contents within. A number of the digital items included in the Collector’s Edition – specifically the SKINs -are also exclusive to the set, making EVE’s 20th anniversary feel like even more of a momentous occasion, beautifully encapsulated with this highly anticipated release.

Due to the cost of shipping, duties and taxes, for anyone who orders the 20th Anniversary Collector’s Edition, a sum of PLEX will be gifted, based on the total extra cost incurred by the customer. Make sure you read all the details in the Collector’s Edition – Update article before ordering.
