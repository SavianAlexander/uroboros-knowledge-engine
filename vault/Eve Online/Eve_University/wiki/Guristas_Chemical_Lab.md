# Guristas Chemical Lab

Angel Chemical Lab is a **hacking** site that can be scanned using Core Probes in Guristas infested territory in Low Sec space. This site appears to Probes as a Gas Site rather than the typical hacking site. It is also defended by hostile NPC ships.
