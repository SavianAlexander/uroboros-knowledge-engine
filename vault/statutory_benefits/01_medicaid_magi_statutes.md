# Medicaid MAGI Statutory Rules & Federal Regulations (Title XIX)

## 1. Statutory Authority & Scope
Under Title XIX of the Social Security Act and federal regulations at **42 CFR § 435.603**, eligibility for Medicaid for children, pregnant women, parents, and non-elderly adults is determined using Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) rules.

## 3. Statutory 5% FPL Income Disregard
Pursuant to **42 CFR § 435.603(d)**:
- An amount equivalent to **5 percentage points of the Federal Poverty Level** for the applicable family size is subtracted from countable income when determining eligibility under the highest applicable income standard.
- Effectively converts the nominal 133% FPL statutory limit to **138% FPL**.

---

## 4. Resource & Asset Test Prohibition
Under MAGI rules:
- **No Asset Test**: Liquid assets (checking accounts, savings, stocks, 401k) cannot be counted toward MAGI Medicaid eligibility.
- Eligibility is strictly determined by verified countable monthly income, residency, and immigration qualification.
