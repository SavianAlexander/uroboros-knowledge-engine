# Probing Question

1. # Warp-in

This mission does not have acceleration gates.  Distances and directions of enemies are relative to the warp-in point of the mission.  Being part of a fleet (even if it's only a fleet of just yourself) and warping the squad/wing/fleet to the mission "to within 30 km" from the southeast will put you at close range to all waves.  Warping the squad/wing/fleet to the mission "to within" some distance from the north, northwest, or west will increase your initial distance from all waves.

The most recent editor of this page forgot to check if the mission is in deadspace; warping to zero on a fleetmate in the mission might instead warp you to zero on the mission warp-in point.
