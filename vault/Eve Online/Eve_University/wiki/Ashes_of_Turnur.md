# Ashes of Turnur

Ashes of Turnur is an NPC **Minmatar** rebel faction that plays a hostile role in some **Homefront operation**s.

Their ships are weak to Explosive and Kinetic, they deal Thermal (62.5%) and EM (37.5%) damage, and employ **ECM** electronic warfare.

They are the hostiles in these Homefront operations:
- **Salvage Research**
- **Raid: Deserters Hideout**
- **Metaliminal Meteoroid: Minmatar Mining**
