The Battle for Caldari Prime raged for the entirety of last week, reaching an apex on Friday with Gallente forces engaging the Leviathan-class titan, the Shiigeru, and its impromptu support fleet above the planet. While the Caldari emerged on top after the fights on the ground in DUST 514, the Shiigeru was destroyed along with the entire Caldari fleet. The wreckage of the titan then crashed into the planet, leaving behind a broken landscape on Caldari Prime, both in DUST 514 and EVE Online. 

Our latest trailer showcases this moment when the two worlds collided in a way never done before. Head on over to the CCP Games youtube channel and [watch this moment](//www.youtube.com/watch?v=eS4rAYrRHWc) in New Eden history.
