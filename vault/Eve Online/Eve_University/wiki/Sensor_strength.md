# Sensor strength

- Sensor strength** is a stat of **ships** and scan probes that is used to determine the effectiveness of **Electronic Countermeasures** (ECM) and **Probe scanning**.

- For info on sensor strength as it applies to ECM, see **Electronic Countermeasures#ECM Mechanics**
- For info on sensor strength as it applies to scan probes, see **Probe scanning#Probe attributes**
  - For info on the minimum sensor strength needed to analyze signatures, see **Probe scanning#Minimum sensor strength**
