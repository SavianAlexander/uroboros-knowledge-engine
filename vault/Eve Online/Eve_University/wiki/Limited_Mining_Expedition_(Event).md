# Limited Mining Expedition (Event)

In a limited mining expedition site, you need to mine 10,000 units of ore and deposit it in the friendly **Orca**, while under attack from enemy frigates.

1. # Site Details
- 10,000 units Ore required
- 3 Pilots max
- 0.2 Standing Gain
- Allowed Ships: **Corvettes**, **Frigates**, **Venture**
