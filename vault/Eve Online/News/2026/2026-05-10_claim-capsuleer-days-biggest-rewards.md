## Overview
DON’T MISS THE BIG SP HAUL

---

Capsuleers, 

The war between empires has spread to highsec, CONCORD safety measures have collapsed, and Capsuleer Day XXIII: Warpath continues to turn New Eden into a battlefield of opportunity. 

Daily login rewards include Skill Points, new SKINs, SKINR components, boosters, and more, while the seasonal reward track offers PLEX, SoCT ships, more SP, item trader tokens, and other valuable loot. 

11 May is the last day to begin logging in and still claim the big Skill Point haul before the end of the event. 

The conflict is set to escalate on 26 May, when the seasonal reward track extends with unmissable surprises for committed capsuleers. Until then, there are complexes to defend, titans to support, intel to hack, and riches to claim. 


## **Start Logging in Today  **


Log in every day of Warpath to claim new Capsuleer Day XXIII SKINs, SKINR design components, boosters, EverMarks, Skill Points, and more throughout the event. 

The biggest SP haul arrives later in the track, so start logging in from tomorrow, 11 May, to make sure you don’t miss it. Omega pilots can claim both Alpha and Omega rewards, and upgrading to Omega during the event unlocks previous Omega rewards retroactively. 

On day 22, Alphas can claim 75,000 SP, and Omegas can claim an additional 250,000, for a grand total of 325,000 SP. In total, the track awards 140,000 SP to Alphas and 525,000 to Omegas, for a whopping 665,000 SP in all! 


## **Push the Warpath **


Capsuleer Day XXIII: Warpath offers activities across combat, exploration, industry, and mining, with challenges suited to both newer pilots and veterans hungry for greater risk. In addition to those Capsuleer Day specific challenges, regular activities like mining Plagioclase and Crokite in empire space, completing empire security missions, and Factional Warfare will also contribute to the seasonal track. 

As mentioned [last week](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/capsuleer-day-xxiii-warpath-is-here), pilots looking for a challenge can take on fleet-based capital support sites, which are now live in lowsec. In these sites, codenamed Warpath: Capital Mayday, you will deliver cap boosters to a defending titan to help it defeat an invading one. 


## **Trade Tokens for Power   **


Tokens earned through the seasonal reward track and by running the new sites introduced in the event can be traded in to item traders for new faction and officer module blueprint copies, empire boosters, new SKINs, SKINR components, and other valuable rewards. 


## **Community Fittings and Guides   **


Updated Community Fittings are now available in-game to help capsuleers take on Warpath’s combat and exploration challenges. Whether you are defending your first highsec Warpath Defense Complex or preparing for lowsec operations, these fittings can help get your ship into the fight faster. 

Capsuleers across New Eden have also been putting together guides, videos, and site breakdowns to help pilots make the most of the event. 


## **Gear Up for the War **


Check out the latest Capsuleer Day offers in the [EVE Store](https://store.eveonline.com/) to stock up on Omega, PLEX, SKINs, and more before pushing deeper into Warpath.
