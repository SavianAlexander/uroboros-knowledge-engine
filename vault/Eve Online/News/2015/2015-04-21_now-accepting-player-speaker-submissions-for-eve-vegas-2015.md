Are you planning to attend EVE Vegas and want to be part of its’ speaker program?

EVE Vegas is an event made for EVE Online players and fans, and has a strong tradition EVE players and community luminaries presenting together on one stage along with EVE devs.

We are currently accepting proposals from player speakers for the event. If you want to submit your proposal please send us the following info to [evevegas@ccpgames.com](mailto:evevegas@ccpgames.com)


- Brief outline of your talk proposal (feel free to submit more than one proposal)
- The proposed duration of  your talk (can be anywhere between 10 to 40 minutes)
- Player character name (primary)
- Your full name, telephone number and e-mail


All player speakers selected will get a complimentary speaker badge to EVE Vegas 2015 and ticket to the Pub Crawl.

We’ve already started work on the speaker schedule for the event so please send us your proposal as soon as you have your outline for it ready.

Final deadline for speaker proposals is September 1st 2015. But we like to point out that speaker slots are limited and you should confirm your slot sooner rather than later to avoid missing out on being a speaker.

EVE Vegas 2015 takes place October 23-25 at Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino, Las Vegas. For tickets and further information please visit: [vegas.eveonline.com/](//vegas.eveonline.com/)

If you have any questions on the speaker submissions please contact; [evevegas@ccpgames.com](mailto:evevegas@ccpgames.com)
