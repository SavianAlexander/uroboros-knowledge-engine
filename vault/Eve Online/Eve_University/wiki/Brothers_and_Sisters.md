# Brothers and Sisters

<noinclude></noinclude>

  - Objective**
Fly back to Arnon and report once again to Sister Alitura at [Arnon IX - Moon 3 - Sisters of EVE Bureau station](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/station/Arnon_IX_-_Moon_3_-_Sisters_of_EVE_Bureau). It's a bit of a trip - 22 high sec jumps away. Be careful when planning. There is a shorter route (17 jumps) but that will travel through 3 low-sec systems. EVE Gatecheck is your friend [EVE Gatecheck, Secure](https://eve-gatecheck.space/eve/index.php#Tanoo:Arnon:secure)

  - Notes**
The mission can be accepted remotely.
