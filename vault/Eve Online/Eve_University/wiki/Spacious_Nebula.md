# Spacious Nebula

- Spacious Nebula*' is a gas exploration site containing gas used in the production of **Medical boosters**.

This is a null sec gas site found through Exploration.

Defenders:

Every time your gas harvesters complete a cycle, the cloud has a chance to send a blast of radioactive particles which will damage all ships within a certain area. It is advised to use a ship with a decent amount of tank if you want to try and complete the site without dying.

Minable Gas Clouds:

4 Amber Cytoserocin (2x 2000 units and 2x 1,000 units gas clouds)

Amber Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Pure Standard Blue Pill Booster.

This site is found only in the[E-8CSQ](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Vale_of_the_Silent/E-8CSQ)constellation of the[Vale of the Silent](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Vale_of_the_Silent)region of space.
