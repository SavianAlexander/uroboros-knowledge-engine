# Keeping Crime In Check

<noinclude>
  - Data center** mission
Agent: Various
Agent location: Various **Data centers**
Standings required: Varies</noinclude>

Your job is to bring the agent the pirate tags that they want. The type and amount of tags vary by agent. The mission can be done only once and can not be retried after failing.

The mission gives faction standing with the faction of the agent. The mission does not give agent standing.

See **Data centers** for details on locations, agents and required tags.<noinclude>
