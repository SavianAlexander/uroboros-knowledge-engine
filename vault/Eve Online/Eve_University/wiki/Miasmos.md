1. # Summary
The Miasmos is designed to haul raw ore, gas, and ice in large amounts.

1. # Tactics
  1. # Hull Buffer Tank
Like other Gallente specialized haulers such as the  and the , the  uses a specialized hold as its main hold instead of a cargohold. This fact, alongside the strong hull hitpoints that Gallente ships already get, means that the Miasmos can fit a decently sized hull buffer tank using Transverse Bulkhead rigs and Reinforced Bulkhead modules, and it will *not* suffer any negative effects on the size of its specialized Mining Hold because those modules only decrease *cargohold* size. A Miasmos with only a simple hull tank buffer can achieve an EHP of about 14,000. While this isn't the largest buffer tank a T1 hauler can achieve, the advantage is that it leaves nearly all of the Powergrid available for fitting, which can include a 50MN Compact MWD and an Improved Cloaking Device II, so the Miasmos can also use the **cloak trick** while fielding a decent tank, which is a challenging combination for most other T1 Haulers.

  1. # Smart hauling practices
- **Don't Haul AFK**
- **Use insta-undocks**
- **3,000 ISK per EHP**.
- **Overload** tank modules when attacked
