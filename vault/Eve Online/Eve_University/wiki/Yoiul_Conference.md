# Yoiul Conference

The **Yoiul Conference** was a gathering of the four major empire **factions**, held aboard the **Jovian** **cruiser** *Yoiul*.

The conference serves as the beginning of the New Eden Universal Calendar, with the Yoiul Conference marking YC0. The New Eden calendar can be mapped to the real-world Gregorian calendar, by adding/subtracting 1898.  For example:

- YC105 - 2003
- YC106 - 2004
- YC107 - 2005
- YC108 - 2006
- YC109 - 2007
- YC110 - 2008
- YC111 - 2009
- YC112 - 2010
- YC113 - 2011
- YC114 - 2012
- YC115 - 2013
- YC116 - 2014
- YC117 - 2015
- YC118 - 2016
- YC119 - 2017
- YC120 - 2018
- YC121 - 2019
- YC122 - 2020
- YC123 - 2021
- YC124 - 2022
- YC125 - 2023
- YC126 - 2024
- YC127 - 2025
- YC128 - 2026
