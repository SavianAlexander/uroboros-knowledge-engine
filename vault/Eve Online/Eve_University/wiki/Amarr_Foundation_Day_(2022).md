# Amarr Foundation Day (2022)

- Amarr Foundation Day** is a limited-time event that takes place from 2nd to 9th August 2022, **downtime** to downtime. During this period, capsuleers can log in to earn fireworks and SKINs. This event features celebrations in select systems of **Amarr Empire** high security space.

1. # Parade
During the event, special event beacons with an Amarr fleet are spawned. They were named after the titan of the fleet, and appeared as a beacon in space as "TES <titan name> Parade". 

The systems where parades will take place are:
- Amarr  (TES Jamyl the Liberator, ~550 km off of Amarr VI (Zorast) - Moon 2 - Theology Council Tribunal)
- Ardishapur Prime  (TES Yonis the Pious, ~500 km off of Ardishapur Prime IX - Moon 17 - Ardishapur Family Bureau)
- Kador Prime  (TES Uriam of Fiery Heart, ~525 km off of Kador Prime VI - Moon 1 - Kador Family Bureau)
- Khanid Prime  (TES Garkeh of the Marches, ~340 km off of Khanid Prime V - Moon 1 - Khanid Innovation Factory)
- Kor-Azor Prime  (TES Articio the Redeemed, ~550 km off of Kor-Azor Prime II - Kor-Azor Family Bureau)
- Sarum Prime  (TES Merimeth the Serene, off of Sarum Prime VI - Moon 7 - Sarum Family Assemvly Plant)
- Tash-Murkon Prime  (TES Catiz of Tash-Murkon Parade, ~325 km off of Tash-Murkon Prime V - Moon 5 - Tash-Murkon Family Bureau)

1. # Special mechanics
All systems in Amarr space (including Khanid and Ammatar space) will receive a Foundation Day system-wide increase in ship warp speed.
