# Outreach Department

- EVE University's** **Outreach Department**'s main aim is to facilitate the exchange of people and knowledge with other groups that are interested in and share our educational mission. Outreach Coordinators are responsible for organizing and support collaboration with other groups in EVE Online, coordinating opportunities for shared content, teaching and learning. In this role, they work closely with the **Teaching Department** to identify avenues in which EVE University can propagate its knowledge and resources and make contacts with other groups.
