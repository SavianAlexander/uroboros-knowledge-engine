# Glass Nebula

- Glass Nebula** is a gas **exploration** site containing gas used in the production of **Medical Boosters**.

1. # Defenders
None - no rats spawn in this site

1. # Minable Gas Clouds

3x Celadon Mykoserocin (2000 units each)

  1. # Celadon Mykoserocin
Celadon Mykoserocin is used in the production of the Exile Booster.

1. # Location
These sites can be found in [Syndicate Region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Syndicate#const) and [Solitude Region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Solitude#const) (both high and low security space), with a hot spot in the [Elerelle](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Solitude/Elerelle#const) constellation. 

Encountered in Phoenix Region 13 January 2022.
Encountered in Domain Region 24 February 2022.
Encountered in Verge Vendor Region 2 February 2024.
