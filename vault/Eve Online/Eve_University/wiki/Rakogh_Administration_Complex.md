1. # Asteroid Room

Warping to asteriod side- The area of deadspace which the Rakogh Administration Complex resides in is filled with pockets full of asteroids.  This provides the complex with easily obtainable resources to build structures and ships used by Pashan and his minions.  These pockets are well defended incase outsiders happen to stumble into them, Pashan is fiercely protective of his territory.

1. # Citadel Room

|}

Rakogh Citadel has 960 DPS shield reps, 0 resists to EM. 1.1k dps cracked it with 7k alpha.

It drops: 23rd Tier Overseer's Personal Effects	and Information Command Mindlink. About 1/7 chance to drop either Pashan's Turret Customization or Turret Handling Mindlink (Jita sell price ~4-5bil as of 4/3/24).
