# Gallente Election YC128

- Gallente Election YC128** is a limited time event from 19th March to 16th April 2026, **downtime** to downtime. Capsuleer Outreach Centers remain active till 23 April 2026.

Choose a candidate to support and complete challenges to influence the election outcome. The candidate whose supporters have earned the most combined seasonal points at the end of the event will be declared President of the Gallente Federation.

Your candidate selection choice will not limit the rewards you can receive. Throughout the event, regardless of your choice, you will receive Political Favor which can be exchanged at Capsuleer Outreach Centers in Dodixie for any of the rewards.

1. # Login Campaign
Capsuleers must log in daily to collect all rewards. The campaign rewards **SKINr** materials, **Boosters** and 145.000 **Skill Points** for  with 535.000 additional Skill Points, and 31,500 **EverMark**s for . Due to a bug which made it impossible to claim the rewards on day 26 the login campaign was extenden by a day.

1. # Combat sites
  1. # Rampant Drone Fabricator
  - Rampant Drone Fabricator** is a **cosmic signature** that comes in different difficulty levels depending on the location. The site NPCs deal (and are weak to) EM and Thermal damage. A strongly tanked T3 Cruiser or better is recommended to make any decent progress through the waves.

1. # Hacking sites

  1. # Drone Iteration Depot
Drone Iteration Depots are former industrial depots that have been converted into data stockpiles by Rogue Drones.

- Can be found in highsec, lowsec, nullsec and wormholes as cosmic signatures. Highsec sites are level II scanning difficulty, while in all other areas of space they are level III scanning difficulty.
- The Highsec version contains Iteration Databanks (70 HP green core). Lower security versions contain the more valuable Iteration Vaults (90 HP red core).
- Drone Iteration Databanks (appear to) contain 3-6x Drone Iteration Data, which can be sold to NPCs for 1M ISK apiece. Iteration Vaults will contain between 7-11x Drone Iteration Data.
