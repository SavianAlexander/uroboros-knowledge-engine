# Following the Blood

- Following the Blood** is an **expedition** that escalates from **Blood Lookout** combat site. The escalation has four locations. Generally three of the locations are in high security space while the last one is in low security space.

1. # 1st location
Ungated area. The rats are arranged in two groups which can be aggressed separately. Group 1 auto-aggros and has the commander among them. Group 2 does not aggro until attacked. Killing the commander frigate can trigger escalation. If no commander frigate is present then killing all ships may trigger escalation. 

|}

1. # 2nd location
Ungated area. Killing the commander frigate can trigger escalation. If no commander frigate is present then killing all ships may trigger escalation.

|}

1. # 3rd location
Note: You will a need mining laser. A Civilian Miner won't work, but a Miner I will do just fine. Alternatively, mining drones also trigger the defender spawn.

Ungated area. A Rock Formation is about 30km away. Close to the rock formation (in a blueish little cloud) there is a small Arkonor asteroid. Mine the asteroid (you have to complete one or two mining cycles) to spawn the defenders.

|}

1. # 4th location
  1. # 1st pocket
Use caution; the rats spawn at 0 on warp-in.

|}

  1. # 2nd pocket

One of the frigates is the overseer but has a generic frigate name. You will know it's destroyed when you see the completion message popping up.

|}
