# FC Development Group

1. # What is the FC Development Group?
The **Fleet Command Development Group** is a a group aimed at supporting and developing PVP FCs in EVE University.

  - Note that the FC Team is focused on PVP FCing only. There are other places you can learn PVE and mining FCing.**

1. # Objectives

The FC Development Group aims to achieve the following objectives:

- Provide a structured and supportive environment for fleet commanders within EVE University.
- Promote the progression and skill development of FCs at various experience levels.
- Facilitate the sharing of knowledge and best practices among FCs.
- Provide practical training to prospective FCs to improve their skills and confidence.
- Enhance fleet coordination, efficiency, and effectiveness in PvP operations.
- Form relationships and collaborate with other groups.

1. # Ranks
On joining the FC Development Group, members will be allocated to one of the following ranks: 
- **Junior FC:** For members new to FCing, with little to no experience, but who have participated in PvP fleets.
- **Core FC:** For members with FCing experience, who are regularly leading fleets, and capable of mentoring newer players.

1. # Support
As well as the above ranks we also have **FC Advisors**, who are experienced FCs who are here to support and mentor newer FCs. We also have **Guest FCs** who are external people who may sometimes come and run fleets for us.

1. # Rules and Expectations
 
To maintain professionalism and ensure a conducive environment within the FC Development Group, the following rules and expectations apply:

# FCs must actively lead fleets. 
# Core FCs must be willing to mentor and support Junior FCs to help them in their progression. This might be by inviting them to 2IC, involving them in command channels, back seating their fleets as support, assisting with practical exercises, providing feedback on AARs etc.
# Group members are expected to maintain respectful interactions with each other and other members. 
# FCs are responsible for ensuring the well-being of new players within fleets. The majority of our fleets should be new player friendly, and thought should be given to which **Flight Deck** ships a new member can fly to join in.

1. # Joining the FC Development Group
[Click HERE to apply to join](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSekLKLDyUofoyleQy-9kviuifbRTAKKCSS-A7NTt4uFQEFUBw/viewform?usp=dialog)

We welcome members of all skill levels, from seasoned PVP FCs to those who are dipping their toes into the world of PVP fleet command for the first time. Our mission is to provide a structured and supportive environment for aspiring and experienced FCs alike. But hold on, there's a small prerequisite: *we kindly ask that you've participated in at least a couple of EVE University PVP fleets before applying, at least one of which you’ve FC’d with an FCbackseat*. This is so we know you have the hang of how EVE University specifically does things, and understand basic fleet commands and movements.

As a member of the FC team you can expect: 

- **Rank Progression:** Allowing you to grow and learn at your own pace.
- **Mentorship and Training:** Our seasoned FCs are here to guide and mentor you every step of the way. We believe in learning by doing, and what better way to learn than under the guidance of experienced pilots?
- **Practical Experience:** From leading frigate fleets to coordinating large-scale battles, you'll get hands-on experience in various fleet compositions and tactics.
- **Collaboration:** We're not just a team; we're part of EVE University as a whole. You'll have the opportunity to collaborate with other EVE University departments and NPSI groups to expand your horizons and share your knowledge.

So, are you ready to embark on an exciting journey as a PVP fleet commander? Join us, and let's make EVE Online history! Remember, no matter your level of experience, a passion for learning, leading, and of course, space explosions, is all you need.
