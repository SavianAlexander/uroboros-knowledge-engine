1. # Summary
The Azariel is the Angel Cartel titan. It requires Gallente and Minmatar skills to fly. 
Blueprint copies for this ship can be acquired from the insurgency LP stores in Zarzakh or the pirate FOB in insurgency systems. As of 10 April 2026, 7 have been lost in EVE history, with two being lost in the battle of Atioth on 7 April 2026, the largest battle in EVE history up until that point.
