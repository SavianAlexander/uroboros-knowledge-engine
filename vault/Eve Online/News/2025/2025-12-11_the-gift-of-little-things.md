## Overview
Nice quality-of-life updates to celebrate the season.

---

Festive capsuleers,

Different cultures have different customs around the season of giving. A certain island in the North, closely tied to New Eden, has 13 Yule Lads who bring presents for kids (not to be confused with the Yoiul LADs roaming the Volatile Ice Storms during Winter Nexus).

The first of these gift-bearing trolls arrives in the early hours of 12 December, and the night before sees Icelandic children putting their shoe in the window to receive a little thing from the Yule Lads.

In that spirit, a collection of “Little Things” are arriving in New Eden, to sweeten the season. So put your shoe in the window (optionally) and get ready for a fine collection of nice surprises.


## ‘Tis the Season


Here are a few new updates coming today:


- You can now add the option to allow a second column in your fleet watchlist.
- You can now restore your watchlist when rejoining a fleet.
- The “simulate from clipboard” option has been added to the fitting window.
- New expiry options for bookmarks have been added.
- “Site name” has been added to the tooltip in the probe scan window.
- [And much more](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/patch-notes-version-23-02).


Enjoy these additions and have a lovely Winter Nexus!
