# Triglavian Invasion Escalates

- **Source**: https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/triglavian-invasion-escalates

## Overview
Alton Haveri reports on the Triglavian Collective's continuing invasion of known space. Activating so-called Conduits in multiple zones, Triglavian ships continue to pour through, with Capsuleers providing the main defense against these attacks. 

Translations of an intercepted message suggest that this is the second phase of the Triglavian advance. CONCORD and the Empires remain passive among calls for them to intervene.
