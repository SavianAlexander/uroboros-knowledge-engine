# Stellar Observation Post

The **Stellar Observation Post** is a combat anomaly found in EDENCOM-controlled systems.

The site is centered around a destroyable structure, the Stellar Observatory. The Observatory projects a system-wide effect. Destroying it will temporarily remove this effect.

The type of rats in the site and the type of effect it provides depends on the empire that holds the system.
