The second day of sessions between the EVE Online Development Team and the Council of Stellar Management occured yesterday, with much talk about many things!

With [Ali Aras](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Ali%20Aras), [Sion Kumitomo](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Sion%20Kumitomo), [Corbexx](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/corbexx), [Xander Phoena](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Xander%20Phoena), [corebloodbrothers](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/corebloodbrothers), [Sugar Kyle](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Sugar%20Kyle) and [progodlegend](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/progodlegend) here at CCP Headquarters to attend the summit, and the rest of the council attending sessions remotely, the second day of presentations and talks were held on Wednesday, January 21st in the new 4 day summit format.

After another 8 hours of talks, we are happy to present the CSM Summit Minutes for the second day of the CSM 9 Winter Summit for your reading pleasure, in both PDF format, and raw text form on request of the community:


- [PDF Version](//cdn1.eveonline.com/community/csm/Meetings/summit/CSM9-WS-D2-F.pdf)
- [Raw text version](//cdn1.eveonline.com/community/csm/Meetings/summit/CSM9-WS-D2-F.txt)


We have two days of the summit left to go and will be releasing the minutes over the course of the rest of the week as talks and presentations continue. To discuss their content, feel free to head on over to [this thread!](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=5421226)
