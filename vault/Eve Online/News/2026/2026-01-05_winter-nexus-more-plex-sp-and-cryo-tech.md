## Overview
Extended reward track, Yoiul LADs drop more loot.

---

Capsuleers, 

Volatile Ice Storms are roaming New Eden, pilots are busy hunting Yoiul LADs, and taking on seasonal challenges. 

Capsuleers have struck a blow to the heart of EverMore’s corruption, but the final push is still ahead. The anonymous source within Verity Enhancements warns that the Paragon Hub discounts, now in the final week, are mere corporate distractions. 

The real focus must be on destroying the Yoiul LADs and striking against EverMore. And this final week, the LADs drop more Cryo Tech than before. 

The seasonal challenge track has been extended and more rewards added, including a whopping 75 PLEX (making the track total 100 PLEX – worth hundreds of millions of ISK on the market), as well as 100,000 Skill Points, a SKIN and Expert System for the new Perseverance ice mining destroyer – itself only available through the seasonal track via BPC – and more. 

In addition, the Succubus (Sansha’s Nation frigate) has gotten a ship balance pass to celebrate the final phase of this year’s Winter Nexus. It has received a boost to its lock range, capacitor, power grid, and a new role bonus, reducing the activation cost of small energy turrets. While this coincides with the event, the change is permanent.

The daily login rewards and continued challenge track offer a wealth of stunning Aurora Universalis SKINs and SKINR components, so this is the perfect chance to decorate your ships.
