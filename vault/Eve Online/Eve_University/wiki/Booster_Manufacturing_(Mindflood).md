Simple trade mission where the agent requests some Cytoserocin and gives a drug BPC as a reward. The gas can be mined locally if you have a **Venture** or **Prospect** and core probes. Alternatively you can use a blockade runner or prospect to haul the gas from elsewhere. This mission can be completed once for each agent (**Marera Arghun**, **Nemphad Azbias**, **Rasa Jaswelu**, **Yoti Haraisha**).
