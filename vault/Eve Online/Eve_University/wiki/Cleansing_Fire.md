# Cleansing Fire

- Blitz:** Destroy the Amarr Cathedral, warp out.

  - Notes:** Mission is in deadspace, you will always be warping to the entry point(at range is allowed) and cannot warp to fleet members/bookmarks directly.

  1. # Warp-in
Between all the spawns, if not controlled properly, there can be over 200 gj/s of neuting.
