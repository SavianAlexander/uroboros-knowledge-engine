# The Curator

- The Curator** is a level 3 **Amarr COSMOS** agent, located at the second stage of **Museum Arcana** in Zimse. You need the "Key to Arcana" to access this room. It can be acquired by hacking the "Trial of Skill" can in the first room.

1. # Unearthing the Ancients (1 of 3)

The Takmahl Centrifugal Primer can be acquired by hacking Takmahl Digital Mezzotint

1. # Unearthing the Ancients (2 of 3)

The Holy Statue can be acquired by hacking Takmahl Strikking Curiosity

1. # Unearthing the Ancients (3 of 3)

The Ritual Texts can be acquired by destroying the Arcana Patron battleships in the room and looting the wreck. 

1. # Notes

The final mission gives 2% Amarr Empire standings () and a **"Key to the Labyrinth"**.
Since there is only a limited chance to find the desired items per can, it might take some time to get them all. A battleship for killing the spawns plus a well tanked fast hacking ship (**Stratios**, T2 or T3 cruiser) works well. There is a high chance to encounter other players here.

Loot Example:

Spending 45 minutes onsite with 2 battleships (one of which was hacking) produced 35 million in loot, 33 million of which were from the cans, only 2 million was from wrecked vessels. Also note that the respawn rate of rats is high enough to exceed the ability of an MTU to collect. You therefore cannot clear a site entirely.
