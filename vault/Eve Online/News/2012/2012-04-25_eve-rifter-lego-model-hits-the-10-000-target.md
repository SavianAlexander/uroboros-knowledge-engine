On Monday, March 5, czar set out to promote the [EVE Rifter model](//lego.cuusoo.com/ideas/view/11619) on the LEGO Cuusoo site. Players soon rallied to the cause and we saw players getting involved in a social media campaign to push for this model to reach the 10,000 supporters mark. Even CCP Guard showed his LEGO skills in our latest video [Dev Blog](//www.youtube.com/watch?v=XNc8VAmtO5E&list=PL5752437CA9E0008D&index=1&feature=plpp_video). In a little over seven weeks, you have helped achieve this goal and the Rifter has now reached the target for a full review. On behalf of czar and everyone at CCP, Thank You!
