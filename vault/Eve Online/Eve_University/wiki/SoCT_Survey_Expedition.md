The SoCT Survey Expedition is a **Capsuleer Day XX** combat event site and a variant of the **Guri Malakim Survey Base** site. You will face 2 waves of **Society of Conscious Thought**, **EDENCOM** and **CONCORD** NPCs that will need to be destroyed in order to spawn the boss wave. The site will appears as a beacon in the overview when warped to. This site does not provide any bounties as you will fight non pirate NPCs. The second wave appears to be timed, so it can spawn the boss if you take too much time to clear this wave. This site features the new Soct frigate : **Metamorphosis**

|}

1. # Notes
NPCs do not drop any loot except the master. Salvaging in this site is around 500K ISK.
All remaining NPCs will warp away when the master is destroyed so go immediately for him when he appears.
All the NPCs orbit quite close, generally within 10km.
All enemies have uniform resistance profile, so use the ammo type you prefer.
