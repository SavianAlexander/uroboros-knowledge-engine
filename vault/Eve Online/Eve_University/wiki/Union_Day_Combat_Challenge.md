# Union Day Combat Challenge

The **Union Day Combat Challenge** is a limited-time combat site that spawns during the **Caldari Union Day (2022)**. It contains 4 rooms with different faction enemies, being the Rogue Drones, Guristas Pirates, Triglavian Collective, and the Gallente Federation. 

Notes:
- You will NOT get any faction standing losses from killing any opponents in this site.
- You will receive a considerable amount of neut pressure while doing this site, especially on the third room.
- Atleast for the first room, all enemies have no resists. Subseqent rats in the following rooms have similar natural resist holes to their usual variants.
