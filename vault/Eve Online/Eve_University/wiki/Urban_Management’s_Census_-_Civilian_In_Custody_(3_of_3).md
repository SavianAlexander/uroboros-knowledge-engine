This mission includes long distances to burn and some light combat. Frigate with MWD is very much recommended.

The warp in area is called "Forward Point" and has three gates labeled Alpha, Beta and Gamma. They are approximately 110km-112km away.
- Other helpsites imply that the initial gates Alpha, Beta and Gamma always lead to the same rooms. This is untrue. Sometimes Alpha will lead to Yttora's Corpse, sometimes Beta, sometimes Gamma. Its random.
- One gate will fly you to a room which has a gate labeled "Gate to Foward Point" and will be guarded by 4 Maru ships (Punks and Hackers). They sometimes use webs and scramblers.
- One gate will fly to a room with an unguarded gate labeled "Ancient Acceleraton Gate" that is approximately 176km away. That gate in turn flys you to a room that has yet another gate labeled "Gate to Forward Point". This Forward Point gate is guarded by 1 Maru Sniper and 5 Maru Punks. These ships can webify.
- One gate will fly you to a room which has an unguarded gate labeled "Ancient Acceleration Gate" that is approximately 226km away. THIS is the correct path to Yttora's Corpse. Flying thru this gate will put you in an unguarded room that has two gates, 240km - 290km away, labeled "Accleration Gate Alpha" and "Acceleration Gate Beta".
- Using Acceleration Gate Alpha will fly to you a room that has a container icon labeled "Ship Wreckage" guarded by 3 drones. The container and drones are approximately 178km - 185km away. The container has Yttora's Corpse inside.
- Using Acceleration Gate Beta will fly you to a room that has an uguarded gate labeled "Ancient Acceleration Gate" approximately 200km away. Flying though this gate will put you in yet another room that has a gate labeled "Gate to Forward Point", guarded by 7 drones that can (but not always) both web and scramble.<noinclude>
