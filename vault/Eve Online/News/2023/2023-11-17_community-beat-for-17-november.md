### Havoc expansion notes from EVE Partners



## EVE Pandora V5


We also saw an extension to the number of opportunities now available to be used by corporations in the expansion, but a community initiative that existed before that, [EVE Pandora](https://forums.eveonline.com/t/presenting-eve-pandora/339592/90?u=det_resprox). DeT Resprox launched V5 of EVE Pandora live last week! It features a new Faction Warfare Suite with live FW stats, setting [objectives](https://evepandora.com/eve-online-pandora-campaigns/top-of-the-pile-angel-incursion/) and [campaigns](https://evepandora.com/eve-online-pandora-campaigns/top-of-the-pile-guristas-incursion/) across the war zones. With billions up for grabs, if you're considering hopping into the Warzone, we recommend checking it out, as you may find you can get a little extra ISK for your efforts!


## Do your part on the frontline!


New services, corporations, and propaganda also started appearing this week! For those interested in mercenary work Merc Net switched over to [Deathless Net](https://discord.gg/Ts2QYy6UvB), [The Rabbit Warren](https://www.reddit.com/r/Eve/comments/17vgc2v/the_rabbits_warren_dedicated_guristas_milita/) were set up for the Guristas, and [**Zarzakh Angel Affiliates / **](https://www.reddit.com/r/Eve/comments/17s7hwy/zaa_npsi_angel_cartel_fw_community/)[The Angel Cartel](https://discord.gg/q8HDNzfdcU) also sprung up for those looking to get more engaged with the Angels. If you're looking to get more involved with some of the piracy work, Deathless Net is a great place to explore some of the different pirated-orientated corps, these are all a great place to start!

EVE Partner Rixx Javix also made a [sleek wallpaper](https://eveoganda.blogspot.com/2023/11/havoc.html) for those looking to join the Guristas!

Despite the push from the pirates, the already established empire factions also continue to run recruitment campaigns, such as the Minmatar Republic, checking in with their regular flyers and newsletters giving updates from the warzone! Even those enrolled in the Gallente Militia are putting out statistics to show that, despite their numbers, they're still holding firm against the tsunami of invading pilots.


## FC can I bring my 'gator?


We finish off with two videos from another EVE Partner, Loru.  When new ships are introduced, there's always a period of experimentation to find out what synergizes best with the ship's traits and available fitting room.
