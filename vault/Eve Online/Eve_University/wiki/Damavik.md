1. # Summary
The Damavik is a precursor ship. It was released in the Into The Abyss expansion on May the 29th 2018, alongside the **Vedmak** and **Leshak**.

Only the precursor ships can fit **Entropic Disintegrators**. The new weapon system occupies a single turret slot and only one can be fitted, limited to Triglavian ships. This opens many possibilities as a large number of utility highs remain free.

The Entropic Disintegrator has unique characteristics such as increasing damage per cycle and no falloff. When a target is out of range of the weapon, or if any event causes the weapon to deactivate, the damage ramp-up is reset. The weapon system uses tracking mechanics and is impacted by tracking modules, using Entropic Radiation Sink modules as damage modules. The damage type is restricted to explosive and thermal. Ammunition is loaded into the Disintegrator like conventional weapon systems and has no reload time.

1. # Tactics
As a part of the Triglavian Collective lineup, the Damavik possesses several unique attributes that incentivize certain tactics. Spider-tanking fleets with other Triglavian ships or logi is very effective, and its **Smartbomb** bonus can prove effective in PvE situations (although small smartbombs are rarely used). Due to the nature of Entropic Disintegrators, staying and focusing on a single target without stopping is strongly recommended as the damage for entropic disintegrators slowly builds up over time. It is strongly recommended to fit Energy Neutralizers in the surplus utility high-slots due to the strong bonus the ship receives for them.

1. # Roles

    - PVE***

The nature of Entropic Disintegrators causes the ship to be more PVP oriented, although it still performs extraordinarily in PVE. The Damavik is useful for null sec belt ratting due to its damage ramping bonus (which takes lots of time, something a frigate has when killing NPC battleships). It is recommended to have a small fleet of Damaviks ratting, due to the remote armor repair bonuses and the relatively close proximity of frigates while orbiting 500m of an NPC battleship. A fleet of fully skilled Damaviks could take down havens with ease. The primary of Damaviks fleets should be frigates, then destroyers/cruisers, and finally battleships. Sig and speed tanking are encouraged.

    - PVP***

The Damavik pairs well with pretty much any other ship. A fleet of Damaviks can spider tank and free up a low slot - something that can be utilized for extra DPS, more tank, or more fitting resources for other modules. The Damavik is also a great kiter - able to switch to long range ammo with no delay. The no-falloff/tracking is a huge advantage for the Triglavian ships, as they are practically immune to all but the most talented kiters. A tracking disruptor can help the Damavik survive in slightly larger scale fleet operations, although if that happens the pilot should upgrade to a Kikimora/Vedmak/Leshak. The nemesis of the Damavik is the **Garmur**.

1. # Notes
Like all Tech 1 Triglavian subcapital ships, the Clade that designed the Damavik, Perun, has their name engraved atop the ship in Triglavian font (). In Slavic mythology, a Damavik is the alternate Belarusian naming of a Domovoy, a benevolent household spirit of a given family, tasked to look after its ancestors.
