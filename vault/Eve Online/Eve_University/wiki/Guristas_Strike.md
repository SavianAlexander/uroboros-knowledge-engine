# Guristas Strike

- Guristas Strike** is a 10 part **storyline mission** mission chain. It consists of 3 courier and 7 encounter missions. Like all storyline missions there is no **loyalty points** reward for any of the missions. However the faction standings, normally received for storyline missions, are only rewarded to the final mission. The other missions in the chain only increase corporation standings and, unusual for storyline missions, agent standings.
