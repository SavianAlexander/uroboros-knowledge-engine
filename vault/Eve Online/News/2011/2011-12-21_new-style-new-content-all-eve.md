Along with the launch of the new [EVElopedia](//wiki.eveonline.com/en/wiki/Main_Page) design, we are proud to reveal the completely revamped and revitalized [Fiction Portal](//wiki.eveonline.com/en/wiki/Portal:EVE_Fiction). The Fiction Portal is a collection of over a thousand new EVElopedia articles all focused on EVE backstory and lore. Their content has been drawn from the myriad of EVE-related published material, completely rewritten for the wiki, and expanded on considerably with brand new background material never before seen. The Fiction Portal will be an ongoing project for CCP, and we encourage our players to head over and dive in at the deep end of EVE lore.

For more information please read [this dev blog](//www.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&nbid=3334) by CCP Abraxas, Senior Writer for EVE Online.
