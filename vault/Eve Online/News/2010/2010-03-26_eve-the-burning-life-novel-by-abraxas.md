Hjalti "CCP Abraxas" Daníelsson's pen has come to rest, his very soul drained to paper to provide capsuleers a true glimpse into the darkest corners of EVE Online. ***EVE: The Burning Life***, the second novel set in New Eden (Tony Gonzales' *EVE: The Empyrean Age* novel was the first), is now available in Europe and will be in the US on March 30th. Read his [dev blog](//www.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&bid=747) to learn more about the novel.

*EVE:The Burning Life* is available in several formats in bookstores near you as well as [Amazon UK](//www.amazon.co.uk/Eve-Burning-Life-Hjalti-Danielsson/dp/0575090162/ref=sr_1_2?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1269349629&sr=8-2) and the [Macmillan store](//us.macmillan.com/evetheburninglife) in the US.
