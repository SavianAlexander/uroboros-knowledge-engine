A small number of changes were deployed to the EVE forums in an effort to improve the EVE community's interaction experience earlier today.

Based on feedback from EVE players, the link to report a forum post to the Community team has been shifted to the right, thus preventing accidental mis-clicks. Reporting a post for moderation review updates a page viewable only by the Community team.

Also added today is a check box in the top left of the forums, 'Display Images'. Leaving this option unselected will prevent the display of any images in forum posts, including emoticons and signature images. Dial-up users should appreciate the decreased forum load times, while any user who does not want to see forum signatures will now have this option.

More improvements will be made in the future. Please visit this [forum thread](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=684097) for further discussion and updates.
