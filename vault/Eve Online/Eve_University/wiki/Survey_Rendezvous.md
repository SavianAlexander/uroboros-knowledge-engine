# Survey Rendezvous

- Survey Rendezvous** is the starting mission of a 3 part mission chain, The other missions are **Listening Post** and **Kicking the Nest**.

  - Blitz:** Take the gate at warp-in. Hack the survey ship in the pocket. Grab the item and return to agent.

  1. # Warp-in
The acceleration gate is not locked. You can proceed to the next pocket without killing the drones.

|}

|}

  1. # Pocket
The survey ship(hackable container) contains the mission item. Hack it, grab the item and return to agent to complete the mission.

If you have low tank you can drag the rats away from the survey ship and then fly past them with MWD.
