# Swarm Nebula

- Swarm Nebula** is a gas exploration site containing gas used in the production of **Medical boosters**.

This is a null sec gas site found through Exploration.

Defenders:

None

Minable Gas Clouds:

5 Golden Cytoserocin (200 units in each the gas cloud)

Golden Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Standard Crash Booster Booster.

This site is found only in the[Tenal](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Tenal)region of space.
