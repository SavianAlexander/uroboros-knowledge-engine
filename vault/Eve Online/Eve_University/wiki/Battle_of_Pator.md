# Battle of Pator

- Battle of Pator** is the fourth mission of the AIR epic arc **Vision of Greatness**

  - Supplies: Battle of Pator** will be given after accepting the mission which includes (when opened):

- 1x Rifter
- 1x Data Analyzer I
- 1x 125mm Gatling AutoCannon I
- 1x 1MN Afterburner I
- 1x Target Painter I
- 2,000x EMP S

Tasks
- Hack 3x Imperial Defense Platform using the Data Analyzer
- Destroy 8x Imperial Fighters
- Target Paint the Governor Aeon to aid in its destruction
