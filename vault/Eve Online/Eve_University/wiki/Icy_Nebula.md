# Icy Nebula

Apart from being a gas site, **Icy Nebula** is a COSMOS site as well.

1. # Defenders
This site is guarded by Mercenaries and **Rogue Drone** rats.

|}

|}

1. # Gas Clouds
The site contains 2 Viridian Cytocerosin clouds of 3000 units, separated by a distance of 160 km, spotted with several rocks and structures.

1. # Location
This site is found in [Cloud Ring region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Cloud_Ring#const) in Null-sec space.
