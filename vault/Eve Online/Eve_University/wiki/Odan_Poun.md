# Odan Poun

- Odan Poun** is a level 3 mission agent in **Amarr COSMOS** located at Chanoun in the Governor's Audience Chamber complex. This agent offers five missions which include trade, courier, and combat components.

1. # Manel's Disappearance (1 of 5)

1. # Manel's Disappearance (2 of 5)

1. # Manel's Disappearance (3 of 5)

1. # Manel's Disappearance (4 of 5)

1. # Manel's Disappearance (5 of 5)

Odan will give you 6.6% Amarr Empire standings when the final mission in the five mission chain is completed.
