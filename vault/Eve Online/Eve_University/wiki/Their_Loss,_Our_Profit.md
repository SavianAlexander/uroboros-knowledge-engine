# Their Loss, Our Profit

<noinclude>  </noinclude>

Video: [[Typhoon](https://youtu.be/4hJBcIhi1Cw) January 2021], [[Jackdaw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0zcv6pbjqsY) July 2022]

Accept the Mission remotely.

Ungated semi-deadspace pocket one jump away in Obanen (or Liekuri?).

  - Blitz:** destroy the Hyasyoda Research Facility and pick up the S.I Formula Sheet.

Tips:
- You do not need to destroy any ship
- If you can't survive the incoming damage destroy a few Battleships, but make sure to leave one Core High Admiral alive
- Hyasyoda Research Facility has about 150 000 Hitpoints and 0% resistance
- You will lose 3% Serpentis Faction Standing when you destroy the Hyasyoda Research Facility
- You can freely warp inside the rooms
