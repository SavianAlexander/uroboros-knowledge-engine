We examine the [costs of war and the horrible decisions people must make](//www.eve-online.com/background/potw/05-08-08.asp) when confronted with the reality of collateral damage, physical and mental.

This is a new EVE Chronicle. They are short-stories written by CCP Abraxas, published every other Monday, and intended to examine the various aspects of life in New Eden. The entire list is contained [here](//www.eve-online.com/background/potw/archive.asp). These days we're also throwing in various pieces written by other developers, including CCP Gnauton and CCP Ginger, so be sure to check back on Mondays for new updates.
