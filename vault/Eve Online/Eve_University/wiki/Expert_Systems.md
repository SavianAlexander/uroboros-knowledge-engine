# Expert Systems

- Expert Systems** give a character access to a set of skills for a limited time. The purpose is to let someone try out an aspect of EVE before committing to it by training the skills normally. A character may have up to three Expert Systems active at one time. They are not lost upon death or jump cloning. Your character can train the skills given by the Expert System while it is active.

Various Expert Systems can be obtained from the **AIR Career Program**, bought from **New Eden Store** or redeemed from seasonal log in events. 

Expert Systems are not tradeable as they are consumed automatically on redemption.

1. # AIR Expert Systems
The AIR Expert Systems are handed out through the **AIR Career Program** for finishing their respective Career Agent mission chains:
- You get the Free AIR Enforcer Expert System for completing the **Enforcer Career Agent**.
- You get the Free AIR Explorer Expert System for completing the **Explorer Career Agent**.
- You get the Free AIR Industrialist Expert System for completing the **Producer Career Agent**.
- You get the Free AIR Soldier of Fortune Expert System for completing the **Soldier of Fortune Career Agent**.
