# Rimy Nebula

- Rimy Nebula** is a gas site found by scanning in the null security region of [Catch](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Catch).
1. # Walkthrough
This site has a couple of Lime Cytoserocin gas clouds containing 3000 units of gas in total. However, it is guarded by Mercenary and **Rogue Drone** NPCs.
