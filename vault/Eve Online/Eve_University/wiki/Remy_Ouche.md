# Remy Ouche

- Remy Ouche** is a level 2 COSMOS agent in **Minmatar COSMOS** at beacon "Sanctum Psychosis" in Lanngisi and requires 2.00 faction standing. (If you can't see them, enable overview elements "Beacon" and "Agents in Space")

This agent offers a mixture of courier and combat missions.
