# Guristas Troop Reinvigoration Camp

- Guristas Troop Reinvigoration Camp** is a DED rated site found through **exploration**. It can also be received as an escalation from the **Guristas Rally Point** and **Guristas Hidden Den** **combat anomalies**.

800 DPS T3 Cruiser is sufficient to clear the site in ~20-25 minutes.

1. # Walkthrough

  1. # 1st pocket

The acceleration gate is locked until all defenders are dead. All ships are ~15 km away and auto-aggro.

|}

  1. # 2nd pocket

The NPCs are spread out in 3 groups. The closest group (2 battleships, all frigates and 3-4 destroyers) will auto-aggro on landing, the other two groups will not attack unless approached within 25 km and will shoot drones. Combined damage output will be quite high if you aren't careful with pulling room aggro. The acceleration gate is locked until all defenders are dead.

|}

|}

  1. # 3rd pocket

Sentries have limited aggro range of ~20-25 km, you land 40 km away from them. Like the previous pocket, the NPCs are spread out in 3 groups and one group will auto-aggro on landing while the rest will shoot drones. The acceleration gate is locked until all defenders are dead.

|}

|}

  1. # 4th pocket

Total of 3 cruisers, which may or may not jam. No auto-aggro on warp-in. You land roughly 35-40 km away from the spawn.

The good stuff drops from Captive Fighting Arena.

|}

|}

1. # Loot
Clearing the entire site gives roughly 13 million ISK in bounties before risk modifiers.
