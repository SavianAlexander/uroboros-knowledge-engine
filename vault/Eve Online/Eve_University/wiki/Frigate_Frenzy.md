# Frigate Frenzy

- Frigate Frenzy** ran from 18 March until 22 March 2022, featuring an **Abyssal Proving Ground** and a short login campaign, coinciding with [Stay Frosty’s 8th annual Frigate Free-For-All](https://eveoganda.blogspot.com/2022/02/youre-invited-8th-annual-stay-frosty.html?m=1).

1. # 2v2 Frigate Frenzy Proving Grounds
Allowed ships per team were: **Punisher**, **Tormentor**, **Merlin**, **Kestrel**, **Incursus**, **Tristan**, **Rifter**, **Breacher**, **Executioner**, **Condor**, **Atron**, and **Slasher**.

Module choices for the event were limited to Meta 0 base modules only. Ships entering this Proving Ground could have a maximum of one local repair module fitted (shield or armor).

The following modules were banned:
- Sensor dampeners
- Shield rechargers
- Shield power relays
- Shield flux coils
- Core defence field purgers

The following arena effects were in place for the event:
- +25% Turret damage and tracking speed
- +50% Shield, Armor, and Hull HP

1. # Login Campaign
  - Frigate Frenzy** was a short login campaign that ran alongside the Frigate Frenzy **Abyssal Proving Grounds** event. The campaign rewards **Boosters**, and 5,000 Skill Points for Omega clones.
