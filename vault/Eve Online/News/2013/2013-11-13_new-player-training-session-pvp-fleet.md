On November 16th at 17:00 EVE Time, CCP will be running a New Player Training Session PVP fleet for rookies to learn the basics of PVP and fleet warfare in a live combat environment. These fleets will be FC’d by CCP Community developers with assistance from our ISD STAR team. If you are interested in joining one of these fleets, you may find further information in [this forum post](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=3871001#post3871001).
