1. # Summary
The Avalanche is a capital-sized hull like other freighters, but its Powergrid and CPU is slightly lower than the average T1 battleship, which means it cannot fit true capital-sized modules -- with the exception of Capital Flex Shield Hardeners for which it receives a 90% reduction in fitting requirements due to its Role Bonus. A 500MN Microwarpdrive is the largest prop module it can support, and battleship-sized weapon modules are the largest it can fit.  

A combination of good navigation skills, skill hard-wiring implants, agility modules, and/or an overheated 500MN MWD II, the Avalanche can achieve a 10-second align time to warp by pulsing the MWD.  

The Avalanche can also fit an impressive buffer tank and can also deal a great amount of missile damage due to its bonuses.

1. # Skills
The ship greatly benefits from perfect navigation skills (, , , ) as these make the 10 second align via a MWD able to be performed. A deficiency in these skills will require a higher commitment of resources (implant slots, low slots, etc) to meet the threshold. Low skills will make it impossible regardless of the modules you have access to. 

Unlike other freighters, skills in missiles and shield modules are needed to bring your survivability to its full potential. Shield skills have a more immediate impact, as Hardeners provide a massive EHP increase and are needed to take advantage of the Capital Flex Hardener. Better missile skills add DPS, but the *threat* of the missiles is often more effective than the missiles are in practice. Prioritize tank.  

1. # Tactics
A 10 second align time can be reliably achieved with a cold MWD II if the combined effects of skills/implants/agility modules can bring the base alignment time to sub 25 seconds. A mix of Mid-Grade Nomads (Slots 1-5), a Rogue EM-706 (Slot 7) and a single Inertia Stab II can meet this threshold. While this setup sacrifices some tank and/or warp speed, it dramatically reduces the time spent on gate for active pilots.  This reduces the opportunity to be cargo scanned and also forces any ganking squad to maintain tackle, which an Avalanche pilot has means of breaking via its missiles. This dramatically improves the safety of travel compared to other freighters in hi-sec. 

The enormous specialized hold is not reduced when using Bulkheads, which means that substantial buffer tanks can be fitted without sacrificing the primary benefit of the ship. These can be combined with a DCU, making for a very potent hull buffer tank. Mid slots should be fitted with Hardeners. The script in the Capital Flex Hardener can be loaded with different scripts, which can target holes in resists, or better prepare for known ganker fits. Active tanks are unlikely to outlast a dedicated ganking squad; your best bet is to aim for buffer to buy time for either a CONCORD response, or for your missiles to break tackle.  

Rapid Heavy Missile Launchers will deal better damage to enemy ships that don't move (many gankers stay still to maximize tracking), but will have a harder time applying to moving targets (particularly fast frigates that may be holding tackle). Use auto-targetting missiles to take advantage of the bonus AND to increase response time (they deal damage without a lock). 

1. # Notes
While the Avalanche appear combat ready compared to other freighters, it is still poorly suited for unsupported travel outside of High Sec. The tools to lock down a slow ship like a freighter are abundant in low sec, and the ability to call in support via cynos means that even a single pilot can be a major threat.
