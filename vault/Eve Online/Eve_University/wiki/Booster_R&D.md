1. # Third Location
[Dominix](https://youtu.be/FKKWrM7dzEs)

Ungated normal space pocket. For blitzing pick a asteroid/trash object near the shadow serpentis and warp to it. The trigger for escalation in case no commander is present is unknown.

There are damps. The elite cruisers and battlecruisers will approach to 6 and 20 km orbits. Once enemies get in range the incoming damage is quite high. Each group can be agressed separately. The reinforcements spawn in same locations where initial groups were.
