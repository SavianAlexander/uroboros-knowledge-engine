# Metaliminal Meteoroid

The objective of Metaliminal Meteoroid sites is to mine out the large central asteroid while being attacked by hostiles. The hostiles do not need to be killed to complete the site.

The asteroid contains 5,000 units (20,000) of one of five mutanite ore types. This ore can be sold to NPC buyers in the region for an additional 25 million ISK. Residue (such as on Miner IIs) will help complete the site quicker at the cost of some lost ore.

The composition of the enemy forces varies from site to site, see **Hostiles** below. No new enemy ships will spawn unless any are destroyed, in which case they will eventually be replaced by reinforcements. 

Some enemy ships will aggressively target drones. Mining drones are fragile!

{{Expansion past|Prior to the [Version 23.03 Release 2026-03-19.1](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/patch-notes-version-23-02) update, the asteroid contained 3,500 units or 14,000 of ore.}}

1. # Fleet size & composition
- Optimal fleet size: 5 ships
- Fleet composition: as many miners as possible with any necessary **logistics** and off-grid hauler support

1. # Type of ships allowed
This homefront operation takes place in a **deadspace pocket** with an acceleration gate that has ship restrictions.

- Tech 1 frigates, navy and pirate frigates
- Tech 1 destroyers, navy and pirate destroyers
- Tech 1 cruisers
- Tech 1 haulers, including **Squall**
- **Venture**, **Venture Consortium Issue**
- **Pioneer**, **Pioneer Consortium Issue**, **Perseverance**
- **Noctis**

1. # Hostiles
A fleet of about 15 NPC's consisting of frigates, destroyers, cruisers, and battlecruisers will be present upon arrival. They will concentrate their fire on one capsuleer ship at a time, beginning with the first vessel to enter the site, and may periodically switch targets as a group.

1. # Interaction & standings
Destroying any hostile ships encountered in Metaliminal Meteoroid Homefront operations does not result in any standing gains or losses. 

However, when flying these Homefronts in 0.8 and lower hi-sec security systems, additional **belt rat spawns** can be triggered. These NPCs will split their damage across your fleet, and destroying them will result in a loss of faction standing.

1. # Rewards

Rewards for Metaliminal Meteoroid sites come in two forms: (1) ISK payout upon site completion, and (2) one of five site-specific Mutanite ores that can be sold to NPC buy orders. ISK payout amount is less than other 5-pilot Homefront sites, giving the maximum payout per pilot of 12,000,000 ISK with a fleet of 5.

Every site contains 25,000,000 ISK worth of Mutanite ores, not counting sales tax, residue, and crits.
