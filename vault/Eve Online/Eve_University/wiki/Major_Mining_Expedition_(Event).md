# Major Mining Expedition (Event)

In a major mining expedition site, you need to mine 40,000 units of ore and deposit it in the friendly Orcas, while under attack from enemy frigates and cruisers.

1. # Site Details
- 40,000 units Ore required
- 6 Pilots max
- 0.2 Standing Gain
- Allowed Ships: **Corvettes**, **Frigates**, **Venture**, **Expedition Frigates**, **Destroyers**, **Cruisers**, **Mining Barges**, **Battlecruisers**, **Exhumers**
