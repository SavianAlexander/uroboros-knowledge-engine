As part of our ongoing support of the [Child's Play charity](//www.childsplaycharity.org/), CCP is offering one player and their guest the opportunity to win a Tour of the CCP Headquarters in Reykjavik, Iceland!

As part of the silent auction, fans arriving on or before November 5th 2008 have the chance to net themselves an exclusive view of the world of CCP through a unique tour of the office led by CEO Hilmar Pétursson. Explore the hub of all that is EVE while meeting the many multitalented team members in our Reykjavik's HQ. We're starting this one early so, until 24:00 on October 22, simply email [officetour@ccpgames.com](mailto:officetour@ccpgames.com) with your bid. Bidding starts at $1000 (USD). The tour will take place on November 5 2008, before everyone rushes out to have fun at Fanfest. You may bid as many times as you want, so if you want to make sure you'll win that golden ticket to the backstage of the galaxy, just raise your bid! Good luck!
