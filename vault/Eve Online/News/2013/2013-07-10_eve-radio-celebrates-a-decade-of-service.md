2013 is a year for celebration as many of our longest surviving ingame entities turn 10 years old.  Today the EVE Community celebrates a decade of bringing music to the masses from EVE Radio.

Initially founded as a fledgling fan radio station in March 2003, EVE Radio has moved from strength to strength in its 10 years of operation, with many high profile guest stars, CCP interviews and enough content to leave listener's heads spinning.

To celebrate 10 years of broadcasting, EVE Radio will be hosting a set of competitions this September and are inviting every member of the EVE Community to get involved.

You can check out what's up and coming at [www.eve-radio.com](//www.eve-radio.com/everadio10).
