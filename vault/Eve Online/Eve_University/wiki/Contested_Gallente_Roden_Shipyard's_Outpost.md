# Contested Gallente Roden Shipyard's Outpost

- Contested Gallente Roden Shipyard's Outpost** is a **Gallente COSMOS** Deadspace Site Beacon in the Fluekele system in the Algintal constellation.

  - Nilla Elermare** waits at the warp in.

Acceleration Gates limit ship size to Faction and T1 Cruisers and below. All Acceleration Gates are unlocked.

1. # The Shipyard

The gate is unlocked and you don't have to kill any ship.
