# Guardian's Gala (2021)

- Guardian's Gala** is a recurring event. In 2021 it ran from 11:00 February 11 to 11:00 February 23.

1. # Combat sites

Event combat anomalies spawn in all areas of space. The easier sites spawn in high/low/null security space while wormhole space spawns harder sites. The combat sites will appear as a warpable beacon on overview once someone has initiated warp to it.

The rats in the site use combination of target painters, sensor dampeners, stasis webifiers, energy neutralizers, warp disruptors and warp scramblers. The rats will also use warp bounces to get on top of their target making kiting practically impossible.

The rats orbit their target at 5-30 km range. The rats will also prioritize killing drones.

Incoming damage is omni with Serpentis ships dealing Th/Kin and Angel ships dealing Ex/Em.

All rats can drop valuables such as SKINs and agency drugs. The boss ship will also drop Overseer's Personal Effects.

  1. # Loot

- 'Bonding' cerebral accelerators
- Agency drugs
- Spirit SKINs
- 6th - 13th Tier Overseer's Personal Effects
- Angels Arisen and Cherubic Heartburst Fireworks
- Alcohol related commodities, exotic dancers
- Meta modules and T1 ammo suitable for Angel or Serpentis ship

  1. # Guardian's Gala
This site spawns in normal space.

- Five waves or rats.
- Each wave usually contains 1x battleship and 5-11 total rats. Sometimes there may be two battleships at once or no battleship in a wave.
- Fifth wave has the boss: Guardian's Gala VIP Emcee Vindicator or Guardian's Gala Emcee Machariel
- Killing the boss ends the site and the remaining rats warp away.

1. # Data sites

Event data sites spawn in normal and wormhole space. There are two types of event sites.

- Gala Coordination Center - shows up as a L3 data site on probe scan. System core has a strength of 70/10, site can be scanned down with a probe strength of 93. There's six cans in the site in total.
- Gala Network Support Center
- Gala VIP Coordination Center - Higher quality site. 6x hackable cans of the highest hacking difficulty.

Possible loot includes:

- 'Bonding' cerebral accelerators
- Agency drugs
- 6th - 17th Tier Overseer's Personal Effects
- Angels Arisen and Cherubic Heartburst Fireworks
- Alcohol related commodities, exotic dancers
- Spirit SKINs
- Normal data site loot (decyptors, datacores, materials, science and rigging skillbooks, blueprints, filaments)

This combat anomaly spawns in wormhole space. It consists of a single ungated normal space pocket.

1. # Login campaign

Ran from 10th December 2020 to 5th January 2021.

Capsuleers must have logged in for 7 out of the 12 days to collect all rewards. A total of 80k **Skill Points** were available to  accounts, while  accounts had access to an additional 250k SP (plus up to ~17k SP from the accelerator).
