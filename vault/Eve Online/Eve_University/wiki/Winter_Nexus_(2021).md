# Winter Nexus (2021)

- Winter Nexus** is an event held from 09 December 2021 to 4 January 2022 (downtime to downtime) and includes **Volatile Ice Storms** which feature Wightstorm Sites, **13 Days of Nexus Login Campaign**, the **Winter Nexus Agency Challenge**, the **Yoiul Chilling Spree Daily Campaign**, and **Yoiul Festival-themed** **Abyssal Proving Grounds**.

1. # Login campaign (13 Days of Nexus)
Capsuleers must log in for 13 out of the 27 days to collect all rewards. A total of 85k **Skill Points** are available to  accounts, while  accounts have access to an additional 425k.

1. # Volatile Ice Storm

Volatile Ice Storm is a **metaliminal storm** that moves between High, Low and Null Security systems. The center system and its neighbors experience the Strong effect, while systems 2-3 jumps out from the center get a Weak effect. Volatile Ice Storms can overlap. The sites spawned depend on the security status of the center system (e.g. a low security system in a highsec-centered ice storm will have highsec sites).

  1. # Ice Storm Filaments
Ice Storm Filaments transport capsuleers to a System affected by the Volatile Ice Storm.

There are separate Ice Storm Filaments for High, Low and Null security systems, capable of taking up to one, five, or ten ships.

  1. # Wightstorm Combat Anomalies
  1. ## Wightstorm Forward Base
These sites appear in Highsec within systems with the Volatile Ice Storm Effect. Similar to the event site from 2020, the site is doable with a well-fit **Battlecruiser** with application support.

  1. ## Wightstorm Transit Site
These sites appear in Nullsec within systems with the Volatile Ice Storm Effect. Similar to the event site from 2020, the site is doable with a well-fit **Battleship** with application support.

  1. ## Wightstorm Muster Point
These sites appear in Lowsec within systems with the Volatile Ice Storm Effect. Similar to the event site from 2020, the site is doable with a well-fit **Battleship** with application support.

  1. ## Wightstorm Anchorage
These sites are rare and appear in Low- and Nullsec within systems with the Volatile Ice Storm Effect.

  1. # Volatile Ice Fields
Volatile Ice Fields contain Ice that cannot be processed but can be traded in 7 at a time for a package of Salvage and Sansha items at the Site.

Different areas of space have different sites:
- High Security: Fading Volatile Ice Field
- Low Security: Fresh Volatile Ice Field
- Null Security: Friable Volatile Ice Field

Only the following ships can fit through the Acceleration Gate:
- Expedition Frigates
- Mining Barges
- Exhumers
- Industrial Ships
- Blockade Runners
- Deep Space Transports
- Porpoise

  1. # Wightstorm Data Sites
All Sites are:

- Level IV Data Sites. 86 scanner probe strength is able to scan them down but higher is recommended.
- only have one container that requires a Data Analyzer to unlock

Different areas of space have different sites:

- High Security: **Wightstorm Communication Relay** is the easiest
- Null Security **Wightstorm Cryptographic Array** is of intermediate difficulty
- Low Security: **Wightstorm Strategic Node** is the hardest

1. # Yoiul Chilling Spree (Daily Campaign)
During the event, you will earn 5,000 Skill Points a day for hitting other Capsuleers with Snowballs. During the Proving Grounds event, an additional 5k SP per day can be earned for entering the Proving Grounds.
