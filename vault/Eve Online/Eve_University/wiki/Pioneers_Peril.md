# Pioneers Peril

- Pioneers Peril** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **Angel Military Complex**.
1. # Walkthrough

  1. # First Location
You land at an acceleration gate.

|}
  1. ## First Pocket
Single enemy 140 km away in this pocket. Simple warp-in may trigger escalation.

|}

  1. # Second Location
No acceleration gate. Enemies are approx. 50 km away, though the Stasis Towers are 140 km off.

Destroy Angel Outpost station if no escalation is triggered from the Domination commander.

|}

|}

|}

|}

|}

  1. # Third Location
You land at an acceleration gate.

|}

|}
  1. ## First Pocket

|}

  1. # Fourth Location
Ungated non-deadspace pocket. No rats initially present.

|}

|}
Jorun 'Red Legs' Greaves deals significantly high Explosive and Kinetic damage at a fairly short range. Try to outpace her if you can. She has fairly high resists, the weakest being 66% Explosive resists, plus a strong shield tank. She has an MWD and will try to chase you at around 1700 m/s.

|}
Attack the Space Truckers Pit Stop to spawn an optional defensive wave. This is only worth doing for extra bounties/salvage.

|}

|}
