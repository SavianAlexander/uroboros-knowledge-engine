# Infiltrated Outposts

Video: [[Ishtar](http://youtu.be/YrASIikT2D0) July 2014], [[Golem](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5YmyvOAlTk) March 2013], [[Raven](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YlTtdYoTyMQ) December 2014], [[Machariel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xiR0UJoANCs) December 2014], [[Sentry Dominix](https://youtu.be/m3VMT74uz5E) July 2018], [[Rattlesnake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UpgpJDukhWw) August 2020], [[Gila](https://youtu.be/roD2_wqQ73Y) May 2021]

The number of ships in groups may vary.

  - Tip:**
- The drone bunkers in the 1st pocket are not part of the mission objective, so they can be ignored.
- Fitting an Afterburner might be useful to burn to the gate.

  - Blitz:**
- Eliminate all ships in the 1st pocket to unlock the warp gate.
- Eliminate all ships in the 2nd group and destroy the Bunker, done.
- You do not have to kill the third and furthest group in the second pocket.

  1. # 1st Pocket
Auto aggro from the 1st group, 2nd group remains neutral. The warp gate to the next pocket is ~33km away and locked until all hostiles are cleared.

If you get stuck colliding with the structures when you try to use the acceleration gate, just blow up the structure that is stopping the warp. The large structure has less HP than you may assume.

|}

  1. # 2nd Pocket
Destroy group 2 and the bunker next to them to complete the mission. The battleships on the side belong to group 2.

- Attacking the 2nd Group (or its bunker) will aggro the entire pocket.
- Attacking either the 1st or 3rd group will aggro the other group (3rd or 1st).
- Drones that get too close to any remaining neutral group will be attacked.

|}

  1. # Loot
Salvage: ± 1.7 Mil ISK

Bounty: ± 4.1 Mil ISK
