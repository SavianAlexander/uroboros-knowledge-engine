# Guristas Owned Station

- Guristas Owned Station** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **Minor Guristas Annex**.
1. # Walkthrough
The site has all four of its parts in low security space, and often leads up to 20 jumps away from the starting system.

  1. # First Location
Mixture of Gurista and mercenary rats.

|}
