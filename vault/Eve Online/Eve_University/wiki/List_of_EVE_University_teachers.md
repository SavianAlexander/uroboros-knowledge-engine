# List of EVE University teachers

Anyone in EVE University can teach a class, and those with Member status can schedule them in our **EVE University Calendar**. "Professors" are our most dedicated educators, recognized for their quality and consistency in teaching and mentoring UNI pilots by awarding the **EVE University Professor medal**(retired). "Teaching Officers" are members of the **Teaching Department** who work to support teaching with resources, advice, and administrative help.

If you are interested in teaching, please see **Teaching Classes at EVE University**.
