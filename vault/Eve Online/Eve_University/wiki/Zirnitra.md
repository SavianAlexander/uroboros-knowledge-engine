1. # Summary
The **Zirnitra** is the Triglavian **Dreadnought**, featuring damage and rate of fire bonuses to Ultratidal **Entropic Disintegrators** similar to other T1 disintegrator-bonused Triglavian ships.

1. # Skills
- to V. Unfortunately, there is no other variant of the ultratidal entropic disintegrator other than the tech 1 version. There is no tech 2, faction, or officer variant. So the only way to increase the damage of the Zirnitra is through more levels of Capital Precursor Weapon and ensuring that your gunnery support skills are all maxed out.

The Zirnitra's disintegrator has high tracking for a capital weapon but still struggles to apply to sub-capital-sized targets. Seeing as there is no HAW variant of the disintegrator, the only way to improve your application is through skills or tracking computers/enhancers.

With maximum skills and implants and a full damage ramp, the Zirnitra can out-dps any **titan** fit any way, including the faction titans. With Mimesis implants, RoF implants, and officer Damage Mods, Zirnitras can output 60k up to potentially 80k dps at full ramp.

1. # Notes
The ship's name (Zirnitra) is written in Triglavian () on the top starboard side of the ship in a slow pulsating red. Unlike the clade names on Triglavian subcapitals, this name is not in all caps. In Slavic mythology, the Zirnitra was a black dragon, worshiped as the god of sorcery.
