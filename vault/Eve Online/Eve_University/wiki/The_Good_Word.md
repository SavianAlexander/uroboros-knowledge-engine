The damage output is significant. Good tank recommended.
No faction standings penalty for destroying the enemies. No bounties for destroying ships.

  - Blitz:** Kill all enemies of wave 1 in the first pocket. Ignore NPCs in the second pocket, grab the preacher from the Preacher's Quarters (container), and warp out.

  1. # Pocket 1
The pocket starts off empty. After a few seconds, 8 enemy cruisers spawn, but two of them are scripted to blow up when the friendly cruisers arrive. The friendly cruisers simply stay in the pocket and do not engage the enemies.

About 20 seconds after out of warp.
