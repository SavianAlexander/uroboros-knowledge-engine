# Emergent Ruins

- Emergent Ruins** are escalations that have a small chance of occurring after completing a **detected relic site**.

1. # Information

Each site contains:
- 3 Proven Remnant hackable containers - Medium difficulty yellow core
- 4 Unfit Remnant hackable containers - Hard difficulty red core

A relic analyzer is required to hack both types of container.

There are no enemies.

1. # Loot
The containers have the potential to contain:
- Salvage loot
- Triglavian Survey Databases
- Atavums
