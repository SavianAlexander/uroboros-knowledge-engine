# Minor Mining Expedition (Event)

In a minor mining expedition site, you need to mine 20,000 units of ore and deposit it in the friendly Orcas, while under attack from enemy frigates and cruisers.

1. # Site Details
- 20,000 units Ore required
- 4 Pilots max
- 0.2 Standing Gain
- Allowed Ships: **Corvettes**, **Frigates**, **Venture**, **Expedition Frigates**
