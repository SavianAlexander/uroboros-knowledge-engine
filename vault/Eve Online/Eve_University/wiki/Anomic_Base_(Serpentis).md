# Anomic Base (Serpentis)

1. # Warp-in

|}

  1. # 1st Room

The main danger of this mission is the sheer distance you will be required to travel in order to kill the Burner Talos trio. Their railguns have poor tracking but 330km range and each deal 800 DPS Kinetic/Thermal. The sentries activate within 10km distance of your ship and each do 50 DPS EM damage out to 300 km. The most common method to complete this mission is using a brawling-fit Assault Frigate and relying completely on speed tanking to avoid the Talos' fire, since they cannot hold up to one or two lucky hits.
