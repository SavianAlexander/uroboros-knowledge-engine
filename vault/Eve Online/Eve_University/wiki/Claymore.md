1. # Summary
The **Claymore** is the Minmatar fleet command ship, designed as a missile boat. It has lower damage capability than the **Sleipnir**, but its extra mid and resist bonus allows for a better shield tank, making it the skirmish booster of choice for large shield fleets. The Claymore is also the fastest Command Ship, allowing it to more easily keep up with Heavy Assault Cruiser or tech 1 Battlecruiser fleets.
