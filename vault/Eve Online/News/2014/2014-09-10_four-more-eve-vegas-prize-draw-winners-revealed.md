We are happy to announce the name of the next four winner of the [EVE Vegas Player Prize Draw!](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/eve-online-news/eve-vegas-competition-2/)

All four of the following ticket holders have won a Complimentary Roundtrip Limousine Transfer!


- [Alekseyev Karrde](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Alekseyev%20Karrde) of [Noir.](//gate.eveonline.com/Corporation/Noir.)

The next prize to be drawn will be for a [Panorama Suite Upgrade](//www.planethollywoodresort.com/content/scaffold_pages/rooms/planet_hollywood/phv/en/ph_panorama_suite/_jcr_content/cards/card.roomhighlights.-_-etc-_-designs-_-cet-_-properties-_-planet_hollywood.phv.1.html). This draw will take place and the winner will be announced on Wednesday, September 17th, 2014.
