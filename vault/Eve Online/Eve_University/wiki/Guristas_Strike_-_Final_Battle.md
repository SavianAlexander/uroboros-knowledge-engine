# Guristas Strike - Final Battle

<noinclude>  
  - Guristas Strike - (The) Final Battle** is the 10th and final mission of the mission chain **Guristas Strike**. This is a "normal" **storyline mission** unlike the other missions in this chain e.g. it gives faction standing and the usual derived standing for friendly and hostile factions.
</noinclude>

Space Type: Deadspace with gates. No hostiles at initial gate.

  1. # 1st pocket
The warp-in point is in the middle between the two lines.

|}

  1. # 2nd pocket

|}

  1. # 3rd pocket
  - Blitz:** Destroy Groups 1, 2 and the Central Control Hub, warp out.

The Central Control Hub may drop a +3 implant.

Groups 3 and 4 only aggro when approached, attacked, or when Central Control Hub is destroyed.

If you plan on fighting the battleship groups, make sure your ship can tank around 200-300 incoming DPS.

Group 3 is more dangerous to tackle; the battleships have significantly longer range than the battleships in Group 4.

Despite the mission objective saying that the fleet will attack when firing on the Central Control Hub, groups 3 and 4 will not aggro until the Central Control Hub is destroyed.
