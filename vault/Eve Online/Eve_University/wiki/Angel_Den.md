# Angel Den

The Angel Den is a **combat anomaly** found in **Angel** infested regions. Easily done with a Faction Cruiser such as a **Gila**.

1. # Walkthrough
Single open normal space pocket.

There are initially 2 x Sentry Angel Heavy Missile Battery.  Another 2 will spawn in their place when they are destroyed. These deal 100% Explosive and have low resist to EM & Thermal
