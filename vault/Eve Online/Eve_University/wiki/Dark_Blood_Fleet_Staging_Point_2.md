# Dark Blood Fleet Staging Point 2

- Dark Blood Fleet Staging Point 2** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **Dark Blood Fleet Staging Point**.

1. # Walkthrough
As of 9-30-23 the site has been changed to deadspace. You cannot warp directly to objects in either room and will land around 30KM from rats in the second room.
The site is difficult and should be taken with extreme caution due to heavy neuting.
