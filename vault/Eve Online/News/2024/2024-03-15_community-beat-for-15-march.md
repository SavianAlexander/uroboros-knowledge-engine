Hello spacefriends! Welcome to the latest installment of the Community Beat. We will start this edition by congratulating [Eve University](https://www.eveuniversity.org/) with their 20th anniversary! Exactly 20 years ago today, Eve University was founded by [Morning Maniac](https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Morning_Maniac) and they are still going strong.


## Celebrating 20 years of Eve University


Obviously, Eve University is not going to let this anniversary pass by without notice. They are organizing a bunch of in-game activities that are open to all players. 


- **15 March - **Sightseeing tour and fireside chat with [Chribba](https://twitter.com/chribba). Visit the [Eve University Forum](https://forum.eveuniversity.org/viewtopic.php?f=104&t=128733) for more information.
- **16 - 17 March - **The EVE University 24h Teachathon. A marathon of public classes on a variety of EVE-related topics. All scheduled classes can be found [here](https://forum.eveuniversity.org/viewforum.php?f=72).
- **16 March - **Pirate Fleet with [Rixx Javix](https://twitter.com/RixxJavix). Keep an eye out on the Eve University Forum for more information.



## Curious capsuleers


For the curious explorers and roam enthusiasts, we recommend checking out [this post](https://www.reddit.com/r/Eve/comments/1bbz73j/selling_bookmarks_telling_a_story/) on the [EVE Online subreddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Eve). [Jet-Perches](https://www.reddit.com/user/Jet-Perches/) shares his stories from visiting all Low and Null-sec systems in New Eden! During his travels he also made gate perches that are now for [sale](https://forums.eveonline.com/t/service-jet-perches-bookmarks-null-and-low-sec-gate-perches/442504). 

We've also come across some capsuleers experimenting with different ships. First off, we have [Merdaneth Uber-Sarum](https://www.youtube.com/@merdanethubar-sarum9031), who after 18 years in EVE Online, is trying out the Rifter for the first time. 

If Rifters are not of interest to you, [Friendly Targets](@friendlytargets1522) is showing what a Thrasher Fleet Issue can do in Faction Warfare. 


## Just About


We'd like to give a shout-out to our partner Just About and give you an update on their bounty program that you can take part in! Capsuleers have been busy creating content, entering more than 950 submisssions so far, earning $5600 all together. 

Head over [Just About](https://justabout.com/eve-online) to see what sort of bounties are available!
