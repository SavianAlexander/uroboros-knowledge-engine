The bar has been raised and we look forward to what the coming weeks will bring in tandem with [Alliance Tournament VIII](//www.eveonline.com/events/alliances/tournament/t8) and the Planetary Interaction [land rush](//www.eveonline.com/news.asp?a=single&nid=3928&tid=1).
