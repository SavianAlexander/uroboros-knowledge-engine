# Gallentia Primacy

Galentia Primacy is a **Gallente** criminal faction that plays the part of the hostiles in some **Homefront operation**s.

Their ships are weak to Thermal and Kinetic, they deal Explosive (62.5%) and EM (37.5%) damage, and employ **energy neutralizers** and **ECM** electronic warfare.

They are the hostiles in these Homefront operations:
- **Dread Assault: Primacy Base**

1. # Ship Prefixes
The prefix of the ship names as they appear in the Overview correlate to their role. The rest of the ship name is typically the normal hull name of the ship. For example, the "Bastion Exequror" is an armor logistics **Exequror** cruiser. Prefixes often appear on ships of different sizes, for example the ships that target capsuleer drones ("drone killer" in the table below) come in both frigate and destroyer sizes.
