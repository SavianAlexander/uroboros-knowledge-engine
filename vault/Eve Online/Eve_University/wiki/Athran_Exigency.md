# Athran Exigency

- Athran Exigency** is a five part mission chain. Involving combat with the **Minmatar Republic**.

1. # Part 1 of 5

  1. # Warp-in
- Single pocket without gate.
- Approach the mission objective (Abandoned Mining Station). Make it visible in the overview by enabling 'Types - Celestial - Large Collidable Object'.
- The Station is 40km away from warp-in. The mission is flagged as complete when within 5km of the station.

|}

1. # Part 2 of 5

  1. # First pocket

|}

  1. # Second pocket
- A Battleship, the Republic Tribal Ymir, is exploding when you arrive in the second pocket, leaving behind no wreckage.
- Group 2 appears after the explosion of the above mentioned battleship.

|}

1. # Part 3 of 5

  - Blitz:**  Destroy the Amarr Starbase Control Tower, leaving the ships alone.

  1. # Single pocket
- The mission objective is the Amarr Starbase Control Tower. Show it on your overview with "Entity - Large Collidable Structure".
- Despite the wording of the mission briefing, you do not need to destroy the Stationary Bestower to complete the mission.

|}

|}

1. # Part 4 of 5

  1. # First pocket
  - Blitz:** Go directly to the acceleration gate. Destruction of the "Repair Crew" is not necessary.

|}

On activating the acceleration gate the following message appears:
"Something just isn't right here. Several Minmatar ships circle the outpost, and your scanners clearly indicate artillery being fired off in the vicinity. Best be careful here."

  1. # Second pocket
- Five 'Minmatar kamikaze pilot' frigates destroy themselves upon warp-in to the second pocket.
- Group 1 spawns after the explosion of the above mentioned kamikaze frigates explode

|}

|}

1. # Part 5 of 5

  1. # Warp-in
  - Tips:**
- Part 5 of 5 is a good mission to mine with large amounts of Veldspar and Scordite very near warp-in point.
- Several Athran Proximity Mines are scattered throughout the asteroids. Be careful as they do explode and do a decent amount of damage.

  - Structures:**
