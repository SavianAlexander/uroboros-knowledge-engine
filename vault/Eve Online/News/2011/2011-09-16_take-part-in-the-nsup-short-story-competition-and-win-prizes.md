We are glad to announce that the second NSUP short story competition has begun. Until the end of September you can submit your EVE-related short stories and have a chance to win a developer-signed copy of the EVE Online: Commissioned Officer Edition and other great prizes!

To get more information on the contest and to submit your entries, please visit [this forum thread](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=6423).
