# Sansha Forsaken Sanctum

- Forsaken Sansha Sanctum** is a **combat anomaly** found in Sansha infested null security regions.

1. # Site
Consists of one ungated pocket. Warping in from certain directions can cause you to decloak; even at 100km.

Centus Doom Lord will deal Kinetic damage in addition to the normal EM/Thermal.
