On the heels of Oveur's recent (and epic) [blog post](//myeve.eve-online.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&bid=603), we're pleased to announce that **Quantum Rise**, the 9th free expansion for EVE Online,  will be coming your way on Nov. 11th. The press release is available [here](//www.eveonline.com/pressreleases/default.asp?pressReleaseID=49).

You can learn more about **EVE Online: Quantum Rise** by visiting the [feature page](//www.eveonline.com/features/quantumrise/).

Feel free to discuss our newest expansion in our forums [here](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=914831).
