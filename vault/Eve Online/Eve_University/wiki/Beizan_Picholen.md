# Beizan Picholen

- Beizan Picholen** is a multi part level 1 mission for **Gallente Federation**.

The mission can be started in any of the three Gallente Federation **data centers**.

The mission chain will reward the player with one random +3% hardwiring implant and Gallente Federation faction standings.

The mission chain can be completed only once. Failed mission can not be attempted again. But the missions themselves are fairly easy - as long as you pay attention and don't let the offered or accepted missions expire, they can be done without too much of a hassle. The missions are easy enough for a brand new player in their first frigate to complete them without problems.

The mission chain is supposed to continue after Beizan Picholen (6 of 6), but the next agent "Boismevier Mals" is not active in the game.
