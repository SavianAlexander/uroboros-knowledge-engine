  - Torpid Sleeper Hive** is a **Pochven** combat anomaly that spawns through out Triglavian space.

This site is very difficult to clear and should only be attempted by a very well-tanked ship or a small fleet with logi support.  Frequently a gang of **Crucifier**s with tracking disruptors along with several sniper battleships are used to run the site.

Sleepers deal very high damage from long range.  They primarily use long-range energy neutralizers, webs that reach out to 10 km, and warp scramblers that shut down MWD/MJDs.  **Their individual damage is approximate to that of C5-C6 Sleepers, beware.**

The Sleeper rats are primarily categorized into three distinct groups; Faded Hypnosian (weaker), Hypnosian (normal), and Aroused Hypnosian (stronger).  The stronger Sleepers have more effective EWAR and deal considerably higher damage, in addition to extra armor and structure HP.  Aroused-type cruisers and battleships also have an additional shield layer, though it is very weak and barely accounts for their tank.

Sleeper frigates deal around ***100*** DPS, Sleeper cruisers deal around ***200-300*** DPS, and Sleeper battleships deal around ***450*** DPS.

The site may have trigger NPCs, the second wave may be triggered early if the cruisers are destroyed.  This has not been confirmed.

Hypnosian ***Agonizer*** frigates use moderately strong webs and deal primarily turret damage, ***Watcher*** uses warp scramblers, webs and weak neuts and spreads damage out between turrets and missiles, and ***Preserver*** uses remote repairs and uses strong missile attacks.

Hypnosian ***Avenger*** cruisers use short-ranged neutralizers and deal strong turret damage, ***Upholder*** remote repairs other ships and fires high-damage missiles, and ***Warden*** uses combination of warp scrambling, webs and neutralizers with equal turret/missile damage.

Hypnosian ***Eristikon*** battleships use long-range energy neutralizers with high turret damage, ***Psychopomp*** remote repairs other ships and deal strong cruise missile damage, and ***Sentinel*** uses warp scramblers, long-range webs and neutralizers with damage spread out between turrets and missiles.

Drifter cruisers or battleships may spawn as part of the second and final waves.  Scout cruisers deal around 500 DPS while Raider battleships deal around 1000.  All Drifters warp scramble, web and neut.  DO NOT TAKE DRIFTERS LIGHTLY, THEY CAN EASILY KILL BATTLESHIPS WITHIN A FEW SHOTS.

  - Note:** If a Drifter spawns, it is highly recommended to bring ships with very strong tank and logi support.  There is no safe engagement range as they can hit you out to 450 km.

Initial wave deals around 200-400 DPS.

1. # Rewards
Clearing the site increases your standings with **EDENCOM** and **Triglavian Collective**.  It also gives a scaling payout that peaks at 27M ISK per player.

A hackable container will also spawn which can be hacked to retrieve Stability Telemetry loot items that can be sold to CONCORD/DED buy orders for 300,000 ISK/ea.
