# Tracking or Scanning

<noinclude>
</noinclude>

This is the first mission in the arc that gives you a choice in where you want to pursue the story.

This choice will only affect your next three missions and will not change the final reward.

"Accept option" auto completes Tracking or Scanning and starts next mission.

<noinclude>You can choose either **Tracking the Queen (Part 1)** or **Bag of Blood**

The Tracking the Queen 3 parts supports Gallente, Bag of Blood supports Caldari, neither changes your standings immediately.

</noinclude>
