The Quarter Finals are complete and only four teams remain in the tournament.

Although most contestants have relied on familiar ships and tactics to make it through the Quarters, Pandemic Legion surprised everyone by bringing out a heavily tanked "bait" Curse. By drawing fire to the Curse, Pandemic Legion was able to focus on taking down the Cult of War's Slepnirs and seizing the win.

Semi Finals will resume shortly, in which **R.U.R.** will face **G00DFELLAS** and **Ev0ke** will face **Pandemic Legion**.
