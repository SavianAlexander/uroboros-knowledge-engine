# Caldari Prime Breakout

- Caldari Prime Breakout** is the second mission of the AIR epic arc **Vision of Greatness**

  - Supplies: Caldari Prime Breakout** will be given after accepting the mission which includes (when opened):

- 1x Merlin
- 1x Multispectral ECM I
- 1x 75mm Gatling Rail I
- 1x 1MN Afterburner I
- 1x Small Remote Shield Booster I
- 2,000x Antimatter Charge S

Pocket 1

- ECM on NPCs to assist the 5x Megacorp Transport Vessels escaping

Pocket 2

- Remote Shield Boost the Megacorp Transport Vessels
- Destroy 10 hostile NPC when fired upon
