1. # Summary
The **Tholos** is a pirate faction **Destroyer** from the **Deathless Circle**. The Tholos has six mid slots, the most of any destroyer. Paired with a shield booster bonus, the Tholos offers strong active tanking potential for its class, while a faction-unique stasis webification resistance helps it get close into brawls to take advantage of its bonused rockets, bonused projectiles, and breacher pod launching capabilities. To top it all off, support for a covert ops cloaking device affords it tactical positioning opportunities unavailable to any other destroyer, opening up the potential to set up ambushes with more firepower than an **Astero**, but with a smaller hull than a **Strategic Cruiser**.

Blueprint copies for the Tholos can be acquired in **Zarzakh**.
