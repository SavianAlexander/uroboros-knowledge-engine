# Angel Strike

- Angel Strike** is a multi-part Level 3 mission with multiple faction variants. The first four parts are always the same, but parts five to ten are different for each faction.

- **Angel Strike (Blood Raiders)**
- **Angel Strike (Guristas Pirates)**
- **Angel Strike (Sansha's Nation)**
