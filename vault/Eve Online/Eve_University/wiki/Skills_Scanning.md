# Skills:Scanning

# Overview
This group contains all skills tied to scanning: probe scanning, the hacking mini-game, and survey skills. Most of the skills in this group are relevant to **exploration**.

| Skill (Multiplier) | Purpose | Alpha Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

{{Skill
|skill=Astrometric Acquisition
|desc=Skill at the advanced operation of long range scanners. 5% reduction in scan probe scan time per level.
|1=Intelligence
|2=Memory
|pre=
|reqII=Scan Acquisition Array I
|reqV=Scan Acquisition Array II
|notes=Currently the least useful of the three Astrometric skills used for exploration, and a holdover from previous versions of scanning.  Scan times are already very short, so you may not want to train this skill at all past what is required by other skills or items. It can be more important for combat probing, when every split second reduction in the time that your probes might be visible on a target's directional scanner counts.
}}
