1. # Summary
The Velator is the Gallente **Corvette**. This ship is given free of charge to Gallente players who click on the "Board my Corvette" button while docked in a station. It has no blueprint and can not be manufactured or reprocessed.

As with all Corvettes, the Velator is a cheap and disposable hull, but is outclassed in every regard by almost any other player-piloted ship. Gallente pilots wishing to advance into different hulls may want to upgrade to one of the following depending on their preferred piloting style:
- Pilots focusing primarily on drones may find the  to be an effective replacement.
- For pilots more interested in hybrid turrets, the  is a good choice.
- For pilots looking to work with a fleet in a **tackle** role, the  will likely be the next step.
- For pilots aspiring to be proficient at **exploration**, the  exploration frigate is an excellent tool for the job.
- For pilots interested in supporting fleets by using **Sensor Dampeners** on enemy ships, the  is a useful ship.
- Last but not least, the  offers powerful remote repair capabilities to those who prefer to **aid their allies** instead of dealing damage themselves.

1. # Skills
The Velator is meant to be flown right from character creation - as such no real skills are "required" to pilot it, and by the time you've trained some early skills, you'll likely have acquired several better ships through the **career agents**.

1. # Tactics
The Velator has a quiet, rarely-noticed advantage over the other corvettes: it has double the drone capacity and drone bandwidth. This gives it a possible advantage in the extremely niche situation of pre-agreed corvette battles. It can field two light scout drones, or one medium drone.
