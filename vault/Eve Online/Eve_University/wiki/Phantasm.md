1. # Summary
The **Phantasm** is the Sansha pirate cruiser. Like the **Succubus** and **Nightmare**, the Phantasm has a large damage bonus to lasers and has an abundance of midslots to fit a shield tank. Unlike the Succubus, you can actually *do* something about the cap issues you would normally run into fitting both lasers and shields. The utility high allows for equipping a Nosferatu or Energy Neutralizer module.
