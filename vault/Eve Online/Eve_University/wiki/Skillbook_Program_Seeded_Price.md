This page lists all available skillbooks and their NPC prices. See the **Skillbook Program** for rules and instructions on obtaining reimbursement.

Skillbooks over 3,500,000 ISK cannot be refunded. If applicable, the LP cost is additional to the ISK cost. Skillbooks marked as N/A are not available in LP stores and are not seeded by NPC vendors.

Skillbooks marked in red in the table below aren't reimbursable even if found in market for less than 3,500,000 ISK.
