# Location Location (Gallente Federation)

Ruyman2, the original author, at [EVE-Survival](https://eve-survival.org/wikka.php?wakka=LocationLocation5ga)

1. # Part 1 of 5

Space type: Deadspace with Gate

Acceleration Gate into Deadspace

  1. # Single pocket
  - BLITZ:**
- Destroy the frigates
- Destroy the DAMPENING cruisers
- Destroy the Stasis Tower
- Destroy the LEFT Cruise Missile Battery, then the triggered spawns one after the other until objective is completed

  - Tips:**
- Use a Passive Tank or plan a capacitor that receives an additional 120 units/s
- Maximum damage dealing of initial group is about 2000 hp/s (TH 60%, KIN 40%)
- Do not shoot Missile Batteries first (unless you can tank over 4000 hp/s...)
- Mission totals 6 Frigates, 3 Cruisers, 16 Battlecruisers and 33 Battleships
- Mission flagged complete when all "Battery 2 (Left)" spawns are destroyed

  - Initial group**
Contact’s ship is present at Warp-in at 15km away. But 20 seconds later you are notified in Local that, Oh sh--, “It’s a Trap!”
The Contact's ship vanishes and instead a Gallente Fleet uncloaks near the same spot. Ships are 25-40km to the right of Warp-In
