## Overview
EVE Guardian, one of EVE's most prominent player run news sites has secured an interview with Amarr's newly crowned emperor, Doriam Kor-Azor. Doriam talks about his plans for the future, the power vacum in the Sarum family and is views on slavery. You can read it here.
