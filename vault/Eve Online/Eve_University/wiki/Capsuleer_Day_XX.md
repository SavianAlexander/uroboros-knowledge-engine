# Capsuleer Day XX

- Capsuleer Day XX** was a limited time event celebrating EVE Online's 20th birthday that ran from 2nd May to 30th May 2023, **downtime** to downtime. Starting from the 2nd of May  when taking part in the agency campaign a capsuleer could choose between supporting the Society of Conscious Thought(SoCT) or the Guri Malakim faction, with each featuring their own sites and rewards.

1. # Login Campaign
Capsuleers must have logged in for 20 out of the 29 days to collect all rewards. The campaign rewards **SKIN**s, **Boosters** and 85.000 **Skill Points** for  with 425.000 additional Skill Points, 12,000 **EverMark**s, and ships for .

1. # Agency Campaign
The Agency Campaign for this event was split between running sites for the Society of Conscious Thought or the Guri Malakim pirate cooperative. Capsuleers could choose which faction they wish to support for the event. Both factions reward the same Skins, but different combat boosters. It is possible to run both faction's sites, though the ones of the allied faction won't award points for completing challenges.

All challenges were repeatable.

  1. # Rewards
Rewards for completing the Capsuleer Day event challenges for the corresponding factions. Both factions award the same SKINs, only the types of boosters are different.
- SoCT give the following boosters with different strengths:
  - Scan Booster: increased probe strength and probe resolution
  - Armor Booster: increased armor resistance
  - Agility Booster: increased warp speed, better Inertia

- Guri Malakim pirate cooperative give the following boosters with different strengths:
  - Observability Booster: increased Sensor strength, reduced signature
  - Shield Booster: Shield boost bonus
  - Turret Booster: increased turret damage

1. # Combat sites
The combat sites were limited to ships of Battlecruiser size or smaller.
- **Guri Malakim Survey Base** in High-sec.
- Guri Malakim Raider Base in Low-sec, Nullsec, Pochven and Wormhole space.
- **SoCT Survey Expedition** in High-sec.
- SoCT Retrieval Expedition in Low-sec, Nullsec, Pochven and Wormhole space.

1. # Hacking sites
Guri Malakim sites droped the event loot Smuggler Lockboxes, which could be sold on the market to NPCs for 1,000,000 ISK a piece.

- Guri Malakim Survey Expedition in High-sec, contains 3x Guri Malakim Survey Data Vault.
- Guri Malakim Raiding Expedition in Low-sec, Nullsec, Pochven and Wormhole space.
- SoCT Survey Outpost in High-sec, contains 3x SoCT Survey Data Relay Tower.
- SoCT Retrievals Repository in Low-sec, Nullsec, Pochven and Wormhole space.

1. # Game mechanics
- The loot drop rate for all player ships and POS's has been increased from 50% to 75%.
- The Market Sales Tax has been lowered from 8% to 4% for 4 days, to celebrate Capsuleer Day! From downtime 5 May 2023 till downtime 9 may 2023.
