# Associate Director Community Engagement

1. # Role Overview
The Associate Director of Community Engagement is responsible for enhancing PvP content and participation across EVE University. This includes developing structured PvP opportunities, mentoring members, and fostering a vibrant PvP culture in lowsec, nullsec, Thera, and beyond.

1. # Responsibilities

  1. # PvP Content & Community Enablement
- Promote accessible and engaging PvP opportunities across the University.
- Develop initiatives to broaden PvP participation.

  1. # SIG Leadership & Mentorship
- Oversee the creation and development of PvP-focused SIGs.
- Mentor SIG leaders to foster thriving PvP communities.

  1. # Event Coordination & Collaboration
- Coordinate with the Events team to deliver PvP-oriented content.
- Ensure events align with educational and gameplay goals.

  1. # Newbro Onboarding & Integration
- Help new players access and enjoy PvP content.
- Reduce barriers to participation and support skill development.

  1. # Community Feedback & Engagement
- Gather feedback to refine PvP offerings.
- Represent PvP community interests in decision-making.

  1. # Cross-Department Coordination
- Collaborate with Combat and FCTM leadership.
- Align PvP initiatives with the University’s strategic direction.

  1. # Promotion of PvP Culture
- Reinforce PvP as a core element of the University's identity.
- Encourage engagement from all experience levels.

1. # Key Qualities
- Strong leadership and mentoring capabilities.
- Excellent communication and PvP knowledge.
- Creative and proactive in building content.
- Collaborative and strategic mindset.
