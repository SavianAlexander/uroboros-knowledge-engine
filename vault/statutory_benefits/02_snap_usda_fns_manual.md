# USDA SNAP Statutory Policy & Deductions Manual (7 CFR 273)

## 1. Statutory Income Tests
Under **7 CFR § 273.9**, households must satisfy two income tests (unless categorically eligible or containing an elderly/disabled member):
1. **Gross Income Test**: Total gross monthly income $\le 130\%$ of the Federal Poverty Level.
2. **Net Income Test**: Net countable income after allowable deductions $\le 100\%$ of the Federal Poverty Level.

---

## 2. Statutory Deduction Structure (2026)

### 2.1 Standard Deduction
- **1–3 Person Household**: **$198.00 / month**
- **4–5 Person Household**: **$208.00 / month**
- **6+ Person Household**: **$246.00 / month**

### 2.2 Earned Income Disregard
- **20% deduction** applied to all gross earned wages/salary (incentivizes employment).

### 2.3 Excess Shelter Deduction
- **Shelter Costs**: Actual rent/mortgage + Standard Utility Allowance (SUA: **$150.00**).
- **Excess Calculation**: $\text{Excess} = \text{Total Shelter} - (0.50 \times \text{Adjusted Income})$.
- **Cap**: Capped at **$672.00 / month** (uncapped for elderly/disabled households).
