1. # Summary
Sukuuvestaa Heron is a special edition of the **Heron** given out during the 2012 Christmas season similar to the **Sarum Magnate**, **Vherokior Probe**, and the **Inner Zone Shipping Imicus**. It appears to be functionally equivalent to the standard Heron but with a different skin and additional flavor text.
