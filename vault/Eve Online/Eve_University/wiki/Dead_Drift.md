# Dead Drift

- Blitz: Warp in, fly to Armageddon (Oberon), pick up Scientists and place them in Splendor. Warp out.**

  1. # Single Pocket
- Abandoned Armageddon 20km from warp-in, next to a wormhole.
- Group 1 is triggered by approaching the Armageddon.
- The mission is flagged as complete as soon as the Scientists from the cargo container are picked up and placed in the Splendor.
