# Signal Source

- Signal Source** is an limited time **Expedition** that can escalate from **EDENCOM Covert Research Headquarters**. Warping to the site location makes it public, visible as a celestial beacon in overview that anyone can warp to.
1. # NPCs and site overview
Triglavian rats are exclusively featured in this site. High Explosive/Thermal resists are strongly recommended. 

Rats generally drop usual Triglavian loot that drops from invasion rats.

Around 6-7 rats are present in each wave.

The boss battleship (Voivode Hospodar Leshak) has extremely strong neuting and warp scrambling. It also boasts a strong armor tank that requires quite a bit of DPS to break. Additionally it deals very high Explosive and Thermal damage and is very capable of destroying even a well-tanked T3 Cruiser.

- [Example T3 Cruiser kill by the boss](https://zkillboard.com/kill/85009073/)

EWAR is highly varied in this site, although there is usually at least one warp scrambling rat.  The site itself is ungated and ship size limits are based on system security.

1. # Walkthrough
You land at 0 on rats near a spatial rift like the ones seen in Triglavian invasions. 

Each wave spawns a few seconds after the previous one is destroyed.
