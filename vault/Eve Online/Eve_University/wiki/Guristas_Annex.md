# Guristas Annex

- Guristas Annex** is an **Unrated Complex** that can be found in Low Security systems with Gurista presence through **exploration**. **Battleships** and their T2 counterparts can enter the site.
1. # Walkthrough
This site is similar to **Guristas Vigil**. Warping to the site will land you on an acceleration gate.
