As the [EVE Fanfest 2007](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2007/), 1-3 November, comes closer we've updated the Fanfest section with some additional information on what's going to happen.

[2007 Fanfest PvP Tournament](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2007/pvp.asp) - As usual we will have a Tournament, the details and rules for this is at the linked page.

[EVE CCG at Fanfest 2007](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2007/ccg.asp) - This year we will have demonstrations of the CCG, card giveaways for those who wish ot learn it and of course we will have a tournament.

[Jita - Alliance and Corporation area for Fanfest](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2007/jita.asp) - New for this year is a special corporations and alliances area, where you'll be able to meet friends and foes, relax from the Fanfest activities and maybe get access to some special services and perks. More on this and how to register can be found on the page linked above.

[RoXor](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2007/roxor.asp) - The in house Dev band has entertained us at the Fanfest for a while, and we felt they deserved some extra attention on our site. We will also be selling their t-shirt in the Fanfest EVE Store.

So, for those of you who have not yet ordered, make sure you get a [Fanfest package](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2007/packages.asp), an [EVE FanFest 2007 Access Pass](//secure.eve-online.com/fanfest2007/) and join us in Iceland for the Fanfest!
