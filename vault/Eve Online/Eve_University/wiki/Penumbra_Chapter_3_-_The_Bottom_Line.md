# Penumbra/Chapter 3 - The Bottom Line

- The Bottom Line** is the third and last chapter of **Penumbra**.

If you picked:
- Hyasyoda's **An Honorable Betrayal** you'll start the Chapter with **The Knowledge to Act**
- Nugoeihuvi's **Playing It Safer** or **Two Steps Into Hell** then you'll start the Chapter with **A Difference of Opinion**

It would be helpful to have:
- a **Blockade Runner** if you pick the Caldari path, rather then Hyasyoda or Nugoeihuvi

1. # Mission 15a - The Knowledge to Act (Story Arc Branch) - (Hyasyoda path)

1. # Mission 16a - Slipping Away - (Hyasyoda path)

1. # Mission 17a - Across the Line - (Hyasyoda path)

1. # Mission 15b - A Difference of Opinion (Story Arc Branch) - (Nugoehuvi path)

1. # Mission 16b - Learning by Doing - (Nugoehuvi path)

1. # Mission 17b - The Price of Silence - (Nugoehuvi path)

1. # Mission 16c - Home In Peace - (Caldari State path)
