# Guristas Military Complex

1. # 1st Pocket
3 NPC groups, with two more spawns upon attacking and killing the control tower. Without diamond tag, blitz is still possible, but not recommended, incoming damage becomes *very* high and the frigates point.

It seems you can skip the room and activate the gate with a Guristas diamond tag in your cargo.
Destroying the EM forcefield will destroy your drones.
