## Overview
Revenue sharing for your favorite content creators has been enabled.

---

### Supportive capsuleers,


New Eden is home to a multitude of creative, innovative, and helpful pilots who share their adventures, tips, tricks, tools, and other escapades with their fellow travelers through various types of content. These talented creators are an invaluable part of the EVE community, and the [Partnership Program](https://www.eveonline.com/partners) is a means for them to prosper and bring even more quality information and entertainment to the cluster. 

The latest addition to the Partnership Program allows you to toss a coin to your favorite content creator to support their work. In partnership with [WeHype](https://www.wehype.com), EVE Online now allows you to identify an EVE Partner to receive a 5% revenue share of any [EVE Store](https://store.eveonline.com) purchase whenever you enter their creator code. Ask your friendly neighborhood EVE Partner for their code and start showing your love! Supporting passionate EVE players and content creators is an ongoing goal for CCP Games, and this allows us to compensate for community-created content, channeling the passion and creativity that lives in the community into a viable source of income. 


> *"At CCP Games, we've always believed in the power of our community to drive the evolution of EVE Online. The passion and creativity that lives within our player base is unmatched, and it's our responsibility to support that energy in meaningful ways. With the introduction of initiatives like WeHype, we're taking significant steps to channel this incredible community-driven content into viable sources of income for our players. This initiative marks an important move toward empowering our creators, ensuring that they can continue to contribute to the rich tapestry of New Eden while also benefiting from their efforts." **-Eyrún Jónsdóttir, VP of Publishing *



## STUNNING NEW T’AMBER SKINS


Members of the Partnership Program have given away a wealth of stunning, exclusive SKINs to their followers since the conception of the program from the Scope Syndication SKIN line. That has now been retired, making way for a brand new batch of designs by T’amber, called the Media Miasma SKIN line. This is designed by the community, for the community, so make sure to tune into your favorite content creator and keep your eyes peeled for these exclusive giveaways.  

Supporting your favorite EVE Partners, allows you to actively contribute to the vibrant ecosystem of EVE Online, ensuring that the creators who make New Eden a richer and more engaging universe are rewarded for their efforts. Choose to support your favorite content creator today, and let’s continue to build a thriving New Eden together.
