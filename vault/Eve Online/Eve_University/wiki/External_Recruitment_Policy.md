# External Recruitment Policy

- EVE University** is a teaching corporation, and we encourage our members to move on to other corporations once they have learned what they need to in order to play the game as they wish. As such, we welcome other corporations recruiting from our member base. However, we have some set criteria for corporations who wish to recruit from EVE University.

We do not allow recruitment from corporations in our chat channels or in Mumble. The only approved place for corporation advertisements is our *#work-fair* Discord channel. You do not need to be an alumni to access this channel, all you need is an EVE University Discord account, which you can do by logging into [EVE University Auth](https://auth.eveuniversity.org) using CCP's "single sign on" (SSO). Go to Services and activate **Discord**.
If you are interested in helping EVE University we would encourage you to register as a **Guest Lecturer** or as a **Guest FC**.
