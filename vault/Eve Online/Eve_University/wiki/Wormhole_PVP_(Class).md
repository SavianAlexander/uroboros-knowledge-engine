1. # Class Overview
Wormhole PVP introduces the basics of PVP in Wormholes. It will give an overview of what PVP in Wormhole space can look like, from Solo PVP to Fleet PVP.

This class covers the following topics:

- Why PVP in Wormholes?
- Anatomy of a Wormhole fight
- Wormhole Effects
- Doctrines and Ship Choices
- Wormhole Bushido
- Q&A
