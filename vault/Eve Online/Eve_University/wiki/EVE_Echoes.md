# EVE Echoes

[EVE Echoes](https://www.eveechoes.com/) is a next-gen mobile spaceship MMO game developed by [NetEase Games](https://www.neteasegames.com/) and based upon hallmark EVE Online design principles. In EVE Echoes, players are able to participate in a massive space sandbox environment mirroring EVE Online, while forming alliances with other pilots from across the galaxy to shape the game’s persistent universe.

1. # EVE University and EVE Echoes

EVE Echoes is a stand alone game with no direct interaction with the main EVE Online game. **EVE University** and the **Ivy League Alliance** remain focused on the education of new and existing capsuleers in EVE Online only and do not have a presence in EVE Echoes.
