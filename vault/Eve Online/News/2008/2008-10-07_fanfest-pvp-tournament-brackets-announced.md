Attention Fanfest attendees with a keen interest in this year's PvP Tournament! The draw has been completed, the brackets (mostly) filled, and the schedule has been [posted](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2008/brackets.asp). You can even watch an awesome video of Mindstar and Claw as they draw the names.

If you're attending Fanfest and have yet to sign up for our tournament, fret not: we have eight slots available and you will be able to sign up for the tournament that Thursday.

You can check out our rules [here](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2008/pvprules.asp), and if you have any questions be sure to check out the official thread [here](../ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=858247).
