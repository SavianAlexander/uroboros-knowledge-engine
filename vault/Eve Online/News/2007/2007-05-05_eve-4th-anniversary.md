On this day, 4 years ago, EVE Online was released and the masses rejoiced because a new age of MMOG gaming and enlightenment was upon them - "The Age of EVE".

To commemorate this blessed event we would like you all, players, developers and volunteers alike, to post your most memorable event in the world of EVE. A lot has happened during these 4 years and we would like to celebrate this with a trip down memory lane.

Please head over to [this forum thread](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=515927) and post your most memorable event in EVE.
