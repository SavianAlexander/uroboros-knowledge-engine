# Wiki Department

The **Wiki Department** is a group of staff members who make and monitor changes to the UniWiki to ensure that the information contained therein is as accurate and beautifully presented as possible.

Unlike most other departments in **EVE University**, staff positions in the Wiki Department are not restricted to members of EVE University. While the Wiki Manager does not technically wield any authority over out-of-corp staff beyond their title and administrative privileges, out-of-corp staff are otherwise treated as honorary University members for the purposes of their work with the UniWiki.

In-game, Wiki Department staff members who are also members of EVE University carry the title of "Wiki Curator".
