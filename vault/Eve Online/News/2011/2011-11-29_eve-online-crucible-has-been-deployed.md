EVE Online: Crucible has been deployed. The latest expansion to the game turns up the heat on the space combat experience in EVE Online, bringing greater balance and diversity to large and small scale conflicts and evolving the [graphics](//www.eveonline.com/en/crucible/article/3065/introducing-new-nebulae-into-eve), [interface](//www.eveonline.com/en/crucible/article/3079/size-matters-scaling-the-user-interface-ui) and [game mechanics](//www.eveonline.com/en/crucible/article/3033/hybrid-weapons-and-tech-ii-ammo-balancing) with over [three dozen updated and improved features](//www.eveonline.com/en/crucible/features) based on feedback and challenges put forth by its players. This is EVE re-forged.

For more information about the latest EVE Online expansion, please visit the [Crucible feature page](//www.eveonline.com/en/crucible). Full patch notes are available [here](//www.eveonline.com/updates/patchnotes.asp). An Issues thread is available [here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=432623&#post432623) and you can give general feedback on Crucible by clicking [this link](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=432621&#post432621).

Please note that you will be offered a client update once you patch which fixes several issues discovered during deployment.
