# CHIP, WIC, CCDF & HUD Section 8 Statutory Guidelines

## 1. Children's Health Insurance Program (CHIP - Title XXI)
- **Target Population**: Uninsured children under age 19 in families whose income exceeds Medicaid thresholds.
- **Income Ceiling**: Standard **250% FPL** (infants under age 1: **300% FPL**).
- **Zero Cost-Sharing**: No copayments for preventative, well-child, or immunization visits.

---

## 2. Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children (WIC - 7 CFR 246)
- **Categorical Target**: Pregnant, postpartum, breastfeeding women, infants (<1yo), and children (<5yo).
- **Income Ceiling**: **185% FPL**.
- **Adjunctive Eligibility**: Automatic income eligibility if certified for Medicaid, SNAP, or TANF.
- **Nutritional Risk Screening**: Requires clinical determination of anthropometric, biochemical, medical, or dietary risk.

---

## 3. Child Care and Development Fund (CCDF - 45 CFR 98)
- **Income Standard**: Must not exceed **85% of State Median Income (SMI)** or **200% FPL**.
- **Activity Requirement**: Parents/guardians must be engaged in employment, education, or approved job training.
- **Sliding Scale Copayment**:
  - $\le 100\%$ FPL: **0% copay** (exempt).
  - $101\% - 138\%$ FPL: **2% of gross income**.
  - $139\% - 175\%$ FPL: **4% of gross income**.
  - $176\% - 200\%$ FPL: **7% of gross income**.

---

## 4. HUD Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher (24 CFR Part 982)
- **Area Median Income (AMI) Tiers**:
  - Extremely Low Income: $\le 30\%$ AMI.
  - Very Low Income: $\le 50\%$ AMI (Primary Voucher Gate).
  - Low Income: $\le 80\%$ AMI.
- **Tenant Rent Calculation**: Tenant pays the highest of **30% of monthly adjusted income** or **$50.00 minimum rent**.
- **Housing Assistance Payment (HAP)**:
  $$\text{HAP} = \text{Payment Standard} - \text{Tenant Rent Contribution}$$
