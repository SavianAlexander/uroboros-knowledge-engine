Another big step has been taken to introduce a democratic structure to EVE, our applicants for [Council of Stellar Management](//myeve.eve-online.com/council/voting/) (CSM) has now become candidates. Xhagen, the developer in charge of this, has written a nice [Dev Blog](//myeve.eve-online.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&bid=554) where he presents some statistics on the applications and the dates for the election.

Please join us in [this forum thread](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=752283/) to discuss the CSM and your candidates.
