# Downing The Slavers (2 of 2) (Blood Raiders)

<noinclude>

 </noinclude>

  - Blitz:** In the last pocket you only have to kill the Slavers-- this completes the mission and you can warp out afterwards. All other kills are optional.

  1. # Warp-in

Acceleration gate is locked until all hostiles are destroyed.

|}

  1. # Single pocket
  - Blitz:** Kill the Blood Raider Slavers, warp out.

- The gate lands you right in the middle of a defensive, square structure with sentries and ships on each corner at 5 to 16km with a 15 second-ish delay before being aggro'ed.
- Note that sentries have 250km range though and will still hit you at 100km. This should however be tankable long enough to pick off the ships as they come toward you.

|}

  - Structures**

Walls & Elevators do not have loot.

  1. # Loot
- Loot: ~100k ISK + Salvage: ~350k ISK (2024/09/08)
- Bounty: ~780k ISK
