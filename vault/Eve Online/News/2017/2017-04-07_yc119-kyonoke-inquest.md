The Scope Galactic News Network reports live from the site of the YC119 Kyonoke Inquest in the Federal system of Postouvin.
With thousands of capsuleers having arrived to contribute to research and development efforts, in a quest to resolve the ongoing quarantine crises across the cluster, the Inquest Center Keepstar has become a hive of activity.
