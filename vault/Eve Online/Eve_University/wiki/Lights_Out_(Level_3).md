# Lights Out (Level 3)

- Blitz: Kill the Gallente Lieutenant, then warp-out.**

  1. # Single Pocket
- No rats on warp-in. Agent will tell you to attack the outpost, which will spawn a group.
- Additional groups are time-based spawns, ranging from 30 seconds after previous to several minutes.
- Group 5 will spawn after destruction of groups 1-4.
