For this storyline you are asked to retrieve a mission objective (Salvaged Electronics, 20 m³) which is inside a cloaked cache.

After destroying the whole group present, a new spawn will appear.
In total there are one initial group (Angels) and three waves (Sanshas) equals four groups.
All groups will autoaggro after some seconds.

Note: All NPC may aggro your drones, need to be verified.

  - Blitz:** Not possible as all the ships have to be killed in order for the cloaked cache to be revealed.

|}
