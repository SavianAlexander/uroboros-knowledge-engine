At 11.00 GMT today, 1 November, we opened the doors to the [EVE Fanfest 2007!](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2007/)

This marked the start of 3 days of fun with presentations, roundtables, a trip to the Blue Lagoon, PvP tournament, CCG demonstration and tournament, silent auction charity, the dev band RöXör and of course a chance to meet friends and foes and devs. For the full schedule, please download the [Fanfest brochure](//ccp.vo.llnwd.net/o2/www/fanfest/2007/EVE_FanFest_2007.pdf).

Everything will culminate on Saturday with the famous Party At The Top Of The World™!
