## Overview
Celebrate Liberation Day with a free gift that includes PLEX, SKINR components, character background, and fireworks. Plus, get stunning SKINs, and a Minmatar Pilot Expert System in the NES.

---

Liberated capsuleers, 

Liberation Day is here, which is the perfect time to show your support for the Minmatar Republic! 

In that spirit, a gift* has been left for you in the New Eden Store! 

Inside your Minmatar-themed crate you’ll find fireworks, SKINR components, a new character background, and **10 free PLEX**! 

*This gift is available to all of you who played for 12 hours or more between 10 July 2025 and 9 July 2026.

## Minmatar Pilot Expert System


If you truly want to get into the spirit of the Republic, you can choose from a multitude of Minmatar ships to fly, and the NES is now offering a new Minmatar Pilot Expert System, which gives **access to 64 skills to fly 15 different T1 Minmatar sub-capital ships** for 7 days. 

Minmatar ships are recognized for their exceptional speed, high manoeuvrability, and versatile use of Projectile Turrets. With a signature industrial aesthetic, they offer pilots high tactical flexibility. 

This is a fantastic way to try out Minmatar vessels before committing to the full training queue. The Expert System will remain available in the future as well.
