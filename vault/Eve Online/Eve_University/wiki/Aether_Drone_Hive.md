}}  **Aether Drone Hive** is a **Gallente COSMOS** combat site that spawns in Parchanier system. It shows up as a warpable beacon in space.

This site is a bit special. It doesn't exist on its own but instead it will spawn every time someone starts the second mission for **Drone Mind**. The loot mechanics inside are also unique as the site "owner" automatically owns all the wrecks inside regardless on damage dealt. If multiple people are running the mission the system can have multiple sites.
