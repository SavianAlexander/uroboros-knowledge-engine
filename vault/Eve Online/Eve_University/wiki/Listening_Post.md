# Listening Post

- Listening Post** is the second mission of a 3 part mission chain, The other missions are **Survey Rendezvous** and **Kicking the Nest**.

Destroy all Rogue Drones.

  1. # Warp-in

The acceleration gate is ~about 90 km from the warpin.
The acceleration gate is not locked. You can proceed to the next pocket without killing the drones.

When you are about 30 km from the acceleration gate a group of drones will warp in.
