# Tamoko Raytio

- Tamoko Raytio** is a level 3 agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located in Otomainen - Red Rock Outpost.

1. # Wagon Christ - Scanning The Heavens (1 of 3)

Transport 1x System Scanner (5 m3) from Ihakana VIII - Moon 6 - Nugoeihuvi Corporation Development Studio to agent.

1. # Wagon Christ - Lost Supplies (2 of 3)

Raytio Family Supplies can be obtained by fighting spawns in the **Otomainen VIII – Asteroid Belt 1**.
