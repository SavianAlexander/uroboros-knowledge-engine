# Vengeance (Blood Raiders)

Video: [Dominix](https://youtu.be/VlgriWM1xbg) 

  1. # 1st Pocket
All groups within 15-30km. Group 1, 2, and 3 can be attacked separately. Attacking Group 5 aggroed Group 4.

|}

  1. # 2nd Pocket
All groups within 15-20km. Group 1 and 3 can be attacked without drawing aggro from others. Attacking Group 4 aggroed Group 2.

|}

|}

  1. # 3rd Pocket
Killing the 1st Reinforcement flags the mission as complete. The turrets spawn behind the warp-in at ~20km. Destroy the 'EM Forcefield' to remove the shield, though it is not necessary to complete the mission. Faramon is particularly strong.

Faramon Mundan stats: DPS: 230.20hp/s (EM: 85hp/s, Expl: 0hp/s, Kin: 43.20hp/s, Therm: 102hp/s) + Wrath cruise missile

|}

|}

|}
