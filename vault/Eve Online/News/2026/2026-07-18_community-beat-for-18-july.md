## ... in Raptors!


FUN Inc/Even Better with Friends is celebrating their [450th Shrinkwrapped roam](https://npsi.rocks/events/5462) on 25 July. You're invited to participate in this public PvP fleet setting off from Jita at 18:30 UTC.

If you've never tried PvP before or just want to knock the rust off an NPSI (**N**ot **P**urple **S**hoot **I**t) fleet like this is the perfect on-ramp! No commitment beyond joining the fleet and following the fleet commander's orders is necessary, so when you get back to Jita - whether that's in your ship or your pod - you can go back to your normal life in New Eden. Although you might find once you get a taste for fleet combat in EVE you'll be craving more!

Join their in-game channel **EBWF Public** to find out more. Oh, and check out this sick promo [Keacte](https://evewho.com/character/1376548045) made:

Make sure to also check out the [npsi.rocks calendar](https://npsi.rocks/) which aggregates the schedules of multiple NPSI communities - there are public fleets going out all the time!

## This EVE Life


[Ceema](https://evewho.com/character/585213969) has been well known in the community for his prolific and *creative *posts on the EVE subreddit for some time, but he has recently embarked on a new enterprise - hosting an EVE talk show called **This EVE Life**. He writes:


> The purpose of the Twitch show and podcast is to interview 100 folks from all walks of EVE Life; from nullsec to highsec, from EVE Online to EVE Frontier, by 2027 (if not sooner).  There are a thousand stories out there and not all of them get to be heard, so I'm going in search of these tales to present them in the form of interviews with those who lived them as a way to give back to a community that has entertained me for close to 20 years now.


Ceema already has several recordings up over on his [Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/@CeemaGPT) channel, and you can catch new ones as they're recorded on Twitch. A full schedule of upcoming episodes is on the [This EVE Life website](https://thisevelife.space), with the next one all about the Alliance Tournament with match commentators [Rahne](https://evewho.com/character/94293543) and [Rose](https://evewho.com/character/2122546403) being recorded live on Sunday at 20:00 EVE time so [be sure to tune in](https://www.twitch.tv/ceemagpt)!

## Grab the popcorn, it's movie time


First up let's check out this [catch and kill](https://zkillboard.com/kill/136795801/) of a very rare [Cybele](https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Cybele) - one of the coveted prize ships from Alliance Tournament XIX. This video from [Grunt Kado](https://evewho.com/character/91251313) shows the lead up to and execution of the kill but then goes on to break down the preparation and planning that goes into big game hunting an exotic spaceship like this.

Sticking with Grunt and [Strix Ridens](https://evewho.com/alliance/99014466) here's nearly 30 minutes of unbroken real time lowsec brawling with fleet comms. Grunt throws his Vargur into a group of gate campers who stand and fight. Reinforcements from his corp show up and a prolonged engagement plays out.

And *still* sticking with Strix Ridens (they're so prolific!) next up is a video from STRIX-adjacent capsuleer [Hunter Hughes](https://evewho.com/character/165332291) featuring a pair of engagements against [WOMP](https://evewho.com/alliance/99010468) which showcases their Flykiller comp.

Finally check out this this tech 3 cruiser 4 v 1 from PvP maestro [iBeast](https://www.twitch.tv/ibeast_eve). Even though it's not in English the PvP speaks for itself.

## FC Bee Heading Down Under


[EVE Down Under](https://www.evedownunder.com) is only a few weeks away and they were recently able to announce that **FC Bee** will be traveling all the way from Iceland to Australia for the event. Taking place from 7 to 9 August in Melbourne, EVE Down Under has been running in one form or another since 2012. If you live in or around Melbourne or you're willing to make the trip [grab yourself a ticket](https://www.evedownunder.com/tickets/) and spend awesome with some spacefriends!
