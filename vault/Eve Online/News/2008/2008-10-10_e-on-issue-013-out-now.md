E-ON, EVE Online’s official quarterly magazine is back with its latest issue. E-ON #013 is jam packed with a slew of news, interviews, previews, guides and exclusive fiction all focused on EVE’s in-game economy and there is even a little bit about the upcoming Winter Expansion. E-ON subscribers will also receive six postcards featuring artwork from classic E-ON Chronicles.

You can learn more about Issue #013 [here](//www.eve-online.com/eon/). If you’d like to order a copy of the latest issue, back issues or sign up for a one year subscription, you may do so [here](//secure.eve-online.com/eon/).
