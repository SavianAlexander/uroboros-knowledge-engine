# Gone Berserk (Level 3)

- Blitz:** Kill right spawn, then all Cruisers and "Demon" Frigates reinforcements in that chain.

It is recommended to target one EoM Prophet from each group and kill them first. Then kill the remaining ships one at a time and eliminate the chain of spawns after it. This means you get a total of three of each of the Reinforcement Spawns!

An easy way to tackle this is to approach from either the left or right hand side and just pick off the ships as they approach.

  1. # Warp-in
Like the level 4 variant, the spawns appear in chains.

All spawns appear 45-55km from warp-in point and automatically aggro.
