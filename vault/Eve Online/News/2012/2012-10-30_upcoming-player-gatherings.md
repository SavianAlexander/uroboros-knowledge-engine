We'd like to draw your attention to a few player gatherings taking place in the coming weeks and encourage anyone in the vicinity to join and party with fellow pilots!

**Manchester, November 9 - 12: EVE Blob**

A gathering of Her Majesty's finest pilots intending to learn about EVE, talk about EVE, and of course, party! More information [here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=154835).

**Sydney, Australia, November 23 - 25: EVE Down Under**

If you live close to Sydney or if you're planning a walkabout in the area around this time, don't miss this one! More information [here](//eveonline-au.com/index.php/template/lorem-ipsum/eve-down-under-details-1).

**Tokyo bar meet, November 23**

Of all the things happening in Tokyo that night, this will be the best thing. More information [here.](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=164537&find=unread)
