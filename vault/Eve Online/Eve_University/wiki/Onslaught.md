# Onslaught

Single pocket without gate.
Video: [Punisher](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hv1vrP94_kU&index=83&list=PLFFC73B5464ACDC53)

  - Walkthrough:**
- Pick up the 4x scientists (4m³) at the Survey Post container (10km) then place them in the Mining Outpost container (30km).
- Kill all the drones that spawn
- Killing the last Drone flags the mission complete.

  - Note:**
The dust cloud here causes area damage to any player-owned entities nearby, including drones.
Triggered by the first rogue drone spawn, the damage inflicted seems to continue until the last rogue drone is killed.
After that, the area is safe for mining.
Overall damage seems to be around 400-500 HP total, so it's quite manageable even in a frigate.
You can also use an afterburner or Microwarpdrive to move approximately 35 to 40 km 'above' or below' the dust cloud and wait for the drones to come to you. This is useful if using a ship armed only with drones.

Whilst fighting drones are unaffected by the dust cloud, do **not** try to use salavage drones to salvage the frigate wrecks as these will be destroyed by the damaging effects. If you want to salvage, fly just outside the dust cloud (approximately 57kms from the mining outpost) and then pull the wrecks towards you using a Mobile Tractor Unit.
