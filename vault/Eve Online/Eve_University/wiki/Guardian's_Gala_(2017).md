# Guardian's Gala (2017)

- The Guardian's Gala** was an **Angel Cartel**-themed event that ran from February 14th to February 28th, 2017.

1. # Participation
The event sites are warpable beacons. The site contains one wave of frigates, one wave of cruisers and finally one battlecruiser.

Next wave spawns after previous wave is destroyed.

|}

1. # Loot
The battlecruiser always drops one "YC119 Guardian's Gala 'Spirit' Edition SKIN (Permanent)" for a gallente ship.
