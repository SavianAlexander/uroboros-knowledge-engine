### **Skilled capsuleers,**


Get ready to boost your skills and skyrocket your New Eden career faster than ever before! Time is ISK, and these new Boost Bundles offer you the best of both worlds. Get instant access to skill points to kickstart your training, and then continue to enhance your skills by utilizing boosters. The higher the bundle level, the more instant skill points you will get.

Here's what you can expect from our five new Boost Bundles:

**Apprentice Bundle **


- 250,000 Skill Points
- Genius 'Boost' Cerebral Accelerator - +12 to all attributes, lasts for 12 days or 24 days with the Biology 5 skill


**Novice Bundle**


- 500,000 Skill Points
- Expert 'Boost' Cerebral Accelerator - +10 to all attributes, lasts for 10 days or 20 days with the Biology 5 skill
- Standard 'Boost' Cerebral Accelerator - + 4 to all attributes, lasts for 4 days or 8 days with the Biology 5 skill


**Graduate Bundle**


- 750,000 Skill Points
- 2 x Specialist 'Boost' Cerebral Accelerator - +8 to all attributes, lasts for 8 days or 16 days with the Biology 5 skill


**Prodigy Bundle**


- 1,250,000 Skill Points
- 2 x Advanced 'Boost' Cerebral Accelerators - + 6 to all attributes, lasts for 6 days or 12 days with the Biology 5 skill


**Master Bundle**


- 1,750,000 Skill Points
- 2 x Standard 'Boost' Cerebral Accelerators - + 4 to all attributes, lasts for 4 days or 8 days with the Biology 5 skill


To make the most out of these Boost Bundles, we recommend training your skills while actively playing the game. By utilizing boosters, you can significantly speed up the learning process and reach your desired skill levels, ships and career goals faster than ever before.

Don't miss out on this opportunity to power up your journey in EVE Online. Head over to the [**EVE store**](https://secure.eveonline.com/store/skill-training) and grab the Boost Bundle that best suits your needs, and watch your skills skyrocket!
