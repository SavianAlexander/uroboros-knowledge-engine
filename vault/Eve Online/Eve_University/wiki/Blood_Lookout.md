# Blood Lookout

1. # First Room
----
Destroying all the defenders will unlock the acceleration gate.

|}

  1. # Second Room
----
All **Group 1 - 3** are present on warp in. Destroying **Group 2** (the group to the left at around 50 km) will trigger the commander spawn. If no commander spawns then the "Blood Raider Bunker" is the second escalation trigger.

|}

|}

|}

|}

|}
  1. # Escalation
----
Destroying the Dark Blood Frigate may trigger an escalation to **Following the Blood**. If the Dark Blood frigate does not spawn then the escalation trigger is the Blood Raider Bunker.
