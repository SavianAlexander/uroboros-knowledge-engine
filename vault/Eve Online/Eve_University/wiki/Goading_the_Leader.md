# Goading the Leader

<noinclude> 
</noinclude>

  - Objective**
Take out the Auxiliary Power Array. Location is within the system, follow the warp link. 	

  - Notes**
Don't worry about the number of pirates on your overview - the smugglers and the Nidhogger will warp out as you fly towards the Array, leaving 4 watchmen remaining. These will aggress you when you get close to the array.

Mission is completed once the power array is destroyed. The pirate ships do not need to be destroyed.

|}

|}

If the Power array is destroyed before the trigger the reinforcements will not arrive.
