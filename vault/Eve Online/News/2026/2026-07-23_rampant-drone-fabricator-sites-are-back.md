## Overview
Rampant Drone Fabricator sites, introduced temporarily in the Gallente Election event, are now permanently in C1-C6 Wormholes, but with a bigger challenge and more waves! Take them on with your friends!

---

Rampant capsuleers, 

The Gallente Election was your first chance to experience Rampant Drone Fabricator sites. 

Thanks to your feedback they are now being made permanently available in C1-C6 wormholes, with a couple of changes, specifically tailored to wormhole space. 

Face escalating waves of Rogue Drones, with the threat level now building based on a point system known as “Rampancy”. The escalating threats are determined by the ships in proximity to the Fabricator. 

The more waves you destroy, the bigger your rewards will be. Not only that, but because you were so good at these, we had to up the ante and increase the wave cap from 30 to 100. **Translation: It’s hard!** 

Can you prove your worth? Who will be the first to outlast all 100 waves? 

The extremely brave can attempt to tackle the sites solo (at least the first half – you’re unlikely to survive past 40 waves or so), but they are tailored for small teams to tackle together. Push your luck and see how long you can hold out, but know that you can fleewith the loot you’ve acquired at any time, or keep going and hope to survive for even more! 

For more details, refer to the [patch notes](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/patch-notes-version-24-01). 

We hope you enjoy this addition to wormhole space, and look forward to bringing more improvements in the near future. 

Happy hunting, capsuleers!
