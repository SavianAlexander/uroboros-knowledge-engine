This coming Friday, March 7, we're hosting an official player gathering at [Goodman's Field](//www.jdwetherspoon.co.uk/home/pubs/goodmans-field) near the Tower of London.

And since it´s UFC weekend in London starring our very own [Gunnar Nelson](//icelandreview.com/news/2013/02/08/gunnar-nelson-signs-sponsorship-deal-ccp), Iceland's best solo PvPer, we're bringing a lucky attendee with us to the fights on Saturday!

If you have any questions or just want to let us know you're coming, please visit [this thread.](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=323279&find=unread)

Come hang out and let us buy you a beer.
