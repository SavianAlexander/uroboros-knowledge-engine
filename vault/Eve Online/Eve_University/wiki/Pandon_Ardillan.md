# Pandon Ardillan

- Pandon Ardillan** is an level 2 **Gallente COSMOS** agent located at The Ebony Tower Beacon in Barmalie System and requires 2.00 Gallente Federation Faction Standing.

She offers four low volume hauling missions. As the final reward you will receive 1x "FON Strike Scene Evidence" that is needed in **Preaux Gallot's** COSMOS Missions.
