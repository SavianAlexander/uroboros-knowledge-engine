As Fanfest 2011 approaches we are offering players the opportunity to win a fantastic VIP tour of the CCP offices in Reykjavik. As always, this tour will be conducted by our CEO, Hilmar Veigar Pétursson, with the winner receiving a tour of all departments, lunch with the Devs and a bunch of cool goodies will be provided to the winner. The tour will be held on Wednesday, March 23 from approximately 10:30 to 13:00 UTC.

Our annual office tour is part of the Fanfest drive for charity and this year we are proud to support [Get Well Gamers](//www.get-well-gamers.org/), a charity formed in 2001, with the goal of bringing video game systems and games to children's hospitals.

You can submit a bid for the chance to win this tour by sending an e-mail to [officetour@ccpgames.com](mailto:officetour@ccpgames.com) with the amount that you would like to bid. The minimum starting bid is $1,000 and the closing date for bids is 23:59 UTC on Tuesday, 8 March, 2011. The winner will be contacted by mail on Wednesday, March 9, 2011.
