## Overview
Voting for CSM 19 is live now!

---

Democratic capsuleers,

Voting is now open for the 19th Council of Stellar Management! We have [47 candidates](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/csm-19-candidate-announcements) vying for your vote, so head on over to the [CSM voting page](https://community.eveonline.com/community/csm/vote/) and cast your vote for those candidates who best represent your interests in New Eden. 

During the campaign period, candidates were interviewed in community podcasts and streams, as well as engaging in short discussions on [CCPTV](https://www.twitch.tv/videos/2268237514).  For deeper insights into their platforms, each CSM 19 candidate has a [dedicated campaign thread](https://forums.eveonline.com/c/council-of-stellar-management/csm-campaigns/71) where you can learn more about their candidacy. Voting ends at 11:00 UTC on 24 October, so make sure you cast your vote in time so your voice is heard!

**All capsuleers with Omega status and accounts active for at least 60 days are eligible to vote in the elections.**

Every account that votes will be awarded with a SKIN for the Council Diplomatic Shuttle - the SARO 'Black Troop' SKIN. The SKIN will be available for redemption via NES after the election is over.
