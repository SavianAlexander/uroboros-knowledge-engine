# Hitami Magye

- Hitami Magye** is an agent in one of the chains of agents in **Caldari COSMOS**. The chain consists of (ordered):
- **Akira Helkelen**
- **Ryoke Aura**
- **Goru Nikainen**
- **Kochi Utrainen**
- **Hitami Magye**
- **Mintu Oshima**
Each agent in the chain provides items required for starting or completing missions for the next agent in the chain.

1. # Mercantile Settlers - Confirmation For Your Good Intentions (1 of 5)

You will get Utrainen's Employment Voucher by doing missions for **Kochi Utrainen**.

1. # Mercantile Settlers - Jorek Lephny (2 of 5)

Jorek Lephny is located in the second pocket of Otomainen - **Rush Town Ruins** complex.

1. # Mercantile Settlers - To The Health Clinic (3 of 5)

Transport 1x Jorek Lephny (1.0 m3) to Otsela VI - Moon 13 - Ishukone Corporation Factory.

1. # Mercantile Settlers - Laser Shortage (4 of 5)

You can buy 5x Miner II's from the market.

1. # Mercantile Settlers - Outlaws (5 of 5)

Ancient Treasure Map is requested by **Mintu Oshima**. Outlaw spurs are dropped by various NPCs (primarily Guristas Outlaw) in COSMOS complexes in [Okkelen constellation](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/The_Forge/Okkelen)
