1. # Summary
The **Wreathe** is the entry level **hauler** provided to Minmatar capsuleers during the tutorial missions. Like the other starter transports it has a relatively small cargo hold but more than makes up for it with speed and tank. Its cargo hold is not to be underestimated either, as with high skills and a proper fit it is able to carry a packaged **battlecruiser** and still have plenty of extra space.

The Wreathe is notable for being the fastest Tech 1 hauler available, to the point that with maximum skills, two inertial stabilizers will allow it to align as fast as a **blockade runner** or **interceptor**. It does this while having the second largest base cargo hold of the fast haulers, making it arguably the best overall ship in its class. A properly fit Wreathe will be able to zip through most lowsec gate camps, assuming they aren't packing **smartbombs**. Running distribution missions and trading goods in lowsec can provide some practical experience for future blockade runners. Ultimately, the Wreathe possesses unmatched versatility among the haulers while remaining incredibly cheap, making it an invaluable tool for any industrialist.
