# Angel Burrow

The Angel Burrow is a **combat anomaly** found in **Angel** infested regions.

1. # Walkthrough
Single normal space pocket. Auto-aggression from each wave. The last ship killed triggers the next wave. Waves spawn at 30 to 60km from warp-in.
