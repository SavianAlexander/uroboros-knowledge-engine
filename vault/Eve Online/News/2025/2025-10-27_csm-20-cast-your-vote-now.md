## Overview
The polls are open and your vote matters!

---

Capsuleers,

Voting is now open for the 20th Council of Stellar Management! 

A total of [55 candidates](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/csm-20-candidate-announcements) are in the running, so head on over to the [CSM voting page](https://community.eveonline.com/community/csm/vote/) and choose the candidates you feel best represent your interests in New Eden. 

Each CSM 20 candidate has a [dedicated campaign thread](https://forums.eveonline.com/c/council-of-stellar-management/csm-campaigns/71) where you can learn more about their candidacy. You can also check out interviews and discussions on [CCPTV](https://www.twitch.tv/videos/2594242286).

CSM voting utilizes the [Wright-STV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wright_system) voting system, allowing you to select up to 10 candidates on your ballot. For more details on how to cast your vote, watch the video below:

Voting ends at 11:00 UTC on 10 November, so make sure to cast your vote in time so your voice is heard! Winners will be announced on 14 November.

**All capsuleers with Omega status and accounts active for at least 60 days are eligible to cast their vote in the elections.**

All capsuleers who vote will receive the SOCT Metamorphosis frigate (limited to one per account). The vessel will be claimable in the NES between 10 and 21 November.
