## Overview

### Chilly capsuleers,


Prepare for the wintry thrills of Winter Nexus as New Eden’s coolest event returns from 5 December to 7 January. Expect icy storms, exclusive loot, and the chance to showcase your skills through combat, exploration, and mining adventures. 


## THE STORMS ARE COMING


Storms are forecast across highsec, lowsec, and nullsec, transforming systems into stunning, wintry arenas for you to navigate, offering unique challenges and treasures. Get ready to fight fearsome Sansha forces with your battlecruiser, battleship, or heavy assault cruiser. Prepare your scanners to find and unlock riches in frigid exploration sites and put your hacking prowess to the test. Start warming up your warp drives to dive into profitable Yoiul Festival resource sites to gain resources and rewards.

In the heart of the Volatile Ice Storms, capsuleers will also benefit from enhanced thermal resistances and increased module overheating durability, turning every system into a new tactical experience.


## POWERFUL PRESENTS


Every day of Winter Nexus will bring a new reward, inluding EverMarks, design components, SKINs, and up to 650,000 Skill Points, with Omega pilots earning both Alpha and Omega track rewards. The 25th day of entering the winter storms brings the ultimate boost: 75,000 SP for Alphas and a total of 325,000 SP for Omegas!


## REAP THE RICHES


Increase the size of your Winter Nexus stocking by upgrading to Omega and getting even bigger and better presents. Enjoy more Skill Points, EverMarks, exclusive SKINs, and much more as you dive into the storms, seize your riches, and make this a season to remember!

Stay frosty.
