# Lord Manel's Mansion

- Lord Manel's Mansion** is an **Amarr COSMOS** site (DED unrated)

1. # Entrance
The Key to Lord Manel's Mansion is a reward of **Manel's Disappearance (4 of 5)**.

|}

1. # The First Check Point

Blood Phantom frigates have pretty high velocity and are orbiting at close range. Cruiser sized guns have a difficulty hitting them, even with tracking enhancing modules. It is recommended to either use missiles, drones or guns + Stasis Webifier.
