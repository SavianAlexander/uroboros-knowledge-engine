# Million Little Pieces

Gate restriction to Command Ships and down. Spawns are all time based. No hostiles at initial gate.

  - Blitz:**
- Destroy Ore Processing Plant and warp out.
- Via this method no Gallente Federation standing loss is incurred.

  1. # 1st Pocket
Destroying the Communications Relay prevents the timed waves. Auto-aggro after one minute.
