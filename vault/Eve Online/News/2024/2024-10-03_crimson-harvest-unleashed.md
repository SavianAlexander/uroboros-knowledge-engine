## Overview
Blood-soaked battles and brutal rewards await.

---

### Ruthless capsuleers,


[**Crimson Harvest**](https://www.eveonline.com/now/crimson-harvest-2024) has begun, and with it comes a blood-drenched conflict that will tear across New Eden until 5 November. Prepare for hellish combat and hacking sites, as well as a constant barrage of challenges as you navigate this season of slaughter. The rewards are savage, the battles merciless - will you survive?


## A WAR OF DEVOTION AND BLOOD


Crimson Harvest throws you into a deadly confrontation between the zealots of the [**Order of Tetrimon**](https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/st-tetrimon) and the twisted [**Blood Raider Covenant**](https://universe.eveonline.com/factions/blood-raider-covenant). Blood flows through highsec, lowsec, nullsec, and wormhole systems, including Pochven, as combat and hacking sites appear throughout. Only the fiercest capsuleers will dare to face the greatest challenges in Blood Raider controlled systems within the Delve region and within the Eugidi, Angils and Essin constellations.

For those brave enough to confront the rarest sites, beware - these dungeons may escalate, leading you deeper for an even greater prize. Will you survive, or fall among the countless wrecks left in the wake of this massacre?


## A GALAXY AT WAR


As Crimson Harvest sweeps through New Eden, intercepted transmissions reveal the scale of the conflict. The Blood Raiders urge capsuleers to embrace chaos: ***"*****You are called upon to slaughter the weak on behalf of the Blood Raiders. Harvest the unbelievers and emerge victorious!”**

Meanwhile, the Order of Tetrimon rallies its forces, declaring: ***"*****Do not fear the heretic Blood Raiders, but rather trust in St. Tetrimon and follow his path. Destroy the blasphemers and you shall be rewarded!”**

The wreckage from their violent clashes drift like tombstones across the stars.


## SLAUGHTER FOR SPOILS


Crimson Harvest is a season of bloodshed. Every kill will reward you greatly, with a **90% loot drop rate in PvP combat**. Mark the wrecks of your fallen rivals as trophies of your victories and claim their plunder for yourself. But beware—others may be lurking in the shadows, ready to ambush you when you least expect it. In this season of violence, the strong prey on the weak—don’t let yourself be counted among the hunted.


## GET UNDER THEIR SKIN


The season of slaughter brings SKINdustrialists a cornucopia of design elements to bring their most twisted creations to life, including the Brutal Splatter pattern, Steel Violet, Ritual Gourd Satin, Savant Carmine Satin, Anointed Dead pattern, and much more. Log in daily, complete brutal challenges, and loot the wrecks of your victims to uncover each design element. Begin training the new “Paragon Partnership” skill today to reduce Paragon Hub vendor fees when you are ready to list your twisted creations.


## GRIM REWARDS AWAIT


Each day you log in during Crimson Harvest, you’ll be greeted with rich rewards. Both Alpha and Omega capsuleers can claim exclusive gifts, but Omega pilots will get greatly increased spoils with both reward tracks and up to 650,000 SP. [**Upgrade to Omega**](https://store.eveonline.com/#omega) at any time to unlock every prior reward you missed.

Your daily spoils include boosters, SKINs, EverMarks, Skill Points, and more. Will you claim what is rightfully yours and rise to dominance in this season of chaos?


## SKIN THEM ALIVE


Until 5 November every purchase from the [EVE Merch Store](https://merch.eve.com/) will come with a free Sanguinary Savant SKIN. Order two items or less and get the Devoter Sanguinary Savant SKIN, and order three or more to get the Prophecy Navy Issue Sanguinary Savant SKIN.


## TREATS OF TERROR


Keep your eyes on [**CCP TV**](https://www.twitch.tv/ccp) for terrifying Twitch drops throughout Crimson Harvest. But the treats don’t stop there—some of New Eden’s top streamers will be offering insights and strategies to help you conquer the horrors of Crimson Harvest. Stay tuned for your chance to learn and earn!
