# Hidden Dark Ochre and Gneiss Deposit

Hidden Dark Ochre and Gneiss Deposit is one of the **scannable** ore sites introduced in the [Catalyst](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/catalyst-expansion-notes) expansion. It is level II **scanning** difficulty and currently (November 2025) only confirmed to be found in **low-sec** systems. The site contains 11 Gneiss and Dark Ochre asteroids of different rarity and size. NPC's were not observed spawning in the site.
