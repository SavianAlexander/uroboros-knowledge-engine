  - Rapture Nebula** is a gas exploration site containing gas used in the production of **Medical boosters**.

This site can be found by probe scanning it in low-sec systems in the [Molden Heath region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Molden_Heath)

Defenders:

None

Minable Gas Clouds:

2 Azure Cytoserocin (700 units each). 
Clouds are approx. 35-40km from warp-in point. 

Azure Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Sooth Sayer Booster.
