# Kador Military Outpost

- Kador Military Outpost** is a COSMOS combat site found in Chanoun system. The site shows up on overview as a beacon.

The rats in the site are all passive unless they are approached or shot at. Killing the Imperial Navy ships will likely incur standing losses.

You are likely here to collect Amarr Light Marines. There is no combat at all so bring a fast ship that has enough room to haul the marines (50x Amarr Light Marines = 100).

1. # 1st pocket

The defenders will not attack unless you approach them or attack them. Only interesting thing is the acceleration gate leading to the next room.
