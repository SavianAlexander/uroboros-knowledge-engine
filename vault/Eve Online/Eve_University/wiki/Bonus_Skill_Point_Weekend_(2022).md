# Bonus Skill Point Weekend (2022)

- Bonus Skill Point Weekend** was a limited time login campaign, lasting from 2 December to 5 December 2022, **downtime** to downtime.

1. # Log-In Campaign

Capsuleers had 4 days to log in for 3 days worth of rewards. Alpha Clones could claim 45k **Skill Points**, while Omega Clones had access to 150k Skill Points.
