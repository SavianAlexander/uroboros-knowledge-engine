# Blood Forsaken Rally Point

The **Blood Forsaken Rally Point** is a **combat anomaly** found in **Blood Raiders** infested regions. It is a level 3 variant of the **Blood Rally Point**.

1. # Walkthrough

Single ungated normal space pocket.

This site has triggers, most notably 2 cruisers in the initial defending fleet.
If these cruisers are killed, they will spawn the next wave(s) regardless of whether the battleships are killed.
Note that one random cruiser in the initial fleet will trigger Wave #2, and another will trigger Wave #3.
