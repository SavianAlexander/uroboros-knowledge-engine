# Sansha Fortress

- Sansha Fortress** is an **Unrated Complex** found by scanning in Null security regions with Sansha presence. T2 **Battleships** and below can enter the site.
1. # Walkthrough
Upon warping to the site you land at an acceleration gate.

Destroying the True Sansha commander and/or the Sansha Control Center may escalate the site to **Shady Operation**.
