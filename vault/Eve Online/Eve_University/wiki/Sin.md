1. # Summary
The **Sin** is the Gallente **Black Ops**. It maintains the strong drone bonuses of the **Dominix** upon which it is based. The flexibility of the Dominix is even increased with a balanced slot layout of both six mid and six low slots. This allows both shield and armor tanks. If used in a group, a buffer armor tank is usually the preferred choice, while solo or with less support an active shield tank is popular. The high slots can be used for blasters, neuts, or remote reps. When fitted for maximum DPS (with blasters and shield tanked), it reaches impressive DPS but is still outgunned by a typically fitted **Redeemer**. Therefore you rather see it in a neuting or remote repair role.
