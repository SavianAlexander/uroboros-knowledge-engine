1. # Summary
The **Providence** has the largest capacitor, the 2nd fastest speed / alignment time, the 2nd highest EHP and the 3rd largest cargo hold (same as the Fenrir) of the four **freighters**.

The cargo hold of the Providence starts with a base of 435,000; and ends with a maximum of 1,127,015;. This makes it the 3rd largest cargo hold size tied with the Minmatar freighter (**Fenrir**). Providence has a velocity starting at a base of 70 m/s, placing it as the second fastest behind Fenrir. It is also the second fastest to align.

Providence has the second highest EHP (behind the Gallente **Obelisk**) and has the largest capacitor of the four freighters, at maximum skill level.
