# Guristas Strike - The Kidnapping

<noinclude> 
  - Guristas Strike - The Kidnapping** is the third mission of the mission chain **Guristas Strike**. Unlike most storyline missions this mission gives no faction standing, but it does give agent standing. The faction standing is awarded for the final mission in the chain.
