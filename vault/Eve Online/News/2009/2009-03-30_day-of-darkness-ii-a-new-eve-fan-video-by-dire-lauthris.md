Based on the Chronicle "[The Breakout](//www.eveonline.com/background/potw/default.asp?cid=aug01)", Dire Lauthris newest fan video tells the story of Admiral Yakiya Tovil-Toba. With nothing more than a Chimera and a few dozen attack craft, Tovil-Toba attempted to save millions of lives on Caldari Prime while it was under attack by Gallente forces some 200 years ago. The actions of the Admiral gave the Caldari enough time to finish the evacuation of citizens trapped by the Gallente assault.

Featuring stunning in-game footage and a fabulous narration by StevieSG, this video is one that will not disappoint. Full details can be found [here.](//www.eveonline.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=1037737)
