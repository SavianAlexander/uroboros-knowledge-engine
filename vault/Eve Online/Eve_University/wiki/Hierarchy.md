# Hierarchy

Hierarchy is an **Unrated Complex** found in **nullsec** via **exploration**.

The site is behind an acceleration gate. Warping to the site lands you at 0 on the gate. All rats must be cleared to activate the acceleration gate to the second room.

1. # Second Room
  - Blitz:**
The entire site has no warp disruptors or scrams so most rats can be ignored if you can tank them.
Upon landing approach the Warning Sign near the Hierarchy structure. Getting close will trigger the first spawn.
Each new wave is triggered by simply damaging a single rat so you can quickly spawn the battleship wave. 
Kill all the battleships, spawning the Sentient, kill it, bookmark the wreck and warp off. After the site has despawned grab your loot and possibly salvage the wreck (average 9-11m in Salvage).
