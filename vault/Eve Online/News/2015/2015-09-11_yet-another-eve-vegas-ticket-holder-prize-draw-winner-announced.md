On August 18th, week we announced the [second round of EVE Vegas 2015 ticket holder prize draws](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/eve-online-news/eve-vegas-2015-ticket-sales-draw-round-2/). Today, we're ready to annouce the fourth winner of this draw!

As outlined in the news article linked above, this winner will receive a Resort Vista room upgrade and complimentary airport limousine transfers. The winner has been drawn.

Congratulations to [DrunkenOne](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/DrunkenOne) of [Burning Napalm](//gate.eveonline.com/Corporation/Burning%20Napalm) on being selected as a winner!

Winners of the prize draw will be contacted via email with full details on how they can claim their prize. 

Tickets for EVE Vegas 2015 are close to being sold out, and there is only one more prize to be awarded in this draw, so be sure to keep your eyes peeled for announcement of the final winner next week!

Tickets are also selling fast, so be sure to pick up yours today in order to avoid disappointment and make sure that you are included in the last prize draw.

There's still one last chance to win!
