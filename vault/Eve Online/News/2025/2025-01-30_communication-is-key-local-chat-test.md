## Overview

---

Chatty capsuleers,

The communications channels of New Eden are about to become clearer. As part of EVE Evolved, local chat, the lifeblood of social connections and battlefield intelligence, is moving to the state-of-the-art [**Quasar infrastructure**](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/introducing-quasar). This evolution will deliver increased reliability and stability, so you can focus on what truly matters: strategy, survival, and supremacy.

To ensure this system is battle-ready, your presence is needed in the upcoming Singularity Mass Test on 31 January at 18:00 UTC. Answer the call and earn 150 Large Skill Injectors, as a token of gratitude for your service. The Skill Injectors will be awarded on Singularity, which will remain open until 3 February for further testing, so you can have some fun with your SP during that time. Note that only the participants of tomorrow’s mass test are eligible for the Skill Injectors.

Find more details about the test and rewards [here](https://forums.eveonline.com/t/mass-test-on-singularity-friday-january-31st-18-00-utc-local-chat/475277).


## Information is Power


A dependable local chat system is essential for maintaining situational awareness, identifying threats, and forging alliances. A failure in communication could cost you a ship, a fleet, or a war. This is your chance to be on the frontline of improving a tool that shapes the very fabric of New Eden.

Fly safe and stay in touch!
