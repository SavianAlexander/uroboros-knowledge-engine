# Blood Raider Base

- Blood Raider Base** is an **Unrated Complex** found by scanning in Null Security regions with Blood Raider presence.
1. # Walkthrough
This site is very similar to **Provisional Blood Outpost**.
Upon warping to the site you will land at an acceleration gate.
  1. # First Pocket
Acceleration gate is approx. 30 km away. Timed wave spawns in a few minutes after warpin.
