# Wispy Nebula

The **Wispy Nebula** is a gas site found in null sec through Exploration.
1. # Defenders
None - no rats spawn in this site

1. # Minable Gas Clouds

The site spawns 2 Vemillion Cytoserocin clouds (1500 units each) about 150 Km apart, with some rock formations in the middle.

This site can only be found in the Catch region of space. The site has no defender NPCs.

1. # Gas Clouds

2 Vermillion Cytoserocin clouds (1500 units). When the gas harvesters on your ship finish cycling, there is a high chance to receive 500-700 thermal damage.
