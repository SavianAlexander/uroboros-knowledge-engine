# Sansha Annex

- Sansha Annex** is an **Unrated Complex** found by scanning in Low Security systems with Sansha presence.

1. # Walkthrough
The site is very similar to **Sansha Vigil**.
Upon warping to the site you will land at an acceleration gate.
