# ST 60 Hideout

- ST 60 Hideout** is a static COSMOS deadspace complex found in Hjoramold system. It has no beacon and requires scanning. Alternatively you can warp to planet 12 moon 1 and land on same grid with the site, then you can warp to a structure on site. The site is ungated and has no limits on ship size.
