# Digital Complex

- Digital Complex** is a **chemical lab** found in Delve region.

The site can also be actually a site called "Pirate Hideout" but there is not much information available. The only source being this [Archived eveinfo article](https://web.archive.org/web/20170806202011/http://www.eveinfo.net:80/complexes/inde~139.htm).

1. # Walkthrough

The site contains both combat and **hacking**. The aggro range on the rats here seems small or the aggro timer long. The gates in the first room can be activated without killing the defenders. There's an initial acceleration gate, so warp in at 10km. The neut range of the rats in the early rooms is at least 30km and the neut pressure in the Stone Temple Learning Zone is intense.

  1. # 1st pocket - Delivery Zone

Either gate can be activated without killing rats.
