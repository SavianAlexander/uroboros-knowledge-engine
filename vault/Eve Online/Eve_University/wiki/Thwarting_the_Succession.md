# Thwarting the Succession

<noinclude> </noinclude>

  - Objective**
Kill mercenaries to let the Krusual spy escape.

  - Notes**
Warp to the encounter. You will land about 25km away from three **Mercenary Rookie** ships, which you will have to destroy. Then three **Mercenary Fighter** ships and **Mizara** will spawn. After you destroyed **Mizara** the mission is complete.

When you turn in Kaita Eslin says: *"You've destroyed a pirate ring that would have taken us months to work through. High marks all around."*
