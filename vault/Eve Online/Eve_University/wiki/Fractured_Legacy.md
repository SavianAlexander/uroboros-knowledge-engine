# Fractured Legacy

- Fractured Legacy** is the ORE **epic arc**, introduced with the Catalyst expansion in November 2025. It is the shortest Epic Arc, being only three missions long.

1. # Ship and equipment
- The first mission requires the use of a  fit with **mining laser**s and a **Data Analyzer**. You will also want a **Microwarpdrive** to move faster.
  - Upon accepting the mission you are granted the  skill which should be trained in preparation for the second mission.
- The second mission requires a  with a Mining Survey Chipset fitted.
  - A Basic Mining Survey Chipset is granted on acceptation of the mission.
  - The first mission has a Pioneer as reward.

1. # Rewards
Completing the first mission along the arc awards a  mining destroyer, which was also introduced in the Catalyst expansion. Completing the final mission will reward you with a 1-run Mobile Phase Anchor blueprint copy as well.

1. # Starting agent
Elias Peltonnen of ORE Technologies offers the first mission of the arc in Kisogo VII - AIR Laboratories in The Forge. All of the missions will take place in high security systems within 5-6 jumps of that area.
