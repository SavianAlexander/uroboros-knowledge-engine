# Gone Berserk (Level 1)

- Blitz:** Destroy the EoM Imp from the right group, as seen from the warp-in point. Destroy its reinforcements. Get out.

Deadspace single pocket with gate.

  1. # Single pocket
Each initial Fiend will trigger reinforcement 1, and killing the wave will trigger reinforcement 2.

Best to kill all Imps first, then one Fiend, then complete its waves before killing another of the initial Fiends.
