# My Sweet Privateer

{{MissionBriefing 
|A gang of pirates has been terrorizing the <System. system for some time. And they've been using their base in that system as a launching point to strike out into neighboring systems as well. Of course, pirates are to be expected these days, but these pirates are different. They are well funded, well equipped, and commanded by some canny ship commanders. 

A survivor of the pirate raids told us that at least two of the ship captains — including the pirates' leader himself — were once high-ranking officers in the Gallente Navy. In fact, we believe the Gallente government has sanctioned these pirates as privateers. The damage they have already caused to Amarrian interests is incalculable. Go out there and wipe them out. Yes, this one's a “terminate with extreme prejudice.”

The bounty on the pirate commander is sizeable, but you'll need proof of his death to collect it. Bring back his head and you'll be well-rewarded for your efforts. 

}}

  - Blitz:** Clear first room and activate gate. Kill scrambling frigates, then kill Privateer Commander, scoop the Privateer Commander's Head and warp out.

  - Tips**
- Kill the Privateer Specialists first just in case you have to warp out
- Destruction of any Sentry will result in -0.3 standing loss
- And last but not least don't die
