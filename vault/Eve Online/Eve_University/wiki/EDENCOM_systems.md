# EDENCOM systems

- EDENCOM systems** are systems in which **EDENCOM** forces have permanent presence. These systems were won by EDENCOM side during **Third chapter of Triglavian invasion**.

These systems are patrolled by EDENCOM rats and are dangerous for people with negative EDENCOM standing.

EDENCOM systems come in two varieties: Minor victory and Fortress.

1. # Features

Features that set EDENCOM systems apart from normal systems:
- Patrolling EDENCOM fleets. These warp around the system and can be found on stargates, stations, asteroid belts and POCOs.
- Fortification Orcas that deploy Gunstars sentry turrets on stargates, stations, Upwell structures, asteroid belts and POCOs. The Gunstars are bumpable.
- (?) EDENCOM combat anomalies may also spawn: EDENCOM Forward Post, EDENCOM Staging Area, EDENCOM Field Base

  1. # Fortress differences

EDENCOM fortresses are very similar to EDENCOM minor victories. The differences are:
- Stronger roaming fleets.
- Orca deploys Heavy Gunstars instead of normal Gunstars.
- **Accelerator Flashpoint** combat site can spawn.
- **EDENCOM Staging Area** and **EDENCOM Field Base** combat anomalies can spawn.

1. # System wide effects
