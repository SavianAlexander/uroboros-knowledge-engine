# Federal Inspection (3 of 5)

<noinclude>
  - Gallente COSMOS** mission
Agent: **Onreun Coen**
Agent location: Central Administration (Fluekele)
Standings required: 5.00 Gallente Federation
Parts: **(1 of 5)**, **(2 of 5)**, **(3 of 5)**, **(4 of 5)**, **(5 of 5)**</noinclude>

You need to find a rat called "Pourpas Aunten" in asteroid belts in Jolia system, kill him and loot the mission item. Alternatively you can buy the mission item from contracts.

Pourpas flies a cruiser and is escorted by two frigates.<noinclude>
