# Surprise Surprise (Minmatar Republic & Gallente Federation)

Video: [Vargur - Minimal Standing Loss](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PxZhj6iiioE)

  - Blitz**: Trigger wave 3 by killing the battleship Republic Sigur 100ish KM away from warp in. In wave 3 kill the battleship Federation Xenan that is not part of the main group on spawn ie. the one not next to the web battery.

  - Notes**: The pocket is **semi-deadspace**. You can use this to ping to an on grid bookmark to get in range of the final battleship. If blitzed the standing loss seems to be limited to -0.002% towards Minmatar for destroying the Republic Sigur.
