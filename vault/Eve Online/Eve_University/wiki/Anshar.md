1. # Summary
The **Anshar** is the Gallente Jump Freighter. Unlike its more mundane brother, the **Obelisk**, the Anshar has an onboard jump drive, similar to the one on a carrier or dreadnought. This makes it much more popular among null- and low-sec alliances, where having a regular freighter make the trip would be too risky to attempt.
