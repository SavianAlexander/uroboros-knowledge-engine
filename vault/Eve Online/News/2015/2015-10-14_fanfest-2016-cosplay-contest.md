We're happy to announce that after the awesome success of the Fanfest 2015 cosplay contest, which you can see the results of in [CCP Affinity's devblog here](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/i-take-off-my-robe-and-wizard-hat-fanfest-2015-cosplay-results/), we'll be hosting a second annual cosplay contest at Fanfest 2016!

Rather than being split over three days, requiring a lot more attention from attendees who we'd rather see enjoying more of the panels and roundtables in store at Fanfest, the contest will focus on one single day, with a whole host of sweet prizes on offer for those who choose to attend Fanfest in style.

We'll be sure to post a blog closer to fanfest that will detail prizes, how to enter, where to come find myself and CCP Affinity at Fanfest 2016 and how to get involved. In the meantime, [here's the blog from last Fanfest](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/i-put-on-my-robe-and-wizard-hat-cosplay-at-fanfest-2015/) that shows off the prizes that were given for last year's contest.

With that in mind, start preparing your threads for Fanfest 2016, and we'll see you there!
