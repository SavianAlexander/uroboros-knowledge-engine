With [Fanfest 2014](//fanfest.eveonline.com/en/default) swiftly approaching, we are happy to announce the release of the 100th EVE Newsletter.

Including highlights and links for Dev Blogs from April including ship skins, Team Security, and The 49-U6U Slaughterhouse, along with a nostalgic look back at our first ever [EVE Online Newsletter](//web.ccpgamescdn.com/eveonlineassets/newsletters/vol100/vol001.jpg), this one is packed with information on Fanfest 2014 and what's coming in the summer.

Check your email inboxes for further details, or check it out online [here!](//links.mail.eveonline.com/servlet/MailView?ms=NDU2OTYyOTgS1&r=MTgyNzU5MDEzODES1&j=NDIyODM0MzMyS0&mt=1&rt=0)
