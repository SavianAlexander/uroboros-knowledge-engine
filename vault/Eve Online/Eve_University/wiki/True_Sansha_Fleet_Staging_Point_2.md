# True Sansha Fleet Staging Point 2

- True Sansha Fleet Staging Point 2** is an **Expedition** that may escalate from **True Sansha Fleet Staging Point**.
1. # Walkthrough
The site is NOT **deadspace**. You can warp around the site, and any potential hunter can warp directly to you with combat probes. The site can be difficult due to the presence of Energy Neutralizer sentries.

1. # Escalation
Destroying all enemies gives a fairly high chance of escalating the site to **True Sansha Fleet Staging Point 3**. Unlike normal escalations, the system in which the escalation appears in may be up to 12 jumps away from the current system.
