## Overview
Alton Haveri reports on events in Period Basis, after the attack an advanced shipyard belonging to the Blood Raider Covenant that appears to be based on a Sotiyo class engineering complex.
With the Covenant forming a fleet in anticipation of the final attack, and local forces currently in the process of forming up for an attack, the situation remains volatile, and very likely to escalate in the coming hours!
