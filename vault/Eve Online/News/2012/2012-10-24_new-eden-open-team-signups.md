Time is running out to register teams and finalize rosters for the upcoming [New Eden Open](//community.eveonline.com/en/tournament/). Teams will have the opportunity to vie for the title of the best tournament team in EVE Online as well as win the following amazing prizes:

1st Place: $6,000 and an in-game medal

2nd Place: $3,000 and an in-game medal

3rd Place: $1,000 and an in-game medal

4th Place: 25 Billion ISK and an in-game medal

For those teams worried about ISK being a barrier to their entry, do not forget we have added a new secure sponsorship program to let them fly and earn their sponsor some extra PLEX as well. Sponsors have the opportunity to win 150, 75 or 50 PLEX if their team comes in 1st, 2nd or 3rd.

The auction for the much sought after slots begins on Saturday, October 27 at 19:00 UTC where we will see who will compete to be declared the very best in EVE Online and walk away quite a few dollars richer. $10,000 dollars and the pride of the universe is on the line; will you be the team to seize victory?
