The four empires in EVE Online are launching an assault on a dangerous target on November 7! They are inviting all capsuleers to join them in this assault and bring down what they claim is a threat to the entire cluster. You can join them in the systems of Sarum Prime or Meves at 18:50 UTC on November 7. Representatives from the empires will be in those systems to gather up fleets of interested players. Read more about it [here](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/world-news/empire-navies-put-out-call-for-capsuleer-assistance/).
