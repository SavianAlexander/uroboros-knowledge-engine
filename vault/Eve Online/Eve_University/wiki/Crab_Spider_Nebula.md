# Crab Spider Nebula

This is a null sec gas site found through **Exploration**.
Malachite Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Pure Standard Mindflood Booster.
1. # Defenders

|}

1. # Gas Clouds
5 Malachite Cytoserocin clouds: 5 of 400 units around some boulders and coral formation.

1. # Location
This site is found only in the [Delve region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Delve#const) of space.
The gas clouds of this site do not cause damage by being mined.
