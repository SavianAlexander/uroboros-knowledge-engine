# Blood Yard

The Blood Yard is a **combat anomaly** found in Amarr low and null security regions with **Blood Raider** presence.

1. # Walkthough
The site is an open Deadspace pocket, one may warp to preferred distance. Warping to beacon for close range would require a very good tank to handle the swarm’s incoming damage. Using a propulsion module or a Micro Jump Drive is recommended. Reinforcements spawns are triggered by the last ship killed.

1. # Rewards
The site pays one million isk in bounties and the rare faction spawn can drop faction modules, implants or blueprints.

1. # Escalation
This site can escalate to **Crimson Hand Supply Depot**.
