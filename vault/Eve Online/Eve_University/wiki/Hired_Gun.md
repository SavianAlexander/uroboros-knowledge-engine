# Hired Gun

- Hired Gun** is an **expedition** that can escalate from **Gurista Fortress**

1. # Hired Gun Part 1
You will warp in to Area 1 close enough to deactivate any cloaks. Bookmarking the position will allow you to warp in at any range you choose.

  - Blitz:** Kill the Dread Guristas Battleship if it spawns. Otherwise ignore all ships and activate the acceleration gate. This strategy is applicable to all four parts of the escalation.

|}

Inside is nothing, but you will get either an escalation or DEAD END.

1. # Hired Gun Part 2
Warping to this stage places you in a cloud around 20-30km from the nearest enemy ships. Bookmarking the location will allow you to warp to any distance you choose.

|}

There is a chance the escalation will end after this site.
