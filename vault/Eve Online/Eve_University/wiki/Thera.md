# Thera

- Thera** ([Dotlan](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/system/Thera)) is a wormhole solar system in **shattered wormhole space**. It has features making it different from any other system in **New Eden** or **wormhole space (Anoikis)**. Thera is receiving some focus for new in-game lore connected to the **Sleeper**'s mysterious past. The Thera system was introduced in the **Rhea** expansion (December 2014). Player interaction within Thera quickly evolved the system into a very PVP focused arena<http://www.themittani.com/news/blood-bath-thera>.

1. # Features

- It is accessible only via wormholes (no gates).
- It is the only **wormhole space** system with NPC stations, and the stations have normal services including markets.
- Many of the planets are **shattered**, and there are no moons.
- No **Upwell structures**.
- Increased connectivity to K-Space, with a total of a dozen or so wormholes to high-, low-, and null-sec at any given time.
- It is a very large system, 342 AU in diameter.
- Mobile warp disruptors (bubbles) may not be anchored.
- Sleeper occupation equivalent to Class 3 and 4 **W-Space** systems.
- **Capital ship**s may not enter Thera or be built there.
- Like all other W-Space systems, Thera has -1.0 **system security**.

1. # Helpful Tips for Visitors

- You can set your **home station** to any station in Thera (provided you're docked there).
- Be mindful that while the Local chat channel in Thera is regularly chatty (unlike the rest of W-Space), not every person present in the system will appear there.
- [EvE Scout](https://www.eve-scout.com/) seeks to maintain a list of active wormhole connections to/from Thera. This makes it convenient to use Thera as a shortcut across space, with no scanning necessary since they provide a **bookmark** folder.
- Also be mindful that the exits to the stations are often camped so try and set up **insta-undock bookmarks** if you are going to use them. Or wait at a **safe spot**, cloak enabled, if you are forming up for a fleet.

1. # Stations

All four stations are operated by the NPC corporation [The Sanctuary](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/npc/The_Sanctuary), a research branch of the **Servant Sisters of EVE** faction. These stations have several available station services. Those services include Loyalty Points store, however, there are no mission agents.

- Bounty Office
- Clone Bay
- Fitting
- Industry
- Insurance
- LP StoreShip
- Market (Thera System Only)
- Re-Customization
- Repairshop
- Reprocessing Plant (50% base yield)

Station guns will shoot any ship using interdiction bubbles within 250km.

The stations are: 
- [Thera XII - The Sanctuary Institute of Paleocybernetics](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/station/Thera_XII_-_The_Sanctuary_Institute_of_Paleocybernetics)
- [Thera XII - The Sanctuary Surveillance Observatory](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/station/Thera_XII_-_The_Sanctuary_Surveillance_Observatory)
- [Thera XIII - The Sanctuary Applied Gravitation Laboratory](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/station/Thera_XIII_-_The_Sanctuary_Applied_Gravitation_Laboratory)
- [Thera XIV - The Sanctuary Fullerene Loom](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/station/Thera_XIV_-_The_Sanctuary_Fullerene_Loom)

1. # Player Owned Starbase
  - Player-owned starbases (POS)** are not anchorable in Thera because there are no moons. Neither are **Upwell structures** due to Thera being a shattered Wormhole Space (also, explicitly forbidden as per this [Helpdesk Article](https://support.eveonline.com/hc/en-us/articles/208289335-Citadel-Deployment-and-Unanchoring).)

1. # Planets

  - Planetary Industry** is unavailable in Thera due to the nature of it being **shattered wormhole system**. This restriction includes the remaining five non-shattered planets, Thera X - XIV. When using the 'View in Planet Mode' menu option, you will get a "Planet unavailable. Thera [*n*] is not available to the general public." message.

1. # Other sites in Thera

Thera hosts 4 additional sites, seemingly remnants of past activities and endeavours in the system. Each of these sites is usually protected by 3 sleepers.

  1. # Epicenter

The **Epicenter** contains 4 "violent wormholes" as well as assorted Sleeper/Talocan debris. The site itself is approx 250 km across.
