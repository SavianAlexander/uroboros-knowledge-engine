# Beizan Picholen (6 of 6)

<noinclude> 

</noinclude>

The agent sends you to fetch a criminal from an luxury resort.

The resort sits in an ungated area protected by a few frigates. Take out the frigates if you can't tank them and proceed to destroy the luxury resort. The station has some considerable hitpoints, so be prepared to shoot a while. A minute or two after the station blows up, your target will arrive - blow it up and grab the Govarde Alourtine from the dropped container.

|}

|}

|}

You have now completed the Beizan Picholen mission chain. The mission text suggests that the chain was originally supposed to continue beyond this mission, but the next agent in the chain is not active in the game, rendering Avrue's Token useless for the moment.
