# Guristas Hub

Guristas Hub is a cosmic anomaly of moderate difficulty, found in the areas inhabited by the Guristas Pirates.

1. # Walkthrough
All enemies will aggro upon warping to the anomaly. There are 4 waves, with each new wave spawned by killing the battleship class NPCs of the previous spawn.
