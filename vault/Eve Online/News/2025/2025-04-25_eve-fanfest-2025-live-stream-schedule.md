## EVE 2025 Frontier Hackathon Live Stream


A fantastic way to kickoff Fanfest 2025. Following the massive success of the first-ever in-person Frontier Hackathon in 2023, the hackathon is back from the 29th of April to the 1st of May. We’re welcoming talented hackers both in-person and remotely to this three-day event to develop new tools using the EVE Frontier API that will support the community and expand the vision for EVE Frontier.

Come compete for incredible prizes, present your work to industry experts, and have a hand in building truly amazing resources for the EVE Frontier community. Learn more about the [**Frontier Hackathon 2025**](https://evefrontier.com/en/hackathon-2025).

11:30 [**Helping to Make the EVE Universe a Reality: The NASA Innovative Advanced Concepts**](https://evefanfest2025.sched.com/event/20wrp) (NIAC) Program 

Dr. Ronald Turner

Dr. Turner, aka Fa’ile Callandor in Eve, has been supporting the NASA Innovative Advanced Concepts (NIAC) program since 2011. This far reaching program is sometimes referred to as “NASA’s Dream Shop” funding studies of advanced concepts that border on science fiction, but could offer real promise to change the future of aerospace.

14:30  [It's Not Easy Growing a Supermassive Black Hole](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/dr-becky-smethurst-at-eve-fanfest-2025) 

Dr. Becky Smethurst  

How in the universe do supermassive black holes get so big? Dr. Becky Smethurst will challenge some of the common misconceptions about black holes and explain her cutting-edge science to understand how these mysterious objects actually grow. Along the way, she will revisit some iconic pop culture mentions of black holes and wormholes to reveal how scientifically accurate they really are.
