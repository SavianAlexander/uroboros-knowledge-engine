# Haunted Yard

- Haunted Yard** is an unrated **combat site** belonging to the **Rogue Drones** faction. It can be found via **exploration** by scanning anomalies in high security and low security systems.

This site can escalate to **Pulverize the Pioneers**

1. # Walkthrough

To blitz:
The leaking caskets are unlocked so head straight for them. The commander wave spawns near the warp in so boost back there.
