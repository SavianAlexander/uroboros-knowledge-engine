The CSM campaign season officially kicks off today, **10 October**, and will run until voting ends on **10 November**. This is your opportunity to get to know your potential representatives and understand their visions. 

Each candidate has a dedicated forum thread on the [EVE Online forums](https://forums.eveonline.com/c/council-of-stellar-management/csm-campaigns/71), outlining their campaign objectives. Many of our EVE Online partners and other content creators will also spotlight these campaigns, providing diverse perspectives on the candidates' messages. 

As in previous years, CCP will host brief interviews with the candidates during this campaign period, live on [CCPTV](https://twitch.tv/ccp) on **17-19 October**. We’ll also share these interviews on YouTube, so you can hear from your favorite candidates even if you can’t catch the live broadcast. 

The CSM is more than just a representation of player voices; it’s a powerful collaboration that shapes the future of New Eden. The strength of EVE has always resided at the intersection of our passionate players and talented developers. Together, we’ll continue to build a universe shaped by those who inhabit it—your voice, your ideas, your EVE.
