After a weekend of incredible matches, [the Alliance Tournament](//www.eveonline.com/events/alliances/tournament/t8) continues this weekend with its [second round of qualifiers](//www.eveonline.com/events/alliances/tournament/t8/schedule2.asp).

If you want to catch up with last week's action, all match replays are available on CCP's [YouTube Channel](//www.youtube.com/ccpgames) within a couple of days of broadcast. You can also join us on [Twitter](//twitter.com/evetournament) and the official EVE Online [Facebook](//www.facebook.com/eveonline) page to keep up-to-date on every win, loss and [Jita Cam](//www.youtube.com/watch?v=3UrIJpNXR4k).

To watch all of next week's matches in high definition, you can [tune in here](//www.eveonline.com/events/alliances/tournament/t8/viewerSD.asp) on June 12 and 13, and be sure to keep an eye out for [news items](//www.eveonline.com/news.asp) on [upcoming matches](//www.eveonline.com/events/alliances/tournament/t8/schedule1.asp). The **Finals** **Studio Show** on June 19 and 20 will include mini features on CCP as well as interviews with some of EVE's key developers.

Stay tuned to the [Alliance Tournament Discussion](//www.eveonline.com/ingameboard.asp?a=channel&channelID=422285) forum for updates on matches, match discussion and more!
