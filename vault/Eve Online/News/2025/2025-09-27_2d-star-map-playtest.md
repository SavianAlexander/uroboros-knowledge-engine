## Overview
Try the 2D map on Singularity until 1 Oct.

---

Experimental capsuleers,

You can test the highly anticipated 2D map on Singularity right now! Join the test before 1 October and help shape the future of navigation in New Eden by putting this eagerly awaited interface to the test.

For more details on the new map, check out the Dev chat podcast. 


## What to Do


Change the server from Tranquility to Singularity in the bottom-right corner of the launcher and log in as normal (more detailed instructions are available [here](https://support.eveonline.com/hc/en-us/articles/13171882385820-Test-Servers-Singularity-and-Others)). Join the in-game channel “masstesting” and follow the MOTD instructions. Then it’s time to kick the proverbial tires of the star map. See how it feels, whether it’s clear, intuitive, and useful. From region layout to visuals, zoom thresholds, and data scaling, your feedback will guide improvements.

After the test, share your thoughts through [this survey](https://eu.surveymonkey.com/r/PD6BVWM), on the [forum](https://forums.eveonline.com/t/mass-test-on-singularity-2d-map-project-discovery-27-sept-to-1-october/498130/1), or in the dedicated [Discord channel](https://discord.gg/eveonline). Pilots from all corners of space, and especially Explorers, are encouraged to join the test.

There is no set release date for Tranquility, but we’ll keep you posted on eveonline.com.


## Project Discovery Needs You Too


This test also brings Project Discovery updates. More diverse training samples and improved accuracy scoring. Check out how gold standard tasks affect your accuracy score and whether task variety increases at higher accuracy. [Your input](https://forums.eveonline.com/t/mass-test-on-singularity-2d-map-project-discovery-27-sept-to-1-october/498130/1) will help refine this vital program.

Join the discussion and see you on Singularity!
