# Gone Berserk (Level 2)

- Blitz**: Kill the left group of EoM ships and each subsequent spawn. Do not touch the center and right groups.

Warp to Acceleration Gate.
  1. # Single Pocket
Like the level 4 variant, the spawns appear in chains.

For each initial spawn group you kill, you'll get an additional reinforcement chain consisting of 4 waves.

  - Note:** The frigates may target your drones, but will deal very little damage to them, so using something like a **Vexor** with T1 Drones will work just fine.
