1. # Summary
An undeniable upgrade from the base **Naglfar**, the faction variant sports an additional bonus to stasis grappler range, as well as a 7.5% bonus to both shield booster and armor repairer amount compared to the Naglfar's 5% bonus to shield booster cycle time. The fleet version also has a 50% higher damage bonus per level of . The Fleet version also sports an extra high slot, more base tank, higher scan resolution and sensor strength, and more CPU and powergrid, and a slightly longer maximum targeting range.
