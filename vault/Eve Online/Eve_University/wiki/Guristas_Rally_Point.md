# Guristas Rally Point

- Guristas Rally Point** is a **combat anomaly** found in Low and Null security regions with **Guristas** presence. This site can escalate to the DED 6/10 complex **Guristas Troop Reinvigoration Camp**.
==Walkthrough== 
There are two variants of this site, one with several fuel silos and another in a residential area.
