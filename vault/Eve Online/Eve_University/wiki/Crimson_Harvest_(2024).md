# Crimson Harvest (2024)

- Crimson Harvest** is a recurring event lasting from October 3 to November 5, 2024, **downtime** to downtime. The seasonal **loot drop buff** started a day early

It involves the **Blood Raiders** pirate faction and the **Order of Tetrimon** mercenaries.

1. # Agency campaign
Each character can choose between two options for the Agency challenges for this event. You will receive points for completing tasks for the one you choose in the agency. Be aware that although you will only receive points for completing task for the side you chose, there is no penalty for shooting both sides and taking the loot from both their sites.

1. # Activities
  1. # Combat
- **Crimson Gauntlet** - Lvl 3 Blood raiders site.
- **Tetrimon Base** - Lvl 3 Order of Tetrimon site.

  1. # Hacking

There are 2 different types of level III data sites for each faction: Crimson Harvest Network Node/Hub and Tetrimon Network Node/Hub. Nodes spawn in high sec, while Hubs spawn in low sec. The site consists of one room with three cans approximately 20 to 40km from the warp in point surrounded by some appropriately themed objects. You will receive agency points only from the sites opposing your faction of choice but both sites can be completed for loot without penalty.

Non exhaustive list of possible loot:
- Overseer Sabik Vial (Can be sold to NPCs (CONCORD) for 1M ISK a piece)

- Crimson Scythes, Blood Dagger and Scarlet Embrace Fireworks

- Basic 'Radiance' Cerebral Accelerator

- Abyssal and Jump Filaments

- Normal data site loot such as datacores, decyptors and components.

1. # Special Mechanics
During the Crimson Harvest all player ship kills will have an increased loot drop rate, with an average of 90% compared the normal average of 50%.
