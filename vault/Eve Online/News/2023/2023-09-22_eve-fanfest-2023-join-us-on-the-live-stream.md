### Calling all Capsuleers,


Every pilot globally is invited to join us for Fanfest 2023, with the CCPTV live stream taking you to the heart of the ultimate EVE Online gathering, while keeping you up-to-date with all the major announcements and news from the Laugardagshöll Arena.

The stream – found over on [**Twitch**](https://www.twitch.tv/ccp) – will also bring live **interviews with developers, players**, and special guests, as well as a bounty of entertainment and hijinks direct from Fanfest. What’s more, the stream will host prize raffles, and thanks to the new [**Twitch Drops support**](https://www.eveonline.com/twitch), pilots can claim in-game rewards simply by watching.

In other words, CCPTV will transport the spirit of Fanfest to the entire global pilot community, letting you be part of an event like no other! The CCPTV team is also hard at work to make sure **translated streams are available for Fanfest 2023’s** biggest and best content – such as the EVE keynote, which will be **broadcast on Twitch in French, German, Russian, Spanish, Korean, Japanese, and Mandarin**.

Wherever you are, be sure to join us live on CCPTV, where the Fanfest schedule runs for two days, **starting 22 September at 9:45 AM UTC**.
