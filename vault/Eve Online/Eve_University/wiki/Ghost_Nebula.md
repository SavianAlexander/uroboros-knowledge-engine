# Ghost Nebula

- Ghost Nebula** is a null/high/low sec gas site found through **exploration**.

Reminder: **Gas cloud harvesting** in low sec can be dangerous, although the built-in +2 warp core strength of the **Venture** makes it reasonably safe.
