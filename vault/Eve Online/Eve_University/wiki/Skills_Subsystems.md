# Skills:Subsystems

# Overview
Subsystems skills have to do with the fitting and use of Tech III ships such as **Strategic Cruisers**.

Intelligence and Memory improve your abilities to learning to use subsystems.

The following are the skills in the Subsystems section (in alphabetical order):
