# Minor Angel Annex

- Minor Angel Annex** is an unrated Complex occupied by the **Angel Cartel** pirate faction. It can be found by probing in Lowsec. Its signal strength is 10%, making it moderately easy to scan down.

The site is accessed through an acceleration gate. While a well fit battlecruiser can solo the first part, it would be difficult if not impossible to do so for the second part. This complex is unrated and is of a difficulty similar to a 5 or 6/10 DED Rated Complexes

1. # Walkthrough

  1. # First room

More defenders appear as the communications officers are agressed. The militant commander appears in last wave. The destruction of militant commander unlocks the acceleration gate.

1. # Loot

- Bounty: Approx 7 M ISK in the first room, 10 M ISK in the second room
- Loot and Salvage: Approx 6 - 8 M ISK in the first room, 6 - 8 M ISK in the second rooom
- The Domination ship has a chance to drop faction or Gist mods.

1. # Escalation

This site may escalate to **Angel Owned Station**.
