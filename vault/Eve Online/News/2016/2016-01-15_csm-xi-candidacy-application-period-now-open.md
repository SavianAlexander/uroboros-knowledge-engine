We are happy to announce that the election period for CSM 11 has begun, and the candidacy application period is now open!

Players who wish to submit their candidacy for CSM 11 can now do so through the [candidate application form](//community.eveonline.com/community/csm/apply-for-csm/) which will be active until February 9th, 2016.

Those who intend to run for CSM 11 should remember to read the instructions on the candidate application form page carefully to ensure that the correct information is submitted.

For more information on the CSM, you can visit the [CSM Portal](//community.eveonline.com/community/csm/), and for current events, check the [CSM News page](//csm.eveonline.com/).

For further details on the election schedule and timings for the CSM 11 elections, please see [this dev blog](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/csm11-elections/).
