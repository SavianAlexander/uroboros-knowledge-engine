# Winter Nexus (2025)

- Winter Nexus** is an event held from 9 December 2025 to 12 January 2026 (**downtime** to downtime) and includes **Volatile Ice Storms** which feature Wightstorm Sites, **Login Campaign**, and the **Winter Nexus Agency Challenge**,.

1. # Login campaign
Capsuleers must log in daily for 34 days from 9 December 2025 onwards to collect all rewards. A total of 130k **Skill Points** are available to  accounts, while  accounts have access to an additional 525k skill points and 26,500 **EverMark**s. The big reward is on day 26, so even if you do not manage to log in for all 30 days, as long as you manage those 26 days you will only miss out on some Boosters and Ice Storm Filaments.

1. # Volatile Ice Storm
Volatile Ice Storms have begun to appear across highsec, lowsec, and nullsec space. These ice storms spread out across the stargate network two jumps from a central system, and occasionally move from system to system just like **metaliminal storm**s. These storms provide bonuses to ship thermal resistances and module overheating for all capsuleers flying within them.

Sansha's Nation and Outer Ring Excavations have both mobilized to take advantage of the novel forms of stellar ice found within these storms.

  1. # Ice Storm Filaments
Ice Storm Filaments transport capsuleers to a System affected by the Volatile Ice Storm.

  1. # Mining sites
Volatile Ice Field anomalies can also be discovered within Volatile Ice Storms. These fields of stellar ice have been captured by ORE and Mordu's Legion forces who have driven away the Sansha harvesting fleets. These ice fields can be accessed through a special ORE-commandeered acceleration gate that only allows medium and small mining and hauling ships to enter (**Venture** and variants, **Pioneer** and variants, **Expedition Frigates**, **Mining Barges**, **Exhumers**, **Haulers**, **Blockade Runners**, **Deep Space Transports**, **Noctis**, **Outrider** and the **Porpoise**, but **not** the Upwell haulers **Squall**, **Deluge**, or **Torrent**).

The volatile ice found within these fields cannot be reprocessed by standard facilities, however Outer Ring Excavations has set up mobile ice collection operations within each of these ice fields, and is willing to trade with capsuleers who contribute to their mining operation. Capsuleers can trade seven units of volatile ice with the ORE representatives in the ice fields to receive a package of valuable salvage and items that ORE forces collected from the wrecks of Sansha ships.

Rumors suggest that other valuable Sansha salvage has frozen directly into some of the ice chunks and can be occasionally picked up along with the ice by normal mining activities.

  1. ## Fading Volatile Ice Field
- Appear in high-sec
- Contains Faded Volatile Ice which can be exchanged for:
  - ORE Green Salvage packages (at a ratio of 7 to 1). The packages, like **containers** have a capacity that is greater then their volume. They contain 2 to 7 items:
    - Common (i.e. in almost every package)
      - Nation Stormhive Shard
      - Salvaged materials
    - Less common
      - Ice Storm Filaments
      - **SKINR** materials

      - Festival Charges (Snowballs and fireworks)

  - ORE Faded Cache (7 ice and a Cryograph Diffractor)
    - (Loot to be reported)
- Asteroids can respawn
- **Belt rat**s have been spotted

  1. ## Fresh Volatile Ice Field
- Appear in low-sec
- Contains Fresh Volatile Ice which can be exchanged for ORE White Salvage packages. The packages, like **containers** have a capacity that is greater then their volume.
  - Common (i.e. in every package)
    - Overseer Personal Effects (9th-16th tier)
    - Salvaged materials

- **Belt rat**s will spawn

  1. ## Friable Volatile Ice Field
- Appear in null-sec

  1. # Yoiul LADs
Needs to be located using combat probes or skillful D-Scan usage —make sure InterBus Yoiul LADs are enabled in your overview. They will usually hang around moons, planets and stations, and frequently warp around. Most LADs only have a chance to drop decent loot and can sometimes be empty, except in the cases of LADs with the **Express** prefix, which guarantee a loot drop.

Destroying them yields Verity Cryo Tech, an item used to unlock enhanced versions of event content and increase rewards.

  1. ## Using Verity Cryo Tech
- Combat Sites
  - Deposit Verity Cryo Tech into a Sansha's Transit Modulator to reroute the acceleration gate to a hidden high-difficulty pocket in space with greater rewards.
  - Normally, **Marauder**s cannot enter highsec sites, but they can enter if the gate is rerouted.
  - Cryo Tech:
    - Cryostorm Clarifier for Wightstorm Forward Base
    - Cryostorm Reconciler for Wightstorm Muster Point
    - Cryostorm Mapper for Wightstorm Transit Site

- Data Sites
  - Use Verity Cryo Tech in a Sansha's Stormfield Projector to unlock an additional data relay, providing extra loot opportunities.
  - Cryo Tech:
    - Cryostatic Estimator for Wightstorm Comms Relay
    - Cryostatic Nullifier for Wightstorm Strategic Node
    - Cryostatic Suppresssor for Wightstorm Crypto Facility

- Mining Sites
  - Trade Verity Cryo Tech alongside volatile ice at the on-site item traders to receive an upgraded salvage package.
  - Cryo Tech:
    - Crygraph Diffractor for Fading Volatile Ice
    - Crygraph Resolver for Fresh Volatile Ice
    - Crygraph Exactor for Friable Volatile Ice

  1. # Special game mechanics
All of the storm systems will feature a special set of environmental effects that reduce heat damage suffered by **overheating** modules and increases Thermal resistance.
