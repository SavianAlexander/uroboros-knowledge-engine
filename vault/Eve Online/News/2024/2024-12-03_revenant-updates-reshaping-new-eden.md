### Powerful capsuleers,


The Revenant expansion continues to evolve with new enhancements and updates, many of which were shaped by your feedback, designed to empower pilots and corporations alike. Automate your ship replacement program with the Ship Insurance Corporation Project type, enjoy the freedom to complete AIR Daily Goals with EverMarks, engage in Mercenary Tactical Operations, plus a multitude of additional changes arriving now and in the near future.


## FEARLESS FLIGHT


Building on and adding to the Ship Loss Corporation Project type introduced in the Equinox expansion, the new **Ship Insurance **project type brings automated ship replacement programs for corporations. Based on feedback from corp leaders, this offers more ways to compensate your members with a streamlined system that guarantees seamless payouts based on loss value. Now your fleets can charge fearlessly into battle, knowing their investments are covered, and they are guaranteed a chance to fight another day, whatever the peril.


## FREEDOM TO PROGRESS


For pilots seeking greater freedom, EverMarks can now be used to complete AIR Daily Goals. Focus on the activities you enjoy the most, while still progressing and achieving your ambitions. Use your EverMarks strategically and turn your stockpile into meaningful opportunities. With this new update and the increased flexibility it brings the number of daily goals has been streamlined to four, ensuring that capsuleers have meaningful choices without feeling overwhelmed, striking a balance between flexibility and focus. AIR remains dedicated to monitoring and fine-tuning the system to ensure it empowers you with the perfect blend of control and agency, letting you shape your path in New Eden. 


## BASK IN YOUR SHIPS’ GLORY


Spaceships are at the heart of the EVE experience, and boarding a new ship for the first time is about to become even more of a celebratory moment – one your hard-earned vessels deserve! Starting 5 December, cinematic ship introductions will bring your ships to life, showcasing their power and beauty in a dynamic and immersive way, celebrating and highlighting the ship’s unique character. These scenes can be replayed, skipped, or disabled based on your preference.


## NEW EDEN EVOLVES


Your feedback has driven both immediate improvements and laid the groundwork for future evolution across New Eden. Sovereignty anomaly warp-in points for NPCs have already been adjusted, enabling quicker and more responsive strategic engagements. In addition, a number of wormhole activities have been enhanced – more on those in the Patch Notes.

Since the launch of Revenant, nullsec has been a hive of activity, with over 100 mercenary dens destroyed daily. Now, Mercenary Tactical Operations (MTO), have begun to spawn in systems with mercenary dens, creating new opportunities for capsuleers to drive development and reduce anarchy by completing objectives, boosting (or hindering) the production of infomorph decryption keys. As covered in last week’s update capsuleers can secure valuable rewards while stabilizing planetary workforce and mitigating disruptions.

Also detailed in last week’s news item, various improvements and “Little Things” inspired by your feedback have been introduced across New Eden. Ship balance changes have refined and boosted several vessels, miners have gained the ability to compress resources directly from the right-click menu, while exhumers and barges enjoy better locked target capabilities and faster excavation drones.

Multiple updates to Observatory Flashpoint sites in Pochven have been made recently, including enhanced drone aggression and reliable red loot amounts, and these ongoing changes remain priorities, as well as continued ship balancing efforts. Furthermore, adjustments have been made to Factional Warfare and Insurgency systems, driven by your feedback, aiming to keep these conflicts dynamic and engaging.
