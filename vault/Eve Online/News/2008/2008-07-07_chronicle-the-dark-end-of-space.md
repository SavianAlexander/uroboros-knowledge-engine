After the great wars started, the capsuleers were pulled in and brought to the fore, [here is how it came to be](//www.eve-online.com/background/potw/07-07-08.asp). This is the third in a series of five weekly [chronicles](//www.eve-online.com/background/potw/archive.asp) that give you an inside look at the highest political level of New Eden. We have published stories on the leaders of the Minmatar Republic and Gallente Federation, and now show you how the world went from empire skirmishes in hi-sec space to capsuleer warfare in low-sec areas.
