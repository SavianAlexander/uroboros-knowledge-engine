# Fluttering Nebula

- Fluttering Nebula** is a gas exploration site containing gas used in the production of **Medical boosters**.

This is a null sec gas site found through Exploration.

Defenders:

None

Minable Gas Clouds:

5 Amber Cytoserocin (100 units each gas clouds)

Amber Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Pure Standard Blue Pill Booster.

This site is found only in the[Vale of the Silent](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Vale_of_the_Silent) region of space.
