# Wenda Lamort

- Wenda Lamort** is an COSMOS agent in **Gallente COSMOS**. She is located at Federal Data Center in Abenync. She is a level 1 agent and requires no standings.

This agent gives only one mission that gives no notable rewards and is not connected to any other mission chain.
