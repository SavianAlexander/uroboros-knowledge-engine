**The Paragon of Duty pack contains:**


- 1,000 PLEX
- 18 True Blue Loyalty Nanocoatings
- 18 State Concrete Nanocoatings
- 18 Silver honor Metallics
- 18 Mountain Wind Spirit Patterns
- 5,000 Kerr Sequencers
- 4,000 Fermionic Sequencers
- 4,000 Alignment Sequencers


**Please note that this pack can only be purchased once per account in the EVE Store.** 

However, the same pack (minus the PLEX) is also available in the **New Eden Store**, so you could use the PLEX from this pack to purchase more of the design elements over there.

**The offer is available until 6 September.**

To further add to the excitement, watch out for [Twitch drops](https://www.eveonline.com/twitch) containing the Mountain Wind Spirit pattern and Dark Duty Gloss nanocoating.
