# Anomic Base (Caldari)

1. # Warp-in

|}

  1. # 1st Room

Dragonflies deal mostly Thermal and Explosive damage with some Kinetic. The Mantis waves' torpedoes have extremely high EM damage alpha but they have the explosion radius and velocity of battleships. As long as you stay constantly moving their damage should be mitigated a significant amount. It may be a good idea to avoid automatic orbit or keep at range commands and prioritize manual piloting to kill them so you won't slow down after destroying one if you're using an automatic orbit maneuver (and therefore become vulnerable to being volleyed through your tank due to your reduced speed).
