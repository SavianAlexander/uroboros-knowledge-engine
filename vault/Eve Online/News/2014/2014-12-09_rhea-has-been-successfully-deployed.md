We are happy to announce that the [Rhea release](//community.eveonline.com/releases/rhea) has been successfuly deployed!

The Rhea release brings a long list of changes to the world of EVE Online, including [User Interface updates](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/a-new-look-for-eves-ui-feedback-needed/), [new exploration content](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/new-exploration-sites-new-modules/), [101 new wormhole systems](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/thera-and-the-shattered-wormholes/), [manual flight control](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/quick-give-me-manual-control/), [changes to cloning mechanics](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/a-new-era-of-clones/) and a [new opt in beta starmap](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/a-map-to-treasure/) that we're looking to gather feedback on!

For more information on the Rhea release, and to see a full list of features, take a look at the Rhea release page [here](//community.eveonline.com/releases/rhea), and read more about what's coming in CCP Seagull's ["Coming to EVE in the Rhea release"](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/coming-to-eve-online-in-the-rhea-release-on-december-9th/) dev blog. For a full and detailed breakdown of changes please see the [patch notes for Rhea](//community.eveonline.com/news/patch-notes/patch-notes-for-rhea).

For general feedback on this release you can post and discuss in this [forum thread](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=5285425). To discuss issues, please see [this thread](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=5285424). For those of you playing with our Mac client, please report your Mac specific issues in [this thread](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=5285436).
