# Dalitat Dakpor

- Dalitat Dakpor** is a level 4 **COSMOS** agent located in CX8-6K.

1. # Death's Door (1 of 2)

 

Fly to the ship wreck and retrieve Dalitat's clone. Return him to Dalitat Dakpor in Blood Reach. Do NOT stray too close to the Pagera Manton or you will be punished!

No rat are initially spawned, wave 1 spawns in but can be ignored. If you do decide to attack the Pagera Manton, it spawns in a massive wave.

Some of the rats will use energy neutralizers.

Standing lossess for killing Blood Raiders.

  1. # 2nd pocket
Rats, sentries, and station are 40km off from warpin. Completing the mission requires bashing the Amarr Battlestation, which has 800k EHP! Reinforcement waves spawn when the battlestation is first hit, at 2/3 shields, 1/3 shields, and 1/2 structure.

Both the battlestation and High Priest Heftah Nekmar can drop Imperial Navy modules. Heftah Nekmar can also drop a 5% hardwiring implant.
