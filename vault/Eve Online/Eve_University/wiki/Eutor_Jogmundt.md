# Eutor Jogmundt

- Eutor Jogmundt** is a level 2 agent in **Minmatar COSMOS** located in Traun at Thin Red Line. Instead of offering the normal kind of COSMOS mission she will offer a simple trade mission that can be repeated with no limit but offers only a small amount of isk and corp standing as a reward. If you can't see them, enable overview elements "Beacon" and "Agents in Space".
