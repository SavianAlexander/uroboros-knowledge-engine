# Varma Fujimo

- Varma Fujimo** is a level 4 agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located in Otitoh - Devil's Dig Site.

1. # Station In Crisis - Caldari Troublemakers (1 of 3)

Provide 60x Bandit Spur (6 m3) to agent. Note that although the mentioned system is low-sec, the Bandit Spurs can also be obtained from high-sec COSMOS sites such as **Contested Kazka Headquarters**.

1. # Station In Crisis - Militia Equipment (2 of 3)

Transport 2,000x Pistols (4,000 m3) from Vouskiaho or Vasala NPC station to Ihakana VI - State and Region Bank Depository.

1. # Station In Crisis - Hooligan Gang Leaders (3 of 3)

Provide 25x Gunslinger Spur (2.5 m3) to agent. Note that although the mentioned system is low-sec, the Gunslinger Spurs can also be obtained from high-sec COSMOS sites such as **Contested Caldari Mining Facilities**.
