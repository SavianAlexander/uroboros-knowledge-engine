## Overview
The Industry features announced in a series of development blogs and during the EVE Online Keynote at Fanfest 2014, scheduled for the Kronos release on June 3rd, have been moved to the Crius release. To find out more about the decision to move these features, read the new devblog by CCP RubberBand

---

The Industry features announced in a [series of development blogs](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/reprocess-all-the-things/) and during the [EVE Online Keynote](//www.youtube.com/watch?v=k07Uu7qUEa0) at Fanfest 2014, scheduled for the Kronos release on June 3rd, have been moved to the Crius release. To find out more about the decision to move these features, read the new devblog by CCP RubberBand [here](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/delivering-the-industry-new-eden-deserves/).

To accommodate for these changes, we have moved the Crius release forward by a month, originally scheduled for August 19th, to July 22nd. To learn more about the new release model, also announced at Fanfest, read the latest [dev blog](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/from-2-expansions-to-10-releases-eve-onlines-new-release-model-explained/) from EVE Online’s Senior Producer, CCP Seagull.

The Kronos release, coming out on June 3rd, will include all other features announced in development blogs and during the [EVE Online keynote](//www.youtube.com/watch?v=k07Uu7qUEa0) at Fanfest, such as the 3 new Mordu's Legion ships, the [new advanced mining frigate](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/fortune-favors-the-bold/), changes to low sec sites and faction warfare, new warp-in effects, rebalanced and redesigned ships, [drone rebalance](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/giving-drones-an-assist/), faction themed stations and sound customization.
