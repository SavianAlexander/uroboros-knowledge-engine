}}  The **Sansha Raiding Party** is a Null-Security **Mercenary Tactical Operation** site that appears when (TBA)

When a capsuleer initiates warp to the site, it will appear in the Overview of every capsuleer in the same System.

If left undamaged, Sansha ships will slowly repair their shield overtime. On waves 1-3, they often target drones. They deal EM & Thermal damage with lasers, and Kinetic & Explosive damage with their missiles. This site has four waves in total.

Always make sure to take care of the the neuting ships first. They can and WILL neut your capacitor until there is nothing left; make sure you choose your targets wisely.
