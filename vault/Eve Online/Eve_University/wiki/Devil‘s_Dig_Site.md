# Devil‘s Dig Site

The Devil‘s Dig Site is a static site in **Caldari COSMOS** in Otitoh. At the warp in you find several Caldari COSMOS agents and about 90 km off an acceleration gate. T1 battleship can enter the site.

1. # Pocket 1 - Infested Excavation Site
The **Battered Artifact Crates** and **Excavation Notepads** are easy difficulty (70 HP green core), and the **Sealed Excavation Caches** and **Relic Digital Installers** are medium difficulty (70 HP yellow core).

You need a relic analyzer to hack those cans.  One of them drops an *Ancient Ciphering Totem* that is required to progress into the next room.  This is a rare find, though.

1. # Ancient Temple
The site consist of two big group of cans which are each guarded by rats. You land between those groups. If the site has been activated an additional group of rats is at the warpin. Their DPS is relatively high and some of the frigates point and web. So be careful. If this group is present best try to warp off as quick as possible and reevaluate.
