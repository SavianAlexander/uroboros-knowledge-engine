1. # Summary
Inner Zone Shipping Imicus is a special edition of the **Imicus** given out during the 2012 Christmas season similar to the **Sarum Magnate**, **Sukuuvestaa Heron**, and the **Vherokior Probe**. It appears to be functionally equivalent to the standard Imicus but with a different skin and additional flavor text.

All models were converted to a regular Imicus and a corresponding Inner Zone Shipping **SKIN** with the Mosaic release in April 2015. Despite this, the model was given a unique quote with the release of the Revenant expansion in November 2024, over nine years after all copies of the ship were converted out of existence.
