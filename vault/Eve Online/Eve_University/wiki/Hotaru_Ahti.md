# Hotaru Ahti

- Hotaru Ahti** is a level 4 agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located in Otomainen - Red Rock Outpost.

1. # Hotaru's Dilemma - Trespasser (1 of 4)

Provide 1 x Gatti's DNA (0.1 m3) to agent.

1. # Hotaru's Dilemma - Empty The Load (2 of 4)

Transport 10,000 x Veldspar (1000.0 m3) from the agent to Ihakana VIII - Moon 6.

1. # Hotaru's Dilemma - Break The Blockade (3 of 4)

Provide 1 x Pata Wakiro's DNA (0.1 m3) to agent.

1. # Hotaru's Dilemma - Moa Parts (4 of 4)

Transport 21 x Moa Parts (630.0 m3) from Otsela VI - Moon 13 - Ishukone Corporation Factory to agent.
