# Pattok Nortul

- Pattok Nortul** is an level 1 **Thukker Tribe** in **Gallente COSMOS**. he is located at beacon "Nickel & Dime Store" in Augnais. He requires no standings from mission runners. You'll need to have "Beacons" and "Agents in Space" visible in your overview.

He offers hauling and combat missions. you can skip the combat part if you buy the reqiuired item from other players. His last mission rewards an "Trust Partners Business Card" that is required in **Iliere Angetyn's** mission in Gallente COSMOS.

1. # Concerned Citizens - Lost Stash (2 of 3)

1. # Concerned Citizens - Run, Boy, Run! (3 of 3)
