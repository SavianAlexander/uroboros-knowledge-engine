# Drone Herd

- Drone Herd** is an unrated **combat site** belonging to the **Rogue Drones** faction. It can be found via **exploration** as a **cosmic anomaly** in low-security and null-security systems across New Eden. As an ungated complex, the limitations as to which ships can complete this site are tied to the security rating of the system where the anomaly spawns (T2 **Battleship**s for high-security systems, and up to **Titans** in low-security and null-security systems). This combat site has a chance to spawn a Sentient commander rogue drone, which drops rogue components and may drop faction drone modules and items. There are two minor variations with slightly different wave compositions.
