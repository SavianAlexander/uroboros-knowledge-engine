# Abyssal Community Fits

1. # Introduction

  1. # Purpose of this article
On this page you can find Abyssal Community Fits - created by EVE University pilots interested in **Abyssal Deadspace**. The aim was to create fits that are capable of running T0 Abyssals without need for advanced piloting, and with reasonable safety. Price of fits was also a consideration, as Abyss is difficult, especially when starting Abyss adventures.

  1. # Contents
Each base fit includes:
- Notes: Short notes from person who was testing the fit. Each fit was tested extensively, especially against two spawns that tend to end T0 runs - Skybreaker Disparu Troop and Devoted Hunter.
- Link to fit in Alliance Auth. All fits in AA contain recommended ammo, **Nanite Repair Paste** and correct **Abyssal filaments**
- Skills - base skill plan which was used for testing the fits. All skill plans are below 1M SP, so it's possible to use 1M SP from Referral Link. With lower skills your safety margin will be lower, but it still might be feasible to have successful runs.
- Youtube link - with video(s) showing runs with specific fit. Do not treat them as definitive guide, but use them to see how others fly specific ship
- Abyss Tracker link - base fits are uploaded to Abyss Tracker, and we did several recorded runs to get feeling about profitability. You can record your own runs using these fit IDs, to help expand the data available. Since upgraded fits might differ from each other, they are not uploaded to Tracker.

  1. # Next steps
For most of the fits there are also upgraded versions provided. They were all designed with "Max Alpha" skillset in mind, so there are no Omega-only modules used, or Omega-only skills required to fit them. Upgraded fits might require better fitting skills - you should check the upgrade before buying new modules, if it matches your skills. All upgrades can be bought separately, module by module, as your skills increase and allow you to use them. They are also not 'final' versions - you might find that with your skills some upgrades could be changed or replaced according to your needs, which is perfectly valid approach.

  1. # Joining the EVE University Abyssal community

If you wish to join the Uni Abyssal community please join us in Discord at PvE Study Area - **#abyssals**.

You can also reach out directly to one of **Community Coordinators** specializing in Abyss if you would like to help with expanding this page.
