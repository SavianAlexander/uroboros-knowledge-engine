Dear Fanfest adventurers,

The blueprint copy for the first of the [Three Fanfest Ships](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/three-ships-of-fanfest-2015/), the Quafe Tristan, has been distributed. If you already have a ticket to Fanfest, you can redeem it with the character registered for the ticket. If you buy a ticket from here on, the ship will become immediately available to you.

The Quafe Vexor and the Quafe Dominix will be unlocked and distributed on the  February 11 and February 27 respectively.

See you at Fanfest in March!
