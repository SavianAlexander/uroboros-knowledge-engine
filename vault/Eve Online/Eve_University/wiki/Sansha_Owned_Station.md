# Sansha Owned Station

- Sansha Owned Station** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **Minor Sansha Annex**.
1. # Walkthrough

  1. # First Location
Ungated pocket. None of the structures drop loot.

  1. # Fourth Location

The Sansha Research Outpost has a smartbomb that triggers when it is attacked. It does not have that many hitpoints (4000 in shield, 4000 in armor and 400 in structure), so taking it out is actually fairly easy.

1. # Loot
Typical loot from the True Sansha commanders include criminal dog tags, faction ammunition and meta modules. They also have a chance to drop True Sansha faction modules. The Sansha Research Outpost can drop Centum A-Type cruiser modules, True Sansha modules and possibly a rare 1-run **Phantasm** BPC.
