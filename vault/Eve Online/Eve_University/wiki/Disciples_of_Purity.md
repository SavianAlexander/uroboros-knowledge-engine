# Disciples of Purity

Disciples of Purity is an **Amarr** criminal faction that plays the part of the hostiles in some **Homefront operation**s.

Their ships are weak to EM and Kinetic, they deal Thermal (62.5%) and Explosive (37.5%) damage, and employ **ECM** electronic warfare.

They are the hostiles in these Homefront operations:
- **Emergency Aid: Holy Mission**
- **Metaliminal Meteoroid: Amarr Mining**

1. # Ship Prefixes
The prefix of the ship names as they appear in the Overview correlate to their role. The rest of the ship name is typically the normal hull name of the ship. For example, the "Righteous Omen" is a damage dealing **Omen** cruiser. Prefixes often appear on ships of different sizes, for example the ships that target capsuleer drones ("drone killer" in the table below) come in both frigate and destroyer sizes.

Not all prefixes are present in every type of site, refer to the site-specific pages above for details.
