# Blood Raider Fortress

- Blood Raider Fortress** is an **Unrated Complex** found in null security systems with **Blood Raider** presence. Up to T2 **Battleships** can enter the site through the initial acceleration gate.
1. # Walkthrough
  1. # First Pocket
All three groups of NPCs are initially passive until attacked.

|}
The acceleration gate to the next pocket is 25 km from warp-in. All defenders must be destroyed to use it.

|}
  1. # Second Pocket
Large number of ships. Close proximity of spawns means that incoming damage can be quite high.
 
Destroy Dark Blood commander battleship to trigger escalation. Alternatively destroy the Blood Raider Battlestation to trigger escalation. Note that it has around 200k EHP and may take a long time to destroy.
