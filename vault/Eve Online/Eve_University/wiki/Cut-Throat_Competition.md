# Cut-Throat Competition

- Tips:**
- Use two Sensor Boosters with ECCM scripts to reduce chances of being jammed. (Only if needed, sometimes jammers never spawn.)
- Kill the Recon Ships (Jammers) before triggering the third wave.

  - Blitz:**
- Destroy all ships in group 2 (Blackbirds only, not the Moa), and destroy the repair outpost.
- If second wave is triggered, continue to focus on Blackbirds until mission completes.

Enemy cruisers in the first pocket stay at 35km range.
