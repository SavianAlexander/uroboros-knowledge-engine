# Nullsec Border Rare Asteroids

- Nullsec Border Rare Asteroids** is a **anomaly** that spawns in nullsec systems on the border with lowsec (or, in rare cases, directly with highsec).

It contains special "border ores" Ducinium, Eifyrium, Mordunium and Ytirium, which have high **reprocessing** value per. Moreover, they are a source of **minerals** otherwise not available in nullsec. In addition they contain Crokite, which is available otherwise.
