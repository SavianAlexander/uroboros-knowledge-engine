# EVE University Killboard Ribbons

1. # Adding them to your signature

# In the forums, go to "User Control Panel", "Profile", "Edit Signature"
# Right-click the image of the ribbon you want to add from the wiki, and select "Copy Image URL"
# In the signature edit box, paste the url in the middle of an image tag
# Silvonus recommends a space in-between each image
# Hit the "Enter" key when you want to start a new row of ribbons
# You can add the center tags around the whole thing so it is in the center rather than the left
# You can also add wiki medals listed on the **EVE University Ribbons** page. Since the KB ribbons and Wiki ribbons are styled differently, you might want them on separate rows in your signature. 

- A note from Silvonus: Please do not go crazy, limit the number of ribbons in your signature. I find 12 to be a good number, pick your favorites. There are listed here in order of rank. You are not required to follow this order, but it is highly recommended.*
