# Intaki Chase

<noinclude> </noinclude>

Ungated semi-deadspace pocket three jumps away in Pemene. Destroy everything in sight.

Complete the Mission remotely.

  - Blitz:** kill tower, then only the Syndicate Veteran Battleships. Be prepared to tank well and kill the neuting Cruisers if needed.

Tips:
- Intaki Settlement Control Tower has about 45000 hitpoints
- You will lose 3% The Syndicate Faction Standing for destroying the Intaki Settlement Control Tower
- All enemies spawn at the location of the Control Tower
- Syndicate Veteran Battleship deals 300 DPS 20 km away and approach at 1000 m/s
- Syndicate Veteran Battleships attack and destroy Drones very quickly
- Keep an eye on your Drones; at very high speeds, they will give up attacking the target
