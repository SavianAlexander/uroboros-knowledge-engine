# Guristas Guerilla Grounds

- Guristas Guerilla Grounds** is a DED 3/10 complex that can be found in high and low security caldari systems.

1. # Walkthorugh

  1. # First Room

3 groups of 5 frigates separated at long distances from each other.

|}

  1. # Second Room
----

3 groups of 6 frigates separated at long distances from each other.

|}

  1. # Third Room
----

4 groups of 7 frigates separated at long distances from each other. All NPCs auto-aggro.

|}

  1. # Fourth Room
----

5 groups of 7 frigates separated at long distances from each other. All NPCs auto-aggro.

|}

  1. # Fifth Room
----

Kill Dread Gurista, either loot it or bookmark the wreck and wait other NPCs to despawn. T2 salvage loot from Dread Gurista.

|}
