# Skills:Rigging

# Overview
This group has all the skills related to **rigs**.

Rigs *can* be used without training the relevant skills for them. The rigging skills do reduce rigs' associated drawbacks. The rigging skills function almost like **fitting skills**, i.e. weapon rigs increase your weapons' fitting requirements while the associated rigging skills reduce that drawback.

| Skill (Multiplier) | Purpose | Alpha Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Armor Rigging|mult=yes}} | Reduces drawbacks of armor rigs. | Armor Rigging}} |
| Astronautics Rigging|mult=yes}} | Reduces drawbacks of astronautics (navigation) rigs. | Astronautics Rigging}} |
| Drones Rigging|mult=yes}} | Reduces drawbacks of drone rigs. | Drones Rigging}} |
| Electronic Superiority Rigging|mult=yes}} | Reduces drawbacks of EWAR rigs. | Electronic Superiority Rigging}} |
| Energy Weapon Rigging|mult=yes}} | Reduces drawbacks of energy weapon rigs. | Energy Weapon Rigging}} |
| Hybrid Weapon Rigging|mult=yes}} | Reduces drawbacks of hybrid weapon rigs. | Hybrid Weapon Rigging}} |

| Launcher Rigging|mult=yes}} | Reduces drawbacks of launcher rigs. | Launcher Rigging}} |
