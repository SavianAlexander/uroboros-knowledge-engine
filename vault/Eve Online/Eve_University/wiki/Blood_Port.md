# Blood Port

The Blood Port is a combat anomaly found in Blood Raiders infested regions.

1. # Walkthough
  1. # Slave Mine Variant
The site consists of single ungated pocket. Reinforcement spawns are triggered by the last hostile ship destroyed. With the initial defending fleet and 3 triggered spawns, a total of around 25 ships.
