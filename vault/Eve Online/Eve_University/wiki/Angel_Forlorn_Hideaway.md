# Angel Forlorn Hideaway

The **Angel Forlorn Hideaway** is a **combat anomaly** that can be found in **Angel** ingested territory of High Sec space. The site itself is a small, open area with no Acceleration Gate.

Upon warping to this site, you receive the following message:

1. # Warp-in

|}

|}

|}

|}

1. # Rewards
- Bounty: about 900,000 ISK
- Loot/salvage: uncertain

1. # Escalation
This site does not appear to escalate.
