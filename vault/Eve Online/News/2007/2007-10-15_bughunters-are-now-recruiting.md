Over the past few months ISD have gone through changes, the BugHunter team is no exception and our aim is to grow it well beyond its previous state. As some of you may have noticed, we have created a subdivision in BugHunters called the Localization Team, which works on the Localized clients due out later this year. At the moment the Localization Team has reached the goal we set out, but we still want to grow the general BugHunter team.

This is why we need you. You are the player base, you’ve played for a long time, and you know the issues, you can tell bug from whine from inaccurate information. If you are 18 years or older, have an good understanding of the English language, have been playing EVE for a minimum of 4 months and have a good understanding of EVE mechanics, if you are experienced or willing to learn how to use IRC, have good communication skills and feel you could keep your status as a volunteer secret from your friends, then [sign up](//myeve.eve-online.com/isd.asp) for the volunteer BugHunter team!
