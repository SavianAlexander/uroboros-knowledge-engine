# 13 Days of EVE (2018)

13 Days of EVE is a login reward event from December 11th to December 28th 2018. Although this is more than 13 days players only have to login on 13 different days to get all the rewards.

The rewards are:

| Day | For Alpha Accounts | Additional for Omega |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Apotheosis Icecloud Investigatos SKIN | Gnosis & Punisher SKIN Crate |
| 2 | Yoiul Blizzard Firework (Large) | Impetus Potency-50 Glamourex Booster |
| 3 | 3 Calm Abyssal Filaments | Tristan Chromodynamic Candy SKIN |
| 4 | Small Navy Ammunition Crate | 'Eico Flamjaw' Augmentation Crate |
| 5 | Sunesis Icecloud Investigators SKIN | Praxis Icecloud Investigators SKIN |
| 6 | Yoiul Festival Fireworks | Large Faction Ammunition Crate |
| 7 | 'Domino Turquoise' Augmentation Crate | 50,000 Skillpoints |
| 8 | Small Navy Ammunition Crate | Fierce Abyssal Filaments Crate |
| 9 | Yoiul Blizzard Firework (Large) | Impetus Potency-75 Glamourex Booster |
| 10 | Men's & Women's Frostline Exploration Suite | Drake & Rupture SKIN Crate |
| 11 | Medium Faction Ammunition Crate | 'Mistborne Rime' Augmentation Crate |
| 12 | 'Bitware Glacier' Augmentation Crate | 100,000 Skillpoints |
| 13 | 50,000 Skillpoints | 'Absolute' Injection Augmentor |
