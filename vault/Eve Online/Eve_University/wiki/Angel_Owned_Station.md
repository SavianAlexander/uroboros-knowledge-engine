# Angel Owned Station

1. # Locating & Difficulty
Angel Owned Station is a low/null-sec expedition that can be found by completing **Minor Angel Annex** unrated complexes, triggered by destroying the commander battlecruiser.

This site's difficulty is on par with a DED 6/10, and multiple battlecruisers with logi support are recommended to complete it.
