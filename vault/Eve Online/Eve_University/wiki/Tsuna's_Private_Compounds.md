  - Tsuna's Pirvate Compounds** is a **Caldari COSMOS** site located in Otomainen and has a beacon that you can warp to. You will land at a locked acceleration gate and will need **Private Citizen Tsuna's Passcard** in order to unlock it (not consumed on use). The card can be obtained by doing missions for Caldari COSMOS agent **Akira Helkelen**. The agent provides it as a reward for completing his first mission.

  - Note that this article is incomplete and this site may be camped, so its lucky to find the Science Lab there.**
