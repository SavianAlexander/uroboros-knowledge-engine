  - Sparking Nebula** is a high/low/null sec gas site found through **Exploration**. It contains gas used in the production of **medical boosters**.

  1. # Defenders
None

  1. # Minable gas clouds
3 Viridian Mykoserocin (2000 units each)

Viridian Mykoserocin is used in the production of the Synth Drop Booster.
