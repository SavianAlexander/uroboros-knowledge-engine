# Crimson Harvest (2022)

- Crimson Harvest** is a recurring event lasting from October 20, 2022 to November 1, 2022, **downtime** to downtime.

It involves the **Blood Raiders** pirate faction and the **order of Tetrimon** mercenaries. It copies last year's mechanics, including the 90% loot drop from PvP kill mechanic.

1. # Login campaign
Pilots need to log in for 9 of the 12 days to get all login rewards.

1. # Agency campaign
Each character can choose between two options for the Agency challenges and reward track for this event. You will receive points for shooting the opposing side to the one you choose in the agency, and you will receive a reward track themed around your chosen faction. Be aware that although you will only receive agency challenges and points for shooting the opposing side to the one you chose, there is no penalty for shooting both sides and taking the loot from both their sites.

1. # Activities
  1. # Combat
Combat sites for both sides appear as anomalies in the probe scanner, when warped to they create a beacon that can be seen from the overview. Both sides are very similar to each each other, structured as three rooms with the second room being bypassed by a locked gate in the first room and the battleship being in the third room. The second and third rooms contain some Cthonic Attar that can be mined.

There are 2 different types of level III data sites for each faction: Crimson Harvest Network Node/Hub and Tetrimon Network Node/Hub. Network Nodes spawn in high sec, while Hubs spawn in low sec. The site consists of one room with one can approximately 30km from the warp in point surrounded by some appropriately themed objects. You will receive agency points only from the sites opposing your faction of choice but both sites can be completed for loot without penalty.

  1. # Proving grounds
A special Crimson Harvest themed Abyssal Proving Ground will start on the 28th of October and runs until the 31st of October. This event features a 4-player T1 Frigate, Meta 0 Free-For-All format, with a special set of bonuses to webifier range, energy warfare strength and turret damage that lets every frigate harness the power of a Blood Raider ship!

1. # Special Mechanics
During the Crimson Harvest all player ship explosions will have an increased loot drop rate, with an average of 90% compared the normal average of 50%.
