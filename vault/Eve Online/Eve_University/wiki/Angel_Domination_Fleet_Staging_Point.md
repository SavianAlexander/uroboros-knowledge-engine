# Angel Domination Fleet Staging Point

The **Angel Domination Fleet Staging Point** is an **unrated cosmic signature site** that can be scanned using Core Probes in **Angel** infested regions of Null Sec space. It may also be received as an **escalation** from an **Angel Forsaken Hub** and 
  - Angel Forlorn Hub** anomaly. The site is of medium difficulty, it can be done with a small fleet of Faction, T2 or T3 Cruisers or Battleships, but e.g. a single Ishtar can also do it.
