## Overview
Unified PLEX trading across New Eden.

---

Capsuleers, 

The global PLEX market is now active, creating a single unified market region where all PLEX trades are visible and accessible, regardless of location (Abyssal Space excluded). All PLEX trades are now pooled into one unified market, with purchases still delivered to your Vault. No hauling or travel needed, just pure liquidity. 

With today’s release all outstanding PLEX orders were cancelled, and fees will be reimbursed within a few days.  

Please note, that global PLEX market prices will be visible in private structures (e.g. citadels and keepstars) across New Eden, but trading from private structures with ACL restrictions will remain the same; you will not be able to buy items from, or sell items to, orders in stations that you cannot access. Listing taxes are still based on standings and location, no changes here. 

For all the details on what has changed, check out the recent [dev blog](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/global-plex-market-and-friction-free-trade) or today’s [patch notes](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/patch-notes-version-23-01).
