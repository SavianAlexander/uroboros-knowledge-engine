The BIG Lottery is hosting a 7 year anniversary edition. They will have 7 places and the winners will get everything from ISK to EVE Store items. Some of the items include The Burning Life novel and a ship model of the winner's choice. The round to qualify for these prizes is from May 3, 2010 to May 16, 2010. [Find out how you can win](//www.eveonline.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=1310346) in the announcement post on the forums or [visit The BIG Lottery website](//biglottery.big-eve.com/).
