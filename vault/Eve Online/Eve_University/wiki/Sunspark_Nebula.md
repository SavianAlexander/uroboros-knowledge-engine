# Sunspark Nebula

This is a high/low/null sec gas site found through **Exploration**.

Gas Harvesting in low sec is perilous.

Defenders:

On initial warp in

None

Minable Gas Clouds:

2 Amber Mykoserocin (1000 units each), 2,000 units total (Confirmed - HighSec)
2 Amber Mykoserocin (1000 units each), 2,000 units total (Confirmed - LowSec/NullSec)

Amber Mykoserocin is used in the production of the Synth Blue Pill Booster and Axosomatic Neurolink Enhancer.
