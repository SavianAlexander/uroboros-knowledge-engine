1. # Summary
The Prowler is the Minmatar **Blockade Runner**.

Like all Blockade Runners, the Prowler is a popular underway support ship to **Stealth Bomber** fleets. It can provide ammunition reloading and transport of Mobile Warp Disruptors. Adding a Salvager, it can also clean up after successful bomb runs.
