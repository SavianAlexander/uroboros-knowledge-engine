# Gurista Hideout

The **Gurista Hideout** is a **Cosmic Signature** of the **Unrated Complex** type, occupied by the **Guristas** pirate faction. It can be found via **exploration** by **probing** in **Highsec** systems.

1. # Walkthough
The site consists of two deadspace pockets with **acceleration gate**s, various structures and their defending pirates. Using the gate allows access to other areas within the site. The names and amount of ships listed may vary slightly.
