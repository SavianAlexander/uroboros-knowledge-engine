## Overview
Applications for the 19th Council of Stellar Management are now open.

---

Applications for CSM 19 are now open! 

For more information about the CSM, the process, and the expanded 12-person format, please visit the [official CSM 19 announcement page](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/csm-19-shaping-the-future-of-new-eden). 

**Key dates:** 


- 17 September – 1 October: Candidate submissions open and application processing begins  <-- **We are here**
- 4 October: Candidate announcement
- 4-14 October: CSM 19 campaign period
- 17-24 October: CSM 19 voting period


Potential candidates for the 19th Council of Stellar Management may now head on over to the [CSM 19 applicational page](https://community.eveonline.com/community/csm/apply-for-csm/) to complete the application form. 

Please note: the CSM page does occasionally time out, so we strongly recommend completing your application in your favorite word processing application first. Upon completion, you should be met with a message that your application was submitted. 

Following the application, please send a scan of your passport/ID to [CSM-Contact@ccpgames.com](mailto:CSM-Contact@ccpgames.com) with the subject “CSM 19 Application”. You may announce your candidacy publicly as soon as you get confirmation that your account is in good standing and your application has been approved. This will be done approximately within 72 hours of your application. 

For those unable to change their names on the account management site – worry not! You can include this information with your passport or ID submissions, and we’ll make sure your account is up to date. 

We’re excited to start the CSM 19 process and can’t wait to see the applicants!
