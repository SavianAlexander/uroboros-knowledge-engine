# Twitch Integration

1. # Removal of twitch integration
In the September 2017 release Twitch was removed from the EVE client. It was not used as often as anticipitated. Streamers preferred external programs with additional features (delayed broadcast, possiblity to cover parts of the game screen).

1. # Streaming Background

On Tuesday, December 10, 2013, Twitch.tv Integration was deployed as part of Rubicon 1.0.8.  The CCP Dev Blog article is located [here](http://community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/twitch-integration-is-here/).  Be sure to read the UniWiki on the **Streaming and Video Publishing Policy** for EVE University.

Streaming is a way of broadcasting media of your choosing (anything from EVE Online to your favorite political protest) live via the magic of the internet to as many viewers as you can attract to your channel.  Game streaming has been growing rapidly in popularity over the last few years as people get their hands on better hardware and streaming services like Twitch keep improving.  By integrating an SDK created by twitch.tv into EVE, you can now completely bypass 3rd party software and stream directly from the EVE client.

1. # Twitch Background
If you aren’t familiar with twitch.tv specifically, it’s a web-based streaming service focused exclusively on games.  At any time of day there are more than 100,000 people watching other people play games on Twitch and there are hundreds of people streaming.

Twitch offers a lot of functionality through their web interface that you should make sure to check out:
# A simple chat room attached to your channel where your viewers can give you questions, offer feedback, or trashtalk.
# Twitch will archive your live streams and allow you to set new titles and highlights for them.
# You can set your stream to private on the Twitch stream page if you like.
# Many other options.

Twitch is an excellent service for E-Uni instructors to be able to show live content while giving classes.
