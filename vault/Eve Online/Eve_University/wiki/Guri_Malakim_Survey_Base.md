The Guri Malakim Survey Base is a **Capsuleer Day XX** combat event site and a variant of the **SoCT Survey Expedition** site. You will face 2 waves of **Guristas** and **Angel** NPCs that will need to be destroyed in order to spawn the boss wave (2 possible spawns). The site will appears as a beacon in the overview when warped to. The second wave appears to be timed, so it can spawn the boss if you take too much time to clear this wave. This site will provide you 6.5M in bounties if you kill the 2 waves plus the boss

|}

1. # Notes
NPCs do not drop any loot except the boss. Salvaging in this site is around 500K ISK. 
All remaining NPCs will warp away when the boss is destroyed so go immediately for him when he appears.
