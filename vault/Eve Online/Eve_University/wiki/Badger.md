1. # Summary
Offering considerably more defense and powergrid capacity over the larger **Tayra**, the **Badger** is very versatile. Its 185 MW powergrid lets it mount substantial defenses or even meaningful weapons in rare comedy 'combat Badger' fits.

The Badger is much more nimble than the Tayra, and with proper equipment and **support skills** the alignment time can be brought down from a glacial 13.88 s to less than 8s.

When all the low slots are used for expanded cargoholds, the Badger will have over 7500 capacity while being no less agile than a Tayra. The shield can be boosted with extenders, boosters, resistance modules, or rechargers.

1. # Notes
Visually, the Badger seems to share the chassis and propulsion unit with the **Tayra**, with a shorter cargohold that does not extend very far forward. They also share the same launcher and turret hardpoints. Its variant, the **Crane**, seems to have additional components to the rear, as well as a different painting scheme, perhaps to house the necessary modules for cloaking.
