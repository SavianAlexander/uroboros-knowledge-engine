# 10.000 simultaneous players

- **Date**: 2004-04-25T19:02:08.000Z
- **Category**: news
- **Author**: Svarthol
- **Source**: https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/10.000-simultaneous-players
- **Tags**: #news

## Overview
This evening at 19:00, 10.000 played EVE Online at the same time. This is a world record for highest number of players together, at the same time, in the same persistent 3D world. However, the night is not over and we might go even further! Thank you and congratulations all!
