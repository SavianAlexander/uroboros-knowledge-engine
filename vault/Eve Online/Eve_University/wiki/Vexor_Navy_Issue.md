1. # Summary
In July 2019, the **Vexor Navy Issue** was changed from a glass cannon drone boat to a tanky, brawling focused fit. Its previous bandwidth that could control 5 heavy or sentry drones has been reduced down to 3 heavies/sentries, so capsuleers now must make use of the damage bonused turret slots alongside drones to get the strong DPS amount it could get before.
