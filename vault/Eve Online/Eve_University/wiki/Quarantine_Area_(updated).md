# Quarantine Area (updated)

146,900,000 ISK in Blue Loot (without capital escalations or Drifter escalations).
1. # Walkthrough
The initial wave contains C1-C2 Sleeper frigate rats. Unlike most Sleeper sites, there are no trigger ships in all three waves.

The capital escalation rats can damage you from 320 km away, and their EWAR reaches out to 100 km.
