# Guristas Extravaganza (Level 4)

Videos: [[Gila](https://youtu.be/IqUvXs504n0) 26-08-2021], [[Ishtar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O2ocSgmZOKU) 4 August 2014], [[Navy Raven](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=waeFZ_nqBdU) 20 March 2014], [[Vargur Full Clear](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Id-w6-KG9dM) 26 August 2012]

  - Blitz**:
- Pocket 1 - Destroy all ships.
- Pocket 2 - Destroy the Ion Field Projection Battery and Moon Harvester, then kill all ships.
- Pocket 3 - Destroy the Guristas Medium Refinery, Subspace Frequency Generator, and Asteroid Mining Post. Then kill all ships.
- Pocket 4 - Destroy the Starbase Storage Facility, Starbase Reactor, and Guristas Starbase Control Tower. Then kill all ships.
- Pocket 5 - Kill all Battleships and Battlecruisers from the first spawn.

  - Notes:**
- Structures drop no loot, with the exception of those in Pocket 5 and the Ion projections and Moon harvester.
- Asteroid Factory has a lot of armor and there's no point in shooting it.
- As of the **Trinity 1.2 Update** the Guristas Storage Facility no longer drops faction loot.
- Many of the frigates (Kyoukan/Webifier) in the earlier waves of each pockets only do little damages and doesn't web (Jan 5th 2025).
