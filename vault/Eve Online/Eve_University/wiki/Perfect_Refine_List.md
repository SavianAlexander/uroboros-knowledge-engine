# Perfect Refine List

The capsuleers below will happily reprocess your materials for you. Make sure to read their individual entries for where they can reprocess your stuff, and exactly *what* they can reprocess for you. Follow the individual instructions.

  - EVE Uni Refine Locations:** While the perfect refiners on this list are welcome to refine wherever they agree to do so - the use of allied structures in the Community areas (Korsiki, LSC etc) is common for this service.  Please see the notes below for how to decide WHICH structure you should contract your ore from:

  - Asteroid Ore, Moon Ore and Ice:** Krabworks - Korsiki

- The highest refine rate available requires the correct rig to be installed at the structure the refining is taking place at, and there are different rigs for Asteroid Ore, Moon Ore, and Ice.  The list above shows which structures are rigged for what purpose - taking, for example, Moon ore to Furirams Furnace, will result in lower refine rates.*

1. # Joining the list

Classification:
- You are a "Perfect" refiner if you have eight or more refining skills trained to V
- You are a "Pretty Good" refiner if you have eight or more refining skills trained to at least IV
- Otherwise, you are a "Good" refiner

After you figure out how you are classified, copy the syntax block at , put it in alphabetical order under that section, and fill in the template parameters. Feel free to look at examples of how other refiners have filled out the template.
