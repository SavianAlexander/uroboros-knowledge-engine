# Serpentis Rally Point

- Serpentis Rally Point** is a Class 6 **combat anomaly** that can be found in low and null sec space.

Total bounties amount to around 4,500,000 ISK.

There are two variants of the site.
1. # Walkthrough (silo spawn)
The site is an open deadspace pocket that can be warped to at any range. Tower Sentry Angels give Serpentis standing hit despite belonging to the **Angel Cartel**.

Sentries spawn 35-40 km away from the warp-in point.  All other ships spawn at 0 on the anomaly.
