# Guristas Forlorn Den

- Guristas Forlorn Den** is an unrated **combat site** belonging to the **Guristas Pirates** pirate faction. It can be found via **exploration** as a **cosmic anomaly** within systems owned by the **Caldari State** or occupied by the **Guristas Pirates**. As an ungated complex, there are no limitations so up to **Titans** are allowed. Completing this complex may award the player with valuable Dread Guristas **faction modules** and ammunition.

This combat site consists of a single ungated pocket of space containing a variety of **Frigate**-, **Cruiser**-, **Battlecruiser**- and **Battleship**-sized enemy ships. Destroying all hostile ships does not trigger an escalation. Three other variations of this combat anomaly exist: **Guristas Den**, **Guristas Hidden Den** and **Guristas Forsaken Den**.
