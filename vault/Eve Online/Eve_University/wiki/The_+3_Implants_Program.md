# The +3 Implants Program

- EVE University** members can take advantage of the +3 Implants Program, which offers up to three full set of basic +3 **implants** for free (per player, not per EVE Uni character). The benefit over lower quality implants or no implants at all is quite obvious: faster skill training. The market price for +3 implants typically ranges from 8–10 million ISK per implant.

| :--- |

1. # How to Participate
# Fill out this [Request Form](https://auth.eveuniversity.org/forms/form/7/) and include the list of implants you'd like to acquire.
# Select either "Full Set" *OR* the **names of the implants** you want.
# Wait for a private contract of your chosen implants issued by a member of the Supply Chain team **to your denoted main character**. Please note that depending on implant stock, processing times can be up to 7 days.
# The pickup location of the implants will always be Korsiki - Horizon.

1. # Other Rules and Notes
- If you have any questions regarding the program send them to a Supply Chain team member on Discord by opening a thread in #support and tagging @Supply Chain Officer. Do not request implants in this manner.
- This program is restricted to 3 individual requests (full set or select implants) per player. This covers free implants for two alts, or the loss of your +3 pod on your main character twice, or any combination thereof. Once you have reached three individual requests, you are no longer eligible to benefit from the program.
- These implants are for your personal use only, you must not sell or trade them. Storing them in a **Jump Clone** is allowed though.
- Those found to be abusing the +3 Implants Program or any other Uni program may be subject to removal from the corporation.
- Your character requires  to use these implants.

1. # Program Manager

The implant program is administered by the **Supply Chain Lead**.

1. # Donating specifically to the Program
If you want to donate ISK or implants directly to the program, send donations to Ivy League Alliance Holdings with +3 Implants in the description. We would request that any implants are donated at Korsiki - Horizon.
