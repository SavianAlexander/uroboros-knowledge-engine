Others channel their creativity into guides, like Rixx Javix who shared an infographic on stargates in one of his latest [blog posts](https://eveoganda.blogspot.com/2025/07/guide-to-stargates.html). 


## Celebratory Explosions


To celebrate 15 years of the Black Rebel Rifter Club, they are handing out 4000 free Rifters and inviting everyone to rain hell on Bosboger. This celebration, dubbed the Bosbo Bloodbath, kicks off tomorrow, Saturday 5 July, at 20:00 UTC. 

As if that won't result in enough wrecked Rifters, Ushra'Khan launches their Liberation Day Celebrations from 7 July onward. Among the events are not one, but two Rifter Free-For-Alls, complete with T1-fitted handouts 

All events and reward details will be listed on [PANDORA](https://www.evepandora.com/), so keep an eye out if you’re looking to jump in.
