# Retribution (Serpentis)

- Blitz:** Destroy the Outpost, Warp Out

- No hostiles at initial gate

  1. # Single pocket
- [NPC distribution](http://eve-survival.org/screenshots/retribution3se-1.jpg)
- Four groups, all peaceful. Can be separately engaged without drawing aggro from the other groups.
- Attacking the outpost will aggro the entire pocket
