# Guristas Hallucinogen Supply Waypoint

- Guristas Hallucinogen Supply Waypoint** is a **Cosmic Signature** of the DED Complex (rated 5/10) type, occupied by the Guristas pirate faction. It can be found via exploration by probing in Highsec and Lowsec systems.

The site consists of a volume of space with an acceleration gate and contains various structures and their defending pirates.

This site's acceleration gate allows all subcapitals to enter.

  - Warning: The DPS in the Second/third rooms becomes quite heavy. Do not attempt this site if you do not know what you are doing or you risk losing your ship quite easily.**

1. # Walkthrough
  1. # Room 1
All ships will auto-aggro.

|}

|}

  1. # Room 2

Significant amount of (mostly) kinetic damage in this room. Prioritize battlecruisers and battleships first since their missiles make up most of the incoming damage and have long range.

|}

|}

  1. # Room 3

Kill Guristas Distributor then either loot his wreck or bookmark it, warp out, and come back a few minutes later to pick up the loot. Triple Stasis Tower spawn will reduce your speed to <10 m/s, making you take a LOT of damage, especially if you rely on speed-tanking.

|}
