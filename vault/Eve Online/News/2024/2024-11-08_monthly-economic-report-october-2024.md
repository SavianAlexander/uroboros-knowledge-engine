Economic Capsuleers!

The Monthly Economic Report for October 2024 is now available!

For October, we have made the following changes to the MER:


- The issue with certain items being excluded from the economic indices calculation has been identified and corrected.
- The Deathless Custodians faucet has been replaced by broker fees in the Top 8 faucets & sinks chart following the conclusion of the Zarzakh siege in late September.


The economic summary shows the Ship Index continuing to drop, with a ~4.5% drop over the last 12 months. Conversely, the Mineral Price Index has reached a new record of 287.
