# Longing Leman

<noinclude> 
</noinclude>

Video: [[Probe](https://youtu.be/jq2-yJ9tIwg) June 2021]

  - Blitz:** Create **bookmark** while warping in. Warp to the bookmark. Warp to the Encrypted Communications Array at 0 km. Hack the Container and Warp out.

  - Tips:**
- You will need a Data Analyzer module to hack the Encrypted Communications Array. The mission does not provide you with one, so you'll have to get one yourself.
- This is not a combat mission. It is not necessary to destroy any NPC ships, and they seem to not attack you unless you get close.
  - If you do wish to engage the NPC fleet, either kill the elite frigates quickly and speed-tank or be prepared to tank an extreme amount of incoming damage.
    - The Outpost Defender fighters alone do a combined total of ~500 DPS EM/Thermal. The battleships will use microwarpdrive to quickly close in on you and do a combined total of 1500 DPS at 60km range.
- This mission can be accepted remotely.

Single ungated pocket. The defenders are 100 km from warpin while the container is 20 km away.

  - Loot:**
Approximately 30M isk in loot and salvage.

Loot+ Salvage: 40.7mil 9-June-2024
