# After The Seven - "Replacement"

<noinclude>
This mission is issued by a Security division agent and is part 1 of the 5 part mission chain **After The Seven**</noinclude>

Single ungated pocket. When you warp in there are 3 groups of pirates attacking the neutral transports. Group A and B auto aggro shortly after the message from the team leader. Group C warps out after the destruction of "their" transport.

As you kill the hostile frigates the neutral transport ships are able to warp away.
