# Vengeance (Serpentis)

Video: [Golem](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dHc4xRCOn-k), [Maelstrom](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-IYVUPLwkCw), [Kronos (Bastion)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tfrOF5h3jDA)

You will warp into a large swarm of Hostiles in the first 2 pockets, a MJD is highly recommended to snipe.
Groups will aggro Drones, keep a close eye on them.

  - Tip:**
- Xevni Jipon is weak against thermal damage but has high resistances against kinetic and is immune to EW.
- Be careful not to get stuck on the gate and asteroids in the 1st pocket.

  - Blitz:**
- 1st and 2nd pocket: destroy all ships to unlock the gates.
- 3rd pocket: destroy the 3rd group (All ships by the station. Tagging these ships makes it easier). No other kills are needed to complete the mission.

No Hostiles at initial gate.

  1. # 1st pocket (Outer Perimeter)

|}

  - Structure:**
Serpentis Narcotics Warehouse drops 10 Drugs.

  1. # 2nd pocket
Attacking the 2nd group will aggro the 4th group.
Recommended order of attack: 1st, 4th, 2nd and 3rd group.

|}

  1. # 3rd pocket (To the Coven)

Blitz: Destroy group 3 to complete the mission.

|}

  - Structure:**
Serpentis Stronghold as high hp and only drops basic T1 Modules and random ammo (not worth the time).
