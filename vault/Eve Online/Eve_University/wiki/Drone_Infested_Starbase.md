# Drone Infested Starbase

- Drone Infested Starbase** is a DED 4/10 rated combat site that is found in null security space. It consists of a starbase protected by a shield bubble, a large number of sentries with neutralizers, and several waves of drones. Despite being listed as a level 4/10 site in game, it requires capital scale damage to complete. It can be found as an escalation from Rogue Drone anomalies. It lasts 3 days once earned in the Opportunities journal.
