As reported in a [recent dev blog](//community.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&nbid=3392), we've been working on adding corporation forums to the EVE forums. The launch got pushed back from the original date of February the 8th because we wanted to do more load testing and security testing, but now the results are in and we will deploy the corporation forums on Thursday, March 8th.

And just earlier today we deployed a few changes to the forums, the most notable being an autosave feature which will save a draft of your post every 45 seconds in case something happens that would otherwise cause you to lose your post. Bring on the long posts!

We also replaced the subforum "Breaking the Language Barrier" on the localized forums, with a "Features and Ideas" subforum.
