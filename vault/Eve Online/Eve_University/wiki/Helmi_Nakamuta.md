# Helmi Nakamuta

- Helmi Nakamuta** is a level 2 agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located in Friggi - Sentinel Rise.

1. # Ranch Hand – Building Blocks (1 of 3)

Transport 6x Crates of Construction Blocks (420 m³) to a station in Ihakana (LowSec!). Consider taking your ship first to Otitoh before heading to Ihakana as the Friggi <> Ihakana gate is often camped.

1. # Ranch Hand – Relief Work (2 of 3)

Transport wheat (644 m³) to a station a few jumps away.

1. # Ranch Hand – Oasis Bringer (3 of 3)

Transport water (360 m³) to a station a few jumps away.
