## Overview

---

### Attention capsuleers,


Coming soon, EVE Online will be offering [Twitch Drops](https://www.twitch.tv/drops/inventory), meaning that all pilots can claim free rewards just for watching [EVE Online content on Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/directory/game/EVE%20Online).

Get ahead and pre-link your EVE Online account to Twitch today, to ensure you're first in line for earning those rewards.

Please follow these easy steps to link your EVE account to Twitch:


- **Step 1: **[Login](https://www.eveonline.com/twitch)to your EVE Account from our special Twitch page
- **Step 2:** Click ‘Link Twitch’
- **Step 3:** Head to Twitch, [login](https://www.twitch.tv/login), and/or confirm the authorization to link your EVE and Twitch Accounts
- **Step 4:** With the account linking confirmed, you can watch content on the official CCPTV Twitch channel, before claiming your rewards via Twitch’s ‘[Drops & Rewards](https://www.twitch.tv/drops/inventory)’ section
- **Step 5:** Head back to your EVE account, and your rewards will be ready and waiting for you in the Redeeming section


Stay tuned for the grand launch of Twitch Drops - watch your favourite content and get rewarded!
