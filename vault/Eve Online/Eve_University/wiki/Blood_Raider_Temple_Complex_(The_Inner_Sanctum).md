# Blood Raider Temple Complex (The Inner Sanctum)

- Blood Raider Temple Complex (The Inner Sanctum)** is an **Expedition** that has a nearly 100% chance to escalate from **Blood Raider Temple Complex**.
1. # Walkthrough
This expedition only has one part, but it is VERY hard compared to its **Dark Blood Fleet Staging Point** variant. Advise bringing a friend or two in ships with a very strong tank and remote capacitor transmitters. Alternatively, bring ships that require little to no cap to operate. The first section is ungated, so capitals can be brought in. Past the acceleration gate, though, only T2 **Battleships** and below are allowed to enter.
  1. # Warp-in point
Due to heavy neuting, the energy neutralizer towers should be a very high priority. They have high armor and structure hitpoints, though, so your ship should be able to deal enough DPS to break them quickly. There are a lot of elite frigates in this room which like to target drones, so watch them and recall them if necessary.
