# Serpentis Fortress

- Serpentis Fortress** is a **combat site** that can be scanned down with probes, and can be found in null security space in the Serpentis regions.

The site is behind an acceleration gate.  Warping to the site lands you at 0 on the gate.

1. # Walkthrough
Warping to the site lands on an acceleration gate at 0.

  1. # Room 1
The enemies spawn in three groups.  The groups can be aggressed individually.  
The acceleration gate to Room 2 is 25 km from warp-in.

The large Serpentis Station can be destroyed, but drops regular modules.  There is a chance of getting the **Suspicious Job** escalation when you destroy the Shadow Serpentis battleship if it spawns, or the Station if it doesn't.

1. # Loot
You can get about 30 million ISK in bounties from fully clearing the site. The faction battleship may drop additional valuable loot.

1. # Escalation
Destroying the Serpentis Stronghold structure in Room #2 may cause the site to escalate to **Suspicious Job**.
