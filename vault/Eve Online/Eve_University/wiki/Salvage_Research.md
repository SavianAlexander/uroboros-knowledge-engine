# Salvage Research

The pocket contains 30 wrecks that must be salvaged within 20 minutes. **Mobile Tractor Units (MTU)** and tractor beams do not work on the wrecks—you must approach each wreck and use high-slot salvage modules or salvage drones directly. Be aware that hostile ships can target and destroy salvage drones.

Hostile ships will spawn and engage you. These can be ignored if your tank is strong enough, speed-tanked if you’re fast, or destroyed depending on your fleet composition. 

A **Malfunctioning Werpost** is located in the pocket. It can be activated with a **Data Analyzer** module; once online, it will attack enemy ships with its disintegrator weapon. For more info please read: **Hacking**.

Salvagers must have at least a 20% Access Difficulty Bonus to function (Salvaging IV or a rig is required). At 20%, these sites are quite difficult; 30–35% is recommended.  For more info please read: **Salvaging**.<gallery>

1. # Fleet size & composition

- Optimal fleet size: 3 ships
- Fleet composition: T1 Frigates, T1 Destroyers fit for salvage / tanking / speed-tanking / hacking / killing enemies.

1. # Type of ships allowed
The operation takes place in a **deadspace pocket** with an acceleration gate. The gate allows these ships:
- T1 Frigate (including Navy and Faction)
- T1 Destroyer (excluding Navy and Faction)
- Venture, Venture Consortium Issue

1. # Regional differences
Salvage Research sites have no regional differences.
1. # Hostiles

A fleet of 8-10 **Ashes of Turnur** frigates and destroyers will be in the site on arrival. They will focus on a single capsuleer ship at a time, starting with the first ship to land in the site, and will occasionally switch targets as a group. 

Hostile drone aggro ships (see list below) will target any drones that are deployed, but will revert to attacking the currently focused capsuleer ship if no drones are present. 

Hostile damage types & **EWAR**:
- ~62.5% ~37.5%
- ECM

The hostile fleet is composed of a random selection of the following ships. The composition from site to site varies widely, for example in one site there may be 4 Skald Bursts and in the next none at all.

- [Atgeir Rifter](https://everef.net/types/77560): Drone aggro. Shoots drones if any are launched, otherwise follows the room target
- [Atgeir Thrasher](https://everef.net/types/77628): Drone aggro. Shoots drones if any are launched, otherwise follows the room target
- [Blight Vigil](https://everef.net/types/77579):  ECM
- [Blight Wolf](https://everef.net/types/77610):  ECM
- [Blight Talwar](https://everef.net/types/77622):  ECM
- [Skald Burst](https://everef.net/types/77594): remote shield repairs

1. # Malfunctioning Werpost
A single Malfunctioning Werpost sits in the site. Hacking the Werpost gives no loot, but activates its weapon. It works similarly to the **entropic disintegrator** turrets found on Precursor (**Triglavian**) ships, it is single-target with spooling damage. The Werpost will pick a target to shoot and switch to a new target when the first is destroyed or after about 1 minute. The amount of damage it deals is relatively low and is not enough to overcome the repairs from 4 Skald Bursts

The hack is of medium difficulty, with a gold **System Core**.<gallery>

1. # Rewards
Rewards for Salvage Research sites come in two forms: (1) ISK payout on site completion and (2) site-specific items salvaged from the wrecks. Each pilot must salvage at least 1 wreck in order to be eligible for the payout, and the payout amount is determined by the number of pilots being paid. The highest payout is with 3 pilots, giving 12,000,000 ISK per pilot upon timely completion of the mission.

The 30 salvaged wrecks each yield one Encrypted ‘Istina’ Data item, it can be sold to NPC buy orders for an additional 3,000,000 ISK (100,000 ISK each).
