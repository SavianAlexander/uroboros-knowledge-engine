# Mirmon Gorgoz

- Mirmon Gorgoz** is a level 2 Minmatar agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located in Friggi - Clear Water Spring.

1. # The Medicine Man – A Pinch of Spice (1 of 3)

Powdered Cubensis drops from Wolf Skarkert's cruiser. They can be found at the **Contested Caldari Mining Facilities** - Contested Scordite field.

1. # The Medicine Man – Snake Bite (2 of 3)

Clear some mission pocket from rats.

1. # The Medicine Man – Satisfaction Guaranteed (3 of 3)

Bring 2500 Liberation Elixir (500 m3) to a given destination.
