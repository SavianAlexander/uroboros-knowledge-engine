# Digital Convolution

- Digital Convolution** is a **chemical lab** found in Tenal region.

1. # Walkthrough

The site contains both combat and hacking. Using the acceleration gates in the site requires hacking IV to be trained.

  1. # 1st pocket - Guard Station
----
One NPC group on each gate.

Rats are initially passive until attacked.

|}

|}

  1. # 2nd pocket - Lounge
----

Initially two groups are present. Reinforcements arrive in few minutes.

|}

|}

|}

  1. # 3rd pocket - Distribution Center
----

Reinforcements arrive after the Guristas Production Manager dies.

|}

|}

  1. # 4th pocket - Packaging Center
----

Gate ambushers will autoaggro.

The Packaging Center has a lot of hitpoints and only drops T1 ammo. Don't bother destroying it.
