Crucible 1.5 has gone live, and it has brought with it a set of [sleek redesigned rookie ships](//community.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&nbid=3435), giving new players to EVE about the coolest looking starter gear in MMOs. Players looking to bring a friend into the EVE universe  get to see their buddies sporting some neat new ships and, with the [Buddy Program](//secure.eveonline.com/buddy/), can set them up with an extended 21-Day Trial. Even better, if the buddy subscribes, the player that invited them gets 30 days of game time added to their account. Bring a new ally (or adversary) into EVE Online with the EVE Online [Buddy Program](//secure.eveonline.com/buddy/).
