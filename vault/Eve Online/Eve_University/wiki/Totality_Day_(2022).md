# Totality Day (2022)

- Totality Day** is a limited time event running between 13th  and 18th October 2022, **downtime** to downtime. During this period, capsuleers can log in to earn Boosters, **Filaments** and **Skill Points**.

1. # Login campaign
Capsuleers can log in for four of the five days within the event to collect all the rewards.

1. # Proving Grounds: Dialectic Demolition
  - Dialectic Demolition** is a 24 hour proving ground event running from 11:00 UTC on 15 October till 11:00 UTC on 16 October.

Rule set:
- Pilots receive 10m ISK for every match where they deal at least 2000 damage
- All ships gain +500 shield, armor, and hull HP
- No more than one local repair module per ship entering the arena
- Damage from turrets and missiles is increased by 50%
- Only the **Kikimora** may enter – show your strength on even ground!
- 100% bonus to the benefits of overheating tackle, propulsion, repair, resistance, and energy warfare modules, as well as turrets and missile launchers
- Shield rechargers, boost amplifiers, power relays, and flux coils, core defense field purgers, sensor dampeners, and weapon disruptors cannot be fitted
- Modules are restricted to meta level 5 and below

1. # Special mechanics
During the event, a warp speed bonus  is applied to all ships in **Pochven**.

A special 90% PVP Loot Drop rate, in Pochven, for the duration of the event
