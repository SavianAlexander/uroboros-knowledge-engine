# Federation Detention Facility

- Federation Detention Facility** is an unrated **combat site** belonging to the **Gallente Federation** faction. It can be found via **exploration** by scanning signatures in high security systems in Black Rise within the Aokinen constellation. The site is level II scanning difficulty.

This site appears to be the target for the Caldari epic arc mission **The Breakout**, and will only spawn when the respective mission has been accepted. One site will spawn in every system in the constellation and will respawn after completion.

  1. # First Pocket

The non-Warden frigates may not spawn.  The Warden frigates are very weak (<100 HP) and can't stand a lot of damage. 

The Federation Detention Facility containers must be hacked with a Data Analyzer to retrieve the "Caldari Prisoners of War" item required to complete **The Breakout**. The containers are Easy difficulty (70 HP green System Core). After the first container is successfully hacked, an invisible 60-second timer starts, after which the containers will all explode and the site despawns. The containers are grouped relatively close together in a straight line, so if you have a fast ship and good hacking skills, you can hack multiple containers and pick up multiple mission items.
