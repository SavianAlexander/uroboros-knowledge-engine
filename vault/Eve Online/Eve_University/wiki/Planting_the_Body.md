# Planting the Body

<noinclude> 
</noinclude>

Warp into the deadspace mission room and place 1 unit of Wolf Burgan's Body Double into “Wolf Burgan's Hideout” container. There will be 12 enemies protecting this container and they will fire on you. Few more will also spawn once the body has been placed in the container.

You can choose to fight them or just rush in, place the DNA, and warp out. You will take a lot of damage as you get in range of the drop-off container. Use an afterburner, if you can, to maximize your speed.
