# Guristas Haven

The **Guristas Haven** is a **combat anomaly** found in **Guristas** infested regions. There are 2 variants of this anomaly, commonly referred to as "gas" or "rock" haven by players. The rock haven variant is readily identified by the warp-in popup received. Each also feature different structures within the site.

1. # Walkthrough (rock)

The site is an open Deadspace pocket, one may warp to preferred distance. Killing the last hostile ship triggers a reinforcement spawn.
