[Nere: Schwerelos und Planlos](//nsup.blogspot.com/) is hosting a writing contest on the [EVE Germany](//www.eveger.de/forum/showthread.php?t=36018) forums. This is in celebration of Nerevar Dwemor’s [100 blog post](//nsup.blogspot.com/2011/01/nsup-100.html). The contest will run Friday January 7 through Friday January 21, 2011. One lucky winner will receive a CD key from the EVE Online: Commissioned Officer Edition and one PLEX. Full details of this contest can be found [here](//www.eveger.de/forum/showthread.php?t=36018) and the discussion thread for the contest is [here](//www.eveger.de/forum/showthread.php?p=570653#post570653).
