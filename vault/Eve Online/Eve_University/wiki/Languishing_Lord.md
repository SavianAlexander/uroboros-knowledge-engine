# Languishing Lord

<noinclude> 
</noinclude>

Video: [[Probe](https://youtu.be/zHCBXTBDWEg) June 2021]

You'll be told to fly to an asteroid (about 20k away). Then you watch for a minute, and the mission completes. Do not engage any of the ships that appear. Bring a fast ship like a shuttle or a frigate.
