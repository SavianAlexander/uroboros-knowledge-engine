|}

  - Blitz:**
Take out all the ships. You don't have to take out the towers.

  - Tip:**
Damage from Sentries is minimal, take them out to draw aggro from the ships so they'll close in.
Either fit an AB, MWD or MJD to go after them or bring Warden II to snipe.
