CCP are proud to present the trailer for [EVE Online: Rubicon](//www.eveonline.com/rubicon), our 20th free expansion for EVE Online.

The Empires attempt to dissuade Capsuleers from entering the new [ghost sites](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/ghastly-hotbed-of-spaceship-trauma/), fearful of the technological secrets that are locked within. Ignoring their warnings, two curious pilots decide to breach the defenses of an [Angel Cartel](//wiki.eveonline.com/en/wiki/Angel_Cartel) ghost site, equipped with the [most cutting edge starship technology](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/sisters-of-eve-faction-ships/) in the cluster and comfortable with the risk that comes along with the potential reward. 

Find out how they fare, in the [EVE Online: Rubicon trailer.](//www.eveonline.com/rubicon/videos/eve-online-rubicon-trailer/?utm_source=Community+news+feed&utm_medium=Banner&utm_term=Rubicon+Trailer&utm_content=Rubicon+Trailer&utm_campaign=Rubicon+Trailer)
