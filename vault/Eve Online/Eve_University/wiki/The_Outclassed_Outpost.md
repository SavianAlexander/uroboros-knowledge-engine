# The Outclassed Outpost

<noinclude> 
</noinclude>

Video: [[Typhoon](https://youtu.be/ZEJH2wFK3SA) June 2021]

Single ungated pocket. The last ship destroyed in each group will trigger a new wave. Destroy all ships to complete mission.

After the ships are destroyed the agent will contact you directly. You can then remotely complete this mission and remotely accept the next mission.
