# Rocky Nebula

- Rocky Nebula** is a gas exploration site containing gas used in the production of **Medical boosters**.

This is a null sec gas site found through Exploration.

Defenders:

None

Minable Gas Clouds:

6 Amber Cytoserocin (300 or 400 units in each gas cloud)

Amber Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Pure Standard Blue Pill Booster.

Occasionally there will be a message in local which states "A thermal column has burst from the Amber Cytoserocin cloud, blasting all ships in the vicinity" and high enough damage to destroy a venture in one shot will be dealt.

This site is found only in the[Vale of the Silent](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Vale_of_the_Silent)region of space.
