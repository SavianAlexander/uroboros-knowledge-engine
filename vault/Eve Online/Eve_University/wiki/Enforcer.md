1. # Summary
A 1-run **Enforcer** BPC can be obtained as a recruiter benefit, for recruiting ten friends, via CCP's [Recruit a Friend program](https://www.eveonline.com/recruit)
An Enforcer was issued to those who bought their **Fanfest** 2017 tickets before 2017/01/01.
A 1-run Enforcer BPC was a reward for reaching level 150 in **Project Discovery: Exoplanets**.

As the CONCORD Force Recon, the Enforcer possesses a balanced resist profile; coupled with its bonus to armor and shield reps as well as its bonus to EWAR range and warp speed, the Enforcer should perform as a potent cyno ship. However this is not the case, in part due to several problems with the ship: While all ships in the CONCORD tech tree have less raw HP than their counterparts, this flaw is particularly evident on the Enforcer (the Pacifier, as a frigate, is less affected by this inferiority, while the Marshal is barely affected at all). Even considering base resists, this Recon ship has less HP than the famously un-tanky Stabber. At the same time, this ship also suffers from low base speed and extremely weak capacitor; this combined with its relative rarity means that what could have been a powerful ship is instead underwhelming.

1. # Tactics
With its warp speed and active tank bonus, as well as its bonus to security status, the Enforcer is sometimes used as a very blingy ratting ship in null or highsec.
For those wishing to use the Enforcer in a more generic PVP setting, please refer to **Recon Ships**.

1. # Notes
According to its description, the Enforcer is based on a hybridization of the **Caldari State**'s **Moa** class cruiser and the **Gallente Federation**'s **Vexor** class cruiser.
