We're proud to say that EVE Online has been nominated for a [Golden Joystick Award](//www.goldenjoystick.com/) in the Online Game of the Year category. It is you, the gamers, who decide who wins by voting for your favourite game in each category.

To vote you simply go [here](//www.computerandvideogames.com/joystick/shortlist.php?), select your favourite games, EVE Online of course, and vote! There's also a contest where you can win a chance to attend the awards, go to the legendary after show party and return home with an Xbox 360 Elite and all the games in the Xbox category!
