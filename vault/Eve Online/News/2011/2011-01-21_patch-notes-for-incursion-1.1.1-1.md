Patch Notes for EVE Online: Incursion 1.1.1

Deployed January 21, 2011

**FIXES**

**Mac**


- Fixed an issue where drop down menus were not being displayed correctly when using the in-game browser.
- Players should no longer experience a client crash when exiting.
- Fixed an issue where closing the in-game browser, would spawn an Exception raised issue on the Mac.


**User Interface**


- Characters flagged for re-customization should no longer lose their facial hair.
- Made small fixes to the color picker to make it more responsive under high load or on lower end machines.


**EVE Gate**


- Under certain circumstances some players were losing their sessions in EVE Gate, this has been corrected


**Graphics General**


- The frame rate issue with Extractor Control Units has been optimized.


**CHANGES**

**Character Creation and New Player Experience**


- Players who have previously created their character will be allowed one free re-customization.


**User Interface**


- The image cache for portraits has been updated - Characters: 2 hours, Corps: 1 day, Alliances: 1 week.
