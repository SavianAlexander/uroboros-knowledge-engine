# Hazy Nebula

- Hazy Nebula** is is a null sec gas site found through **exploration**.

Note: Use a combat ship first to clear the NPCs.

The Blood Raider Sentinel has strong armor repairers.

|}

Minable Gas Clouds:

1 Malachite Cytoserocin cloud (18,000 units)

Malachite Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Standard Mindflood Booster and Ultradian-Cycling Neurolink Stabilizer.

This site is found only in the[OK-FEM](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Delve/OK-FEM)constellation of the[Delve](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Delve)region of space.

Mining the gas clouds requires an appropriately tanked ship, due to the occasional area-of-effect EM and Thermal damage.

Message: Harvesting the unstable Malachite Cytoserocin cloud has caused a violent thermal reaction, damaging all ships in the vicinity.
Message: A blast of radioactive particles spurts out of the Malachite Cytoserocin cloud, damaging all ships nearby.
