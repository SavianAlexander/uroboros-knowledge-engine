# Police Invasion (Minmatar Republic)

- Blitz:** Eliminate all ships from Group 4. You might want to destroy the frigates in group 1 as they point and web.

Note: It is a faction mission so no bounty.

  1. # Single Pocket
Auto-aggro from Group 1 at warp-in.
Group 2,3,4,5 remains neutral as long as you do not move from the warpin.
Drones will get agro.
