We are happy to announce that the candidacy period for the tenth Council of Stellar Management is now open.

Candidacy applications will be accepted from 11:30 UTC today, January 30th 2015, through until 11:30 UTC on 15th of February, 2015.

For further information on the Council of Stellar Management, please see the [CSM Website](//community.eveonline.com/community/csm/), and for further information on the candidacy period, take a look at [CCP Leeloo's latest Dev Blog](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/csm-x-candidacy-now-open)!
