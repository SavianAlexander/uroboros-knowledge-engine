# Hidden Enemy Base (Amarr)

Deadspace with multiple gated pockets. Two Remote Cloaking Array's must be destroyed before a hidden gate that leads to the Enemy Base will appear.

  - Tips:**
- Use a Passive Tank and save time by not having to destroy Energy Neutralizer Sentries
- When being Tracking Disrupted, your turrets can still be used effectively against stationary Sentries and Batteries (while your drones handle the frigates).
- For Pockets 5 & 6, wait until all ships have spawned before bringing in a logistics ship

  1. # 1st pocket - Entry Point
Empty pocket with 3 gates that lead to the pockets.

|}

  1. # 2nd pocket

Warp disruption, neuting, tracking disruption and heavy webbing. All ships will orbit at close ~10km orbit. Destroy the Remote Cloaking Array (50,000 HP) and take the Gate to return to the Entry Point Pocket.

|}

|}

  1. # 3rd pocket

Frigates will orbit at ~4 km while battleships stay at ~45 km. Destroy the Remote Cloaking Array (50,000 HP) and take the Gate to return to the Entry Point Pocket.

|}

|}

  1. # 4th pocket

A gate to pocket 5 will be visible here if you destroyed the two Remote Cloaking Arrays in 2nd and 3rd pockets.

Battlecruisers orbits at 10-20 km and battleships at 50 km.

|}

|}

  1. # 5th pocket

Timed spawns. It takes 30 seconds for all 10 cruisers to spawn. Cruisers orbit at 10-22 km.

|}

|}

  1. # 6th pocket - Enemy Base

Timed spawns. It takes 60 seconds for all ships to spawn.

Destroy the Amarr Station (150,000 hp). Mission is flagged complete when the station is destroyed.

Frigates and cruisers orbit at ~10 km, battlecruisers at 10-20 km and battleships 15-50 km.

|}

|}
