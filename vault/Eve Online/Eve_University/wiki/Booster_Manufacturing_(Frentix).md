Simple trade mission where the agent requests some Cytoserocin and gives a drug BPC as a reward. The gas can be mined locally if you have venture or prospect and core probes. Alternatively you can use a blockade runner or prospect to haul the gas from elsewhere. This mission can be completed once for each agent (**Enna Ahruneh**, **Desra Nekri**, **Galhar Lahara**, **Rahli Saronu**).
