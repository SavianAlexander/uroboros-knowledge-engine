# Angel Capital Staging

Angel Capital Staging is an escalation site released on the 11th of June 2024 under the [Equinox](https://www.eveonline.com/now/equinox) expansion and is designed to encourage the usage of capital class ships for running PVE within **Nullsec**.

The site features three Angel Dreadnaughts as well as a large number of sub-capital ships and is designed to be run by a small fleet of players using both capital and sub-capital ships however this site can be easily cleared by a Supercarrier or Dreadnought pilot solo.
