# Clear the Way

<noinclude> </noinclude>

Single gated deadspace pocket. Initial defenders and all subsequent waves will be roughly 30 km away from warp-in.

The mission can be completed and the next mission accepted remotely.

|}

|}

|}

After wave 2 is cleared you'll receive a completion message from the agent and a friendly **Angel Recovery Team** hauler will spawn in. This is purely cosmetic and you can complete the mission immediately.
