# Break Their Will (Blood Raiders) (Level 3)

Repair Station repairs itself and rats. Destroying the Aux Power Arrays disables the Repair Station's repair function.

  - Blitz:**
- Destroy Repair Station, warp out.

  1. # Warp-in
At warp-in there are no rats. Wave 1 spawns when the repair station is hit. Wave 2 is spawned when the Repair Station is destroyed. Auto-aggro on waves spawning.

|}

|}

|}

Destroying the Repair Station (Mission Objective) results in a standing loss of 0.4 with the Blood Raider Covenant. Destroying the Missile Batteries results in a further 0.4 Standing loss with the Blood Raiders.

Mining: 1,500,000 Scordite and 2,834,000 Veldspar
