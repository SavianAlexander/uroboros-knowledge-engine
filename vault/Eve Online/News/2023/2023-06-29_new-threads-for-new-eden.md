Announcing the Winners of the T-Shirt Design Contest

---

### Capsuleers,


The creativity of the EVE community never ceases to amaze and delight. Recently, in celebration of 20 years of EVE Online, we reached out and asked talented capsuleers to come up with original artwork, the best of which would be featured on special T-shirts, sold at the EVE Fanfest 2023 and in the EVE Merch Store. In addition to this, the best designs got pretty sweet prizes, which you can read more about here: 

To no one’s surprise, the submissions were stunning and it was thrilling to see so many submissions from our talented community of players. We would like to thank everyone who took part, and congratulate the winners.
