Yet again you will have the opportunity to blow stuff up in front of a big crowd at the Fanfest, as you read in a [dev blog earlier](//myeve.eve-online.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&bid=576), there is a new element to the tournament - mining! We have now put up the full set of rules for this year's tournament which you can see [here](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2008/pvprules.asp). For discussions and questions regarding the tournament, please check [this thread](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=858247).

We have also revised the Jita area this year. Jita is an area at the Fanfest where you will be able to gather your corporation or alliance, or why not your militia if you're into Factional Warfare? Instead of having you sign up before the Fanfest you will be able to rent an office directly at Fanfest, just like in EVE! All information you ever wanted about renting an office in Jita can be found [right here](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2008/jita.asp).
