# Raid

The Raid site features a central structure, a friendly NPC Freighter, and a defending hostile fleet tasked with protecting the area. During the encounter, hostile haulers will periodically spawn in close proximity to the structure and immediately attempt to align and warp out to safety.  

The primary objective is to tackle and destroy at least 4 of these haulers before they can escape, then recover their cargo (each with a volume of 3500) and successfully deliver it to the freighter located on the field. All of this must be accomplished within the strict 20-minute time limit, adding pressure to act quickly and efficiently.  

Be advised that some enemy ships (see **Hostiles** below) will aggressively target any launched drones.

1. # Fleet size & composition
- Optimal fleet size: 5 ships
- Fleet composition: T1 Cruisers (DPS), T1 Logistic Cruisers (Logi), T1 Hauler

1. # Type of ships allowed
This homefront operation takes place in a **deadspace** pocket with an acceleration gate that has ship restrictions.

- Tech 1 frigates, navy and pirate frigates
- Tech 1 destroyers, navy and pirate destroyers
- Tech 1 cruisers
- Tech 1 haulers, including the **Squall**
- **Venture**, **Venture Consortium Issue**
- **Pioneer**, **Pioneer Consortium Issue**, **Perseverance**

1. # Interaction & standings
Standing is lost when killing the pirate haulers in Raid operations in Amarr (**Blood Raiders**), Caldari (**Guristas**), and Gallente (**Serpentis**) space. However, in Minmatar space, engaging the **Ashes of Turnur**, a criminal Minmatar faction, does not result in any standing gains or losses.

1. # Rewards
Rewards for Raid sites consists of raw ISK payout upon completion, following the standard 5-pilot fleet payout curve with in a maximum payout of 15,000,000 ISK payout per pilot with a fleet of 5. 

To be eligible for payout, pilots need to contribute to the site in one of three ways:

- Deal 1000 damage
- Remote shield or armor repair 1000 damage
- Deliver cargo to the NPC freighter

Any pilots in the site who did not contribute will not get paid and will not count in the calculation for the rest of the fleet.
