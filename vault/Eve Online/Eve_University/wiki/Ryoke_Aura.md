# Ryoke Aura

- Ryoke Aura** is an agent in one of the chains of agents in **Caldari COSMOS**. The chain consists of (ordered):
- **Akira Helkelen**
- **Ryoke Aura**
- **Goru Nikainen**
- **Kochi Utrainen**
- **Hitami Magye**
- **Mintu Oshima**
Each agent in the chain provides items required for starting or completing missions for the next agent in the chain.

1. # Wrongfully Accused - Prove Your Worth (1 of 4)

Provide 1x Neophite from **Akira Helkelen**.

1. # Wrongfully Accused - Who Shot the Sheriff (2 of 4)

Provide 1 Sheriff Togany (1 m3) from Otitoh VII - Moon 5 to agent.
