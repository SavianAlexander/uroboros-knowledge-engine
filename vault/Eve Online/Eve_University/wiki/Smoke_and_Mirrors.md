# Smoke and Mirrors

<noinclude> </noinclude>

Gated deadspace pocket up to eights jumps away. Your task is to play a game for Eroma Eralen. Follow their instructions to complete the Mission.

  - Blitz:** approach the Guristas Warehouse, destroy the Cruiser Pithoriate, repeat three time. Finally, destroy the 4th Guristas Warehouse and approach Eroma Eralen.

Complete the Mission remotely.

Tips:
- Get within 4 km of the first Guristas Warehouse to spawn enemies
- When you destroy the Pithoriate Cruiser, the Pithoriate Frigates warp out
