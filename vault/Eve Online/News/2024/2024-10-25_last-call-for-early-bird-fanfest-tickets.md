## Interstellar Guest Speakers


Fanfest 2025 will feature an exceptional lineup of guest speakers, including prominent voices from both the scientific community and the EVE universe.  

[Dr. Becky Smethurst](https://rebeccasmethurst.co.uk/), an astrophysicist and renowned science communicator, is set to share her expertise on the cosmos. Known for her weekly astronomy and space-related content on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@DrBecky), Dr. Becky’s videos draw up to 4 million views and she boasts over 766,000 subscribers. She also posts engaging science content on [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@drbecky_s), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/drbecky_s/), and [X](https://twitter.com/drbecky_). At Fanfest, Dr. Becky will delve into the wonders of the universe, linking real-world astrophysics with the vast and imaginative cosmos of New Eden, making complex science both accessible and exciting for all capsuleers. 

Equally anticipated is [Dr. Ronald Turner](https://www.nasa.gov/people/ronald-turner/), a senior scientist with extensive experience in space radiation and human spaceflight. As a longtime advisor to NASA, Dr. Turner brings unparalleled insight into the realities of space exploration. But more than that, he’s also a veteran EVE Online player, offering him a unique perspective on how real-world space challenges parallel the adventures and dangers capsuleers face in the EVE universe.  


## Amazing Activities


As ever, Fanfest attendees will have the option to participate in [amazing activities](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/eve-fanfest-week-2025), including the infamous Party at the Top of the World, stunning tours with EVE Devs, a pub crawl, a charity dinner, and much more. That’s in addition to the fun-filled schedule in [Harpa](https://www.harpa.is/en/about-harpa), the majestic conference hall in the heart of Reykjavik. 

Friday and Saturday are the main program dates, but lots is happening in the lead-up. Fanfest week 2025 kicks off on **Monday 28 April**, with extra events and social gatherings happening throughout the week.
