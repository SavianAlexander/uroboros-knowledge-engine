# Rancorous Researcher

- Rancorous Researcher** is a 5-part **mission chain** issued by Distribution Division agents. The chain consists of a Courier and 4 Encounter missions. While they are classed as Encounter there is no combat, except for destroying an unguarded structure. A T1 Exploration Frigate can complete the missions. Make sure to have MWD or Afterburner equipped for mission 3, as you will be flying between beacons as they appear.

1. # Rancorous Researcher (3 of 5)

  - Note:** You must have Large Collidable Structure selected in the Overview to see the Beacon. You will find it listed under Entity - not Celestial.

Approach the 5 beacons in order. The next one will appear once you are close to the current one.

  - Remark:** Sometimes, even when the "Device" is inside the cargohold, the opportunities overview may present a tooltip saying that you need the "Device" in your cargohold. If you indeed have the device, you may ignore the tooltip and approach the 5 beacons in order.
