# Scandal In Barkrik - Retrieve The Inspector (2 of 4)

<noinclude>**Minmatar COSMOS** mission
Agent: **Mwaku Ristiger**
Agent location: The Hyperbole Nexus(Barkrik)
Standings required: 4.00 Minmatar Republic
Parts: **(1 of 4)**, **(2 of 4)**, **(3 of 4)**, **(4 of 4)**</noinclude>

In this mission you will need to travel through multiple deadspace pockets and burn hundreds of kilometers to reach the next gate in them. The briefing says that MWDs won't work but that is outdated information and they do work.

No enemy ships need to be destroyed (you should not engage any ships in the mission). Just fly until you reach the final pocket and grab the mission item. No NPC will aggress, even when you are at 0 on the can in the final pocket. <noinclude>
