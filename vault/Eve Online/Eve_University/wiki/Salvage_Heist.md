# Salvage Heist

<noinclude> </noinclude>

Single gated deadspace pocket.

Wave 1 is triggered when Gath Renton is first damaged, Wave 2 when Gath Renton's armor is damaged, and Wave 3 shortly after Gath Renton's hull is damaged.

  - Blitz:** Kill Gath Renton while tanking the other spawns.

The mission can be completed and the next mission accepted remotely.

No standing loss for destroying Angel sentries. Initial defender spawn is 60 km from warp-in and does not auto-aggro. Sentries have limited aggro range out to ~25 km.
