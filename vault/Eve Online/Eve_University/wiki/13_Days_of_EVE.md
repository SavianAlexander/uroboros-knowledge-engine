# 13 Days of EVE

- 13 Days of EVE** is an annual login campaign, which so far has been ran mainly through December starting in 2018.

Links to specific annual campaigns:
- **13 Days of EVE (2018)**, 2018-12-11 – 2018-12-28, partially overlapping with **Operation Permafrost**
- **13 Days of EVE (2019)**, 2019-12-12 – 2020-01-07, part of **Naughty or Nice**
