# A Wolf by the Ears

Mission is in normal space so bookmarks and fleet members can be warped too freely. The carrier has 150km energy neutralizer range for 40gj/s.

Small drones are very good at speed tanking the fighters. The fighters will aggro drones if they are the closest thing when they spawn.

  1. # Warp-in
Carrier and fighters aggro immediately. The rest of ships aggro on engagement. Ships can be aggroed separately if you warp in far enough from the right group. Only the Imperial Templar Seer has tracking disrupter and energy neutralizer.
