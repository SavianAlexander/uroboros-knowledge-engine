# Raging Sansha

<noinclude> 
</noinclude>

Video: [[Typhoon](https://youtu.be/6iwXj9puNAc) June 2021]

  - Tips:**
- Initial aggro can be as low as a single frigate.
- To minimize incoming damage during this mission, prioritize battleships (except trigger), then battlecruisers , and leave cruisers for last.
- Attacking Sansha Commander (usually) triggers wave 1. Wave 1 does not need to be triggered so if you can tank the commander you can ignore him.
  - Clearing waves 2 and 3 before attacking the Commander did not trigger wave 1 (19 May 2026). The wave 1 trigger may be chance based.
- Mission is completed when the cruisers and the frigates in wave 4 are destroyed, ignore the battleships.
- Mission can be accepted and completed remotely.
