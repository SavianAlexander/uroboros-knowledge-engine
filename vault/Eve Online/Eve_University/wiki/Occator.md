1. # Summary
The **Occator** is the Gallente Deep Space Transport. Due to its tank and warp strength bonuses, it's a difficult ship to suicide gank in **hisec**. It has a fairly small cargohold, but instead has a 50,000 m³ Fleet Hangar, which can carry anything including assembled ships. The Fleet Hangar size is increased by the  skill to 62,500 m³ (at level 5). Notably, Transport Ships IV gives 60,000 m³ and allows for transporting 3 packaged industrial ships, 4 battlecruisers or 6 cruisers (at 20,000 m³, 15,000 m³ or 10,000 m³ per hull respectively).

The Occator can also fit a Medium **Micro Jump Drive**.
