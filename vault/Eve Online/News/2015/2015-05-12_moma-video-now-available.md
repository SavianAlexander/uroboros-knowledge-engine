In early 2013, EVE Online was inducted in The Museum of Modern Art (NY) in the Applied Design artistic category as one of the first 14 inaugural video games added to the permanent collection alongside Pac Man, Tetris, The Sims, and more.

From March 2, 2013–January 20, 2014 it was part of an installation at MoMA, showcased by an infographic video, which features recordings made by EVE players going about their lives in New Eden on one particular day: December 9, 2012. 

Now that the exhibit has concluded, we'd like to share this video with the EVE Community and the world at large, so you can now find it in all its glory on our [YouTube channel here!](//www.youtube.com/watch?v=EGuDUbZIo_o)
