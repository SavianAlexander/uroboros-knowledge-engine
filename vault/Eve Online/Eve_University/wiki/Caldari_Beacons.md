# Caldari Beacons

Each neut tower hits for 40gj/s for a combined 80gj/s. With the heavy jamming either a marauder or a drone boat is ideal.

Even though the mission says the objective is to destroy the Subspace Beacons, it is flagged completed upon clearing Pocket 2 even if the beacons and their spawns are not triggered or killed.

No hostiles at initial gate.

  1. # 1st Pocket
Ships are arrayed between 50km and 90km away and deal a combined 2,280dps (53%kin/47%therm).

|}

  1. # 2nd Pocket
Ships are arrayed between 30km and 75km away and deal a combined 2,480dps (48%kin/52%therm). An additional group is spawn for each Subspace Beacon destroyed. State Shukuro Nagashin's deal heavier then average thermal damage.
