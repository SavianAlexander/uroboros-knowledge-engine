# Triglavian minor victory

- Triglavian minor victory** systems are systems in which **Triglavian Collective** forces have a permanent presence in empire space. These systems were won by the Triglavian side during the **third chapter of the Triglavian invasion** but were not the correct type to be eligible to turn into **final liminality** and be integrated into **Pochven**.

Prior to the **“Equinox”** expansion (June 2024) these systems were inherently dangerous for people with zero or negative Triglavian standing. The gates in these systems were sometimes camped by Entropic Disintegrator Werpost deployment fleets which considered players with 0 or negative standing with The Triglavian Collective as hostile.
 
The [Equinox Expansion Notes PvP](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/equinox-expansion-notes#h2-16) section identifies some aspects of Minor Victory systems that have changed, including the following:
- Entropic Disintegrator Werposts and their deployment fleets no longer consider players with 0 standing with The Triglavian Collective as hostile.
- The Triglavian Collective no longer deploy Entropic Disintegrator Werposts at Stargates, Stations and Upwell structures in Minor Victory systems.

1. # Features

Features that set Triglavian minor victory systems apart from normal systems:
- Patrolling Triglavian fleets. These warp around the system and can be found on stargates, stations, asteroid belts and POCOs. Always scout the gates if you have to travel through these systems and don't have positive Triglavian standing, although most of them can be avoided by the autopilot setting.
