# Serpentis Forsaken Hub

- Serpentis Forsaken Hub** is an **combat anomaly** found in Serpentis-infested low and null security regions.

1. # Walkthrough
The site consists of one ungated pocket. There are three Broken Orange Crystal Asteroids near the warp-in. New wave spawns once ships from previous spawn are dead.
