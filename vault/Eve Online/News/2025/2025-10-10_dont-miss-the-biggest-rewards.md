## Overview
Make the most of the Crimson Harvest event and loot.

---

Capsuleers,

The Crimson Harvest is raging, and the conflict between the Blood Raiders and the Order of St. Tetrimon shows no signs of slowing down.

The last day to begin logging in with a chance to claim the big Skill Point haul is 11 October, so make sure you don’t miss out. The day 24 haul gives Alphas 75,000 SP, and Omegas an additional 250,000 for a grand total of 325,000 SP. In total, the track awards 125,000 SP to Alphas, and 525,000 to Omegas, for a whopping 650,000 SP in all!

Capsuleers upgrading will also receive all previous Omega rewards retroactively from the beginning of the event.

Then, on 28 October, the conflict will intensify and the seasonal reward track will extend, with a crate featuring a Blood Raider ship blueprint dropping at the end, so start tackling the different sites available today. From familiar, returning combat and exploration sites, to new Biocybernetic Incident sites across all space – highsec, lowsec, nullsec, wormholes, and Pochven - there’s something for capsuleers of all experience levels.

Tetrimon Base and Crimson Gauntlet sites are now capable of triggering escalations across all normal space. When you defeat the Tetrimon Commander or Harvest Overseer enemies in those respective sites, be sure to check out the decloaked transmission relay structure for more loot, and destroy it for a chance to trigger the escalation sites. Also, don’t forget to check out any suspicious wrecks at these sites as any resulting encounter will bring even more riches!

New Deathglow Remnant SKINs in all their crimson glory are obtainable by logging in every day and through the seasonal reward track for completing event challenges, alongside other treasures including PLEX, Skill Points, SKINR components, and boosters.

As usual during the Crimson Harvest, PvP combat becomes just that bit more exciting, with 90% loot drop now live – but only outside highsec . In addition, pods continue to have a 50% implant drop rate. This adds even more risk and reward to the chaos.

If you need help to tackle the many activities on offer during the Crimson Harvest, perhaps even moving on to the more challenging and lucrative sites, updated Community Fittings are now available. 

If you’re looking for more help, capsuleers across New Eden have already put together excellent community guides and videos to get you through any stumbling blocks.

Head to [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/directory/category/eve-online)to find many different players trying out the Crimson Harvest sites, where they are very welcoming to question, and track your progress through the sites using [EVE Journal](https://evejournal.com/) from the same people who brought you [EVE Workbench.](https://eveworkbench.com/)
