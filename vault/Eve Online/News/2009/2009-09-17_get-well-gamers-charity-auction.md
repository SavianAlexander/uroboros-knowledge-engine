[Get-Well Gamers](//www.get-well-gamers.org/), a charity that provides video games for children’s hospitals, will be selling raffle tickets at this week’s Austin Game Developers Conference. A mere $5 donation gives you a chance to win a [Caldari Raven](//store.eve-online.com/Caldari-Raven-EVE-Battleship-Collection-P121.aspx) from the EVE Battleship Collection. Raffle tickets may be purchased at the CCP booth or online via [Network for Good](//www.tinyurl.com/getwellgamers). Read this [post](//www.eveonline.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=1178895) for more information.
