# Hidden Riches

- Hidden Riches** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **Sansha Outpost**.
1. # Walkthrough
If True Sansha cruiser does not spawn take acceleration gate. The first three locations are usually in lowsec and the last one in nullsec but this can vary.
  1. # First Location(lowsec)
You land at 0 on rats and an acceleration gate. 

|}
  1. ## First Pocket
You land among some rocks and a ghost ship. There are no enemies present.

  1. # Second Location(lowsec)
Again you land at an acceleration gate at 0 on rats.

|}

  1. ## First Pocket
You land among the ruins of an Amarr station. No rats are present.
  1. # Third Location(lowsec)
You land again at 0 on the rats and another acceleration gate.

|}

  1. # Fourth Location(nullsec)
Warping to the location lands you at an acceleration gate.
  1. ## First Pocket
Acceleration gate is ~45 km away.

|}
  1. ## Second Pocket
