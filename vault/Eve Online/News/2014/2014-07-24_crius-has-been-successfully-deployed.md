We are delighted to announce that the Crius release has been successfully deployed.

Crius brings radical [changes to industry](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/eve-industry-all-you-want-to-know/), new [industrial functionality and endpoints](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/industry-3rd-party-developers/) in the API, [in-space user interface improvements](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/in-space-brackets-revisited-with-crius/), more [little things](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/little-things-coming-to-eve-online-in-crius/), the [introduction of teams](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/teams-and-the-revamp-of-industry-in-eve-online/), an [overhaul of reprocessing](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/reprocess-all-the-things/), more[starbase changes](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/starbase-changes-for-crius/), and a whole host of other improvements. This release is set to reshape industrial gameplay in EVE, streamlining processes and giving more power to the most important part of EVE Online; our players.

For further information you can read CCP Seagull's [Coming in Crius](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/coming-in-crius-introducing-whats-in-eves-big-industry-release/) overview blog, or you can take a look at the [Crius feature site](//community.eveonline.com/releases/crius/). For a more granular look at the changes, see the full patch notes, which [are available now](//community.eveonline.com/news/patch-notes/patch-notes-for-crius).

A general feedback thread is [available here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=4827431), and a known issues thread is [available here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=4827417). For Macintosh specific issues and feedback, please see [this thread](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=4827450).
