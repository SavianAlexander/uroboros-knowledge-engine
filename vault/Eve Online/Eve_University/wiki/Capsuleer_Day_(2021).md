# Capsuleer Day (2021)

Capsuleer Day is celebration of EVE Online's birthday. On May 6th 2021 EVE Online celebrated its 18th birthday. This event is part of an annual **Capsuleer Day** event. The Capsuleer Day celebration ended on the May 18th 2021. During the event, the 2 special sites **EoM Rogue Capital Shipyard** and **EoM Cyno Network Spoofing Facility** were available.

1. # Login Campaign
The  login campaign ran from May 6th to may 18th. The SKIN rewards are redeemed directly to the character and can't be traded.

1. # EoM Rogue Capital Shipyard
The site appears as a combat anomaly in the probe scanner window. Once warp to the site is initiated the site will also appear as a beacon in the overview, revealing to everyone that someone has warped to it. Up to T2 Battleships can enter the site. It consists of two deadspace pockets, both behind **acceleration gates**. The first room has three waves of frigates, destroyers, cruisers and rarely a battlecruiser. After destroying all rats of the second wave, the **Acceleration gate** to the second room becomes active. The second room has again three waves of frigates, destroyers, cruisers and rarely a battlecruiser. After destroying all rats of the second wave, the defense commander spawns in a battleship. His destruction makes the shipyard structure vulnerable. Destroying it drops a cargo container with valuable loot and completes the site. All remaining rats warp of.

The second and third waves for both rooms appear to be timed.

The easiest site is located in high-sec.  Medium-difficulty sites can be found in null-sec and wormhole space, and the hardest, high-reward sites are found in low-sec. Expect around 300 DPS in High-sec sites, 400 in Null/W-Space, and 500 in Low-sec. Low and nullsec sites will have more enemies spawn (usually around 2 battlecruisers).

1. # EoM Cyno Network Spoofing Facility
These data sites appear as cosmic signatures and require scanning with probes. All Sites have a scanning difficulty of 3 (around 62 Probe Strength). The site contains one hackable structure, which can be opened using a **Data Analyzer** and solving the **hacking** mini-game. Failing the hack twice will destroy the structure.

Easier variant can be found in high security, null security and wormhole space. The better and harder variant can be found in low security space.

1. # Loot
The combat and hacking sites drop various items.

The data site drops additional normal data site loot such as datacores, decryptors and blueprint copies.

  1. # Common loot from both site types
- Overseer's Personal Effects 6th Tier - 18th Tier
- Agency drugs
- Capsule Cascade Fireworks
- Capsuleer Day Signal-18 'Needlejack' Filament
- Caldari Navy, Republic Fleet, Imperial Navy and Federation Navy SKINs
- Versus Blueforce and Versus Redforce SKINs
- Forward 4-4 SKINs for Griffin Navy Issue, Scorpion Navy Issue and Raven Navy Issue
