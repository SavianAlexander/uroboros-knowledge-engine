Tomorrow, December 7th, following an idea originally suggested by Duracell on our forums, Eve players are planning an all-day event they have dubbed "The Big Log-On" in an effort to take the number of simultaneous users to a record-breaking 10,000.

Eve Radio will be doing a 24-hour live simulcast (see [//www.eve-radio.com](//www.eve-radio.com) for details) featuring give-aways, guest DJ TomB of the Eve dev team, and other surprises.

Log in and be a part of gaming history!
