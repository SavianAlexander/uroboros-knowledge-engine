# Duo Nebula

The **Duo Nebula** is a gas site found in null sec through Exploration.

This site can only be found in the Catch region of space. The site has no defender NPCs.

1. # Gas Clouds

2 Lime Cytoserocin clouds (2,500 units + 500 units). When the gas harvesters on your ship finish cycling, there is a chance to receive 1,000 EM + 1,000 thermal damage.
