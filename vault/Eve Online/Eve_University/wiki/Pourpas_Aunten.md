  - Pourpas Aunten** is a **Gallente COSMOS** site found in Jolia system. It has no beacon and requires scanning. Alternatively you can warp to planet VII asteroid belt 1 to find the site. The site has a single cruiser in it that deals a fair amount of damage compared to other cruisers its size. It may be escorted by two frigates.
