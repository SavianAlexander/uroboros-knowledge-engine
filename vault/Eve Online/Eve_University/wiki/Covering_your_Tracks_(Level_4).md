# Covering your Tracks (Level 4)

1. # Warp-in
Single, ungated pocket - you can warp in at preferred distance.

|}

1. # Rewards
Loot & Salvage: ~4M (chance of faction loot on top)

Bounties: ~1.3M 

- While fairly low value in bounties and loot, this mission is quick to complete and rewards 0.9 faction standings.*

  1. # Notes
- Ratah Niaga is weakest to  on shield and  on armor.
- Tauron is weakest to  on shield and armor.
- Taisu Magdesh weakest to  on shield and  on armor.
- A faction booster shield worth more than 40 million ISK "may" drop from the BS.
- The Abandoned Stargate drops metal scraps.
