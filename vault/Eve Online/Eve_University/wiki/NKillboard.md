# NKillboard

- nKillboard** is a third-party web utility designed to track Player vs Environment (PVE) activity across New Eden. Operating similarly to traditional killboards, it is dedicated exclusively to logging and analyzing NPC combat interactions, while also providing comprehensive industrial and combat guides for the community.

1. # Core Features
  1. # PvE Kill Tracking
The primary function of **nKillboard** is to aggregate and visualize a pilot's complete PVE combat history. By processing historical data and bounties, it offers an analytical breakdown of a capsuleers' ratting performance, including:
- **30-Day Bounty Tracking:** A comprehensive list of all NPC kills accumulated over the past 30 days and onwards, organized cleanly by individual bounty ticks.
- **Ship-by-Ship Bounty Breakdown:** An itemized look into every individual bounty tick, showing the exact NPC ship types destroyed and the precise ISK earned from each specific hull.
- **Bounty Tick Statistics:** Instant visibility into your performance metrics, highlighting your highest recorded bounty tick alongside a detailed earnings breakdown of your last 10 consecutive ticks.
- **Combat Hotspots & Targets:** Clear analytics displaying your top solar systems for NPC hunting and your most frequently destroyed NPC ship types.
- **Activity Heatmap:** A visual 90-day activity feed, allowing you to track your daily PVE engagement and consistency at a glance.
