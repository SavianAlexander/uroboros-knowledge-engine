1. # Summary
The InterBus shuttle is a special release version of the standard Gallente shuttle. Originally released as a bonus to those who bought the boxed version of EVE Online, it has since only been available in the InterBus Collector’s Pack released in September of 2022. It does have an advantage over the standard shuttle by having twice the cargo capacity, but due to the rarity it is better to use a frigate or larger ship if you need the extra capacity.
