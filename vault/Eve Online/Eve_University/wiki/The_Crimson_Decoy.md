# The Crimson Decoy

<noinclude>   </noinclude>

Ungated normal space.

  - Blitz:** Put the NOH Signal Operator into the container and return to the agent. For fast delivery you can create a bounce BM as you land, warp to the bounce BM and then warp to an asteroid near the container.

  - Tip:** Warp to 100km and lead the initial group out to 150 to 175km range. Keep at least 30km range from the 7 Corpus battleships (Neut). Destroy the 2 Dark Corpus Harbinger Battleships last to trigger spawn.
