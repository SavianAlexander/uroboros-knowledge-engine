# Links

- Links** may refer to:

- **Command Bursts**, sometimes referred to as links as shorthand from its predecessor, Warfare Links
- **Entosis Link**, a module used to capture & defend **Sovereignty** structures
- Remote assistance modules, such as **Remote Tracking Computers**
