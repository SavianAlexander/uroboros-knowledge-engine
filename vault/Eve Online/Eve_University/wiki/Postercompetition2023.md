# Postercompetition2023

1. # Results of the 2023 Poster Competition - Staff Recruitment in EVE University

Find following the results and all submissions for the EVE University Poster Competition with the topic: Recruiting Staff.

Unistas were asked to submit posters with general call to actions for recruitment or with department-specific slogans.

  1. # Results
- 1st Prize - **Shaemus O'Conor** - won 2x Advanced Cruiser or Battle Cruiser of EVE University's Kiting Small Gang doctrine & 1x Scope Syndication Skin
- 2nd Prize - **Traven O_Toole** - won 2x Intermediate Cruiser or Battle Cruiser of EVE University's Kiting Small Gang doctrine & 1x Scope Syndication Skin
- 3rd Prize - **Amoni Panala** - won 1x Intermediate Cruiser or Battle Cruiser of EVE University's Kiting Small Gang doctrine & 1x Scope Syndication Skin

Following Unistas also participated and won 1x Scope Syndication Skin each:
- **Psychotic Fickity**
- **Rebel Unista**
- **Red u1**

On behalf of the **Communications Department**, we would like to thank all participants!
