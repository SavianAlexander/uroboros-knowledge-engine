# Maekor's Plantation

- Maekor's Plantation** is a static COSMOS deadspace complex found in Barkrik system. The site needs to be scanned down with probes. Alternatively you can warp to Planet V moon 3 where the site is located.

The complex is two pockets deep and allows all ships up to battleship size to enter. However, you need to bring an **Imperial Navy Captain Insignia I** to use the acceleration gate to the first pocket. The insignia is consumed on entering, but they are available in the market.

1. # Site details
You will face Imperial Navy forces in the COMPLEX and you will get a heavy -0.24 standing hit to the Amarr empire for shooting the ships, whether in first or second pocket. Beside that, it's a good place to farm Imperial Navy insignias, you also should be able to find a Captain Insignia to reenter the first gate another time.

  1. # Defensive Barrier
----
The first pocket. Amarr frigates and cruisers in 4 groups. The groups can be pulled individually. Imperial Navy Gamme Support and Imperial Navy Delta Support frigates web you when they get near. Nothing special in this pocket, the gate to the next pocket is not far from the entrance. 

  1. # The Plantation
----
Very similar to the first pocket. 4 groups of frigates and cruisers, Imperial Navy Gamma Support frigates web. Groups can again be pulled individually, but watch out for drone proximity aggro. The one spawn container in the pocket contains Bono Zakan Corpse, needed for a COSMOS agent mission. Beside that nothing fancy.
