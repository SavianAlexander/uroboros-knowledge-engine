Mission is flagged complete when you finish destroying the Raider Outpost

- Use a Passive Tank for Pocket 2 or plan a capacitor that receives 320 GJ/s.
- Second pocket maximum damage dealing of all ships is 1574 hp/s (EM: 53% Therm: 47%)
