# Drifter Hive(Barbican)

This page is geared towards the Barbican(Cataclysmic Variable) Drifter Hive in J110145. For an overview of the Conflux Drifter Hive, go to **Drifter Hive Anomaly**. Sleeper rats will drop Sleeper blue loot, Autothysian Lancers drop Sleeper Data Libraries and sometimes an Antikythera Element, and Drifter battleships drop Antikythera Elements and Sleeper blue loot.
1. # Warpin
You land on a beacon named 'Barbican'. No rats are present here, although wandering Drifters may show up. The acceleration gate to the lobby is 51 km from warpin.

|}
1. # Lobby
Few Sleeper rats at ~60 km distance. In some occasions, wandering rats featuring a battleship and sometimes two cruiser escorts will spawn.

|}

|}

|}

1. # Barbican Alpha Transfer
A Sleeper hive anteroom that contains several elaborate Sleeper constructs as well as Sleeper defending rats and hackable containers.

|}

|}
1. # Barbican Beta Transfer
A secondary Sleeper anteroom defended by cruisers and battleships. The warpin is close to an acceleration gate leading back to the Alpha Transfer.

|}

|}
1. # Barbican Gamma Transfer
Large number of Sleepers 70-100 km from warpin. DPS is very high if all ships aggro.

|}

|}

1. # Central Drifter Hive
The main Drifter Hive structure. Drifter battleship close to warpin will orbit you at about 2 km/s with MWD. Rest of the sleepers are 150-200 km away.

|}

|}

|}

|}
1. # Nexus Area
A large area of space consisting of hundreds of 'bars' slowly floating in space and flanked by two massive whirlpool-like constructs.
