# Blood Hidden Den

The **Blood Hidden Den** is an **unrated cosmic signature combat site** that can be freely warped to in **Blood Raiders** owned territory of Low and Null Sec space. The site itself is a small area with no Acceleration Gate. Each wave spawns after the destruction of a specific trigger.
