On 13 September 2025 A different amount (45) and volume (4500) were reported. This seems to indicate that there is a version for another level (3?). Analysis of other storyline missions leads to the following:
- level 1 storyline courier missions appear to have a max cargo volume of 320
- other storyline courier missions with multiple levels seem to come in the combination of level 1 and level 3. Having volumes of 320 for level 1 and 4500 for level 3 i.e.:
  - **A Different Drone**
  - **A Greener World**
  - **Ancient Treasures**
  - **Culture Clash**
  - ...
Most reported level 3s in these combinations also have a little bit different description of the cargo usually at least containing the word "large".
