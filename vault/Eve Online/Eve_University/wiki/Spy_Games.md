# Spy Games

<noinclude> </noinclude>

Gated deadspace pockets are one jump away in AJCJ-1.

  - Blitz:** kill Eroma Eralen, take Acceleration Gate then another Acceleration Gate. Arc around to the opposite side of the Forcefield, destroy the Caldari Bunker with a 16 km range weapon and Tractor Beam the Correspondence Log KL-513 Cargo Container.

  - Alternative Blitz:** kill Eroma Eralen, loot the Caldari Navy Overlay Transponder, take Acceleration Gate then another Acceleration Gate. Approach the Caldari Bunker, destroy each wave as it spawns and then destroy the 150 HP/s regenerating Forcefield.

Tips:
- Orbit Eroma Eralen at 3 km to avoid damage
- If you don't attack the friendly ships, they won't attack you
- Caldari Bunker reinforcement waves spawn as you approach the Caldari Bunker
- All Caldari Bunker reinforcement waves will spawn when you attack the Caldari Bunker
- Enemy ships can't pass through the Forcefield (unless you encounter a bug where all ships can pass through the Forcefield)
- Caldari Bunker drops two Cargo Containers: one has ammo and the other the Correspondence Log KL-513
- You will lose 2.4% Caldari State Faction Standing when you destroy the Caldari Bunker (Note: Also lost standing for destroying the forcefield AND NOT the bunker)
