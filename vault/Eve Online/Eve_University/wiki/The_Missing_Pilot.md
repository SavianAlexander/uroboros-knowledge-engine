# The Missing Pilot

- The Missing Pilot** is the first part of the ORE epic arc **Fractured Legacy**.

Video: [[Fractured Legacy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t_ECwuKHIVY) 18 November 2025]

To start the mission, go to Kisogo VII,  dock up at the AIR Laboratories station and talk to Elias Peltonnen.

Accepting the mission will grant the  skillbook. The skill is required for the second mission. So if not know yet, start training immediately.

The actual mission is within 4-6 jumps away.

You will need to fully mine all 15 **Veldspar** asteroids, followed by the single Scordite asteroid, before you can attempt to hack the can.  You cannot use a Civilian Data Analyzer for the hacking and must use at least a Data Analyzer I.
