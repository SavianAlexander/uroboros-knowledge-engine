# Shadow of the Serpent

Shadow of the Serpent is an event introduced with patch 118.6 and introduces a new campaign against the rising threat of the **Serpentis** organization. With the recent revelation of Serpentis Capital ships, **CONCORD** and the Empires see the organization as much more of a threat. **The Scope** act as a broker for capsuleers to find opportunities to contribute to the campaign. 

This is to be the first in a sequence of events. 

The current event started at 11PM on 29th June 2016 and will run for 40 days (11PM on 8th August 2016). 

# Participation
1. # Challenges
Capsuleers can participate in Challenges that help in the campaign against Serpentis. The challenges are available via the Scope Network interface. Scope Network is visible in the Character selection screen and displays all active challenges available to the account. Scope Network is also available via the in-game Neocom menu.

- Only 4 active challenges are available to an account.
- Incomplete challenges will expire after 24 hours (although patch notes mentions 3 days).
- Points awarded are only valid until the end of the event.
- Completion of kills and sites are only tracked for active challenges.
- If multiple active challenges have similar goals then the kill/site is marked against only single challenge until it is complete.

1. # Rewards
Capsuleers unlock rewards based on the points achieved from challenges.

| Reward | Points needed |
| :--- | :--- |
| Copper Container | 10,000 |
| Silver Container | 25,000 |
| Gold Container | 50,000 |

  - Vehement** blueprint copies may be found in Gold Containers.

# Combat
1. # Sites
The following sites have been included for the Shadow of the Serpent event:
- Serpentis Shipyard and Research Facility
- Serpentis Mobilization Point
- Angel Cartel Assembly Point
- Serpentis Information Relay
- Angel Cartel Research Bunker
- Serpentis Transportation Facility
- Angel Cartel Mining Outpost
- Serpentis Sortie
- Angel Sortie

1. # Ratting
Roaming Serpentis and Angel Cartel Guards have been sighted at asteroid belts and stargates in High, Low, Null and Wormhole space.

# Ships
The Serpentis corporation have introduced 3 new capital ships:
    - Vehement** (Dreadnought)
    - Vendetta** (Supercarrier)
    - Vanquisher** (Titan]
