# Pandemic Horde Guides

This page was created to index the different Pandemic Horde Guides.

Most of these guides were made over the period of 2018 to 2025 by the first group inside Pandemic Horde, the New Bean Initiative, aka NBIs.  NBIs were focused on helping new players get better.  Some have drifted out of date, but the fundamentals are rarely changed.  These were trapped behind a walled forum garden, in a time before Discord only groups were a thing.

All Pandemic Horde Guides will be labeled: Horde-Guide_Name (subject to change)

- **Horde-Complex Reactions**
- **Horde-Favorite Ships for PVP**
- **Horde-Flying Logistics**
- **Horde-Multiboxing 101**
- **Horde-Planetary Industry Templates**
- **Horde-Reporting Intel**
- **Horde-SMT Guide**
- Your Guide here!  (Tell Kismeteer, I converted several as an experiment.)
