# Buyback Department

1. # What is the Buyback Department?
The **Buyback Department** is responsible for the buyback programs of the various communities and ensures that EVE University has an income and that members get paid.

The Buyback Department was split out of the **Quartermaster Department** in early 2024.

The prior iteration of the Buyback Department was closed and merged into the **Quartermaster Department** in May 2021.
