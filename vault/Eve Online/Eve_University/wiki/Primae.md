1. # Summary
The Primae is a limited run ship that has become available to players on very few occasions. It was first released as a promo for **Planetary Industry**, as it's the only ship that doesn't require *any* skills to fly. However, the **Epithal** vastly outperformed it at hauling for the purpose of PI, the very thing the Primae was marketed towards. Nowadays, it's just a collector's item and rarely seen undocked.
