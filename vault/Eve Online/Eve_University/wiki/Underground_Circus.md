# Underground Circus

<noinclude> </noinclude>

Gated deadspace pocket one jump away in Caslemon.

  - Blitz:** clear Room 1, take the Gate, destroy The Ringmaster's Pleasure Hub Structure, kill the The Ringmaster and pick up the Ringmaster from their ship wreck.

Tips:
- You cannot warp inside the Rooms
- There are three different Factions that you need to destroy
- The Ringmaster's Pleasure Hub has a lot of Structure Hitpoints
- Clear both Rooms if you are unsure about surviving
- Destroy the Sensor Dampening ships first
- The Ringmaster's smallest resistance is 74% Kinetic
