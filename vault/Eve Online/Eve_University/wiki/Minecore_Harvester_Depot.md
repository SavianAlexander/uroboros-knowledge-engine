# Minecore Harvester Depot

- Minecore Harvester Depot** is a **Angel COSMOS** combat anomaly located in the 3L-Y9M system. It is not static and needs to be scanned down.

Of note is the rather large ore belt of high sec material (Veldspar, Scordite) in the mission area. No information is available on the content of the rocks though, so it is not certain whether this is a viable mining site or not.
