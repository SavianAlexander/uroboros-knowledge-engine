# The Blockade (Blood Raiders) (Level 1)

1. # Single Pocket
Single non-gated pocket (warping at range and in-pocket warping are possible).

Reinforcements spawn at about 100-120km from the warp-in point in several groups, so it's recommended to use a Microwarpdrive or Afterburner.
