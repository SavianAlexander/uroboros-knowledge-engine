1. # Summary
The **Apocalypse Imperial Issue** (abbreviated "**Impoc**") was a limited special issue of the Apocalypse battleship of which only 5 have existed. Four were given out as prizes to the winners of the Amarr Succession Tournament in YC 105 (2003) to the champions representing House Kor-Azor. The winning team consisted of Kileak, Grikl, BlackViper, and team leader Ecliptical. The fifth was given out in a lottery by BIG corporation to Ionstar.
