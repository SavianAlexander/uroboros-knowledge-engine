# Fanning the Flames (1 of 3)

<noinclude>
  - Gallente COSMOS** mission
Agent: **Croir Arghe** 
Agent location: Arid Park (Alsottobier)
Standings required: 6.00 (or perhaps 5.00)
Parts: **(1 of 3)**, **(2 of 3)**, **(3 of 3)**</noinclude>

Simple hauling mission. An explorer frigate can easily get enough cargo space and they are quite fast compared to hauling ships. The mission can be completed remotely but you need to travel back to the agent to accept the next stage.<noinclude>
