# Minmatar Alliance Defense Brigade

- Minmatar Alliance Defense Brigade** is a **Minmatar COSMOS**  site and the location of the mission **The Audesder War Effort, Striking At The Blockade (7 of 8)** in the **Minas Iksan** mission series. Both Minmatar Republic and Gallente Federation ships will be found in this brigade.

|}
  - WARNING:** In order to get to the Minmatar Command Post, you need to destroy the Extremely Powerful EM Forcefield which will cause -0.24 standing hit towards Minmatar Republic.
