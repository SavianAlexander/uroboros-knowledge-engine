## Overview
Make the most of the Winter Nexus event and loot.

Capsuleers,

The winter storms are raging, and the Yoiul LADs are roaming in storm-affected systems across known space. Today is the day to jump in, as the biggest SP haul will drop on day 26.


## Hunt ALL the Yoiul LADs


If you haven’t noticed, the Yoiul LADs can unlock the Aurora Universalis SKIN for the Outrider and Odysseus if you hunt them in the order they are presented to you as seasonal challenges.

Also, keep your eye out for rare “express” versions of the LADs, which are guaranteed to drop Cryo Tech, and may have other valuables in their hold.


## Start Logging in Today


The last day to begin logging in with a chance to claim the big Skill Point haul is 17 December, so make sure you don’t miss out.

The day 26 haul gives Alphas 75,000 SP, and Omegas an additional 250,000 for a grand total of 325,000 SP. In total, the track awards 125,000 SP to Alphas, and 525,000 to Omegas, for a whopping 650,000 SP in all!

Capsuleers upgrading will also receive all previous Omega rewards retroactively from the beginning of the event.

Take on combat, hacking, mining, and other challenges to earn points on the seasonal track, giving you rewards including the new Perseverance ice mining destroyer. Hunt Yoiul LADs for points, but also Cryo Tech that unlocks greater challenges and loot in Winter Nexus sites.

On 5 January, the seasonal track will extend, giving you more reasons to keep active in the ice storms.

Stunning new Aurora Universalis SKINs are obtainable by logging in every day and through event challenges, alongside other treasures including PLEX, Skill Points, SKINR components, and boosters.


## Paragon Hub Discounts


SKINdustrialists can rejoice, since there are great discounts in the Paragon Hub, making sequencing your SKINR creations cheaper and faster than ever! These discounts are active for the duration of the Winter Nexus – until 12 January.
