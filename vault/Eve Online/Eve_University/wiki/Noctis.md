1. # Summary
The **Noctis** is the last word in salvaging, and is a considerable upgrade over a salvage fitted destroyer. Besides its bonuses to tractor beams and salvagers it also sports a large cargo bay, able to store loot from even the most destructive level 4 missions. The Noctis is expensive however, and is unlikely to be worth it for a player who is not yet running at least level 3 missions.

1. # Skills

 - to be able to fit Small Tractor Beam I

1. # Tactics
Accept the mission, complete the mission with your battle-fitted ship, bookmark a wreck before you leave, and turn in the mission. Hop in the Noctis, warp to the bookmark and salvage and loot all the wrecks.

Remember to bookmark a wreck in each deadspace pocket if there are multiple pockets.

For extra efficiency do several missions, then hop in the Noctis and salvage them all. Remember not to leave it longer than 2 hours!

1. # Notes
- Noctis* is the genitive form of the Latin noun *nox*, meaning "night"; the ship's name means "of night".
