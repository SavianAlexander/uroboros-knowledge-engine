# The Lai Dai Research Project - Ore Shipment (1 of 3)

<noinclude>
  - Caldari COSMOS** mission
Agent: **Matani Jitainen**
Agent location: Frontier Stockade (Sakkikainen)
Standings required: none
Parts: **(1 of 3)**, **(2 of 3)**, **(3 of 3)**</noinclude>

Simple courier mission. The cargo easily fits in a normal frigate. You can also complete the mission instantly if you brought your own ore or buy it - but be careful: Market traders often offer the Veldspar for a very expensive price at mission hubs.<noinclude>
