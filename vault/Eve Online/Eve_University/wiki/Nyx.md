1. # Summary
The **Nyx** is the Gallente **supercarrier**. With a damage bonus to fighters and fighter-bombers of the **Hel** but with two more low slots for damage or maneuverability, it is immensely popular among null-sec and low-sec alliances. This makes it a common sight in null-sec supercapital fleets.

1. # Skills
- Due to skill bonuses, it's recommended to have  trained to at least III, and getting IV/V could be high priority for any Nyx pilot.*

1. # Notes
In Greek mythology, Nyx was the goddess of night, consort of **Erebus**, and mother of **Thanatos**, **Moros**, and **Nemesis**, among others.
