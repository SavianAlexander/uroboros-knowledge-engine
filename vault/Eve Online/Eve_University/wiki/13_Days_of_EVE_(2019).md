# 13 Days of EVE (2019)

- 13 Days of EVE** was a login reward event that ran from December 12th, 2019 to January 7th, 2020. Capsuleers had 26 days to log in for 13 days of rewards. The event rewarded **Skill Points** (up to 60k SP for Alpha clones, and up to 720k SP for Omega clones), Aurora Universalis SKINs, Yoiul Festival snowballs, and special nullsec holiday filaments. This event was part of the **Naughty or Nice** holiday event, alongside the Chilling Spree.

1. # Rewards Table

All rewards groups with more than 1 item were distributed in a crate. The crate names are in *italics*, with the items inside each listed below them. Rewards were also assigned tiers, with each tier having a different color.

The filaments will expire on January 14th. This information is not visible in the item attributes.

1. # Notes

In the initial rewards list, all of the SP rewards came inside crates. Since there are no trade restrictions to items, this meant the free SP could be moved from one account to another. These crates were:

- Festival Skill Points and Snowballs Crate (53506) (50k SP & 2 Snowballs) (Day 3 Omega)
- Festival Skill Points and Snowballs Crate (53510) (10k SP & 6 Snowballs) (Day 6 Omega)
- Festival Skill Points, CPD-06 Filament and Snowballs Crate (53511) (50k SP, CPD-06 Filament, 2 Snowballs) (Day 7 Omega)
- Festival Skill Points and Snowballs Crate (53497) (10k SP & 2 Snowballs) (Day 11 Alpha)
- Festival Skill Points and Snowballs Crate (53515) (50k SP & 2 Snowballs) (Day 11 Omega)
- Festival Skill Points and Snowballs Crate (53499) (50k SP & 6 Snowballs) (Day 13 Alpha)
- Festival Skill Points and Snowballs Crate (53517) (500k SP & 2 Snowballs) (Day 13 Omega)

All crates above were replaced from the rewards list with their raw SP reward part way into the event.
