EVE Online is featured at the world famous The Musem of Modern Art 's ([MoMA](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_of_Modern_Art)) new *Applied Design* installation. This installation opened to the public this afternoon, March 1, 2013, and showcases a collection of video games selected by a panel of industry experts, amongst other media and artwork.

CCP Games created a "day in the universe" visual experience that brings the museum-goer on a journey in EVE Online's **New Eden universe** by displaying various player contributions from a 24 hour period  together with a compilation of 1.2 terabytes player interaction data during the same period.

Please visit [MoMA's *Applied Design* video](//www.youtube.com/watch?v=9D_oeKPBd4A) and our [press release](//www.ccpgames.com/en/public-relations/press-releases/article/74570/eve-online-featured-in-applied-design-at-the-museum-of) for further information.
