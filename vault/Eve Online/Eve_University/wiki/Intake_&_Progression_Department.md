# Intake & Progression Department

The **Intake & Progression Department** was the department responsible for bringing new students into **EVE University** and processing progression titles. They also helped monitor and moderate our public in-game channels.

1. # Intake & Progression Officers
Our Intake & Progression Officers were tasked with:

~ bringing in new members to our organization.  They processed the Applications and conduct any follow up that may be needed before joining. 

~ handling the progression titles for our members. They reviewed and applied **titles within EVE University**.

1. # Senior Intake & Progression Officers
Our senior officers also took on the less straightforward Applications to join, and processed the Graduate applications.
