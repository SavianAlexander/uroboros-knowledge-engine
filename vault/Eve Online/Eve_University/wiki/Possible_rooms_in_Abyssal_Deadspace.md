# Possible rooms in Abyssal Deadspace

This is an overview of the different groups of NPCs that can be faced in the **Abyssal Deadspace** in the different tiers.

1. # General notes
Vila Swarmers DO NOT need to be destroyed; they will deactivate when their linked ship is destroyed and if they are still there, if all other ships are destroyed, the conduit will still unlock. Sleeper frigates and cruisers, Drifter cruisers, and rogue drone frigates and battlecruisers like to target drones, so watch them and recall them if necessary. Generic damage dealer ships (Ephialtes Lancer, Lucid Aegis, etc.) and tackle ships (Anchoring/Tangling Damavik, Lucid Upholder, Ephialtes Spearfisher, etc.) will target your drones the most often.

  - Harrowing Vedmaks** do not use target painters despite their prefix normally indicating so.

Triglavian and Rogue Drone rooms may not be fully accurate as the enemies will typically spawn interchangably regardless of faction.

Rooms labeled **DPS check rooms** typically take the longest on an EHP-average, but this may vary in reality due to the different NPC AI mechanics.

1. # Loot
Triglavian Extraction Nodes/SubNodes are not present in T0 Abyssal sites.

The amount of loot are determined with the number of filament used. (e.g. Frigate gets 3 times the loot of a Cruiser because Frigate uses 3 filaments per run.) Extraction Node/SubNode loot is not modified by number of filament used, but increases with difficulty tier. Bioadaptive/Biocombinative Cache will always drop loot, Nodes/SubNodes do not guarantee loot drops.

1. # Tier 3 Abyssal Deadspace
<tabs>

<tab name="EDENCOM">
Up to 3 Marshals can spawn.  Interchangable with Thunderchild Disparu Troop, though usually only one Thunderchild will spawn.  Enforcers may replace the battleship spawns; if they spawn together it will usually be split into 1-2 Marshals/1 Thunderchild and 1-2 Enforcers.

Having at least one battleship in the room will be the case for 9/10 of CONCORD spawns in this tier. If there are none, expect up to 3 Enforcers/Stormbringers and 3-4 Skybreakers/Pacifiers.

Highest DPS spawn recorded: 2x Marshal, 2 Thunderchild.

|}
</tab>

<tab name="Rogue Drones">
Swarm size approximates 10 ships with varied EWAR frigates in between.  This tier is the first where the drone swarms comprise primarily of Lance-prefix drones that deal double the individual damage of weaker Needle variants.

|}
  - DPS check room, one of the tankiest rooms besides Sleeper battleship room.** Frigate escorts will usually spawn in a group of 4-5.  Battleship prefers to orbit at roughly 40 km.

|}
Frigate swarm size decreases with more Rodivas present.  Easy and fast room that requires little tanking effort.

|}
Usually will spawn three battlecruisers + remote repair frigates.  Frequently can spawn Snarecasters (Web) which pose a speed danger due to the larger ships' high point-blank tracking and DPS. Frigate escort will always be either Snarecasters or Fieldweavers/Plateforgers. Every tier above this follows the same logic.  Maximum of 4 Tessera battlecruisers observed. 

|}
</tab>

<tab name="Angel Cartel">
Lucifer Cynabal and its elite variant are interchangable.  Typical spawn includes 1-2 Cynabals and roughly 5-6 frigates.  Strongest spawn recorded: 2 Lucifer Cynabals, 2 Elite Lucifer Cynabals, 1 Lucifer Swordspine. Elite variants are a common sight in this tier and subsequent ones and will frequently target drones.

|}
</tab>

<tab name="Sleepers">
  - DPS check room, most likely to kill via timeout.  This tier is the first to spawn multiple Deepwatcher battleships.** Roughly 450 DPS needed to clear within a safe time limit, +100 in Firestorm (546 DPS cleared room in 6 minutes.)

|}
Sleeper spawns can include up to 5 Sleeper cruisers.  If such a spawn occurs, no frigates spawn, though some cruisers may be replaced with Ephialtes or Drifter cruisers.
Typical layout involves 1-2 cruisers and approximately 6-7 frigates.

|}
Typical Drifter layout is 4-6 cruisers and 1 battleship, rarely any more or less.  Cruiser types are interchangable. Karybdis Tyrannos has roughly 40% shield, 33% armor (50% in Firestorm), 48% hull.

|}
  - EWAR management room.** Spawn caps out at around 12 cruisers.

|}
</tab>

<tab name="Triglavians"> 
Max 3 Drekavacs.  Note that in some cases Drekavacs and/or Rodivas will never spawn and instead be replaced with a large Kikimora swarm.  They ramp damage incredibly fast and are as dangerous as the Vedmak rooms.
Kikimora spawn averages around 6-8 ships, spawn size decreases if Drekavacs are present.
Rodivas will sometimes spawn alongside Rogue Drone frigate swarms. Rodivas and Drekavacs will never appear together.

|}
Up to 4 Leshaks can spawn.  Layout includes either 3 Leshaks and 3-4 rogue drone frigates, or 4 Leshaks and no frigates.

|}
Number of Vedmaks appears to be capped at 2. This room can spawn with a varying number of Rogue Drone frigates (usually the Needle type.)

|}
Largest DPS spawn is 3 Harrowing Vila Vedmaks. Generally this is the easiest variant of the Triglavian rooms other than Rogue Drone/Rodiva combinations, though the drone swarms may pose a problem for frigates.

|}
</tab>

<tab name="Sansha Nation">

Assuming no Knights spawn, the frigate group size approximates around 10-12 ships - with 3 Knights there will be only 1 or 2 frigates.  DO NOT attempt to fight at brawling range if your fit isn't specifically tailored to that.

1. # Tier 4 Abyssal Deadspace
This tier and all subsequent ones are highly inadvisable to run without Omega skills (making them implicitly Alpha clone inaccessible). You simply will not be able to reliably get enough DPS/tank to do this as an Alpha without very impractical blingy fits.

Tier 4 Drone BS is the hardest one to speed tank as it has the best tracking of the six variants (4.33), though it still struggles to apply at close range or to AB-fitted cruisers. It also orbits closer to you compared to most other Overmind variants except the T1 one (AB-fit Gila recorded 30km orbit average).
