Once again, the time has come for the bi-annual EVE London player gathering!

The longest running EVE Player gathering still on the calendar, EVE London plays host to veterans and new players alike in a traditional pub meet in central London.

Come stop by at Goodman's Field Wetherspoons, right by tower bridge on Saturday, February 27th for a drink or two, the opportunity to meet up with fellow pilots, and the chance to win free EVE Online game time!

For further details you can check out the [EVE London facebook page](//www.facebook.com/EVELondonMeets/), and the forum [announcement thread here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=462701).
