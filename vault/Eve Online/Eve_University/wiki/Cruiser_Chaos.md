# Cruiser Chaos

- Cruiser Chaos** ran from 1 April until 5 Arpil 2022, featuring an **Abyssal Proving Ground** and a short login campaign.

1. # Cruiser Chaos FFA Proving Grounds
This special Proving Grounds event pits you against 3 other Capsuleers. Modules were limited to meta 0. Ships entering this Proving Grounds could have a maximum of one local repair module fitted (shield or armor). If you dealt at least 4,000 damage to other ships during the event you would receive 10m ISK. There was also an Agency reward track running with the Proving Grounds – the more you fight, the more **SKINs** and **Boosters** you could win.

Allowed ships were: **Omen**, **Caracal**, **Thorax**, **Stabber**, **Maller**, **Moa**, **Vexor**, and **Rupture**.

The following modules were banned:
- Sensor Dampeners
- Weapon Disruptors
- Shield Rechargers
- Shield Power Relays
- Shield Flux Coils
- Core Defense Field Purgers

All participating ships will be afflicted with one of the following unique bonuses:
- Chaos Bonus A:
  - +100% Ship Velocity
  - +100% Ship Inertia
  - +100% Capacitor Capacity
- Chaos Bonus B:
  - +50% Shield and Armor Hitpoints
  - +50% Turret and Missile Damage
- Chaos Bonus C:
  - +100% Stasis Webifier Range
  - +50% Turret Optimal and Falloff
  - +50% Missile Flight Time
  - +50% Ship Mass
- Chaos Bonus D:
  - +5000 Hull Hitpoints
  - -50% Module Heat Damage from Overheating
  - +100% benefits gained from overheating:
    - Tackle Modules
    - Propulsion Modules
    - Repair Modules
    - Resistance Modules
    - Energy Warfare Modules
    - Turrets
    - Launchers

The arena also featured new Mystery Nexuses which provided massive buffs or debuffs to nearby ships when approached:

Mystery Nexus Buffs:
- +50% Ship Velocity
- -75% Ship Inertia
- -75% Capacitor Recharge Time
- -50% Signature Radius
- +50% Turret and Missile Damage
Mystery Nexus Debuffs:
- -25% Ship Velocity
- +100% Ship Inertia
- +50% Capacitor Recharge Time
- +25% Signature Radius
- -25% Turret and Missile Damage

1. # Login Campaign
  - April 1st Cruiser Chaos** was a short login campaign that ran alongside the Cruiser Chaos **Abyssal Proving Grounds** event. The campaign rewards **Boosters**, Proving Grounds filaments, and 5,000 Skill Points for Omega clones.
