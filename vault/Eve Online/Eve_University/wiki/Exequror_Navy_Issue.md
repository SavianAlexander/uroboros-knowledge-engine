1. # Summary
While significantly more expensive than the **Thorax**, the **Exequror Navy Issue** trades out the tracking bonus for increased damage, range, fitting, speed, a utility high, and an extra low slot. With a bonus to armor plate mass reduction, the Exequror Navy can fit an armor buffer tank and still remain speedy, all while packing a punch with blasters or railguns with eight effective turrets at max skills.

Historically, sporting double damage bonuses and barely more fitting than the Thorax, the ship was left to be fit as even more of a glass cannon than the already squishy Thorax. Given that upshipping to a **Brutix** could bring more DPS and tank for a similar price, the Exequror Navy was very unpopular compared to peers such as the **Vexor Navy Issue**, **Osprey Navy Issue**, or the **Omen Navy Issue**. A balance pass in the Uprising expansion in 2022 lead to several buffs to the Exequror Navy that have catapulted the ship into wider use.
