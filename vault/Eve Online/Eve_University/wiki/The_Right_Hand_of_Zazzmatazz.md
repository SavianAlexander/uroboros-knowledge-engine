# The Right Hand of Zazzmatazz

Video: [[Ishtar (Blitz)](https://youtu.be/u-ur94sJy4s) July 2014], [[Nightmare](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kLOEbyaMR4k) March 2013], [[Apocalypse Navy Issue](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pub8RalgwDw) April 2013], [[Dominix](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ipVhF5Ooo0w) October 2013], [[Raven Navy Issue](https://youtu.be/HrCbQYJ01P8) April 2016], [[Sentry Dominix](https://youtu.be/aDQbebIeF_E) July 2018], [[Gila](https://youtu.be/cCkKogeCs6s) May 2021], [[Manticore (Blitz)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qDKJErVcPWU) April 2024]

  - Blitz:** Kill Zor, destroy the Outpost Headquarters and warp out.

Single ungated pocket. You can [Fleet] warp at preferred range.
Mission is flagged completed after you have destroyed the Outpost Headquarters and Zor.

  - Tips:**
- Incoming DPS will get intense at close range, make sure your ship is tanked properly or fitted with MJD to jump to sniper range if needed.
- Zor drops ''Zbikoki's Hacker Card, which is used to blitz the **Worlds Collide** missions.
- Zor occasionally drops *Zor's Custom Navigation Hyper-Link* - a 53 mil implant. But usually he just drops *Zor's Custom Navigation Link*, an almost worthless implant.
- Zor deals long range damage, so some tank is needed even on sniper fitted ships.

  - Optional Approach:**
Different approaches that works well:
- Kill Zor first to reduce the incoming DPS. If he is out of range, kill the closest Cruisers and hit Zor once he is in optimal.
- Start with the Frigates from group 2, then the Cruisers before they get into their optimal range (15km) and finally the two Battleships.
- Take out several Cruisers to quickly manage aggro, shoot the Battleships and then the rest of the Cruisers and the Frigates.
