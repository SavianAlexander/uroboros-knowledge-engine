1. # Summary
The **Dominix Navy Issue** (**Navy Domi**, or **DNI**) is an upgrade on the regular **Dominix**. It has an extra mid slot, a slightly bigger drone bay, slightly more CPU, powergrid and a significantly better base tank. It trades the Dominix's drone optimal range bonus for a bonus to large hybrid damage and a bonus to local armor repair amount. While the Navy Domi has much improved survivability due to its armor repair bonus and additional shield, armor, and hull hitpoints, and the extra mid and additional fitting space give more flexibility, the Navy Dominix is more of a sidegrade than an upgrade in terms of damage - while it gains a bonus to gun damage, it loses the optimal range drone bonus of the Dominix, which is very powerful when used with sentry drones. Thus the Navy Domi is more suitable as a gun and heavy drone brawler, while the regular Dominix is superior at sniping with sentries.

1. # Tactics
The Navy Dominix's bonuses suggest brawling with heavy **drones** and large blasters while using the active **tank** bonus to shrug off hundreds of dps. A microwarpdrive is recommended for range control, while a web will greatly help with damage application. Buffer fits are also viable, using the ship's 7 low slots, its faction 50% hitpoint increase, and its **Battleship** armor plate hitpoint bonus to achieve well over 200,000 hp.

1. # Notes
The 2022 Uprising update greatly improved the Navy Dominix's performance, adding bonuses to armor repair rate and drone tracking speed, as well as increasing it's hybrid turret damage bonus.
