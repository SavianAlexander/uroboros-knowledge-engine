  - Calabash Nebula** is a gas **exploration** site found in High-sec, Low-sec and Null-sec, containing gas used in the production of **Medical Boosters** and Components.

1. # Defenders
No rats spawn in this site.

1. # Harvestable Gas Clouds

The site spawns 2 Celadon Mykoserocin clouds (1000 units each) about 75km apart, with some rock formations in the middle.

  1. # Celadon Mykoserocin
Celadon Mykoserocin is used in the production of the Synth Exile Booster and Cogni-Emotive Neurolink Enhancer.

1. # Location
These sites can be found in the [Syndicate Region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Syndicate#const),[Outer Ring Region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Outer_Ring#const), [Solitude Region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Solitude#const), [Placid Region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Placid#const) and [Essence Region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Essence#const) (both high and low security space) with a hot spot in the [Elerelle](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Solitude/Elerelle#const) constellation.
