# Angel Strike (Guristas Pirates)

- Angel Strike** is a multi-part mission. Parts 1 through 4, as well as part 6, are always the same, but part 5, and parts 7 through 10 have variants for each pirate faction.

1. # Ambush in the Dark (1 of 10)

1. # The Interrogation (2 of 10)

1. # The Kidnapping (3 of 10)

1. # Possible Leads (4 of 10)

1. # Incriminating Evidence (5 of 10)

1. # The Flu Outbreak (6 of 10)

1. # The Secret Meeting (7 of 10)

1. # Defend the Civilian Convoy (8 of 10)

1. # Retrieve the Prisoners (9 of 10)

1. # The Final Battle (10 of 10)
