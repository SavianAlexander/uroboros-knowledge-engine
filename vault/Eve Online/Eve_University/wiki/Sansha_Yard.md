# Sansha Yard

The **Sansha Yard** is an **combat anomaly** that can be found in **Sansha** infested territory of Low and Null Sec space. The site itself is a small, open area with no Acceleration Gate. There are two variations of this site, the **LCO Habitation** and **Dark Shipyard**.

1. # Rewards
- Bounty: 4 million ISK for LCO Habitation variant, 2.5 million ISK for Dark Shipyard variant
- Loot/salvage: typical Sansha salvage materials

1. # Escalation
This site may escalate into the expedition **Sansha War Supply Complex**, a 6/10 rated **DED** complex. The escalation may be triggered by destroying all ships.
