# Yan Jung Relic Site

- Yan Jung Relic Site** is a COSMOS combat site found in Deltole system. The site shows up on overview as an beacon. Entering this site requires "Passkey to Yan Jung Relic Site", which is obtained on **Sebast Mathon**'s mission line (involving other mission lines before him). The passkey is not consumed on use.

The Gargoyle Passkey is required to progress into the final room. It is consumed on use.

  1. # Relic Site
This room contains the Yan Jung cans, which can be hacked using a Relic Analyzer. While the hacks can be challenging, they can be attempted unlimited amounts of times without loss of loot.
The pirates respawn very regularly, and each group is on its own timer. Therefore, it should be expected that rats will always be on grid while hacking. A ship that can permanently tank the rats is adviseable.
Additionally, the pirates primary drones, and the webs from the frigates can be problematic in that regard. Guns or missiles are preferable.
