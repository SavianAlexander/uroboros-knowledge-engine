# Crystal Nebula

- Crystal Nebula** is a gas exploration site containing gas used in the production of **Medical boosters**.

This is a null sec gas site found through Exploration.

You have a randomized chance of taking 400 raw thermal damage at the end of a cycle. This can be decreased with higher thermal resists.

Defenders:

None

Minable Gas Clouds:

5 Azure Cytoserocin (5x 400 units gas clouds)

Azure Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Sooth Sayer Booster.

This site is found only in the[Wicked Creek](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Wicked_Creek)region of space.
