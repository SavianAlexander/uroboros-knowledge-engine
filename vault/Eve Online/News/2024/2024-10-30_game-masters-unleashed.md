## Overview

---

### Capsuleers,


An incursion is underway, and this time it’s not space pirates, but EVE Game Masters! The GMs have broken free from their cages and taken to the stars. For three days, they will roam New Eden, shaking things up with red text in official channels and appearances in ships much larger than you’re used to seeing in starter systems.  

The unleashed Game Masters will chat up a storm, engage in impromptu pyrotechnics, and give out goodies. They will also go on fun-filled roams with overflowing cargo holds, ready to drop loot for daring capsuleers. 

That’s not all! If you have a story to share, you can head over to [General Discussions](https://forums.eveonline.com/c/communications/general/83) on the EVE Online forums to share the tale of your favorite killmail. Prizes will be awarded for the most captivating yarns from New Eden’s finest storytellers. 

Prepare for the GM Incursion! Gear up, stay sharp, and dive into the fun.
