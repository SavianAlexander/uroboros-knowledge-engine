The manuscripts can be found by hacking the containers in **Contested Minmatar Military Depot** or by purchasing them from the Market. This mission gives no faction standings and can be repeated.

To obtain the manuscripts directly instead of from the market a relic analyzer is required. Travel to the Contested Minmatar Military Depot gate and then on to the next gate which is the ancient relic site gate. When inside you will use the analyzer to scan the data cores which will contain the manuscripts.
