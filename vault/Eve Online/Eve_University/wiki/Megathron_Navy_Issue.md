1. # Summary
The **Megathron Navy Issue** (also called the **Navy Megathron**) is a simple upgrade on the regular Megathron. It has an additional high slot, allowing for a neut or smartbomb, more CPU and powergrid and more base tank. Probably most useful, it also has an expanded drone bay, allowing it to carry a heavy drone flight and a medium or light drone flight.

1. # Tactics
When flown in vanguard sites, in the absence of a **Vindicator** this ship will be focusing on the close-orbiting frigate enemies, using its tracking bonus and rate of fire bonus to quickly clear them off the field. On the flip side, keep an eye on your ammo levels, as the rate of fire bonus will eat through your ammo stockpile much more quickly than usual.
