# Drone Mind - Embryo (2 of 2)

<noinclude>
  - Gallente COSMOS** mission
Agent: **Drone Mind**
Agent location: Parchanier planet IV moon 5 
Standings required: none
Parts: **(1 of 2)**, **(2 of 2)**</noinclude>

The *agent* send you away to retrieve a "Drone Mind Embryo". This item is found in **Aether Drone Hive** complex in the same system.

Note: This mission is harder than a normal level 4 mission. The last boss is notoriously hard, and has repairs over 500 dps. Prepare to be contested doing this site with thieves that compete to steal and ransom the mission item.

Even though you may see multiple beacons for the site in the overview they are not for you. The mission gives a unique location for you that you can warp to like normal mission areas. But as you warp to it the location becomes public and anyone can warp to it. All wrecks in your site will belong to the site owner no matter who deals what damage to them.<noinclude>
