1. # Summary
The **Leshak** is the **Triglavian Collective**'s battleship. From its introduction in 2018 through the **Marauder** improvements in 2021, the Leshak was *the* highest damage battleship in New Eden, while also featuring very thick armor, four utility High slots, Blaster-grade Tracking out to Mega Pulse Laser ranges, and a mass low enough to make it surprisingly fast but also bad at accelerating to that speed.

In the time since the 2021 Industry overhaul and Marauder changes, the Leshak has fallen in popularity, due to the new supremacy of Marauders as the uncontested highest-damage battleships, and the surge in hull prices. Prior to these changes however, the Leshak was one of the kings of Wormhole Space fighting (with its low mass and high armor, damage, and utilities), and one of the kings of high-end PVE (Triglavian Invasion, Sansha **Incursions**, high-class Wormhole sites,).

Like all Triglavian combat ships, the Leshak uses the unique Entropic Disintegrator weapon, a single turret with high damage and tracking, whose damage increases the longer it remains focused on a single target. Maximum damage is reached after 30 shots, or about two minutes. However, if the Disintegrator stops firing for any reason (manual deactivation, target lock broken, capacitor depletion, target out of range), the damage bonus is reset. Fortunately, due to its size, the Leshak is the most resistant of all Triglavian ships to having its Disintegrator deactivated.

1. # Tactics
Spider-tanking with other Triglavian ships or logi can be effective, and bonuses to **energy neutralizers** and **smartbombs** make them attractive uses of the ship's utility highs.

Due to the damage-ramping mechanic of Entropic Disintegrators, it is advisable to focus fire on a single target without stopping. Damage halts and the damage ramp resets if a target leaves optimal range, so range control is vital in any engagement.

1. # Notes
Like all Tech 1 Triglavian subcapital ships, the Clade that designed the Leshak, Veles, has their name engraved atop the ship in Triglavian font ().
