# Sheroz Amokin

- Sheroz Amokin** is a level 4 agent in **Amarr COSMOS** located in Koona.

The first two missions require Bug-Ridden Corpses and Antiseptic Biomass, which you probably have from some earlier missions. For the fourth mission, you'll have to go up against multiple battleships, so it's worth adding some extra tank to your battlecruiser; battleships aren't allowed into the complex.

The final mission gives 7.5% Amarr Empire standings.

1. # Blood Hunt - Symptoms (1 of 5)

Bug-Ridden Corpse can be found in **Contested Amarr Sanguine Vaults** and **Contested Amarr Bastion of Blood**.

1. # Blood Hunt - Morbid Burden (2 of 5)

Antiseptic Biomass can be found in **Contested Amarr Bastion of Blood**.

1. # Blood Hunt - Morbid Burden (3 of 5)

Simple transport mission to transport an item to a location four jumps away.

You must not fail to get the bonus reward. You will need the Bastion Master Key in future missions. The key allows you to enter the fourth room of the **Contested Amarr Bastion of Blood** once.
The key is single use and only two can be acquired (second one from **Ormon Parsik**).

1. # Blood Hunt - Cut to the Heart (4 of 5)

Generator Debris can be found in the fourth room of **Contested Amarr Bastion of Blood**. Remember to take the key you received from the previous mission.

Remember that the key can be used only once. If you fail to get the item you most likely fail this mission. A second key can be received from **Ormon Parsik** if you need it.
