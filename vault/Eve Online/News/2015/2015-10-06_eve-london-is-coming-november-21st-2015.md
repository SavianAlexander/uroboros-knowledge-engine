As the oldest EVE player gathering still on the calendar, EVE London has developed a following all its own over the course of the last decade. Hosted in the center of London right next to Tower Bridge, this gathering has always been easy to find and close to the hearts of many of our European players.

This year is a special year for the gathering as it celebrates both its own 10th anniversary, and the anniversary of the player group that operates it. It's time to head to London again, this time on November 21st, 2015, and you can find more information about the time and location in [this forum thread.](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=423609)

This time around, we'll also be bringing Gunjack, the first person VR shooter for the Samsung Gear VR to London, for all EVE players to try out.

Be sure to sign up in the thread linked above if you're going to come on down, and we'll see you there to raise a glass to internet spaceships!

Also, if you're looking for player gatherings in your area, check out the amazing player run website [www.evemeet.net](//www.evemeet.net/) for further information on internet spaceships related events going on close to you!
