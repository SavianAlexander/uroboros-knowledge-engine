**Fixes and Improvements**

**Eve Voice**


- It is now possible to use the middle mouse button to talk without EVE having focus.
- Port can be specified by adding 'voiceport = 5062' to the **prefs.ini** file.
- The Troubleshooter can be run from settings.
- The 'Move to top always on regardless of settings' issue has been fixed.


**Miscellaneous**


- A number of recently discovered and announced exploits have been resolved. - It is no longer possible to create courier contracts with a duration of 0 days.
  - It is no longer possible to use lag as a means to clear the items or ISK out of an active trade window, thus robbing your trade partner.
  - NPC Faction police will now re-agress properly after an automatic or manual (GM-forced) respawn.
