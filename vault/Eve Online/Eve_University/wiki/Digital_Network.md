# Digital Network

- Digital Network** is a **chemical lab** found in Wicked Creek region.

1. # Walkthrough

The site contains both combat and hacking. Using the acceleration gates to the site requires hacking IV to be trained. Rest of the gates can be activated without killing the defenders.

The gate to fourth pocket is locked and requires a key. The key is found in the third pocket.
