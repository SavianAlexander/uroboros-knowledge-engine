# Nocxite Deposit

- Nocxite Deposit*' are periodically spawned in **sovereign nullsec** systems that contain an active **Ore Prospecting Array**. These are part of the Nocxite Prospecting Array.  This is one of the [Equinox release](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/sovereignty-updates-transition-and-upgrades) sites.
