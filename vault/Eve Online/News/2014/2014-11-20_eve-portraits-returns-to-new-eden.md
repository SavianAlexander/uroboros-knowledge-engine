#### by ISD Tedra Kerrigan


Certain sectors of New Eden were rocked last year by the sudden disappearance of [Reality X](//eveportraits.wordpress.com/x-the-judge-2/), proprietor of EVE Portraits and the organizer of the [EVE Portraits competition](//eveportraits.wordpress.com/winners-gallery/), which rewards innovative design choices when it comes to capsuleer headshots.

More than a year after his sudden disappearance Reality X has returned to New Eden, and is currently running Round 8 of the competition.

The Grand Prize for this round, which closes December 1st,  is a Megathron Quafe Edition and Men’s Quafe T-shirt valued at 2.7 billion ISK. Second place will take home a PLEX and a men’s Quafethron T-shirt. Third place earns a Quafe Catalyst and men’s Quafe T-shirt.

To celebrate the re-launching of the competition, Reality X has waived the entry fee for Round 8.

Prospective entrants are advised to send a blank evemail with the word ‘contest’ in the subject line to Reality X. For more details on Reality X's disappearance, visit the [EVE Portraits site](//eveportraits.wordpress.com/).
