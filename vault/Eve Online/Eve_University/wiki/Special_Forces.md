# Special Forces

- Special Forces** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **Angel Provincial HQ**.
1. # Walkthrough
The second, third and fourth locations are ungated, and either strongly tanked T3C, **Battleships**, Marauders or a carrier are recommended due to the VERY high DPS in the third and fourth locations.

  1. # First Location
You land on an acceleration gate. Take it if no Domination battleship spawns or if destroying it triggers no escalation.

|}

|}
  1. ## First Pocket
No enemies in this pocket. Simple warp-in can trigger escalation.

|}

  1. # Second Location

The site is NOT in deadspace, so you can grid warp in this location. Destroy the Amarr Control Center if no escalation is triggered from the Domination battleship. 
  - Warning: Destroying each cruise missile battery triggers a smartbomb wave that deals around 8000 Explosive damage. Recommend bringing a ship with a strong tank and over 80% resists to Explosive damage if you are going to destroy the cruise missile batteries.**

|}

|}

  1. # Third Location

'''Warning: The DPS in this room can become quite heavy if all ships get in range. Make sure you know what you're doing or risk losing your ship. Clear all rats before engaging the Military Outpost.
The Domination Commander battleship will approach you with MWD, so it can be kited away from the other rats.

|}

|}

  1. # Fourth Location

| **56px|link=** | **WARNING:** There are a LOT of ships in this room, and the DPS can become very heavy if they get in range. Advise bring 3-4 ships with very strong tanks to survive this part of the escalation. |
| :--- | :--- |

The rats are spread out in 5 groups. **Avoid triggering 2 waves at once; if that happens, the site is practically impossible. Try to eliminate the Siege Autocannon sentries as soon as possible, there are 10 of them and they deal VERY high DPS.**

|}

|}
The third and final wave is the commander wave. It is triggered by bringing the *Angel Military Outpost* into structure. This is a hard wave, as the large number of elite frigates can keep you halted in one spot, while the overseer battleship (Einhas Malak) deals a LOT of damage. Recommend keeping over 15 km from the elite frigates so you won't get webbed and pointed while Einhas Malak puts your tank to the acid test.

Einhas Malak does about 3000 points of Explosive damage and 700-900 points of Kinetic damage.  He also repairs both shield and armor, with strong passive shield and armor tanks with his shield recharging at about 300 DPS and his armor at about 250. Recommend over 85% resists to Explosive and Kinetic damage and at least 550 DPS to break Einhas. He has 76% EM and Kinetic resists and 66% Explosive and Thermal resists.

|}
