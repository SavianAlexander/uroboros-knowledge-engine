# Sinogor Nitrut

- Sinogor Nitrut** is a level 2 COSMOS agent in **Minmatar COSMOS** at beacon "Dream Port" in Inder. He requires 2.00 Minmatar faction standing. (If you can't see them, enable overview elements "Beacon" and "Agents in Space")

This agent offers a mixture of courier and combat missions.

1. # Operation Rattlesnake - Filling the Ranks (2 of 3)

1. # Operation Rattlesnake - The Scanner Tower (3 of 3)
