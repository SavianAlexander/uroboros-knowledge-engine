# Nullsec Blue A0 Rare Asteroids

- Nullsec Blue A0 Rare Asteroids** is a **anomaly** that spawns in nullsec systems whose star is of type A0 (referred ingame also as Small Blue Star). It was introduced in patch 20.11 to "encourage further growth of the ramping capital manufacturing [and] easing a few bottlenecks in T1 industry"

It contains special "border ores" Ducinium, Eifyrium, Mordunium and Ytirium, which have high **reprocessing** value per. Moreover, they are a source of **minerals** otherwise not available in nullsec. In addition they contain Jet Ochre.
