## THIRD PARTY APPS


During Fanfest we announced renewed support for ESI and our third party developer ecosystem. It seems only fitting to highlight some of the newest tools that have emerged from the community.

[Theorycrafter](https://theorycrafter.pro) is a fitting tool for EVE Online. Now at version 1.6.0, Theoycrafter is a next-gen fitting tool for EVE Online and "aims to be fast, ergonomic and intuitive". Give it a try and see what you think!

Meanwhile, [EVE Guru](https://eveguru.online) is a trading and manufacturing app which the creators claim will give you the edge in EVE Online's dynamic and complex market system.

During the course of Fanfest 2025 we had some [XFX Mercury Radeon RX 9070XT video cards](https://www.xfxforce.com/shop/xfx-mercury-amd-radeon-rx-9070xt-oc-gaming-edition-with-rgb) to find homes for, courtesy of our partners AMD. Two of them went to the winning team of the EVE Leaderboards PvE event made up of **Itaer **and **Meridthal **from [IIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII](https://evewho.com/alliance/99012243) (aka Barcode; although we suspect based on their alliance name it's a Starcraft 2 smurf alliance). Another two went to **smokieakm **from Empyrean Edict and **Pandalope **from Goonswarm Federation who were the lucky winners of raffles held in the Fanfest livestream Twitch chat on Friday and Saturday.

Congrats to the winners of these awesome prizes!


## LLOYD GEORGE CAN'T STOP, WON'T STOP


Famous EVE Online watercolor artist Lloyd George aka El_Geo had a booth at Fanfest this year where he met with EVE players and sold some of his awesome paintings. He came out of the gates again with this fantastic Thunderchild painting:

You can pick up this stunning piece as well as many others at [his website](https://www.lloydgeorge.art/category/all-products). We also caught him sneakily painting watercolors for [other sci-fi games](https://www.reddit.com/r/homeworld/comments/1kqqmaw/destroyer_hw3_watercolour/) but we agreed it would be fine as long as he painted a Taiidan missile destroyer for us. We're still waiting.


## CALLING SKINR ARTISTS


On the [official EVE Online Discord](https://discord.com/channels/940573867192221696/1250048935343427696/1374469565731569787), **Varag **from Kitchen Sinkhole issued an invitation for SKINR artists to "be involved in a ship fashion show in game and live streamed on Twitch, prizes for the designers."

We suspect this is secretly a trick to recruit people since the forwarding address was the [Kitchen Sinkhole Discord server](https://discord.gg/gxEku3EJ) but what's the worst that could happen? Pro tip: we learned on an airboat tour during [EVE New Orleans](https://discord.gg/YVQBfTPhJr) that you can lure [alligators](https://evewho.com/alliance/99010980) using marshmallows (no, really!). So perhaps you can use that knowledge to bait a Kitchen Sinkhole fleet out of a wormhole to get a good fight.


## GONE FISHING
