# Forgotten Outpost

Video: [Tengu 2022-08-27](https://youtu.be/5hAVwXNu7Ow)

  - Blitz:** Not possible, you have to destroy all hostiles.

  1. # Single Pocket

- No hostiles.
- 10 stations arranged in a pattern, all roughly 35-40km from warp in.
- At about 10km from each station, you get another communication of whether there is anything of interest
- Station names are: Ancient Caldari Outpost: Urvuulen, Umkken, Uetama, Trekos, Sikken, Shioksa, Otseni, Orieku, Korami, Arishii
- The station to approach is Orieku.
