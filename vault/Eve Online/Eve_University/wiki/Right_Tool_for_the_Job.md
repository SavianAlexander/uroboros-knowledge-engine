# Right Tool for the Job

<noinclude></noinclude>

<noinclude>   </noinclude>

Video: [[Myrmidon](https://youtu.be/GO09SPQ-u2E) January 2021]

Ungated deadspace pocket one jump away. *Do not forget to equip a Data Analyzer*.

  - Blitz:** Warp at range and bounce to the Federation Navy Shipyard, destroy all the frigates guarding it, and loot the FedNav F.O.F Identifier Tag AC-106V:FNSBR (*The shipyard needs to be hacked through the Data Analyzer & Hacking minigame before you can loot*).

Tips:
- You can warp in at range and land closer (or farther) from the Federation Navy Shipyard. If more than 150km away, you can warp directly to it; create a bookmark before you land so you can quickly bounce to it
- The Broadcast Tower Group will not shoot at you unless you get close (they will if you warp to 0)
- Broadcast Tower Group receives reinforcements every 2 minutes three times
- All ships will drop Gallente Navy Tags
- If you plan on tanking, destroy the Cruisers first
- Negligible standing loss to the Gallente Federation for destroying enemy ships
- Buy Caldari Prisoners of War and bring them with you so you can instantly complete the next mission
