1. # Defenders
There are no defenders in these clouds.

1. # Minable Gas Clouds
The site spawns 5 clouds of Viridian Cytoserocin, each of them has 400 units, all of them about 40km apart from each other.

1. # Location
This site is primarily found in [Cloud ring](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Cloud_Ring#const) region.
