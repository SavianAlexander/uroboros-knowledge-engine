# AIR Career Program

The **AIR Career Program** rewards players for reaching certain goals. The rewards for a single goal are a small amount of **ISK** and career points (CP) but sometimes also items. The goals are grouped into four categories which each have ten sub-categories. Reaching all goals in one category gives a total of a thousand CP. When you reach three-quarter completion of a category, i.e. 750 CP, you graduate for that category and receive 100k skill points (SP) plus **SKIN**s. Furthermore, you gain SP based upon your total CP. You can gain a total of 750k SP per character through the AIR Career Program.

While you will reach most goals through your normal activities as a new player, you probably have to strive beyond those to obtain all the available SP. Thus the system encourages you to try out activities which you otherwise would not have tried.

1. # Rewards
There are rewards for completing particular goals, but also for graduating from a career by completing 75% of its goals, and for reaching certain milestones in the overall program.

**Career Points : Each goal provides an amount of Career Points, which are used as the measurement of progression in each Career and the AIR Career Program overall. There are 4,000 CP to collect, 1,000 in each career.**:

**Additional rewards include**:
> ; free Expert Systems for each Career : **Expert Systems** are handed out for finishing a **career agent** with skills corresponding to that agent. These Expert Systems expire after three days from redeeming.
> ; Boosters: for completing some of the Goals
> ; Various ships, modules, and skills: are all useful in a specific Career choice. Some of the rewards are intentionally associated with a different empire than the character who claims them is.

Item rewards will be delivered to ship cargo, home station or to the current station the character is currently in. Skill Point and ISK Rewards will be automatically redeemed to the Characters account .
