[EVE University Shop](https://www.redbubble.com/people/EVEUniversity/shop/t-shirts)
- Various T-shirts, Hoodies, Stickers and even Kids' clothes are available.
- [Ships Worldwide](https://support.redbubble.com/kb/top20/shipping-and-delivery)
- [Money Back Guarantee](https://www.redbubble.com/about/guarantee)

1. # FAQ
  1. # What is this?
EVE University clothing! With more people going to fanfest we figured it would be good to add shops.

  1. # What is the quality like?
The reports so far from the stickers and t-shirts seems to be fine, both with the printing quality and the quality of the shirts. The logos and text have been adjusted specifically to ensure they are clear and don't have any odd artifacts from the printing process, although being full colour images with a lot of detail they will have a certain amount of 'grain' up close. And besides, they have a satisfaction guarantee.

  1. # Wait a moment, who gets my real life money?
RedBubble gets all of it. They have a base price for their products, and while we have the option to increase the price (and get the money difference ourselves) we have chosen not to do this to keep the price as low as we can.

  1. # But what if I want to make a donation?
Then there's a few ways to do so, covering real cash and items - just visit the **Donating to EVE University** article.

Remember: You can always make your shirts on this website - you don't have to use our shop.
