# New Eden Store

- thumb|400px|The **New Eden Store** has various items available for [[PLEX**(in-game) or real money(out of game.]]
The **New Eden Store** (NES) is an in-game store where you can buy various cosmetic items for both your character and your ship, as well as various account service tokens.

1. # How to get to the store
The NES can be reached in-game through the **Neocom**, the **PLEX Vault** in the Inventory or the Pilot's Services tab in the character window.

Out of game: [New Eden Store](https://secure.eveonline.com).

1. # Payment
In-game payment is in **PLEX** and out of game is in real money. It is possible to buy PLEX directly in-game through buy PLEX option in the NES, the **PLEX Vault** in the Inventory or the Pilot's Services tab in the character window.

1. # Items available in the store
The New Eden Store in-game has the following categories:
- Ship **SKIN**s (timed and permanent skins for your ships)
- **Apparel** (clothing and other "wearable" items)
  - Pilot's body resculpt certificate (allows you to re-shape your capsuleer's body and face)
- **Services** (tokens for account services)
  - Accelerators (**Cerebral accelerator**)
  - Character training
    - Daily Alpha injectors (**Skill Injector**s for **Alpha clones** only)
    - **Skill Extractor**s
  - **Expert Systems**
  - MCT (**Multiple Character Training** certificate)
  - Omega time
  - Starter packs
  - 15,000 SP store visit weekly reward to Omega accounts for zero PLEX. (see Patch Notes for 2024-07-23.1) Weekly reset is each Tuesday

The out of game store has the following categories:
- Omega - Add Omega time
- PLEX
- Packs - Combination of different "items", among them Omega time, PLEX, skill points, Skins, MCT and cerebral accelerators
- MCT
- Skill training
    - Boost bundles - Combinations of skill points and cerebral accelerator
- Skill management
    - Daily Alpha injectors (**Skill Injector**s for **Alpha clones** only)
    - **Skill Extractor**s

1. # History
The store was first introduced as the **Noble Exchange** (NeX) with the Incarna release back in 2011, but this new version of the **New Eden Store** was introduced in the Kronos release in 2014. Originally a place for purely cosmetic items that had no real effect on your performance ingame.

The choice to open up for **skill trading** with **skill injectors** and **skill extractors** in February 2016 changed that. Additionally the store changed from using **Aurum** to **PLEX** as of the removal of Aurum as a currency and the introduction of the **PLEX vault** in May 2017.
