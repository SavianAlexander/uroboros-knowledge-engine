# Wispy Ruins

- Wispy Ruins** is a combat relic site involving both combat and **hacking**. It appears to Probes as a scannable **combat site**. Unlike most combat sites, it is only found in the Feythabolis nullsec region, specifically in the I-3ODK constellation.  A dedicated combat ship like the **Vargur** is highly recommended for this site. Avoid taking **frigates** into the site, as the smartbombs in the third and fourth pockets can easily destroy any frigate. The smartbomb damage can even be high enough to destroy a cruiser. Pirates from the **Rogue Drones** and **Angel Cartel** are featured.

1. # Walkthrough
There are four rooms in this complex but only three need to be completed. The second stage(Portal) can be skipped since the central deadspace pocket can be accessed more quickly by taking the other gate in the first pocket.

  1. # Warp-in
3 "Pirate Grave Robber" Angel Battleships are present on warp-in. You will land at a locked acceleration gate to the Terrace.

  1. # First Pocket - Terrace

The acceleration gates deeper into the complex are guarded by three groups of rats. They can be aggressed separately. The named battleship(Angel Raid Commander) triggers reinforcements. The acceleration gates are not locked, and the room can be skipped.

  1. # Fourth Pocket - Sanctum

The Sentinel Chimera Strain Mother has a strong active armor tank and many structure hitpoints, as well as a strong web that reaches out to 50 km. It fires Mjolnir XL Torpedoes that hit rather hard. It is recommended that you bring a friend or two to take it down.
