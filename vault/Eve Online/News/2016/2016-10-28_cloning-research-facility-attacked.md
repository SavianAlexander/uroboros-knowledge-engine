## Overview
Lina Ambre reports on Blood Raider attacks against shipping convoys after an intelligence leak from the Republic Fleet shines light on a cloning facility that was sieged by Covenant forces. 

With footage of new Blood Rader vessels now in the public domain, the Directive Enforcement Department has urged capsuleers and shipping pilots alike to be on high alert, and report any sightings to the authorities.
