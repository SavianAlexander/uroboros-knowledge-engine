# Colony Under Fire

- Colony Under Fire** is an **expedition** found by completing the **Serpentis Military Complex** signatures.  It contains four parts, each of which will be 4-8 jumps from the previous system, in null sec space.

1. # Walkthrough
The first 3 parts of this escalation are quite easy, but the 4th part can be somewhat challenging.

1. # Part 1
Access: Gateless, Open Access 

In the first room you find a warp gate. Killing the NPCs allow usage of that gate to the second room.
You might have a Shadow Serpentis spawn in the first room. Killing this triggers an escalation. 
If it is not there, going to the second room and killing the scout can trigger the escalation.

1. # Part 4
Access: Gateless, Open Access 

This can be quite hard. The first 3 waves spawn on a 4 minutes timer. The third wave have a Relay Commander, killing this spawn the Relay Wave. Killing the second Relay Commander spawns Black Ceasar and his followers. 

You want to clear each wave before spawning the next wave.

1. # Rewards
The first part:
- Can drop shadow serpentis modules, ammunition and tags.
- Gives around 28.000.000 ISK in bounties.

The second part:
- Can drop shadow serpentis modules, ammunition and tags.
- Gives around 36.000.000 ISK in bounties.

The third part:
- Can drop shadow serpentis modules, ammunition and tags.
- Gives around 15.000.000 ISK in bounties.

The fourth part:
- Can drop Core A-Type Modules, shadow serpentis modules, ammunition and tags.
- Gives around 50.000.000 ISK in bounties.
