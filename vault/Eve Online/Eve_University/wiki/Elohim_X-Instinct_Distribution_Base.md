# Elohim X-Instinct Distribution Base

- Elohim X-Instinct Distribution Base** is a **hacking** site found in low security space in the [Heimatar](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Heimatar) region. The site appears to Probes as a Level IV gas site but is in fact a combat site.
1. # Walkthrough
The pocket is ungated. There is quite a bit of optional combat. If you want to avoid combat with the NPCs, warp at a distance to the site and draw the NPCs away from the hacking containers, then warp at 0 to the containers and hack while they try to slowboat back to the containers.  

Otherwise make a bouncing bookmark 150-200 km away from the NPCs and containers, warp into the site (you should fit for some tank) and hack the containers while ignoring the NPCs. The containers can be opened even if the NPCs nearby are left alive. Note that if you draw the NPCs too far away (~200 km) they will teleport back to their starting positions.

The NPCs have special names but are just renamed Angel deadspace rats so they don't drop anything extraordinary.
