Greyscale has written an [overview](//ccp.vo.llnwd.net/o2/pdf/File_Faction_Guide.pdf) of the four main Empires and their political positions the day after they went to war - June 11th, for those who don't remember when we released Empyrean Age. It also contains a brief overview of the smaller factions.

This guide is an out of character document designed to give you an understanding of the political situation, to help you define your characters' personal and political stance in relation to various groups within the Empires. This document offers a solid foundation to build your characters stories and will help both new and experienced players.

We invite you to discuss the document in [this forum thread](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=831043).
