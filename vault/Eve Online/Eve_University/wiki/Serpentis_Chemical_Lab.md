# Serpentis Chemical Lab

1. # Overview

This is a Cosmic Signature **chemical lab** found in **low-sec**. To access the chemical laboratories, you need a Data Analyser module. 

1. # Walkthrough
Single ungated pocket. 
The damage at first can be considerable.  The missile launchers can be destroyed rapidly, and once they are destroyed the damage output drops substantially.  Destroyed Missile Batteries may drop Ammo.

The cans can be hacked and looted without killing any of the defenders.

|}

Reinforcements arrive once the ships from initial derenders are destroyed.
