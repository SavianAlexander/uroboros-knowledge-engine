# Hauling Department

The **Hauling Department** aimed to provide a space where new Haulers can learn efficient **Hauling** and to support **EVE University** and its members in transporting goods between communities, trade hubs and fleet staging systems.

1. # Staff

  1. # Hauling Management
The  oversaw **EVE University's** hauling staff and coordinates hauling services required by EVE University, IVY League and its holding corporations

1. # Hauling Services
  - EVE University Hauling Service** provided Unistas free shipment of goods between EVE University communities, graciously supported through a group of dedicated hauling staff volunteers.
