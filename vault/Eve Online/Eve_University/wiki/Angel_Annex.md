# Angel Annex

The **Angel Annex** is a **Cosmic Signature** of the **Unrated Complex** type, occupied by the **Angel Cartel** pirate faction. It can be found via exploration by probing in **Lowsec** systems.

This site's acceleration gate allows only Battleship class ships or lower and their Tech 2 equivalents to enter the site.
