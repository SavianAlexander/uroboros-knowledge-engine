# Large Mercoxit Deposit

- Large Mercoxit Deposit** are periodically spawned in **nullsec** systems, and are a guaranteed spawn in systems with any tier 2 **Ore Prospecting Array**. There is also a chance for the site to spawn in sovereign nullsec systems with a tier 3 array installed.

The respawn time on guaranteed sites is 8 hours.

3 types of Mercoxit spawns with this site, regular, Magma (5%), and Vitreous (10%).
