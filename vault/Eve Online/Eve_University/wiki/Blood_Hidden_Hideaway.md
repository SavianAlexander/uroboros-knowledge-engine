# Blood Hidden Hideaway

- Blood Hidden  Hideaway** is an unrated **combat site** belonging to the **Blood Raiders** pirate faction. It can be found via **exploration** as a **cosmic anomaly** within systems owned by the **Amarr Empire** and **Ammatar Mandate**. As an ungated complex, the limitations as to which ships can complete this site are tied to the security rating of the system where the anomaly spawns (T2 **Battleship**s for high-security systems, and up to **Titans** in low-security and null-security systems).

This combat site consists of a single ungated pocket of space containing a variety of **Frigate**-, **Destroyer**- and **Cruiser**-sized enemy ships. Three variations of this combat anomaly exist: **Blood Hideaway**, **Blood Forsaken Hideaway** and **Blood Forlorn Hideaway**.
