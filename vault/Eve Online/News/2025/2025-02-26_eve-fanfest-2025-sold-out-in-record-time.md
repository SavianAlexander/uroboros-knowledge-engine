- **Author**: EVE Online Team
- **Source**: https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/eve-fanfest-2025-sold-out-in-record-time

## Overview
All the tickets are gone, but you can catch the live stream.

---

Fanfestive capsuleers, 

The response has been nothing short of extraordinary and EVE Fanfest 2025 is now officially sold out! Never before in the history of this legendary gathering has the festival reached capacity this far ahead of the event. The passion and dedication of the EVE community continue to shape New Eden, and this milestone is yet another testament to its strength. The 17th edition of Fanfest promises an unforgettable celebration of all things EVE, with new reveals, deep-dive discussions, and the unparalleled camaraderie of fellow capsuleers.  

For those unable to attend in person, the journey isn’t over, however. The [Fanfest 2025 live stream](https://www.twitch.tv/ccp) will bring the excitement straight to you on 2-3 May. Don’t miss your chance to witness major announcements, developer insights, and all the action as it unfolds. 

Thank you for continuing to make Fanfest history.
