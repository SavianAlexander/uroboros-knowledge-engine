The final day of the [New Eden Open](//community.eveonline.com/en/tournament) $10,000 cash tournament has arrived!

Watch mad spaceship action as the [final four teams](//community.eveonline.com/en/tournament/schedule/day-six) fight tooth and nail for the incredible $10,000 prize money sponsored by [own3d](//www.own3d.tv). The winner of the tournament will be determined in a best-of-five match series between *Asine Hitama's Team* and *RONIN and Pixies*. The third place will be determined by a best-of-three match series between *Goggle Wearing Internet Crime Fighters* and *Expendables*.

Additionally we will host a series of competitions on our [forum](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=topics&f=264) and live on stream with prizes [sponsored by Razer](//apps.facebook.com/sweepstakeshq/contests/303189).

This awesome spectacle is available live from 18:00 UTC on Sunday, December 2, at our live stream [here](//www.own3d.tv/CCPGames). Don't miss it!
