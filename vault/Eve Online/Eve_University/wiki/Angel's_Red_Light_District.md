# Angel's Red Light District

- Angel's Red Light District** is a DED Complex (rated 5/10), occupied by the Angel Cartel pirate faction. It can be found via exploration by probing in Highsec and Lowsec systems, or as an escalation from Angel Refuge or Angel Den.

The site consists of a volume of space with an acceleration gate and contains various structures and their defending pirates. Using the gate allows access to other areas within the site.

This site's acceleration gate allows all subcapitals to enter.

The location of this complex can be received as an escalation from Cosmic Anomalies, in particular the **Angel Refuge** and **Angel Den**. Upon such a discovery, the following message appears:
