Oveur, TomB, activeALLI and kieron will be attending the Player Gathering in Las Vegas next month, 15 and 16 March. If you missed out on FanFest because of the travel involved, this is an opportunity to join other EVE players in one of the entertainment capitals of the world.

There will be prizes, interviews, discussions, mingling and a party! Think about it, Devs and Las Vegas, who knows what insanity will result!
