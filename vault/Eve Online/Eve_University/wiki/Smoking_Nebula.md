This is an **Exploration** gas site that spawns in all security bands of K-space.

1. # Defenders

None - no rats spawn in this site. 

1. # Minable Gas Clouds

2 Golden Mykoserocin (1000 units each), 2,000 units total

Golden Mykoserocin is used in the production of the Synth Crash Booster and the Axosomatic Neurolink Enhancer.
