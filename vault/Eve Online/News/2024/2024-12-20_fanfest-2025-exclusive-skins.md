### Capsuleers,


Fanfest 2025 is approaching fast! From 1-3 May, Reykjavik will once again become the focal point of the EVE community as capsuleers from all over the world gather at the Harpa conference center to celebrate the legacy, camaraderie, and boundless future of EVE Online. 

This year, the excitement begins early for those who act fast. Tickets purchased by 15 January will come with an exclusive gift: the highly anticipated Fanfest SKINs, delivered on 10 February. These stunning designs, crafted to honor the spirit of EVE’s biggest in-person event, are sure to turn heads across New Eden. 

Don’t wait too long – if you buy your Fanfest ticket after 15 January, you won’t receive your special SKIN until after the event concludes, so you will miss out on the chance to show your Fanfestive pride early. Regardless, though, these SKINs are sure to be a badge of honor for all attendees. 

Secure your place among capsuleers from around the world, immerse yourself in the EVE Universe, and claim your part in Fanfest history.
