CCP is pleased to announce the return of [PLEX to the Buddy Reward](//www.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&bid=907) system. As of today you can once again [send Buddy invites](//secure.eveonline.com/Marketing/Buddy/Default.aspx) with PLEX as a reward type. As before, you have the choice as the sender of either 30 days of game time or a PLEX. The brief hiatus allowed us to measure the effects of PLEX on the program and we're happy to say the program is a pretty big success; lots of new players and lots of PLEX going into Buddy inviter's hands.
