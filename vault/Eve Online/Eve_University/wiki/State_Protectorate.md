# State Protectorate

We are the State, and we have claimed our long-lost homeland. Now we are at war; united, whole and full of fire and purpose. The State calls you, capsuleer, for it needs your strength and your leadership to fend off the encroaching Gallente menace. The State will not fall. Join us. Fight. Conquer.

  1. # History
The Caldari were once part of the **Gallente Federation**. Soon after the technology for interstellar travel was rediscovered in the Gallente Federation, the Caldari corporations started secret colonization of their own. Once the Gallente Federation found out about these colonies, the Federation of course demanded that they be put under the authority of the Federation. The Caldari corporations declined. The relationships were tense and Caldari Prime was put under blockade. Eventually, Caldari partisans in Caldari Prime sabotaged an underwater Gallente city. The Gallente retaliated with orbital bombardment and the **Gallente-Caldari War** began. The war lasted for a hundred years until the newly formed CONCORD was finally able to negotiate peace between the two factions. The Caldari Prime is still under Gallente control to this day and Gallente-Caldari relations are extremely hostile.
