## Projects & Tools


There's been a steady stream of updates and projects coming out recently, with both newcomers and veterans putting their own spin on tools. 

**Zorath
**[Ithika Zorath](https://evewho.com/character/2124123129) is a brand new player who decided to document his journey from day one. Rather than just writing things down, he built a [custom roleplay terminal](https://neocom-zorath.pages.dev/) to track his story as it unfolds. 

By his own admission, it's a bit of a geeky project, but the result is a fully immersive take on EVE. 

**A worm-whole new world ****
**Two known wormhole tools have received updates. [WormholeSystems](https://wormhole.systems/)now includes options to share maps publicly or via time-limited access. It also introduces more flexible layout customization and a UI refresh. 

[Whtype.info](http://whtype.info/) was updated accordingly after recent updates. It remains a useful reference tool; simply hover over any wormhole to get a quick breakdown of important information.

## "What happened?! I'm still alive!"


A number of recent fights made their way from YouTube onto our radar. 

OnlyFleets continues to work through footage from their war with Phoenix Co., this time covering the destruction of Legion of xXDEATHXx's Keepstar in 49-U6U. Of course, the video wouldn't be complete without a battle report. Check it out [here](https://warbeacon.net/br/related/30004009/202601132000/). 

Meanwhile, Warlock Industries covers another round of fighting between WinterCo and the Imperium in Geminate, this time with capitals on grid. 

iBeast shared a new video featuring commentary and analysis while taking fights in quite possibly the most sparkly Hyperion in New Eden. Hearing the low armor alarm going off so many times and seeing the heat gradually creep round on the modules kept us on the edge of our seats!

## Everything and the Kitchen Sink


Spectre Fleet's GANKED is throwing a farewell party. This last fleet under [Virion Stoneshard](https://evewho.com/character/92556095) as FC is set to form up today (28 March) at 19:00 UTC in [Airaken](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/system/Airaken). As always, it's a kitchen sink fleet. Bring whatever you're ready to lose. 

*Screenshot by *[*Razorien*](https://www.flickr.com/photos/razorieneve/)*, taken during the 600th edition of GANKED.*

For those looking to continue flying NPSI (Not Purple, Shoot It) after this, [NPSI.Rocks](https://npsi.rocks/) remains an active hub for public fleets.

With the Gallente Elections underway, a number of SKIN designs inspired by the ongoing campaigns have popped up. The examples below are from [Val en Thielles](https://evewho.com/character/96466946), showcasing some of the available options.
