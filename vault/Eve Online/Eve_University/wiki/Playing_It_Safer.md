# Playing It Safer

<noinclude>   </noinclude>

Ungated deadspace pocket up to twenty nine jumps away in Faurulle.

Complete the Mission remotely.

Tips:
- Remember to take the S.I Formula Sheet (0.1 m3) from the Item Hangar
- If S.I Formula Sheet is destroyed you will fail the Epic Arc and you will have to try again in ninety days
- You can get to Faurulle in Solitude either through Aridia or Syndicate Regions
