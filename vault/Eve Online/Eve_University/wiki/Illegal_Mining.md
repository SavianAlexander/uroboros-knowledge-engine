# Illegal Mining

Destroying the mobile refinery incurs a 3.0% standing loss with the Gallente Federation. Additional small standings losses may occur for destroying ships if it takes a while to complete.

  - Blitz:** You don't need to kill the miners. Destroying the Mobile Refinery completes mission.  Attempting to Blitz the mission through all of the spawns results in a very high level of incoming damage.

  1. # Single Pocket
- No auto-aggro on warp-in, attacking anything aggros initial spawn.
- Reinforcement 1 spawns when the Mobile Refinery goes below 25% shield.
- Reinforcement 2 spawns when the Mobile Refinery goes below 50% hull.
