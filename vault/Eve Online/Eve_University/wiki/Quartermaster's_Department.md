# Quartermaster's Department

1. # What is the Quartermaster's Department?
The **Quartermaster's Department** maintains fuel levels in University Structures, and stocks the **Flight Deck**s. They also deal with various EVE University programs: 

- **Mining ship program**
- **+3 Implants Program**
