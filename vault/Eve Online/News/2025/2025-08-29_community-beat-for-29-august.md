### **Cpt Amarlio - Golem**


We'd like to start by showcasing Cpt Amarlio's incredible artwork. This piece features a Golem with the bastion module active.

As he outlined in his [reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/Eve/comments/1moo4re/cel_shaded_golem/?share_id=SqGwcqDq243ptCtDSl5It&utm_medium=android_app&utm_name=androidcss&utm_source=share&utm_term=1), this piece took him 62 hours to finish! Taking inspiration from the Studio Ghibli cel-shading art style, Cpt Amarlio keeps impressing us with work after work. What's even cooler, is that he is officially licensed by CCP to sell his artworks for you to own & put up on your wall at home! Head over to his [etsy page](https://www.etsy.com/uk/listing/1390556637/eve-online-golem-caldari-state-marauder?ref=shop_home_active_8&crt=1&logging_key=826c15a490d9dccacfd452f7dd7f4b5e016c34e9%3A1390556637) to pick up a piece of your favorite ship <3

Next up, we'd like to give a shout-out to [Warlock industries](https://www.youtube.com/@Warlockindustries), and his entertaining video documenting the AO Keepstar destruction in Providence with witty commentary, and promising to provide *"Cinematic battles, semi-reliable reporting, and absolutely unreliable frame rates straight from the front lines!"* 


### **1T fight in Old Man Star**


Nullsec wasn't the only place with massive action! As has become tradition, Lowsec is quick to prove that it is popping with action, often out-destroying the massive battle reports we see from Nullsec.

A [1 Trillion ISK fight](https://br.evetools.org/br/68ab8ba32213cf0012acaeff) took place in the system of Old Man Star, after a Snuffed Out Titan fell to a dreadbomb. Snuffed Out were quick to respond with their own dreads, and the grid turned to carnage as dreads and subcaps clashed.

The fight was recorded from several perspectives, but we wanted to give a shoutout to [Ph33rfactor](https://www.youtube.com/@Ph33rfactor) with his POV from the Fl33T side. Enjoy!


### **Pando - "Zap & Clap" NPSI Fleet**


Then there is [Pando](https://www.twitch.tv/zehpando)'s recent NPSI fleet, made up of 12 Stormbringers, 18 Loki's, 10 Eagles, logi & support which we'd like to highlight. There's something about fleetcomps that incorporate a healthy dose of "Zappy Boi's", whilst retaining enough single target dps ships to finish targets, that really butters our sandwich.

In the fight below, they take on Brave Newbies in Ferox Navies.

The comp is reminiscent of the "Tidebringer" comp brought to us by [Amelia Duskspace](https://www.youtube.com/@AmeliaDuskspace) & Goryn Clade a few years ago.
