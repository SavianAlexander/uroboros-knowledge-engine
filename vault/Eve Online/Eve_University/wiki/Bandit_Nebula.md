# Bandit Nebula

This is a low sec gas site found through Exploration.

Defenders:

None

Minable Gas Clouds:

2 Amber Cytoserocin (200 units and 300 units gas clouds)

Amber Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Pure Standard Blue Pill Booster.

This site is found only in The Forge region of space.
