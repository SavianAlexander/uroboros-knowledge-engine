# The Plantation Workers - Save The Plantation (4 of 4)

<noinclude> 
</noinclude>

Destroying NPCs in this mission will inflict Minmatar Republic standing loss of about -0.012 total. NPCs are dropping Republic Fleet insignias worth a lot of ISK, so it is recommended to loot everything. Loot is including Freedom Fighters and Freed Slaves, dropped randomly. Total value of loot and salvage is 130+ million ISK, according to the in-game estimate.

  1. # First pocket
Incoming DPS is pretty decent, so don't engage all groups at the same time.

|}

  1. # Second pocket
No aggro on warpin. You have to clear all rats before activating the Acceleration Gate to the third pocket. Freedom Stabbers are doing a significant amount of DPS.

|}

  1. # Third pocket
You have to clear all rats before activating the Gate To The Plantation (Acceleration Gate).

|}

  1. # Fourth pocket
All aggro on warpin with a significant amount of DPS.

|}
