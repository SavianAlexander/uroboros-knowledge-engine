# Expedition Frigates

- Expedition Frigates** are **Tech II** **mining** **frigates** specialized in activities other than basic ore mining.

  1. # Prospect

The  is a Tech II variety of the , and sports the ability to fit a **Covert Ops Cloaking Device**. It has an increased cargo and mining hold capacity, even more bonuses to mining yield, more tank, and 3 additional low slots, but no drone bay and no innate warp stabilization.

The Prospect is designed to operate in dangerous or hostile space. A Covert Ops Cloaking Device will allow it to warp while cloaked, making it very difficult to catch. However, it lacks even the rudimentary defense of a small drone bay that the Venture has, so if caught, it is completely helpless. It can't even handle any **belt rat**s that might spawn without refitting.

The Prospect retains the Venture's bonus to **gas cloud harvesting**, and due to its larger mining hold, is the best ship in the game for collecting gas.

The  is a Tech II variety of the Venture, and has bonuses to Cloaking Devices. It has an increased cargo and mining hold, even more bonuses to mining and ice harvesting yield, more tank, and 2 additional low slots, but no innate warp stabilization.

The Endurance is designed to operate in dangerous or hostile space.
