Everyone who goes to EVE Vegas is already a winner, but we are happy to announce the name of the next winners of the [EVE Vegas Player Prize Draw!](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/eve-online-news/eve-vegas-competition-2/)

[Cadinie](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/KiLLAPiNO) of [Blackwater USA Inc.](//gate.eveonline.com/Corporation/Blackwater%20USA%20Inc._) and [Theslapchop](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Theslapchop)  of [Hole Violence [SV.H]](//gate.eveonline.com/Corporation/Hole%20Violence) each win a [Resort Vista Upgrade!](//www.planethollywoodresort.com/content/scaffold_pages/rooms/planet_hollywood/phv/en/resort_vista_room/_jcr_content/cards/card.roomhighlights.-_-etc-_-designs-_-cet-_-properties-_-planet_hollywood.phv.1.html)

Prize winners in this draw will be contacted by CCP in the coming days with further details on how to claim their prizes. The next prize draw will be for 4 Limosine rides! This draw will take place on Wednesday October 1 and the 4 lucky winners will be announced the same day.

Prizes left in the pool: 


- 2x [Panorama Suite Upgrades](//www.planethollywoodresort.com/content/scaffold_pages/rooms/planet_hollywood/phv/en/ph_panorama_suite/_jcr_content/cards/card.roomhighlights.-_-etc-_-designs-_-cet-_-properties-_-planet_hollywood.phv.1.html) (valid for the following nights; arriving Thursday, October 16, 2014 and departing Monday, October 20, 2014)
- 4 x Complimentary Roundtrip Limousine transfers left, from and to Las Vegas McCarran International Airport and Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino, Las Vegas, to be used over the program dates of Thursday, October 16, 2014 and departing Monday, October 20, 2014.
