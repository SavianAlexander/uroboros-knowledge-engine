Capsuleers, 

All good things must come to an end, and Fanfest 2026 has officially been wrapped up. 

This year saw the usual amazing presentations, panels, keynotes, and activities, on and off-venue. In addition, attendees donated their blood to the creation of the Capsuleer Edda, and the first annual Creator Awards. 


## Cradle of War is Coming


One of the highlights of the EVE Fanfest keynote presentation was the announcement of Cradle of War, coming on 9 June. It introduces Military Campaigns, eight new ships, the first non-PvP region of New Eden, and titles and achievements.

## Keynotes Galore


Some say it’s the highlight of the year. Others say it’s the highlight of the decade. In any case, excitement always runs high for the EVE Keynote, which was kicked off by FC Orca. 

Top billing went to the Cradle of War expansion, with other highlights including a deep dive into the Capsuleer Edda, a discussion of the recently announced partnership with Google DeepMind, and a look at the overarching Theaters of War, unfolding over the next three expansions. 

The EVE Vanguard keynote introduced Operation Avalon, the first alpha playtest for the game, and an important step toward its always-on release next November. Operation Avalon launches 7 July, and will introduce rebuilt combat, new and challenging enemies, an expanded arsenal, and a deeper risk-and-extraction loop. In addition, FC Collins announced that Military Campaigns will be a part of EVE Vanguard in the near future. 

The EVE Frontier keynote announced Cycle 6, which brings modular ships, expanded exploration, new environmental dangers that shape how players move and interact with the Frontier, and much more. 


## Honored Guests


As always, some of our guest speakers stole the show. Guest keynote speaker Paul M. Sutter took us on a life-threatening choose-your-own-adventure journey that taught us How to Die in Space, and Dr. Beth Healey, our other guest keynote presenter, took us on a journey through some of the most hostile environments in the universe. 

Among this year’s fantastic player presenters, the Oz dove deep into how politics, patches, and panic make EVE’s markets move, and Warlock Industries walked us through the creation of cinematic content in New Eden

And of course, we were visited by incredible partners like Adrian Bolton from DeepMind and Kevin Boon from Sui, along with Her Excellency, Halla Tómasdóttir, President of Iceland. 


## Other Highlights


Some of the other highlights of Fanfest 2026 included: 

The Capsuleer Edda – capsuleers donated their blood to be mixed in with the ink used to commit the epic poem of New Eden to velum. 

The Trade Hub had really cool things happening throughout the festival, including the Knitadel, where crafty capsuleers worked from EVE-themed knitting patterns; the Hyper Wheel, which rewarded lucky visitors with some amazing prizes; the Icelandic Gaming Industry showcase, which gave attendees a glimpse into amazing things happening in the local gaming scene; a miniature painting station, where capsuleers could try their hand at painting EVE ships in a real-life SKINR; tattoo booth, silent auction, and more. 

Extracurricular activities are always popular at Fanfest, and this year was no exception. Capsuleers went on a multitude of exciting tours, including the famed pub crawl, guided excursions showcasing some of Iceland’s most iconic locations.
