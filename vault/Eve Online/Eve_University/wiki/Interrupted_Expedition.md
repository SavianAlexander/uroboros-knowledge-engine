# Interrupted Expedition

- Interrupted Expedition**s are escalations that have a small chance of occurring after completing a **detected data site** or **detected relic site**.

1. # Information

When a capsuleer warps to the site an informative message is received.

On grid you can see the remains of the failed expedition and hackable containers. There are no defenders, pirates on site.

Each site contains:
- 3 Expedition Research Cache hackable containers - Yellow Core 70
- 4 Expedition Secure Supplies hackable containers - Red Core 90

A data analyzer is required to hack both types of container.

1. # Loot
The containers have the potential to contain:
- Datacores & Decryptors
- Industrial Goods
- Blueprint Copies
- Materials
- Triglavian Survey Databases
- Abyssal Filaments
