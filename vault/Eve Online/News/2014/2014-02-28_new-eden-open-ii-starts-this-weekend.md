Hello capsuleers!

The moment you have all been waiting for is rapidly approaching. Who will rise to the challenge and emerge victorious from the second [New Eden Open](//community.eveonline.com/community/new-eden-open/), claiming their share of the $25.000 and 700+ PLEX prize pool? Follow all the action and root for your favorite team on [CCP's Twitch channel](//www.twitch.tv/ccp). So make sure you tune in and don't miss any of the action!

The New Eden Open is an online PVP tournament in EVE where the best and most dedicated pilots put their skills to the test in an 8v8 deathmatch format. A series of fights where the amount of friends or titans doesn't matter. All broadcast live with commentary from CCP Dolan, CCP Fozzie and CCP Rise and your new host CCP Bro.

If you want to learn more check out the [New Eden Open website](//community.eveonline.com/community/new-eden-open/).
