# Orientation Department

The mission of the **Orientation Department** was to reach out to newly accepted members and help them settle in and feel at home in EVE University.

Orientation Officers followed up with new members once they were accepted. They reached out to new members to ensure they were settling in and provide them with information useful for the individual situation. They acted as a contact point for a member's first few weeks in the corporation, answered any questions a new member might have, and helped to point them in the right direction.
