# Serpentis Extravaganza (Level 4)

Video: [Gila](https://youtu.be/sBmCiW7XCJo), 12-04-2021; [Praxis](https://youtu.be/glEyftE2ya4), July 2024

  - Blitz:**
- 1st and 2nd Pocket: Eliminate all ships to unlock the warp gates.
- 3rd Pocket: Kill or ignore the 1st Group. Eliminate the 3 Spider Drones and Krayek Sarpanti.

  1. # 1st Pocket
The gate to next pocket will be locked until you have eliminated all hostile ships.

All groups can be attacked independently

|}

  1. # 2nd Pocket
- No auto-aggro on warp-in, reinforcements do not auto-aggro
- The 2nd Group can be attacked independently without drawing pocket aggro.
- Attacking the 1st Group will aggro the 3rd Group and vice-versa.
- The warp gate will be locked until you have eliminated all hostile ships.

|}

  1. # 3rd Pocket
Auto aggro from Group 1.
 
Attacking the 2nd Group will aggro the 4th Group (Krayak Sarpanti) and vice versa.

Serpentis Stronghold structure has over 200,000 HP, does not have to be destroyed and drops nothing of value.

|}

1. # Bounties
  - Bounties:** ± 9.8 Mil ISK

1. # Loot & Salvage
  - Loot:** ± 6.53 Mil ISK

  - Salvage:** ± 8.89 Mil ISK
