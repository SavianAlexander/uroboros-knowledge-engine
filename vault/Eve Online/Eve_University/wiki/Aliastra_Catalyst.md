1. # Summary
Aliastra Catalyst is a special edition of the **Catalyst** given out as a Buddy Program reward, similar to the **Inner Zone Shipping Catalyst**, **Intaki Syndicate Catalyst**, **InterBus Catalyst**, and the **Quafe Catalyst**. It is functionally equivalent to the standard Catalyst but with a different skin and additional flavor text.

All models were converted to a regular **Catalyst** and a corresponding Aliastra **SKIN** with the Mosaic release in April 2015. Despite this, the model was given a unique quote in the Revenant expansion in November 2024, over nine years after all models were converted.
