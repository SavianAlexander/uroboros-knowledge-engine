# A Matter of Decorum

<noinclude></noinclude>

  - Objective**
Bring Mizara's doll to the colony where her family lives, in [Tanoo](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/system/Tanoo).

  - Notes**
- --The mission can be completed remotely and the next mission can be accepted remotely.--*

You will receive 1x **Mizara's Doll** to deliver. Don't forget to put it from your items hangar into your cargo before undocking. Warp to the encounter. You will be placed about 18km away from **Mizara Familty Hovel**. Aproach it and drop **Mizara's Doll** into their home. Mission complete. Hopefully, it will help the family in their grieving process.

On turn in, Dovrich Anselm says: *"Thank you for doing this, my friend. To see others suffer is never easy."*
