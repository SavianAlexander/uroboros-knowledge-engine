## Interstellar Speakers


Two guest speakers will be featured at Fanfest 2026: 

**Paul M. Sutter** is a cosmologist, award-winning science communicator, author, and NASA advisor. He hosts several podcasts and a YouTube series as well as consulting for television and film productions. 

**Dr. Beth Healey** is an emergency medicine doctor, a research physician, and an extreme environment specialist. She has worked for the European Space Agency, researching the effects of physical and psychological isolation on a group of people.
