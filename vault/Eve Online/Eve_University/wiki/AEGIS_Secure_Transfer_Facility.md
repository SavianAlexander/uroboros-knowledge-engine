# AEGIS Secure Transfer Facility

than it appears here }}

The AEGIS Secure Transfer Facility appeared at the 4th quadrant in EVE Online, the New Dawn, Age of Prosperity. It is a Lowsec/Nullsec/Wormhole space Level 3 data site with hostile CONCORD ships guarding the site.

Once the site is completed the capsuleer running the site may receive an escalation and AEGIS databases.

Please note that this site requires a data analyzer to receive the rewards as well as a well tanked T3 Cruiser or a Marauder with over 80km range to safely run the site.

  1. # Gate B

Note: all rats here are diamond rats including the Bowhead. Once the Bowhead is killed the NPCs will warp off.

When the shield projector is successfully hacked the Bowhead will attempt to escape.  You must point it with at least 3 points of scrambling (a faction warp scrambler will suffice) else it will warp off with your loot prospects and the site will be considered failed.  The Bowhead itself has a strong buffer shield tank and high damage resists.  It is weakest to EM damage.

Alternatively you can destroy the Power Generator structure to make the Bowhead vulnerable. It has low HP and dies quickly, but be aware destroying it will give you a heavy EDENCOM standing penalty.

No penalty for failing the shield projector hack.
