# Hrada-Oki Bivouac

The Hrada-Oki Bivouac is a unique **Cosmic Signature** that appears in wormholes.

There are no hostiles in the site.

Two **Nomad**s (Hrada-Oki Mobile Decryption Hub and Hrada-Oki Atavum Transport) can be interacted with, using the open cargo button as with containers, to open Item Trader windows, see below.

1. # Item Trader

[Atavum](https://everef.net/types/85316) or [Infomorph Decryption Keys](https://everef.net/types/85746) can be traded for skills, **BPC**s, and **SKINR** components related to the **Deathless Circle**. This includes:
- **Cenotaph** BPC
- **Tholos** BPC
- **Breacher Pod** Launcher BPCs
- Breacher Pod skillbooks

The Hrada-Oki Mobile Decryption Hub accepts Infomorph Decryption Keys and the Hrada-Oki Atavum Transport accepts Atavum.
