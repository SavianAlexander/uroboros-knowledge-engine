# The Wildcat Strike

Video: [[**CNR**](https://youtu.be/6bAqgFSL5d4) April 2016]

  - Blitz:** Destroy ships at warp-in, take acceleration gate no kills necessary in the room.

  1. # Warp-in
Auto-aggro from all after a few seconds.
If you need more range make a bookmark and warp out. Then warp to your bookmark at your optimal range.
Respawn is triggered by killing the last drone from the initial spawn.

|}

|}
The gate is 41km from warp-in

  1. # Pocket

|}
Aggroing the left or right group first will also aggro the middle group, but not the other side. So if you shoot at the left group first, the right one will not aggro, but shoot at the right one first and the left will not aggro. Shooting the middle group __will__ agro both the left and right groups as well.

Drones do not pull aggro from left side.

Mission is marked complete once you land in the second pocket.

  - Structures:** No Structure drop loot.

  - Mining:** In Pocket 1 are 5 Roids of Veldspar 55466 Units

  - Bounty:** 3,558,934 ISK

  - Loot:** 0 (Rogue Drones drops nothing)

  - Salvage:** 1,450,000 ISK
