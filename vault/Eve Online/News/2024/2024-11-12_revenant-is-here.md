## Overview

### Luminous capsuleers,


The Revenant expansion is here, and with it come new and fearsome ships, powerful weapons, mercenary dens that harness new resources, as well as ways to activate and unify your corporation. The time has come to band together with the Deathless to strike at the heart of darkness.


## FLY FEARSOME DEATHLESS SHIPS


Your enemies won’t see you coming as you swoop in piloting new Deathless ships, the Tholos destroyer and the Cenotaph battlecruiser, unleashing fearsome SCARAB breacher pods to bleed targets from within over time. Start building your new weapons of war today!

Dive into the ship details in the expansion [**patch notes**](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/revenant-expansion-notes).


## DEN OF VILLAINY


The new mercenary dens will allow capsuleers in nullsec to connect to skyhooks orbiting temperate planets and convert their workforce into encrypted infomorphs, which are highly sought after by the Deathless. Deploy these personal structures for your own gain or to sabotage your enemies – starting right now!


## COMMAND YOUR LEGACY


Corporation Projects are evolving to meet the grand ambitions of New Eden’s most visionary leaders. Set bold new and lofty goals, unite your members in purpose, and watch as every task transforms into a defining act of loyalty. Enhanced control, flexible project scopes, deadlines, and equitable participation limits make it easier than ever to forge a legacy built on the contributions of capsuleers who stand ready to answer the call.  


## THE UNITED COLORS OF PARAGON


Unify your organization’s identity and make your unique colors soar across New Eden with exclusive, customizable corporation SKINs. The creative possibilities are endless with the addition of a second pattern slot, amazing blending options and 45 new patterns, the sky is certainly not the limit when it comes to outfitting your corpmates (or your alts). 

Your **brand managers** can now get your corp’s signature identity and flair into the hands of the right capsuleers – for however much or little PLEX or ISK they want. Paragon waives the fees for brand managers but collects a small EverMark fee from the corp member, so get synchronized in purpose and style. Brand managers' listings will be highlighted with a special background, because standing out is half the battle. 

To celebrate, **Paragon is halving sequencing time for the next two weeks!** Plus, the New Eden Store offers a Brand Manager Expert System, helping your team jumpstart their Paragon Hub journey.

Empower your corporation, stand together and stand out in New Eden.


## COMMUNITY INSPIRED IMPROVEMENTS


The community has spoken, the Council of Stellar Management has amplified the message, and the impact is here! A wave of enhancements, adjustments, and thrilling additions are taking flight, many inspired directly by feedback from the pilots of New Eden.

A variety of ships have been adjusted to improve combat dynamics, including marauders, as well as the Myrmidon, Harbinger, and Exequror Navy Issue.

New Rogue Drone relic sites have been added to nullsec for more action and rewards, and combat anomalies in nullsec get a stunning visual overhaul as well as allowing you to track your progress with the objectives panel. In addition, Observatory Flashpoint sites in Pochven get even more exciting than before.

Daring pilots who frequent Abyssal Deadspace have a treat in store, as abyssal modules can now be reprocessed into mutaplasmid residue. This opens up myriad possibilities as you can use them with freshly introduced blueprints to construct new and powerful mutaplasmids.

Modules are the heartbeat of your ships, and it is essential to identify them quickly in the heat of battle. To that end, several modules have been given unique icons, helping you pick them out at a glance.

Explore more new enhancements in the expansion [patch notes](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/revenant-expansion-notes).


## IMMERSIVE SHIP INFO


The redesigned ship information window delivers a more immersive and intuitive way to explore your vessels, putting the spotlight squarely on the ships themselves. Select your preferred view mode and get all the details, insights, and options you need as you set your fleet up for domination.


## THE IMPACT OF VANGUARD


The war has come to EVE Vanguard, and from **28 November**, warclones will take to planetside battlegrounds, impacting the ongoing war in New Eden on a grander scale than ever before. More details will be revealed soon. Stay up to date by wishlisting EVE Vanguard on [**STEAM**](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1872800/EVE_Vanguard/) and join the [**Discord server**](https://www.eveonline.com/discord) to become a Founder so you can shape the game’s development.


## THE JOURNEY BEGINS


The launch of Revenant is just the beginning as the expansion takes off, starting a journey packed with thrilling advancements and new opportunities across New Eden in the coming weeks and months. Soon you will be able to engage in tactical operations around mercenary dens, using the dens to build, disrupt, or raid enemy systems, and exchange your spoils for exclusive Deathless ships, weapons, and ammo. Empower your members to go fearlessly into battle with automated ship replacement programs, fast-track your progress with EverMarks to complete AIR Daily Goals, and much more.
