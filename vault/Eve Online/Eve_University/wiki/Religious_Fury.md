# Religious Fury

- Religious Fury** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **Blood Raider Base**. It is a rare expedition compared to similar ones in nullsec.
1. # Walkthrough
The expedition consists of two parts.

  1. # First Location
Ungated pocket. You land among four Blood Raider Chapels. Shoot them to trigger escalation if none is gotten from the Dark Blood commander.

|}

|}

  1. # Second Location
There are two possible scenarios for this second part: one which is fairly easy, and a harder one with the overseer spawn.
  1. ## Easier scenario

The Blood High Priest repairs other enemy ships until it is destroyed. Advise destroying it first if you want to take out the other rats.

|}

There is no overseer spawn for this scenario; you will only get Dark Blood faction ammo and dog tags, as well as a chance for a Dark Blood faction module.
  1. ## Harder scenario

|}

Dark Templar Uthius hits for about 760 EM and Thermal damage at a long range, although his tank isn't anything special. He orbits you at 38 km.

|}

1. # Loot
Typical loot from the Dark Blood battleship and the Blood High Priest includes dog tags and faction ammunition. Dark Templar Uthius can drop Corpus C-Type modules and in rare cases a **Bhaalgorn** BPC.
