1. # Summary
The **Armageddon Imperial Issue* (or *Imperial Issue Armageddon**) is a very rare ship. As with its counterparts in the other factions, the Imperial Issue Armageddon can only be acquired if given to a player by CCP Developers during live events; it cannot be acquired in-game. Presented during the Amarr Championship contest hosted in YC105, four of these magnificent ships were awarded to the four teams that made it to the final rounds. Three still remain; two are in the hands of Entity and one is owned by The Antiquarian. The fourth, previously owned by Tyrrax Thorrk, was destroyed on July 24, 2007 to a roaming 0utbreak gang.
