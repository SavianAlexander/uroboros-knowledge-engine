# Shaman Secrets

- Shaman Secrets** is a multi part level 1 mission for **Minmatar Republic**.

The structure of the mission chain suggests that it was supposed to be started in any of the three Minmatar Republic **data centers**, but this pre-requisite is not implemented in game. Instead, you can talk to **Pinala Adala** without having completed any of the data center missions to initiate the chain.

The mission chain will reward the player with one random +1 attribute implant and Minmatar Republic faction standings.

The mission chain can be completed only once. A failed mission can not be attempted again. But the missions themselves are fairly easy - as long as you pay attention and don't let the offered or accepted timers expire, they can be done without too much of a hassle. The missions are easy enough for a brand new player in their first frigate to complete them without problems.
