# Cowardly Commander

<noinclude> 
</noinclude>

Video: [[Typhoon](https://youtu.be/ZMn1S73Ul6U) June 2021]

  - Blitz:** Take the acceleration gates to the last room. Kill elite frigates if they point you. In last room triger the wave 2 and destroy it.

The mission is completed when last ship in third room wave 2 dies. This mission can be completed remotely and the next mission can be accepted remotely.

  - Tips:**
- The pockets are not deadspace. You can bm them and warp back if you take too much damage.
- The acceleration gates are NOT locked.
- Be prepared for very heavy dps and large amounts of EWAR
- Make sure to prioritize targets (destroy EWAR ships first)
  - Energy neutralizer towers are always primary targets
- To avoid getting neuted and scramed while traveling to the next gate, you can create **pingspots** while warping in into every room. Warp back to your ping, then warp to the next gate at 0 km.
- The Sansha Commander will be present in each room as you warp-in and will warp away without engaging.
