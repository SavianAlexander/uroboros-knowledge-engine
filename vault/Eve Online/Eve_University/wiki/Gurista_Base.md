# Gurista Base

- Gurista Base** is an **Unrated Complex** found by scanning in Null Security systems with Gurista presence.

1. # Walkthrough
The site is very similar to **Provisional Gurista Outpost**.  This site is doable in an Extender fit **Drake**. MWD or long-range weapons are recommended due to enemies spawning very far away.

Warping to the site lands you at an acceleration gate.

  1. # First Pocket
The acceleration gate to the second room is approximately 65 km away. It unlocks when all enemies are destroyed.
