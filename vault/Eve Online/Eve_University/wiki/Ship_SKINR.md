# Ship SKINR

In the **Equinox** expansion, released on 11 June 2024 CCP added SKINR Ship Personalisation to EVE Online. 
"(Super-Kerr Induced Nanocoating Resequencer) for ships. Purchase design elements, make your own designs, and trade your creations with other capsuleers."

1. # SKINR ship personalization
Capsuleers can create fully customized SKINs for their ships using SKINR. Express your identity in **New Eden** like never before, as an extension to SKINR unleashes a universe of possibilities by empowering creative capsuleers to design their own ship SKINs. Make each ship or fleet a declaration of your identity, explore a new career path, and strengthen the collective identity of your corporation or fleet. Through SKINR, the art of war takes on a new hue, allowing for unparalleled expression of identity in the theater of space.

  1. # Design at your fingertips
The SKINR interface can be accessed from anywhere in Space! Getting bored while gate camping? Sequence a new SKIN! Does your **Fleet Commander** have you sitting in staging for too long? Design a new masterpiece!

The Ship SKINR is located in the Paragon Services section in the **Neocom**. A shortcut to it can be put in the main Neocom Bar!

With the SKINR Paragon introduced a robust palette of Design Elements, consisting of Basic and Metallic Nanocoatings as well as Patterns to Pilots across the Universe. Each SKIN design includes five customization slots: four for Nanocoatings and one for Patterns. Patterns also have a slot nested in them to add a Nanocoating onto the Pattern. Each Design Element comes in 2 flavors: Unlimited or Limited usage. The Unlimited Design Elements will require Sequencing Binder items to be in your current Inventory. New Design Elements can be bought and sold on the Local Market, acquired in events, **daily goal**s, and through loot drops in various locations in game.

  1. # Naming custom SKINs and SKIN lines
SKINs are named during the design process, including the option to add them to a SKIN line. Use your creations to express your unique identity and share them with other capsuleers. Players can also save up to 5 designs for future manufacturing.

  1. # Sequencing
Gathering design elements and sequence binders allows capsuleers to sequence ship SKINs for themselves or to sell to other pilots. Sequencing costs are based on a tier system and calculated in **PLEX** based on the rarity of design elements and complexity of the SKIN. Sequence Binders can be bought and sold on the local market, acquired in events, daily goals, and through loot drops in various locations in game just like their design element counterparts.

If you have the skill  trained up, you will be able to sequence multiple copies of the same SKIN at once. Skill levels determine the quantity allowed. You will also need to have enough Design Elements and/or Sequencing Binders for the amount of SKINs being sequenced.

What if you want to sequence a few different SKINs at once? Look no further than the skills  and : These allow the sequencing of multiple SKIN designs simultaneously. Skill levels determine the number of simultaneous jobs.

  1. ## Sequence binders
These are items that are required when using Unlimited use Design Elements.
- Kerr Sequencer: Consumed by basic Nanocoatings when sequencing a SKIN.
- Fermionic Sequencer: Consumed by Metallic Nanocoatings when sequencing a SKIN.
- Alignment Sequencer: Consumed by Patterns when sequencing a SKIN.

  1. ## Amount of Glossyness
- Gloss materials: 0.75 gloss
- Satin materials: 0.60 gloss
- Matt materials: 0.40 gloss

  1. ## PLEX cost and sequencing times
The amount of PLEX you have to pay in order to sequence a skin depends on the complexity of the skin, which in turn depends on how many elements are being modified, what colors and/or patterns they are modified with as well as what hull the skin is for. Sometimes switching to a slightly different shade of a certain color can increase or decrease the complexity tier and thus impact the sequencing cost.

The sequencing times shown above are based on a character with no skills trained. The times can be significantly reduced by training some of the skills listed further down. Note that you can sequence multiple skins at the same time which would multiply the PLEX cost accordingly but not the sequencing time.
Note that according to [Equinox Update: Tweaks & Balances](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/equinox-update-tweaks-and-balances) both sequencing costs and times are temporary and subject to changes in the future.

Once you started sequencing a skin you have the option to finish the sequencing process immediately for an extra amount of PLEX. The exact amount of PLEX needed for this depends on how much time is left on the sequencing job.
