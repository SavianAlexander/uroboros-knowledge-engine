The Overmind Nursery Grove is a Rogue Drone site in **Pochven** that was introduced during the 4th quadrant of 2021 with the New Dawn expansion. It can be warped-to with a Beacon (Named "Celestial Beacon"), or a Cosmic Signature (Data type). The scanning difficulty for this site is Level III.

  1. # Warp-in
Acceleration Gate (Unknown ship limits)

  1. # 1st pocket
A massive swarm of Salvaging Phase-0xZ Forager drones will be 30 km off warpin. They will not attack you and the gate is already unlocked so the pocket can be skipped without destroying a single ship.  The next acceleration gate is roughly 35-40 km from warpin.

  1. # 2nd Pocket
Upon warp-in you will land 100 km off from a large group of frigates that autoaggro on landing, guarding your objective (the Overmind Nursery Nest, which appears on the overview as a Large Collidable Structure). They will approach you at roughly 1500 m/s with MWD and will attempt to swarm you.  Their damage range is small but the swarm size makes them dangerous to tank.  However their inertia is extremely poor and as a result they will change directions very slowly, so a wide spiraling orbit to approach the nursery nest will easily outpace the enemy ships.

In the case that you do want to fight the swarm, they deal 400 DPS in omni damage and have an individual signature radius of 45m (frigate sized). Their tank is extremely poor (175 HP with T1 hull resists), so a well-fit smartbomber can finish off the drones easily.

The nest itself has only 500 structure HP and is quick to destroy. Please do note the site will be removed from the probe scan window once the Nursery Nest is destroyed, **and the wreck needs to be salvaged in order to collect the loot.**
