Ungated normal space pocket. Rats listed are Guristas, other factions spawn based on region.

Random bursts of damage from Asteroid (Polygypsum) when mining - approximately 20% chance for 100-150 damage at range below 5km.  **Mining Drones may be destroyed by the blast**.  If they have ore on them it will also be destroyed and you *will not* be able to complete the mission.
