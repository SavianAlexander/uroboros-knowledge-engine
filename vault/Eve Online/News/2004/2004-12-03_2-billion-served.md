Some information to appeal to database-types, number crunchers and geeks in general, the size of the EVE Online database reached 2 billion items early this morning. One of the members of the EVE community manufactured a Cataclysm Cruise Missile I bearing the database serial number 2,000,000,000.

The player that manufactured this Cruise Missile will be receiving a gift package from CCP in his mailbox.
