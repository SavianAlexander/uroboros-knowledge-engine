We are happy to announce that the [Proteus release](//community.eveonline.com/releases/proteus) has been successfully deployed!

This release brings a whole host of [module rebalancing and the introduction of several new module variants](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/rebalancing-modules-round-two/), as well as a [redesign of the Exequror cruiser hull](//cdn1.eveonline.com/www/newssystem/media/66780/1/ExequrorHullRedesign.jpg), [new visual effects in asteroid belts](//cdn1.eveonline.com/www/newssystem/media/66780/1/PrettyAsteroidBelt.png) to improve immersion, a [re-balance of Recon cruisers](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=5318918), new features for the Beta Starmap, the introduction of small scale mining anomolies for rookie pilots in starter systems, the [retiremement of Teams](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/teams-removal/), new audio for Aura in Russian and German, and an awesome [new theme](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=warning&l=http%3a%2f%2fsoundcloud.com%2fccpgames%2feve-online-proteus-2015&domain=soundcloud.com) tune from CCP RealX.

For more information on the Proteus release, and to see a full list of features, take a look at the [Proteus release page](//community.eveonline.com/releases/proteus), and read more about what's coming in CCP Seagull's "[Coming to EVE in the Proteus release](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/dev-blog-coming-to-eve-online-proteus/)" dev blog. For a full and detailed breakdown of changes please see the [patch notes for Proteus](//community.eveonline.com/news/patch-notes/patch-notes-for-proteus).
