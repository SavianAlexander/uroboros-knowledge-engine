1. # Summary
The **Vanquisher** is a **Serpentis** titan. It requires both Gallente and Minmatar titan skills to fly. Predictably for a Serpentis ship, it is bonused for stasis webification strength and hybrid turret damage.
The Vanquisher is the highest DPS Titan and the second highest DPS ship in the game to only the **Zirnitra**. Seeing as it is an extremely expensive faction ship that very few people are privileged to fly, it is essential to have tech two capital hybrids and a full faction/deadspace (preferably officer) tank. It is common to dual tank this ship with both shield and armor mods so as to push the ship's EHP to the absolute ceiling. The Vanquisher can fit both the Gjallarhorn and Aurora Ominae doomsday devices, but the Gjallarhorn is recommended due to the shorter firing delay. Railguns are common due to the long range that titan brawls occur at, but blaster fits with null are also possible.
