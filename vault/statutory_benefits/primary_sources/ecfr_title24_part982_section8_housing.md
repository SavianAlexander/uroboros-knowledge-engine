### § 982.505 - How Housing Assistance Payment (HAP) is calculated
**(a) Housing Assistance Payment (HAP).** The monthly housing assistance payment by the PHA on behalf of the family is calculated as:
$$\text{HAP} = \text{Payment Standard} - \text{Total Tenant Payment (TTP)}$$
**(b) Total Tenant Payment.** TTP is the highest of:
(1) **30 percent** of the family's monthly adjusted income;
(2) **10 percent** of the family's monthly gross income; or
(3) Minimum rent established by the PHA (statutory baseline: **$50.00**).
