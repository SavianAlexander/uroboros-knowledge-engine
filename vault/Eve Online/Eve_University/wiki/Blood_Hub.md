# Blood Hub

The Blood Hub is a **combat anomaly** found in **Blood Raiders** infested regions. The site is easily done with sniper Battleship T2 fit.

1. # Walkthough
The site consists of single ungated area. Warping to beacon for close range would require a very good tank to handle the swarm’s incoming damage. Using a propulsion module or a Micro Jump Drive is recommended. With the initial group and 3 triggered spawns, the site totals of 27 ships.

1. # Escalation
The anomaly can escalate to **Blood Raider Prison Camp**.

1. # Rewards
The site rewards 9 million in bounties and the possible commander can drop faction modules, implants or blueprints.
