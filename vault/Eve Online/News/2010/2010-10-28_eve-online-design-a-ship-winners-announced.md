The "EVE Online Design a Ship" contest has ended and [winners](//news.deviantart.com/article/133951/) have been chosen from thousands of entries. But it doesn't end there. The responses to the deviantART contest has been as inspiring as the designs have been creative, and we have listened closely to what you have said. In the near future, we will hold a new contest where players can vote on their favorite entries. Information regarding this new contest will be announced at a later time.
