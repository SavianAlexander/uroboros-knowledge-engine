# Ystvia Lamuette

- Ystvia Lamuette** is a level 2 **Gallente COSMOS** Agent located at The Ebony Tower Beacon in Barmalie System and requires 2.00 Gallente Federation Faction Standing.

Ystvia Lamuette requires Preaux's Letter from **Preaux Gallot**. Her Missions do not lead to another Agent.

Her Combat Missions can be done in a Cruiser or a well tanked Destroyer.
