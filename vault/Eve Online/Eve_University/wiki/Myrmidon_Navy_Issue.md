1. # Summary
The **Myrmidon Navy Issue** is the faction variant of the drone-focused Gallente Battlecruiser, the **Myrmidon**. It retains all of the drone and repair bonuses available to the Myrmidon while also receiving bonuses to Medium Hybrid Turrets and Webification Drones. Though this ship still has a typical T1 resist profile, it does receive a significantly larger amount of buffer in the form of more base shields, armor, and hull.

1. # Skills
Although the Myrmidon Navy Issue can technically be flown with very low skills, it would be wise to train the skills necessary for a T2 armor tank before flying this ship in any PvP encounter. The highest damage can be achieved with a mix of Medium and Heavy drones due to this ship's 100mb drone bandwidth, so good drone skills such as , , , and  should also be trained to maximize this ship's potential. **Gunnery skills** are necessary if you wish to get the most out of the ship's bonus to Hybrid Turrets.

1. # Tactics
The Myrmidon Navy Issue is somewhat more suited to PvP than its T1 counterpart due to a speed buff and an overwhelming amount of DPS potential, but it is still a big, slow target which can quickly become prey to larger gangs of enemy ships. This ship may punch above its weight when it can lock down an enemy target and put blasters on them at close range, so use game mechanics such as ESS gates to pick the fights you actually want to take.
