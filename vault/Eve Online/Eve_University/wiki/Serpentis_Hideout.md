# Serpentis Hideout

Serpentis Hideout is an Unrated **Deadspace** Complex that is found in **High Security** and Low Security **Gallente** Space, via **Probe scanning**. The site consists of an entrance pocket, and two main pockets, separated by acceleration gates.
If running the site in a T1 frigate, or if using light drones, take care against the Elite Frigates (Coreli Guardian Agent/Defender/Guard/Protector) as they will do higher damage than the regular frigate enemies, and will target light drones.

1. # Walkthrough
When you warp to the site you will land on an acceleration gate. There are no enemies on it, and nothing around other than the acceleration gate that leads to the first main room of the site.

1. # Main Room 1

This room contains just a couple of frigates when you warp in. Destroying them will spawn additional waves of frigates.

1. # Main Room 2

Upon warp in this room contains a couple of frigates and a structure called Serpentis Armory. After an amount of time more enemies will spawn. Additionally, damaging the armory will cause a wave of enemies to spawn. Destroying the initial defenders has a chance to spawn a Shadow Serpentis faction frigate. Shadow Serpentis frigate rarely drops valuable Snake implant.

This site can escalate to **Mindflood**. Escalation chance ~25% (3 out of 13).
