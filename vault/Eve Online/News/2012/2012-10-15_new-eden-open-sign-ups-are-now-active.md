The [New Eden Open](//community.eveonline.com/en/tournament/tournament-info/entry) sign-up process is now live. Players can submit a team by clicking on [this link](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=tournament_index) from October 15, 2012 through to October, 26 2012. Once the sign-up process is complete the submitted teams can participate in an online auction in the ‘Live Dev Blogs’ channel on Saturday, October 27 at 19:00 UTC.
