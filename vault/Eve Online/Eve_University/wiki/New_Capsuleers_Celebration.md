# New Capsuleers Celebration

- New Capsuleers Celebration** was a limited time login campaign, lasting from 22 November to 1 December 2022, downtime to downtime to "celebrate the launch of the Spanish language being added to eve, and to welcome our new Spanish pilots to New Eden".

1. # Log-In Campaign

Capsuleers have ten days to log in for seven days worth of rewards. Alpha Clones could claim 95k **Skill Points**, while Omega Clones have access to 245k Skill Points.
