# Suspicious Job

- Suspicious Job** is an **expedition** that escalates from **Serpentis Fortress** and can lead into both low-sec and null-sec regions.

1. # Walkthrough
This escalation consists of four parts.

1. # First Part
Warp in. If Shadow Serpentis is there: kill him, possibly get escalation. Else (or If didn't get escalation): Use acceleration gate, possibly get escalation.
