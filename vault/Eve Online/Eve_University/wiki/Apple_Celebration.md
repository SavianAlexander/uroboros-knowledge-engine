# Apple Celebration

- Apple Celebration** is a login campaign that runs from 13th October to 20th October 2021 to celebrate EVE's native Macintosh client being released. The campaign rewards SKINs and boosters for everyone with up to 10,000 **Skill Points** for Omega clones.
