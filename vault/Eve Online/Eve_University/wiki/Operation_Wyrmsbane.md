# Operation Wyrmsbane

Video: [Barghest Blitz Walkthrough (Feb 2025)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gWoqXQ20Ark&list=PLr-IftNTIujQFGN_KtZKCcC2CTXTPVnbY&index=9)

  - Blitz: Destroy the Serpentis Stronghold.**

  1. # Single Pocket
- The First and Second reinforcement spawns are at 4 minute intervals.
- The Third reinforcement spawn is triggered by hacking the Info Shard, and spawns at a distance of 2,000 - 2,500 m from Shard. (The Info Shard does not always appear.)

  1. # Notes
- It is strongly suggested that you use Logistics support to keep your tank alive.
- Video: [Carrier Blitz](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4VVvhcmoqKY)
- 3rd Reinforcement must be eliminated to be able to access the can, after it is hacked successfully.
- Structure Loot:
  - Cruise Missles from the Bunkers
  - Serpentis Info Shard will drop regular data site loot (e.g. datacore, decryptors or decryptor parts)
  - Serpentis Stronghold drops nothing of interest
