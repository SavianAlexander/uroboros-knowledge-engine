1. # Summary
The **Flycatcher** is the Caldari **Interdictor**. It can be fit with a sizeable tank compared to other dictors, making it a viable option for shield fleets.

1. # Notes
Although the ship name aptly and simply describes its job, it is also probably named for the wide group of birds classified as [flycatchers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flycatcher).
