# Limited Mining Expedition

In a limited mining expedition site, you need to mine 10,000 units of ore and deposit it in the friendly **Orca**, while under attack from enemy frigates.

1. # Site details
Rewards are based on total time taken to complete the site.

| - | Ore Required | 10,000 units |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Time | 12 minutes |  |
| Max Pilots | 3 |  |
| Rewards | 150,000 ISK, 200 LP (240 LP or 260 LP with bonuses) |  |
