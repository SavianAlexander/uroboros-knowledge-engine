# Rabbit Hole

<noinclude> </noinclude>

This mission can be accepted remotely.

  1. # Room 1
Shortly after entering the pocket, the acceleration gate to the next stage will disappear.
The sentries have proximity aggro of 65 km and are spread out far apart. Destroying roughly 9 of them reveals the acceleration gate and causes -0.24 standing penalty to the Caldari State.

|}

  1. # Room 2
- Reinforcements 1 will spawn shortly after you enter the room.
- Reinforcements 2 will spawn upon destruction of the Caldari Navy Destroyer from reinforcements 1.
- Destroying one of the Point Defense Batteries (possibly the one on the right from warp-in) will make the Large EM Forcefield structure disappear and you can attack the Caldari Navy Storage Bunker structure directly instead.
- Destroying the storage bunker will make it drop Lieutenant Kipo “Foxfire” Tekira. Note that it will drop two cargo containers and only one has the mission objective.
- Destroying Caldari Bunker causes -0.24 standing loss to the Caldari State.

|}

Reinforcements will arrive soon after entering the pocket.

|}

|}

Bring back Kipo Tekira to Irichi Tsunikabda to complete the mission.
