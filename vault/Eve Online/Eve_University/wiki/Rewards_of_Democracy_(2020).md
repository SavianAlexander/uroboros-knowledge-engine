# Rewards of Democracy (2020)

- Rewards of Democracy** was a login campaign that ran from 1 June to 8 June 2020, corresponding with the voting period of the 15th **Council of Stellar Management** (CSM15). The campaign rewarded up to 45,000 **Skill Points** for Alpha clones, and 105,000 Skill Points for Omega clones (plus up to 17,280 Skill Points from the Basic Accelerator inside the gift box).
