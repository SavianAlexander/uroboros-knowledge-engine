# Airkio Yanjulen

1. # Pirate Escapade (1 of 4)

Mission: Bring Item 1 x Tantima Areki (5.0 m³). Remember to equip a Probe scanner.

Reward: 10,000,000 ISK + 5,000,000 ISK (Time Bonus)

1. # Pirate Escapade (2 of 4)

Mission: Bring Item 1 x Hakiro's Scanner Data (0.1 m³) +1 x 350mm Carbide Railgun I (Time Bonus)

Reward: 1 x Guristas War Plans.

== Travel to G5ED-Y to continue the missions with **Knaaninn Aranuri** ==

1. # Pirate Escapade (3 of 4)

Mission: Bring Item 1 x Guristas War Plans (0.1 m³)

Reward: 1 x Skirmish Command Mindlink + 6,000,000 ISK (Time Bonus)

1. # Pirate Escapade (4 of 4)

Mission: Destroy the State weapons convoy as soon as it arrives through the stargate. It will arrive in 3 stages. You have a few minutes to clear the guards before the first stage arrives. Location  G5ED-Y ()

Reward: 1 x Memory Augmentation - Improved + 1,000 x Dread Guristas Iridium Charge L (Time Bonus)
