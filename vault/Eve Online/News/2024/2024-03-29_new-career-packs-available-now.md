### Enterprising capsuleers,


Do you want to mine for the most valuable resources? Go on epic expeditions to discover the hidden secrets of New Eden?  Fight against other capsuleers, or become a pirate? It’s all on the table with the proper training, but if you just want to try your hand at something or only need the skills for a brief amount of time, you can get that done with Expert Systems. These give temporary access to skills that would otherwise require time commitment. They can also serve as templates for future skills training. 

Career packs take this a step further by bundling together Expert Systems, boosters, Cerebral Accelerators, Omega, and more. This is the ideal way to explore [careers](https://www.eveonline.com/eve-academy/careers), whether to accomplish a temporary goal or use them as a template for future skill training.
