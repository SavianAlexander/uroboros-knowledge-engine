# Guardian's Gala (2022)

- Guardian's Gala** is a recurring event. It went live on February the 10th, 2022 and ended on February the 22nd, 2022.

1. # Combat sites

Event combat anomalies spawn in all areas of space. The easier sites spawn in high/low/null security space while wormhole space spawns harder sites. The combat sites will appear as a warpable beacon on overview once someone has initiated warp to it.

The description text of the site suggests smaller enemies, orbiting close. It's recommended to bring a Battleship or a Heavy Assault Cruiser. A Battlecruiser is suggested by CCP as well, however doing the sites in a Battlecruiser, especially the harder variants, may prove difficult without excellent character skills and a good fit.

  1. # Loot

As of now, the following items are supposed/assumed to drop:

- 'Bonding' cerebral accelerators
- Agency drugs
- 6th - 13th Tier Overseer's Personal Effects

  1. # Guardian's Gala
This site spawns in normal space. The sites in highsec are the easiest, nullsec is intermediate, lowsec is the hardest of the K-space sites.

  1. # Guardian's Gala VIP Site
This site spawns ONLY in J-Space as well as in the Phoenix (Fountain) and Heaven (Curse) constellations in Null-Sec and is the hardest event site, but also features the best rewards.

1. # Data sites

  1. # Gala Coordination Center
These Level 3 event Data Sites spawn in normal and wormhole space. They also spawn in NullSec. Each site contains 6 cans that need be hacked (green and yellow difficulty). 

  1. # Gala VIP Coordination Center
Like their combat brothers, these Level 4 event Data Sites only spawn in J-Space and in the Phoenix and Heaven Constellations in the Null-Sec Regions of Fountain and Curse repectively.
Each Site contains 6 Cans of higher (yellow and red) difficulty. They generally hold more and more valuable loot than the standard event Data Sites

Possible loot is assumed to be:

- 'Bonding' cerebral accelerators
- Agency drugs,
- 5th - 8th Tier Overseer's Personal Effects, Guardian's Gala VIP Sites can drop up to 13th Tier Personal Effects,
- ORE Research Data Fragments, which can be sold on any market or exchanged for Skillbooks in ORE Loyalty Point Stores in the Outer Ring region,
- rarely Spirit SKINS,
- Fluff Items, mostly of alcoholic or festive nature,
- Fireworks,
- normal data site loot (Decyptors, Datacores, Materials, Science and Rigging Skillbooks, Blueprints, Filaments)

1. # Login campaign

Ran from 10th February 2022 to 22nd February 2022.

Capsuleers must have logged in for 9 out of the 12 days to collect all rewards. A total of 55k **Skill Points** were available to accounts, while  accounts had access to an additional 175k SP (plus up to ~17k SP from the accelerator).
