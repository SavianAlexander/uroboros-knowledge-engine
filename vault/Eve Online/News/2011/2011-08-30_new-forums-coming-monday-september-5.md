On Monday, September 5, 2011 after downtime, the new EVE Online forums will be deployed. Extensive testing has occurred, and many new features have been added. These new features include a compact view, a character dropdown list to keep you in contact with your fellow forum friends, the highly anticipated hide posts option, RSS feeds, new search functions, and a selection of backgrounds to customize your EVE Gate and forum experience. Full details of these additions can be found in the [patch notes](//www.eveonline.com/updates/patchnotes.asp?patchlogID=233).

Please note that the current forums will be disabled and placed into archive mode, where they can still be accessed, on Friday, September 9, 2011.
