# Angel Forsaken Den

The Angel Forsaken Den is a **combat anomaly** found in **Angel** infested regions. It is a level 3 variant of the **Angel Den**. The site is easily done with sniper Battleship T2 fit. The site pays estimately 2.5 million ISK in bounties.

With the initial group and 5 triggered spawns, a total of 34 ships.

1. # Walkthrough
Single ungated normal space pocket.
