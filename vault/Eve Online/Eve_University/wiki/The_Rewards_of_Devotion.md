# The Rewards of Devotion

- The Rewards of Devotion** is an **expedition** from the **Blood Vigil** **Unrated Complex**, which can be found at a **Cosmic Signature** via **exploration**.

The expedition has a chance to trigger upon the destruction of the **Corpus Station Manager**, a Dark Blood Cruiser Commander found in the **Blood Vigil**.
