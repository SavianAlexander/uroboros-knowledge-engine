# Lowsec Community

The **Low Sec Community** is one of several communities within EVE University. What makes this community different is that it focuses on life in Low Security space in New Eden. It is currently located in the **Faction Warfare** space of Minmatar/Amarr with good connections to the Gallente/Caldari space, Jita/Dodixie, as well as our High-Sec community in Korsiki.

1. # Community Details

  1. # Why LSC?
Lowsec space offers some of the highest risk-vs-reward gameplay in *EVE Online*.

  - Faction Warfare (FW) & PvP:**

- **Faction Warfare:** Capture sites and fight for territory to earn loyalty points and support your militia.
- **PVP Small Gang Roams:** Small, mobile groups hunting targets and engaging in fast-paced fights.
- **PVP Fleets:** Organized group combat with defined roles, coordination, and fleet command.

  - PvE & ISK Making:**

- **Combat Sites & Escalations:** Run NPC combat sites for loot, with chances to trigger higher-value follow-up encounters.
- **Mission Running:** Complete agent missions for ISK, loyalty points, and standings.
- **Exploration:** Scan and hack data/relic sites for valuable loot across space**.**
- **Clone Soldiers:** Hunt Clone Soldier NPCs in lowsec belts for tags used in security status repair.

  - Industry & Resource Gathering:**

- **Gas Harvesting:** Collect gas from nebula sites used in booster and industry production.
- **Moon Mining:** Extract moon ores from refineries for valuable minerals and reactions (Close to LSC in Audearne).
- **Mining:** Harvest asteroids for raw materials used in manufacturing.

You will learn to evaluate danger, gather piloting experience, and develop your own methods of handling dangerous space; fight, flight, or mitigate. You don't need to bring anything, other than a sense of adventure and a willingness to ask questions.

Isbrabata sits next to Turnur, a hub for wormholes connecting to many regions. These are mapped by EVE Scouts and available via their public bookmarks [EVE Scout website](https://www.eve-scout.com/).

1. # Joining & Travelling to LSC
While LSC is open to all EVE University members with no titles or Omega status required. We do recommend the following before you start your adventures in LSC.

- Attend both Welcome to EVE University classes and Intro to fleets and Intro. These are very informational and provide a solid foundation that will help you in your LSC journey.
- Pick up a FREE skillbook shuttle form Korsiki and inject the skills this save you having to remote inject skills saving you a little isk early on.
- If you are coming to LSC for Factional Warfare content, please check your standings for your chosen FW militia. You need a base standing of 0 or higher unmodified to enlist; if this is not the case, **Gaining faction standings fast** has information on how to get your standings up.
- Read EVE Universities **Rules of Engagement**

Then pop yourself into a shuttle (Not a Corvette) and make your way to LSC HQ  - *Isbrabata VIII - Moon 5 - Republic Security Services Assembly Plant* once there create a clone in the station clone bay and make the station your home station for when you inevitable explode.

Utilities that can help on your travel:

- [GateCampCheck Website](http://eve-gatecheck.space/eve/) and look for any smartbomb kills
- Join the LSC **Standing Fleet**
- Set up your **Overview** to the EVE University standard overview

At this point Welcome to LSC!

LowSec life centres on PvP, making communication and individual performance essential. To support all Unistas, LSC provides structured skill plans outlining key skills and ships.

  - Overview**

LSC Skill Paths offer a clear progression from early PvP to advanced fleet roles. Each stage builds practical, immediately usable skills based on real EVE University doctrines and Flight Deck ships—train what you fly, fly what you train.

- **Starter (Alpha + Omega add-on):** Core, universal skills and basic ships
- **Intermediate:** T1/T2 frigates, destroyers, and early doctrine roles
- **Advanced:** T2 cruisers, battlecruisers, and specialized fleet roles

The goal is to develop confident, adaptable pilots ready for anything from solo fights to organized fleets.

1. # Faction Warfare
EVE University’s Low-Sec Community (LSC) participates in Faction Warfare on both the Amarr and Minmatar sides. We engage in complexes (“plexes”) and very occasionally battlefields to help Unistas learn Faction Warfare mechanics and gameplay. Whenever possible, we encourage members to collaborate with militia groups, giving them valuable experience working alongside established FW players and fostering connections with the wider community.

All activities follow EVE University’s **Rules of Engagement** and are primarily focused on NPC-driven objectives.

Faction Warfare is a dynamic and sometimes challenging environment. While we provide guidance where possible, some situations require adaptability.

  1. # Nomad Offices
Faction Warfare is highly dynamic, with Frontline system status shifting frequently. To support both Amarr and Minmatar militias from a fully serviced base, LSC aims to keep its HQ in Isbrabata as a Frontline system—this is a key objective for Unistas.

To ensure continued participation when Isbrabata is not Frontline, LSC maintains **Nomad Offices**. This provides access to free Flight Deck ships and selected FW ships via contracts, allowing Unistas to dock elsewhere while enlisted and fight their way back to reclaim Isbrabata.

When Isbrabata is Frontline, only a limited number of ships are stocked locally, with additional contract ships made available if Frontline status is lost. During conflicts, the Nomad Offices will be actively restocked to support militia efforts. It includes basic services (e.g., a clone bay), while full services like buyback and fleets remain centralized at HQ.

  - Important:** Non-enlisted characters can always dock in Isbrabata. Enlisted characters cannot dock in NPC stations controlled by the opposing militia. Frontline systems are considered neutral and accessible to both sides.

  1. ## Ship Naming Schema
LSC uses a standard naming format to identify friendly ships on d-scan:

  - Format:** AAA 123 + optional name

- **AAA:**     3 letters from your main character’s name
- **123:**     Any three digits that add up to 13
- **Optional:**     Personal text

  - Example:** JOH 733 Pew

Alts should use the same 3-letter identifier as the main.

  1. # LSC Bookmarking Schema, WHs and Wanderer
LSC uses a shared bookmark folder:

- **LSC (EVE Uni):**  Open, community-maintained
- It is linked in the Standing Fleet MOTD and should be active. **All bookmarks use a 2-day expiry** (no “never”).

  - Site Bookmarks (Non-WH)**

Use prefixes with the default site name:

- **C**     – Combat
- **D**     – Data
- **R**     – Relic
- **G**     – Gas
- **O**     – Ore

  - Format:** P XXX-### Site Name

- **C/D/R     →** “Explo & Combat Sites”
- **G/O     →** “Gas & Ore Sites”
  - Perches & Saves**
- Placed     in root folder (not subfolders)
- Temporary,     2-day expiry
- **P:**     Perches (e.g. “Perch 1”)
- **S:**     Safes (recommend personal folders for long-term use)
  - Wormholes**
- Bookmark     **on-grid** (not from probe window)
- Stored     in root folder
- **Expiry:**     2 days (or 4h if EOL <4h or mass <10%)
- Maintain     regularly; wormholes change frequently

----**Outbound (leaving system)**

  - Format:** .SYS# XXX-### DEST SIZE [EOL]

- . =     outbound (top of list)
- SYS#     = system + number (e.g. .ISB1)
- DEST:     HS / LS / NS / TR / C1–C6
- Add     ship size + EOL if needed

  - Example:** .ISB1 XYZ-123 NS
----**Inbound (return path)**

  - Format:** *SYS# DEST SIZE [EOL]

- * =     return (“lifeline”)
- Bookmark     from the other side

  - Example:** *ISB1 LS

  1. # **LSC Wanderer & Mapping**
LSC uses **Wanderer** (https://wanderer.eveuniversity.org/lsc), a browser-based wormhole mapping tool for visualizing J-Space and tracking wormhole chains.

Wanderer does not sync automatically—**all scanned signatures, connections, and changes must be updated manually** both in-game and in the tool.

Using wormholes is optional but highly beneficial for navigation and mobility.

  - Chain Naming**

Numbers link in-game bookmarks to Wanderer chains:

  - Outbound example:**

- .TUR1     XYZ-123 M C2
- .TUR13     XYC-234 C3
- .TUR133     XYZ-345 HS

  - Return path:**

- *TUR133     → *TUR13 → *TUR1 (back home)

----**Important**

- Always     **mirror chain numbering in Wanderer**
- Remove     outdated bookmarks and delete old chains in Wanderer
