# Cargo Delivery (Blood Raiders) (Level 2)

Single ungated pocket. The mission item is inside the warehouse (container). 1st Wave appears when you reach the warehouse.

  - Blitz:** You don't have to destroy any of the ships, so you can just warp in, grab the goods and warp out. Mind the Elite frigates may warp disrupt.
