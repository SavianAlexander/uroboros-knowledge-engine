# Find a Corporation

Corporations (corps) are the player-run organisations that form the foundation of EVE Online's social and strategic gameplay. Choosing the right corporation can significantly enhance a player’s experience in New Eden, offering community, guidance, and shared objectives. This page outlines how to find a corporation that fits a player’s goals and playstyle.

1. # Why Join a Corporation?

While it is possible to play EVE Online solo, joining a corporation provides access to:

- Shared resources, fleets, and infrastructure
- Social interaction and teamwork
- Training and mentorship
- Protection and support in hostile environments
- Opportunities for progression and leadership

1. # How to Search for a Corporation

Players can use several tools and channels to explore corporations:

- **In-Game Corporation Finder Tool**: Found under the Social menu. Filters by activity, region, time zone, and size.
- **EVE Forums – Corporation & Alliance Recruitment** ([Link](https://forums.eveonline.com/c/corporations-alliances/recruitment-center/63))
- **EVE Discord Servers and Reddit** ([EVE Online Discord](https://discord.gg/eveonline), [/r/evejobs subreddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/evejobs/))
- **Third-Party Tools**: Sites like [Eve Workbench](https://www.eveworkbench.com/) and [Dotlan](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/) can help evaluate corps and alliances.

Before joining, it is advisable to research a corporation's:

- Activity level and time zone alignment
- Rules and expectations
- Communication tools (Discord, Mumble, forums, etc.)
- Tax rate and available infrastructure
- Culture and leadership style

1. # EVE University: A Training Corporation

- Regular classes and Q&A sessions

1. # Tips for Evaluating a Corporation

- Attend a public fleet or event if available
- Ask questions before applying
- Read any public websites, Discord, or recruitment posts
- Be wary of vague or overly ambitious claims
- Trust your instincts—culture fit is important

1. # Leaving a Corporation

There is no penalty for leaving a corporation. Players may freely leave or switch corps at any time (except during certain war or alliance scenarios). Many players move through several corporations over the course of their EVE career as their goals and playstyle evolve.
