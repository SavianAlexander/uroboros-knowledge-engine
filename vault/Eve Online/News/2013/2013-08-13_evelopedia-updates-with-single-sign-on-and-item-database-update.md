We are happy to announce that we updated the [EVElopedia](//wiki.eveonline.com/en/wiki/Main_Page), our player and developer driven EVE-Wikipedia, on August 13, 2013, with Single Sign On (SSO), the EVE Odyssey 1.0 Item Datebase and various other software and hardware upgrades.

The content of the pages is no longer locked for editing, so you can now log in and edit as normal again.

Happy editing!
