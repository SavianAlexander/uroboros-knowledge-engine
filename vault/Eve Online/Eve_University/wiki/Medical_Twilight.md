# Medical Twilight

- Medical Twilight** is an **expedition** that can be obtained by running **Blood Watch** combat sites.  It will take you to several new systems, each about 4 jumps from the previous one.  Only the escalation trigger ship needs to be killed to continue the escalation.

1. # First escalation

|}									
The next wave will spawn upon attacking the Ritual Center.

|}
The final wave will spawn when the Ritual Center is at half shield.

|}														
Do not underestimate the final wave. Being neuted, pointed and webbed can get you killed quickly! A suggested tactic is to fight at range and only go in to brawl when the elite frigates are off the field. To blitz this site, shoot the Ritual Center until all waves are spawned and then take out the elite frigates followed by the Dark Blood frigate. Be prepared to tank DPS under neut pressure.

1. # Fourth escalation (Lowsec|Highsec)
The warp in is within neut range of the Elite Cruisers. The DPS combined with the heavy neuting and webs is a serious problem for active tanked ships. Dropping a bookmark and warping in at range to the bookmark is recommended. Warping inside the site itself is possible and there are plenty of small asteroids to warp between. You can draw the Blood Factory Overseer and the Elite Frigates away from the rest by kiting (the Overseer BS has a top speed of 700+ m/s). [Jan 2025 (0.5 System)]

|}									

1. # Escalation failure
- Another Blood hideout and more slaves. You find no clues as to where the medical facility is, nor can you find any information on other parts of this Blood operation. It looks like your expedition has come to an end. *

1. # Rewards
Completing all four escalations gives the possibility of a Cruor BPC or Corpii (Frigate) A-Type module drops in the final escalation site.
