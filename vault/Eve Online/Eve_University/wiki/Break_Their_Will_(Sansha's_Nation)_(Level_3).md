  - Blitz:**
- Destroy Repair Station, warp out.

  1. # Warp-in
At warp-in there are no rats. Wave 1 spawns when the repair station is hit. The web tower will spawn approximately 50km below. Wave 2 is spawned when the Repair Station is destroyed. Auto-aggro on waves spawning.
