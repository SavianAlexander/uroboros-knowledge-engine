The Monthly Economic Report for October 2025 is now available!

**MER**


- Minor bug fixes around labels on various plots.
- There’s a discrepancy between `9_sinks_and_faucets` and `9b_money_supply.png`, this is due to two separate data-source for each plot. We’re working to rectify this data and having a unified data-source. This will take some time.


**Economic Trends**


- There has been a significant increase in commodities faucets due to the Crimson Harvest event.
- MPI continues to fall.
