# Drone Surveillance

- Drone Surveillance** is an unrated **combat site** belonging to the **Rogue Drones** faction. It can be found via **exploration** as a **cosmic anomaly** in low-security and null-security systems across New Eden. As an ungated complex, the limitations as to which ships can complete this site are tied to the security rating of the system where the anomaly spawns (T2 **Battleship**s for high-security systems, and up to **Titans** in low-security and null-security systems).

There are two variations of this combat site. The first variation takes place in the ruins of a megastructure and includes Light Missile Batteries in the initial wave. The second variation takes place inside an asteroid-filled cloud and includes Defense Vampire (nosferatu) batteries instead. Both variations have a chance to spawn a Sentient commander rogue drone, which drops drogue components and may drop faction drone modules and items. This site has a chance to escalate to **Rogue Drone Asteroid Infestation**.
