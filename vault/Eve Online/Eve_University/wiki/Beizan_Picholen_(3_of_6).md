# Beizan Picholen (3 of 6)

<noinclude>

Gallente **data center** storyline mission
Agent: **Avrue Auz**
Agent location: Lirsautton I - CreoDron Factory 
Standings required: none
Parts: **(prequel)**, **(1 of 6)**, **(2 of 6)**, **(3 of 6)**, **(4 of 6)**, **(5 of 6)**, **(6 of 6)**</noinclude>

The agent tells you to retrieve a message from **Mamo Guerre**. He is located at **Mamo's Backyard** in the same system. The location can be scanned down, or you can simply warp to the moon of Lirsautton VI as mentioned in the Mission Briefing. It is static - once found, the bookmark you put there will work forever. To receive Mamo's message you must do a few missions for the new agent.
