# Drifter Hive Anomaly

This page is mainly geared towards the overview of the Conflux(Pulsar) Drifter Hive in J200727.  Info about the other hives are not specified.  All Sleeper enemies will drop blue loot.

1. # Warpin
You warp in on a beacon named 'Conflux'.
The warpin to the complex is not guarded, and features a large shattered debris field and an acceleration gate ~80 km away to the lobby of the site.

1. # Lobby
The lobby is guarded by a few Sleeper frigates.  The acceleration gates are not locked.

|}
There are three acceleration gates leading deeper into the complex, and a spatial rift that takes you back to the warpin.

|}

|}

1. # Conflux Alpha Transfer
A few hackable containers, plus two acceleration gates and a rift going back to the warpin.  The gate is locked until the Alignment Unit is successfully hacked.

|}

|}

1. # Hive Anteroom
Features two large Sleeper structures at warpin. Sleepers drop blue loot.

|}

|}

1. # Conflux Gamma Transfer
Larger number of Sleepers. DPS can be high(around 500-700).  All Sleepers will drop blue loot. Gate deeper in is 74 km from warpin.

|}

|}

1. # Central Drifter Hive
The Hikanta Tyrannos battleship has a microwarpdrive and will orbit its target at about 7-10 km.  All other ships are 150-200 km away and can be ignored.

|}

|}

|}

1. # Nexus Room
This is an easy room near the central hive.
