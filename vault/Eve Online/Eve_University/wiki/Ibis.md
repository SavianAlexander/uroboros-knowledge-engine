1. # Summary
The Ibis is the Caldari **Corvette**. This ship is given free of charge to Caldari players who click on the "Board my Corvette" button while docked in a station. It has no blueprint and cannot be manufactured or reprocessed.

As with all Corvettes, the Ibis is a cheap and disposable hull, but is outclassed in every regard by almost any other player-piloted ship. Caldari pilots wishing to advance into different hulls may want to upgrade to one of the following depending on their preferred piloting style:

1. # Notes
Non-Caldari ship collectors can obtain a copy of this ship by visiting a Caldari starting station and completing Aura's tutorial missions, or it can be obtained via contract from another player.
Ibis is sometimes preferred as an extremely cheap ECM boat because of its 30% ECM strength bonus.
