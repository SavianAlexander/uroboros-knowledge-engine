# Winter Nexus (2024)

- Winter Nexus** is an event held from 5 December 2024 to 7 January 2025 (**downtime** to downtime) and includes **Volatile Ice Storms** which feature Wightstorm Sites, **Login Campaign**, and the **Winter Nexus Agency Challenge**,.

1. # Login campaign
Capsuleers must log in daily for 33 days from 5 December 2024 onwards to collect all rewards. A total of 125k **Skill Points** are available to  accounts, while  accounts have access to an additional 525k skill points and 26,500 **EverMark**s. The big reward is on day 25, so even if you do not manage to log in for all 30 days, as long as you manage those 25 days you will only miss out on some Boosters and Ice Storm Filaments.

1. # Volatile Ice Storm
Volatile Ice Storms have begun to appear across highsec, lowsec, and nullsec space. These ice storms spread out across the stargate network two jumps from a central system, and occasionally move from system to system just like **metaliminal storm**s. These storms provide bonuses to ship thermal resistances and module overheating for all capsuleers flying within them.

Sansha's Nation and Outer Ring Excavations have both mobilized to take advantage of the novel forms of stellar ice found within these storms.

  1. # Ice Storm Filaments
Ice Storm Filaments transport capsuleers to a System affected by the Volatile Ice Storm.

  1. # Combat anomalies
Wightstorm combat anomalies have begun appearing within the Volatile Ice Storms. These sites are full of hostile **Sansha**'s nation vessels, and **CONCORD** advises capsuleers to bring a **battlecruiser** or better when attempting to engage with these forces.

  1. ## Wightstorm Forward Base
Found within storms that originate in highsec space appears to be guarded by the weakest Sansha fleets.

  1. ## Wightstorm Muster Point
Found within storms that originate in lowsec space appears to be guarded by the strongest Sansha fleets.

  1. ## Wightstorm Transit Site
Found within storms that originate in nullsec space appears to be guarded by intermediate strength Sansha fleets. 

  1. # Hacking sites
Wightstorm data signatures can be found within Volatile Ice Storms. These Sansha's Nation data sites require scan probes and data analyzer modules.

  1. # Mining sites
Volatile Ice Field anomalies can also be discovered within Volatile Ice Storms. These fields of newly discovered stellar ice have recently been captured by ORE and Mordu's Legion forces who have driven away the Sansha harvesting fleets. These ice fields can be accessed through a special ORE-commandeered acceleration gate that only allows medium and small mining and hauling ships to enter (**Expedition Frigates**, **Mining Barges**, **Exhumers**, **Haulers**, **Blockade Runners**, **Deep Space Transports**, **Noctis** and the **Porpoise**, but **not** the Upwell haulers **Squall**, **Deluge**, or **Torrent**).

The volatile ice found within these fields cannot be reprocessed by standard facilities, however Outer Ring Excavations has set up mobile ice collection operations within each of these ice fields, and is willing to trade with capsuleers who contribute to their mining operation. Capsuleers can trade seven units of volatile ice with the ORE representatives in the ice fields to receive a package of valuable salvage and items that ORE forces collected from the wrecks of Sansha ships.

Rumors suggest that other valuable Sansha salvage has frozen directly into some of the ice chunks and can be occasionally picked up along with the ice by normal mining activities.

  1. ## Fading Volatile Ice Field
- Appear in high-sec
- Contains Faded Volatile Ice which can be exchanged for ORE Green Salvage packages. The packages, like **containers** have a capacity that is greater then their volume. They contain 2 to 7 items:
  - Common (i.e. in every package)
    - Overseer Personal Effects (5th-8th tier)
    - Salvaged materials
  - Less common
    - Ice Storm Filaments
    - Wightstorm Boosters
    - Festival Charges (Snowballs and fireworks)
    - Skins
    - **Agency Boosters** (Rare)
- Asteroids can respawn
- **Belt rat**s have been spotted

  1. ## Fresh Volatile Ice Field
- Appear in low-sec
- Contains Fresh Volatile Ice which can be exchanged for ORE White Salvage packages. The packages, like **containers** have a capacity that is greater then their volume.
  - Common (i.e. in every package)
    - Overseer Personal Effects (9th-16th tier)
    - Salvaged materials

- **Belt rat**s will spawn

  1. ## Friable Volatile Ice Field
- Appear in null-sec

  1. # Special game mechanics
All of the storm systems will feature a special set of environmental effects that reduce heat damage suffered by **overheating** modules and increases Thermal resistance.
