Economic Capsuleers!

The Monthly Economic Report for March 2024 is now available!

This month we changed the valuation of asteroids to be closer to the actual market value and less affected by price fluctuations caused by low volume trades that highly affect the asteroid prices. We also removed items from the Consumer Price Index that we feel should not be in there, because these items are mostly traded between players and NPCs.

Asteroids are now valued at 70% refining value. The following graphs are affected by this change:


- Production vs. Destruction vs. Mining
- Summary of Key Economic Figures by Region


Overseer Personal Effects, Sleeper Components, Triglavian Data, Rogue Drone Analysis Data and Bounty Encrypted Bonds were removed from the CPI. These changes have minor effects on the following graphs:


- Economic Indices - Full History
- Economic Indices - Since 2018
- Decomposition of Consumer Price Index
