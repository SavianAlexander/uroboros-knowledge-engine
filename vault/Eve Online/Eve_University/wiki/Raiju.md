1. # Summary
The **Raiju** is a special edition **Electronic Attack Ship**, introduced as one of the prize ships of **Alliance Tournament** XVII, alongside the **Laelaps**. A total of 140 ships were given out, distributed among the top 16 teams of AT XVII.

The ships stats and bonuses are a souped up marriage of the  and the . It can be looked at as a better Garmur with additional kiting tools in longer targeting range, longer point range, and bonused damps, or as a Keres that can more reliably deal damage at kiting ranges. Given the rarity - and thus value - of this ship, these kiting boons will likely be appreciated by its pilots.

1. # Notes
With six mid slots, the Raiju has the most mid slots of any frigate. In addition, it has a 15% EM shield resistance bonus on top of Caldari Tech II **natural resistances** for reasons unknown.

In Japanese mythology, the Raijuu (雷獣, "thunder beast") is a doglike creature with a body made of lightning. "Raijuu" should not be confused with the slang term *riajuu* (リア充), "normal, socially-successful person", a word with no particular relevance to EVE.
