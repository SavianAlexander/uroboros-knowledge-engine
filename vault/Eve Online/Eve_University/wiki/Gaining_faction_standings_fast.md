# Gaining faction standings fast

This guide explains how to quickly gain **Faction Standings** with the four major empires without (or at least lessen) the need for endless mission grinding. This guide will be particularly useful for pilots wishing to join **Factional Warfare**, which requires +0.0 base faction standing (before skills).

1. # Sister of EVE Epic Arc (Repeatable)

One of the best ways to gain **Faction Standings** is to run the **Sisters of EVE Epic Arc - Blood Stained Stars**. This arc of 51 missions provides you with an impressive +0.7 (+0.875 with ) to the empire faction of your choice with no derived standing changes. You can repeat the SoE Arc every 90 days.

1. # Career Agents

Career Agents Missions are comprised of five agents that provide you a set of beginner missions, linked to the five careers - Exploration, Enforcer, Soldier of Fortune, Industrial Entrepreneur and Industrial Producer. 
While the Career Agents are **unrepeatable**, each empire faction has **five sets of five agents** and can thus be used to quickly raise your Faction Standings with your desired faction. 

Each set of agents offers a total of 7.5% standings gain, with 3 sets per faction. 

Tips on how to complete sets of career agent missions quickly can be found at the bottom of **Career Agents**. 

1. # Data centers

Data center missions involve you giving the agent a set of pirate tags. This allows you to effectively exchange ISK for standing. The data center missions can be completed only <em>once</em>. See the main article for more details.

1. # COSMOS

COSMOS missions can be completed only <em>once</em>. Each agent will offer a short chain of missions where the last mission gives standing bonus similar to storyline missions. To speed up the process most of the items required for the missions can be acquired beforehand.

However COSMOS missions require you to have high enough standing (either corporation or faction) to accept the missions so only few of them can be used for repairing below -2.00 faction standing.

- Caldari
  - Okkelen constellation (**guide**)
- Gallente
  - Algintal constellation (**guide**)
- Amarr
  - High Security - Araz constellation (**guide**)
  - Low Security - Kenobanala System
- Minmatar
  - High Security - Ani constellation (**guide**)
  - Low Security - Audesder System

1. # "Circle" Missions

Circle missions offer the player small cargo courier missions and when completed, a referral to another Agent in the group. That Agent then refers the player to another Agent who also refers the player to another Agent, etc. Eventually, the player will end up back with the original starting Agent. The 4 major Factions (Amarr Empire, Caldari State, Gallente Federation, Minmatar Republic) each have their own group of 5 to 8 Circle Agents located in high security systems. One to two Agents in the group will give a <em>once-per-character</em> Faction Standing increase.

1. # Faction Warfare

Being promoted in faction warfare grants a solid amount of Empire Faction Standings. Promotions are linked to your standing with the NPC Militia corporation (for example "State Protectorate" for Caldari) and can be increased by completing **agent missions for the corp**, and for capturing complexes. See the main article on **Faction Warfare standings** to get an understanding on how participation will impact your standings.

1. # Regular Mission Running

Every sixteenth completed Mission of the same level for the same faction will generate a **storyline mission** of the same Level. **Blitzing** Level 4 **Security Missions** will help you speed up their completion and gain you more storyline missions.

1. # Tips and tricks
- Avoid accepting Missions against another **Empire** Faction. Example: The Gallente Federation wants you to fight the Caldari State? Simply decline these type of missions.
- There are several service providers around New Eden that offer to improve Faction Standings for you by e.g. running missions or Epic arcs for you in exchange for ISK. All you need to do is fleet up with another pilot and they will share standings with you.
