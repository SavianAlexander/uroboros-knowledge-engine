EVE-Pirate are running a competition in which you, the talented Capsuleer, can earn one of four $50 coupons to be used in the EVE Store. On top of the $50 prize, winning authors will also be given senior author membership which gives them the ability to post new entries to EVE-Pirate.com at will. For further details please see this [link](//www.eve-pirate.com/index.php?/archives/1155-End-of-summer%21-New-EVE-Pirate-CCP-sponsored-competition-You-can-have-our-stuff-%21.html).
