# Crimson Harvest (2016)

- The Crimson Harvest** is an recurring event involving the **Blood Raiders** pirate faction that was first introduced in October 2015. In 2016 the event was running from October 20th through to November 2nd.

The event returned in October 2016. 

The site info is based on 2016 sites.

1. # Participation
During the crimson harvest "Blood Raider Gauntlet" sites will spawn in all systems. The sites are visible on overview as beacons.

  1. # Warp in
The acceleration gate leading to next room is locked until all defenders are destroyed.

1. # Loot
Blood Raider Cerebral Accelerators gives +10 bonus to all attributes for duration of 24 hours. The advanced variant gives +12 bonus for 24 hours. The duration for both is extended by 20% for each level in biology.

The sites drop Cold Iron skins for all Amarr ships except the Legion and Shuttle. Also various clothing can drop such as the Blood Raider beret, Blood Raider cap and 'Sterling' shirt.
