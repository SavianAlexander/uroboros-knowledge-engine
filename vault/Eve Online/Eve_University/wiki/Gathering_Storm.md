# Gathering Storm

  - Gathering Storm** was a login campaign that ran from 9-15 June 2020, downtime to downtime. Pilots could log in for up to five days within the six day campaign to get up to 17,500 **Skill Points**, as well as skill books and boosters focused around the new Vorton Projector weapons and EDENCOM ships. Neither were on Tranquility for the duration of this event.

This event was announced concurrently with **Lightning Strikes**, with the two events together introducing EDENCOM ships and their corresponding skills, weapons, materials, and other modules.
