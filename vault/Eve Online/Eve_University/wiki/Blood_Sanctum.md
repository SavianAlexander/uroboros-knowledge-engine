# Blood Sanctum

- Blood Sanctum** is a **combat anomaly** that can be found in null security space with **Blood Raiders** presence..

1. # Walkthrough
  1. # Ring spawn Variant
The site is an open Deadspace pocket, one may warp to preferred distance. Killing all NPCs will trigger the next wave.
