1. # Summary
The **Armageddon Navy Issue is** an upgrade to the standard Armageddon hull, now featuring bonuses to battleship-size lasers and missiles. Like its T1 sibling, it can project capacitor warfare out to impressive ranges. Combined with the bonused weapons and drones, the Armageddon NI is one of few battleships capable of easily punching down onto smaller ships in PvP.

Whilst it is possible to fit an Armageddon NI with turrets, the lack of powergrid (especially for an Amarr ship) makes this somewhat of a fruitless endeavor. Those who use it therefore generally lean towards missiles, usually Rapid Heavies or Torpedoes like the normal Armageddon.

1. # Notes
Prior to the 2022 Uprising navy ship rebalance, the Armageddon Navy Issue was similar to the pre-rework regular Armageddon. It was intended as a battleship-size heavy laser boat with high DPS and a generous drone bay; however, the lack of fitting room made it hard to utilize the ship effectively, and it did not perform that well compared to the alternative T1 **Abaddon** or pirate **Bhaalgorn**. The Armageddon Navy Issue now has a larger damage bonus to both missiles and turrets, but it has lost the bonus which previously provided a reduction to Large Energy Turret activation cost, a change which pushes it even more toward using missiles rather than turrets.
