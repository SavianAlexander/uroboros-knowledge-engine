# Mistaken Identity

<noinclude></noinclude>

  - Mistaken Identity** is the Amarr way of starting the Angel Sound Epic Arc.

It can be picked up from Arajna Ashia at the Abandoned Research Station Sigma Nine Beacon.

To accept the Missions you need 3.00 Standing with either:
- Arajna Ashia
- Amarr Navy
- Amarr Empire
