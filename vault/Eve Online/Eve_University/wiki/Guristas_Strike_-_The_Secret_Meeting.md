# Guristas Strike - The Secret Meeting

<noinclude>  
  - Guristas Strike - The Secret Meeting** is mission 6 of the mission chain **Guristas Strike**. Unlike most storyline missions this mission gives no faction standing, but it does give agent standing. The faction standing is awarded for the final mission in the chain.
</noinclude>

Deadspace with gates. No hostiles at initial gate.

  1. # 1st pocket

Optional Fight. May drop a +4 implant.

|}
As indicated in the mission briefing and the transmission received when arriving in this pocket the envoy is the covert contact you were send to meet. There is no need to engage .

  1. # 2nd pocket
  - Blitz:** destroy group 4 pickup the prisoners and get out.

Group 4 drops the prisoners in a can.

|}
Each group can be individually, at least at range, engaged without aggro-ing the other groups.
