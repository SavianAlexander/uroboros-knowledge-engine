1. # Summary
The Thrasher Fleet Issue is one of four faction destroyers added to the game in the 2022 Uprising expansion. As the most powerful ships allowed in small NVY complexes, they are very popular in low sec PVP. The Thrasher Fleet Issue sets itself apart from the T1 Thrasher with new bonuses to rate of fire and MWD signature bloom.
