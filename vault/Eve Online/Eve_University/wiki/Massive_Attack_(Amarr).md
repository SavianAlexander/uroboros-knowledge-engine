# Massive Attack (Amarr)

Space type: Deadspace

Video: [[Nightmare](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LEBQ_OYH798) 2013-03-24], [[Machariel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zWqEArbUfNw) 2011-11-04]

  - Note:** The mission is reported to be against Amarr, but the Tracking Disruptor ships given are Sansha's Nation They do not appear in the details..

The majority of hostile ships are frigate/destroyer/cruiser class with only a handful of battlecruisers and only a few battleships.

  1. # Warp-in
The warp gate into the pocket is guarded by a small group of ships. They will aggro immediately after you have warped in.

|}

  1. # Pocket 1
Warp in is very near the Acceleration Gate (3500m)

Deploying drones to attack Group 1 will aggro Group 2.

Group 1 does not always aggro, if it doesn't shooting group 1 will also aggro group 2.

|}

Group 2 has a proximity aggro of 25km.

|}
Note: No loot from structures

  1. # Pocket 2
Warp in is very near the Acceleration Gate (3500m)

Deploying drones to attack Group 1 will aggro the entire pocket.

|}

|}

  1. # Pocket 3
Auto aggro from Group 1. Group 3 will aggro after several seconds.

Group 2 (located in the middle) remains neutral.

Any drones attacking Group 3 will be targeted and attacked by Group 2.

  - Blitz:** Eliminate all ships from Group 3 in pocket 3. Warp out.

|}

|}

|}

  - Tip:** Mission is flagged completed after you have eliminated all ships from Group 3.

  1. # Notes
When first posted, (on EVE-Survival) there were questions about whether an Angel Cartel Gistum Defeater was outside of the gate for an anti-Amarr mission. If anyone else getting this mission could confirm it that would be useful.
