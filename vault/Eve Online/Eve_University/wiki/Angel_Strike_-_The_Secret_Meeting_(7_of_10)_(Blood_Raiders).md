<includeonly>=</includeonly>===Pocket 1===<includeonly>=</includeonly>

Upon entering the first mission pocket, click through any dialogue boxes, then proceed through the acceleration gate without engaging.

<includeonly>=</includeonly>===Pocket 2===<includeonly>=</includeonly>

Hostile targets are separated into four groups, with one of the middle groups composed of transport ships. Attacking the transport ships will not aggro enemies in other groups. The mission objective item () will drop from the transports upon their destruction. It is not necessary to destroy any other ships once the mission item has been obtained.
