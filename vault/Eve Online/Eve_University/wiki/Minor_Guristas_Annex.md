# Minor Guristas Annex

The Minor Guristas Annex is an **Unrated Complex** found by scanning in Low Security regions with Guristas presence.

1. # Walkthrough
This site is very similar to **Guristas Watch**. Recommended high resists for kinetic and thermal damage.

Video: [[HAM Tengu](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tk_hO76WXOI) February 2014]

Upon warping to the signature you will land at an acceleration gate.

  1. # First Pocket
Either kill the Pith Militant Commander to unlock the acceleration gate or use a Guristas Crystal Tag to proceed into the second room.
