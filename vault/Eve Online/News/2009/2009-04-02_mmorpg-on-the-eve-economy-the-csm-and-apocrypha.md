Petur speaks about the nature of the Council of Stellar Management and its impact in this [video interview](//www.mmorpg.com/gamelist.cfm/game/14/view/videos/play/1391/The-Council-of-Stellar-.html). Dr. Eyjo leaves no economic stone unturned as he ruminates on the economic impact of Apocrypha and real world financial issues in this [video interview](//www.mmorpg.com/gamelist.cfm/game/14/view/videos/play/1384/Interview-with-CCP-Economist-.html). MMORPG finishes out the trilogy with a good [review of Apocrypha](//www.mmorpg.com/gamelist.cfm/game/14/view/reviews/load/75/Apocrypha-Review.html).
