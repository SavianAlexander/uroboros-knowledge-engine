# Quelling The Insurgency (4 of 5)

<noinclude>

Amarr **data center** storyline mission
Agent: **Arizam Gimit**
Agent location: Lossa II - Ministry of Assessment Information Center 
Standings required: none
parts: **(prequel)**, **(1 of 5)**, **(2 of 5)**, **(3 of 5)**, **(4 of 5)**, **(5 of 5)**</noinclude>

{{MissionBriefing
|I think I've found our lead. According to the prisoner, the man we're after is Mamin Choonka. He runs a diner in Ashab, at the moon of Ashab II to be exact. It offers a hefty discount to students of the academy, hence it's quite popular among their ranks.

I want you to pay him a visit. But don't go in with guns flaring, I want you to get to know him. Tell him you just graduated from the academy, and let's hope he tries to convert you to his cause. If all goes well, he will offer you a place within the Khanid insurgency. Perhaps that will lead us to one of their bases, or even better Deza Yobili.

}}

The trip requires a few hi-sec jumps to Ashab II - Moon 1.
When you warp to the area, you are placed some distance away from an unnamed and unmarked site inhabited by a few friendly mercenaries and **Mamin Choonka**. He will be your agent for a chain of four missions, after which you will receive Choonka's Coordinates needed for the current mission. Note that you have to be within 100km of the agent to start conversation.
