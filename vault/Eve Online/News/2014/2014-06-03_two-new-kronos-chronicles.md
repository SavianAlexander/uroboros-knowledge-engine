Two new chronicles have been released to coincide with the Kronos release!

The first, by CCP Delegate Zero, is titled [**Yetamo**](//wiki.eveonline.com/en/wiki/Yetamo_%28Chronicle%29) and has to do with two Minmatar orbital engineers who get into trouble with nanotechnology.

The second, by CCP Abraxas, is titled [**The Station and the Bazaar**](//wiki.eveonline.com/en/wiki/The_Station_and_the_Bazaar_%28Chronicle%29), and deals with the new type of pirate research installation that's going to crop up with Kronos.

You can discuss these chronicles [here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=349366).
