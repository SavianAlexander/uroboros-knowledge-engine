# Fara Bokh

- Fara Bokh** is a level 1 agent in **Minmatar COSMOS** located at Lanngisi in Sanctum Psychosis. Instead of offering the normal kind of COSMOS mission, he will offer a simple trade mission that can be repeated with no limit but offers only a small amount of ISK and corp standing as a reward.
