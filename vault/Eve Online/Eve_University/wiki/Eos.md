1. # Summary
The **Eos** is a Gallente fleet command ship, designed primarily as a drone boat. It has the 125mb drone bandwidth required to field a flight of sentry or heavy drones, and a respectable-sized drone bay, though not quite the equal to the Dominix or Ishtar.

The Eos finds use in advanced PvP fleets and gangs, where its resistance-bonused tank and capacity to boost both armor tanks and (for instance) tackle range are valued. It also brings considerable drone DPS, though given its cost it's a bit of a waste to use the ship for DPS alone.

1. # Skills
As the Eos is a drone boat, it is highly recommended that the following skills be trained before a pilot attempts to fly the ship:
- Drones V
- Gallente Drone Specialization IV
- Light Drone Operation IV
- Medium Drone Operation IV
- Scout Drone Operation V
- Drone Interfacing V
- Drone Navigation V
- Drone Sharpshooting V
- Drone Durability IV
- Sentry Drone Interfacing V
- Heavy Drone Operation V

Additionally, as the Eos is a (rather expensive) T2 ship, the pilot should be able to fit a full T2 armor tank. The armor compensations skills listed below are highly recommended but not required:

- Hull Upgrades V
- Mechanics V
- Repair Systems IV (for active armor tanking)
- Armor Rigging IV (for T2 armor rigs)

- EM Armor Compensation IV
- Explosive Armor Compensation IV
- Kinetic Armor Compensation IV
- Thermal Armor Compensation IV
