## Overview
Try out different paths and accelerate your training.

---

### Expert capsuleers,


In the vastness of New Eden, the possibilities are endless. The best way to quickly experience new activities without waiting for a long skill queue to run its course is to try them out with Expert Systems. Maybe you need to haul massive loot, and don’t want to invest into hauler training, or maybe you want to complete a mining objective without changing your training path, and Expert Systems are the way to go. 


| **Expert Systems*** | **Accelerators*** |
| --- | --- |
| Space Exploration Expert System | Basic ‘Boost’ Cerebral Accelerator |
| Enforcer Operations Expert System | Standard ‘Boost’ Cerebral Accelerator |
| Faction Warfare Expert System | Advanced ‘Boost’ Cerebral Accelerator |
| Hauler Operations Expert System | Specialist ‘Boost’ Cerebral Accelerator |
| Mining Operations Expert System | Expert ‘Boost’ Cerebral Accelerator |
| Core Ship Operations Expert System | Genius ‘Boost’ Cerebral Accelerator |


 **Activation of an Expert System or a Cerebral Accelerator is automatic upon redemption of the item.* 

These are designed to help you discover new paths by providing specific skills for a limited time that cannot be extracted through the use of Skill Extractors. Then, once you are ready to embark on the path you enjoy the most, Cerebral Accelerators will accelerate your training. Furthermore, you can look at the skills you’ve had while using the Expert System, and use those as a template for your future skill training.  

Cerebral Accelerators and Expert Systems are now available in the NES, so head over and check them out.
