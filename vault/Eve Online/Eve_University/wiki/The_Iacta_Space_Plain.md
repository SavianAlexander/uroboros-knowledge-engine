# The Iacta Space Plain

- The Iacta Space Plain** is a static **Guristas COSMOS** deadspace complex found in O-LR1H.

  1. # Warp-in

|}

  1. # 1st Room
The rats will use warp disruptors and energy neutralizers. The defenders will respawn quickly after being destroyed.

The Acceleration gate to deeper parts of the complex is locked until all the rats are dead.

|}

|}

  1. # 2nd Room
The shielded prison facility drops Guristas War Plans which is needed for **The Admiral's Task (1 of 3)**. It will also drop a corpse.

The pocket also contains Guristas rats.

|}
