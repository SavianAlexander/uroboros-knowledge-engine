# Ambush the Convoy (Blood Raiders) (Level 4)

- Ambush the Convoy** is a combat mission with multiple racial and level variants. The **Blood Raiders** variant can be Level 3 or 4.

Objective item (50 Prisoners, 375) drops from the last hostile NPC destroyed.
