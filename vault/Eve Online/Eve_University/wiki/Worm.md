1. # Summary
The **Worm** is one of the most popular pirate frigates, and is roughly comparable to assault frigates, both in DPS and tank. It can only use 2 light drones, but its role bonus makes them as effective as 8.

The Worm is effective in PvE (up to Level 3 missions with care, and some lower-tier **Abyssal Deadspace** pockets) as well as in PvP. It is significantly more costly than Tech 2 frigates such as the **Ishkur**, but it doesn't have such high character skill requirements.

1. # Tactics
Speed and the low signature radius are key to evading damage, while drones and launchers apply damage. The heavily-bonused **drones** are the key to the ship, so watch their HP very carefully: if they're threatened, you must pull them back to the drone bay at all costs. Navy drones or tech II drones are recommanded.
