# No Quarter

- No Quarter** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **Gurista Provincial HQ**.

1. # Walkthrough
The first two locations are doable solo, while the last two are much harder, with the final location requiring at least 1200 DPS minimum to break the overseer's tank. Even then it takes a long time due to the boss's strong active shield tank and high hitpoints, as well as high DPS that can tear apart a well-tanked T3 Cruiser with ease.

  - Warning:** The DED 10/10 **The Maze** combat site is comparatively easy to this escalation. Use extreme caution unless you want to risk losing your ship.

  1. # First location
  1. ## Warp-in
You land on an acceleration gate. Take it if no escalation is triggered from the Dread Guristas commander.

|}

  1. ## First pocket
No enemies in the pocket. Spy drones will warp away shortly after entry.

|}

  1. # Second location

Destroy the Guristas Control Tower if no escalation is received from the commander battleship.

|}

|}

  1. # Third location

There is considerably higher damage in this pocket; the Guristas Annihilation Missile Battery does extreme EM damage and the Siege Railgun Sentries do high alpha Therm/Kin damage. Together they can destroy an improperly tanked T3C in as few as three volleys. Thus, mainly tank against EM and Kinetic damage.

Attacking the Guristas Annihilation Missile Battery triggers a defensive wave. Destroying it or the commander battleship may trigger escalation and allows you to blitz through this location.

|}

|}

  1. # Fourth location
This location is very hard, requiring around 3-4 people in high-DPS strong tank ships. The overseer battleship has extremely strong shield reps which require the damage to exceed 1200 DPS to thermal.

Attacking the Guristas War Installation spawns two defensive waves, the second wave including the overseer.

The Siege Sentries deal a high alpha damage of 1200 mostly therm/kin per turret but fire every 16 seconds. Since there are at least 8 turrets firing at warp in, they can destroy a single T3C in two volleys if improperly tanked. It is advised to make a bookmark on-grid as you land at the site to warp to in case of danger and to reset aggro.

|}

|}

|}
Terrorist Overlord Inzi Kika deals 500 DPS with turrets and nearly 1500 DPS with missiles. He has 89% EM resists, 79% Explosive and Kinetic resists, and 69% Thermal resists.

It is strongly advised to keep your ship aligned to a bouncing bookmark 200 km off, warp to it as soon as the overseer wave spawns, and snipe the spider drones, the elite frigates and then the Terrorist Overlord. His turrets reach out to 200 km and his missiles to 130 km.
