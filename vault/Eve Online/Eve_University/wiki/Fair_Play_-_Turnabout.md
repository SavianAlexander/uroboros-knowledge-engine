# Fair Play - Turnabout

<noinclude> 
This mission is part 5 of the mission chain **Fair Play**</noinclude>

  - Walkthrough:**
- When you warp in a battle is taking place between Mercenary Pirates and CONCORD
- Most of the ships are over 50km away, but three frigates are conveniently close to you (around 20km)
- You only need to destroy three frigates, CONCORD take care of the rest

  - Notes:**
- The DED ships are about 60km away when you warp in. The DED ships warp out as soon as all the pirates are dead. Provided that you have a range of 50-70km, you can take them out. You can only shoot at them until the pirates are dead. The DED will aggro you if you attack them and can deal a lot of damage; you need a marauders to take them out quickly enough. My battleship took a few thousand damage from them.
- All Mercenary ships will be destroyed, only a few of the DED ships ones will be.
- This is one of the few level 1 missions worth looting, as the loot is worth more than the mission reward.

|}
