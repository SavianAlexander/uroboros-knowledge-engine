# Blood Rally Point

The **Blood Rally Point** is a **combat anomaly** found in **Blood Raiders** infested regions. There is another version of this site **Blood Raider Rally Point**.

1. # Walkthough

The site consists of single ungated pocket. Reinforcement spawns are triggered by the last hostile ship destroyed. All enemy ships spawn around the Blood Raider Lookout structure.

With the initial defending fleet and 4 triggered spawns, a total of 21-31 ships.
