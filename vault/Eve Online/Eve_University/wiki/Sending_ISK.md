# Sending ISK

Pilots who just want to transfer ISK to another character, whether their own alt or another player, can do so from the Wallet or from elsewhere in the interface.

If you are reading this page and are about to send ISK to another player, especially another player you do not know well, **pause to double-check what you're doing**. Unlike an item exchange contract, which can be named deceptively and can have confusing contents, but will always deliver its contents on payment of its set price, an informal ISK transfer imposes no in-game mechanical obligations on its recipient. Make sure you have familiarized yourself with **common in-game scams**, especially "**ISK doubling**".

  1. # To a character who is not online
This works for alts.

- Locate them in the **Contacts** panel
- Right click on their name
- Choose *Give Money*
- Fill in the amount and (if you want) a reason
- Send it

  1. # To a character you can see

- Right click on their name
- Choose *Give Money*
- Fill in the amount and (if you want) a reason
- Send it

  1. # From the Wallet

- Open the Wallet
- Select "Give ISK"
- Enter the exact character name in the "Transfer to" box
- Click on their name in the automatic drop-down box that opens up
- Fill in the amount
- Click "Transfer"
