Being able to see the world of EVE Online through the eyes of their characters has been a dream of many EVE Online players for years. The wait is finally over; we are pleased to announce that EVE Online: Incarna will be released on June 21st.

The journey towards making EVE the ultimate science fiction world begins with Captain's Quarters. For more information on this feature and plans for the future please read the [latest dev blog](//www.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&bid=914) by CCP Zulu.
