# Ritualist Raids

Video: [Sentry Dominix](https://youtu.be/wf7F44KoptY)

  - Blitz**
- Clear first pocket
- In second pocket destroy the battleships, destroyers and the cathedral.

  1. # 1st pocket
No aggro at warp-in. Proximity aggro range for the Blood Cruise Missile Batteries is about 30km.
The warp gate is locked until all hostile ships have been eliminated.

Notes:
- Eliminate the battlecruisers, destroyers and cruise missile batteries first, to reduce incoming DPS.
- If you do not move when you first warp-in, Cruise Batteries will not aggro you.

|}

  1. # 2nd pocket
At warp-in, you will get proximity aggroed from the sentry guns and missile batteries that sits within 30km range. No NPC ships will aggro UNTIL you start your attack.

- The sentry guns/missile batteries, each have an aggro & shooting range of 30km. Before starting your attack, move away > 30km to remove them as a threat.
- Eliminate the battlecruisers and destroyers first, to reduce incoming DPS.
- Mission is flagged completed once you have destroyed the Blood Raiders Cathedral and eliminated all defending battleships and destroyers.

|}

|}
