## Overview
The CSM are currently asking players to provide feedback on over 200 issues that they feel are important to players. The results of this vote will shape what topics the CSM will bring up with CCP in future meetings. If you want to have your say, please visit this thread for further details.

---

The CSM are currently asking players to provide feedback on over [200 issues](//wiki.eveonline.com/en/wiki/November_2010_Prioritization_Crowdsourcing_%28CSM%29) that they feel are important to players. The results of this vote will shape what topics the CSM will bring up with CCP in future meetings. If you want to have your say, please visit [this thread](//www.eveonline.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=1414376) for further details.
