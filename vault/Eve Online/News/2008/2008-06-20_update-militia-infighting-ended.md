**UPDATE**: Phoenix Tribe and Pillowsoft have just informed us that after a heated discussion, they have decided to end their conflict for the good of the Federation.

Understanding the need for a unified front against the Caldari menace, the principal parties of the conflict decided to end their feud. During the meeting, Nyphur said “Shake hands and we can all go out…”. Elijah Ghost finished the thought with: “and kill some Caldari”. RareEmperorAssKicker agreed and has initiated the war declaration retraction.
