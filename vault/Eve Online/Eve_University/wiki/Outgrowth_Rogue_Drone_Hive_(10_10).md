# Outgrowth Rogue Drone Hive (10/10)

- Outgrowth Rogue Drone Hive** is a DED 10/10 rated combat site that is found in null security space.

This site can be found by scanning cosmic signatures or as an escalation from **Drone Squad**, **Drone Patrol**, or **Drone Horde**.

Note that the DED 5/10 Rogue Drone site found in low is also called Outgrowth Rogue Drone Hive. See **Outgrowth Rogue Drone Hive (5/10)** for details on that. The two sites are very similar.

The room contains two acceleration gates. Ancient acceleration gate will take you to the third room while the surging acceleration gate will take you straight to fourth room but requires  to use. It also requires Zbikoki's Hacker Card (consumed on use) found in **The Right Hand of Zazzmatazz**. In a fleet only one member needs to have the hacker card. Once opened the gate will stay open for 10 seconds an allow passage to everyone with the required skill without requiring new hacker card.

The Surging Acceleration Gate can be used to blitz the site by skipping the fourth and fifth pockets, but you will miss out on some of the site's loot. If you want the full loot from the site, take the Ancient Acceleration Gate.
