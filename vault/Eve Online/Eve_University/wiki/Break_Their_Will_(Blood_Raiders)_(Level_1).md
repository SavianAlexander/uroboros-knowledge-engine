# Break Their Will (Blood Raiders) (Level 1)

Repair Station repairs itself and rats. Destroying the Auxiliary Power Arrays disables the Repair Station's repair function.

  - Blitz:**
- Destroy Repair Station, warp out.

  1. # Warp-in
At warp-in there are no rats. Wave 1 spawns when the repair station is hit. Wave 2 is spawned when the Repair Station is destroyed. Auto-aggro on waves spawning.
