# The Blockade (Blood Raiders) (Level 4)

Video: [[Dominix](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLFJjQ4mqXk) October 2013]

  - Blitz:** Kill all the trigger Battleships. You will need to snipe as the waves spawned will put out an incredible amount of DPS. Kill the final Corpus Pope Battleship.

  1. # Single Pocket
- All groups aggro on spawn.
- May (Fleet) warp to preferred distance.
- *Warning: The marked Cruisers may neut your capacitor heavily, best to kill them first.*

  1. # Loot & Bounties
- The Starbase Ship Maintenance Array drops random commodities.
- Approximately 20.16 million in bounties (if all rats are killed)
- Approximately 8.4 million in salvage (100% Jita sell value, 2021/09/28, Evepraisal).
- Approximately 10.2 million in loot (100% Jita sell value, 2021/09/28, Evepraisal).
