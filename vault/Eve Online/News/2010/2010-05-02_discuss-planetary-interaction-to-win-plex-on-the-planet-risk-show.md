[The Planet Risk Show](//www.planetriskshow.com/) is running a Planetary Interaction contest with a *"Dare to be us, pilot!"* twist. Record yourself impersonating hosts Luminus Aardokay or Quivering Palm while discussing Planetary Interaction to [win PLEX and get air time](//www.planetriskshow.com/pi-it-like-lumi-and-qp-and-win-yourself-a-plex/) on The Planet Risk Show! The contest ends May 18th and winning entries will be played during a special Tyrannis expansion launch show.
