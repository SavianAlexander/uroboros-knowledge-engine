# The Anomaly (2 of 3)

- The Anomaly (2 of 3)** is the second of three sub-missions comprising the Level 4 mission **The Anomaly**. It does not involve combat against hostile ships, but a ship with minimal tank is still required, as there will a brief period where the player ship is attacked by a sentry turret and also will need to survive the damage cloud around .
