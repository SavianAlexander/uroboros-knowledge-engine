# Ratan Saturi

- Ratan Saturi** is a level 4 agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located in Sakkikainen - Settler's Waystation.

1. # Cattle Thieves - Brigands at Large (1 of 3)

It is important to note that loot from this mission's rats is useful for other Caldari COSMOS missions. It consists of roughly (numbers may slightly differ): 16x Bandit Spur, 18x Desperado Spur, 15x Gunslinger Spur, 8x Marauder Spur, and 20x Outlaw Spur.

No enemies at first gate. Must have 1x Desperado Spur to activate gate.

  1. # First Pocket
No aggro on warpin. Attacking Group 3 with drones draws drone aggro from Group 4.

|}

  1. # Second Pocket
Group 1 aggro on warpin.
Proximity aggro between groups 2-4.

|}

  1. # Third Pocket
No aggro on warpin.

|}

  1. # Fourth Pocket
All enemies aggro on warpin. Last ship destroyed drops the cattle.

|}

1. # Cattle Thieves - Making Ends Meet (2 of 3)

Transport 2,600x Cattle (2,600 m3) to agent. The [shortest route](https://eve-gatecheck.space/eve/#Sakkikainen:Kiainti:shortest) travels through low-sec, but a [longer high-sec route](https://eve-gatecheck.space/eve/#Sakkikainen:Kiainti:secure) is available.

1. # Cattle Thieves - Revenge Is Sweet (3 of 3)

Loki's DNA drops from Loki Machedo's battleship in **Contested Guristas Outrider Post** - Prison Facility.
