# Sansha Military Complex

- Sansha Military Complex** is an **Unrated Complex** found by scanning in Null security regions with Sansha presence. T2 **Battleships** and below are allowed to enter the site.
1. # Walkthrough
Upon warping to the site you will land at an acceleration gate.
  1. # First Pocket

Attacking any battleship triggers wave 2.
