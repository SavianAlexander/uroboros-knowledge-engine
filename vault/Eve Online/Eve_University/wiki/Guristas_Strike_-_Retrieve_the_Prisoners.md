# Guristas Strike - Retrieve the Prisoners

<noinclude>  
  - Guristas Strike - Retrieve the Prisoners** is mission 9 of the mission chain **Guristas Strike**. Unlike most storyline missions this mission gives no faction standing, but it does give agent standing. The faction standing is awarded for the final mission in the chain.
