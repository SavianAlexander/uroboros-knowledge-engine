1. # Summary
The **Vigilant** is the Serpentis faction cruiser. It is capable of immense damage dealing thanks to its role bonus and strong tracking, as well as having an incredibly powerful web that can bring an enemy close to a dead stop. Unfortunately, as with the other Serpentis ships, its cost is very high even when compared to other pirate faction ships.
