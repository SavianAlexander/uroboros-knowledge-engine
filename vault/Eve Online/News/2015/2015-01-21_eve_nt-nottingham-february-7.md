EVE_NT will be held for the second time on February 7 in downtown Nottingham, England.

The one day player-run event will have plenty in store for attendees;Food and drink for sale all day, Q&A with devs and CSM, EVE PVP tournament with big prizes and 3v3 Valkyrie demos on the Oculus rift courtesy of CCP...etc.

Four CCP Developers are attending the event:

*CCP Guard - EVE Community Developer**CCP Gargant - EVE Community Representative**CCP Punkturis - EVE Software Engineer**CCP Bosun - Valkyrie QA Analyst*

Come hang out with us and all the other players (133 confirmed with 35 tickets remaining at this time)! We look forward to seeing you there.
