[EVE Fanfest 2007](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2007/) has ended, but we'd like to hear from you. What happened to you at the Fanfest? If you brought your camera, where are the pictures? What did you think about the Fanfest this year, what was good so we don't change it, what was bad and where can we improve it for next year? Please share it all in this [forum thread](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=629742).

We'd also like to thank everyone who came and made this the best Fanfest to date; we hope we get to see you all next year. We also hope that those of you who couldn't make it this year will come as well, and help next year be even better!
