# Mamin Choonka

- Mamin Choonka** is a level 1 Sarum Family (Amarr Empire) **data center** storyline agent located at a site near Ashab II - Moon 1 name Choonka's Diner. The site can be found using core scanner probes. His missions form a part of the **Quelling The Insurgency** mission chain.
