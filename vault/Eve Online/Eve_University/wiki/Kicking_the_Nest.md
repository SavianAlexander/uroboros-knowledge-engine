# Kicking the Nest

- Kicking the Nest** is the final mission of a 3 part mission chain, The other missions are **Survey Rendezvous** and **Listening Post**.

  1. # Warp-in
The acceleration gate is ~about 20 km from the warp-in.

|}

  1. # Pocket
  - Blitz:** Kill the Drone Hive.

When the Drone Hive is destroyed before the last ship of group A there will be no reinforcement waves.

Reinforcement 1 spawns after group A is destroyed.
Reinforcement 2 spawns after reinforcement 1 is destroyed.
