# True Power Shipyards

- True Power Shipyards** is an **expedition** that can be obtained by completing **Sansha Vigil** combat sites. This **expedition** consists of 4 different open area locations (no Acceleration gate) starting in a **High Security System** and ending in a **Low Security System** but it can suddenly end at any step in the chain. **NPC** class encountered are (Elite) Frigate, Destroyer, (Elite) Cruiser, Battlecruiser and Battleship hulls.

  1. ## Note
Warning: Your ship can be freely attacked by others - NO CONCORD INTERVENTION - in Low Security Systems (0.1 to 0.4) and Null Security Systems (0.0). Always check Star Map statistics (Active ships, Ship kills, Pod kills, etc) of Low Security and Null Security systems first before entering. Fitting a Cloaking Device module on your ship and making more than one Safe spot will provide more safety.

The Sansha's Battletower has a lot of HP (50.000 HP Shield + 150.000 HP Armor), make sure to clear the site first or be able to tank the site for 5-10 minutes.

Put the Sansha Battletower to structure to spawn the Foreman.

1. # Tactics and Hints
- The Sites have no gates, so there are no ship restrictions
- You can warp freely inside the site
- make a bookmark on warp-in to bounce
- Site 1 and 2 have no spawn at warp in
- Site 3 has high incoming DPS at warp in, but you can get out of range fairly easy
- You can warp in cloaked at Site 4 and bounce back to stay out of range (45km) of the towers
- Site 1-3 can be done in good tanked **Tactical Destroyers**
- Site 3 has faction loot only in the reinforcement spawn. The faction cruiser can be sniped as there is no initial aggro at 50km away after the group spawn.
- Site 4 can be done in an **Stratios**
