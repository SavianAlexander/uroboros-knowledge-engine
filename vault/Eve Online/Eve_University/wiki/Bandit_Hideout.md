# Bandit Hideout

The **Bandit Hideout** is a small COSMOS combat site in Sakkikainen. It is marked with a warpable beacon that is visible in the overview. This site is a part of **Caldari COSMOS**.

1. # Walkthrough

Single ungated pocket. Destroying the ships will not result in a standing loss. This site is a source of Bandit, Drifter and Marauder Spurs.
