# Sansha Forsaken Den

Sansha forsaken Den is a **combat anomaly** found in **Sansha** infested regions. It is level 4 variant of **Sansha Den**. Estimated bounty payout of 4.5 mil isk.

1. # Walkthough

The site is an open Deadspace pocket, one may warp to preferred distance.

The anomaly contains a lot of elite frigates and cruisers, which will actively target drones.
