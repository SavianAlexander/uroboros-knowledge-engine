Remember, if you haven't finished the [first](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=254551&find=unread) or [second](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=3317423) week of images, you still have until the end of July to go out, snap those screenshots, and get free stuff and a chance to have your name permanently engraved on a new site in New Eden!
