# Forgotten Ruins

- Forgotten Ruins** is a very rare combat relic site found only in the Delve region, especially in the OK-FEM constellation.  Up to T2 **Battleships** can enter the site, and it often features large groups of **Rogue Drone** and **Blood Raider** rats. This combat site is harder than many lowsec and nullsec ones, and should only be attempted by skilled players.

1. # Walkthrough
The site is similar to the **Bloated Ruins** and **Wispy Ruins**. Try to avoid triggering two waves at once, as the site can then become very difficult.

  1. # Warp-in

The rats at warp-in are passive unless attacked, but have to be destroyed to unlock the gate.

  1. # Fourth Pocket (Sanctum)

The Drone Creation Compound fires Citadel Explosive torpedoes that deal very high Explosive damage. Recommend at least 70% resists to Explosive damage.

There are 2 Ancient Ruins containers that must be hacked with a Relic Analyzer before getting access to their contents. Hacking can trigger a spawn of Blood Raiders (4xBS,3xCruisers). Approaching a container can also trigger an explosion.

Hive has an active tank that requires about 400-500 DPS to break.
Rare chance Kasham Murdec arrives.  No loot.
