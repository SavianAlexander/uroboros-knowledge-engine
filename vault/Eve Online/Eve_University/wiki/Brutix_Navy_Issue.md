1. # Summary
The **Brutix Navy Issue** is an iteration over the regular **Brutix**. The Armor Repairer amount bonus has been swapped for a 7.5% Medium Hybrid Turret tracking bonus per level, another low slot has been added and it is a bit more maneuverable as well.
