# Sansha Rally Point

- Sansha Rally Point** is a **combat anomaly** found in **Sansha's Nation** infested low and null security regions. There exist **Sansha Hidden Rally Point**, **Sansha Forsaken Rally Point** and **Sansha Forlorn Rally Point** variants of this site.
There is also a **Sansha's Rally Point** site.

1. # Walkthrough
Ungated pocket with multiple waves of rats.
