Following  the Alliance Panel at Fanfest 2012 , an incident was broadcast live on air by a panelist during the Q&A which broke the EVE Online EULA/TOS. The panelist was a member of the CSM who has subsequently resigned from CSM6 and, as a result, will forego his seat on CSM7. GM Salmon, the VP of Customer Relations Management, provides further details in [this dev blog](//community.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&nbid=28576).
