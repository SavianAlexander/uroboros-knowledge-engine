# Blue Pill

- Blue Pill** is an **expedition** that is triggered by the destruction of the Drug Lab in the second area of an **Angel Hideout** combat site with this message:

This expedition consists of four different open area locations, all in high security systems (although some low security travel may be required to reach a location).  All locations will have a Newly Constructed Acceleration gate leading to a second room. Accessing this gate is not required to complete this expedition.  Gates permit all non-capital ships except capsules.

The ships encountered during the expedition are **Frigate** and **Destroyer** class. The names and amount of ships may vary from what is listed here.
