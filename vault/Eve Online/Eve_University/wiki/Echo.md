1. # Summary
The Echo is a **corvette** (corvettes were formerly called rookie ships) provided by the Angel Cartel to teach new pilots fleet combat doctrine and tactics.

1. # Notes
The Echo was given as a promo at Pax East 2013, having been reworked for the event. Prior to this it required Jove Frigate 5 in order to operate, making it impossible for players to use even if they somehow acquired it, as well as a different description:
 The Echo assault frigate was developed and produced by the Indocon Corporation early on in the first era of galactic conquest. Asteroid mining had become increasingly dangerous due to pirate traffic, and the first prototype of the Echo was made.
