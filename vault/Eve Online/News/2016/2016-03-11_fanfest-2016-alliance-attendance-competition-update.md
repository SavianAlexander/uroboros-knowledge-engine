It's time for an update on the [Fanfest 2016](//fanfest.eveonline.com/) Alliance Attendance Prize Draw!

As previously announced, alliances headed to Fanfest with 10 or more members are eligible for this prize draw. You can read more about the [prizes available here!](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/eve-online-news/fanfest-2016-alliance-prize-draw/)

Alliances with 20 members or more present will double their chances. If you bought a ticket and didn't enter your alliance affiliation, please go to [Eventbrite](//www.eventbrite.com/), sign in, and update your order appropriately to give yourself and your alliance mates a better chance of winning. There should be a link to your order in the confirmation E-mail from Eventbrite.
