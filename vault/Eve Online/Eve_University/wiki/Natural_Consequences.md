# Natural Consequences

<noinclude> </noinclude>

Single gated deadspace pocket.

The next wave may not spawn instantly after previous one has been destroyed. All subsequent waves after wave 4 seem to spawn on timers and will often appear while the previous wave is still alive. 

The mission can be completed and the next mission accepted remotely.
