# Guristas Sanctum

- Guristas Sanctum** is a **combat anomaly** found in nullsec regions with Gurista presence.
1. # Site Details
The site is an ungated pocket with no deadspace and multiple waves of rats.
  1. # Ring spawn

The site consists of five rings in a line, each about 20 km apart. The warp-in point is between rings 1 and 2.
