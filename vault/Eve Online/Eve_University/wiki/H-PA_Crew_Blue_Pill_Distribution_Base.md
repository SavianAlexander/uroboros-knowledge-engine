# H-PA Crew Blue Pill Distribution Base

- H-PA Crew Blue Pill Distribution Base** is a **hacking** site found in [The Forge](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/The_Forge) region of space in low security systems. The site appears to Probes as a gas site.
1. # Walkthrough
The site is ungated, like most low security **chemical labs**.

All rats (except for the boss cruiser/frigate) have special names but are just renamed Guristas deadspace rats.
