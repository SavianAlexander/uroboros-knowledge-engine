# Advisor's Master Cheat Sheet & Talking Points Guide
## Explaining the Dental Practice QuickBooks & Puerto Rico Tax Suite Without Hesitation (*Sin Titubeo*)

## 1. Document [01]: Meeting Preparation Guide
*   **Spanish Title:** *Guía de Preparación: Reunión con Oficina Dental*
*   **Key Concept:** Initial discovery and setting expectations regarding Puerto Rico tax rules.
*   **Elevator Pitch:** "We are here to align your dental software data with QBO so that tax filing is automated, legal withholdings are met on time, and we set the stage for major tax optimization."
*   **Core Talking Points:**
    *   **IVU Exemption:** Dental services are **100% exempt** from IVU (PR IRC Art. 4010.01). Patients and insurance are never charged sales tax.
    *   **OTC Retail Sales:** Retail products (toothbrush kits, teeth whitening) sold over-the-counter are **taxable at 11.5%** (10.5% state, 1.0% municipal).
    *   **10% Professional Withholding:** Payments to independent contractors (associate dentists, dental labs) exceeding $500/year require a **10% withholding at source** unless they provide a SURI waiver (Form SC 2756) for 0%, 3%, or 5%.

## 2. Document [02]: Tax Optimization Report
*   **Spanish Title:** *Informe de Optimización Contributiva - Práctica Dental*
*   **Key Concept:** Converting from an individual/partnership to a Professional Services Corporation (PSC) with a Ley 60 decree.
*   **Elevator Pitch:** "By restructuring your practice into a regular corporation and securing a Ley 60 decree, we can legally drop your effective tax rate from 33%+ down to approximately 9.12%."
*   **Core Talking Points:**
    *   **Qualified Physician Decree (Ley 60):** Flat **4% tax rate** on clinical net income, and **100% tax exemption** on dividend distributions up to $250,000/year.
    *   **The Federal Tax Trap (Form 1040-SS):** PR bona fide residents are exempt from US federal income tax but **still pay the 15.3% US Federal Self-Employment Tax (FICA/Medicare)**. If they use a pure pass-through entity (LLC or Partnership) with a Ley 60 decree, the entire $300,000 profit is hit by the 15.3% federal tax, erasing local savings.
    *   **The regular PSC Mitigation:** By using a regular corporation (PSC):
        1. The owner takes a "Reasonable Salary" (e.g., $60,000). Only this salary is subject to the 15.3% tax.
        2. The remaining profit ($240,000) is taxed at the flat **4% corporate rate** under Ley 60.
        3. The post-tax profits are distributed as **100% tax-free dividends**, completely avoiding the 15.3% federal tax.
    *   **Business Meals & Break Room Supplies:** 
        *   Standard business meals: **50% deductible** (Hacienda allows entertainment; US federal does not).
        *   Employee holiday events/Christmas parties: **100% deductible** (PR IRC Sec. 1033.17).
        *   Employee break room (water, coffee, snacks): **100% deductible** to employer, tax-free to staff as *de minimis* fringe benefits (PR IRC Sec. 1032.06).
        *   Patient waiting room refreshments: **100% deductible** as promotional expenses (PR IRC Sec. 1033.17(a)(2)(E)).
    *   **Fringe Benefits & Luxury Cars:**
        *   Lunch Vouchers: Tax-free to employee up to **$8.00 per day**, 100% deductible to employer.
        *   Luxury Auto Depreciation: Maximum capitalized value is **$30,000** (depreciated straight-line over 5+ years, max **$6,000/year**). Operating expenses (gas, insurance) are deductible only in proportion to the $30k limit ($30,000 / actual cost).

## 3. Document [03]: Mapping Template: QuickBooks to SURI
*   **Spanish Title:** *Plantilla de Mapeo: QuickBooks a SURI*
*   **Key Concept:** Reconciling QBO transactions into SURI filings.
*   **Elevator Pitch:** "This guide bridges the gap between your daily bookkeeping in QBO and your monthly/quarterly tax filings on the SURI portal."
*   **Core Talking Points:**
    *   **Laboratories & 10% Withholding:** When booking a lab invoice, we debit the expense for the full amount, but credit 10% to `2100 - SURI 10% Withholding Payable` and pay the net to the supplier. We deposit the withholding in SURI by the 15th of the next month.
    *   **IVU Monthly Reconciliation:** Sales in QBO Account `4000` (Exempt) and `4200` (Taxable) must perfectly reconcile with the SURI Form SC 2915 on the 20th of every month.
    *   **HIPAA Patient Privacy:** Never input patient names or treatment descriptions in QBO. Book sales as a consolidated daily total (e.g., "Daily Clinical Collections") backed by Open Dental reports.

## 4. Document [04]: Guia Referencia Catalogo de Cuentas QBO (COA & ADP)
*   **Spanish Title:** *Guía de Referencia: Catálogo de Cuentas de QBO*
*   **Key Concept:** Technical chart of accounts design and payroll integration.
*   **Elevator Pitch:** "We designed a customized 66-account chart of accounts matching QBO's database parameters and integrated it with ADP to automate your payroll booking."
*   **Core Talking Points:**
    *   **CRIM Depreciation Floor:** Unlike financial reporting where assets depreciate to $0, the CRIM enforces a **minimum residual value floor**: **10%** for dental machinery and furniture, and **20%** for computer hardware. We must not write these accounts down to zero.
    *   **ADP Journal Accruals:** Net payroll drafts clear to `2095 - Direct Deposit Clearing`. Federal FICA/FUTA taxes draft to `2090 - Federal Payroll Taxes Payable`. Local state taxes draft to SURI `2500`.

## 5. Document [05]: Municipal Tax & Compliance Audit Guide
*   **Spanish Title:** *Guía de Auditoría de Arbitrios Municipales y Cumplimiento*
*   **Key Concept:** Navigating local city hall regulations (CRIM, Patentes Municipales).
*   **Elevator Pitch:** "State tax decrees (like Ley 60) do not cover municipal taxes. We must apply for local town hall exemptions separately under Ley 107-2020."
*   **Core Talking Points:**
    *   **CRIM Property Exemption:** The CRIM grants a **$50,000 personal property tax exemption** on business assets. We must actively claim this on the property return filed on May 15 to avoid paying the full municipal tax rate.
    *   **Evertec/ATH Movil Gross Booking:** Credit card processors deposit sales net of processing fees (e.g. depositing $97 instead of $100). We must book the **gross sales amount ($100)** to QBO income and record the processor fees separately. Otherwise, we under-report sales volume on our Patente Municipal return.

## 6. Document [06]: Incentives, Grants, and Legal Loopholes Guide
*   **Spanish Title:** *Guía de Incentivos, Subsidios y Estrategias Fiscales Legales*
*   **Key Concept:** State/federal incentives and financial arbitrage.
*   **Elevator Pitch:** "We want to take advantage of government payroll subsidies, write off 100% of equipment purchases immediately, and set up tax-free cash flow loops."
*   **Core Talking Points:**
    *   **Section 179 Equipment Expensing:** Write off **100%** of heavy clinical machinery (tomographs, CAD/CAM, dental chairs) in the year of purchase rather than depreciating it over 5-7 years.
    *   **Self-Rental Loop:** The dentist leases their building (owned via a personal real estate LLC) to their dental corporation (PSC) at Fair Market Value. The clinic deducts the rent expense (saving up to 37.5% tax), while the LLC offsets the rent income using building depreciation and mortgage interest, allowing the doctor to draw tax-free cash.
    *   **WOTC & Law 120-2014:** Get up to **$9,600** in federal tax credits for hiring eligible candidates, and a **50% salary subsidy** for hiring youth aged 16-35.

## 8. Document [08]: STAR Technique Communication Guide
*   **Spanish Title:** *Guía de Comunicación: Método STAR*
*   **Key Concept:** Structuring emails, chats, and calls for executive clarity.
*   **Elevator Pitch:** "We write all our advisory communications using a 4-step framework to ensure the client gets the context, understands the problem, knows the action required, and sees the outcome."
*   **Core Talking Points:**
    *   **S - Situation:** State the background facts in 1-2 sentences.
    *   **T - Task:** State the problem or objective.
    *   **A - Action:** State who needs to do what, with a deadline.
    *   **R - Result:** State the benefit of doing it or the cost/penalty of doing nothing.
