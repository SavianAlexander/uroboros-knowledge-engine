### Celebratory capsuleers,


The schedule for Fanfest Week 2025 was announced recently, and it’s shaping up to be just as epic as ever. Returning Fanfest classics and brand-new adventures will take place around Reykjavik, Iceland, in the leadup to the main event from 1-3 May, where the Friday and Saturday will serve as the main program days. 

[**Ticket sales are now open**](https://www.eveonline.com/fanfest) for every extra event for Fanfest Week, the last additions being the Pub Crawl, Foodie Walking Tour, and the Friday Fleet-Up Party.  

EVE Fanfest is a unique opportunity for capsuleers to get together and celebrate all things New Eden. The community is what makes any MMO great, and this is especially true for EVE Online. It is therefore always a pleasure when players come together in Reykjavik for the greatest event in the EVE Universe. 

Tickets to EVE Fanfest 2025 are still available and now is the time to plan your trip to Iceland and secure your place. With many of the extra experiences selling out quickly in previous years, be sure to grab your tickets before they're gone. 


## FANFEST 2025 PROGRAM STRUCTURE


The program format in 2023 was well received by attendees, and is making a return for 2025, with Friday and Saturday being the main program dates. Fanfest week 2025 kicks off on Monday 28 April, with extra events and social gatherings happening throughout the week. Pre-registration starts **Wednesday 30 April**. The official festival opens on **Thursday 1 May**, with ticket registration, social events, player presentations, tours, and more.
