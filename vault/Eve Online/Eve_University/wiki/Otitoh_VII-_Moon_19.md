# Otitoh VII- Moon 19

Otitoh VII- Moon 19 is a COSMOS site with Kazka Enemies guarding the Kazka Loot Container, which contains **Goru's Shuttle**, for **Goru Nikainen**. You will have to destroy all Kazka ships before being able to open the Container. After about 15 minutes, Kazka ships will respawn. After about 10 minutes, Kazka Loot Container will refill itself.

Apart from **Goru's Shuttle**, Kazka Loot Container also drops random commodities and industrial components.
