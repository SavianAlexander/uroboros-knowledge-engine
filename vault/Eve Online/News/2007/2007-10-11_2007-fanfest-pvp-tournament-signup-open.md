We've hosted an annual [2007 Fanfest PvP Tournament](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2007/pvp.asp) every year for the Fanfest and this year is no exception. Come to Iceland, get the latest news about EVE, meet friends and foes and... kill them. We have now opened [signup](//myeve.eve-online.com/fanfest/2007/pvp/signup.asp) for this event. Remember that the number of teams are limited and first come first served applies.

Discussion of the 2007 Fanfest PvP Tournament may be found in this [forum thread](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=599305).
