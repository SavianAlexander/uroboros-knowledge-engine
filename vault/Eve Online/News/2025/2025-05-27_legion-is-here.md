## Overview

---

Illustrious capsuleers,

The Legion expansion has landed in New Eden, unleashing a wave of new opportunities to energize your next chapter among the stars.

Freelance Jobs help corporations grow while giving new capsuleers meaningful ways to connect and contribute. Two powerful new ships enter the battlefield, while new sovereignty upgrades enable deeper customization to nullsec space. Corporations can now establish a corporate palette, crafting a unique visual identity to boost pride, unity, and recognition. A massive ship and module balance pass refreshes fleet combat and logistics. In addition, visual updates to ships and the map bring style and function together.

Check out the **In Focus** and the [**patch notes**](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/legion-expansion-notes) for more information about the innovations and improvements in Legion.


## Get Your Daily Rewards


Celebrate the launch of Legion with 14 days of thrilling login rewards! From exclusive SKINR components like Atomic Sheen Gloss and Exo-Spinal, to substantial Skill Point drops and EverMarks, both Omega and Alpha pilots can reap substantial rewards every day, with Omegas getting up to 550,000 SP! Keep an eye out for iconic boosters like Quafe Zero and the Standard Cerebral Accelerator as well. 


## Great Deals Abound


Capsuleers can claim a special Omega offer from the New Eden Store: seven days of Omega for just 10 PLEX. It’s the perfect boost to help you seize the moment, fly the new ships, and sharpen your edge. This limited-time offer ends on 3 June.

Meanwhile, the [**EVE Store**](https://store.eveonline.com/) offers two themed packs. The Initiate Ascension Pack is tailored for rising capsuleers, offering Omega access, a cerebral accelerator, and two expert systems including the Kikimora Pilot. Veterans looking to cement their legacy can grab the Ascendant Legacy Pack, which includes 500,000 Skill Points, 1,000 PLEX, a Genius accelerator, and an exclusive set of Azdaja Extirpated SKINs.

Finally, new [**Twitch Drops**](https://www.eveonline.com/twitch) are available until midnight on 10 June, so keep your eyes firmly on CCP TV and select Twitch partners. Tune in and claim your in-game rewards. 


## Hype for the Hub


An abundance of new components have arrived in the Paragon Hub, expanding your creative arsenal with striking new colors and patterns. In addition, you can sequence SKINs faster in the Paragon Hub until 16 June, getting your unique designs out there even quicker. 


## Power Awaits You


Whether you're a seasoned veteran or a new capsuleer, Legion offers unparalleled opportunities to impact New Eden and shape the future. Warp in today to start making your mark and claim the rewards, offers, and opportunities waiting for you.
