1. # Agency Campaign
The seasonal challenge track offers trader tokens (Warpath Operational Intel, Warpath Command Intel), PLEX, SoCT ships, skillpoints, and more. Progress through the track by completing the following challenges:
- Complete Security Missions for empires.
- Mine Crokite or Plagioclase in empire space.
- Kill Elite Mercenaries in new combat sites
- Hack Strategic or Intelligence Databanks in new exploration sites
- Make a trade with Quartermaster Traders
- Capture or Defend FW Complexes

Challenges feature five tiers, ranging from Hireling to Warpath Master. Completing each tier awards trader tokens which can be exchanged with **Quartermaster Trader**s.

1. # Quartermaster Trader
Quartermaster Traders are the empires' primary tool for winning Capsuleer loyalty. Earn tokens (Warpath Operational Intel, Warpath Command Intel) by participating in event activities, then exchange them at empire-specific traders found widely throughout each empire's space for a choice of various rewards. They wil be available until a 9 June 2026, a week after the end of the event.

Quartermaster Traders are located in the following systems:
- Amarr space: Amarr, Gergish, Tash-Murkon Prime, Nare, Mehatoor, Kor-Azor Prime
- Caldari space: New Caldari, Ikao, Onnamon, Malkalen
- Gallente space: Dodixie, Luminaire, Intaki, Faurent
- Minmatar space: Rens, Hek, Pator, Amo

The Quartermaster location shows up at the **overview** as a beacon (<empire> Quartermaster Trader). To see the actual exchange object on the overview "Large Collidable Object" must be activated.

The quartermaster sites have a passive proximity effect (presumably as an anti-gank measure) that will add a flat 50,000 shield or armor hitpoints to any ship that warps in and prevents Microwarpdrives from being used.

1. # Combat sites
Enemy forces are attacking up to three complexes that control this system's War Grid, a network of local combat installations.

Defend and secure as many complexes as you can before the operational window closes. Kill all hostile ships at each complex and **remain on grid** to make progress in defending. Once a complex has been secured, an extraction point will become available to warp to, containing compensation & rewards. The value of loot in the supply cache increases with the number of complexes secured.

Each complex within the site will become visible as a public Celestial Beacon once a player warps to the initial anomaly. In the Defense and War Grid, players will always land at the Alpha Complex beacon when warping to the anomaly. The sites are all ungated and not deadspaced, so any ship can theoretically enter, however only specific ship classes can contribute to capture progress.

There will always be a few friendly and hostile NPCs present on initial warp-in. The hostile NPCs have poor tank and very low damage output, but some of them will warp-disrupt you. A named Elite Mercenary NPC will also show up ~30 seconds after initial warp-in flying a T1, Navy or Pirate Faction ship equivalent to the maximum capture-eligible ship class size. They do a significant amount of damage alongside warp disruption and additional EWAR that varies depending on ship type. All ships will periodically respawn every minute or so.

The non-mercenary NPCs will **deal damage according to their faction**. Mercenary NPCs can do wildly varying damage types, so tank for omni damage.

  1. # Warpath: Defense Complex
- Found in high security space.
- One complex (Alpha) to defend. 20 minute operational time limit.
- Can be captured by T2, Navy, Pirate, and T1 frigates.
- All NPCs within the site are flying Frigates. Mercenary NPCs will average around 50-100 DPS.

  1. # Warpath: Defense Grid
- Found in low-security space and high-security space.
- Three complexes (Alpha, Beta, Gamma) to defend. They initially start at 90, 120 and 150 defense progress, with 25 minutes to capture as many of them as possible. The starting number corresponds to different complexes across sites. Rewards scale based on how many complexes have been successfully captured when the timer is up.
- Can be captured by T2, Navy, Pirate, and T1 cruisers and below.
- Friendly and non-mercenary hostile NPCs are flying cruisers in groups of three. Mercenary NPCs fly T1, Navy and Pirate Faction Cruisers and average around 200 DPS, with more powerful ship types corresponding to higher DPS and more dangerous EWAR.
  - Highest DPS spawn: Ortega's Vexor Navy Issue with 270 DPS EM/Thermal.
  - Can also spawn Talman's Ashimmu with 200 DPS EM/Thermal and 30 GJ/s cap neut.

  1. # Warpath: War Grid
- Found in low-security space. Now can also spawn in high-security systems as of 26 May 2026.
- Can be captured by Battleships and below.
- Three complexes (Alpha, Beta, Gamma) to defend. They initially start at 90, 120 and 150 defense progress, with 30 minutes to capture as many of them as possible. The starting number corresponds to different complexes across sites. Rewards scale based on how many complexes have been successfully captured when the timer is up.
- Defender and non-mercenary hostile NPCs are flying Battlecruisers. Mercenary NPCs fly T1, Navy and Pirate Faction Battleships and average around 500 DPS, with more powerful ship types corresponding to higher DPS and more dangerous EWAR.
  - Highest DPS spawn: Ruud's Vindicator with ~700 DPS Kinetic/Thermal and -75% web at 10 km.
  - Can also spawn Talman's Bhaalgorn with 500 DPS EM/Thermal and ~38 GJ/s cap neut.

1. # Exploration sites

Explorers can seek out exploration sites, which can be found in highsec, lowsec, nullsec and wormhole space. The highsec sites contain no hazards, while in all other areas of space they contain sentries and proximity mines that will activate upon triggering a site "lockdown".

All site variants require a Data Analyzer to access the loot.

  1. # Warpath: Strategic Infiltration

| Nodes !! colspan="1" | Tools Available |
| :--- |
| 32px|link=|System Core]]70 10 |

Found in lowsec, nullsec and wormholes. After the release 2026-05-26.1 the sites can also be found in high-sec. The site is functionally very similar to **Observatory Infiltration**, with similar lockdown mechanics that activate upon wandering too close to certain structures or failing any hack. In nullsec the site is gated, allowing only T1, Pirate and Faction ships up to Cruiser-sized hulls. 

There are several hazards to keep watch for, which are:

- **Sentry Nodes:** They activate upon triggering lockdown and deal 60 DPS omni damage each with high tracking speed. They can be hacked to prevent them from activating upon triggering lockdown. The hacks are Easy difficulty (70 HP green System Core).

- **Sentinel/Tripwire Nodes:** They act as proximity mines that will automatically trigger lockdown if a player enters their area of effect.

- **Confinement Nodes:** **Hack and disable these structures before doing anything else.** They will deploy interdiction probes when lockdown is triggered leaving you a sitting duck for the Sentry Nodes to destroy. The hacks are Very Easy difficulty (50 HP green core). 

- **Siren Node:** When lockdown is triggered, it will summon a hostile response fleet. It can be hacked to prevent the response fleet from spawning, however this is a Medium difficulty hack (yellow System Core).

- **Master Security Node:** Acts as a "buffed" Sentinel Node that will trigger lockdown within its area of effect. If lockdown is active it can be hacked to turn off the lockdown protocol, however this is a Hard difficulty hack (red System Core).

The site contains:
- 8x <Faction> Strategic Databank, where <Faction> corresponds to the enemy of the faction controlling the solar system (e.g. Republic Strategic Databank in Amarr space).

Cloaking up or leaving grid after entering the site will start a 10-minute timer after which the site will despawn. 

Possible loot:
- Warpath Strategic Data
- Warpath Operational Intel
- Infinite Celebrations and Capsule Cascade fireworks
- Capsuleer Day SKINs
- Skill hardwiring implants

Totals about 30M ISK without implant drops. Hacking all eight containers will also give a fleet payout of 7M ISK per player.

1. # Game mechanics
  1. # System-wide Bonuses
The empires aren't just looking for your support, they're encouraging Capsuleers to fly their ships. When flying a ship in its own empires' space, you can benefit from unique bonuses.

- Imperial Warpath Zeal - Applies to Amarr ships in space controlled by the Amarr Empire
  - +7.5% Energy Turret Damage
  - +7.5% Armor Hit Points
  - +10% Warp Speed

- State Warpath Discipline - Applies to Caladri ships in space controlled by the Caldari State
  - +7.5% Missile Launcher Rate of Fire
  - +7.5% Shield HP
  - +10% Warp Speed

- Federal Warpath Fervor - Applies to Gallente ships in space controlled by the Gallente Federation
  - +7.5% Hybrid Turret Tracking
  - +7.5% Local Armor Repair
  - +10% Warp Speed

- Republic Warpath Pride - Applies to Minmatar ships in space controlled by the Minmatar Republic
  - 7.5% Projectile Turret Falloff
  - +7.5% Velocity
  - +10% Warp Speed
