These are exciting times, not only for EVE but for the future of virtual worlds in general as the first democratic elections began today for the Council of Stellar Management. EVE Online subscribers may vote for their representatives who will then represent them as council members interacting directly with CCP. Visit our [election page](//myeve.eve-online.com/council/voting/) to cast your vote now or at any time during the two week voting period. Elections will end the 19th of May.
