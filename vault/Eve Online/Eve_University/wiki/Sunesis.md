1. # Summary
The **Sunesis** class Society of Conscious Thought destroyer is a special edition ship. It has been given out on a number of different occasions, including:
- EVE Online: Ascension in November 16 2016
- 15th Capsuleer Anniversary (along with an **Apotheosis** shuttle, **Gnosis** battlecruiser, and the new **Praxis** class battleship).
- The [Luminaire Snowball Fight](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/luminaire-snowball-fight-vi) on December 15, 2019.
- Every Capsuleer day festival (EVE Online's Birthday).

Despite fast align times associated with SoCT ships they are notoriously slow in overall speed. The Sunesis sacrifices overall resist levels against all types of damage in order to eliminate resist "holes". This arrangement means shield and armor tanking for the Sunesis would be handled slightly differently than with standard destroyers. While the only skill required to fly this vessel is Spaceship Command I, further skill progression in drone related, armor or shield tanking, scanning, and weapon specialization (specifically progression to the point of allowing Tech 2 modules) will **drastically** increase the effectiveness of the ship.
