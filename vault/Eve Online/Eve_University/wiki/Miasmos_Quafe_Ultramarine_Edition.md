1. # Summary
This ship is a not simply a cosmetic variant of the **Miasmos**. When the **Odyssey** expansion converted the Iteron Mark IV into the modern ore-hauling Miasmos, these special edition ships had their generic cargo hold grandfathered in and not converted to ore-specific holds.

Because of this, the special edition Miasmos variants offers a unique combination of large cargo bay and relatively high warp speed.
