# Winter Nexus (2020)

- Winter Nexus** is an event that ran from 10 December 2020 to 5 January 2021 (**downtime** to downtime).

The festivities include:
- Volatile Ice Storms which feature Wightstorm Sites
- 13 Days of Nexus Login Event
- Yoiul Festival YC122 Daily Challenges
- Chilling Spree
- Yoiul Festival-themed **Abyssal Proving Grounds**

1. # Agency campaign (Yoiul Festival)

Daily Challenges earn you points. When you accumulate enough points you will be given a reward. This system was last seen in 2019's **Guardian's Gala**, and marks the first time it has returned since the latest version of **The Agency** was released in May 2019. Tasks involve the event sites or throwing snowballs, and are categorized as follows:

- Travel
- Combat
- **Exploration**
- **Mining**
- Filament

1. # Volatile Ice Storm

Volatile Ice Storm is a **Metaliminal Storm** that moves between High, Low and Null Security systems. During the event, there are approximately 14 highsec, 5 lowsec, and 14 nullsec storms. The center system and its neighbors experiences the Strong effect, while systems 2-3 jumps out from the center get a Weak effect. Volatile Ice Storms can overlap. The sites spawned depend on the security status of the center system (e.g. a low security system in a highsec-centered ice storm will have highsec sites).

  1. # Ice Storm Filaments
Ice Storm Filaments transport capsuleers to a System affected by the Volatile Ice Storm.

There are separate Ice Storm Filaments for High, Low and Null security systems, capable of taking up to one, five, or ten ships.

  1. # Wightstorm Combat Anomalies
If a capsuleer has warped to a combat anomaly, it will appear in the overview as a beacon.

Different areas of space have different sites:
- High Security: **Wightstorm Forward Base** is the easiest
- Null Security: **Wightstorm Transit Site** is of intermediate difficulty
- Low Security: **Wightstorm Muster Point** is the hardest

  1. # Volatile Ice Fields
Volatile Ice Fields contain Ice that cannot be processed but can be traded in 7 at a time for a package of Salvage and Sansha items at the Site.

Different areas of space have different sites:
- High Security: Fading Volatile Ice Field
- Low Security: Fresh Volatile Ice Field
- Null Security: Friable Volatile Ice Field

Only the following ships can fit through the Acceleration Gate:
- Expedition Frigates
- Mining Barges
- Exhumers
- Industrial Ships
- Blockade Runners
- Deep Space Transports
- Porpoise

  1. # Wightstorm Data Sites
All Sites are:
- Level IV Data Sites. 86 scanner probe strength is able to scan them down but higher is recommended.
- only have one container that requires a Data Analyzer to unlock

Different areas of space have different sites:
- High Security: Wightstorm Communication Relay is the easiest
- Null Security Wightstorm Cryptographic Array is of intermediate difficulty
- Low Security: Wightstorm Strategic Node is the hardest

Rewards:
- Normal Data Site loot like Tech 1, Tech 2 Salvage and Decryptors
- SKINs: Glacial Drift, Aurora Universalis
- Filaments:
  - Yoiul 2v2v2v2 Destroyers Proving, Yoiul 3v3 Frigates Proving, Calm Abyssal
  - CMT-05N Nullsec Ice Storm, DSHR-01H Highsec Ice Storm, DNNR-01L Lowsec Ice Storm
  - Noise-5 'Needlejack', Signal-5 'Needlejack'
- Overseer's Personal Effects
  - High: 5th - 8th Tier
  - Low: 6th - 12th Tier
  - Null: 5th - 11th Tier
- Fireworks: Yoiul Festival Snowballs, Snowstar Crystalburst, Rimefrost Starburst
- Basic/Potent/Extended 'Brainfreeze' Cerebral Accelerators (Basic/Potent/Extended)
- Agency Drugs: Agency 'Hardshell', Agency 'Pyrolancea',
- Sansha Faction modules and ammo
- Blueprint Copies: Rapture (Lowsec only)

1. # Yoiul Chilling Spree
From 10th December 2020 and 5th January 2020 you will earn 5,000 Skill Points a day for hitting other Capsuleers with Snowballs. During the 2v2v2v2 T1 Destroyer Proving Grounds event, the daily 5k SP reward was replaced with 5 Proving Ground filaments, with the 5k SP reward moved to entering the Proving Grounds.

From 12th December 2020 to 24th December 2020, Capsuleers had the option of snowballing an NPC to receive a lore display item. Each day had a different item as a reward. The items rewarded were:

1. # Abyssal Proving Grounds
From 18th December 2020 to 22nd December 2020 four teams of two Capsuleers will do combat in Tech 1 Destroyers.

From 1st January 2021 to 5th January 2021 two teams of three Capsuleers will do combat in T1 Frigates.

Both events feature a modifier to heat damage.
