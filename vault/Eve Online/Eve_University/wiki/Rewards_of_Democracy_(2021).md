# Rewards of Democracy (2021)

- Rewards of Democracy** is a login campaign that runs from 8 June to 16 June 2021, corresponding with the voting period of the 16th **Council of Stellar Management** (CSM16). The campaign rewards up to 15,000 **Skill Points** for Alpha clones plus 45,000 more Skill Points for Omega clones.
