1. # Summary
The **Council Diplomatic Shuttle** was given out as a ten run blueprint in celebration of the tenth **Council of Stellar Management**, and a single shuttle as part of the **Rewards of Democracy** login event for the fifteenth till seventeenth Council of Stellar Management elections. The Council Diplomatic Shuttle has identical stats to the standard shuttles except for a slightly stronger tank.

1. # Notes
The Council Diplomatic Shuttle is one of very few ships in the game with multiple sensor strengths with 8 radar, magnetometric, gravimetric and ladar sensor strength.
