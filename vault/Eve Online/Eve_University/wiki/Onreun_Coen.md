# Onreun Coen

- Onreun Coen** is a level 3 agent in **Gallente COSMOS**. He requires you to have 3.00 Gallente Federation standing before he will give you any missions.

Onreu offers in total five missions. He does not require any recommendations from other agents and his missions do not lead to another agent.
