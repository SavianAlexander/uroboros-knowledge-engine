Special LADAR and thermal imaging equipment allow Ishukone Watch ships to serve as a distant eye-in-the-sky for planetary security forces. 1. # Summary
The **Scorpion Ishukone Watch** is a special release of the standard Scorpion battleship that is purchasable through the Noble Exchange and extremely expensive if traded in ISK.
