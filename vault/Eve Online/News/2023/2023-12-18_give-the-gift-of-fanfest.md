### Season’s greetings, festive capsuleers!


Early Bird **Pioneer Pass** tickets for EVE Fanfest 2025 are almost sold out! There are a limited number of **Pioneer Passes Early Bird tickets** still available, but they won’t last long. It’s time to lock ‘em down.

The next EVE Fanfest will return to Harpa in the heart of Reykjavik from **1-3 May 2025**, and we’re thrilled to welcome you either in-person, or via CCP TV. 

In the spirit of the holiday season, why not secure tickets to EVE Fanfest for the special capsuleer in your life? Bring joy to your friends in the EVE community by giving something you know they’ll love. A trip to Iceland for Fanfest 2025 makes for a truly unforgettable gift.
