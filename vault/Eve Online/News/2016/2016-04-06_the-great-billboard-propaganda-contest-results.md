On March 10th, we announced [The Great Billboard Propaganda Contest](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/eve-online-news/the-great-billboard-propaganda-contest/) and began accepting entries for player created videos for recruitment and propaganda to play on ingame billboards.

Today, after viewing many entries, some of which were fantastic and some of which were questionable in their nature of content, we've decided on a set of winners who claim the prizes on offer:


### 1st Place


[Visari Qahlt](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Visari%20Qahlt) [Requiem Eternal](//gate.eveonline.com/Alliance/Requiem%20Eternal) alliance promotional video


- 12x PLEX
- Signed EVE: The Second Decade Collector’s Edition
- Signed EVE: The Art Of New Eden Hardcover
- A selection of five SKINs from the New Eden Store (two frigate, two cruiser, one battleship)



### 2nd Place


[Dmitry Nazarov](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Dmitry%20Nazarov)[Demezes Enterprise](//gate.eveonline.com/Corporation/Demezes%20Enterprise) promotional video


- 6x PLEX
- Signed EVE: The Art Of New Eden Hardcover
- A selection of three SKINs from the New Eden Store (one frigate, one cruiser, one battlecruiser)



### 3rd Place


[Nikel Ivanovich](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Nikel%20Ivanovich)[Legion of xxDEATHxx](//gate.eveonline.com/Alliance/Legion%20of%20xXDEATHXx) alliance promotional video


- 3x PLEX
- Signed EVE: Into The Second Decade Hardcover
- A selection of two SKINs from the New Eden Store (one frigate, one cruiser)



### Best In character video


[Noctuam Caelum](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Noctuam%20Caelum)Sisters of EVE promotional Video


- 6x PLEX
- Signed Limited Edition EVE: Source
- A selection of three SKINs from the New Eden Store (one frigate, one cruiser, one battleship)


All winners will be notified via email within the next 48 hours and contacted about their prize SKIN selections. In game items will be delivered as soon as possible, while physical prizes will ship on Thursday, April 14th.
