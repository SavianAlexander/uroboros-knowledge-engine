The second day of the New Eden Open $10,000 cash tournament continues this Sunday, November 18 at 18:00 UTC. This day will see vigourous battles as from now on teams will be eliminated from the tournament. Our studio crew with CCP Soundwave as host will analyse the battles live and give you further insight.

Addtionally we continue with our New Eden Open competitions, this time with "*Beyond the Edge of Glory*". You can win a Razer sponsored prize by creating the most appealing fan art - give it a try! More information about this competition is available [here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=173604).

Watch the New Eden Open tournament [here](//community.eveonline.com/en/tournament/live-stream)!
