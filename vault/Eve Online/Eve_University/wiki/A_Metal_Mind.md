# A Metal Mind

<noinclude> </noinclude>

Video: [[Stabber](https://youtu.be/SfXgCHYsnr4) June 2021]

Single ungated pocket.

Kill enemy ships until a container appears. This container will contain the Sansha Command Signal Receiver.

Keep killing enemy ships until they stop spawning. Then the Business Associate will appear. Drop the signal receiver in his cargo to complete the mission
