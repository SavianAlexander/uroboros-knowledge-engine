# Veiled Asteroid Field

- Main article: **Asteroids and ore***

Veiled Asteroid Fields are ore anomalies that can spawn in Low Security systems as an escalation after the completion of either a hidden mining signature or mining anomaly. Like other ore anomalies, it is neither gated nor deadspace, so making bookmarks and warping around in it are possible. the site is unscannable, so anyone attempting to attack you must first **Combat Scan** your ships.

The anomaly is Linear in shape, and stretches over roughly 60km end to end. The warp-in beacon is 20-30km from all asteroids.

A Cloud Engulfs the Majority of the Site with periodic lighting strikes rippling through it.
