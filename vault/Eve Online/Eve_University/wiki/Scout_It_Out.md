# Scout It Out

- Blitz: Destroy the 4 Warehouses.**

  1. # Single Pocket
On Warp-In you will be told to destroy the Warehouse. Mission detail journal may say "Kill Occupied Amarr Bunker.", you still need to destroy the warehouse to mark the mission as complete.

  - Note:** Even though the mission only requires you to destroy a warehouse, you will still get a standings loss with the Amarr Empire for a "Ship Kill". -0.24 Standing Loss with Amarr Empire for destroying the bunker (on par with other structure destruction missions).
