# Structure

- Structure**** (also called "hull") refers to the third and final set of hit points on all ships.

"**Structure**" may also refer to:
- **Player-owned starbases**, groups of anchorable structures deployable at moons.
- **Upwell structures** such as citadels, engineering complexes and refineries.
- *Outposts*, capturable player-owned structures only deployable in nullsec space
