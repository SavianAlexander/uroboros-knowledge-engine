# Operation Wyrmslayer

1. # Single Pocket
Direct warp into ungated deadspace pocket. Full room aggro on first shot.
There are 2 Ship groups and 2 Tower groups. In an attempt at simplicity, the total numbers of hostiles are combined. (I.e., there are only 12 ships per Ship group and 5 towers per Tower group.)

|}

|}

Destroying the non-elite (not Guardian) frigates of that spawn causes a random Shadow Serpentis Battleship to appear which must be killed to complete the mission.

After the frigate spawn, there will be another tower spawn.

|}

  - Blitz**: Shoot the initial frigs and the Maulus frigs that spawn in order to spawn the Shadow Serpentis BS. Kill this BS to trigger completion.

  1. # Notes
- This mission is easy to solo and the lack of Neut towers makes it active tank friendly.
- The Shadow Serpentis BS can drop Snake implants and several Shadow Serpentis modules.
- Bounties from the rats are approximately 20 mil.
- The Stronghold takes very long to destroy and does not drop good loot.
