1. # Summary
The **Avatar** is the **Amarr** **Titan**. The Avatar is a popular choice for Titan pilots due to it having 8 low slots, allowing it to fit the heaviest armor **tank** in its class, and the meta favoring armor for big **capital ship** fights for many years makes it a default choice. Because of the way that bonuses (especially for damage) work on Titans, having  is a huge boost to the effectiveness of this hull.  and under are wholly insufficient **skills** to sit in a Titan.

The Avatar's popularity has fallen due to its low DPS relative to other empire titans, and it only being able to fire Em/Thermal, leading to many pilots tanking their ships specifically against these types. Most Alliances will favor having a mix of all empire titans, spreading damage across all damage types.
