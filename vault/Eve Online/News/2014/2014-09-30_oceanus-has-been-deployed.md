[Oceanus](//community.eveonline.com/releases/oceanus/) has been successully deployed to Tranquility!

The release brings a host of additions and fixes to the game including the launch of French localization; new cloaking effects; the beginnings of the module Tiericide; an overhauled opt-in notification system; new burner missions; easier importing and exporting of fittings; and many more. Go here for the [full patch notes](//community.eveonline.com/news/patch-notes/patch-notes-for-oceanus).

You can give your feedback with the new release in [this thread](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=5065474). If you should come across any issues, please [report them here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=5065475) if they are not listed among the known issues.

Enjoy the new the release! Fly forth and conquer.
