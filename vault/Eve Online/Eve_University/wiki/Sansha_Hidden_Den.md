# Sansha Hidden Den

The Sansha Hidden Den is a **combat anomaly** found in **Sansha** infested regions. It is a level 2 variant of the **Sansha Den**. Easily done with sniper Battleship. Estimated bounty payout 3 mil isk.

1. # Walkthrough
Single ungated normal space pocket. Warping to beacon for close range would require a very good tank to handle the swarm’s incoming damage. Using a propulsion module or a Micro Jump Drive is recommended.
