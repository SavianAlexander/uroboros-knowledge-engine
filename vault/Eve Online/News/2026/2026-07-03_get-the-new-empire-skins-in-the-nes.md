## Overview
Complete your collection of empire SKINs

---

Collective capsuleers,   

Celebrate the Cradle of War expansion and complete your collection of new empire SKINs, now available individually in the NES. 

Each empire has unique SKIN designs, created by the legendary [T’amber](https://m.twitch.tv/caldariprimeponyclub/home), that turn every engagement into a spectacle, with dynamic effects that respond to repairs, deceleration, and raw distance traveled. 


| Empire | Ship Hulls |
| --- | --- |
| The Minmatar celebrate the Starkmanir masters of ancient strategies with the Stark Strategies line, available for the following hulls | Bifrost, Wolf, Vagabond, Maelstrom, Tornado |
| The Gallente Presidential Agent SKINs are designed for a unique military unit of independently operating special agents, available for the following hulls | Magus, Ishkur, Hyperion, Deimos, Talos, Hecate |
| On the Caldari side, State Regulator SKINs embody security forces with the best pilots from all eight megacorporations, available for the following hulls | Cerberus, Rokh, Stork, Harpy, Jackdaw, Naga |
| The Amarr Mandate of Faith line are SKINs adorned with scriptures, inspired by holy warriors, available for the following hulls | Zealot, Abaddon, Pontifex, Vengeance, Oracle |
