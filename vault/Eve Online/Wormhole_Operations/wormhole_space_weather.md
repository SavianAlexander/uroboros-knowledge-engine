# Wormhole Space Weather Anomalies & Environmental Effects

Environmental tactical modifiers dictating fleet compositions and weapon choices across Anoikis.

---

## 🛰️ Space Weather Phenomona Matrix
### 🌌 **Wolf-Rayet Star**
- **Tactical Buffs**: `+100% Small Weapon Damage, +100% Armor HP, +50% Small Remote Rep`
- **System Debuffs**: `-50% Shield Resistances, +50% Signature Radius`
- **Recommended Doctrine**: **Confessor / Kikimora / T3D Small Brawler Wolfpack**

### 🌌 **Pulsar**
- **Tactical Buffs**: `+100% Shield HP, +50% Capacitor Recharge Rate`
- **System Debuffs**: `-50% Armor Resistances, +100% Signature Radius`
- **Recommended Doctrine**: **Shield Marauders (Golem) / Shield Battleships (Rokh / Cerberus)**

### 🌌 **Magnetar**
- **Tactical Buffs**: `+100% Turret & Missile Damage, +100% Target Painter Effectiveness`
- **System Debuffs**: `-50% Turret Tracking, -50% Missile Explosion Velocity, -50% Drone Tracking`
- **Recommended Doctrine**: **Alpha Strike Artillery / Heavy Missile Kite Fleets**

### 🌌 **Cataclysmic Variable**
- **Tactical Buffs**: `+100% Remote Armor & Remote Shield Repair Amount`
- **System Debuffs**: `-50% Local Repair Effectiveness, -50% Capacitor Recharge Rate`
- **Recommended Doctrine**: **Heavy Spider-Tanking Battleship / Triage Dreadnought Cap Chain**

### 🌌 **Red Giant**
- **Tactical Buffs**: `+100% Smartbomb Damage & Range, +100% Overheat Bonus`
- **System Debuffs**: `+100% Heat Damage Taken`
- **Recommended Doctrine**: **Smartbombing Battleship Pipebombs & Assault Frigates**

### 🌌 **Black Hole**
- **Tactical Buffs**: `+100% Ship Sub-warp Velocity, +50% Missile Velocity`
- **System Debuffs**: `+50% Inertia (Slower Align), -50% Targeting Range`
- **Recommended Doctrine**: **Fast Nano-Cruisers (Vagabond, Orthrus, Cynabal)**
