# Sansha Hidden Rally Point

- Sansha Hidden Rally Point** is a **combat anomaly** found in **Sansha's Nation** infested low and null security regions. There exists **Sansha Rally Point**, **Sansha Forsaken Rally Point** and **Sansha Forlorn Rally Point** variants of this site.

1. # Walkthrough

Ungated pocket with multiple waves of rats.

The site can have quite high incoming damage.  Players running this site are advised to bring ships with strong tanks.  T3 Cruisers perform very well.
