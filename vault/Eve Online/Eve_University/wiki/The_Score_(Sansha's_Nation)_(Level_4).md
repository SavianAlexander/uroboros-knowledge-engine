# The Score (Sansha's Nation) (Level 4)

Video: [Machariel](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C1haMNaD7AU), [Sniper Maelstrom](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g3ezMO8iyDg), [CNR](https://youtu.be/di21ItKLrIo), [Sentry Dominix](https://youtu.be/ztILdEWcbFE), [Gila](https://youtu.be/M9xyEY3sfjk) 2021-06-09

  - Blitz:**
- Clear the 1st room
- Clear the 2nd room
- Kill the battleship in the final room (the Centus Plague Lord if present, otherwise the farther battleship).

  - Note**: This is a time consuming mission as your ship will need to travel a total >100km to reach the respective warp gates. Fitting an afterburner is highly recommended.

  1. # Pocket 1
No aggro at warp-in.

Group 1 can be independently attacked without drawing aggro.
Drones can be safely deployed as per normal.

Distance to warp gate = 58km

|}

  1. # Pocket 2
No aggro at warp-in.

Attacking Group 3 will aggro the entire pocket. Attacking Group 1 or Group 2 can aggro Group 3.

Distance to warp gate = 54km

|}

  1. # Pocket 3
Group 1 may or may not aggro at warp-in.

Group 1 & 2 can be independently attacked without drawing aggro.
Attacking Group 3 will aggro group 1 but not 2.

  - Tip**: Mission is flagged completed once you have eliminated the Centus Plague Lord or the closest battleship in Group 3 when there is no Centus Plague Lord present.

|}

  - Bounty:**
5,425,000.00

  - Loot:**
± 3.7 Mil ISK

  - Salvage:**
± 4.5 Mil ISK

  1. # Mining
- Pocket 1: 32 Asteroids Pyroxeres (1,315,672 Units)
- Pocket 2: 25 Asteroids Veldspar (1,224,709 Units)
- Pocket 3: 11 Asteroids Veldspar (531,329 Units)
