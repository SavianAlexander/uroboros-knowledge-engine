# Minor Blood Annex

- Minor Blood Annex** is a low security unrated site found through **exploration**. Excercise extreme caution due to multiple waves potentially having been spawned before you enter.
1. # Walkthrough

Upon warping to the site you will arrive at an acceleration gate.

  1. # 1st pocket
The webbing drone in each group will spawn the next group. On some occasions multiple groups will already be present when you warp in.
In final wave the destruction of Corpus Militant Commander unlocks the gate.
The gate can also be unlocked with a Blood Crystal Tag. The tag is consumed.
