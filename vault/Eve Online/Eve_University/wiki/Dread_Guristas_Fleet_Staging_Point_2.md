# Dread Guristas Fleet Staging Point 2

- Dread Guristas Fleet Staging Point 2** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **Dread Guristas Fleet Staging Point**.
1. # Walkthrough
The site is considerably harder than the unrated complex due to heavy neuting and webbing, as well as high DPS. Again, a fleet of T2, T3 or Faction **Cruisers** or a small group of **Battleships** is recommended for this site.
  1. # Warp-in
You land on a beacon.

|}
Wave 2 triggers when the penultimate battleship in initial spawn is attacked.

|}
Wave 3 triggers when the last battleship in initial spawn is attacked.

|}
The Guristas Scout Commander does not have significant tank, like a regular Overseer Cruiser. Its wreck may produce T2 salvage materials, though.

|}
Destroying the commander (and possibly, failing that, all ships) will trigger an escalation to **Dread Guristas Fleet Staging Point 3**. To blitz, then, kill battleships until the elite frigates spawn, then kill the Overseer cruiser.

The acceleration gate to the first pocket does not have to be used; the first pocket is optional, and only worth doing for extra bounties/salvage.

|}
  1. # First Pocket
Only go in here if you want bounties/salvage. No escalation is triggered here.

Highly recommend destroying the stasis tower and neuting sentry first; they add a lot of difficulty to this room.

|}
