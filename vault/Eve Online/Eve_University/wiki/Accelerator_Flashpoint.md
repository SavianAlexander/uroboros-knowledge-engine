# Accelerator Flashpoint

- Accelerator Flashpoint** is a combat anomaly that can be found in EDENCOM fortresses.

The site comes prelocked so you always fight against Triglavian Collective.

1. # Warpin
Small number of Triglavian and EDENCOM ships fighting on the acceleration gate.

|}

  1. # 1st pocket
In this room there is a Stellar Observatory, this must be defended. The Triglavian will attempt to destroy it over two waves and a final wave with a Dreadnought. Destroy the Zirnitra Dreadnought to defend the Stellar Observatory.
