# Pioneering Spirit

- Pioneering Spirit** is the second part of the ORE epic arc **Fractured Legacy**.

Video: [[Fractured Legacy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t_ECwuKHIVY) 18 November 2025] [[Fractured Legacy guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4377OZPGjH0) 22 November 2025]

Accepting the mission will grant a Basic Mining Survey Chipset.

The actual mission is within 2-6 jumps away.

4000 units of Veldspar Isotope needs to be mined and transferred to an SOE **Odysseus** (You may need to update your overview if you don't see it).  You need to mine the ore with a volume of 2,100. Use rightclick on the Mining Surveyor (button next to your HUD on the right when in a mining ship) to open the Mining Survey Results (after clicking it once) to distinguish between the rocks you need to mine and which are decoys.
