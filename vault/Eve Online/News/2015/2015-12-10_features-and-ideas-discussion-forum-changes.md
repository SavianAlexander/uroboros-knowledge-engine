Hello Capsuleers

Recently we have made a change to the Features and Ideas Discussion forum on the EVE Online Forums. This forum has been renamed “[Player Features and Ideas Discussion](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=topics&f=270)”, and we have added a new forum called “[Upcoming Feature and Change Feedback Center](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=topics&f=7291)”. Most feedback threads that were previously posted by CCP Developers in the Features and Ideas Discussion forum will now be posted in this new forum section.

For more details or to discuss this change, please see [this forum thread.](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=459837&find=unread)
