# The Spy Stash (Angel Cartel)

- **Blitz:** Jump the first two gates, then kill everything except sentries and batteries, and then loot the Officer's quarters.
- No hostiles at the initial gate

  1. # Pocket 1
- Shooting the Light Missile Batteries aggroes all groups
- Gate not locked @ 30km

|}

|}

|}

|}

  - Structures:**
Structures have no loot

  1. # Pocket 2
- Attacking Group 1 aggroes Group 2 as well
- Killing Group 2 completes the mission
- The Officer's quarters @ 21km, will contain 5 units of Classified Reports required to bring back to agent and 10 units of narcotics (not required)

|}

|}

- Some have reported that if you do the mission in a battleship, you will encounter a Mach class battleship as the final spawn. However, I have run the mission in a battleship, and not gotten the spawn, so it's probably random.
