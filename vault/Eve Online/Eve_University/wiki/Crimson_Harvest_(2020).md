# Crimson Harvest (2020)

- Crimson Harvest** is a recurring event involving the **Blood Raiders** pirate faction and the **Shining Flame** mercenaries. In 2020, the event ran from October 29th through to November 10th, downtime to downtime, and was announced as part of EVE's Halloween Horrors. The event merged the traditional Crimson Harvest event site and rewards with a higher loot drop from PvP and structure losses (90% instead of 50%) and special wreck icons from the **Trick or Treat** event from the year before. The event ran concurrently with a Blood Raiders themed **Abyssal Proving Grounds** event from October 30th through November 3rd, featuring double web range.

1. # Rewards
Blood raider site:
- Cold Iron SKINs
- Blood Raider SKINs
- Crimson Scythes and Blood Dagger Fireworks
- Apparel
  - Men's and Women's 'Outlaw' Jacket (Blood Raiders extra red)
  - Men's and Women's 'Crimson Harvest' Jacket
  - Men's 'Sterling' Dress Shirt (red/black leather)
- Blueprint copies for a Mobile Tractor Unit that tractors corpses not wrecks
- Dark Blood faction modules and implants
- 5th - 15th Tier Overseer's Personal Effects
- Agency boosters

Shining Flame site:
- Sarum SKIN
- Cold Iron SKINs
- Warclone Blanks (can be sold to Blood Raider NPC orders)

  1. # Daily Login Campaign
Starting a day after the event, on 30 October, capsuleers had 11 days to collect 7 days of rewards. The event rewarded up to 60k **Skill Points** for Alpha clones, and 235k Skill Points for Omega clones (plus up to 17.28k SP from the cerebral accelerator).
