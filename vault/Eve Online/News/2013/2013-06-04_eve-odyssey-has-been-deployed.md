EVE Online: Odyssey has been deployed. The 19th free expansion for EVE Online brings [a new age of discovery](//www.eveonline.com/odyssey/new-age-of-discovery/) to New Eden with [new exploration challenges](//www.eveonline.com/odyssey/exploration-site-challenges/) and [improved career missions](//www.eveonline.com/odyssey/exploration-career-mission/) to support and guide pilots through the new changes.

EVE Online: Odyssey also brings a whole host of visual and usability improvements including the [new radial navigation tool](//www.eveonline.com/odyssey/radial-navigation-tool/), an [improved soundscape](//www.eveonline.com/odyssey/broadening-the-soundscape/), and [dynamic transitions](//www.eveonline.com/odyssey/dynamic-travel-transitions/) that replace many of the loading bars of old, as well as four new [Navy Issue Battlecruisers](//www.eveonline.com/odyssey/navy-battlecruisers/), a [rebalance of Navy Issue ships](//www.eveonline.com/odyssey/navy-issue-rebalancing/), improvements to [starbases](//www.eveonline.com/odyssey/starbase-improvements/) and [null sec outposts](//www.eveonline.com/odyssey/null-sec-station-services/), and a [new spacescape](//www.eveonline.com/odyssey/a-new-spacescape/) for New Eden.

Along with these changes, Odyssey introduces [dual character trainining](//www.eveonline.com/odyssey/dual-character-training-dct/), and further additions to the recently released crimewatch system bring [tags for security status](//www.eveonline.com/odyssey/tags-for-security-status/), opening the door for those on the wrong side of CONCORD to pay penance to the long arm of the law for their wrongdoing.

For more information about the latest EVE Online expansion, please visit the [Odyssey feature page](//www.eveonline.com/odyssey/). Full patch notes are available [here.](//community.eveonline.com/news/patch-notes/patch-notes-for)
