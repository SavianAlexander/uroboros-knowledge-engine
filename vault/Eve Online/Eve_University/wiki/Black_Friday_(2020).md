# Black Friday (2020)

- Black Friday** was a compilation of limited time events, including a PLEX sale, a **sales tax** cut, and a booster login campaign. Collectively, the event lasted from 26 Novemeber to 2 December, downtime to downtime.

1. # PLEX Sale
Between 26 and 30 November, select PLEX packages were given a 15% discount.

- 1100 PLEX - 15% off
- 2860 PLEX - 15% off plus free SKIN (**Garmur** Redclaw Sable SKIN)
- 7430 PLEX - 15% off plus 3 free SKINs (Garmur, **Phantasm**, & **Bhaalgorn** Redclaw Sable SKIN)
- 15400 PLEX - 15% off plus 5 free SKINs (Garmur, Phantasm, Bhaalgorn, **Obelisk**, & **Leviathan** Redclaw Sable SKIN)

1. # Black Friday Tax Haven

From 27-30 November, the base sales tax for market transactions was halved from 5.00% to 2.50%. Like last year, 's tax reduction still applied. Meant to be introduced at the beginning of the event, it was rolled back due to the patch breaking markets and contracts, and was introduced the following day.

1. # Bonus Booster Gifts

Capsuleers had five days to log in for four days worth of rewards. Omega Clones had access to rewards worth ~37k **Skill Points**, counting the maximum possible SP gain from the Cerebral Accelerator.

1. # Cyber Monday

In celebration of Cyber Monday, CCP offered a 15% discount on select packs from 30 November to 2 December, downtime to downtime.
