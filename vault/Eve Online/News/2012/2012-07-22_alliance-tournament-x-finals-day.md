The culmination of weeks of qualifying and group stages have led to the Finals day in Alliance Tournament X. Tune in to [EVE TV](//community.eveonline.com/en/tournament/evetv) today from 15:00 UTC to catch the first match of HUN Reloaded vs. FEARLESS along with coverage of all remaining matches. Every team remaining now has a chance of securing a 50 run copy of the fabulous Etana. Who will take home the prize?

Our viewers will also be able to take part in our ‘Predict the Winner’ competitions which will be run before each match in the [Alliance Tournament Discussion](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=topics&f=264) area of the forums and players can win PLEX or goodies from our friends at MMM Publishing and Logitech.
