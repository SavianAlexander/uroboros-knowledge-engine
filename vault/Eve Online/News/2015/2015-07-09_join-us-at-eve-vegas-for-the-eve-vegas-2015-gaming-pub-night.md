The Friday night Pub Crawl has been an indispensable part of the EVE Vegas conference for years, and many memorable stories have been created in and out of bars in different parts of Las Vegas

The event has proven to be a great night for developers and players to kick things off and socialize over a drink or two, and this year we’re going to take things to the next level. What could be better than having a classic and retro gaming night with players and developers from New Eden?

Instead of a Pub Crawl, we’re going to host a Gaming Pub Night at the unique [Insert Coin(s) Bar & Club](//www.insertcoinslv.com/the-venue/), a gaming paradise located on Fremont Street in downtown Las Vegas. There are obvious benefits for having the night in one venue. As CCP Guard put it, “less walking, but you’ll end up crawling!”.

Joke aside, we are very excited to host the first EVE Vegas Pub Night at this amazing venue, which we think is an ideal setting for us to gather, socialize, drink - and play!

The venue will be ours, and ours alone, from 8-11pm with an open bar and free to play arcade machines. After that you have the option of staying at Insert Coin(s), going to the many bars and clubs located around the venue in downtown Las Vegas, or taking the bus with us back to the Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino on the Strip.

[You can get hold of tickets for the event here](//www.eventbrite.com/e/eve-vegas-2015-gaming-pub-night-tickets-17658083804), but please note tickets are limited to 200 only, so make sure to buy your ticket early!
