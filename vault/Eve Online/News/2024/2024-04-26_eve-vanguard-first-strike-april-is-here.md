### Hail Vanguard,


The Deathless has put out the call once more, doling out new contracts for warclone operatives to earn great rewards for their actions on the ground. The deployment is open to all Omega Founders **until 12:00 UTC on 29 April.** 


## RISE TO THE CHALLENGE


Vanguard have a tangible impact on New Eden, and of course, their actions do not go unrewarded. As in previous First Strike events, there are exciting challenges to face and impressive rewards on the line. Warclones will receive the Condor Arkombine Arisen SKIN for collecting 1,000 biomass as a part of a squad of two or more, and the Augoror Navy Issue Arkombine Arisen SKIN for completing 25 contracts. 

In addition, reaching the top 10 of the leaderboard tracking the “Successful extractions” will reward you with a wealth of PLEX. More than five friendly fire kills over the course of the playtest will disqualify the player from all these rewards. 


| **Leaderboard Rank ** | **PLEX Reward** |
| --- | --- |
| **1** | **3,000** |
| **2** | **2,000** |
| **3** | **1,500** |
| **4-5** | **1,000** |
| **6-10** | **500** |
