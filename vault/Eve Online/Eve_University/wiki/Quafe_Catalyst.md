1. # Summary
Quafe Catalyst is a special edition of the **Catalyst** given out as a Buddy Program reward, similar to the **Aliastra Catalyst**, **Inner Zone Shipping Catalyst**, **Intaki Syndicate Catalyst**, and the **InterBus Catalyst**. It is functionally equivalent to the standard Catalyst but with a different skin and additional flavor text.

All models were converted to a regular Catalyst and a corresponding Quafe **SKIN** with the Mosaic release in April 2015. Despite this, the model was given a unique quote with the release of the Revenant expansion in November 2024, over nine years after all copies of the ship were converted out of existence.
