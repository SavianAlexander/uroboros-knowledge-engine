# Downing The Slavers (2 of 2) (Sansha's Nation)

<noinclude> 

</noinclude>

Video: [[Typhoon](https://youtu.be/_QeofDBpTyk) 2019-10-07], [[Raven Navy Issue](https://youtu.be/npin5HkuH-U) 2016-04-16], ][Sentry Dominix](https://youtu.be/FtkJgJ5tNgk) 2018-06-25], [[Gila](https://youtu.be/GuVn9IQYJNg) 2021-04-20]

  - Blitz:** Clear the initial group to unlock the acceleration gate. Kill the two Sansha Slaver Elite Cruisers, done.

A gated deadspace pocket.The incoming DPS is fairly heavy. Make sure your ship has a proper tank or fit a Micro Jump Drive to snipe.

  - Tips:**
- Take out the Missile Towers and Battlecruisers first, to reduce incoming DPS.
- For speed tank, primary targets are the webifier Frigates and Towers. Eliminate them as quickly as possible using your drones.
- Heavy Missile Batteries can aggro multiple ships simultaneously.

  1. # Warp-in
Auto aggro from the group guarding the acceleration gate.
Eliminate all ships to unlock the gate.
Spawn at 15-25km away, they orbit at 15km

|}

  1. # Single pocket
Heavy incoming DPS and two stasis towers. If you want to move take out the webifying towers ASAP. The mission is completed once the elite cruisers are dead.

|}

  1. # Mining
None.  CCP removed all minable asteroids from this mission sometime before 2023.
