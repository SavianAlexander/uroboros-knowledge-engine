# Mission reports

This list contains missions that you can receive from normal (that is, non-Storyline) **Security** division agents. Individual mission pages describe objectives, enemy details, and the best damage types to deal in the mission.

In addition to the missions listed here you can also get **Anomic missions** (sometimes called "burner missions") from Security division agents.
