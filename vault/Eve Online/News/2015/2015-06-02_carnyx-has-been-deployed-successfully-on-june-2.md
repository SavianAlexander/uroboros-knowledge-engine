The latest EVE Online, [Carnyx](//updates.eveonline.com/date/2015-06-02/), was successfully published on June 2.

Carnyx introduces the [first parts of the completely new sovereignty capture gameplay](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/summer-2015-nullsec-and-sov-status-report/) (more info about the [future schedule](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/new-details-on-july-sovereignty-release-schedule/)), fantastic [new graphic shaders](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/the-many-additions-of-v5plusplus/) making EVE Online more beautiful than ever before, fully [revamped and improved icons in space and the overview](//images.contentful.com/kvd74o0q2fjg/4Hgu1BcYAE22wgokiKeYO4/4001f1c3132cc7749d9d25ec8851798e/NewOvervieBracket-IconsImage.jpg), the new [Caldari Tech-3 "Jackdaw" destroyer](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=423432), [rebalanced modules](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=422450&find=unread) and a lot more.

Check out the feature overview on [EVE Online Updates](//updates.eveonline.com/date/2015-06-02/) and read the [Carnyx patch notes](//community.eveonline.com/news/patch-notes/patch-notes-for-carnyx) for the full details.
