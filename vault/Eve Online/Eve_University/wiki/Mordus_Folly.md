1. # Mordu's Folly (Part 2 of 2)

Video: [Torpedo Golem 2022-06-05](https://youtu.be/RNsZI4nokIg)

  - Blitz:** Destroy the Caldari Supply Depot.

  - Tip:** Even though the mission is against Caldari State, you can run it without standing loss if you only destroy the Caldari Supply Depot. But you have to deal with the ECM from some of the NPCs. Therefore either have good sensor strength or use drones to destroy the structure. As of 23 Aug YC124, Depot defenders do attack drones so watch them carefully. Supply Depot is very tough (above 180k HPs) but explodes when structure strength falls below 75% (armor and shield go to 0%).
  1. # Single Pocket
No aggro on warp-in.

|}

|}
If you destroy the depot early, the first battleship reinforcements wave (Bishamon/Shogun) will consist of 4 Bishamon.

  1. # Loot
The following dog tags dropped in total. The current value is about 50 million isk. The exact amounts might vary with the exact spawn amounts. Just like the market value might also fluctuate. Supply Depot sometimes drops 10x Science Graduates that are used for building Research Lab module for Upwell structures. Other times, it drops 5x Civilians (with no use that I know of), or 5x Janitors (used in 3 blueprints).
