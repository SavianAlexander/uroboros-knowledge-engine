# Dead End Intercept

<noinclude> </noinclude>

There is a counter agent in Republic Security Services. Intercept and destroy them.

The encounter will likely be in one of the neighboring systems.  You can warp in at your desired range, and upon arrival there will be a number of friendly craft.  Wait a moment for Lomar Vujik to appear and recover the Singed Datapad from their Cargo Container.

Tips:
- Lomar Vujik deals up to 500 DPS over 80 km and uses a Microwarpdrive to approach at 1000 m/s
- Lomar Vujik's lowest resistance is 49% Explosive, and has similar tank to a standard NPC battleship
