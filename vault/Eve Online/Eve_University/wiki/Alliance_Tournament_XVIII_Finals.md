# Alliance Tournament XVIII Finals

- Alliance Tournament XVIII Finals** ran from 19 until 20 November 2022, featuring two **Abyssal Proving Grounds** and a short login campaign, coinciding with the finals of **Alliance Tournament XVIII**.

1. # Proving grounds
  1. # Bombastic Battleship 1v1
This Proving Ground lasted 24 hours on Saturday 19 November 2022. The allowed ships were: , , , , , , , , , , , , and .

Ruleset:
- Overheat bonuses for turrets & missile launchers and tackle, propulsion, repair, resistance, and energy warfare modules were doubled.
- Pilots received 100 million ISK payout per match where they dealt at least 15,000 damage.
- Sensor dampeners, weapon disruptors, and shield boost amplifier, recharger, power relay, and flux coil modules could not be fitted.
- Auxiliary nano pump, nanobot accelerator, core defense field purger, core defense capacitor safeguard, and core defense operational solidifier rigs could not be fitted.
- Rigs were restricted to tech I.
- Maximum of one local repair module per ship (shield or armor).
- Modules and implants were restricted to meta level 5 and below.

  1. # New Navy Destroyer 1v1
This Proving Ground lasted 24 hours on Sunday 20 November 2022. The allowed ships were: , , , .

Ruleset:
- Overheat bonuses for turrets & missile launchers and tackle, propulsion, repair, resistance, and energy warfare modules were doubled.
- Pilots received 10 million ISK payout per match where they dealt at least 2000 damage.
- Sensor dampeners, weapon disruptors, and shield boost amplifier, recharger, power relay, and flux coil modules could not be fitted.
- Maximum of one local repair module per ship (shield or armor).
- Modules and implants were restricted to meta level 5 and below.

1. # Login Campaign
  - Alliance Tournament XVIII Finals** was a short login campaign that ran alongside the Alliance Tournament XVIII Finals **Abyssal Proving Grounds** event. The campaign rewarded **Boosters**, and 50,000 - 125,000 Skill Points.
