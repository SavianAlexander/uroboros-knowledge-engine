1. # Summary
The Impairor is the Amarr **Corvette**. This ship is given free of charge to Amarr players who click on the "Board my Corvette" button while docked in a station. It has no blueprint and cannot be manufactured or reprocessed.

As with all Corvettes, the Impairor is a cheap and disposable hull, but is outclassed in every regard by almost any other player-piloted ship.
