# Innocents In The Crossfire

Video: [Ishtar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gJI99LcOyJ4) December 2014]

Single gated pocket.

Reinforcements are as follows:
- Reinforcement 1 spawns after all ships in Initial Group are destroyed.
- Reinforcement 2 is possibly triggered by killing frigs from initial group.
- Reinforcement 3 spawns after Reinforcement 2 is destroyed.

Each Prisoner Holding Cell drops either Janitors or Prisoners.

On the last group only the mission objective battleship auto-aggroes, and can be kited away from the rest of the group

The storyline mission provides a very significant corp standings increase
