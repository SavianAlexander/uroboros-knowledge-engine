# Bloated Ruins

- Bloated Ruins** is a very rare **relic site** involving combat as well as **hacking**, found by scanning in Nullsec. It is only found in the Wicked Creek region. Rats from several factions are present here. Up to T2 **battleships** can enter the site. Damage in the site can easily become excessive if care is not taken in managing triggers.

1. # Walkthrough
The warpin contains a few rats. 

The acceleration gate is locked until the defenders are dead.
