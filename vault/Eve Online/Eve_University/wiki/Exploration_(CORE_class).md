1. # Class Overview
Introduction to Exploration teaches the basics of the exploration system in EVE Online, what ships and skills to start out with, how to scan down signatures and how the Hacking Minigame works!

This CORE class covers the following topics:

- Cosmic Anomalies vs. Cosmic Signatures
- Beginner Exploration Ships and Equipment
- Recommended Beginner Skills
- The Probe Scanner View & how to Scan
- The Hacking Mini-Game
- The "Rules" of Exploration
- Exploration tips & tricks
- Q&A
