# Sansha Provincial HQ

- Sansha Provincial HQ** is a null security **Unrated Complex** found by scanning in Sansha infested space.
1. # Walkthrough
This site is practically impossible to solo, and is often called 'the secret 10/10'. HIGHLY RECOMMEND BRINGING FRIENDS FOR THIS SITE. For running this site, bringing a friend or two is recommended to help tank the DPS in the first pocket or break the Provisional HQ in the second room. T3 **Cruisers** and T2 **Battleships** can enter the site. Like with all unrated complexes, you land on an acceleration gate when warping to the complex.
  1. # First Pocket (The Sansha Minefields)

Destroying the Gate Scrambler will activate a smartbomb that deals heavy Explosive damage. The smartbomb damage is rated at around 10,000 points. Recommend high resists to Explosive damage to tank the explosion.

The Provisional HQ has a strong 50+ km web and will target you upon warpin. It fires Citadel Mjolnir torpedoes that deal around 1500-2000 EM damage to an untanked ship. It repairs both shield and armor to the tune of about 1000 DPS. Multiple ships are recommended to take it down.
