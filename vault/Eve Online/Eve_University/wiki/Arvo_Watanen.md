# Arvo Watanen

Arvo Watanen is a level 3 agent in Caldari COSMOS located in Otitoh - Devil's Dig Site.

1. # Artifact Excavations – Food for the Needy (1 of 5)

Provide 500 Frozen Food (250.0 m3) to agent. The Frozen Food can be bought on the market.

1. # Artifact Excavations - Tools in Short Supply (2 of 5)

Transport 50 Research Tools (50.0 m3) from a station in system to the agent.

1. # Artifact Excavations – Bandits Beware (3 of 5)

Provide 1 Makele‘s Tag (0.1 m3) from the **Otitoh VI – Moon 2** deadspace complex in the Otitoh system to agent.

1. # Artifact Excavations – Final Blow (4 of 5)

Provide 25 Bandit Spur (2.5 m3) , which can be found in various COSMOS sites to agent.

1. # Artifact Excavations – Deliver the Special Report (5 of 5)

Transport 1 Data Sheets (1.0 m3) to given destination.
