# Uprising Expansion Celebration

- Uprising Expansion Celebration** is a limited time login campaign, lasting from 8 November to 17 November, **downtime** to downtime.

1. # Log-In campaign

Capsuleers have nine days to log in for seven days worth of rewards. Omega Clones have access to rewards worth 425k **Skill Points**. This event also coincides with CCP giving out 7 days of Omega time to any account that redeems the free item from the **NES** between the 8th and the 22nd of November.
