# Raidon Setala

- Raidon Setala** is a level 2 agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located in Sakkikainen - Settler‘s Waystation.

1. # The Caldari Migrants - Reconnaissance Point 1 (1 of 5)

Mission in the same system. Consists of three pockets. Last pocket has the Radio Telescope in it which can be looted without destroying it, but you have to kill all rats.

Make sure to fit a Microwarpdrive (MWD) before starting this mission. Acceleration gate in the first pocket is 145km away from your warp-in point. Acceleration gate in the second pocket is 195km away from your warp-in point. Your mission objective in the third pocket is 67km away from the warp-in point. The pockets are DED space, meaning that you can not warp within it to get to acceleration gates.

Update 29/06/2023 - the Surveillance Telescope could <em>not</em> be opened without clearing the rats
