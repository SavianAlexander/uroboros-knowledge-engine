### Calculating capsuleers,


We are hugely excited to announce a collaboration between CCP Games and Titan Forge, a miniature sculpting studio and board game publisher, to create a board game adaptation of EVE Online! This board game project welcomes fans and newcomers, bringing New Eden’s signature space exploration, player-driven narratives, and strategic warfare to tabletops. 

The Kickstarter pre-launch page for the EVE Online board game is [**now live for you to sign up**](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/titanforgeminis/eve-online-the-board-game?ref=39v03f) and follow the project’s progress until the campaign kicks off in Q4 2023, following planned demos of the game at EVE Fanfest 2023 on 21-23 September in Reykjavik, Iceland. 

EVE’s player-written stories, intricate economy, and stunning visuals are represented in this board game, offering a blend of classic 4X strategy elements and Euro-game mechanics. Players command a diverse fleet of starships, each meticulously crafted by Titan Forge, and will delve into the intricacies of interstellar politics, resource management, and tactical combat.
