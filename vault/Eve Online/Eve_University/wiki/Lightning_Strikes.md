# Lightning Strikes

and **Gathering Storm**.]]

  - Lightning Strikes** was a limited time event that run from 15-30 June 2020, downtime to downtime. The event had a **combat site** in low security and null security space, where capsuleers could fight Rogue Drones to earn materials and skill books associated with the then-new **EDENCOM** ships and their **Vorton Projectors**, among other things.

1. # Description
From the CCP news article announcing this event:

1. # Combat Site

Upwell Covert Research Facilities were spawned in low security space and null security space controlled by the **Intaki Syndicate**, **Mordu's Legion**, and **Outer Ring Excavations** (ORE). Multiple waves of rogue drones could be fought, with a rat in the final wave as well as a can in the site dropping BPCs, skillbooks, boosters, and crafting materials for the new **EDENCOM** ships and their **Vorton Projectors**.

1. # Notes
Due to lower than intended spawn rates on the first day, the event was extended by one day from the 29th to the 30th of June.
