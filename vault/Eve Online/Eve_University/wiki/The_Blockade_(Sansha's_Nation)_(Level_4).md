# The Blockade (Sansha's Nation) (Level 4)

Video: [[Sentry Dominix](https://youtu.be/ZIOTyDfZM68) July 2018]

  - Blitz:** Kill all the trigger Battleships. You will need to snipe, or have a perma-tank with dual reppers as the spawns deal an high amount of damage. Kill the final Centus Tryrant Battleship. Do not try to blitz in a turret battleship: the maximum TD range of the elite cruisers is 60km, so ensure you are beyond that.

  1. # Single Pocket
- All groups aggro on spawn.
- May (Fleet) warp to preferred distance.
