# Phoenix Nebula

- Phoenix Nebula** is a gas exploration site containing gas used in the production of **Medical boosters**.

This is a gas site found through Exploration.

Defenders:

None

Minable Gas Clouds:

2 Celadon Cytoserocin (500 units each gas cloud)

Celadon Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Standard Exile Booster Booster.

This site is found only in the low security systems of the[Solitude](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Solitude)region of space.
