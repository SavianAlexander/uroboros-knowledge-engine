# Profiteer Nebula

- Profiteer Nebula** is a gas exploration site containing gas used in the production of **Medical boosters**.

This is a low sec gas site found through Exploration.

Defenders:

None

Minable Gas Clouds:

2 Golden Cytoserocin (500 units in each of the gas clouds)

Golden Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Standard Crash Booster Booster.

This site is found only in the low sec systems of the[Lonetrek](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Lonetrek)region of space.
