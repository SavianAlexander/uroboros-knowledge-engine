# Serpentis Hidden Rally Point

- Serpentis Hidden Rally Point** is a **combat anomaly**. It is a variant of **Serpentis Rally Point**.

1. # Walkthough
The site is an open deadspace pocket that can be warped to at any range. It consists of a rock formation inside a cloud, with the Serpentis rats spawning around it.
