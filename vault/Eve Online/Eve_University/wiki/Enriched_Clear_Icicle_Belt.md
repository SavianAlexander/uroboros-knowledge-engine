# Enriched Clear Icicle Belt

An Enriched Clear Icicle Belt is a **Cosmic Anomaly** found in Null Security Space in the **Amarr quadrant**. It can be **mined** with a properly fitted **mining ship**. An Enriched Clear Icicle Belt can only be found in one of the ice systems.

As an exception from the rule, Enriched Clear Icicle Belts also may be found in Low Security Space in the constellation Aridia in the systems Anath, Feshur, Pemsah.
