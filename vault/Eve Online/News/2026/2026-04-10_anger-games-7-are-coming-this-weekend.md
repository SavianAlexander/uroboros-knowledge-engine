Angry capsuleers, 

The Anger Games 7 tournament is kicking off this following weekend on [CCP TV](https://www.twitch.tv/ccp)! 

Over the next three weekends, a full host of talented player commentators, hosts, analysts, refs, and observers will bring you all the action, so make sure you tune in from 11 April at 17:00 UTC. 

There is a cool SKINR pattern that was made specifically for Anger Games 7 winners! And speaking of cool cosmetics, Twitch viewers can use match predictions to gain channel points, and if you get enough you can redeem them for a Galnet SKIN. 

Make sure to keep your eyes out for Twitch Drops as well: 


- Weekend 1: Mysterious Booster Crate I
- Weekend 2: 2,500 EverMarks
- Weekend 3: Kitsune Shattered Paradigm SKIN


To follow along with all the latest results, keep your eyes on the [Anger Games 7 page](https://www.eveonline.com/community-tournaments). The Anger Games are one of EVE's longest-running player-organized tournaments, designed and operated by community members on Thunderdome. It’s a 7v7 team deathmatch style tournament with a similar structure to EVE’s Alliance Tournament, but different enough to create some unique metas and strategies.
