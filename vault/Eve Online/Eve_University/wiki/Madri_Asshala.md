# Madri Asshala

- Mardi Asshala** is a level 4 **Minmatar COSMOS** agent located in Tvink - **Margin Of Error**.

1. # Criminal Minds - Looking Out for Number One (1 of 4)

1. # Criminal Minds - The Investigation (2 of 4)

Note that there is a chance for the destination station to be in Hagilur (lowsec).
1. # Criminal Minds - A Way Out (3 of 4)

Mission objective can be found in the **Contested Minmatar Guerilla Base** complex and is dropped by Angel Thief cruisers in the second and the third pocket.

1. # Criminal Minds - Our Ways Must Part (4 of 4)

Mission objective can be found in the **Contested Minmatar Guerilla Base** complex and is dropped by Minmatar security officer NPCs.
