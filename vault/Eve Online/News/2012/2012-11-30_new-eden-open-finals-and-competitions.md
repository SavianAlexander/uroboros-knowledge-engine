The [New Eden Open](//community.eveonline.com/en/tournament) tournament continues this weekend, December 1st and 2nd, with the final rounds to determine the winners of $10,000 cash prizes!

We invite everyone to watch the exciting matches of the New Eden Open finals on our [own3d stream](//www.own3d.tv/CCPGames), live from 18:00 UTC. With only six teams remaning, the final rounds promise incredible intense action. Addtionally several studio guests will present further information about our upcoming expansion EVE Online: Retribution - don't miss it!

This weekend we also will run several competitions for the EVE Community during the New Eden Open with great prizes sponsored by [Razer](//www.razerzone.com/). Watch [our forums](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=topics&f=264) and our [live stream](//www.own3d.tv/CCPGames/live/307014) for further announcements!
