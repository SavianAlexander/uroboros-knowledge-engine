# Minmatar Brigands

- Minmatar Brigands** (NLX-810) is a static  **Minmatar COSMOS** site that can be found by **probe scanning** in Nakugard. It is co-located with Nakugard VIII - Asteroid Belt 10 so alternatively you can skip the scanning and warp to the belt.

|}

As the site is co-located with an asteroid belt there is a very real chance of **belt rat**s showing up. As this is a COSMOS area they will be of the special COSMOS variant with a chance of dropping Recruitment Center Data Log or Finger Bone.
