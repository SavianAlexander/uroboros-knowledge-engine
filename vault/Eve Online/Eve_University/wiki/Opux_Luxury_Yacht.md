1. # Summary
The Opux Luxury Yacht is one of the rarest ships in the game with only two in the EVE Universe ever extant belonging to independent capsuleers, [of which only one remains.](https://zkillboard.com/kill/63711228/) One was acquired when an event actor (movie star) ejected from his Opux Luxury Yacht to commit suicide at the Quafe Ultra event, after which Iece Quaan boarded it and later sold it to the current (June 2020) owner Voogru (Tyrrax Thorrk **would** have been the owner, but had neglected to train Gallente Cruiser I). The second was given out by CCP to a couple from New Zealand who got engaged on the stage at the 2005 fanfest, both of whom are members of Celestial Horizon.
