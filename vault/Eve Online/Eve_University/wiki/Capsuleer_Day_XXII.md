# Capsuleer Day XXII

- Capsuleer Day XXII**, codenamed **Nexus Reckoning** is a limited time event celebrating 22 years of EVE from 15th April to 15th May 2025, **downtime** to downtime.

1. # Login Campaign
Capsuleers must log in daily to collect all rewards. The campaign rewards **SKIN**s, **Boosters** and 130.000 **Skill Points** for  with 525.000 additional Skill Points, and 26,500 **EverMark**s for . The big reward for Omega clones unlocks on 6 May, just as the conflict reaches its peak.

1. # Filaments
Special event filaments can be created from blueprint copies that can be obtained as rewards from running **hacking** sites . Creation of the filaments requires 50,000
Eleutrium
- Liberated Barbican Filament - Allows up to 15 ships to make a conduit connection to the J110145 system. Referred to as the "Liberated Barbican" wormhole
- Sentinel MZ Filament - Allows up to 15 ships to make a conduit connection to the J055520 system. Referred to as the "Sentinel MZ" wormhole
- Conflux Eyrie Filament Blueprint - Allows up to 15 ships to make a conduit connection to the J200727 system. Referred to as the "Conflux Eyrie" wormhole
- Azdaja Redoubt Filament - Allows up to 15 ships to make a conduit connection to the J174618 system. Referred to as the "Azdaja Redoubt" wormhole

The filaments will expire on 15 May 2025.

1. # Mining sites
  1. # Small Tyranite Deposit
  - Small Tyranite Deposit** appears as a beacon on the overview. It is found in high-sec. The site contains 9 Tyranite asteroids about 7-19km from the warp-in point. The asteroids contain approximately 84k/140k units of ore each. The site can be visited by NPC mining fleets. A fleet consisting of 6 **Venture**s supported by a **Mastodon** has been seen.

The site has it's own type of **belt rat**s. They are aggressive and will attack. However, regular belt rats have also been reported.

  1. # Tyranite Deposit
  - Tyranite Deposit** appears as a beacon on the overview. It is reported in high-sec. The site contains 18-25 Tyranite asteroids about 23-65km from the warp-in point. The site can be visited by NPC mining fleets. A fleet consisting of 8 **Retriever**s has been seen.
