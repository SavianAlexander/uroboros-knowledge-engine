We are happy to announce the name of the next winner of the [EVE Vegas Player Prize Draw!](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/eve-online-news/eve-vegas-competition-2/)

[Gen MarcusAurelius](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Gen%20MarcusAurelius) of [Risk Breakers [EXOP]](//gate.eveonline.com/Corporation/Risk%20Breakers) has won a [Resort Vista Room Upgrade](//www.planethollywoodresort.com/content/scaffold_pages/rooms/planet_hollywood/phv/en/resort_vista_room/_jcr_content/cards/card.roomhighlights.-_-etc-_-designs-_-cet-_-properties-_-planet_hollywood.phv.1.html)!

Prize winners in this draw will be contacted by CCP in the days after their announcement as winners with further details on how to claim their prizes. The next prize to be drawn will be for four Complimentary Roundtrip Limousine transfers. This draw will take place and the winner will be announced on Wednesday, September 10th, 2014.

We still have plenty of prizes on offer:
