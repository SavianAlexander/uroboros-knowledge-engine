Last week the signups for Alliance Tournament were closed, and the 64 participants for round one were drawn from a manly pink cowboy hat by CCP Claw and CCP Soundwave. You can find the results [here](//www.eveonline.com/events/alliances/tournament/t7/schedule1.asp). In total there were 91 valid signups, of which 11 were automatically granted a place due to their standing in Alliance Tournament VI. Among the remaining 80 signups, 53 were drawn from a hat to see who would get the remaining slots. Additionally, ten alliances have been drawn as reserves, should anyone pull out.

Thank you to everyone who applied, and to those who did not make it in – better luck next time!
