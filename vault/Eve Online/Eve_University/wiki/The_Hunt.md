# The Hunt

- The Hunt** is a recurring limited time event involving the **Guristas** pirate faction.

For info on particular versions of the event:
- **The Hunt (2016)**
- **The Hunt (2018)**
- **The Hunt (2020)** - combat site limited to T1/T2 frigates and destroyers, accessed through keys gotten from killing colored pods throughout New Eden.
- **The Hunt (2021)**
- **The Hunt (2022)**

1. # PvP implant drops
During the Hunt event, capsuleer capsules will have a 50% chance of dropping as loot each of the implants that normally would have been destroyed by the capsule destruction. Implants that are "non-destructible" such as **Genolution 'Auroral' AU-79** or **ISD** and **CCP** exclusive implants will not drop. Among the rare implants that can be obtained through this event are implants that are unpublished in the in-game information windows such as certain discontinued and test implants.
