# Serpentis Gas Processing Site

The **Serpentis Gas Processing Site** is a **hacking** site that can be scanned using Core Probe in **Serpentis** owned territory in Low Sec space. This site appears to Probes as a Gas Site rather than the typical hacking site. It is also defended by hostile NPC ships.
