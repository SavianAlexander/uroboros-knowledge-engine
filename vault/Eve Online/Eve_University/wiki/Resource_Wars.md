# Resource Wars

- Resource wars** is a mining-combat hybrid **PvE** activity in which players mine ore for NPC corporations while fending off NPC pirates.

1. # Rewards
Completing the sites will reward the participants with ISK, corporation standings and LP.

The LP store for mining corporations is available in every station of the same faction. To access the mining LP store open LP window and select the tab for that factions mining corporation.

  1. # Site Rewards
Completing the sites will reward the participants with ISK, corporation standings and LP. Corporation standing gain is equal among the sites at 0.2. 

ISK and LP increase with site difficulty and completion time.
