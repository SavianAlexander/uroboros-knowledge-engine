## **Patch Notes for 2026-08-05.1**



### **Features and Changes:**



### **Rampant Drone Fabricator:**



- Increased the staggering time when spawning an NPC wave.
- Added a new NPC to the site, the Infested Drone Naglfar, with the following behavior: - Only spawns during large Severe and Critical threat waves.
  - Is equipped with a Capital Energy Neutralizer.
  - Has far greater EHP and DPS than existing Fabricator NPCs.
  - Is vertical



> Note that this NPC is equivalent to several battleship-tier NPCs. While the overall DPS of a wave will largely remain the same, the Infested Naglfar spawns will be more focused on specific targets rather than being spread out.  This, combined with its powerful neutralizer, will mean logi will need to stay on their toes.The average number of NPCs spawned during later Severe and Critical threat waves should see around a 10-15% decrease in NPC numbers to compensate for the Infested Naglfar. We've also increased the staggering time when a new wave spawns, and these two changes will help balance the load in later waves where the Rampant Drone Fabricator would sometimes fail to progress.We've been really impressed with the various strategies that groups have taken to complete the Rogue Drone Fabricator site, and we're excited to see them further develop. Let the Rogue Drone annihilation continue!

## **Patch Notes for 2026-07-23.1**



### **Features & Changes:**



### **Rampant Drone Fabricator:**



- Added the Rampant Drone Fabricator site back to C1-C6 wormhole systems.
- Increased the maximum wave to 100 (from 30).
- The site will now escalate threat level based on a point system, dubbed "Rampancy", determined by the ships in proximity to the Fabricator. - The first escalation, equivalent to the previous Battleship Escalation, occurs at 12 Rampancy.
  - The second escalation, acting as a new middle escalation tier, occurs at 36 Rampancy.
  - The final escalation, equivalent to the previous Capital Escalation, occurs at 72 Rampancy.
  - The current Rampancy value will appear in the Info Panel objectives between waves.
  - The threat level of the upcoming wave will now appear in the Info Panel objectives between waves.
  - Each ship will add an amount of Rampancy as shown in the table below:



| Ship | Rampancy |
| --- | --- |
| Capital (other) | 72 |
| Force Auxiliary | 36 |
| Marauder | 12 |
| Black Ops | 8 |
| Battleship (other) | 6 |
| Command Ship | 3 |
| Battlecruiser (other) | 2 |
| Strategic Cruiser | 4 |
| Heavy Assault Cruiser | 2 |
| Logistics Cruiser | 2 |
| Cruiser (other) | 1 |



> Rampancy allows players much more control over the level of risk and reward they want to take on. Given these sites are now exclusive to wormholes, Rampancy also plays into deciding how to make the best use of available mass in wormhole connections. The thresholds have been set so most setups prior to this change will continue to face the same level of difficulty.



- The total quantity of Fabricator Data accrued within the Fabricator will now appear in the Info Panel objectives.
- Reduced the ECM strength of NPCs.
- Destroyer-sized EWAR NPCs will now use Sensor Dampeners rather than ECM. - Note: These NPCs will appear with the "Bewildering" prefix.
- Battlecruiser-sized EWAR NPCs will now use Target Painters rather than Stasis Webifiers. - Note: These NPCs will appear with the "Scrutinizer" prefix.
- Added four new Nanoswarms: - Light Squadron Nanoswarm (Light Fighter Damage Bonus).
  - Rapid Squadron Nanoswarm (Fighter Velocity Bonus).
  - Focuser Nanoswarm (Turret Optimal Range Bonus).
  - Prolonging Nanoswarm (Missile Flight Time Bonus).
- Nanoswarms will now be vulnerable during the following waves: - 5, 10, 15, 25, 35, 45, 65, and 85.
- Drone rewards can now begin dropping from wave 1.
- Reduced the per-wave drop chance of drones.
- The average drop quantity of drones will now scale as the wave count increases. - Note: In effect, while these drones will drop more rarely, the quantity will scale much higher. The resulting average number of these drones dropped by wave 30 has slightly increased.
- All sizes of TP-I, SD-I, and TD-I Hybrid drones have been added as possible drops. - Note: These drones have a significantly lower drop rate than the existing drone types. They have been added as an addition and will not dilute the existing drone rewards.
- Increased the damage applied in C5/C6 sites.
- Smartbombs are no longer blocked within proximity of the Fabricator.
- Smartbombs, Bombs, and Vorton Projectors will now deal 95% less damage to the site's NPCs, Nanoswarms, and Fabricator.



> Bombs were being used to clear waves almost instantly, regardless of the wave number. To avoid having to block the modules themselves, the damage of AoE effects has been reduced significantly when applied to NPCs and objects within the site. This allows players to use these weapons in any PvP that occurs within the site while still requiring active gameplay to progress it.



- Decreased the additional damage applied to Carrier ships to 40% (from 50%). - Note: Command Carriers will receive the full 100% damage increase like other Capital ships.
- Decreased the site's respawn timer.
- The Fabricator can now be repaired.
- Improved the visuals of the site.
- Updated the icon for Rampancy Data Dump commodities.
- Updated various messages for better clarity and to reflect the site's evergreen status.
- Fabricator NPCs are now separated into overview groups based on their hull sizes. - Note: These groups are part of the default Overview, but will need to be enabled for existing custom Overview settings.

## **Patch Notes for 2026-07-07.1**



### **Features & Changes:**



### **Operation Avalon is here!**



### **Avalon Investigations:**


Avalon Investigations will begin appearing throughout Empire space, from high to null-security systems. Additional investigations can be activated by Vanguard warclones as they complete Expeditions. Ongoing investigations appear both in the **Map **and the **Opportunities window**, under the Interstellar Events category.


### **Roaming Haulers and Hidden POIs:**


Within these investigated systems roaming haulers, AEGIS Squalls, will travel between new sites and hidden POIs. Haulers have a variety of valuable loot in their cargoholds. Hidden Points of Interests include:


- Hacking sites
- Salvaging sites
- Combat sites


Hidden POIs can only be located by scanning down haulers as they visit these locations, and contain much more valuable rewards.


### **Standard Sites:**


In addition to the hidden POIs, standard versions of these sites can be found both as anomalies and cosmic signatures. While their rewards are not as high as their hidden variants, they are much more common and make for a reliable way to increase influence within the system.

Influence will be increased with every site completed. Once 100% influence is achieved, a special site containing additional rewards will appear. Completing this site will bring the investigation within the system to an end. Keep an eye out for the AEGIS Director and their exclusive **Aralez combat drones**!


### **Avalon Traders:**


Vanguard Resonant Cyphers can be found in all sites and the roaming haulers. These tokens are highly sought after by Avalon traders, who will be more than happy to exchange them for unique rewards! These traders can be found throughout New Eden in the following systems:


- Amarr
- Sifilar
- New Caldari
- Kinakka
- Poitot*
- Dodixie
- Ostingele
- Rens
- Hadozeko
- SL-YBS


**Did you know Poitot is the only named system in Syndicate?*

The items that can be acquired from traders include:


- Volatile Boosters - extremely short-duration boosters with powerful effects.
- Poteque 'Prospector' Salvaging SV-906 - an Implant that boosts Salvage retrieval by 6%.
- New Vanguard-themed SKINR components.
- Breach Control - A new faction damage control that can be activated in a pinch to reduce incoming Breacher pod damage for 120s.
- A Unique Vanguard-themed character background.



### **Seasonal Challenges:**


Capsuleers can complete various challenges to progress the Season reward track. Rewards for progression include Skill Points, Arkombine Arisen SKINs, boosters, and more. The final reward provides a Capsuleer with a Ranking Vanguard Resonant Cypher. This special token can be exchanged at the previously mentioned traders for additional rewards:


- Salvation Arkombine Arisen SKIN.
- Aeon Arkombine Arisen SKIN.
- Lorharyth’s Modified Inertial Stabilizer - a brand new Officer Inertial Stabilizer!



### **Daily Login Rewards:**


Each day, capsuleers can log in to receive additional rewards. These include more Skill Points, Arkombine Arisen SKINs, boosters, and more!


### **Graphics:**


The Exordium region has seen a few minor updates:


- The environment nebulae color variation between the 4 empire and CONCORD constellations have had a technical update that fixes small glitches in the application of the nebulae color variation.
- The 8 border gates that mark the entry and exits between the 4 empire constellations and the central CONCORD constellations have all received an environment upgrade with additional infrastructure.
- The Paragon Fulfilment Center in Manifest has received a graphical upgrade with additional infrastructure.
- The 4 CONCORD stations in Couth, Manifest, Forte and Rationate have each received a CONCORD SKIN to properly communicate their ownership.

## **Patch Notes for 2026-06-10.1**



### **Features & Changes:**



### **Balancing:**



- Increased the orbit velocity of web drones so they’re less likely to fall behind targets they are webbing when they shut down their MWDs.



### **Visions of Greatness:**


A brand new Epic Arc, Visions of Greatness, is available to experience in Exordium!


- Experience simulated battles from some of the most pivotal moments in the four empires’ history. - Get a taste of each of these factions’ unique identity.
  - Be warned; each empire may have taken some “creative liberties” when constructing these simulations!
- Each simulation features bonus challenges where capsuleers can prove their mettle. - Completing these challenges will increase a simulation’s rewards and how those within the simulation react to you!
- Suitable for both new and returning players.
- Earn ships, modules, and faction standings that will help kickstart a journey in New Eden.
- To get started, head to the AIR Laboratories station in Manifest and speak to Cassandra Ahfrim.



### **Defect Fixes:**



### **Gameplay:**



- Added Open-5 as a highsec insurgency corruption/suppression spawn to replace the Mining Ambush which was prevented from spawning in highsec.
- Fixed a rare issue in the Ice Heist-30 dungeons which could cause only one of the two factions to be able to link to the tanker objects.
- The Algos Navy Issue now applies both HP bonuses per level to web drones.
- Fixed an issue where the achievement Epic Initiate was incorrectly granting progress for a single Epic Arc mission instead of completing an entire Epic Arc.
- Fixed an issue where the Subcapital Operations Expert System was not auto-redeemed from the Redeem Queue.
- Fixed an issue where Onnamon was missing from the Caldari Bulwark hacking objective.
- Fixed an issue where ISK rewards were not being taxed when an objective was completed.



### **Graphics:**



- Removed most Gallente election propaganda advertisements around stations, hangars, and gates.
- Fixed a z-fighting issue on the Zephyr Frigate mesh where it would flicker on the sails, often visible in hangars.
- Fixed a floating antenna on the AIR Hub station.
- Added some improvements and polish to visual effects within the AIR Hub hangar such as hangar traffic, lighting, and cinematic reveal fixes.
- Fixed an issue with the Odysseus where some lights were on too early in cinematic reveal.
- Added new values to colors sets missing entries for various SKINs.
- Updated killmarks on Sabre Republic Police SKIN to be in the same location as the default Sabre.
- Fixed floating docking lights visible on the Moros.



### **Localization:**



- Corrected the description of Crystalline Kangite.



### **User Interface:**



- Fixed an issue where sorting by global progress in the Theaters of War window could get stuck on loading.
- Restored the missing info icons in the Factional Warfare tab of the Agency.
- Fixed an issue where campaign panels in the Theaters of War window could show content from other campaigns.
- Fixed an issue where campaign objectives in the Theaters of War window would not refresh until the Opportunities window was opened.
- Tweaked and polished various areas of Campaigns UI.
- Archon blueprints will now show their Invention information correctly in Information windows.
- Progress bars will no longer overlap with other information in the Epic Arc section of the Agency.

## **Patch Notes for 2026-06-09.1**


The Cradle of War expansion is out!

Full overview of the contents of the expansion can be found here:  [Cradle of War Expansion Notes](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/cradle-of-war-expansion-notes)!


### **Defect Fixes:**



### **Gameplay:**



- Fixed an issue which prevented the Victorieux Luxury Yacht from being able to be manufactured.
- Fixed an issue causing all Special Edition Yachts to be 115,000m3 instead of 10,000m3 as previously.
- Special Edition Yachts can now be combat probed and will now correctly appear on the directional scanner.
- It’s now possible to probe down Mobile Observatory structures with combat probes and they can now be filtered in the deployables section.
- Improved Covert Research Facilities (AKA: Ghost Sites) are now classified as data sites rather than combat sites in the drone regions like all other regions.
- FW Wrecks in FW complexes can now be destroyed.
- Expired Volatile Boosters from the Drifter Crisis event have been removed from the market.
- Guristas Caiman Dreadnoughts which appeared from shipyard content can now be targeted by Titan Doomsday weapons.
- Updated the masteries of the Porpoise, Orca and Rorqual to now include compression modules and the industrial cores for the Porpoise and Orca.
- Fixed an issue preventing the Kronos Inner Zone Shipping SKIN from being able to be activated.
- Fixed the Sleeper Small Intermediate Wreck having it’s access difficulty in scientific notation rather than a human readable value.
- Mutated 'Excavator' Ice Harvesting Drone has been removed from the market as none could be sold as they cannot be repackaged.
- Updated the fitting advisory on the Vargur to no longer warn you about fitting active armor tanking modules to it, since it now has an armor repairing bonus as of Viridian.
- Strip Miner Is and Ice Harvester I modules now only require 5m3 like all the variations.



### **Graphics:**



- Added a rendered icon for the Tetrimon Paladin NPC.



### **Localization:**



- Several Typos and Grammar mistakes have been fixed.
- Clarified that the Porpoise cannot be moved by regular sized Micro Jump Field Generator modules in the description since it is an Industrial Command Ship, rather than just mentioning the Orca.
- Clarified that the Standup Reprocessing Facility service module does not allow compression of Gas and Moon Ores.
- Fixed the Sin traits linking to the drone skills instead of a warp scrambler for one of the warp scrambling bonuses.
- Updated the text of the Gravid and Unstable mining module mutaplasmids to be correct.



### **User Interface:**



- The volume value for courier contracts shown in the header of the contract details window will now always show up to 2 decimal places.
