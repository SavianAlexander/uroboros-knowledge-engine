# Wormhole Farmholes (Class)

<onlyinclude></onlyinclude>
1. # Class Overview
Wormhole Farmholes gives an introduction to the setup and maintenance of ISK generating "farmholes" in J-Space.

This class covers the following topics:

- What is a farm hole?
- What are the player and pilot skill requirements?
- What are typical investment assets required?
- Setup and operation:
- External resources
- Picking a system
- Anchoring and timers
- Risk management and mitigation
- Q&A
