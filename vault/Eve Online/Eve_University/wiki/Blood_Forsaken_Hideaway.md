# Blood Forsaken Hideaway

- Blood Forsaken Hideaway** is an unrated **combat site** belonging to the **Blood Raiders** pirate faction. It can be found via **exploration** as a **cosmic anomaly** within systems owned by the **Amarr Empire** and **Ammatar Mandate**. As an ungated complex, the limitations as to which ships can complete this site are tied to the security rating of the system where the anomaly spawns (T2 **Battleship**s for high-security systems, and up to **Titans** in low-security and null-security systems). Completing this complex may award the player with valuable Dark Blood **faction modules** and ammunition.

This combat site consists of a single ungated pocket of space containing a variety of **Frigate**- and **Destroyer**-sized enemy ships. Three variations of this combat anomaly exist: **Blood Hideaway**, **Blood Hidden Hideaway** and **Blood Forlorn Hideaway**.
