**Patch notes for Apocrypha 1.5.1, released Thursday, 10 September 2009.**

**FIXES**

**Need for Speed**


- During Apocrypha 1.5 we identified and fixed several potential causes for client-server simulation desync. Additional work in this area for Apocrypha 1.5.1 revealed the root cause of the remaining desync issues. We believe that we have now fully eliminated desync issues as related to the physics simulation. More on this in an upcoming Dev Blog.


**Weapons & Ammunition**


- It is now possible to shoot objects that are 0 meters away with turrets, and hit them (this is still subject to tracking.)


**Modules**


- The Medium Targeting System Subcontroller II Blueprint can now be invented.


**Market & Contracts**


- The market deviation filters have now been fixed ensuring that the average price of an item is now calculated correctly at all times. When each scroll is loaded, the average price of the outstanding orders is calculated and that is used to filter out orders that are outside the range you specify. Previously the same average price was used for the sell orders and buy orders but this has now been changed so now there is one average price for the sell orders (average price of the outstanding sell orders in the region) and another one for the buy orders.


**Agents & Missions**


- Epic arc agent conversation windows would sometimes be blank when opened and in some cases those same players experienced problems when trying to dock/undock. This has now been fixed.


**NPC's**


- The description tab of all NPC corporations will display the correct corporation information.


**Miscellaneous**


- A server side issue has now been fixed that could cause node deaths if system administrators pressed certain buttons.


**Exploits**


- Several exploit issues have been fixed, making EVE a better world to live in for us all.


**POST-PATCH CHANGES**

**Patch notes for Apocrypha 1.5.1-1 Server side change, released Tuesday, 29 September 2009.**

**FIXES**

**Miscellaneous**


- Several enhancements have been made to our billing website and infrastructure.
- Login features for the billing service has been improved.
- Updates to character usage in email notifications and better features to handle PLEX within campaigns have both been improved.


**Patch notes for Apocrypha 1.5.1-2 Server side change, released Tuesday, 6 October 2009.**

**CHANGES**

**Need for Speed**


- The Directional Scanner can now only be activated once every 1.3 seconds. It had previously been reduced to every 2 seconds due to performance concerns and exploits but has been increased now to match the visual timer.
- Several database updates have been added to increase performance.


**FIXES**

**Epic Mission Arcs**


- The acceleration gate in the Federation Detention Facility required for the epic arc mission “The Breakout” no longer consumes the key on activation.
- The Federation Detention Facility required for the epic arc mission “The Breakout” is now distributed in each system in the Aokinen constellation.
- The reward for the epic mission “Revelation” has been adjusted and will now give out 25 million ISK upon completion.
- The epic mission “Their Loss, Our Profit” now requires the players to deliver the S.I. sheet before it can be completed.
- Shooting Amarr NPCs in the epic mission “Longing Leman” will no longer cause a standings hit.
- The epic mission “Right Tool For The Job” now requires the players to deliver the Fednav tag before it can be completed.


**Miscellaneous**


- IGAN Radio added as a persistent chat channel.
- Several GM Tools have been improved allowing the Customer Support team to assist you.


**Exploits**


- Several exploit issues have been fixed, making EVE a better world to live in for us all.
