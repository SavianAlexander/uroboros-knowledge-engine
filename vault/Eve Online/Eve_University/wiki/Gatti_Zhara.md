# Gatti Zhara

Gatti Zhara is a COSMOS signature in Otomainen. You can also find it by warping to Otomainen XIII - asteroid belt 1. You find an asteroid belt there (maybe with **belt rat**s) and a structure named Gatti's residence which drops 10 Reports.
