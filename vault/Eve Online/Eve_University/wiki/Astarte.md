1. # Summary
The **Astarte** is a Gallente attack command ship bonused for blasters. With its double DPS bonus and falloff bonus to aid projection of that damage, the Astarte is rivaled only by the Minmatar **Sleipnir** for damage dealing in its class. It is however very focused on blaster DPS, so the more flexible **Eos** is often preferred for fleet boosting roles.
