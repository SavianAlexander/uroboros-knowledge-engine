**Issue Fixes**

The following issues will be resolved in this patch:


- The old machonet cache did not expire after patching the client. This has resulted in a number of errors.
- Changing columns in the overview settings can cause the client to crash to desktop.
- In-Game Browser bookmarks cannot be created.
