# Empire-themed packs for PLEX are back!

- **Date**: 2024-09-06T11:00:00.000Z
- **Category**: news
- **Author**: EVE Online Team
- **Source**: https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/empire-themed-packs-for-plex-are-back
- **Tags**: #offers, #news

## Overview
Luminaire Exalted, Liberation Day, Foundation Day and Union Day packs now in NES

---

### **Loyal capsuleers,**


Last chance to get the four Empire-themed packs in the New Eden Store.

Each pack contains a variety of items including Skill Points, Cerebral Accelerators, or apparel - and there’s a generous selection of themed SKINs inside each one too!

Six sumptuous Luminaire Rising SKINs await Gallente loyalists, while eight Chainbreaker Khumaak SKINs are on offer for the Minmatar. Amarr pilots can pay tribute to their own faction with eight fiery Lavacore Imperial SKINs, and for Caldari pilots eight Union Day Executive ones.

These packs are only available for a limited time, so make sure you grab them while you can.

Everything you’ll find in each of the four packs:


## Luminaire Exalted Pack


Gallente pilots can unwrap six Luminaire Rising SKINs, and an assortment of helpful items in the Luminaire Exalted Pack:


- 250,000 Skill Points
- 1 x Standard ‘Boost’ Cerebral Accelerator
- Catalyst Navy Issue Luminaire Rising SKIN
- Maulus Navy Issue Luminaire Rising SKIN
- Nemesis Luminaire Rising SKIN
- Brutix Navy Issue Luminaire Rising SKIN
- Megathron Navy Issue Luminaire Rising SKIN
- Hubris Luminaire Rising SKIN


Each SKIN in this collection has been designed to pay homage to the birth of the Gallente Federation. Just as the glowing orange dawn of Luminaire bathes the mountains of Gallente Prime, so too can its warming light be found in each nanocoating.


## Liberation Day Pack


The Liberation Day Pack contains eight exquisitely-engineered SKINs, as well as an assortment of useful career boosts:


- Heimatar Rise Accelerator
- 256,000 Skill Points
- Agency ‘Pyrolancea’ DB3 Dose I
- Agency ‘Hardshell’ TB7 Dose III
- Agency ‘Overclocker’ SB9 Dose IV
- Sleipnir Chainbreaker Khumaak SKIN
- Valravn Chainbreaker Khumaak SKIN
- Svipul Chainbreaker Khumaak SKIN
- Wolf Chainbreaker Khumaak SKIN
- Jaguar Chainbreaker Khumaak SKIN
- Stabber Fleet Issue Chainbreaker Khumaak SKIN
- Cyclone Chainbreaker Khumaak SKIN
- Cyclone Fleet Issue Chainbreaker Khumaak SKIN


The Khumaak range of SKINs pays tribute to the Great Rebellion of the proud Minmatar people, which saw the faction liberate itself from the clutches of Amarr enslavement. What better way to assert your own independence in EVE?


## Foundation Day Pack


Proud Amarr pilots can demonstrate their loyalty with eight Lavacore Imperial SKINs, as well as a collection of other very useful items:

- Retribution Lavacore Imperial SKIN

- Women's Amarr Courtly Robe


The Lavacore Imperial SKIN collection recognises the Amarr Empire’s efforts to exploit the magma-mired planets of New Eden. Now you can make your own meteoric impact on EVE with this unmissable collection of SKINs.

Display your loyalty with impressive-looking Executive SKINs, each one of these SKINs is certain to turn heads wherever your adventures take you in New Eden.:


- 256,000 Skill Points

- Kestrel Union Day Executive SKIN
- Tengu Union Day Executive SKIN
- Drake Navy Issue Union Day Executive SKIN

- Scorpion Navy Issue Union Day Executive SKIN

- Women's Caldari Navy Crew Jacket


The SKINs included in the Union Day pack are with bright orange nanocoating, paying homage to the stark yet harmonious contrast between the hard and fast work ethic of the Caldari workers and the virtues of honestly earned leisure time.

All of these packs are available now in the New Eden Store. The offer won’t be available for long though, so get them while you can.
