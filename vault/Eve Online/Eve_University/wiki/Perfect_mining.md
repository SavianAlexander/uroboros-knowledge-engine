# Perfect mining

The perfect **miner** is something that many of us wish to attain. Since we spend all day staring at our mining lasers, we have nothing better to do than to research and analyze how we can get a better yield from our mining lasers, or how to mine faster. It really boils down to: "How can I make as much money in the smallest amount of time?"

The perfect ore miner consists of three things. The perfect **Hulk** pilot, the perfect booster (**Orca** or **Rorqual**) pilot, and the perfect refiner. The booster pilot will be able to boost the Hulk pilot to maximum yield, range, and minimum cycle time.

  - Type B II mining crystals

Firstly, the 'Carpo' Mining Laser Upgrade is a storyline Mining Laser Upgrade that offers a 1% yield bonus over the Tech II variant. Given that Tech II Mining Laser Upgrades often go for under 1M ISK, it would take a significant amount of mining for that difference to be paid off. It is the "perfect" option in terms of yield, but likely not a practical option.

With that aside, beyond a basic setup with Tech II mining laser upgrades and Tech I strip miners, there are two branching upgrade paths that can be done. Which one is the "perfect" option depends on whether one wishes to maximize yield on a per second basis, or to maximize the overall ore amount from mining an ore anomaly or a **moon mining** pull while doing so as fast as possible.

If max yield is desired, using Modulated Deep Core Strip Miner IIs (for Mercoxit) or Modulated Strip Miner IIs (for other ores) with Type B II mining crystals will provide the most ore yield. A max-skilled Hulk with two Tech II Modulated Strip Miners and three Mining Laser Upgrade IIs alone can pull over 50 per second. Under a perfect Rorqual booster, that increases to 130 per second. This, however, comes at great cost to wasted ore, with the Hulk averaging 32 and 82 per second of wasted ore under unboosted and boosted scenarios with perfect skills, respectively.

Given that the most valuable ores in a field are of limited quantity compared to other items, a "perfect" miner may instead wish to mine as fast as possible with zero waste, to ensure that they can mine as much of the most valuable minerals as they can. In that case, using the faction ORE Strip Miner offers a 33% boost in mining rate compared to Strip Miner Is (800 base yield vs. 600), while also having zero waste. Going back to the example of a perfectly skilled Hulk with three Tech II Mining Laser Upgrades, that Hulk with ORE Strip Miners would be able to pull ~37 per second, and ~97 per second under a perfect Rorqual booster. That is notably less than the Tech II Modulated Strips example above. But when there is a situation like a R64 moon pop, where the ore with R64 materials is worth several times more than the other ores in the moon pop, maximizing how much of that a miner gets through not wasting any of it can result in more value gained, despite the lower yield. That of course also depends on that valuable ore to be cleared even with the lower yield. But given the value of such ores, large groups will typically have no shortage of miners ready to pick it all clean.

  1. # Equipment
For maximum yield, a perfect booster requires a Tech II Industrial Core (Large for Orca, Capital for Rorqual) and a Tech II Mining Foreman Burst (see **Command Bursts**) loaded with the Mining Laser Optimization Charge. This charge reduces the cycle time of mining lasers by a base of 15%. Ship bonuses, skills (see Skills), and a mindlink (see Implants) can increase this reduction greatly, up to a maximum of 46.5% cycle time reduction for an Orca, or 61.5% cycle reduction time for a Rorqual. These reductions correspond with a 86.9% increase and 159.7% increase, respectively, in ore mining yield.

Two more Mining Foreman Burst IIs can be used to load the other two Mining Foreman Burst Charges: Mining Laser Field Enhancement Charge to increase range, and Mining Equipment Preservation Charge to reduce mining crystal damage (if they are being used). These don't directly improve yield, but longer range means less moving potentially has to be done, improving efficiency, and reducing crystal damage lowers input costs.

  1. # Implants
- **Mining Foreman Mindlink** - This is available on the market for about 50M ISK. It provides a +25% bonus to Mining Foreman Burst effect strength and duration.
  - This amounts to increasing an Orca's maximum mining cycle time reduction with Mining Laser Optimization Charge from 37.2% to 46.5%, and a Rorqual's from 49.2% to 61.5%. This increases the amount mined by 17.4% and 34.5%, respectively.
