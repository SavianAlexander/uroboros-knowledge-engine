1. # Summary
The **Machariel** is both the ultimate Fast Battleship, and the penultimate projectile turret battleship. It features the fastest engines and warp drive of any battleship, the second-highest damage output of any projectile battleship (25% fire rate and 25% damage on 7 guns; surpassed only by the **Vargur**), and even extended turret range (falloff), making it both one of the best autocannon battleships (by range, DPS, and utility slots) *and* one of the best artillery battleships (by alpha strike power). It also has a very large model, which when combined with its 8 high slots and increased warp speed make it one of the most favored **Smartbomb** ships in the game, to the point that it is commonly found in Lowsec Gatecamps and even running some PvE sites with pure Smartbomb layouts. Its high speed and turret damage make it popular in both high-level mission running and some PvP fleet applications, despite its high price tag. Its greatest drawbacks are, first, while its 8-6-6 slot layout lends it to being either shield- or armor-tanked, it will be somewhat less durable than most other battleship which feature 7 or even 8 mid or low slots; and second, autocannons and artillery in general have issues that interfere with them reaching their maximum potential, which is why Machariels have fallen out of favor in the **Incursion** community.

1. # Tactics
The Machariel can be found in a variety of roles and fleet sizes. In terms of PvE, levels 4 are the main target for this ship. However, due to the high cost of the hull and modules, it is a very risky ship to use as it is a very tempting target for pirates. In terms of PvP, the Machariel shines in small engagements. One potential use would be to team up with a tackle and pick off stragglers from a larger fleet. The warp speed and acceleration bonus helps decrease the chance of losing the tackle before you land on grid.

Other than picking off stragglers, the Machariel has a decent leg up on other comparable battleships. The high DPS and speed tanking abilities make for a deadly combination. Although the **Nightmare** has a larger role bonus for damage, the Machariel has more options for tuning damage types. Also, the Machariel will outperform the Nightmare outside of the 40 km sweet spot in terms of raw damage. Most importantly,the incredible agility of the ship allows for great speed tanking, especially when matched with other battleships.

In the past, the Machariel was also an excellent hull for Sansha **Incursion** fleets. However, after the Surgical Strike patch of 2020, the Machariel began to fall out of favor in Incursion communities, and the **Nightmare** and **Leshak** started to become more optimal Incursion ships. After the Bastions of War patch of 2021 **Marauders** stepped up as the elite option. **EVE University Incursion Community** switched to an armor doctrine at the end of April 2024 and the **Machariel** in not an option to armored fleets anymore.

1. # Notes
When viewed in game as an item, the Machariel's warp speed will be shown as 3 AU/s. This is due to the 25% warp speed bonus only being applied when a capsuleer enters the ship.
