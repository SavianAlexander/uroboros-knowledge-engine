1. # Summary
As expected from a Guristas destroyer, the **Mamba** is the bigger cousin of the **Worm**. Being the next-larger ship class, it has lower speed, more EHP, twice the launchers, thus more missile DPS, but the same drone damage bonus and drone bandwidth. The larger drone bay allows for keeping more drones in reserve and the cargo bay is big enough for keeping missile ammo, loot, and an MTU.
Blueprint copies for the Mamba can be purchased from insurgency LP stores in **Zarzakh** or the Gurista FOB.
