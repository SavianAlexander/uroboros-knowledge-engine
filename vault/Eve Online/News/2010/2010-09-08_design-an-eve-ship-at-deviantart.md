CCP and [deviantART](//about.deviantart.com/) have joined forces with the [Create a Starship Contest](//news.deviantart.com/article/128272/), a chance to show off your creativity that will leave its mark on EVE. The winning entry will make its way into EVE Online as a ship asset. The contest will be judged by both CCP and deviantART, and runs through October 13, 2010.
