1. # Summary
The Apotheosis is a special release shuttle thought to be of Jovian design. One shuttle was gifted to every active capsuleer at EVE Online's 5th anniversary, and is now given out to Omega clones through the login rewards track every year on EVE's Birthday. Besides the higher scan resolution, it functions as any other shuttle.
