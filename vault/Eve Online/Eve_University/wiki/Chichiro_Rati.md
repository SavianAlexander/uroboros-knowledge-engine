# Chichiro Rati

- Chiciro Rati** is a level 4 agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located in Ihakana - Shady Acres.

1. # The Regiment – Stolen Deed (1 of 4)

Provide 1x Shady Acres Deed (0.1 m3) from the **Contested Caldari Mining Facilities**, Contested Plagioclase Field in the deadspace complex to agent. The deed drops from Black Mask Bandits, but has a low drop rate. Note that killing Sanku Pansya in this same field and retrieving his head is required for the last task in this series.

1. # The Regiment - Arms Race (2 of 4)

Provide 1x Rifles (2 m3) to agent. Note that completing this mission requires destroying three battleships in a random deadspace complex in **low-sec**; the rifles cannot be purchased on contract.

Last battleship will drop the rifles in a container.

1. # The Regiment - Break Out (3 of 4)

Destroy all guards and provide 1x Nakyo Fukoren (1.0 m3) from the random deadspace complex in Ihakana to agent. Nakyo is located inside the Bandit jail, located on the asteroid in the center of the complex. It is recommended to turn on all brackets or to use the "General - All" filters if you do not see the jail. Note that this mission requires fighting in **low-sec**.
