With the launch of EVE Online: Tyrannis a new special website, called EVE Gate, has been created for EVE players. EVE Gate is a new social networking site open to the pilots in EVE Online and is now open for business. Head on over to [//www.evegate.com](//www.evegate.com) and get started with managing your contacts, reading your EVE mail, setting up calendar events, and talking with your corporation and contacts all without being logged into the EVE Online client!
