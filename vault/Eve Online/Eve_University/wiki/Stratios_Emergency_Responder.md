1. # Summary
The Stratios Emergency Responder is a special edition version of the **Stratios**. Allegedly, only four copies were released, given by CCP to the now-defunct third party lottery service SOMER Blink for use in giveaways. It has slightly better defenses, fitting capacity, targeting, and drone bandwidth than a regular Stratios.

1. # Notes
The Stratios Sanctuary SKIN, released in the Carynx 1.6 patch from June 16, 2015, allows a regular Stratios to look identical to the Stratios Emergency Responder.
