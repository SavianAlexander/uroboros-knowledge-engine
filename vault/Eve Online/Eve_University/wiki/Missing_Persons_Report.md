# Missing Persons Report

1. # Pocket 1
No spawn on entry, can warp at range.

Spawn is triggered by approaching the Acceleration Gate (13km, inactive until approached) or the Damaged Gallente Passenger Liner (22km).

The gate is unlocked.

|}

  1. # Pocket 2
The Damaged Gallente Passenger Liner is 34km from the warp in beacon but does **NOT** contain the mission objective.

You must destroy all ships in this pocket and loot a Cargo Container that is dropped when the last ship is destroyed.

Enemy types and numbers may vary, below is just one example.

|}

- **Killing the last ship in Group 5 drops a Cargo Container at the Damaged Gallente Passenger Liner. This contains 1 x Survivors which is the mission objective.**

'''Tip:
Triggering Groups 3 and 4 of Pocket 2 may cause an uncomfortable amount of DPS heading your way. If you had difficulty tanking the first 2 groups of that pocket you might want to call a friend.'''

Something (waiting around or looting the objective?) causes a small amount of AoE damage near the passenger liner that can destroy drones.
