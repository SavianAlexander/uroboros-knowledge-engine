If you prefer to read a recap, here’s a summary of their discussion. 


## A Living Expansion


EVE Online expansions are never static. 

Since the release of Catalyst, capsuleers have pushed its systems in unexpected directions, surfaced balance concerns and put fresh pressure on parts of the meta. That feedback is already shaping follow-up changes, with developers continuing to adjust areas that missed the mark and refine systems based on their performance. 


## From Feedback to Action


Okami Tea Time has a simple goal, which is to open up the conversation. 

Capsuleer feedback, along with in-game data, forum discussions, Reddit, Discord, and CSM input, is actively informing what happens next. 

Some changes, including ship balance follow-ups and tuning passes, are already being acted on quickly, while larger topics like force projection and future wormhole improvements remain on the table for later updates. 


## Tuning Catalyst


The Catalyst expansion largely landed where it needed to, but not perfectly. 

The Redeemer and other overperforming ships were deliberately brought down to keep the meta healthy and avoid power creep, while a few changes, such as Panther and Zirnitra tuning, are already being corrected after missing the intended balance. 

Carrier content also arrived overtuned and has since been adjusted to give players more time and breathing room. 

When it comes to force projection, the recent changes to Drifter wormholes were only a small step, and bigger solutions are in the works.
