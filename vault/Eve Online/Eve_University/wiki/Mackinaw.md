1. # Summary
The Mackinaw has the largest mining hold of the Exhumers, which makes it ideal for solo operations, but sacrifices some tank and yield. With maximum  and  skills, the Mackinaw has a total mining hold capacity of 44 296.9 m³.

1. # Tactics

- Due to how vulnerable and cumbersome the mining barges are, it is ill-advised to travel between stargates in lowsec in one, as they make for very juicy targets. Instead, a good idea is to transport your Mackinaw packaged in a hauler, which raises less (albeit still some) suspicion.
  - A **Badger**, even at base skills, has a capacity of 3,900, allowing it to carry a packaged mining frigate, barge, and even an exhumer, and still have enough cargo space for its modules, can perform the cloak trick, and when designed well, can align in under 6 seconds. Beware however, that when packaging a ship, all its **rigs** are destroyed, so either use cheap Tech I rigs or design your ship around not using them.
