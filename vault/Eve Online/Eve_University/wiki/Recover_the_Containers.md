  1. # Notes
- Using Auto-Targeting missiles and setting drones to "aggressive" may help with target jammers.
- Each room's databanks contain 1 of the 3 Sealed Containers.
- Databanks are trapped and can explode if you fail a hack attempt. (Each explosion will cause 350 HP of proximity damage!)
- The 2nd Pocket's maximum damage dealing of all ships is 2,130 HP/s (82% Kin, 17% Therm).
