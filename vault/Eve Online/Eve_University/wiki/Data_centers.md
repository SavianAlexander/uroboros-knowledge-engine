# Data centers

The **Data Center** missions are **mission**s where you hand in pirate tags (you can loot these in missions and buy them on the **market**). Each completed mission rewards you with Corporation and Faction standings, including **derived standings**. Basically, it allows you to convert ISK into standing.

Data Center missions come in 2 flavors: Keeping Crime In Check (pirate tag hand-ins) and Graduation Certificate/Data Transportation (simple courier of 0.1).

Each faction has 3 systems with agents.
- Two systems have between 2 and 3 agents, one of whom offers a simple courier mission of 0.1 of their faction's Graduation Certificate to a station a few jumps away in High Security space.
- The third system has 12 agents for pirate tag hand-ins, with 3 of each being Level 1 (0.0 standing minimum), Level 2 (1.0 standing minimum), Level 3 (3.0 standing minimum), and Level 4 (5.0 standing minimum).

The exception to this are the Amarr Data Centers, which have 2 systems for pirate tag hand-ins with 12 agents each for a total of 24. Both the Amarr Empire and the Ammatar Mandate are represented.

1. # Amarr Empire

Located at the Imperial Data Center beacon in space. The Amarr Empire and the Ammatar Mandate share data centers, therefore there are more agents. 
These agents give standing rewards when you bring them certain tags, e.g. Sansha Copper, Brass, Silver, etc tags. 
Khanid and Ammatar agents will need at least positive standing to their specific sub-faction.

The agents in Amarr space collect **Blood Raider Covenant** tags while the **Ammatar Mandate** collects **Sansha's Nation** tags.
