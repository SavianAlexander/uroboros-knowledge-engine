# Angel Hidden Hub

The Angel Hidden Hub is a **combat anomaly** found in **Angel** infested regions. It is a level 2 variant of the **Angel Hub**. The site is moderate difficulty done with MJD sniper Battleship. The site pays estimately 19,3 million ISK in bounties.

1. # Walkthrough
Single ungated normal space pocket. Warping to beacon for close range would require a very good tank to handle the swarm’s incoming damage.
