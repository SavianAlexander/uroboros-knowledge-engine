# Dust 514: Ambush OMS

1. # Ambush OMS

This game mode is the same as regular **Ambush** but every couple of minutes, installations such as Blaster and Rail Turrets, are air dropped into the battlefield and can be captured by means of hacking them and used against the enemy.
