During downtime on Tuesday, June 8, Command Centers were seeded which will allow players to become fully involved with Planetary Interaction. Over 60,000 planets are now available for everyone to stake their claim. A [tutorial](//www.youtube.com/watch?v=WzSnf8HwQ_U) is available to guide you on your way to exploiting resources from many worlds. We would ask any player who experiences problems with Planetary Interaction to [submit a detailed bug report](//bugs.eveonline.com/newbugreport.asp).
