# Glacial Mass Belt

A Glacial Mass Belt is a **Cosmic Anomaly** found in High and Low Security Space in the **Minmatar quadrant**. It can be **mined** with a properly fitted **mining ship**. A Glacial Mass Belt can only be found in one of the ice systems (see below for the full list of High Sec and Low Sec systems).

While sharing the same name, the contents of a Glacial Mass Belt are different in High Sec, Low Sec higher than 0.1 and in Low Sec with security 0.1.
