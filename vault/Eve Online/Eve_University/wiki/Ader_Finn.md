# Ader Finn

- Ader Finn** is an **Amarr COSMOS** agent. He's located at Munory V - Moon 3.

1. # Contacting Ader (1 of 2)

Odan Poun's Message is  a reward for **Manel's Disappearance (1 of 5)** for **Odan Poun**.

1. # Contacting Ader (2 of 2)

The mission gives 0.17% faction standings and **Ader's Message**, required for **Manel's Disappearance (2 of 5)** for **Odan Poun**. Ensure you grab Ader's message before leaving Gonan.
