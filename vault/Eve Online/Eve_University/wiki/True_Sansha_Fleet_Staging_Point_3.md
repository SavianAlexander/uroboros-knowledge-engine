# True Sansha Fleet Staging Point 3

- True Sansha Fleet Staging Point 3** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **True Sansha Fleet Staging Point 2**.
1. # Walkthrough
This site can be very hard, though the loot may be very rewarding. The site is not in **deadspace**, and it is ungated. Any aspiring hunter can warp directly to you with combat probes, so be careful if running the site.  Unlike the **True Sansha Fleet Staging Point** and **True Sansha Fleet Staging Point 2**, the site has no gates.

The Sansha's Battletower has a fairly strong web that reaches out to 50 km. It deals a 0-2250-4500-2250 damage hit (in total 9000 dph) per 20000 seconds, and will fire the instant it locks your ship. It fires Citadel Mjolnir torpedoes every 120 seconds that deal high EM damage. It recharges shields to the tune of about 300 DPS. It is recommended to keep moving as fast as you can to mitigate as much damage from the battletower as possible.
