# Magye Pirates

Magye Pirates is a static site which can be found in the Otomainen system. You can warp to Otomainen VIII - Asteroid Belt 1 without needing to scan down the site.

  1. # 1st Room

COSMOS rats drop Raytio Family Supplies.

Note: It is possible the non-COSMOS rats were just normal belt spawns.
