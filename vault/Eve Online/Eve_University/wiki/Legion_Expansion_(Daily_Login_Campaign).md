# Legion Expansion (Daily Login Campaign)

- Legion Expansion** is a limited-time log-in campaign in celebration of the release of the Legion expansion. Lasting  from 27 May till 16 June 2025, **downtime** to downtime. It was not mentioned in any promotional material prior to the launch.
