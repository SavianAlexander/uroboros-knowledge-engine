# Flowing Nebula

- Flowing Nebula** is a gas exploration site containing gas used in the production of **Medical boosters**.

This is a null sec gas site found through Exploration.

Defenders:

None

Minable Gas Clouds:

1 Celadon Cytoserocin (2,000 units in the gas cloud)

Celadon Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Standard Exile Booster Booster.

This site is found only in the[Fountain](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Fountain)region of space.
