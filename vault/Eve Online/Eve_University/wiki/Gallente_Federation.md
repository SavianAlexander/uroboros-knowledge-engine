# Gallente Federation

Champions of liberty and fierce guardians of the human spirit, the **Gallente Federation** is the only true democracy in **New Eden**. Its principles are brotherhood, fraternity, and equal rights to all men. Some of the most progressive leaders, scientists, and businessmen of the era have emerged from its diverse peoples. Pioneers of artificial intelligence, the Federation once relied almost entirely on drone fleets to defend its beliefs and borders. But the limitations of this technology and the lack of a human element—both in terms of a military strategy and as a means of spreading influence— eventually reached a critical point.

1. # Lore

Gallente Prime, also known as Gallentia, is the home world of the Gallente. This planet is in the system of Luminaire with Caldari Prime. The Gallente Prime was originally settled by French settlers. The French roots are still clearly visible in Gallente values and names.

The Gallente Federation was formed by multiple different civilizations on year 115 BYC. Most notable members were the Caldari, the Gallente, the Mannar and the Intaki.

But already on 81 BYC, only 34 years after the forming of the Federation, the **Gallente-Caldari War** broke out resulting in forming of the **Caldari State**.

1. # Gallente in PvE
Gallente rats deal kinetic and thermal damage, whether the emphasis is on kinetic or thermal varies ship by ship. They are weakest against kinetic damage with thermal being the second best damage to use.

The EWAR of choice of Gallente rats is sensor dampening. Unless you are sniping it will just make targeting times annoyingly long. But if you encounter multiple elite cruisers with dampening you may lose over 70% of your targeting range.
