# Recon (1 of 3) (Blood Raiders) (Level 2)

<noinclude>
Parts: **1 of 3 (Blood Raiders)**, **2 of 3**, **3 of 3**
</noinclude>

Blitz: Just head right for the acceleration gate in the first Pocket tanking the damage, then after that jump, you're complete so you can dock.

  1. # Pocket 1
Four spawns timed every 20 to 30 sec. All spawns are instant aggro. Ship names may change but ship class are the same in each spawn.

|}

|}

|}

|}

  1. # Pocket 2
You will get mission completed when entering this pocket, so you don't have to kill the spawns there. The spawns are a bit random, ship names may change and you can get one ship more or less in each class ship group.

|}

|}

  1. # Notes
- Use a shuttle (or any other preferred ((fast)) ship), get to the gate fast, use it, then warp out when you get to pocket 2. Mission is completed
- No loot
- Pocket 2 contains many Veldspar.
- Bring a long-range weapons fit or medium drones and stay outside NOS range.
  - Make sure to fit an Afterburner to achieve that.
- Small T2 drones *can* break the Elite Cruisers' tank, but expect that to take a while. Typically 40 minutes total for this pocket.
