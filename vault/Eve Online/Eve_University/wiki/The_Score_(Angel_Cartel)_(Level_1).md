# The Score (Angel Cartel) (Level 1)

Single gated pocket where you kill Angel Cartel ships.

Despite the mission telling you to 'destroy all the ships', only the 'main target' and the surrounding group needs to be destroyed.

  - Blitz:** Kill the destroyer (Gistior Defacer/Haunter) and the group around it by the ghost ship and the mission will be marked as complete.

  1. # Single Pocket

No Aggro at warp in.

|}

  1. # Note
Structures do not drop loot.
