# Angel Forsaken Rally Point

The Angel Forsaken Rally Point is a **combat anomaly** found in **Angel** infested regions. It is a level 3 variant of the **Angel Rally Point**. The site is easily done with sniper Battleship T2 fit.

1. # Walkthrough
Single ungated normal space pocket. Warping to beacon for close range would require a very good tank to handle the Hostiles' incoming damage. You can warp to the site at range instead.
