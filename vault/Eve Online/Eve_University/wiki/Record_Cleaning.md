# Record Cleaning

- Blitz:** Go to the Pirate Station and loot the Hostage.

  1. # Warp-In
You are given the choice between two gates
- Gate to Acidic Cloud (18km)
- Gate to Patrol Area (18km)

When approaching either of the acceleration gates a popup message explains what you have to expect in the area they lead into.

  1. # Pocket 1a (Acidic Cloud)
Empty pocket, no spawn.
- Ship receives damage of unknown type every second.
- Gate to Pirate Base 40k away.
- Gate to Entrance 6k away.

Note: Area deals enough damage to reduce my Rattlesnake's almost perfect passive tank (I have T1 Defense Field Purgers instead of T2, and level 4 in the shield compensation skills) to 56% (stable) by the time I fly to the next gate.

Note: Seamus Donohue calculates, based on his personal observations, that the acidic cloud deals 600 damage per second as perfect omnidamage (that is, 150 EM/second, 150 thermal/second, 150 kinetic/second, and 150 explosive/second).

  1. # Notes
- Maximum bounty when going through the Patrol Area = 4.41 mill
- Loot: About 1200 m³ before cap booster charges and metal scraps.
- Standing increase is excellent with this storyline mission.
- This being a typical mercenary mission, all NPCs will close to a range of 15k or less, similar to **Damsel in Distress L4**. If you can handle the DPS, and deal good DPS, this is very easy and quick (10 minutes) to finish.
