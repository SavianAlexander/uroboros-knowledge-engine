Accept the Mission remotely.

Gated deadspace pocket within 1 jump of Tolle. You meet a shuttle that is trying to get the kid to safety. A hostile fleet arrives. When you land the Shuttle Wreck is surrounded by a fleet of Minmatar Republic ships.

  - Blitz:** take the Gate, take the Gate again, salvage the Shuttle Wreck.

Complete the Mission remotely.

Tips:
- All Acceleration Gates are unlocked
- There is no Minmatar Republic Faction Standing loss
- Depending on your ship and salvage chance you may not be able to survive long enough to salvage the Shuttle Wreck; destroy a few ships first
- Use a Microwarpdrive or a Micro Jump Drive to lure the Fleet way from the Shuttle Wreck
- Remember to fit a Salvager module (Salvage drones do not work)
