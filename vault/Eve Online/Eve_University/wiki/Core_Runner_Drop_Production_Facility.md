# Core Runner Drop Production Facility

The **Core Runner Drop Production Facility** is a **chemical lab** found in the Cloud Ring region. It must be scanned down with scanner probes, and will appear as a gas site in the probe window.  
The site is not in deadspace nor behind a gate, so it is possible to warp around freely and for people to warp directly to you.  

1. # Walkthrough

The site contains both combat and hacking. 

  1. # Warp-in pocket
All the ships will be named Core Runner < something >.

It is possible to tank the initial defenders with a strong enough tank, while you hack the cans and skip the combat. But this will leave you without the possible commander drops. 
Another tactic is to clear the site in a PvE fitted combat ship, and then refit or reship to a hacking ship once the site is cleared.   

Destroy the energy neuting towers first, then the frigates and up in size.
