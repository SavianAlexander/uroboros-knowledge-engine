Hello space friends!

This September we will be visiting/hosting a few EVE player gathering and events under the banner of **EVE Euro Tour 2015**.

We will be demoing [Gunjack](//www.polygon.com/2015/8/3/9089493/gunjack-trailer-samsung-gear-vr-ccp-games) on all locations, and [EVE: Valkyrie](//www.evevalkyrie.com/) will also be present in Nottingham and Amsterdam.

*Tickets are still available for Paris, Berlin and Amsterdam*

Please find further info on the tour [here](//community.eveonline.com/news/dev-blogs/eve-euro-tour-2015-ccp-does-europe/) and the overview below.
