1. # Summary
The **Gecko** is a Gurista super heavy drone designed to be used with the **Rattlesnake** class battleship. Each Rattlesnake can utilize one of these monsters, with the hull bonuses giving them significantly higher damage and tank.

1. # Tactics
It is much easier to control your Gecko as you will usually only have one of them to watch after. Watch the tank levels on your Gecko carefully, if the shields go down to 20-25% then you have to pull your drone, as the Gecko isn't as strong in hull or armor. Gecko's are expensive (180 mil minimum) and losing them is easily preventable. Make sure to target webs first, as ships with webs spell a death sentence for the (relatively) slow Gecko.
