# Trading Spaces

- Trading Spaces** was an event celebrating the new Jita IV - Moon 4 - Caldari Navy Assembly Plant, the largest **trade hub** in the game. It ran from 10-18 November 2020, downtime to downtime.

1. # Daily tasks

Each day, each player was given a task to shoot a Forever 4-4 firework at the titan in one of the parade sites.

The reward from this was 1x Forward 4-4 Celebration Crate. Each crate contained two random items from the following:

- Forward 4-4 SKIN
- Women's or Men's Jita 4-4 Crew Top
- Women's or Men's Jita 4-4 Crew Shoes
- Women's or Men's Jita 4-4 Crew Pants
- Women's or Men's Jita 4-4 Executive Jacket
- Women's or Men's Jita 4-4 Executive Pants

1. # 4-4 Expansion Parades

There were four parades celebrating the Jita 4-4 expansion. One parade in each empire.

Harva - LGS Kolvil's Dream

Tash-Murkon - TES Bountiful Blessings

Jita - PKNS Golden Apple

Lirsautton - QCS Heat of the Moment

1. # Daily Login Campaign
Capsuleers had 7 days to collect up to 5 days of rewards. All rewards were marked as Tier 1 gifts.
