# Next of Kin (1 of 2)

- Next of Kin (1 of 2)** is a **Minmatar COSMOS** level 4 mission site for **Lafuni Oduntra** in DUO-51 ().

1. # Walkthrough
Okelle's Pleasure Hub is the main target. The site provides the required 1x Okelle's Encryption-Protected Hard Drive (0.1 m³). 
This room contains rats from the **Angel Cartel** pirate faction.
