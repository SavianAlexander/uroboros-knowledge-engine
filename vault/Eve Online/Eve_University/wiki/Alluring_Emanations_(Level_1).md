# Alluring Emanations (Level 1)

- Alluring Emanations** is a mission involving combat against **Rogue Drones**. There are multiple level variants.

After killing the first Rogue Drone, a huge number of reinforcements show up. Killing them is not required to complete the mission. Doing so anyway, such as for the 25 non-capsuleer kills **daily**, is quite manageable due to their low small hitpoints pool and their arriving in smaller clumps that can be picked off well before they become a problem simply from strength in numbers.
