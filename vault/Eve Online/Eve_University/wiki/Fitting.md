# Fitting

- Fitting** often refers to the act of installing modules, rigs, or subsystems onto a ship hull.

"**Fitting**" may also refer to:
- A description of the set of modules that are installed on a ship
- **Fitting ships**, modules or rigs that increase a ship's powergrid or CPU
- **Fitting upwell structures**, modules & rigs as well as services unique to **Upwell structures**
