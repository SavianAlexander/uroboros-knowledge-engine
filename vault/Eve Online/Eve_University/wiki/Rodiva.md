1. # Summary
The **Rodiva** is a Triglavian **Logistics** Cruiser, introduced with the Onslaught expansion in Late 2018. Its repair power is initially a bit lower than a regular T1 Logistics Cruiser, but can eventually repair at the rate of T2 Logistics Cruisers if given the opportunity to spool long enough on a single target.
