# Pith-Robux Asteroid Mining Co.

- Pith-Robux Asteroid Mining Co.** is a DED 1/10 Rated Complex. It can be found by scanning cosmic signatures in high security or low security space.

This site's acceleration gate allows only Frigate class ships or lower and their Tech 2 equivalents to enter the site. The "Scratched and Dented Keycard" can also be found in the Leaking Caskets in the **Haunted Yard**.

1. # Walkthrough

  1. # Room 1

The acceleration gate requires "scratched and dented keycard" to activate. The key is consumed on use. The container in the room contains the required key.
