During the Veto player meet in London today, CCP Unifex announced the upcoming 18th expansion for EVE Online: Retribution. This release, due on December 4, 2012, will bring players a new [bounty system](//www.eveonline.com/retribution/bounty-hunting/), [balancing across multiple ship classes and four new destroyers](//www.eveonline.com/retribution/new-ships-new-roles/), updates to [Crimewatch](//www.eveonline.com/retribution/crimewatch/) and much more.

Check out the new [Retribution page](//www.eveonline.com/retribution/) for more details.
