# It's Not Over Yet

<noinclude>
</noinclude>

  - Objective**
Report to a new agent, Keita Eslin, located at [Hek IV - Krusual Tribe Bureau station](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/station/Hek_IV_-_Krusual_Tribe_Bureau).

  - Notes**
Time to see another agent, this time in **Minmatar** space. Set your destination to Hek, make the 13 high-sec jump trip, and report to Agent Eslin at the designated station.

Check your way before starting this journey, as there are several 0.5 systems on the way that often have kills at the gates. [EVE Gatecheck, Secure Route](https://eve-gatecheck.space/eve/index.php#Arnon:Hek:secure)
