# Serpentis Fill-In

<noinclude> </noinclude>

Ungated pocket up to 4 jumps away. Pick up the Sealed Research Cache from the Station and deliver it to the Serpentis Transport Hub Container.

Tips:
- don't forget to pick up the Sealed Research Cache! It is not automatically placed in your cargohold and the agent will not notify you if you undock without it.

Complete the Mission remotely.
