# Freebooter's Haven

- Freebooter's Haven** is a static **Angel COSMOS** combat site in [BJD4-E](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/system/BJD4-E).

1. # Room 1

This room features a large number of sentries, webbing towers, elite frigates and battleships.

An acceleration gate leads deeper into the complex. The gate is locked until all enemies have been defeated.

1. # Room 3
There are three gates in this room, in the left, middle, and right side of the field, and are unlocked using the alpha, beta, and gamma keys respectively, meaning the rooms must be completed sequentially.

  1. # Left Room

This room is unlocked with the alpha key. The acceleration gate in this area is locked until all enemies have been defeated, and enemies respawn within an hour.

|}

  1. # Middle Room
This room is unlocked with the beta key. The acceleration gate in this area is locked until all enemies have been defeated, and enemies respawn within an hour.

|}

  1. # Right Room
The final room is unlocked with the gamma key and has a number of enemy waves triggered by the amount of damage dealt to the Angel Central Command structure.
