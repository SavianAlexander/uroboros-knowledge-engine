# Shadow

- thumb|Sansha's fighter bomber, the **Shadow**.**
# Information
  - Subject:** Compact Nation Vessel (ID:Shadow)

  - Military Specifications:** Heavy fighter-class vessel. Moderate microwarp velocity. Enhanced weapons systems. Reverse-engineering has been made possible through salvage of specific Sansha's Nation vessels.

  - Additional Intelligence:** At this initial stage conclusions are hard to form, but early indications from the recovery of three individual bomber pilots – all of them heavily augmented – strongly indicates that these crew members are the same planetary abductees found in other Nation Compact vessels. Cross-referencing recovered DNA with missing persons reports has so far revealed little, though even at this stage the origin of these pilots is in little doubt.

''Synopsis from ISHAEKA-0107. DED Special Operations.
Authorized for Capsuleer dissemination.''
# Availability
The **Shadow** can be obtained by getting a **blueprint copy** from the loot of **The Kundalini Manifest** or the **Uroborus** (mothership sites in **Incursions**).
