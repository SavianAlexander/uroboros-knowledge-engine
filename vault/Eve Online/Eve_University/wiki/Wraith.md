1. # Summary
Only usable by devs before its removal, there is little information on this ship.

1. # Notes
- According to [a dev post](https://eve-search.com/thread/325685-0/page/all#15) the Wraith was the Jove 'newbie frigate', though comparing the two Jove frigates reveals the Wraith's attributes are far superior to the Spectre's, which indicates this may have been stated in error.
- All Jove ships were removed from the game files in the [Rubicon 1.3 patch](https://www.eveonline.com/article/patch-notes-for-rubicon-1.3).
