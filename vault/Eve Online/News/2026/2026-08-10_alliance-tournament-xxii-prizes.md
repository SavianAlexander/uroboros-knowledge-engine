## Amarr Prize Ships


After the sponsorship and ad-ridden broadcast of Alliance Tournament XXI, the Independent Gaming Commission (IGC) opted to take on a more imperial sponsor this year: The Amarr Empire. The Amarr last sponsored Alliance Tournament X, offering up the Malice Assault Frigate and the Vangel Heavy Assault Cruiser. This year, IGC’s engineers have made adaptations to a small batch of different Amarr vessels and are now ready to make their alterations public. Introducing glorious new Amarr ships, exclusive to ATXXII: 


### The Herald



- An electronic attack frigate, based on the Sentinel, combined with technology used with the Retribution and Vengeance Assault Frigate class. The Herald offers the strongest tracking and guidance disruption of any platform in New Eden, and with eight effective turrets it can easily fight at extended ranges while keeping opponents incapacitated.



### The Penitence



- A Combat Recon Cruiser, based on the Curse, combined with tackling tech found in the Heavy Interdictor class, like the Devoter. The Penitence combines powerful weapon disruption, devastating energy neutralization, and formidable tackling capabilities, giving it all the tools it needs to impose its will across the battlefield.

## Prize Ship Distribution


A total of 140 Heralds and 140 Penitences will be awarded, shared out by final placement: 


| **PLACEMENT** | **HERALD** | **PENITENCE** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st (winner of the grand finals) | 40 | 40 |
| 2nd (loser of the grand finals) | 20 | 20 |
| 3rd | 16 | 16 |
| 4th | 12 | 12 |
| Top 6 | 8 | 8 |
| Top 8 | 6 | 6 |
| Top 12 | 4 | 4 |
| Top 16 | 2 | 2 |
| **Total awarded** | **140** | **140** |


Figures are per team. Teams finishing fifth and sixth each receive eight of each hull, seventh and eighth each receive six, ninth to twelfth each receive four, and thirteenth to sixteenth each receive two. 


## Amarr Victory SKINs


Amarr Victory SKINs are awarded for every match victory. Each match within a best-of-three series counts as one match. 

The Amarr Victory SKINs will be styled off of the design of the Herald and Penitence, and apply to the following ships


- Apocalypse Navy Issue
- Abaddon
- Prophecy Navy Issue
- Pilgrim
- Guardian
- Omen Navy Issue
- Dragoon Navy Issue
- Pontifex
- Retribution
- Imperial Navy Slicer



## Trophies and the Monument


The digital Alliance Tournament trophy will be transferred to the new captain’s hangar. The winning pilots’ names will be etched into the physical Alliance Tournament trophy, which will be on display at Fanfest. In addition, the Alliance Tournament Monument in Manarq will be updated.
