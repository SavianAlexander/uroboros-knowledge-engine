# Friendly Spies

- Blitz**: Destroy the Radio Telescope and warp-out.
That will finish the mission quickly; however, even if you kill all the ships, there is no standings loss incurred (and the tags have a significant amount of value)

  1. # Warp-in
Initial spawn is 20-30km away from warp-in. Upon destruction of the last ship in a group, a new reinforcement wave will spawn.
