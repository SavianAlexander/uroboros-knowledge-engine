The EVE forums, as you know them, will soon be replaced by a brand new forum on EVE Gate. The new functionality provided is currently undergoing testing and we would ask players to log in, get involved and provide us your feedback. To see how you can take part, please see the most recent blog by CCP Paradox and Team Occam's Razor; ‘[The forums are evolving](//www.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&bid=859)'.
