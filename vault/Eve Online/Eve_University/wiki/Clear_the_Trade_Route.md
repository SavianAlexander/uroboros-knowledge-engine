# Clear the Trade Route

- Clear the Trade Route** is part of the Minmatar **circle mission** chain.

Jump to adjacent system, warp to location, kill 3 drones. You do not have to destroy the warp disrupter.

|}

  - Tourists Need A Lift** &gt;&gt; **Clear the Trade Route** &gt;&gt; **Call to Arms**
