# EVE and DUST 514 nominated at the Golden Joystick Awards

- **Date**: 2013-09-02T17:12:44.000Z
- **Category**: news
- **Author**: CCP Guard
- **Source**: https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/eve-and-dust-514-nominated-for-the-golden-joystick-awards

## Overview
We're proud that both EVE Online and DUST 514 have been nominated at the Golden Joystick Awards for Best Online Game and Best Newcomer respectively. We would welcome your support and encourage the community to partake in selecting their favorite games of 2012/2013!

---

We're proud that both EVE Online and DUST 514 have been nominated at the [Golden Joystick Awards](//www.computerandvideogames.com/joystick/homepage.php) for Best Online Game and Best Newcomer respectively.

We would welcome your support and encourage the community to partake in selecting their favorite games of 2012/2013!
