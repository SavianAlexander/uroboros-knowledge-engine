# Trouble in Paradise

- Trouble in Paradise** is a four part **expedition** that can escalate from the **Hierarchy** combat site. The four parts are each about 5-8 jumps apart, and can take you far from the starting point. The rewards can be worth it though. The rogue drones in these combat sites do mostly explosive damage, with kinetic a close second. They are weak to EM damage.

1. # First Location
Access: Gateless, Open Access

The Sentient is the trigger for the escalation and contains loot. It is spawned from the beginning. This part can be blitzed by destroying the Sentient fast.
