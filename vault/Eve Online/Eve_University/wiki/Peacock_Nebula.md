# Peacock Nebula

- Peacock Nebula** is a gas exploration site containing gas used in the production of **Medical boosters**.

This is a null sec gas site found through Exploration.

Defenders:

None

Minable Gas Clouds:

2 Celadon Cytoserocin (3000 units in each gas cloud), 80km off each other.

Celadon Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Standard Exile Booster Booster.

This site is found only in the[Pegasus](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Fountain/Pegasus)constellation of the[Fountain](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Fountain)region of space.
