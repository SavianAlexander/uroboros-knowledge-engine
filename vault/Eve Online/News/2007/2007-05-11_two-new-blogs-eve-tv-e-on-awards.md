Are you feeling like there is something missing in your life? Are you bored? Well, lucky for you, we posted not one, but two Dev Blogs today!

The first one is about the E-ON Awards, it contains information on the venue, how to vote, and when ticket sales will start! Read the [Final round of voting for the E-ON Awards](//myeve.eve-online.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&bid=462) blog, and feel free to post in the [discussion thread](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=519016).

We also have one about EVE TV, mainly discussing what will happen in the future. The concept is changing a bit, so read [The Return of EVE TV](//myeve.eve-online.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&bid=463). And of course, we have a [discussion thread](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=519031) for this one as well.

Make sure you check the [in-game news](//myeve.eve-online.com/news.asp), [the Dev Blog section](//myeve.eve-online.com/devblog.asp) and the [EVE Information Portal](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=channel&channelID=3512) forum as often as you can, for all the latest news in EVE!
