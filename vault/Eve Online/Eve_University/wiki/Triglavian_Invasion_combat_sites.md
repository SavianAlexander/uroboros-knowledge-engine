# Triglavian Invasion combat sites

This is an overview of the combat anomaly sites found in the various phases of the **3rd chapter of the Triglavian Invasion**. These combat anomalies can be found in the probe scanner window or in the Agency window in the systems undergoing a invasion by the Triglavian Collective. As the invaded systems escalate into later phases of the invasion, harder combat anomalies open up. For an overview of the NPCs to be found in these combat anomalies, **look here**. For a overview of the structures special to the invasion, **look here**.

Although the combat sites in the 3rd chapter share many similarities to the found combat anomalies in earlier chapters of the Triglavian invasion, they aren't the same. 

1. # Triglavian Invasion Combat Anomalies
The Cosmic Anomalies found in the systems invaded by the Triglavian Collective comes in three tiers or classes of difficulty. At first, in the Stellar Reconnaissance phase only class 1. of the combat sites are available, but as system progresses towards either Triglavian or EDENCOM victory higher classes will show up.

1. # Emerging Conduit
This combat site, together with the EDENCOM Forward Post, are the primary combat sites found in the Stellar Reconnaissance phase. 

They contain 3 waves of Triglavian NPCs containing 3-6 ships in each. 

These combat sites also spawn in minor victory systems

Clearing this site helps the EDENCOM forces.

1. # EDENCOM Forward Post
This combat site, together with the Emerging Conduits, are the primary combat sites found in the Stellar Reconnaissance phase. 

These combat sites also spawn in minor victory systems.

Clearing this site helps the Triglavian Collective.

1. # Minor Conduit
This combat anomaly is found when a system is either leaning towards the Triglavian Collective or the EDENCOM forces. It is a harder and more advanced version of the Emerging Conduit site.

They contain 5 waves of Triglavian NPCs containing 12-15 ships in each. 

Clearing this site helps the EDENCOM forces.

1. # EDENCOM Staging Area
This combat anomaly is found when a system is either leaning towards the Triglavian Collective or the EDENCOM forces. It is a harder and more advanced version of the EDENCOM Forward Post. 

These combat sites now also spawn in minor victory systems after the 20/07/02 patch.

Clearing this site helps the Triglavian Collective.

== Major Conduit == 
This combat anomaly is found in either Bulwark or Second Liminality systems. It is a harder and more advanced version of the Minor Conduit site.

They contain 6 waves of Triglavian NPCs containing 16-20 ships in each. 

Clearing this site helps the EDENCOM forces.

1. # EDENCOM Field Base
This combat anomaly is found in either Bulwark or Second Liminality systems. It is a harder and more advanced version of the EDENCOM Staging Area site.

These combat sites now also spawn in minor victory systems after the 20/07/02 patch.

Clearing this site helps the Triglavian Collective.

1. # World Ark Deployment Site
The world ark deployment site appears during escalating liminality phases.

The site contains the world ark and some Triglavian ships.

Capsuleers on EDENCOM side can attack the site. Capsuleers on Triglavian side can mine Bezdnacine.
