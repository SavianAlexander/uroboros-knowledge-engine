# Intercept The Saboteurs (Guristas Pirates)

Video: [Paladin](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w-qAgQZquQs), [Sentry Dominix](https://youtu.be/BGJEDHhULTI), [Gila](https://youtu.be/IfuA7O1gnMI), 28-08-2021]

  - Blitz:**
- Kill ships at entry room, use gate.
- Kill all ships in group 2, collect viral agent, return to agent.

  1. # Warp-in

|}

  1. # 1st pocket
- Attacking group 2 aggros entire pocket.
- Group 2 is actually two groups so the whole group doesn't need to be killed. Though they spawn at the same spot so it's impossible to see what ships are in what group.
- Attacking groups 1 or 4 will usually aggro that group and group 2.
- Drones will get aggroed by neutral groups due to proximity. Elite frigates will also agress drones.
- Attacking an elite frigate in group 3 will only aggro that group, which can be sniped at leisure.
- Confiscated Viral Agent is dropped by the last defeated ship in group 2 (mission completion trigger).
- Armories drop T1 Ammo, Habitation Modules may drop Trade Goods
