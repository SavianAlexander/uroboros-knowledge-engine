The Amarr Championship Award Cerimony will take place tomorrow ( Saturday ) at 16:00 GMT in Amarr Prime. Several dignitaries have announced their appearances as well as the Champion for Doriam Kor-Azor, Ecliptical. Attendees are required to patch their clients to the latest available version ( 1246 ) if they want to see the prizes given.
