# Hidden Hope

- Notes:** There are many Gallente structures and SoE ships. There is no loot from industrial or freights. The freighters have about 100,000 HP and industrial have about 20,000 HP. Don't waste your time and ammo on them.

  1. # 1st pocket
There are a number of SoE transports upon warp-in. Serpentis attackers show up shortly after and aggro.
Spawns appear to arrive when all ships are destroyed. Spawns aggro drones.

10km to gate.
