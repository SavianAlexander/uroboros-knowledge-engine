# Sansha Forsaken Hub

The **Sansha Forsaken Hub** is an **unrated cosmic signature combat site** that can be freely warped to in **Sansha** owned territory of Null Sec space. The site itself is a small area with no Acceleration Gate. Each wave spawns after the destruction of the previous wave.
