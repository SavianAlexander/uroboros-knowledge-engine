1. # Summary
The **Sigil** is the faster, more agile sibling of the **Bestower**. Its cargo hold can expand to 11,277 (no rigs), its align time can be reduced to 3.7 s, or its shield tanked to ~60k EHP (w/shield rigs).

However, compared to the other T1 fast **Haulers**, the Sigil is below average in almost every category, generally having the worst capacity, tank, and velocity depending on fit, as well as mediocre align and signature radius.
