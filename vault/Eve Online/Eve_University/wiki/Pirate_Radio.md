Destroy all hostile ships (incl. the industrial) in the initial spawn and the FTL Transmitter to complete the mission.

The reinforcement spawns are on an unknown timer. A Gnosis can clear the mission before they spawn.

Total standing loss for destroying the ships in the initial spawn + Radio Telescope is 0.305 (unadjusted). -0.003 Minmatar faction standing for destroying the reinforcement spawns.

By 2026 the damage dealt by the initial group of enemies is, atypically for Minmatar, primarily EM.

1. # Loot
This mission drops a tag for each ship killed in the reinforcement spawns, corresponding to its class and firepower. The following were obtained from a full sweep:

Republic Fleet Captain Insignia I x2
Republic Fleet Commander Insignia I x2
Republic Fleet High Captain Insignia I x15
Republic Fleet Private Insignia II x4

In addition, various other loot is dropped in the form of meta modules and assorted Minmatar ammo. Salvage is also decent. Total loot value comes up to about 29,300,000 ISK. Most of this value, 27 million of it, comes from the tags, with salvage of 1.3 million, and 1 million of modules.
