Single pocket with gate.

  - Walkthrough:**
- You arrive approx 25km from a small group, including two Personnel Transports. No auto aggro.
- Group 2 and 3 spawn upon approaching the Personnel Transports.
- Destroy the convoy and loot at least one of the Personnel Transports to obtain the Militants, then return to your agent.
