# Puerto Rico ERP, Tax & Compliance Knowledge Vault Master Index

## 1. Subsystem Architecture & Partition Topology
- **`vault/pr_tax_erp/master_pr_tax_erp_index.md`**: Central Cross-Reference & Statutory Dispatcher.
- **`vault/pr_tax_erp/quickbooks_tax_guides/`**: 16 QBO Puerto Rico tax guides, SURI mapping, deductions, and municipal audit workflows.
- **`vault/pr_tax_erp/payroll_compliance/`**: PR Statutory Payroll rules, withholding brackets, SINOT, CFSE, SUTA, Choferil, and Ley 148 Christmas bonus models.
- **`vault/pr_tax_erp/government_clearance_compliance/`**: 15 Government Compliance Clearance Specifications (ASUME, CRIM, Hacienda SC 2888, OGPe Permiso Único, DTRH, State Good Standing).

## 2. Statutory Legal Citations Key
- **PR IRC / Rentas Internas**: Código de Rentas Internas de Puerto Rico de 2011 (Ley 1-2011, según enmendada).
- **Código Municipal**: Ley 107-2020 (Código Municipal de Puerto Rico).
- **Incentivos Fiscales**: Ley 60-2019 (Código de Incentivos de Puerto Rico).
- **Laboral & Nómina**: Ley 148-1969 (Bono Navidad), Ley 45-1935 (CFSE), Ley 139-1968 (SINOT), Ley 74-1956 (Desempleo), Ley 428-1950 (Choferil).
- **Corporativo & Notarial**: Ley 164-2009 (Corporaciones), Ley 161-2009 (Permisos OGPe), Ley 75-1987 (Ley Notarial).
