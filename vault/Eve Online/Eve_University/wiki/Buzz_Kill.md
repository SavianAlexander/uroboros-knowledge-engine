# Buzz Kill

Video: [Ishtar blitz](https://youtu.be/A-nEl4l1wYM), [Cerberus](https://youtu.be/cngQij0H3nI), [Dominix](https://youtu.be/g_jNL-VHW4E?t=6), [Gila](https://youtu.be/Kt83qk2sow8) 2021-04-23]

Single ungated pocket.

Important Note: All rats in this mission have 60%+ (often 70%+) lowest resistance (explosive) and resistance is same for shield and armor!

  - Tips:**
- Snipers can warp [fleet] at range. All groups and spawns will auto-aggro.
- Set your drones to passive to keep them from taking out trigger ships that could cause swarming, and watch them for frequent aggro.
- The last Hostile killed of a ship class triggers the next spawn unless marked otherwise.
- Following the trigger sequence is advisable for a sniper too, since even the Battleships move in fast.
- The Frigate spawn may not show up.
- Mission is flagged completed after you have killed the last Battleship.
- As usual divert Drone aggro by pulling them in for a second. Remote Shield Booster is an option for Sentries.
- The naming convention under the Overview for *Type* and *Name* are swapped. This results in *Angel Destroyer* being seen as the default name, instead of *Gistior Seizer*.

  - Optional Approach:**
- Use Micro Jump Drive as it's much easier to take everything out from a distance.

  - Blitz:**
- Kill the initial group, ignore the elite frigates, and focus on the reinforcement wave (triggered by the frigate).
- You may want to kill some or all of the elite cruisers and battlecruisers to reduce incoming damage.
- Kill the disruptor frigates and all the battleships, done.

1. # Loot, Salvage and Bounty
Loot: 1-14 million
Note: Loot for this mission is highly luck dependent. You can get several Clutch Restrained/ Pitfall Compact Warp Field Disruption Generators, worth around 2.7-2.8 million each. On one run I got 4 of these in total and ended up with 13.9 million ISK in loot as a result.
Salvage: ~1.4 mil
Bounty: ~10 mil
