# To the Front Lines

This mission *will* pass through at least one low security system (Sosala) and one occupied by Trigs (Gammel), and Edencom (Sassieko).

Check the route from Youl to Myyhera [here](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/route/2:Youl:Myyhera). Safe route is only two jumps more than shorter/unsafe, which passes through a couple more low-sec systems.

Transport cargo (2 m<sup>3</sup>) to a station 14 jumps away (safer route).
