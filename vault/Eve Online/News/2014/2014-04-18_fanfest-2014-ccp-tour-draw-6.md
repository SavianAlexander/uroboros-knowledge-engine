We are happy to annouce the sixth name that has been drawn from our pool of Fanfest ticket holders to be part of a group that will receive an exclusive tour of CCP Headquarters at Fanfest 2014!

This week's winner is Braaken of Axton Industries [AXTON].

The tour will be given by CCP Guard and CCP Falcon, at 11:00UTC on the morning of April 30th, 2014, the day before Fanfest, and the day of the unveiling of the [EVE Universe Monument.](//eve.com/monument) Those winners who come along for the tour will also be invited to eat lunch at CCP Headquarters, and may also be in for a few extra treats!

The names drawn so far are shown below, and subesequent names will be drawn from the pool of ticket holders on the following list of dates: 


- 14/03 - Luna Moses
- 21/03 - Ashmedi
- 28/03 - Artemis Septem
- 04/04 - Lord Xander
- 11/04 - Arik Redrum
- 18/04 - Braaken
- 25/04 - *Winner #7*
