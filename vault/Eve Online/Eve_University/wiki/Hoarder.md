1. # Summary
This ship is designed to fill a very exclusive niche: the transportation of munitions. Whether from corporate stock piles to the front lines of large-scale engagements or from the trade hub to eager patrons, this vessel is equipped with an extra-large ammo hold specialized for ammunition of all sorts in order to deliver the fuel of conflict efficiently to its destination.

The Gas Hold as the name suggests, holds huffed gas inside a hermetically sealed off part of the ship. The Hoarder holds less gas than the  whose mining hold can hold all harvestable resources.
