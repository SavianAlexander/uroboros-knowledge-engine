# Asteroid Belt Remnants

- Asteroid Belt Remnants** is an ore **anomaly** that spawns in 1.0 and 0.9 **security systems**, usually near "starter systems". A list of starter systems list can be found in the CCP helpdsek article  [Rookie Griefing](https://support.eveonline.com/hc/en-us/articles/203209712-Rookie-Griefing)

It only contains **Veldspar** asteroids and a few of its richer variants (II- and III-Grade). 

There are about 60 asteroids on the grid and they are quite scattered. This anomaly can also spawn in 0.8 and 0.7 but never below.
