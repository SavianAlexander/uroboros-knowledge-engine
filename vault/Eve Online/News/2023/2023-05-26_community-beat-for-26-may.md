## Down the Rabbit Hole EVE Online documentary


As you may recall, at Fanfest 2022 we were joined by famed Youtube documentarian Fredrik Knudsen of [Down the Rabbit Hole](https://www.youtube.com/@FredrikKnudsen)  fame. At that Fanfast we exhibited a teaser of the EVE Online documentary he's been working on for a few years at a theatre in downtown Reykjavik. That "teaser" went for about 2 hours and Fredrik has just announced that the full documentary is almost complete - likely to be released next month and with an expected runtime of nearly 6 hours!

## EVE Singapore


The first EVE player gathering in Singapore is happening in June! You can pick up a ticket on [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.sg/e/eve-singapore-tickets-601890008217). There'll be an opportunity to mingle with other EVE players, hear from local EVE experts and from CCP as well!
