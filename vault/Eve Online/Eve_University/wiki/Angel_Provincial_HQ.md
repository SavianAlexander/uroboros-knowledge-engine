# Angel Provincial HQ

- Angel Provincial HQ** is a null security unrated site found through **exploration**.

1. # Warp-in
Upon warping to the site you will arrive at an acceleration gate.
  1. # 1st pocket
Attacking the four Gist Arena Guardian battleships will trigger an additional wave each. 

Killing the battleships that spawn in reinforcement waves does not trigger reinforcements. 

Killing the Gist Gate Guardian or having a Domination Crystal Tag in your cargo unlocks the gate.

Do not try to blitz, it is really hard to tank.
