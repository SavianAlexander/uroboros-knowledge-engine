# Half Price Barge Program

- EVE University** members with the **student title** can take advantage of the Half Price Barge (HPB) Program, which offers up to 5 mining barges, per player, for half their Jita price. EVE University members can view the barge fits under Corporation Fittings in-game. This program is available in Korsiki - Horizon.

1. # Available ships
The following mining barges are available:
- Covetor (T1/T2 - Ore/Ice)
- Procurer (T1/T2 - Ore/Ice)
- Retriever (T1/T2 - Ore/Ice)

1. # Requesting a mining ship
To request a half price mining barge (limited to five per EVE University member - player not character)) complete this form: [Half Price Barges](https://auth.eveuniversity.org/forms/form/8/)

If the link above gives you a permission denied message and the form does not show up in the [list of available forms](https://auth.eveuniversity.org/forms/) on Alliance Auth, then you likely do not have the student title yet.

The requested barge will be contracted to you in Korsiki. This normally takes a few days, so please be patient.
