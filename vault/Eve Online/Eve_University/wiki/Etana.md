1. # Summary
Along with the **Cambion**, the **Etana** was awarded as one of the prizes for the tenth **Alliance Tournament**.

The Etana is essentially a **Basilisk** that's able to fit a covert ops cloak. It would be a compelling accompaniment to any black ops gang, however, its pricelessness means it will likely very rarely be seen in action.
