Attention: Costs do not include the **Command Centers** or its upgrades.

The defining aspect between an extractor and a factory colony is that extractor colonies have.... well, extractors. Factory planets rely on resources that have been imported from space. Hybrids are possible, but rare. The presented loadouts are considered working barebones. Modify to fit your preferences.

1. # Extractor colonies

  1. ## R0 -> P1 very Low resource planet
- minimum  skill level: III*

| Facilities | CPU | Power | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Extractor Control Units]] | 800 tf | 5200 MW | 90K ISK |
| Basic Industry Facilities]] | 600 tf | 2400 MW | 225K ISK |
| Launchpad]] | 3600 tf | 700 MW | 900K ISK |
| Total | 5000 tf | 8300 MW | 1215K ISK |

Upgrading: Remaining and additional fitting space is for 12 to 19 **Extractor Heads** depending on skillevel. On higher skilllevels in Command Center Upgrades, an additional **Storage Facility** as buffer could also be considered. A second **Extractor control unit** is only useful if more than 10 Extractor heads are needed. Otherwise it's better to only build one and use the fitting space on additional Extractor Heads instead.
