# Angel Gas Processing Site

- Angel Gas Processing Site** is a **hacking** site found by scanning in Low Security systems with **Angel Cartel** presence. The site appears to Probes as a gas site. This site appears in the Heimatar region, especially in the Hed constellation.
1. # Walkthrough

Ungated pocket not in deadspace. Sentries make it hard to combat hack the sites and ignore the rats.
