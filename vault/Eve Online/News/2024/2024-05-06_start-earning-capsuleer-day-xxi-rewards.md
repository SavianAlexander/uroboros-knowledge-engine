### Reward-hungry capsuleers,


As New Eden continues to blaze with fireworks and festive pilots in celebration of the 21st anniversary of EVE Online, make sure you don’t miss out on the massive rewards coming down the pipe during Capsuleer Day XXI. This event has brought both new and seasoned pilots together in pursuit of formidable challenges and bountiful loot across the cluster.
Today is the best opportunity to start accumulating your daily logins so you don’t miss out on the monumental cache of 650,000 Skill Points on offer, as well as exclusive Capsuleer Day SKINs for ships like the Praxis, Gnosis, Mekubal, and Vexor.

To help you get into the right Capsuleer Day mood, this Friday’s Live2Feed stream on [CCPTV](https://www.twitch.tv/ccp) will be extra celebratory, and viewers will have the chance to snatch up great loot in the form of Twitch drops, so make sure to tune in at 14:00 UTC.

If you need some tips to make the most of your Capsuleer Day experience, check out [this helpful guide](https://youtu.be/sz-UQScYnHI) from Nth Dimensional.
