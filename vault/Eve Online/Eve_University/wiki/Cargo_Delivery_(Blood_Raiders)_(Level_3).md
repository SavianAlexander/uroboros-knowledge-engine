# Cargo Delivery (Blood Raiders) (Level 3)

- Blitz:** Warp-in, grab the goods, warp out.

  1. # Warp-in
The goods are in the container named Warehouse.

On warp-in, no rats are present. Wave one appears when picking up the goods from the container.

Wave 2 cruiser neut for 5-8gj/s depending on variant.
