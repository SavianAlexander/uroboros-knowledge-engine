# Bonus Skill Point Weekend (2021)

- Bonus Skill Point Weekend** is a login campaign that runs from 20th August to 23rd August 2021. The campaign rewards up to 15,000 **Skill Points** for Alpha clones and up to 50,000 **Skill Points** for Omega clones.
