1. # Summary
A radically different ship to its regular **Scorpion** counterpart, the **Scorpion Navy Issue** (commonly abbreviated **CNS**) is a combat-oriented missile boat, with more in common with a **Raven** than a Scorpion. With its hull bonus to resistances and 8 mid slots it can be fit with a monster tank and still have slots left over for tackle and utility modules. The Scorpion doesn't have the range bonus of the Raven, but with missile supports to 4 can still fire cruise missiles 120km.

The Navy Scorpion and **Navy Raven** are similar ships and are often compared. The Navy Scorpion has a stronger tank and is easier to fit, while the Navy Raven has a longer range and does more damage to smaller ships.
