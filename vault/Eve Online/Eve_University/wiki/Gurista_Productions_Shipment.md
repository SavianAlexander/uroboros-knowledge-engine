# Gurista Productions Shipment

- Gurista Productions Shipment** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **Gurista Outpost**.
1. # Walkthrough

  1. # First Location

|}

If there's no Faction cruiser in the pocket, jump through the Newly Constructed Acceleration Gate to get an escalation.

  1. # Second Location

|}

  1. # Third Location
You land on an acceleration gate. There is no reason to fly through it unless it is for the escalation.

|}
  1. ## First Pocket
No enemies present, just a wrecked stargate in a brown cloud.

  1. # Fourth Location (Null sec)
This location can be in null security space but this can vary. You land on an acceleration gate.
  1. ## First Pocket

|}
The acceleration gate is 45 km from warp-in.

|}
  1. ## Second Pocket
The Dread Pithum Vindicator does not have significant tank, and can be taken out fairly easily. It does somewhat higher damage with its turrets and missiles, though.

|}
