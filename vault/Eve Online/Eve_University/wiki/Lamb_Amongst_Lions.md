# Lamb Amongst Lions

No aggro upon warp-in.

Spawn triggers:
- Wave 1 spawns when initial defenders are agressed.
- Rest of the waves spawn after the previous wave has been destroyed. Ships MIGHT have triggers, but the writer went from frigates to BS's.
