# Lunar New Year (2023)

- Lunar New Year** (a.k.a. Season of red stargazing) is a limited time login campaign, lasting from 19 January to 31 January 2023, **downtime** to downtime to "celebrate the Lunar New Year".

1. # Log-In campaign
Capsuleers must log in for 7 out of the 12 days to collect all rewards. Alpha Clones could claim 11,000 **Skill Points**, while Omega Clones have access to an additional 29,000 Skill Points.
