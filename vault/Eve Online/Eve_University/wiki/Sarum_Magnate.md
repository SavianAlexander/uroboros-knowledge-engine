1. # Summary
Sarum Magnate is a special edition of the **Magnate** given out during the 2012 Christmas season similar to the **Sukuuvestaa Heron**, **Inner Zone Shipping Imicus**, and the **Vherokior Probe**. It appears to be functionally equivalent to the standard Magnate but with a different skin and additional flavor text.

All models were converted to a regular Magnate and a corresponding Sarum **SKIN** with the Mosaic release in April 2015. Despite this, the model was given a unique quote with the release of the Revenant expansion in November 2024, over nine years after all copies of the ship were converted out of existence.
