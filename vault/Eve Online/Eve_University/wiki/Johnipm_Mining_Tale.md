# Johnipm Mining Tale

- *Date: xx-xx-2008*
- *Place: Stormwind City, Azeroth*

I don't remember the full details but all I can remember is that he was caught making out with Lady Katrana.

He has been sentenced to not only death but also his soul stripped of his body and sent into the vastness of space, never to be inside a human body ever again.

- *Date: 10-09-2008*
- *Place: New Eden*
- *Johnipm was born a Desteis true blood.*

Somehow his soul got inserted into a medical clone at a cloning facility, sent to Art and trade institute to specialize in being an entrepreneur. After finishing the academy he was sent into the open space once again.

Déjà vu it seems at the time for him.

Trading seemed the right thing to do at that time. As he had a degree and thought he would conquer the Lonetrek region. Being still a newbie to it all a fellow pilot offered him an opportunity to get more training. Get him ready to defend his cargo earned, for it has been a week and his only ship was blown up into pieces.

He decided to join this so called University, here is where his new career started. The 2nd week into the uni there was this mass gathering at one of the many asteroid belts within the home system Korsiki. At first he thought this looks like a warzone or what. The sky was lit by lasers hitting rocks in every direction. Still in his Merlin, his Kestrel was blown out the skies. He was given a free fully equipped Bantam.

For Johnipm mining was boring the first 2 times. His first paycheck came in after 3 days and he was flying around in his pod of joy. For him it’s now more then only mining. It’s the time you get to spend with fellow unitas. Time of piece and have some fun. Next to that they need the materials to make new frigates for the fleet.

Since that first day he dreamed of once being in a Hulk. Yes a hulk but then one day. He’s a proud owner of an retriever now, Full of dust and still in korsiki waiting to be transferred to our new home world. Due to a glitch when transferring his out-casted soul into a med clone, which meant that he could only have 15K of SP. This glitch has finally been corrected and Johnipm is once again flying around in New Eden. Ready to help the new uni members on their first mining expedition.
