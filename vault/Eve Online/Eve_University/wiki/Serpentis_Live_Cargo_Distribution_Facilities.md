# Serpentis Live Cargo Distribution Facilities

- Serpentis Live Cargo Distribution Facilities** is a **DED Rated** **combat site** belonging to the **Serpentis Corporation** pirate faction. It can be found via **exploration** by scanning anomalies in high security and low security systems. **Destroyer**s (and their **Tech 3** equivalents) are the maximum ship class allowed to enter the site. Completing this complex may award the player with valuable Coreli B-type and Shadow Serpentis **faction modules**.
