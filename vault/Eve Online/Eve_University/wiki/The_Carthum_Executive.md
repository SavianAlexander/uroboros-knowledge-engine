# The Carthum Executive

This mission will pass through at least one low security system (Sosala) and one occupied by Trigs (Gammel), and Edencom (Sassieko).

Check the route from Myyhera to Ekid [here](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/route/2:Myyhera:Ekid). Safe route is only two jumps more than shorter/unsafe, which passes through a couple more low-sec systems.

Transport cargo (3 m<sup>3</sup>) to a station 15 jumps away (Safer route).

Note: The reward skillbook **Diplomacy**, for some reason, is delivered where you started the mission 15 jumps back, not in Ekid where you complete it.
