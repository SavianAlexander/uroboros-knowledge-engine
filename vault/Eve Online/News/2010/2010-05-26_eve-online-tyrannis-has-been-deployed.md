## Overview
EVE Online: Tyrannis deployment is complete, Tranquility is open for business, and patch notes are available for review. The Tyrannis feedback thread and issues thread are also available for you to let us know what you think of the latest expansion, or to make us aware of any issues you encounter.

EVE Online: Tyrannis deployment is complete, Tranquility is open for business, and [patch notes](//www.eveonline.com/updates/patchnotes.asp?patchlogID=205) are available for review. The Tyrannis [feedback thread](//www.eveonline.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=1324772) and [issues thread](//www.eveonline.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=1324770) are also available for you to let us know what you think of the latest expansion, or to make us aware of any issues you encounter.
