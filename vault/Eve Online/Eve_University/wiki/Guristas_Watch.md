# Guristas Watch

- Guristas Watch** is an **Unrated Complex** found in high security space through exploration.

1. # Walkthrough
There are two pockets in this site. Due to the site having a lot of triggers monitor any drones closely so they do not shoot the trigger ship on accident.

  1. # First Pocket

It is possible to skip this room by shooting the triggers and destroying the Pithum Militant Commander, but expect high incoming damage.
