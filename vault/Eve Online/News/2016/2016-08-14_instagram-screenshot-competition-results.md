Greetings to all screenshot-loving capsuleers!

A few weeks ago [we announced](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=6565523#post6565523) that we would be hosting a screenshot competition on Instagram. The submission preiod ended on 5th of August and I must admit, it was very hard to select the winners as all screenshots were absolutely amazing!

As you might already know if you were watching the o7 show today, we've selected 10 best submissions and also added another 10 extra prizes due to an astonishing interest. 

Here are some numbers about the competition and of course your list of winners:


- We've received 1,083 screenshots in total!
- User [@razorieneve](//www.instagram.com/razorieneve/) submitted 149 screenshots alone!
- User [@generalstargazer](//www.instagram.com/generalstargazer) was very close to beating that record with 128 submissions total!
