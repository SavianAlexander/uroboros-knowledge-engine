# Mercenary Tactical Operation

- Mercenary Tactical Operations** (MTOs) are combat sites that are available to the owner of a **Mercenary Den**.

MTOs spawn approximately every 3 days from the time they are onlined.

Mercenary Dens were added as part of the Revenant Expansion , intended for a solo player in a T1 or Faction **cruiser**, and are commonly run in a **Vexor Navy Issue** or a **Gila**.

1. # Development and Anarchy levels
Mercenary Dens have two parameters, Development and Anarchy.

  1. # Development
Development is generated over time, and when the owner successfully completes Mercenary Tactical Operations. Higher Development levels will cause the mercenary den to increase production yield of **Informorph Decryption Key** over time.

Successfully completing a Mercenary Tactical Operation increases the Development value by 10 points, or grants 350 bonus infomorphs if already at max level 4.

Development passively increases by 5 points approximately every 24 hours.

| + Infomorph Production Per Development Level |
| :--- |
| Development Level | Development Progress | Production Bonus | IDK/hr |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0 | 0-19 | 0% (base rate) | 65-100 |
| 1 | 20-39 | +15% | 68-105 |
| 2 | 40-69 | +25% | 71-110 |
| 3 | 70-99 | +35% | 74-115 |
| 4 (max) | 100 | +50% | 78-120 |

  1. # Anarchy
Anarchy is also generated over time and decreases when the owner successfully completes Mercenary Tactical Operations. Higher Anarchy levels will cause its host Skyhook to siphon workforce production, preventing the workforce from being used by the sovereignty owner.

Anarchy passively increases by 5 points approximately every 24 hours.

| + Workforce Penalty per Anarchy Level |
| :--- |
| Anarchy Level | Anarchy Progress | Workforce Disruption |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0 | 0-19 | 0% (no penalty) |
| 1 | 20-39 | 0% (no penalty) |
| 2 | 40-69 | -10% |
| 3 | 70-99 | -20% |
| 4 (max) | 100 | -40% |

1. # Sites
MTOs come in the form of the following sites:
- **Drifter Muster Point**
- **Rogue Drone Emergence**
- **Sansha Raiding Party**
