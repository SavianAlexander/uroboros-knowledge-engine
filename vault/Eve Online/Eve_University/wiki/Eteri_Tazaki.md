# Eteri Tazaki

- Eteri Tazaki** is a level 3 agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located in Otitoh - Devil's Dig Site.

1. # Foul Play - Organizing The Search (1 of 3)

Transport 1x Holoreels (0.5 m3) to Poinen IV - Moon 8 - Sukuuvestaa Corporation Factory.

1. # Foul Play - Ryoke's Friend (2 of 3)

Provide 1x Zvarin Karsha (1 m3) to agent. Note that the system in which Zvarin is located is low-sec. You can find her/him near Ihakana VI – State and Region Bank Depository. You have to find "Caldari Tactical Outpost".Once you arrive, look at the table below.

  - Note:** Despite a difference in name this **Ihakana VI – State and Region Bank Investment Bank** appears to be the neighborhood where Zvarin is found.
