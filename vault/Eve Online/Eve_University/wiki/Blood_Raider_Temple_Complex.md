# Blood Raider Temple Complex

- Blood Raider Temple Complex** is an **Unrated Complex** found by scanning in Null Security systems with Blood Raider presence.
1. # Walkthrough
The site is similar to **Dark Blood Fleet Staging Point**. It is much harder than its counterpart, though, and should only be attempted by skilled players in a ship with a very strong tank. Warping to the site you will land at an ancient acceleration gate, which permits T2 **Battleships**.
