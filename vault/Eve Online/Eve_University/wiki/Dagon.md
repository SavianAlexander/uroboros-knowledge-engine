1. # Tactics
The Dagon is unlike any Fax. It is designed to be used in the presence of enemy capital ships so that its Capital Nosferatu Modules can steal capacitor from them and fuel its own local and remote repair modules. The Dagon is less effective in the presence of only enemy subcapital ships as Heavy Energy Nosferatus transfer significantly less capacitor and Capital Nosferatus are effectively useless against subcapitals. 

Dagons do not see much use in nulsec or lowsec due to the inherent weak tank of FAXs compared to other capital ships and the Dagon's very high cost compared to other FAXs. The Dagon is primarily used in wormhole space where there are significantly fewer threats to FAXs and where the small advantage gained by a faction FAX is very valuable.

1. # Notes
Dagon (or Dagan) was a major deity in ancient Syria and Mesopotamia. He seems to have been associated with prosperity and kingship, and may have been a divine ancestor figure for other gods.
