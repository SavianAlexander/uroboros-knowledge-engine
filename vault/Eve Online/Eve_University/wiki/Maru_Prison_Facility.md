  - Maru Prison Facility** is a static **Minmatar COSMOS** complex found in Hjoramold system. The site needs to be scanned down with probes and is found around Hjoramold X. Alternatively you can warp to Hjoramold X - Moon 17.  It allows battleship hulls to enter, but a cruiser should be sufficient.

1. # Site info
On warp into the acceleration gate into the actual landmark, a Maru Prison Guard is waiting. Needs to be destroyed to allow the gate to function. Using the acceleration gate leads into a pocket with a bunch of frigates and a cruiser. The Maru Hacker on the warp in point webs your ship, the rest is about 50km away organized in 3 packs. The outer packs can be aggroed individually, shooting an NPC of the middle pack aggros all.
