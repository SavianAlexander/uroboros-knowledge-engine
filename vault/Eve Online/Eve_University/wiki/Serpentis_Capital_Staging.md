# Serpentis Capital Staging

Serpentis Capital Staging is an escalation available from Serpentis anomalies in both nullsec and lowsec.

The site contains a variety of subcaps as well as three Serpentis Dreadnaughts. The Dreadnaughts warp disrupt. The frigates in the subcap support fleet also warp disrupt.

Each Dreadnaught has a bounty of 60m ISK. The total bounties for the site are the region of 295m ISK. The Dreads also drop capital parts worth around 50m ISK.

Once the three Dreadnaughts have been destroyed the site is marked as complete.
