The Caldari Gaming commission has been a flurry with activity recently in what analysts determine can only be the preparations for the COLOSSUS race finals.

When we spoke to Airas Sukela, head of the commission he would only state the following. “Yes the commission is working hard - however we are not ready to release further information at this time”.

Many a pod faring pilot believes the final to be well overdue and the commission would be good to release more information quickly.
