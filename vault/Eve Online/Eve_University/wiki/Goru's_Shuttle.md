1. # Summary
All shuttles are functionally identical, and have no skill requirements in order to fly, so choose based on price or appearance.

Beyond moving yourself from one location to another rapidly, shuttles are also useful for carrying small items due to a 10m<sup>3</sup> cargo hold, and a small number of missions where combat is not required (and you do not wish to risk a more expensive ship). However, be aware that while you align rapidly in a shuttle, it is possible to be instantly destroyed by some ships before entering warp, so carrying high value items such as implants, skill books, and boosters may be ill advised.

1. # Notes
The Goru's Shuttle can be received as a drop in COSMOS site located at Otitoh VII Moon 19 and one is required in a **Caldari COSMOS** mission. If you want to get one from there you will either leave your ship in site as you take the shuttle or a ship with large enough cargohold to take the shuttle in.
