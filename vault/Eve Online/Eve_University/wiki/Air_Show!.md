# Air Show!

- Air Show!** is a five-part mission involving both courier objectives and combat against **Rogue Drones**.

1. # Part 1

1. # Part 2

The pilot will be directed to a deadspace pocket with collidable objects, but no hostiles.

There are 8 beacons in total. Beacons 1, 4, and 7 may require some amount of **manual piloting** to get to, as they are located in and around a hollow asteroid. It can also be helpful to ensure that beacons are visible in the overview.

1. # Part 3

The pilot will be directed to a deadspace pocket with no hostiles.

Warp in, move to the damaged pod, pick up the stranded pilot, and return.
