# EVE University Management

1. # Chief Executive Officer

| - | 96px|link=]]  
Laura Karpinski | The CEO is responsible for the overall leadership and direction of EVE University. They ensure that the university’s long-term mission, strategy, and vision are being pursued effectively. The CEO represents the organisation externally, managing high-level relationships with alliances and the wider EVE Online community. They work closely with the leadership team to ensure that all departments align with the university’s core objectives, and they make the final decisions on strategic matters. |

1. # Director Team

| - | 96x96px]] 
Spanky Ikkala | **Director of Education**
- Focus: Helping members learn, grow, and develop their skills across all areas of the university.*

- Find and support trainers, whether they’re guest speakers or part of the regular teaching team.
- Make sure training programmes actually achieve results and are engaging and relevant.
- Ensure that education is woven into all university activities—not just in classes, but through fleets, events, and other content.
- Team up with the Director of Communications and Outreach to bring in external speakers or training events.
- Keep refining the training structure to maintain high standards and accessibility. |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

Dunar Dolorgiet | **Director of New Player Experience***Focus: Overseeing the new player journey at EVE University, ensuring it is welcoming, clear, and well-connected to the wider community.*

- Develop and run orientation classes and informal social events to help new players settle in.
- Work with Education, SIGs, and Intake to align resources, messaging, and early engagement opportunities.
- Review and improve the clarity and delivery of new player communications and touchpoints.
- Keep new player-facing wiki content accurate, concise, and useful.
- Build a warm, inclusive experience that helps players find their place in EVE University. |  |

Idris Du'Vet | **Director of Community Development**
- Focus: Supporting and growing the EVE University community through engaging, inclusive, and player-led initiatives.*

- Drive the creation and support of SIGs, social groups, and interest-based communities.
- Develop initiatives to make community involvement more accessible across all Uni spaces.
- Mentor community leaders and organisers, fostering a welcoming and active environment.
- Collaborate with other leadership to shape and support the Uni’s broader community strategy. |  |
| left|thumb|100x100px|Professor Amfion]] | **Director of Combat and PVP Operations**
- Focus: Supporting and developing PVP in EVE University, making combat activities accessible, engaging, and aligned with our core mission of education.*
- Encourage participation in PVP across all experience levels, helping members build confidence and get involved.
- Support the development of a welcoming and inclusive PVP culture within EVE University.
- Help shape and coordinate PVP activities, ensuring there are regular opportunities for members to take part.
- Oversee the direction and accessibility of core doctrines.
- Work with other departments to ensure PVP content is well-integrated with education, events, and community initiatives. |  |
