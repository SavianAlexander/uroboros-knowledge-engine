# Nilla Elermare

- Nilla Elermare** is a level 3 **Gallente COSMOS** Agent located at the **Contested Gallente Roden Shipyard's Outpost** Beacon in the Fluekele System and requires 4.00 Gallente Federation Faction Standing.

She gives one mission only. Her mission is a prerequisite for completing missions for **Aminn Flosin** Missions.
