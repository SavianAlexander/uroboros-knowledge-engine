# Skills:Industry

Prior to the **Odyssey** expansion, the Industry skill category contained the following skills (all of which have since been moved to other categories). This page is kept on the Wiki as a reference to older pilots. 

- (formerly called "Production Efficiency" and "Material Efficiency") - Reduction to material requirements needed for production
- - Further training in the operation of multiple factories
- Arkonor Processing - Reduction in Arkonor refining waste
