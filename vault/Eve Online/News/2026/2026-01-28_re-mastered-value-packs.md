A first iteration on the Mastery Packs, [which were introduced last year](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/mastering-new-value-packs#h2-3), has arrived in the EVE Store, offering you the final boost to complete your training or pivot into uncharted territory. 

The Mastery Pack offerings have been updated to include [recent changes made to MCT cost](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/value-over-fomo-omega-you-and-store-clean-up#h2-4) and now feature content of [Undock](https://store.eveonline.com/product/undock-boost-bundle/verm-8048), [Afterburn](https://store.eveonline.com/product/afterburn-boost-bundle/verm-8049) and [Warp](https://store.eveonline.com/product/warp-boost-bundle/verm-8050) boost bundles, which means they include more SP and the best Accelerator. 

As a result, some prices have been changed, but the packs offer more value for money – up to 40% savings – and will be available at any time. 

Along with the pack refresh, the purchasing count has been reset, so those who got last year’s packs can purchase these again. As before, the packs can be purchased once per account in each store, including resellers, and will arrive in the New Eden Store soon.
