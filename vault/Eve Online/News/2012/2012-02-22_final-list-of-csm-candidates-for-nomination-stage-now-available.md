Per the election [schedule](//community.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&nbid=3386), the list of candidates who qualified for the nomination stage of the seventh Council of Stellar Management elections is [now available](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=72885&find=unread).
