# Angel Military Complex

The **Angel Military Complex** is an **unrated complex** **cosmic signature** found in **Angel** infested regions.

  - Blitz for ranged ship (need lock and weapon range > 65-70 km)**: Warp to first pocket, kill 4 stasis towers, kill Gist Gate Commander to unlock the gate. Warp through the gate to second pocket. Kill first stasis tower (explode with 3k damage, be careful), recharge tank if needed, kill second stasis tower. Flee out from Angel Control Tower in 30-40 km range, shot it. Kill all tackling frigates, kill Domination boss, take loot, warp out.
