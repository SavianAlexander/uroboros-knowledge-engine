# Operation Spring Cleaning

- Operation Spring Cleaning** is an **expedition** that escalates from **Angel Fortress** and consists of 4 parts.

1. # Walkthrough
This escalation consists of four parts.

1. # First Part
Warp in. If the Domination commander is there: kill him, possibly get escalation. Else (or If didn't get escalation): Use go to the next room to possibly get the escalation.

  1. ## Warp-in

|}
Loot may contain: 
High-grade Savior Epsilon Blueprint
Domination MWD module

|}

  1. ## First room
----
Approach either the station or the wreck for a possibility for the escalation to trigger.

|}

1. # Second Location
Ungated pocket. 

|}
The second wave is passive until attacked or approached.

|}

1. # Third Location
Again, another ungated pocket. Group 3 warps off after a short time and can be ignored.

|}

1. # Fourth and Last Location
You land at an acceleration gate at warp-in. No enemies are present.
  1. # First Pocket

|}
  1. # Second Pocket
Enemies are approximately 40 km from the warp-in point. Gist Domination Murderer has a short turret optimal of 21 km, but fires Nova cruise missiles that reach out to 120 km and deal approx. 450 points of damage. It deals Thermal and Kinetic damage with its turrets (with a slant towards thermal) and pure Explosive damage with its missiles. It also recharges shield at around 500 DPS.

|}

1. # Loot
The Domination battleships drop faction ammo and dog tags, and may also drop Domination faction modules or even implants.  The Gist Domination Murderer in the final location may drop Gist A-Type battleship modules and possibly a rare **Machariel** BPC.
