# After The Seven - "Decision"

<noinclude>
This mission is issued by a Security division agent and is part 5 of the 5 part mission chain **After The Seven**</noinclude>

Upon warp-in, Locced (Destroyer) will be within 20km. If you attack him, 2 extra Guristas frigates will spawn at 25km. There is also a bribe container . If you approach the cargo container Locced will despawn. If you attack Locced the cargo container will despawn.

Kill Locced or open the cargo container to complete the mission.
