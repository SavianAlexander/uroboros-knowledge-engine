1. # Summary
The **Cenotaph** is the **Deathless Circle** faction **battlecruiser**. Built to brawl, it features a best-in-class seven mid slots (shared with the **Ferox Navy Issue**), a shield booster bonus, and a faction-unique stasis webifier resistance bonus. Support for a covert ops cloaking devices also gives it tactical opportunities unique to a battlecruiser. However, its split weapons hardpoints means that while it has a lot of firepower potential, it can not significantly lean on any one weapons system in particular.

Aesthetically, the Cenotaph marries the shortened body of a Caldari **Ferox** with the wings of the Minmatar **Tornado** and **Hurricane**, and features a gray and red paintjob.

Blueprint copies of the Cenotaph can be acquired in **Zarzakh**.
