This mission is in ungated normal space.  Players that aren't warp disrupted or scrambled can warp normally.  NPC distances and directions are relative to the mission's warp-in point.  If a greater initial distance is desired, then warp to the mission "to within" a distance from some bookmark or celestial to the west.  Warping "to within" 20 kilometers from the east will minimize initial distance to enemies, if so desired.
