This service is for hauling to and from the Main community in Korsiki for the benefit of the EVE University community. 

1. # Service Cost

The hauling service cost is greatly subsidized for use to all Unistas. There is a mandated contribution rate which can be calculated on Alliance Auth based on volume and the value of the collateral. This helps your hauler cover the operation of the service such as cynos and fuel. If you are a new Unista and are short on funds, please contact the hauling department to have your goods moved at no cost to you. The shortfall in funds is generously covered by the Uni. 

As this is a subsidized service, we ask that it is not used for profit. This service is designed for the benefit of our communities supporting teaching and learning mission.

1. # Packaging Limits
If you have combined volume greater than 60,000 m³, please create multiple packages.
- High Sec to High Sec: Up to 60,000 m³ or 1.4B ISK
- High Sec to Low Sec: Up to 60,000 m³ and no ISK limit
- Low Sec to High Sec: Up to 60,000 m³ or 1.4B ISK
<references></references>

1. # Packaging Requirements

- **Your contract must not have any assembled containers, modules, or ships.** Right-click everything, select "repackage". If you do end up shipping assembled containers, modules, or ships, significant effort is required to get the contents back to you once accepted as these cannot be shipped and must be exchanged back in the originating station.
- **High volume PI (P0 or P1) or cheap minerals (e.g., tritanium) is not supported** for transport.

1. # Supported Stations and Structures

- [HUB] Jita IV - Moon 4 - Caldari Navy Assembly Plant
- <s>[HSC] Korsiki - Horizon</s> (Temporarily Suspended)
- [LSC] Isbrabata VIII - Moon 5 - Republic Security Services Assembly Plant

The Hauling Service supports all the stations listed above. Ad-hoc shipments to alternate stations may be possible if a hauler is available and in the area - post a thread in #support and tag a '@Hauling Officer' to see if that can be arranged, there are usually willing haulers. This would then be outside the Hauling service and an agreement between the hauler and the contractor. The package should be contracted to the hauler directly in this case including collateral and reward. Uni and Hauling leadership can help verify trustworthiness of the volunteers. 

  - Frivolous, low ISK density packages will not be hauled at the discretion of the hauling service.** In this case the contract will be failed and the package contracted back at the source location to the contractor with an explanation.
1. # Shipping Schedule

Trips are generally done when one or more of these conditions are met:
- Enough m³ to fill up a ship in either direction
- Enough contracts to warrant immediate delivery

We will also use our judgement on what packages we combine and which we ship alone; we will not exceed internally directed cargo value limits. Please consider hauling lower value or small volume loot yourself with guidance from the community. If something is of a timing priority, we encourage utilizing priority shipments with PushX or Red/Purple/Black Frog.

1. # How to setup contracts

- Pickup Station must be one of the approved stations above
- Make a courier contract
  - Make it a private contract.
  - Send the contract to "Ivy League Transport" (corporation).
  - Select the items to be included in the Courier Contract with the limits stated above.
  - Destination Station must be one of the approved stations above.
  - Collateral in ISK, must be roughly equal to the est. value of your cargo.
  - Set the shipping cost (reward) from the freight calculator on Alliance Auth.
  - Set expiration to maximum (4 weeks).
  - Set days to complete to maximum (365 days - the box is very small on the UI but this can be typed or pasted in).
  - Enter in the description field
    - The value of the cargo (e.g., 600M). This lets us know whether we can combine packages in a trip.
      - Round UP to the nearest million
      - Abbreviate Million (M), Billion (B)
      - Examples: 1.25B, 233M, 1M
    - If you are shipping via an alt, you must provide your E-UNI main (e.g., "650M (Xobekk Gao)") - note that due to game mechanics, your alt may not see an E-UNI structure. If your alt is not able to select the destination, then try it using your E-UNI main
