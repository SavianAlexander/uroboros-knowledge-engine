# TANK!

This setup focuses on the Heavy being able to soak a lot of damage while dishing damage out at all ranges.

1. Heavy
     Weapon: HMG
     Grenade: Locus Grenade
     Role: Close Combat
2. Heavy
     Weapon: Assault Rifle
     Grenade: AV Grenade
     Role: Mid range/AA
3. Heavy
     Weapon: Laser Rifle
     Grenade: Flux Grenade
     Role: Long range
4. Logistics 
     Weapon: Swarm Launcher/Forge Gun
     Grenade: Any really
     Misc Equipment: Repair Tool, Nanite injector, Nanohive, Drop Uplink (these are in order of importance so if you have 3 slots take the first 3)
     Role:  Healing/Reviving/Resupplying/AA

Squad Tactics.

This squad while slow is great for defending and slowly pushing. The heavy suits while sacrificing speed give the squad a longer survival time. 
Each of the Heavies overlap with another for damage distances meaning at least 2 are in a good range at all times. The grenades are spread out as to be able to handle
all situations of opponents including vehicles. The Logi stays back and focuses on healing and supply while the Heavies just dish out the damage. Only vulnerability is to extreme range (snipers)
