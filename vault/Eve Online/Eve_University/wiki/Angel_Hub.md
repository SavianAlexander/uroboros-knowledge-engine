# Angel Hub

The Angel Hub is a **combat anomaly** found in **Angel** infested regions. Estimated payout 16-22.5 mil. Easily done with a Faction, T2 or T3 Cruiser or sniper Battleship.  

Note: Domination Spawns are much rarer when you are piloting a battleship.  

With the initial group and 3 triggered spawns, a total of 34 to 36 ships in the Small Armory variant and 16- 22 ships in the Dirty Shipyard variant (in parenthesis).

1. # Walkthough
There are  two different variant of this site differentiated by a structure in the site, the Small Armory or the Dirty Shipyard. Ship count may vary as indicated. The site is an open Deadspace pocket, one may warp to preferred distance.
