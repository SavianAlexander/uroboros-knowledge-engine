# Serpentis Refuge

- Serpentis Refuge** is an unrated **combat site** belonging to the **Serpentis Corporation** pirate faction. It can be found via **exploration** as a **cosmic anomaly** within systems owned by the **Gallente Federation** or occupied by the Serpentis Corporation. As an ungated complex, the limitations as to which ships can enter this site are tied to the security rating of the system where the anomaly spawns (up to T2 **battleship**s for high-security systems, and **titans** in low-security and null-security systems).

Completing this complex may award the player with valuable Shadow Serpentis **faction modules** and ammunition. This combat site consists of a single ungated pocket of space containing a variety of **frigate**- and **destroyer**-sized enemy ships, as well as two batteries. Destroying all hostile ships may escalate into the **DED rated** **Serpentis Narcotic Warehouses** complex.

1. # Warp-in
The site is an open deadspace pocket that can be warped to at any range.
