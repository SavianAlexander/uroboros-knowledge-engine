The schedule for the championship pre-trials has been posted on the [player website](//myeve.eve-online.com/championship/schedule.asp).

Check to see the hours for the house you have ( or plan to ) sign up for and see if they match your waking / playing hours. Note that the schedule *may* change, and if it does we will post the changes on the player forums. The times listed are when the fighting starts, you may still need to wait until it is your turn. Exact line-up will be posted after the elections.
