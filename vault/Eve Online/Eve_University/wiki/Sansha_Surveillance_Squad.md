# Sansha Surveillance Squad

- Sansha Surveillance Squad** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **Provisional Sansha Outpost**.
1. # Walkthrough

  1. # First Location
If the True Sansha ship does not spawn, take the acceleration gate.

|}

|}
  1. ## First Pocket
There are two large groups of rats at initial warp-in 50 km away. The larger right group will warp off after ~30 seconds. Destroy the Sansha Security Station to trigger escalation.

|}

|}

  1. # Second Location
Once again, take the acceleration gate if commander cruiser does not spawn.

|}

|}
  1. ## First Pocket
Two groups of rats are initially present 50 km away. One will warp away when you warp in.

|}

  1. # Third Location
Similar number of rats. Warp disrupting rats should be killed first so you can initiate a safe warp-out if the need arises.

|}

|}
  1. ## First Pocket
Simple warp-in may trigger escalation. There are no notable structures in this room.

|}

  1. # Fourth Location
Ungated pocket. You land on a colorful mixture of ruins, structures, and rocks. No rats initially present.  

The Jeroon structure itself triggers a smartbomb wave when initially attacked which reaches out to 30 km.  The structure itself is fairly easy to destroy with only 16k HP.

|}

|}

|}

|}

|}

The Sansha's Berserkers/Demons should be destroyed first, they deal high damage compared to normal rats and have more tank, though not as much as normal elite frigates. Kazah Durn hits hard also, out to 100 km.

|}
