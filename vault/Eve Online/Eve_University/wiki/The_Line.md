# The Line

{{whcobox|
- Note that the final wave will most likely neut you out; passive tank is highly recommended.
- Wave 1 is to the right of the spawn depending on which way you warped into.
- Wave 2 spawns about 90 km away.
- Wave 3 spawns on the other side of the site at about 70-80 km.
- Note that wave 3 may trigger early; this has been confirmed when all but one trigger ship in wave 2 was destroyed.
}}

The site is composed of a large collection of rocks and debris. It is about 100-150 km wide and long; enemies will spawn fairly far away.
