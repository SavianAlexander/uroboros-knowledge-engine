Dear EVE players. Together, we have managed to set a new record for simultaneous attendance in-game. The new record is 6.446, up from the old record of 6.301. This just goes to show what we´ve known all along, that EVE has a bright future ahead of it and that the best is certainly yet to come. Great work everybody!
