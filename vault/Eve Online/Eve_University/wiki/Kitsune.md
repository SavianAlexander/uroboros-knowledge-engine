1. # Summary

The Kitsune is a T2 ECM frigate based on the **Griffin**. Unlike the Griffin, it has a range bonus to its jammers as well as jam strength, allowing it to operate from further out and make jams more likely to land.

1. # Tactics

The Kitsune occupies an awkward niche: it costs many times more than the price of the Tech 1 **Blackbird** ECM cruiser, which can supply a similar or better jam range with a stronger tank. The Kitsune's main advantages are its speed, agility, smaller signature radius, and fast lock time for getting the first jam in. These qualities might make it more useful for small, highly-mobile groups rather than large fleets.

1. # Notes

The Kitsune is named after a type of intelligent fox spirit in Japanese folklore.
