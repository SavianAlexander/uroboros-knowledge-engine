1. # Summary
The Perseverance is a variant of the **Pioneer** given out for completing the 2025 Winter Nexus event. It mines slightly slower than an **Endurance**, but makes up for that by having massive bonuses to ice mining crits. Resulting in much higher resource efficiency.
