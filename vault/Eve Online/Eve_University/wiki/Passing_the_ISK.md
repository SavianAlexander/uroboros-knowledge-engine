Though the mission agent will request that you bring a survey scanner, it is not required to fit on your ship, as it will appear in you overview.

There is a debris field around the asteroid that will slow your movement speed. You may want to take care of the rats away from this area so that you can move freely.

As in all mining operations belt rats may randomly spawn.
