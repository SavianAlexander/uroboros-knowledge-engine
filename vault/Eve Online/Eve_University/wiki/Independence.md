# Independence

Independence is an **Unrated Complex** found in **nullsec** via **exploration**.

The site is behind an acceleration gate. Warping to the site lands you at 0 on the gate.

1. # First room

Acceleration gate is 50 km from warp-in. Gate is not locked. Burn towards the gate while destroying the scrammer frigates.

1. # Second room

  - BLITZ**: burn towards the 3 closest battleships, destroy them, shoot just once one of the two battleships that spawn after the initial 3, Sentient will spawn.

The initial defenders are arranged in three groups around the warp in (by ship size) and can be aggressed separately.

|}
Attacking any Battleships from Spawn 1 triggers Spawn 2.

|}
Attacking any ship from Spawn 3 triggers Spawn 4.

Destroying all NPCs in this site will pay about 22 million ISK in bounties.
