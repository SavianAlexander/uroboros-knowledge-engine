CCP are delighted to annouce that the feature site for [EVE Online: Rubicon](//www.eveonline.com/rubicon?utm_source=Community+news+feed&utm_medium=Banner&utm_term=Rubicon+is+coming&utm_content=Rubicon+is+coming&utm_campaign=Rubicon+is+coming) has been updated to include full details of EVE Online's 20th free expansion.
