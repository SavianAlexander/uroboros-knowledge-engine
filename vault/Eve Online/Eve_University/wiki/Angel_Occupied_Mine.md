# Angel Occupied Mine

The **Angel Occupied Mine** is an **Escalation** found in **Angel**-infested regions. The site is moderately difficult but may be done with a **battlecruiser**.

  1. # Escalation Journal

**Expeditions**:
> You have traced a signal being broadcast from this location. It leads to an Angel occupied mining site. This site is likely to contain high-ranking members of the Cartel.

**Description**:
> An abandoned mine occupied by the Angel Cartel and home to some high-ranking members. This is considered high-risk

**Operational Intel**:
> Difficulty Level 4
> Hostiles Angel Cartel

**Escalation**:
> Escalation Sites are sometimes revealed by your participation in other Deadspace sites.

**Waves**:
> There is a total of 5 waves in this site.

1. # Walkthrough
The **Angel Occupied Mine** is obtainable as an **escalation** while doing **Angel Haven**s; once you get it and warp on site there will be 5 waves of **Angel NPC**s that will come to get you. The site is feasible with anything you used to do the **Angel Haven**.
