# Cargo Delivery (Serpentis) (Level 4)

Video: [Sentry Dominix](https://youtu.be/uHKi1W2kX-M), [Gila](https://youtu.be/4DL9UELyolM) 2021-05-01]

Single ungated pocket. You land 40 km away from the warehouse that contains the item so an Afterburner or Microwarpdrive is recommended. All waves spawn within the belt ~20km from the warehouse.

  - Blitz:** Fly to the pickup point, loot and warp away. Nothing needs to be killed so a fast ship works. Even a Corvette!

|}

Warehouse wave is triggered when you loot the Quafe.

|}

Reinforcements arrive once triggered. Killing the elite cruiser from initial defenders is one trigger. May be other triggers.
Dune Conal can rep a noticeable amount.

|}

1. # Loot & Salvage
  - Loot:** ± 1,3 M ISK

  - Salvage:** ± 2,7 M ISK

  - Bounties:** ± 6,5 M ISK

1. # Mining
- 2,270,238 Unit Veldspar
- 60 Asteroids, average of 35k each. Belt is a ring ~30km across
