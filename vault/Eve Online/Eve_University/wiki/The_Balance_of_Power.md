# The Balance of Power

<noinclude></noinclude>

  - The Balance of Power** is the Minmatar way of starting the Angel Angel Sound Epic Arc.

To accept the Missions you need 3.00 Standing with either:
- Aton Hordner
- Republic Security Service
- Minmatar Republic
