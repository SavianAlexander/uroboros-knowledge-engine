----- Personal advice
Do not do the mission alone, you will immediately find yourself aggro, scrambled and no warp possible, and the damage is considerable.
Example: Ishtar full Armor, with Armor Repairer II X2 - I lasted less than a minute. The frigates are very fast, the drones cannot aggro... also check the **discussion** if you require additional warnings .

  1. # 1st Room

Dramiels deal mostly EM and Explosive damage + some Kinetic, with a similar resist profile to the Burner Dramiel anomic agent. They switch targets frequently and will shoot drones. They have TINY signature radius (13m) and orbit you at 1200m/s, need strong bonused webs and strong damage application support to hit accurately. Triple web **Vigilant** works quite well for this.

Strong faction or deadspace-grade tank modules and high volley damage + webs to quickly win the DPS race is highly recommended.
