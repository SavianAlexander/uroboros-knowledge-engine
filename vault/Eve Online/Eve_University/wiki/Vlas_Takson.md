# Vlas Takson

- Vlas Takson** is a level 2 COSMOS agent in **Minmatar COSMOS**, located in Inder, in the Dream Port, and requires 2.00 Minmatar faction standing.

This agent offers three medium sized courier missions.

1. # Supplies for the Colonies - Transporting the Goods (1 of 3)

1. # Supplies for the Colonies - Tricky Terrain (2 of 3)

1. # Supplies for the Colonies - For The Police (3 of 3)
