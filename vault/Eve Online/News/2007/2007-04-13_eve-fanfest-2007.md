It is yet again coming close to that time of the year when the fabulous, wonderful and amazing people of EVE gather in the capital of Iceland, Reykjavik, for 3 days of partying, beer, lectures, beer, roundtables, beer and much, much more! And beer of course.

EVE Fanfest 2007 will be held November 1st to 3rd this year, so order your flight and hotel package, get your Fanfest pass, pack your bags and then just sit and wait for about 8 months... Maybe you don't need to pack just yet, but make sure you order the package to catch the "early bird special" provided by Icelandair. Please note that you will need to purchase the Fanfest pass separatley from the flight and hotel package.

This year will be bigger than before, better than before and with more Devs (and beer) than before! Head over to the [EVE Fanfest 2007](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2007/) page and read all there is to know about it!
