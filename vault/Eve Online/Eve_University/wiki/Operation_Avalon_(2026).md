# Operation Avalon (2026)

- Operation Avalon** was a limited time event that ran from 7th to 20th June 2026, **downtime** to downtime.

Event stuff must be claimed by MOST likely the 27th.  Redemption Queue turned in by August 6th, 2026.

Item traders, to trade **(Ranking) Vanguard Resonant Cypher**, will be available till 28 July 2026 (one week after the end of the operation.

Only information was in the patch notes, which was weird: Easily missable rewards, 13 days, 13 rewards.

Has Avalon Investigation Map sites across High, Low, and Syndicate.  A button in the event page has links to 'Operation Avalon' map filter, which is under Activity.
