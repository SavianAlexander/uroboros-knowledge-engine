# Fountain of Youth

- Fountain of Youth** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **Blood Provincial HQ**.
1. # Walkthrough
This expedition is a very hard one, and may require several people in strong ships (T3C, well-tanked Battleship or Marauder is advised). The first and second locations can be done solo, but the last two locations have very high incoming DPS that can overwhelm a ship running solo.

  1. # First Location
You land at 0 on an acceleration gate. Take it if no commander spawns or if destroying it doesn't trigger escalation.

|}

|}
  1. ## First Pocket
Simple warp-in can trigger escalation.

|}

  1. # Second Location

Ungated pocket. Incoming damage is VERY high. Recommend using a sniper ship to pick of the enemies or activate an MJD away from the rats when you land. Alternatively use a strongly tanked ship that can tank the DPS (like a well-tanked T3 Cruiser).

|}

Attack the Blood Control Center or the Blood Raider Chapels to trigger escalation if none if received from the commander.

|}

  1. # Third Location

  - Warning:** The two stasis towers web you very hard, so expect high incoming damage.
The Blood Defense Outpost has very strong active shield and armor tank that requires about 1000 DPS to break (the equivalent of the firepower of 3 battleships).  It only drops T1 ammo and meta modules, so don't bother wasting ammo to destroy it unless you need the escalation.

|}

|}

  1. # Fourth Location

This site is very hard, and will require multiple people in strongly tanked ships. Double Stasis towers will make you take a lot of damage. Recommend high resists against EM and Thermal damage. Avoid attacking the Blood Raider Military Outpost unless you can tank the incoming damage. The structure itself drops no loot. The site has 3 waves, and if you spawn 2 at once, the site is almost impossible.

|}

|}

|}

The final wave is the overseer wave. It spawns when the Blood Raider Military Outpost is at 55% structure.
