## Overview
The latest update to Legion brings a long-awaited job type.

---

Timely capsuleers, 

Since the launch of Freelance Jobs capsuleers and corporations have found various incredible ways to put them to good use, nefarious and noble. Creating your own SRP programs, recruiting like-minded capsuleers to corporations, competing in PvE competitions, creating your own missions, focusing efforts in Factional Warfare, and just getting your name out there. Space is active, and you’re the ones creating the action!  

But one thing has been missing, because some deliveries take longer than expected. Others arrive precisely when they mean to. The latest update to the Legion expansion brings the much-anticipated addition of the “Deliver” job type to Freelance Jobs – a logistics addition that will take your creativity to the next level, allowing you to set up buyback programs, regular fuel delivery tasks, and so much more. Many of you have asked for this addition since day one (and we hear you: day one would have been nice). 

A special thank you goes out to everyone who participated in the Singularity test and those who made their voices heard via Discord, forums and Reddit. 


## Delivery made simple


This new job type allows corporations to request item deliveries directly to the Freelance Job, streamlining logistics and stimulating the movement of goods across New Eden. Allowing the delivery of the item to the job itself enables you to run concurrent requests for the same item without issue. No hangar space clashes, no asset pileups, just clean, trackable contributions. And for the freelancer, yes, it all happens ship-to-job, straight from your cargo hold. 

For corporations, it’s a way to tap into the capsuleer market for swift and scalable logistics. For freelancers, it’s a chance to earn ISK by moving materials and making your mark.  

Ownership transfer, contribution records, and payouts all happen seamlessly. It’s the delivery method you’ve been waiting for. Just a bit late.
