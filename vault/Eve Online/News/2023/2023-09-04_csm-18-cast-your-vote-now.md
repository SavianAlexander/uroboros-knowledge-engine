## Overview
Voting for CSM 18 is live now!

---

Vote-enjoying capsuleers,

Voting is now open for the 18th Council of Stellar Management! We have [49 candidates](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/csm-18-meet-your-candidates) vying for your vote, so head on over to the [CSM voting page](https://community.eveonline.com/community/csm/vote/) and make your selection for those candidates who best represent your interests in New Eden. 

During the campaign period, candidates participated in community podcast and stream interviews. Additionally, they engaged in 10-minute discussions on [CCPTV](https://twitch.tv/ccp).  For deeper insights into their platforms, each CSM 18 candidate has a [dedicated campaign thread](https://forums.eveonline.com/c/council-of-stellar-management/csm-campaigns/71) where you can learn more about their candidacy. Voting ends at 11:00 UTC on 11 September, so make sure to head on over so your voice is heard!

**All Omega clones with an account that is at least 60 days old are eligible to vote in the elections.**

To celebrate the expansion of the CSM from 10 to 12, all accounts who vote will be awarded with a SKIN for the Council Diplomatic Shuttle – the **SARO ‘Black Troop’ SKIN**.
