# Massive Nebula

- Massive Nebula** is a gas exploration site containing gas used in the production of **Medical boosters**.

This is a null sec gas site found through Exploration.

  1. # Defenders:

Inside the site are about a dozen Drone Bunker (Large Collidable Structure). Destroying these bunkers will prevent the first and third waves from spawning and will instead trigger the second wave immedietly the first time the Celadon Cytoserocin cloud explodes.

Minable Gas Clouds:

1 Celadon Cytoserocin (14224 units in the gas cloud)

Celadon Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Standard Exile Booster Booster.

This site is found only in the[Pegasus](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Fountain/Pegasus)constellation of the[Fountain](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Fountain)region of space.
