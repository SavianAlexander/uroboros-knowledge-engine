1. # Summary
The **Pioneer Consortium Issue** is an enhanced version of the **Pioneer** mining destroyer. Its mining potential is nearly identical to its T1 counterpart, with the only gain in that respect being a mining critical hit role bonus. The Consortium Issue boasts improved stats otherwise, including a larger ore hold, and bonuses to active **shield tanking** and **salvaging** with drones.

1. # Skills
As it is very similar to its T1 counterpart, the skills that can benefit a Pioneer Consortium Issue are also similar.

  - To improve mining yield**
- : Improves mining laser yield by 5% per skill level.
- : Improves mining yield by 10% per skill level (hull bonus).
- : Allows use of Mining Laser Upgrade low slot modules, boosting yield.
- : Allows for use of mining drones to further boost yield (requires **Omega clone** status)

  - To improve survivability**
- : Reduces align times.
- : Reduces align times.
- : Grants a 5% bonus to shield hit points per skill level.
- : Reduces shield upgrade powergrid needs by 5%, and allows for better shield extenders to be attached.
- : Reduces shield recharge time by 5%, and allows for different types of shield boosters to be attached.

  - To improve self-defense against **belt rat**s**
- : To be able to operate four drones, the amount that the Pioneer Consortium Issue has bandwidth for.
- : Allows use of light combat drones.

  - To harvest gas**
- : Allows for use of three gas cloud harvesters to maximize the harvest potential of the Pioneer.

  - To salvage**
- : Allows for use of salvaging drones.

1. # Tactics
While tankier than its T1 counterpart, if the Pioneer Consortium Issue is fit for resource gathering, it is unlikely that the it will be capable of fending off player threats by itself any better than a base Pioneer could. While harder to defang due to a larger drone bay, it is unlikely that additional flights would threaten anything larger than a frigate. Using tools to avoid potential threats before they come, such as the **D-Scan** and viewing local chat, can help improve survivability in dangerous areas.
