In his [devblog](//community.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&nbid=74037) on December 3rd, CCP t0rfifrans announced that EVE Online was among 14 games that the Museum of Modern Art had acquired for an exhibition. In order to fully demonstrate the complexity of EVE Online in this format CCP t0rfifrans asked you, our players, to make December 9th the most interesting day in the history of New Eden and capture it to be immortalized forever in the halls of the MoMA.

Today is that day. We encourage everyone to contribute in whatever way they see fit or interesting, and help us create a fantastic day in the life New Eden.

For additional information on how to capture your endeavors of the day, refer to CCP t0rfifrans' [blog](//community.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&nbid=74037) or [this thread](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=178116) on our forums.
