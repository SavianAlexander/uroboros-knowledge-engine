# Federation Grand Prix (2022)

The Federation Grand Prix is a recurring ingame event that challenged capsuleers to warp from point to point in the least amount of time. In 2022 it ran from June 16th to June 28th.

1. # Race Routes
The Federation Grand Prix is split into 3 routes: Northeast, Southwest, and History. After completing the first run of those routes, you will unlock the ability to re-run them while being timed where the top 10 competitors times will be recorded ingame. The tables below will use "Prefer Shorter", totalling 392 jumps.

Note that lowsec landmarks are often camped; exercise caution when travelling to lowsec landmarks.

  1. # Northeastern Route
While all destinations are in highsec, the shortest routes will require some lowsec.

1. # Rewards
Each site you arrive at will award you points, 20 in the NE Route, 10 in the SW, while the History Route gives mostly 10s, some 5s and a 15. After gaining the required amount of points you can claim the reward from the Federation Grand Prix window in The Agency and it will be placed in your redeem queue.
