# Serpentis Hidden Hub

- Serpentis Hidden Hub** is an **combat anomaly** found in Serpentis infested low and null security regions.

1. # Walkthrough
The site consists of one ungated pocket. There are Asteroid Mining Post about 60 km from warp-in. New wave spawns once ships from previous spawn are dead.
