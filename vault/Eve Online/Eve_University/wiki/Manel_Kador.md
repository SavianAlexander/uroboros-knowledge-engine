# Manel Kador

- Manel Kador** is a level 1 **Amarr COSMOS** agent. Manel can be found in Chanoun, at Lord Manel's Mansion.

Prerequisites: **Odan Poun** (Part 2), **Bartezo Maphante**

1. # Culprit Revealed!

Bartezo's Message is acquired by finishing all missions with **Bartezo Maphante**.

1. # Neutralizing the Threat (1 of 3)

Blood Grunt Tags can be obtained by looting Blood Phantom and Blood Templar at **Lord Manel's Mansion**.

1. # Neutralizing the Threat (2 of 3)

Blood Lower-Tier Tags can be obtained by looting Blood Arch Sage at **Lord Manel's Mansion**.

1. # Neutralizing the Threat (3 of 3)

Blood Raider Squad Leader's Heads can be obtained by looting Dark Blood Arch Templar at **Lord Manel's Mansion**.

1. # Notes

As you make your way through the complex to Manel, make sure to loot the various ships you pass, as you'll need the tags (10 Grunt, 5 Lower-Tier) and Squad Leader Heads (2) for the missions. If you grab everything then you'll probably have all you need before you reach Manel.

The third mission gives 0.09% Amarr Empire standings and a Lord Manel's Message, which you can turn into Odan Poun for his last mission. After that, Manel will give you a mission to turn in Bartezo's Message from Bartezo Maphante, giving 1.8% standings.
