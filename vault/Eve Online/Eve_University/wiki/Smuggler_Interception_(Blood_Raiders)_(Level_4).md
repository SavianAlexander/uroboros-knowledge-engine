# Smuggler Interception (Blood Raiders) (Level 4)

Video: [[Maelstrom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G8NHrmj6_68) April 2013], [[Rattlesnake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qXfSW0zE_NM) October 2013], [[Sentry Dominix](https://youtu.be/RhPEYIlyEI8) June 2018], [[Gila](https://youtu.be/q58OKOThjaA) April 2021]

  - Blitz:** Kill Group 3 (including the Blood Raider Personnel Transport) of pocket 2 and loot the Militants.

  1. # 1st pocket
Acceleration gate 60 km from warp-in. Gate is locked until all enemies destroyed.

No aggro on warp-in. Proximity aggro on groups 2 & 3 when within 6km of the gate.

Tips:
- Group 4 can be sniped without aggroing anything else.
- Attacking Group 2 aggroes Group 3. The last group will remain neutral and can be aggroed separately.
- Attacking Group 1 OR 3: Aggroes whole pocket.

You can eliminate both groups 4 and 1 without drawing pocket aggro but it gets a bit complicated. You need to fly your ship off to the side putting the station ruins between your ship and groups 1 2 and 3. After your within range eliminate group 4 this will draw group 1 aggro. If you wait for a few minutes after destroying group 4 you will notice that group 1 will be stuck in the station ruins unable to get close to you. Additionally they will have drifted out of aggro range of the other 2 pockets. At this point it should be safe to eliminate group 1 without 2 and 3 aggroing you.

|}

  1. # 2nd pocket
NO aggro on warp in. The transport ships drop the mission objective. Destroying the group in which the transporters are (group 3) marks the mission completed.

Tips:
- Group 1 can be ignored. Though they will aggro your drones from far away if not destroyed.
- Aggro for Group 2 & 3 are tied with each other. Attacking either will aggro the other.
- Attacking Group 4 aggroes group 3

Groups here are not exactly defined.

|}
