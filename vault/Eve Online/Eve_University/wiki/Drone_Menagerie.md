# Drone Menagerie

- Drone Menagerie** is an unrated **combat site** belonging to the **Rogue Drones** faction. It can be found via **exploration** as a **cosmic anomaly** in low-security and null-security systems across New Eden. As an ungated complex, the limitations as to which ships can complete this site are tied to the security rating of the system where the anomaly spawns (T2 **Battleship**s for high-security systems, and up to **Titans** in low-security and null-security systems). This combat site has a chance to spawn a Sentient commander rogue drone, which drops drogue components and may drop faction drone modules and items.

1. # Walkthrough
Drone Wall Sentry Guns can hit you out to 250 km, though they don't do particularly significant damage.
