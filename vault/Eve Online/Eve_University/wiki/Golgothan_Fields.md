# Golgothan Fields

- Golgothan Fields** is the sixth mission of the AIR epic arc **Vision of Greatness**

  - Supplies: Golgothan Fields** will be given after accepting the mission which includes (when opened):

- 1x Punisher
- 1x Small Energy Neutralizer I
- 1x Gatling Pulse Laser I
- 1x 1MN Afterburner I
- 1x Small Remote Armor Repairer I
- 3x Multifrequency S

Pocket 1

- Remote Armor Repair the Nuras' Revelation

Pocket 2

- Destroy 4 hostile NPCs

Pocket 3

- Neutralize the Flagship Naglfar
