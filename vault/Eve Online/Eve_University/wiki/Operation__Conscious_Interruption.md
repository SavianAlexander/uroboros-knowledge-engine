# Operation: Conscious Interruption

- Operation: Conscious Interruption** is part of the **Capsuleer Day** event that runs from May 2nd to May 15th 2018, [celebrating EVE Online 15th anniversary](http://updates.eveonline.com/date/2018-05-06/).

During this event, combat sites can be found as beacons in overview and as scannable combat signatures.

1. # Operation: Conscious Interruption (beacon site)
Large number of frigates. The frigates do not deal very high damage and they have low chance to web. The smaller rats will keep respawning until the site is completed or the site despawns.
One boss cruiser that orbits at ~30 km at 1800 m/s. Thermal damage is the most effective, though the cruiser has quite a strong passive shield tank and is hard to hit well because of its high speed. Destroying this cruiser completes the site.

1. # Operation: Drifter Interruption (combat signature)
Medium number of frigates, few destroyers and few battlecruisers. The smaller rats will keep respawning until the site is completed or the site despawns.
One boss battleship that orbits at ~50 km at 1800 m/s. The battleship has strong passive shield tank and is, again, weakest to thermal damage. Destroying this battleship completes the site.

1. # Operation: Signal Interruption (escalation)

The Operation: Conscious Interruption site can rarely escalate.

The Signal Interruption site contains few sleeper frigates, destroyers and cruisers. three Hypnos Tyrannos boss cruisers and two Drifter Strike Commander boss battleships.

1. # Collecting Points
   

Points can be collected through various means.  
- Killing sleepers
- Collecting bounties from npcs

1. # Rewards
Rats in the sites drop:
- **Agency boosters**
- Capsuleer Dawn Firework (Large)
- Standup Large Festival Launcher
- Sleeper Components (Neural Network Analyzer and Sleeper Data Library)

Additionally the boss rat can drop:
- The boss have a bounty of 500.000 (null-sec)
- Day XV SKINs
- Cerebral accelerators:
  - Basic X-Sense 'Consciousness' Cerebral Accelerator +6 points for 24h
  - Improved X-Sense 'Consciousness' Cerebral Accelerator  +10 points for 24h
  - Advanced X-Sense 'Consciousness' Cerebral Accelerator  +12 points for 24h
  - Extended X-Sense 'Consciousness' Cerebral Accelerator  +10 points for 72h
- Battleship level boss can also drop:
  - Gnosis BPC (5 run)
  - Sunesis BPC (5 run)
  - Apotheosis BPC (5 run)

There are also additional one time rewards for collecting points.
- 25 points: Wings of Victory Firework
- 400 points: Agency Support Drop (random booster)
- 800 points: Advanced X-Sense 'Consciousness' Cerebral Accelerator
- 1200 points: Genolution CA-X  (random genolution implant)
