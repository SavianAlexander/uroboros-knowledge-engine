# Interstitial Ore Deposit

- Interstitial Ore Deposits** are ore anomalies that can spawn in **sovereign nullsec** systems from an escalation after finishing an anomaly which was spawned by an active . Like other ore anomalies, it is neither gated nor deadspace, so making bookmarks and warping around in it are possible. It will appear on the overview of everybody in system once warp has been initiated to it, and it will despawn after 24 hours, regardless of any mining activity.

The anomaly is roughly spherical in shape, with a diameter of about 200 kilometers. The warp-in beacon is in the center.
