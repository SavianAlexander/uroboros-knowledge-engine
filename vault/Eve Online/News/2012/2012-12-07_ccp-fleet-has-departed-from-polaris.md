## Overview
According to Kjersidur Elladall, the CEO of the Directive Enforcement Department, a CCP fleet was detected leaving the Polaris system this morning. The fleet is rumored to be highly organized with a mass of battleship class vessels and logistic support. Capsuleers in New Eden should remain alert.

---

According to Kjersidur Elladall, the CEO of the Directive Enforcement Department, a CCP fleet was detected leaving the Polaris system this morning. The fleet is rumored to be highly organized with a mass of battleship class vessels and logistic support. Capsuleers in New Eden should remain alert. At this time the DED have no leads on the final destination of this fleet although they suspect it may emerge in a Gallente low security system later today.
