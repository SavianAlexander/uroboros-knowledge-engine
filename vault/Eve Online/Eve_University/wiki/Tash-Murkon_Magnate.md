1. # Summary
The Tash-Murkon Magnate is a cosmetic variant of the **Magnate**. It was a reward for the purchase of EVE: The Second Decade Collector's Edition.

All models were converted to a regular Magnate and a corresponding Tash-Murkon **SKIN** with the Mosaic release in April 2015.
