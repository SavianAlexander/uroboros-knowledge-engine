# Besieged Covert Research Facility

The **Besieged Covert Research Facility** is a **Mordu's Legion** combat anomaly. It can be found in any region of empire space, but only in Low Security systems. They are the only normal combat anomaly that can spawn in **Faction warfare** systems.

You will need:
- a ship that can sustain fire from up to five Battleships, a passive buffer Gila with Vespa and Kin/Therm resists can do this site in approx. 10-12 mins.
- a Stealth Bomber with an oversized afterburner and missile precision enhancers. This method relies completely on speed tanking and cannot absorb more than one or two lucky hits from the battleships, and necessitates Tech II torpedo fits and high navigation skills to reliably complete the sites. Requires at least 500 DPS (Kinetic) to complete.

Tips:
- No faction standing penalty for killing Mordu's ships.
- All ships fly up to 380m/s
- All ships have active shield tank
- Kinetic is the best damage to both deal and resist.
- The Cruiser's missiles deal Kinetic damage
- The Battleship's high accuracy and infinite range Thermal/Kinetic/EM turrets deal 350 damage per shot, while their cruise missiles deal 900 Kinetic damage per shot.
- The Commander's turrets deal all damage types, but have poor tracking; and their 54 km range torpedoes deal Kinetic damage
- On paper, the highest possible incoming DPS is 1040 (170 EM, 250 Thermal, 620 Kinetic) with 5 Battleships, before taking into account resists and relative signature radius.

You should deal Kinetic damage because:
- The Cruiser is weak to EM, but has low HP so high Kinetic volley damage can still kill it quickly
- The Battleship's shields are weak to EM and armor is weak to Explosive, but the lowest average resistance is Thermal with Kinetic at close second
- The Commander has the highest EHP of the three ships but its weakest average resistance is Kinetic

You should also put emphasis on primarily resisting Kinetic damage (while maintaining omni tank) since the cruiser lacks turrets and its missiles deal purely Kinetic damage, while the battleships output more DPS in Kinetic than all other damage types combined. 

1. # Warp in

Single deadspaced pocket. You cannot warp around.

The site is empty at first and remains that way until a player enters within 50 km of the Guristas Research and Trade Hub, **even if they are cloaked.** Wave 1 spawns within a few seconds after the proximity trigger, indicated by a message in local.

None of the NPCs drop any loot, though the Mordu’s Legion Commander has a bounty of 900k ISK.

If the site isn't cleared within 20 minutes after Wave 1 spawns, the structures, along with any loot inside them, will explode (though the explosions don't deal damage to your ship), and the site is considered failed. Any remaining rats on grid will instantly despawn.

  1. # Loot table

- Covert Research Tools
- Any type of pirate faction ammo (regardless of region), includes commander ammo types (e.g. Dread Guristas)
- 30-day pirate ship skins
- Low and Mid-Grade implant drops of the following sets: Virtue, Nomad, Centurion, Talisman, Halo, Amulet, Harvest, Hydra, Crystal, Savior, Edge, Asklepian, Snake
- Blueprint copies for Hyperspatial Accelerators, Jump Drive Economizers and any of the implants listed above
- Minerals, in stacks of 10 (from the Thukker Component Assembly Facility) excluding Morphite
- Thukker Capital Component Manufacturing Technical Data Chip, which can be opened to receive Thukker manufacturing rig blueprints for Upwell structures

Loot will usually average between 15-40M ISK per site with some variance through implant and BPC drops, which can jack up the loot value to well over 300M. Lowest loot drop seen was around 6M, so it may be a good idea to warp in at long range to the site and cargo scan the Guristas Research and Trade Hub to see if the site is worth running, since the rewards are highly variable.
