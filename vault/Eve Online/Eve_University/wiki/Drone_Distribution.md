The NPCs arrive in multiple spawns (4-5) of one or two ships shortly after each other. They most likely be all on grid before you can destroy the first.

The first spawn arrives after approximately 40 seconds.

The mission's own NPCs are not a problem for a barge with drones. However, as with all belts, a regular **belt NPC** pack can warp into your mission site. If your mission is in 0.0 space, this may include battleships and warp scrambling frigates.
