1. # Summary
The **Ragnarok** is the Minmatar Titan. It is bonused for Capital Projectile Turrets. The Ragnarok is considered to have one of the best Doomsday devices, due to the speed at which the damage from the Gjallarhorn is applied. The Ragnarok is also the fastest and most agile Titan in the game when in a shield nano fit. When fit with artillery, the Ragnarok has extreme range, on par with the **Erebus**. The Ragnarok is the second squishiest titan, second only to the Erebus. The Ragnarok can be effectively dual tanked, using its 6 low slots to field a respectable armor tank. Alternatively, some pilots fit 5 power diagnostic systems in the lows instead to increase its shield EHP. The second method will result in more EHP but the first offers the opportunity for armor logistics to help save the ship rather than relying on only shield logistics.

1. # Notes
In old Norse mythology, Ragnarok (or *Ragnarök*) was the name for the final, future confrontation between the Norse gods and various monsters. It would result in the deaths of numerous gods including Odin, who would be killed by **Fenrir**, and **Loki**; it would end the known world. **Naglfar** would bring some of the gods' enemies to Ragnarok, and **Lif** would be one of the two humans who would survive the battle by hiding.
