1. # Summary
The **Phobos** is the Gallente Heavy Interdictor. It is designed to be heavily armor tanked, and has a large number of low slots and a hull bonus to help this. The main purpose of the Phobos is to carry the Warp Disruption Field Generator module, which produces a warp disruption bubble around the ship.

The Phobos has one less low slot and one more mid compared to the Amarr **Devoter**, meaning it can field less tank. This makes the Devoter the preferred tackler for capitals (though the Phobos can also perform this role adequately), however the Phobos is often used in low sec gate camps to tackle ships that are equipped with warp core stabilisers due to its extra mid allowing it to carry an extra sensor booster.

1. # Notes

In ancient Greek mythology Phobos was the son of **Ares** and Aphrodite, and twin brother of **Deimos**. He represented or personified fear (thus the modern English word "phobia"), especially fear in battle. The two moons of the planet Mars are named after Phobos and Deimos; [Phobos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phobos_(moon)) is the larger of the two.
