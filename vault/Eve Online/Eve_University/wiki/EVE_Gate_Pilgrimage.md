# EVE Gate Pilgrimage

{{EVEUniEvent
| name = EVE Gate Pilgrimage
| description =
The **EVE Gate** is probably the most famous lore relic in EVE and is truly something special everyone should see at least once.

It is 'tradition' to leave a small anchored can at the EVE Gate, as a sort of "I was here!" message. There used to be excellent displays of can art all around the gate. From EVE written in massive letters to giant bottles of beer.

Unfortunately, with the introduction of a script that removes from the game all cans that are not accessed for more than 30 days, this player art was lost and capsuleers now have to content themselves with more temporary monuments to their visit in the form of a solitary can often labeled with a humorous message.

If you wish to do this, you will need to BRING your own small secure container in your cargo hold and have trained at least  prior to arrival at the EVE Gate (it’s a 25 minute train).

We will also be visiting the Sansha's Wormhole in [Promised Land](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Genesis/Promised_Land) making a small detour to see the **Black Obelisk** in [Dead End](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Genesis/Dead_End#const)!
| scheduling =
    - Duration:** 2 Hours

    - Area:** High-Sec > Low-Sec > High-Sec

    - Destination:** [New Eden](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Genesis/New_Eden#const) via [End](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Genesis/Dead_End#constDead) (for the **Black Obelisk** site)

    - Communication:** You must be on **Mumble** at all times during the pilgrimage. If you are in fleet, you need to be on Mumble.
