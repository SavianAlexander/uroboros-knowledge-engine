Some have heard the dull thud of a book-sized box hitting the doormat already, or witnessed the annoyed face of the postman as he was forced to ring the doorbell, but for the rest who've been awaiting the arrival of their [EVE Strategic Maps book](//www.eve-online.com/eon/strategicmaps.asp), be aware that the waiting is almost over. The books are printed, packaged in postman-proof boxes and on or near the planes, boats or trains that will take them to their owners.

When you get your copy, don't forget to register at [www.eve-maps.com](//www.eve-maps.com/) to get Outpost Alerts, which will help you keep your book up to date (that's what the yellow stickers are for). Be aware, of course, that the book is fully updated already to reflect changes that will be introduced as part of the Empyrean Age expansion, so it's slightly ahead of time – yes, even Black Rise is included. If you haven’t ordered already, now is a good time to do so.

May the maps be with you, always :)
