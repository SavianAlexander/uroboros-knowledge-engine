# Amarr Championship Finals - Only one Round

- **Date**: 2003-11-01T18:01:54.000Z
- **Category**: news
- **Author**: Svarthol
- **Source**: https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/amarr-championship-finals-only-one-round
- **Tags**: #news

## Overview
The Amarr Championship finals have been trimmed down to one Round set to finish this Saturday ( Nov 1st ). There will be no second round. This was done due to scheduling reasons for the contestants and their wingmen. May the best champion win.
