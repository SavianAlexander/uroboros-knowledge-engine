# Broadcast 4 Reps

- Broadcast 4 Reps** (sometimes referred to as "Broadcast for Reps") is a player-run initiative to help players who are depressed, suicidal, or otherwise in need of someone to talk to.

1. # "You Never Fly Alone"

In the fall of 2015, player Coffee Rocks of Brave Newbies posted a video on YouTube titled "[Broadcast 4 Reps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MaL-66nHN9c)". The video included clips of players, CCP employees, and the mother of an EVE player who committed suicide the previous year, all speaking about the importance of seeking help for depression and suicidal thoughts and the "Broadcast 4 Reps" initiative.

Today, the initiative has both an in-game chat channel and a [public Discord server](https://discord.gg/ZhUZd2q), and there are additional resources available on the [old EVE official forums](https://forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=443303) and the [r/EVE subreddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Eve/wiki/broadcast4reps). Volunteers spend time both in-game and in Discord and make themselves available to chat with anyone who visits, either in the public channel or privately.
