1. # Additional Notes
- References to SAFE or NOT SAFE found throughout this guide are not an indication of safety from PVP.  Instead, they are an indication of NPC presence, timers or other dangerous and deadly triggers within a site. **Therefore, you should always exercise caution, even if going to a 'safe' site!**
- Abandoned Research Complexes found in null security space are NOT SAFE.
- Lesser, Standard, Improved and Superior Covert Research Facilities are NOT SAFE.
- Limited, Standard, and Superior Sleeper Cashes are NOT SAFE.
- High and low security space gas nebulas are SAFE.  Null security space gas nebulas are NOT ALWAYS SAFE.  Wormhole gas reservoirs are NOT SAFE.
- There have been reports of a bug where rats could spawn in 'safe' sites. It has not been confirmed whether this bug has been resolved.
- In the above tables,  is replaced in-game with the local pirate group associated with each region. For example, in Verge Vendor, the local pirate group is Serpentis. Therefore, a data site in Verge Vendor would be seen as "Local Serpentis Mainframe".
- For more information about hackable containers, see **Relic and data sites**
