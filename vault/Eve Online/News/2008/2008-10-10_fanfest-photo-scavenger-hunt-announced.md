After fighting off hordes of ninja salvagers preying on your stuff, you thought you were safe at Fanfest? Wrong! Prepare for camera combat with our [Scavenger Hunt](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2008/scavenger.asp) at this year’s Fanfest!

You and your team will have a shot at winning some luscious loot by simply snapping shots of the festivities. All you need is a camera, a Fanfest pass, and a team. If you think you are brave enough to join “The Scope,” be sure to check out our [competition guidelines](//www.eve-online.com/fanfest/2008/scavenger.asp).

We’ll see you there!
