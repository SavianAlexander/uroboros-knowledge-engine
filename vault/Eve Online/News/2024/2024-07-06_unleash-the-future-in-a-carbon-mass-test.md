## Kick the Space-Tires


The **Carbon 1.0** update introduces a revolutionary GPU-driven pipeline, optimizing hardware utilization for significantly improved performance. This means a more immersive and seamless gaming experience, allowing capsuleers to dive deeper into the vast universe of EVE. For a sneak peek at this exciting change, check out [**this clip from Fanfest**](https://youtu.be/ycZX-mqwD9k?t=1803). 

This update also introduces advanced upscaling solutions, including Nvidia's DLSS, AMD's FSR, Intel's XeSS, and MetalFX for macOS. These technologies enable the game to render at lower resolutions and upscale to the desired output, providing higher framerates and smoother gameplay, even in the most intense battles. 

Raytracing is another stunning feature of Carbon 1.0, offering sharper and more accurate shadows. This technology leverages the dedicated hardware of your graphics card, delivering exceptional visual fidelity and enhanced performance, making every moment in New Eden look spectacular. 

For further information on the features being tested, and step-by-step instructions on how to join the test, please visit the [**mass test forum thread**](https://forums.eveonline.com/t/mass-test-on-singularity-tuesday-july-9th-2024/455046). There, you will also be able to submit your feedback and report any bugs.
