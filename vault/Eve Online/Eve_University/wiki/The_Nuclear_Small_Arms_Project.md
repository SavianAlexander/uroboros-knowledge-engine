# The Nuclear Small Arms Project

- The Nuclear Small Arms Project** is an **expedition** that escalates from **Angel Watch** combat site. The escalation has four locations. Generally three of the locations are in high security space while the last one is in low security space.

Usually the first three locations are in high sec and the final one in low sec but there are exceptions.

1. # 1st location
The are has four missile batteries. Attacking each of them will trigger one battery wave. Killing the commander frigate may trigger the escalation.

|}

|}

1. # 2nd location
After warping in you receive a message about interacting with a disabled mammoth (large collidable object). You need to shoot it with a turret/launcher. Drones won't do anything. EWAR modules will work, though.

|}

|}

Wave 2 spawns shortly after the initial wave warps off.

|}

1. # 3rd location

  - Note:** Angel Sentries deal high combined DPS and can reach out all the way to 250 km.

|}

|}

1. # 4th location
Warning: Incoming DPS can be very high due to close proximity of spawns.

The facility webs have 20 km range and -75% speed reduction. Attacking the station will trigger reinforcement wave.

The valuable drops are in the Secret Angel Facility. These include Gistii A-Type modules and Dramiel BPC.

It is possible to blitz this section by dropping a bookmark at the warp-in, warping off, warping at range to the bookmark and sniping the Secret Angel Facility to greatly reduce incoming damage. 

|}

|}

This expedition end message will also appear in local.

1. # Loot
Typical commander loot includes Domination copper and silver dog tags and some faction ammo, as well as possible Domination faction modules or a Mid-Grade Halo implant. The Secret Angel Facility in final location can drop Gistii A-Type modules or a rare **Dramiel** BPC.
