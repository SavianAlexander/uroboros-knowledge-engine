# Gurista Fortress

- Gurista Fortress** is an **Unrated Complex** found by scanning in **Null Security** systems with **Guristas** presence.

1. # Walkthrough

Warping to the site lands you at 0 on an acceleration gate.

  1. # First Pocket
The acceleration gate to the second room is approximately 30 km away. It unlocks when all enemies are destroyed.

|}

The Dread Guristas Dismantler may not spawn in until you reach ~10 km of the Guristas Bunker.

|}

It is unverified, but if you do not trigger an escalation after killing all NPCs you may destroy the bunker which is separate from the other three. Then destroy the middle one among the remaining three bunkers and destroy the last two bunkers. You will still have the chance of triggering escalation after doing these steps. *RigelKent, 2025-10-12: just got the escalation from following the aforementioned sequence: after killed all rats, then, I killed the bunker separated and following that, I killed the bunker in the middle; can't confirm if it's enough always or the order is random.

1. # Escalation
This site can escalate to **Hired Gun**.
