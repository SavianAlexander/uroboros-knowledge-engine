# Save The Slaves

- Save the Slaves** is an **expedition** found by completing **Blood Raider Outpost** combat sites.  It may contain up to four parts, each of which will be about 4 jumps from the previous system.  The first three will be in low-sec space, while the fourth will usually be in null-sec.

1. # First Escalation
You land at an acceleration gate.

|}
  1. # First Pocket
The first pocket is optional.  Killing the Faction Cruiser has the possibility of triggering the next mission on the escalation. If not, take the gate. Simple warp-in may trigger escalation.

|}

1. # Second Escalation
Again you land at another acceleration gate. Take it if no escalation triggers from the Dark Blood commander.

|}
  1. # First Pocket
No enemies present, only the abandoned ruins of an Amarr station. Escalation may trigger upon warp-in. 

1. # Third Escalation
Ungated pocket.

|}

1. # Fourth Escalation
  1. # First Room (Gateless Entry)

|}
Kill all rats to activate the acceleration gate.

  1. # Second Room (Gated)

|}

1. # Rewards
Getting all four escalations gives the possibility of an Ashimmu BPC or Corpum A-Type module drops in the final escalation site.
