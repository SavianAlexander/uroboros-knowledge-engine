# The Ancient City

- The Ancient City** is an extremely difficult escalation from the **Sansha Provincial HQ** and possibly other Sansha sites.

1. # Walkthrough
This escalation has 4 locations, typically each location is 6-10 jumps between each other and **can** cross regional boundaries.  Locations 1-3 are easier to blitz, but Location 4 is one of the hardest sites that exists in Eve.

It is not guaranteed for each location to escalate.  After each location, killing the True Sansha Battleship will either trigger the escalation to the next location or not.  If not, your time with the site is over.

Full details for this expedition are not currently available, the below is provided as best effort because this is a rare escalation.

No rats warp disrupt/scram in this entire escalation as of writing.

  1. # Location 1
- Blitz: Warp in, kill the True Sansha battleship, bookmark the wreck, warp out.  Wait 2 minutes for site to despawn, warp to bookmark and loot.*

No gate, you will warp in and the rats will be on the outside of an acceleration gate that does not work.  No ewar reported in this room

|}

  1. # Location 2
- Blitz: Warp in, kill the True Sansha battleship, bookmark the wreck, warp out.  Wait 2 minutes for site to despawn, warp to bookmark and loot.*

- Tip: True Sansha spawn is off to the right, away from the other groups.  You can warp to the site cloaked (be careful not to be decloaked by the beacon), bookmark the center and then look for a good celestial behind the true sansha spawn.  Warp out to that celestial, then warp to your bookmark at 30km.  You'll land right on the TS spawn for a quick kill. Bookmark wreck and warp out, loot in 2 minutes after the site despawns.*

Rats are in 2 main groups with 2 tiny groups on the side.  Full site aggros on land.  This site has a lot of DPS that will easily break a T3 Cruiser.

|}

  1. # Location 3
- Blitz: Warp in, kill the True Sansha battleship, bookmark the wreck, warp out.  Wait 2 minutes for site to despawn, warp to bookmark and loot.  No need to get Wave 1 unless you want the bounties.*

- This site is ungated and therefore can be run with capitals.  *
When you warp in, you will be 200km from the structure you need to shoot.  This site is NOT deadspace so you can actually warp around on grid.  The various waves will spawn in different spots around the site, so you will need to either warp around or MJD as needed.
