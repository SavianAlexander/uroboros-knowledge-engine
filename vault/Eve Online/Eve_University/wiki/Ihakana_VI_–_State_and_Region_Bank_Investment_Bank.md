  - Ihakana VI – State and Region Bank Investment Bank** is a station. On this station, there are some **Caldari COSMOS** related rats.

The location of these rats can also be found with core probes. The relevant signatures are "Zvarin Karsha" and "?". 

The signature "Zvarin Karsha" is located right on top of your target "Zvarin Karsha". But multiple elite frigates with webs and warp disruptors are also located here.

The other signature will land you on State and Region Bank Investment Bank station undock. Both signatures are now on the same grid.

1. # Zvarin Karsha
    - Warning!*** This is ungated pocket in a low-sec with a known static location, which is also visible on grid from the nearby station. The elite frigates that guard Zvarin Karsha are fast, and have a surprisingly strong tank. If you let them come close and point you, you can be stuck disrupted there for a minute, and it will become an easy kill for hunters who are often sitting cloaked around the system.

No aggro unless Zvarin Karsha is attacked.

Warping out from the grid will turn the frigates back to blue. Zvarin Karsha has a pretty low tank so you can kill him from the long range, warp out, and then come back and loot the wreck without the need to destroy the frigates and risk being pointed by them.

|}

The structure drops only commodities.
