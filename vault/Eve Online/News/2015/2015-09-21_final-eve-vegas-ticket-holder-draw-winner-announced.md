On August 18th, we announced the [second round of EVE Vegas 2015 ticket holder prize draws](//community.eveonline.com/news/news-channels/eve-online-news/eve-vegas-2015-ticket-sales-draw-round-2/). Today, we're ready to annouce the fifth and final winner of this draw!

As outlined in the news article linked above, this winner will receive a Resort Vista room upgrade and complimentary airport limousine transfers. The winner has been drawn.

Congratulations to [Tiberius Varis](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Tiberius%20Varis) of [Blackwater USA Inc.](//gate.eveonline.com/Corporation/Blackwater%20USA%20Inc.) on being selected as a winner!

Winners of the prize draw will be contacted via email with full details on how they can claim their prize. 

Tickets for EVE Vegas 2015 are close to being sold out, so be sure to pick up yours today in order to avoid disappointment!
