# Zabonn Michi

- Zabonn Michi** is a level 4 **Caldari COSMOS** agent located in Otomainen - **Rush Town Ruins**.

The last mission in the chain will reward you with Michi's Excavation Augmentor, worth 2.1 billion (as of June 2025).

  - Be careful while doing the last missions, there is a very, VERY, high chance that someone will try to steal Wei Todaki to demand a ransom.**

1. # Lost Love - Manchura The Pirate (1 of 3)

Manchura's Logs can be obtained by blowing up Manchura (in a cruiser) in the **Contested Guristas Outrider Post** complex in Otomainen.
