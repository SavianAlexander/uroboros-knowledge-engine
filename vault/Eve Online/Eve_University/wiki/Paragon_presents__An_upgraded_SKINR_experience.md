# Paragon presents: An upgraded SKINR experience

- Paragon presents: An upgraded SKINR experience** is a limited time login campaign, lasting from 15 August to 27 August 2024, **downtime** to downtime.

1. # Log-In campaign
Capsuleers must log in for 7 days out of 13 to collect all rewards. Alpha Clones can claim 70,000 **Skill Points**, while Omega Clones have access to an additional 355,000 Skill Points.
