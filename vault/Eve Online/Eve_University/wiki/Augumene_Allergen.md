# Augumene Allergen

- Augumene Allergen** is a level 1 mining division **agent** 5 part mission chain consisting of 1 mining, 1 courier and 3 encounter missions. It involves combat with the **Amarr Empire** and possibly with **belt rat**s in the mining mission..

The whole chain (all 5 missions) is easily doable in a venture with two drones.
