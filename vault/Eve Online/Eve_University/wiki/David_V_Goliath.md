# David V Goliath

- David v Goliath** is an **expedition**  that escalates from **Sansha Military Complex**.
1. # Walkthrough
This expedition is in nullsec, with all four parts in Sansha infested nullsec. A high DPS ship is recommended to take out Naberius Marquis in the final location.

  1. # First Location
If the True Sansha ship does not trigger escalation or does not spawn, take the acceleration gate.
