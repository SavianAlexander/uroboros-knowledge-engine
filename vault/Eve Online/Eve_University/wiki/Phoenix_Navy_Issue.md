1. # Summary
The Faction version of the **Phoenix**, often simply called the PNI. Compared to the base hull, the faction version sports an extra bonus to target painters, a 50% higher damage bonus per level of , an extra mid slot, more CPU and powergrid, more base tank, and higher sensor strength and scan resolution.

The Phoenix Navy is, together with the Revelation Navy, the most popular dreadnought for huge dread fights. This is due to its extremely thick buffer tank and its high damage with x-large torpedoes. Most large dread alliances favor a balanced mix of Zirnitras, RNI's, and PNI's due to all of them combining thick tank, high damage, and maximium number of the ship's primary slot (Mids for the PNI and Lows for the other two).
