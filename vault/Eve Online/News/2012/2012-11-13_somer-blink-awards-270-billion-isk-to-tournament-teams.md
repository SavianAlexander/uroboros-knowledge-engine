Somerset Mahm stunned the teams participating in the [New Eden Open](//community.eveonline.com/en/tournament) when he sent 10 Billion ISK to each team captain, '... earmarked for the purpose of purchasing ships, modules, implants and supplies for the upcoming tournament. This act of generosity will allow teams to sport set-ups and ships worthy of a $10,000 tournament.
