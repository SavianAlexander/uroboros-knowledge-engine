# Blood Forlorn Den

The **Blood Forlorn Den** is an **unrated cosmic signature combat site** that can be freely warped to in **Blood Raider** owned territory of Low and Null Sec space. The site itself is a small area with no Acceleration Gate. Each wave spawns after the destruction of the previous wave.
