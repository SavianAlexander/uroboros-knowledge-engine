# Codename : Redress

- Notes:** To complete this mission, you will require a ship with a hacking module online, as well as a cargo capacity of at least 2,000 m3 volume (and preferably one with a capacity of 6,000 m3).

  1. # 1st Pocket
160gj/s of total neut.

|}

  1. # 2nd Pocket
There are 5 databanks in this room that will either spawn a Wave 1 or deal 400 damage.

|}

|}

  1. # 3rd Pocket
There are 4 databanks in this room, the fourth one hacked will spawn Wave 1.

|}

|}

  1. # 4th Pocket
6 databanks that do not cause any waves to spawn.
