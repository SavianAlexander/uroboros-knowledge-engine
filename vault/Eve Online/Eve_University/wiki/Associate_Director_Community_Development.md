# Associate Director Community Development

1. # Role Overview
The Associate Director of Community Development focuses on fostering a strong sense of belonging within EVE University by developing PvE Special Interest Groups (SIGs) and supporting location-based initiatives. This role is essential in guiding the University's shift towards a SIG-based structure, ensuring members have engaging and meaningful ways to connect with the community.

1. # Responsibilities

  1. # Supporting the Growth of SIGs and Community Initiatives
- Collaborate with the Director to refine and expand the SIG framework.
- Identify and mentor SIG leaders, assisting with content development and sustainability.
- Encourage SIGs that combine education with gameplay, reinforcing the University's mission.
- Promote SIGs as a core aspect of community identity and engagement.

  1. # Developing Location-Based Community Concepts
- Support initiatives such as missioning and mining hubs to create gathering points.
- Facilitate activities and events that strengthen these communities.

  1. # Fostering Engagement and Social Retention
- Build enthusiasm for new and existing initiatives.
- Provide support and resources for player-driven projects.
- Encourage collaboration between SIGs and other University groups.

1. # Key Qualities
- Strong leadership and mentoring skills.
- Excellent interpersonal and communication abilities.
- Creativity and initiative in developing community strategies.
- Deep understanding of EVE University’s culture and goals.
- Ability to work both independently and collaboratively.
