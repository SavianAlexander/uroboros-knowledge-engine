# Major Conduit

This combat anomaly is found in either Bulwark or Second Liminality systems. It is a harder and more advanced version of the **Minor Conduit** site.

They contain 6 waves of Triglavian NPCs containing 16-20 ships in each.

Clearing this site helps the EDENCOM forces. 

Major Conduits no longer exist.
