# The Graduation Certificate (Gallente)

<noinclude>
Gallente **data center** mission
Agent: **Etien Duloure** (Federal Data Center - Ekuenbiron), **Maray Ygier** (Federal Data Center - Muer), and **Vausitte Yrier** (Federal Data Center - Abenync)
Standings required: none</noinclude>

This is the starting mission for the **Beizan Picholen** mission chain. This mission can be requested from three agents (**Etien Duloure**, **Maray Ygier**, and **Vausitte Yrier**) and thus can be completed three times. The ISK value of the reward is low, but it also awards faction standing.

The mission is simple: fetch one unsigned certificate from a nearby station and the agent will sign it for you. The signed certificate is needed to get the next mission from **Avrue Auz**.
