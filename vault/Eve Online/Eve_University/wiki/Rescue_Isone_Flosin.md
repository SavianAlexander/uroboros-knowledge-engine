# Rescue Isone Flosin

<noinclude>
  - Gallente COSMOS** mission
Agent: **Nilla Elermare**
Agent location: **Contested Gallente Roden Shipyard's Outpost** in Fluekele 
Standings required: 4.00 Gallente Federation</noinclude>

The agent wants you to go looking for her friend. Take the Acceleration Gate near Nilla to the **Contested Gallente Roden Shipyard's Outpost**. Return with Isone's body.

Isone Flosin's DNA is needed in **Aminn Flosin's** Mission.<noinclude>
