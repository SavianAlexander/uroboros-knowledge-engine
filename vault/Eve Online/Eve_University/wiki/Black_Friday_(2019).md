# Black Friday (2019)

- Black Friday** was a limited time event combining a **PLEX** sale, a **sales tax** cut, and a login campaign. The event collectively ran from 27 November to 3 December, 2019, downtime to downtime.

1. # PLEX & SKIN Sale
From the announcement article on 28 November:

In addition, select **SKIN**s were given a 50% discount.

1. # Black Friday Tax Haven

Between 29 November - 2 December, the sales tax was reduced from 5% to 2.5%. The percentage reduction from  still applied, up to a minimum tax of 1.125% with Accounting V.

1. # Black Friday Boosts
