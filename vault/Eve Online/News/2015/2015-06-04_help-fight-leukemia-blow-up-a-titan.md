Greetings capsuleers

As part of an effort to raise money towards Leukemia and Lymphoma research, [Dovinian](//gate.eveonline.com/Profile/Dovinian) has vowed to let the pilots of New Eden destroy his Erebus Titan on the 4th of July at 04:00UTC if he manages to reach $20,000 in donations for the [Leukemia Cup Regatta.](//www.leukemiacup.org/wa/) The tally currently stands at $18,065 as of this article. For more details, see [his post](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&m=5777147) on the EVE Online forums.
