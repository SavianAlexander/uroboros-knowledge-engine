# Tech 3 Strategic Cruiser (T3C) Subsystem & Covert Matrix

Modular strategic cruiser configurations engineered for covert black ops drops, bubble-immune scouting, and wormhole dominance.

---

## 🌐 The 4 Empire Strategic Cruisers
### 🚀 **Tengu (Caldari T3C)**
- **Doctrine Role**: Heavy Missile / Kinetic Missile Damage & Shield Boost Multipliers
- **Covert Hunter Configuration**: `Covert Reconfiguration + Interdiction Nullifier (Instawarp Hunter)`
- **DPS Output**: `750 - 900 DPS`
- **Defensive Profile**: `85,000 EHP Shield / Active Pith X-Type Booster`

### 🚀 **Legion (Amarr T3C)**
- **Doctrine Role**: Heavy Energy Laser / Heavy Assault Missile (HAM) & Armor Resistances
- **Covert Hunter Configuration**: `Covert Reconfiguration + Dissolution Sequencer (100% Armor Resists)`
- **DPS Output**: `650 - 850 DPS`
- **Defensive Profile**: `120,000 EHP Armor / Dual Medium Centii A-Type Reps`

### 🚀 **Proteus (Gallente T3C)**
- **Doctrine Role**: Heavy Blaster Hybrid Turrets / Drone Interfacing / Point Blank Scram Range
- **Covert Hunter Configuration**: `Covert Reconfiguration + Drone Synthesis Projector`
- **DPS Output**: `1,150+ DPS (Void M)`
- **Defensive Profile**: `110,000 EHP Armor Buffer`

### 🚀 **Loki (Minmatar T3C)**
- **Doctrine Role**: Medium Projectile Turrets / Heavy Missiles / Stasis Web Range (42km Webs)
- **Covert Hunter Configuration**: `Covert Reconfiguration + Interdiction Nullifier`
- **DPS Output**: `720 DPS (Auto-cannon / Artillery)`
- **Defensive Profile**: `95,000 EHP Active Shield / Fleet Web Support`

## ⚙️ The 4 Subsystem Families
1. **Core Subsystem**: Determines capacitor capacity, CPU output, and sensor booster effectiveness.
2. **Defensive Subsystem**: Dictates Armor / Shield hitpoints and unlocks **Covert Cloak** capabilities.
3. **Offensive Subsystem**: Dictates high-slot weapon counts (Missile hardpoints, Turret hardpoints, Drone bandwidth).
4. **Propulsion Subsystem**: Determines agility, warp speed, and unlocks **Interdiction Nullification**.
