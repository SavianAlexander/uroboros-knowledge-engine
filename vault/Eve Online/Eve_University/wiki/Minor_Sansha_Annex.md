# Minor Sansha Annex

- Minor Sansha Annex** is an **Unrated Complex** found by scanning in Low Security systems in Sansha infested space.
1. # Walkthrough
This site is very similar to **Sansha Watch**.
Upon warping to the site you will land at an acceleration gate.
  1. # First Pocket

|}

Approach the acceleration gate to trigger the proximity wave.

|}

|}

  1. # Second Pocket

  - Blitz:** Destroy the True Sansha battlecruiser, bookmark his wreck, warp out and come back in a few minutes to pick up the loot.

Full room aggro on warp-in. The fleet is spread out in several groups at significant distances across the pocket so immediate incoming damage will be low. Most of them will be ~50 km away; one battleship will spawn at a high angle above you 90 km away and can be ignored.

True Sansha Battlecruiser will not be immediately present on warp-in. After roughly 30 seconds in the pocket a loud alarm will sound in local and (sometimes) spawn the commander. Shoot the Mind Control Tower and kill the Centus Militant Supervisor if no commander spawns to despawn the site.
