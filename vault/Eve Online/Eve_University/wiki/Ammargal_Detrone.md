# Ammargal Detrone

- Ammargal Detrone** is an **Amarr COSMOS** agent located at Nidupad - Imperial Palace Complex. He gives the continuation of the chains of missions of **Zach Himun**

1. # King of the Hill (7 of 13)

This item can be obtained by completing the missions with **Zach Himun**.

1. # King of the Hill (8 of 13)

1. # King of the Hill (9 of 13)

The Enigma Cypher Book can be dropped by **belt rat**s in the Araz constellation.

1. # King of the Hill (10 of 13)

Pretty unique mission. The BPC needs 10x Encoding Matrix Component (from **belt rat**s in Araz) and 1x Standard Decoding Device (from mission 8). It takes approximately 2 hours (depending on skills) to build it.
