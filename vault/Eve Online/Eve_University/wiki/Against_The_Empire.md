# Against The Empire

- Against the Empire** is a Level 3 mission involving combat against the **Amarr Empire**.

Single pocket containing 2 wrecks. Hostile NPCs will begin spawning once a pilot approaches the wrecks within 10km, and new enemy targets will spawn as existing targets are destroyed. Enemy NPCs will auto-aggro on spawn.
