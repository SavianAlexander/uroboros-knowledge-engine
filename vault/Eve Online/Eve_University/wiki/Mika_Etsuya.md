# Mika Etsuya

- Mika Etsuya** is an level 2 agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located in Vahunomi - Cactus Mill Lookout.

1. # Protecting the Vault - Stirring the Nest (1 of 4)

Drifter Spur drop from Kazka Drifters. Those can be found in COSMOS sites.

1. # Protecting the Vault - Bad Money (2 of 4)

Bag of Counterfeit Credits drop from Dry River criminals they can be found in **Contested Kazka Headquarters** - Kazka Control Tower.

1. # Protecting the Vault - Counterfeit Courier (3 of 4)

Transport the 500x Bag of Counterfeit Credits a few jumps to State War Academy station in the Ishisomo system.

1. # Protecting the Vault - Dry Credit Death (4 of 4)

Parts of Printing Machine can be found in the Dry River warehouse at **Contested Caldari Lai Dai Refinery** - Contested Plagioclase field.

  - WARNING**: this item spawns only **once per day** and is usually farmed. It might be difficult to obtain without paying substantial amounts of ISK.
