# Serpentis Forsaken Den

- Serpentis Forsaken Den** is a **combat anomaly** found in **Serpentis** infested regions. It is the level 4 variant of the **Serpentis Den**.

1. # Walkthrough
Single pocket with multiple waves of rats.

With the initial group and 5 triggered spawns, a total of 32 ships.
