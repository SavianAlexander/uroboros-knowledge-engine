## Overview
ISD Tipene, ISD IonCharge, and ISD Athechu recently held the first in a series of seminar based on subjects in EVE Online. This first seminar was about "Survival and Victory in Combat." You can see a log of the seminar here and discuss it on our forums in their New Citizens Q&A thread.
