}}  The *Rogue Drone Emergence* is a Null-Security **Mercenary Tactical Operation** site that appears when activated by the owner of a **Mercenary Den**.

When a capsuleer initiates warp to the site, it will appear in the Overview of every capsuleer in the same System.

If left undamaged, Rogue Drone Ships will slowly repair their shield overtime. On waves 1-3, they often target drones. They deal EM & Thermal damage with lasers, and Kinetic & Explosive damage with their missiles. This site has four waves in total. The boss has the capability to warp off and warp back at a closer location to your ship should you be too far away.

In this site variant, all ships have 0 resists except the cruiser boss, and have effectively little EHP. In return, they have large ship numbers per wave. 

Always make sure to take care of the the neuting ships first. They can and WILL neut your capacitor until there is nothing left; make sure you choose your targets wisely.
