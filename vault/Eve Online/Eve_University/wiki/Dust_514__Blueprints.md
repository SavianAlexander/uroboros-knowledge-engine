# Dust 514: Blueprints

1. # Blueprints
Blueprints are pieces of gear that will never run out. Most of these pieces of gear are bought with **Aurum** but the starting **Militia Gear** are the exception to this requirement for **Aurum**.

Blueprints are designated by their blue background on the item icon as can be seen on the image below.

Players may refer to **BPOs** (Blueprint Original) which is EVE's term for a blueprint that will never run out. As of now dust doesn't have **BPCs** (Blueprint copies) which in terms of EVE are blueprints that have a limited number of copies
