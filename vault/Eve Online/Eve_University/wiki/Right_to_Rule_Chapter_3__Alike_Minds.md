# Right to Rule/Chapter 3: Alike Minds

- Alike Minds** is the Sansha path of third chapter of the Amarr epic arc. It has the dangers or low security space but the missions do not have large ships in them and they are fast.

The missions are about equal in difficulty to the L3 pirate epic arcs so a destroyer is a good choice for a ship.

Additionally, you will need to bring a data analyzer.

The missions will have you fight against Amarr ships but you will not get any standing losses from ship kills.
