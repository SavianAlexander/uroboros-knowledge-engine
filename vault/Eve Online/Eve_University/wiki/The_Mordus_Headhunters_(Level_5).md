  1. # Notes
- No Neuts means this mission is active tank friendly
- Spawns will aggro teammates and drones, so trigger spawns before friendly Logistics ship(s) enter the room
- Maximum damage dealing of all ships in 3rd Pocket is 1,980 HP/s (72% Kin, 28% Therm)
- Orbit ranges:
  - Frigates - 8km
  - Cruisers - 18km
  - Battleships - 50km
- Mordus Fleet Commander might Drop a x03 implant
