We have reached the third level of sharing on [www.eveisreal.net](//www.eveisreal.net). The reward for this is 1,500 Aurum to be distributed to the characters with the highest SP on all active accounts.

If you've not yet checked out the [EVE is Real](//www.eveisreal.net) site, we encourage you to do so. You can see thousands of images and videos uploaded by your fellow EVE Online players, and then spread the word by sharing. Our next milestone will result in another 2,000 Aurum being distributed to every EVE Online player.
