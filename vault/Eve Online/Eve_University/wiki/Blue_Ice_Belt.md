# Blue Ice Belt

A Blue Ice Belt is a **Cosmic Anomaly** found in High and Low Security Space in the **Gallente quadrant**. It can be **mined** with a properly fitted **mining ship**. A Blue Ice Belt can only be found in one of the ice systems (see below for the full list of High Sec and Low Sec systems).

While sharing the same name, the contents of a Blue Ice Belt are different in High Sec, Low Sec higher than 0.1 and in Low Sec with security 0.1.
