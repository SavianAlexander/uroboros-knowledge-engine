1. # Summary
The **Prophecy Navy Issue** is the faction variant of the drone-focused Amarr Battlecruiser, the **Prophecy**. In exchange for its previous bonus to armor resists, it receives a bonus to armor hitpoints which compound with its increased base hitpoints as a faction hull to give this ship an impressive buffer tank. It also receives bonuses to Medium Energy Turrets, though it has a slightly smaller bonus to drones than the original Prophecy. The shuffled bonuses clearly favor turrets over drones, though a mixed damage approach is still viable.

1. # Skills
Although the Prophecy Navy Issue can technically be flown with very low skills, it would be advisable to train the skills necessary for a T2 armor tank and T2 Medium Energy Turrets before flying this ship in any PvP encounter. T2  and  would also be a wise investment, though heavy drone skills are not necessary since the Prophecy Navy Issue does not have the drone bandwidth for them.

1. # Tactics
With its massive buffer tank and middling speed, the Prophecy Navy Issue fits most naturally into a brawling role using close range ammunition in its pulse lasers, but high skill pilots may opt to take advantage of the generous fitting space to fit an oversized propulsion module and kite using Scorch ammunition. In large fleets, beam lasers may also be equipped. Depending on the role it plays on the battlefield, the utility high slot may be filled a command burst, an energy neutralizer, or a drone link augmentor.
