# Ambush the Convoy (Blood Raiders) (Level 3)

- Ambush the Convoy** is a combat mission with multiple racial and level variants. The **Blood Raiders** variant can be Level 3 or 4.

1. # Details

Objective item drops from the last hostile NPC destroyed. The 10 prisoners take up 75 cargo space.
