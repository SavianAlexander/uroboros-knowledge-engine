1. # Treasure Chamber
You will need a Bastion Master Key in order to enter this room. Bastion Master key is provided as a time reward bonus for completing "Blood Hunt - Morbid Burden (3 of 5)" mission for **Sheroz Amokin** or as a reward for completing "Pilgrim - Festival" mission for **Ormon Parsik**. The key is consumed when used.

There are 3 groups of NPCs: 4 destroyers on warp-in point, the group at Altar of the Blessed and the group at Generator building. The destroyers will aggro when you land on the grid, other groups are passive until they are either approached or attacked. After clearing the room, meaning destroying all NPCs and both Altar of the Blessed and Generator Building, both buildings will respawn after about 5 minutes of their destruction. However, building respawn will happen only once until next downtime. After both buildings are destroyed for the second time, only 4 destroyers from the warp-in point will keep respawining. 

Attacking battleship group will aggro all ships. Loot Altar of the Blessed for The Apocryphon, loot Generator Building for Generator Debris.
DPS in this room will be very high and frigates can point and web, giving the battleships the opportunity to wreck you in pieces. Bring a very tanky ship or use long-ranged weapons.
