1. # Summary
The **Ishtar** can deal some serious drone damage and maintain a good tank. Though only cruiser-sized, the Ishtar has the bandwidth to field a full flight of heavy or sentry drones, and it has a 375 drone bay, allowing it to carry several different drone flights for tactical flexibility.

Pilot should note that the Ishtar is not exactly a straight upgrade from its base hull, the **Vexor**. The Ishtar has more bonuses, but *all* of those bonuses apply to drones of one kind or another, while the Vexor has one bonus to drones and one bonus to hybrid turrets. The Vexor's damage ceiling in a blaster brawling fit is very high. What the Ishtar offers beyond the Vexor's capabilities is the capacity to use full flights of heavy or sentry drones.

With no turret bonuses, the high slots on the Ishtar can be used for **capacitor warfare** or **salvaging**, or fitted with projectile turrets for additional (unbonused but capacitor-free) DPS.

1. # Skills
The Ishtar is primarily a drone boat. It deserves T2 drones, and since most of its DPS comes from drones, **decent drone skills** are required to fly the ship to its full potential. Like other HACs, the Ishtar has tight fitting requirements, and pilots should brush up their **fitting skills**; like other HACs, it should also be given a T2 tank.
