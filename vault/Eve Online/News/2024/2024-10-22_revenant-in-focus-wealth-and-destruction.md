## Overview

**Capsuleers!**

Dive into new dimensions of conflict, extract wealth from new resources, and command the combat zones using the powerful tools coming with the Revenant expansion on 12 November. Rise to supremacy in nullsec and beyond with two beacons of opportunity and challenge: formidable new Deathless ships with a deadly weapon that causes damage over time and resource-generating mercenary dens. 


## A Den of Wealth


Get ready for risk and riches, with new **mercenary dens** – small but volatile outposts anchored to orbital skyhooks around temperate planets. Use these dens to turn workforce into encrypted infomorphs, highly prized by the Deathless, and open the doors to smaller, fast-paced skirmishes. 

They’re not just treasure troves though, but powder kegs waiting to explode into fierce battles for control. Anyone can sneak in and set one up, turning nullsec into a playground of covert ops and daring raids. Manage the delicate balance of development and anarchy, and you could reap incredible rewards – or watch your enemies profit from your downfall.


## New Instruments of Destruction


Prepare for close-quarters carnage, ambush your enemies, and exert your dominance in nullsec with the Deathless’ newest creations: the nimble **Tholos destroyer** and the brutal **Cenotaph battlecruiser**. 

A long awaited combination of Minmatar resilience and Caldari precision, these warships thrive on stealth, destruction, and raw aggression. Their secret weapon? The SCARAB breacher pod – a fearsome tool that sends shriker clones to wreak havoc on enemy ships, tearing through shields, armor, and hull from the inside. These deadly ships excel at stealth with the use of covert ops cloaks, and are resistant to stasis webifiers, staying fast and dangerous in the thick of battle.
