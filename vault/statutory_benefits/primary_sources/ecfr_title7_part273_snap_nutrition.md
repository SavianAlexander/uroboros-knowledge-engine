### § 273.9 - Income and deductions (Standard, 20% Earned, Excess Shelter)
**(a) Income eligibility standards.** Participation in the Program shall be limited to those households whose incomes are determined to be a substantial limiting factor in permitting them to obtain a more nutritious diet.
(1) The gross income eligibility standards for SNAP shall be **130 percent** of the Federal income poverty levels.
(2) The net income eligibility standards for SNAP shall be **100 percent** of the Federal income poverty levels.

**(d) Income deductions.** Deductions shall be allowed only for the following household expenses:
(1) **Standard deduction**: A standard deduction for each household that is indexed annually by the Secretary ($198 for 1-3 members, $208 for 4-5 members, $246 for 6+ members).
(2) **Earned income deduction**: **Twenty percent (20%)** of gross earned income.
(6) **Excess shelter deduction**: Monthly shelter costs in excess of 50 percent of the household's income after all other allowable deductions, capped at the statutory limit ($672/month) for households without an elderly or disabled member.
---
### § 273.10 - Determining household eligibility and benefit levels
**(e) Calculating net income and benefit levels.**
(1) Subtract standard deduction, 20% earned income deduction, and dependent care costs to calculate adjusted income.
(2) Calculate excess shelter deduction and subtract from adjusted income to arrive at Net Monthly Income.
(3) Multiply Net Monthly Income by 30 percent (0.30) and subtract from the Maximum Monthly Allotment table.
(4) Enforce minimum monthly benefit of $23.00 for eligible 1- and 2-person households.
