1. # Skills
- It is essential to train  to IV in order to use the Covert Ops Cloaking Device II.
- , ,  and  make a good skills base for scanning.
- If you intend to develop long term scouting skills, which will require fast accurate scans in a ship that's so nimble it warps almost instantly, it will become essential to train the Covops and Scanning skills to V.

1. # Tactics
- Scanning, learn how to scan quickly and effectively by 'Play Hunting' mission ships in High Sec
- Explore Wormholes, with 'Bookmark Breadcrumbs' so you develop skills using probes to navigate
- Practice 'Punting', where you can drop a tight scan formation on a gate, so you can punt your fleet onto an enemy
- But most of all, practice, practice and practice even more the art of re-cloaking in less than the cycle of one second, it will make you almost immortal

1. # Notes
In ancient Greek mythology, Helios was the god associated with the Sun, or a personification of the Sun: in ancient Greek, *helios* was the word for the Sun. Some writers treated Helios as the son of the titan **Hyperion**, while other writers considered Hyperion and Helios to be different names for the same deity.
