The nomination period for the inaugural [E-ON Awards](//myeve.eve-online.com/eonawards/) is soon to come to an end (in about 24 hours actually), so if you were holding off your vote for whatever reason, now’s the time to start thinking about making a decision as to who (or what) you think deserves to go forward as a candidate to maybe win a trophy. If you have no idea what I’m on about, read the [blog](//myeve.eve-online.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&bid=414) about this, then if you have any questions post your comments in the accompanying [thread](//myeve.eve-online.com/ingameboard.asp?a=topic&threadID=458843).

The final round of voting will commence just as soon as the next [E-ON](//secure.eve-online.com/eon/) comes out next month (details of which are [now up](//www.eve-online.com/eon/)), but don’t think you have to be a subscriber to the magazine in order to be able to vote, or to read who the final nominations are – that will be revealed here shortly after E-ON is sent out.
