# Celebrity look alike contest at EVE Report

- **Date**: 2011-01-17T16:50:53.000Z
- **Category**: news
- **Author**: Svarthol
- **Source**: https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/celebrity-look-alike-contest-at-eve-report
- **Tags**: #news

## Overview
Celebrating the release of the new character creator in EVE Online: Incursion 1.1, EVE Report invites everyone to the world of fame and glamour. Until January 21, the players are welcome to create a "look-alike" of a real life celebrity and try to get that 15 minutes of fame, and maybe even a prize.
