# Rogue Swarm Alert (2017)

The **Rogue Swarm Alert** was a **Rogue Drone**-themed event that ran from June 13, 2017 and until June 27, 2017. The event consisted of combat sites that appeared as warpable beacons in the in-game overview. Finishing these sites awarded players with valuable loot such as PLEX, ship skins, drone componets and cerebral accelerators.

1. # Walkthrough

Sometime between the waves, a group of hostile faction drones will spawn. They deal negligible damage and killing them is not necessary for the commander wave to spawn.

  1. # Commander wave
There are three different commander waves for this event, each one spawing a different ship size. Only one of the three commander waves will spawn at any given site.

1. # Rewards
The swarm overmind ship is guaranteed to drop one skin. The skins included this event are Justice, Kador, Wiyrkomi and Valimor Legacy. The overmind can also drop one Rogue Swarm Accelerator that gives +10 to all attributes for 24 hours. The duration can be extended with  to up to 48 hours. Additionally it is possible to gain BPC for drone faction drones, drone materials, drone alloys and PLEX.
