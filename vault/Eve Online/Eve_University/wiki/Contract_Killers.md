# Contract Killers

- Contract Killers** is a expedition combat site that can be gotten from the **Serpentis Base** unrated complex. While the Serpentis Base only spawn in null-sec, this expedition can lead to both low-sec and null-sec. Contract Killers is considered a rare expedition with very low chance of happening.

It consists of 2 parts.

1. # Walkthrough

  1. # Contract Killers - Part 1
The room is ungated, meaning you can warp around in it, and people can warp directly to you.

  1. # Contract Killers - Part 2
The room is ungated, meaning you can warp around in it, and people can warp directly to you.

Take care of the NPCs but ignore the factory, as although it may look like an overseer structure it doesn't actually drop any deadspace items other than random contraband. The factory reps shield and armor so you will need around 1500dps just to break the tank. It fires Citadel torpedos which are relatively weak and can be tanked easily. The factory webs you up to 50km out.

The next stage is to shoot the structure named "Serpentis Drug Lab". To clarify, this is not the structure that shoots you or repairs itself. This will trigger another wave of ships about 160-180k from the beacon.

One battleship is named "Quertah Bleu", and is the overseer for the site. He moves very quickly and hits for a fair amount of damage and has exceptional tracking and damage application against fast moving targets. His tank, however, is nothing special. He is extremely fast and can be kited away from the rest of the wave easily, allowing you to kill and loot him.

1. # Loot Table

Quertah may drop Core C-types, and possibly a Vigilant BPC. Salvage from wreck is Tech 1.
Quertah can also drop a 1-run Vindicator BPC.
