# The Interstellar Convergence (2022)

- The Interstellar Convergence** is an event running from the 13th of January 2022 to the 1st of February 2022, downtime to downtime. It revolves around relic sites (**Warp Matrix Convergence**) that contain blueprints and materials for warp matrix filament and the exploration and combat sites you can access using those filaments.

There are three exploration filaments (Curious Warp Matrix Filament, Enigmatic Warp Matrix Filament, and Mysterious Warp Matrix Filament) that can be used by a single **exploration frigate** or **covert ops** ship only. There are no hostiles in the pocket of spacetime accessed by the filament.

The four combat filaments (Precarious Warp Matrix Filament, Hazardous Warp Matrix Filament, Dangerous Warp Matrix Filament, and Perilous Warp Matrix Filament) can be accessed by one or two T1 Cruisers; the EVE Online websites recommends using the community-supplied fittings.

1. # Sites

- **Warp Matrix Convergence** (high-sec)
