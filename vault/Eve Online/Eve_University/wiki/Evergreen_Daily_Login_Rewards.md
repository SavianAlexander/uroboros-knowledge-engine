# Evergreen Daily Login Rewards

The Evergreen Daily Login Rewards was a system that ran from 2020 until 10 June 2024 with a rework in the middle at 5 April 2022. In the **Equinox** expansion in 2024 it was merged with **AIR Daily Goals**.

  - 512px|right**
The **daily login rewards** were small rewards for logging in each day. Each day's set of rewards became available at **server downtime**, that is, at 11:00 **EVE Time**.

Accounts received one randomly-selected item from a range of **drugs**, **filaments**, and ship **SKIN**s, and skill points. SKINs were preferentially given out based on which SKINs the account didn't had yet if possible. SKINs were pulled from Halcyon Dawn and Carmine Horizon patterns as well as other previous event SKINs such as Strikeflight Red, Frontier Safeguarder, Zakura Hansei, Purity of the Throne, and more. The boosters would pull from the log-in only Halcyon B, G, R, and Y line-up.

Alpha accounts could draw from booster tiers I - IV, and received an average of ~50k Skill Points per ~30 days. Omega accounts could draw from booster tiers II - V, and received ~150k Skill Points per ~30 days.

Once claimed, daily login rewards can be redeemed using the Redeem Items window. Items from login rewards can be redeemed to your hangar in station, if you're in a station. Items can always be redeemed to your designated **Home Station** even if you're in space.

Drugs can be redeemed from the same window, but rather than appearing as items in a hangar, their effects are immediately applied to the character. Drugs powerful enough to have side effects have a chance to apply their side effects when used from the Redeem Items window, just as they would have a chance to apply side effects when used from a hangar or cargo hold. The skills ,  and  affect these drugs just as they affect other drugs.

When **CCP** are running a special limited-time event with daily login rewards, the normal daily login rewards still remained available as well. Support has been known to transfer rare SKIN rewards to a Pilot's main account upon request.
