# Transaction Data Delivery

Simple pick-up and deliver mission. Grab the item and bring it back to the agent.

  1. # Notes
On 5 April 2022 this mission was offered by Sisters of EVE(Corp) agent Bappenalle Ygola with a collateral of 100.000 ISK.

Standings to Bappenalle Ygola: 2.0, Sisters of EVE: 5.13, Servant Sisters of EVE: 5.95.

On 7 February 2024 this mission was offered by Sisters of EVE(Corp) agent Alpedurch Soulobert with a collateral of 100.000 ISK.

Standings to Alpedurch Soulobert: 2.0, Sisters of EVE: 8.03, Servant Sisters of EVE: 7.08.

May 24, 2025 - Offered by Wiyrkomi Corp agent Veinen Hyastilen - Collateral 5 million ISK, Rewards 4 Million ISK plus return of collateral. Bonus 1 million ISK.
