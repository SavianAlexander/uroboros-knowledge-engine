1. # Summary
The **Epithal** is a Gallente **Hauler** specialized in transporting **Planetary Industry** (PI) **commodities**. Besides its enormous PI cargo bay (which can only be used to transport PI commodities) it also has a small "normal" hold, and a **Command Center** hold capable of storing six command centers at a time.

Just like with other specialized haulers, the size of the PI commodities cargo hold cannot be increased by fitting modules (e.g. Expanded Cargoholds) to the ship; these will only affect the ship's "normal" cargo hold.

1. # Tactics

  1. # Hull Buffer Tank
Like other Gallente specialized haulers such as the  and the , the  uses a specialized hold as its main hold instead of a cargohold. This fact, alongside the strong hull hitpoints that Gallente ships already get, means that the Epithal can fit a decently sized hull buffer tank using Transverse Bulkhead rigs and Reinforced Bulkhead modules, and it will *not* suffer any negative effects on the size of its specialized PI Commodities hold and Command Center hold, because those modules only decrease *cargohold* size. An Epithal with only a simple hull tank buffer can achieve an EHP over 14,000. While this isn't the largest buffer tank a T1 hauler can achieve, the advantage is that it leaves nearly all of the Powergrid available for fitting, which can include a 50MN Compact MWD and an Improved Cloaking Device II, so the Epithal can also use the **cloak trick** while fielding a decent tank, which is a challenging combination for most other T1 Haulers.
