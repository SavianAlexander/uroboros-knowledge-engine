## Overview
Discover some new EVE content creators in January!

---

Content-consuming capsuleers,

Last July we held a month-long streaming event where we threw open the doors of the official CCP Twitch channel and invited partnered EVE streamers to showcase their content on our platform. It was a great way to introduce the community to a wider variety of creators and all the feedback we got from the streamers and the viewers said we should do it again... so we are!

For the next four weeks through January and into February we will be running the **Winter Guest Streams** and once again we're hosting EVE partners on the official CCP Twitch channel, so make a warm mug of coacoa, settle in and enjoy some EVE Online content!

We have also expanded the GalNet StreamCast Unit SKIN line and during the event these new SKINs will become available for the first time as Twitch Drops with a different SKIN on offer each week. 

**Drops schedule:**


- Week 1: 6 Jan - 12 Jan: **Pioneer** GalNet StreamCast SKIN
- Week 2: 13 Jan - 19 Jan: **Malediction **GalNet StreamCast SKIN
- Week 3: 20 Jan - 26 Jan: **Gila **GalNet StreamCast SKIN
- Week 4: 27 Jan - 2 Feb: **Praxis **GalNet StreamCast SKIN


Although exclusive to this event for the time being these SKINs will be made available again sometime in the future.

So tune in to [CCPTV](https://www.twitch.tv/ccp) throughout January when the guest streamers are live and catch some of the incredible content you might otherwise be missing!
