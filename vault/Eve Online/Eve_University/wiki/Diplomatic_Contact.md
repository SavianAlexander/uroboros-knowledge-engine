# Diplomatic Contact

EVE University manages all diplomatic matters through its Directorate, ensuring a centralized approach by the very people who can act on requests. This includes processing standings requests for out-of-corp blues, alt corps, and alts, investigating reports of aggression, addressing blue-on-blue friendly fire incidents, and handling any other diplomatic issues that may arise. Alliance-to-alliance and corporation-to-corporation diplomacy is handled by the Directorate. If you are an external group looking to collaborate on content opportunities or exchange teaching material, resources or people, you may be better off reaching out to our **Outreach Department**, who will gladly work with you to arrange this. 

1. # Contact forms

For requesting standings on out-of-corp characters, alt corps, or alts, please use [the Ivy League Standings Requests form](https://forms.gle/tu3py9FXjbHRKpK69).

For diplomatic contact to log aggression reports, blue-on-blue incidents, or other diplomatic matters, please use [the Diplomatic Contact form](https://forms.gle/mGNBMZypd58ocUD29).
