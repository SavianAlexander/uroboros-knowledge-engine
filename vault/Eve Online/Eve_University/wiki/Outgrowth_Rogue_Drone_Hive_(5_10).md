# Outgrowth Rogue Drone Hive (5/10)

- Outgrowth Rogue Drone Hive** is a DED 5/10 rated combat site that is found in low and null security space.

Note that the DED 10/10 Rogue Drone site found in null is also called Outgrowth Rogue Drone Hive. See **Outgrowth Rogue Drone Hive (10/10)** for details on that.

1. # Loot
Like with all DED rated Rogue Drone sites the loot is not very good. You will get Overseer's Personal Effects, mineral alloys and some salvage but no deadspace modules or rogue drone parts.
