# Istei Poyri

Istei Poyri is a level 2 agent in Caldari COSMOS located in Airmia at beacon "Station Foundation Site". In case you cannot see him or the beacon, turn on "Agents in Space" and "Beacon" in your overview settings.

1. # Nugoeihuvi Freelancer (1 of 4)

Transport 1 Nugoeihuvi Station Schematics (0.1 m3) to the destination.

1. # Nugoeihuvi Freelancer – Construction Blocks (2 of 4)

Transport 1x Crates of Construction Blocks (70.0 m3) between the stations.

1. # Nugoeihuvi Freelancer – Workers Need Air… (3 of 4)

Purchase (or use **Planetary Industry** to obtain) 137 Oxygen (137.0 m3) for the agent.

1. # Nugoeihuvi Freelancer – Bad News (4 of 4)

Loot 5 Propel Dynamics Dogtag (0.5 m3) from Propel Dynamics rat wrecks in the first or second stage of the **Contested Caldari Lai Dai Refinery** deadspace complex in the Airmia system and bring them to Istei Poyri.
