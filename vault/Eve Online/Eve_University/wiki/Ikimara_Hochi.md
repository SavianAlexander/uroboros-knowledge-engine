# Ikimara Hochi

Ikimara Hochi is a level 3 agent in Caldari COSMOS located in Sakkikainen Settler's Waystation.

1. # Banker's Stash - Stolen Goods (1 of 3)

Provide 10 Jedon's Hekkiren Belonging (500.0 m3) which is dropped from the first stage at the **Contested Guristas Outrider Post** complex in Otomainen.

1. # Banker's Stash- Complete The Transportation (2 of 3)

Transport 250 x Jedon Hekkiren's Belongings (12,500.0 m3) from the agent to Vahunomi X - Moon 4 - State and Region Bank Vault. You need 12,500.0 m3 spare to accept this mission. The items can only be passed all at onces from the agent to you.

1. # Banker's Stash - Bury The Dead (3 of 3)

Transport Hakkuran Brother's Remains (1.0 m3) from the Agent to Usi VI - Moon 23 - State War Academy School. It is quite far away(14 jumps), but you can just use a shuttle
