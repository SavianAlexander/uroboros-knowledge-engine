Notes:
- There are multiple Tasks named "Making a Deal". They have different objectives (buying vs. selling) as well as different items (Materiels vs. Filaments). 1. # Filaments
The Abyssal Battlefield filaments for both hacking and combat portions of the event follow a separate naming convention from standard Abyssal Deadspace filaments.  In the Market browser they show up as Exotic filaments **regardless of what kind of weather they actually use.**  

The weather applied within the site appears to stay relatively consistent with that of normal **Abyssal filaments** (e.g. Electrical provides an EM resist penalty and a capacitor recharge bonus).

Hacking filaments appear to only be available in the Electrical variant.

Each tier of combat filament is only available in one specific type of weather. 
- Tier 1 - Exotic
- Tier 2 - Electrical
- Tier 3 - Gamma
- Tier 4 - Firestorm

Desolate Collapsed Conduit is a **cosmic signature** **relic site** of Level I scanning difficulty. The site is ungated and not deadspaced, so one can warp between the cans, which are spread relatively far apart.  The site will be removed from the probe scan window once one of the cans is hacked.

The site contains:
- Small Cache Wreck (4x)
- Medium Cache Wreck (2x)
- Tower Vault Wreck (1x)
- EDENCOM Surveyor Stash (0-1x) rare spawn

Possible Wreck loot:
- Metal Scraps
- Salvaged Materials
- Triglavian Encrypted Conduit Data (Can be sold at DED NPC stations for 100k ISK apiece)
- *x-yy* Entangled GQD (Abyssal Battlefield Filament Materials)
- Isolated Dimensional Thread
- Jump Filament Blueprint (Tier 1)
  - Ruined Electrical Filament Blueprint (copy)
  - Sinister Exotic Filament Blueprint (copy)

Possible Surveyor Stash loot: 
- Apparel (e.g. Woman's Capsuleer Networker XV Cap)
- Boosters
- Capsuleer Starburst Firework

Unlike normal Abyssals, these sites have only a single room and no enemy NPCs to fight, only a couple of cans (Battle-Sarred Wrecks) to hack. The exit gate is open upon arrival, and can be activated at any time, regardless of how many cans still remain. Consequently, while there is still a timer (30 minutes) running, this Abyssal Room is only lethal for those who voluntarily or accidentally stay beyond this time limit. While the sites will vary in amount of hacking containers and positioning of said containers, they will generally feature a disposition of Triglavian 'battle-scarred' ship wreckage alongside the cache and vault wrecks from K-Space sites. The loot tables of the Filament sites are the same compared to K-Space ones, only with more higher-quality loot from each type on average as well as blueprints for higher-tier Filaments.  A non-exhaustive list of possible cans is shown on the right.

  1. ## Tier 1
The site contains:
- Small Cache Wreck (1x)
- Medium Cache Wreck (1x)
- Damavik Battle-Scarred Wreck (5x)
- Vedmak Battle-Scarred Wreck (1x)
- Drekavac Battle-Scarred Wreck (1x)
- EDENCOM Scout Stash (0-1x) rare spawn

Possible Wreck loot:
- (Reinforced) Metal Scraps
- Salvaged Materials
- Triglavian Encrypted Conduit Data (Can be sold at DED NPC stations for 100k ISK apiece)
- *x-yy* Entangled GQD (Abyssal Battlefield Filament Materiels)
- Isolated Dimensional Thread (another Abyssal Battlefield Filament Materiel)
- Jump Filament Blueprint (Tier 1 and 2)
  - Ruined Electrical Filament Blueprint (copy)
  - Sinister Exotic Filament Blueprint (copy)
  - Devastated Electrical Filament Blueprint (copy)
  - Vicious Electrical Filament Blueprint (copy)

Possible EDENCOM Stashes have a chance to spawn in every Tier and can contain:
- Historic Capsuleer Day Clothing Crate
- Fireworks
- SKINs

- SoCT Capsuleer Day Boosters
- Capsuleer Day XXI Glamourex Boosters
- Cerebral Accelerators
- Implants, very rarely: Genolution Core Augmentation Implant

  1. ## Tier 2
These sites feature 16 wrecks (hacking sites), 4 of which are the new Leshak Battle-Scarred Wrecks.
Consequently, the loot tends to be better as well, with the rarer Entangled GQD Materiels as well as better salvage Materiels dropping. Like Tier 1 sites, it has a chance to spawn an EDENCOM Scout Stash.

  1. ## Tier 3
Tier 3 sites feature about 21 wrecks in total, including 10 Zirnitra Battle-Scarred Wrecks with even higher difficulty hacks [120 Coherence Red core, 100 Coherence Firewall, 70 Coherence Antivirus].
These drop the highest value Battlefield Filament Materiels and high quality salvage Materiels, possibly including Intact Armorplates, as well as top Tier Event Filament Blueprints. 
Very rarely, instead of an EDENCOM Scout Stash, an EDENCOM Expedition Stash spawns. This can is the same difficulty as a Tower Vault hack (yellow core), and tends to contain even more valuable stuff. It is unknown if it has the chance to spawn at lower Tier hacking sites.
