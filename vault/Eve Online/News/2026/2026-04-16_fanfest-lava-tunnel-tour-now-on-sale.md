## Overview
Travel deep underground for a unique EVE experience.

---

Fanfestive capsuleers, 

Pilots landing in Iceland for Fanfest 2026 have a unique opportunity to venture into the Lava Tunnel in a very special EVE-themed tour at Raufarhólshellir. 

You may remember this lava tunnel from last November, when CCP TV broadcast live from the Lava Tunnel to celebrate the launch of the Catalyst expansion. 

The experience begins with transport from Harpa and a guided journey into Raufarhólshellir, where expert guides will take you through the geology, history, and unique formations of this ancient lava tunnel before the environment shifts into an immersive EVE-Themed light and music experience deep underground. After the tour, you will have the chance to sample a limited edition Catalyst Gin. 


## About the Catalyst Gin


The experience also includes a tasting hosted by Mosi Gin inside the tunnel itself, where their expressions age in the cool, stable air of the cave. One of the barrels used was even part of the original Catalyst livestream, making this a fitting follow-up for capsuleers who remember that moment. 

Limited release. Singular taste. Born at the Mosi Weathering Station inside Raufarhólshellir.
