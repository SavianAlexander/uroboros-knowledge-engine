# Dread Guristas Fleet Staging Point

- Dread Guristas Fleet Staging Point** is an **Unrated Complex** found by scanning in **Null Security** systems with **Guristas** presence. It can also be received as an escalation from a **Guristas Forsaken Hub** or **Guristas Hidden Rally Point**.

1. # Walkthrough

This site is fairly hard due to large number of ships and neuting towers. It can be done in a group of T2, T3 or Faction cruisers or a few battleships.

Warping to the site lands you at 0 on an acceleration gate.
