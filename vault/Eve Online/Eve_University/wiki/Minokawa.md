1. # Summary
The **Minokawa** is the Caldari **Force Auxiliary**, a capital-class heavy repair vessel specialized in remote shield boosters. The Minokawa is popular in alliances that use shield-based capital vessels, as its shield-boosting abilities know no equal. Since the usage of the Triage Module prevents the application of remote supportive effects, the Minokawa fields capital-sized shield boosters to keep itself alive under fire.

1. # Notes
In the indigenous folklore of the Philippines, the Minokawa is a gigantic and dangerous dragon-like bird, large enough that it can swallow (or cover) the sun, explaining the occurrence of eclipses.
