# Breeding Facility

Each energy neutralizer sentry does 40gj/s for a combined 160gj/s in this mission.

  1. # Warp-in
There are many triggers to be aware of in the initial group. The Stasis tower each cause a Wave 2 to spawn. Each neutralizer tower also causes a Wave 3 to spawn. In addition there is also a timed spawn Wave 1.
