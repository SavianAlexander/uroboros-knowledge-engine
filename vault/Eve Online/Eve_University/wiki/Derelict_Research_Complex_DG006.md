# Derelict Research Complex DG006

1. # Location and Difficulty
This site is an escalation of data sites found in the **Etherium Reach** and **Perrigen Falls** regions.
    - Abandoned Research Complex DG003**
    - Abandoned Research Complex DG018**

Escalation message
"Amongst the research material you managed to retrieve, you find the coordinates to a nearby high-security research center."

The site follows many of the same mechanics as normal drone data sites, being impossible to fail hacks and spawning weak rats. This site cannot escalate further. The site has a total of 3 cans with increasing difficulty and higher chances of 'Augmented' BPC dropping.

1. # Loot
High chance for 30/60/120 run 'Augmented' BPC's

Try to cargo scan the cans and pick only the valuable cans. Then return after downtime and hack the cans again. This will give you twice the loot as the site's loot respawns at downtime
