The Angel Project is an initiative founded to help new players get themselves established in EVE by providing them with ISK, skillbooks, ships, advice, and other goodies. And right now, it’s turning 2 years old! In celebration, they are holding a giveaway to hand some awesome prizes out to those same rookies!

Included are PLEX, some awesome ship packs, and more. In addition, CCP is throwing more PLEX, CCP Mystery Codes as well as an EVE Collector's Edition into the pool!

Check out how to enter at the Angel Project website [here](//www.sindelsuniverse.com/happyhappybirthday/).
