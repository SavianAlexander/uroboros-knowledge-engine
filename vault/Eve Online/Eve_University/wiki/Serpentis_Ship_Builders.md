# Serpentis Ship Builders

Video: [[Gila](https://youtu.be/Wowy92tus1Q) 2022-09-01]

  1. # Single Pocket
Kill all the Serpentis ships and the "Partially constructed Megathron" to complete the mission.
Everything is disguised as "Serpentis Looter" except the Lord Admiral which is disguised as "Colonel 'Bat' Guano".
Everything aggroes at jump-in. Easier than most standard missions.

|}

  - Standings loss:**
- Serpentis: -0.3% (Partially constructed Megathron)
