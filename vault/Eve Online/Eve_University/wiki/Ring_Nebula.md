1. # Defenders
None - no rats spawn in this site. 

1. # Minable Gas Clouds

3 Golden Mykoserocin 2k units each, 6k units total. Each cloud is 100 Km away from each other. 

1. # Location
These sites can be found in [Lonetrek Region](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Lonetrek#const) (both high and low security space). They have ruins of old Caldari station on warp in.
