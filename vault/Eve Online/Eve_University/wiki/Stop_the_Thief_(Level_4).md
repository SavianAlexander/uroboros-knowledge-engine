Video guide: [Navy Raven](https://youtu.be/QwEH_wryLys), [Sentry Dominix](https://youtu.be/1ZoJKhnDLd8), [Maelstrom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d-XCXLfuY8A), [Gila](https://youtu.be/1qcPABphcMU), [Manticore (Blitz)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z7l5AbSSM-0) 2024-04-21

Single ungated normal space pocket. Initial Group aggro at warp-in. Spawn may aggro drones.
You may [Fleet] warp to preferred range distance.

"The Shadow" drops the required reports. Unlike in other missions the item is added directly to your inventory instead of dropping a container in space.

  - Tip:** Start with the Grunt Cruisers to reduce incoming DPS from the spawn. Watch out for any scrambling Frigates and shift your primary target accordingly.

- Optional Approach*
- Warp in, align, kill the Overlord, instantly warp out before scramblers lock on, then warp back at 50- 100km, make sure you're outside of Cruiser's range. Then aggro and use a MWD to separate the frigates. Count on webbing if Frigates come too close! After this keep aligning of course.
- Select "warp-to" distance between 50- 100km, kill all the initial ships and the **Overlord** last then pick off the spawn as they approach.
- Tank the Overlord and snipe everything else, Micro Jump and snipe the Overlord and Spawn.

  - Warning:** High EM damage from Shadow's Grunts

  - Blitz:**
- Eliminate the Mercenary Overlord battleship to trigger the spawn. Watch out for any incoming Mercenary Wingman Frigates that web/scramble.
- Eliminate Shadow. The *Reports* are automatically transferred to your ship cargo. Warp out (any *Reports* sold by NPC Corps on the market may be used).
（A Gila with 44k EHP even resist passive shield tank doing this way without destroying ship that web/scramble, remain 5%~15% shield when warp out, YMMV. Small delay in operation may cause ship lost, use at own risk.)
