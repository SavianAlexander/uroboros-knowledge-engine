__NOTOC__<onlyinclude></onlyinclude>
1. # Class Overview
This Class introduces the concept of Gas mining - or Huffing - in EVE. 

This class covers the following topics:

- Why should you mine Gas?
- Required Skills and fitting Ships
- How to find a Gas Site and how to Bookmark
- Gas Mining in Low and NullSec Space
  - Traveling to Low/NullSec Space; Chemical Lab Sites
- Mining Gas in Wormhole Space
  - Entering Wormholes, Creating Safe Spots, Ninja Huffing
- Q&A
