### Frosty capsuleers,


Winter Nexus 2024 is here! Until 7 January, New Eden is transformed by the chilling beauty of the Volatile Ice Storms sweeping across highsec, lowsec, and nullsec space, offering icy arenas to explore, battle, and mine.

Take on Sansha’s forces in your battlecruiser, battleship, or heavy assault cruiser. Explore frozen sites and test your hacking skills or delve into Yoiul Festival resource sites. Industrious pilots can earn up to 100 PLEX by completing seasonal challenges, along with a host of other prizes waiting in the winter storms. In addition, there are exceptional rewards on offer for simply taking flight every day, including EverMarks, unique SKINs, patterns and nanocoatings, and up to 650,000 Skill Points. 

If you’re looking for something extra special to decorate your ships with, you can grab the new and exclusive **Crystalflake Comet pattern from the Paragon Hub** for a limited time! 

Winter Nexus is accessible for veterans and new capsuleers alike! Use the Expert System reward earned by braving the storms to equip yourself with the skills required to dive deeper into the action. Returning players will find this a perfect opportunity to rediscover New Eden with engaging activities and powerful rewards to jump back into the action.

For even greater rewards, upgrade to Omega to unlock dual-track gifts, including bigger Skill Point bonuses, EverMarks, and exclusive SKINs.
