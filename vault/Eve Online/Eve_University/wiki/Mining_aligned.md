# Mining aligned

To be "aligned" in EVE usually means travelling in a straight line towards a potential warp destination at 75% or more of your ship's maximum speed. When a ship is already aligned, it can warp near-instantly on command along its line of alignment.

For mining ships which need to be able to make a quick getaway, it is useful to be able to mine while staying aligned. This page explains how.

1. # What you will need

# A medium Higgs Anchor rig
# A fast ship
# Your **Procurer**

1. # Preparation

# Install the Higgs Anchor rig on your barge. This will slow a Procurer down to 50 m/s max speed.
# In your fast ship, go to your instadock at the structure in which you are based.
# Find the rock you want to mine. It's best if you try to find two of the same type close to each other.
# Warp at 20km to the rock.
# Lock the rocks you want to mine so that you can easily see distances.
# Prepare to create Bookmark 1.   -  to bring it up and type in the name.  Don’t press "ok" yet!
# Manually pilot to pass near to the rock, but not directly into it.  (Double-click in space)
# Watch your distances. Once you’re just within your mining range, make your first bookmark.
# Prepare to create bookmark 2.
# Keep watching your distances. They will go down, then back up. Once you are close to your maximum mining range, drop Bookmark 2.
# Turn your propulsion module on. Keep burning until you are several hundred kilometres away; 400–500 km is good. (This is why you’re using a fast ship!)
# Create Bookmark 2 **safe spot**; call it something like "2 safe"

1. # How to mine

# Warp at 0 to Bookmark 1, If you are too far off, approach Bookmark 1.
# Approach Bookmark 2
# Lock rocks and turn on strip miners
# Set speed to just over 75%. This still lets you instawarp if needed.
# When you get very close to Bookmark 2, approach Bookmark 1.

1. # How to escape
- Have your view set so that you can see the safe bookmark you are going towards:
  - "2 safe" when heading towards Bookmark “2”
  - Instadock when heading towards Bookmark 1.
- When something shows up on grid, or **d-scan** (you are spamming it, correct?) right-click on your safe bookmark and warp at 0. You can then turn off your strip miners and recall your drones. (They might not make it.)
- You’re now either 400km away and can warp to an astrahaus, or you’re tethered and can dock easily.
