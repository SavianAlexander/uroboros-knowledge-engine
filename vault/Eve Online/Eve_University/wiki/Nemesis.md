1. # Summary
The **Nemesis** is the Gallente **Stealth Bomber**. It has the same slot layout as the **Manticore** but considerably lower CPU, making it more difficult to fit. Its saving grace is that it is bonused for thermal damage, which is the least commonly resisted damage type.
