The Abyssal Deadspace speedrun contest is an event template by Devola Gestalt; most of the information on this wiki page is copied from their post on the Events Planning forum.

  1. # Notes for the event organiser

- Filaments can't be activated less than 1000km of each other: it's recommended that the event organiser have a collection of bookmarks in the event system that are 1000km from and still on-grid each other to give out amongst participants.
- The organiser give a different bookmark to each participant at formup: this is where they'll light their filament.
- 1st fit check: people link their fit in chat.
- Every participants gets into a cruiser (compliant with fit rules below) with a T1 filament of their choice, get into space and warp to their bookmark.
- At the organiser's signal, everyone lights his/her filament at the same time.
- The first one to get back alive (coming back by the clone bay doesn't work :) ) is the winner and get a price! To decide between close finishes, participants are required to X-up in local as soon as they come back from their pocket. Local chat order determines the winner.
- Optional 2nd fit check: people can get ship/cargo scanned on grid. Either before a round, or after (much faster, as you only need to check the 1st few people who won a prize).

  1. # Event goals
- An introduction to abyssal deadspace sites for newbro abyss runners...
- ... while still being interesting/challenging to veterans, by being a speed contest
- This is also a fitting/theorycrafting event. Event rules allow many fits/playstyles.

  1. # Fitting guidelines
This event will have restrictions on allowed hulls & fits. The goal of these restrictions is to prevent this event from turning into a bling contest with multi-billion ISK cruisers, to keep it accessible and fair for everybody:
- -- subjective/arbitrary restrictions. Other organisers can use different rules if they wish so*
- Only T1 hulls. No faction/pirate/T2/T3 allowed.
- Only T1/T1-meta/T2 modules. No mutaplasmid modules. No polarized weapons.
- Only T1 rigs.
- Only T1/T2 ammo, or faction ammo from the 4 main faction (Caldary/Gallente/Amarr/Minmatar). No pirate ammo.
- Only T1/T2 drones. No Integrated/Augmented/Faction/Pirate.
- Nanite paste & (navy) cap boosters allowed.
- No implants / no drugs.
