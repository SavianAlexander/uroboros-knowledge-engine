# Christer Fuglesang

- Christer Fuglesang** is a level 3 agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located at Autaris VIII - Moon 5 - CONCORD Bureau.

1. # Song Of Birds

Location: Kaimon {{ColorSecurityRating|}}

Fairly easily done despite the prompt to bring friends solo with a t2 ab beam tormentor. Initial wave after popping the structures, finishing a group of 2 will spawn more frigates, at a certain point the mission was done despite more NPCs on grid, may only require the initial group be killed. Be warned; if you attack the particle accelerator, it will kill your ship. Mission competed after I destroyed the Drone Energy Succubus.
