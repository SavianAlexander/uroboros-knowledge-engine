# Sydri Namian

- Sydri Namian** is a level 4 **Minmatar COSMOS** agent located in Hjoramold - **Lord Bastion**.

1. # Treasure Hunter - The Vherokior Medallion (1 of 4)

Mission objective is dropped by Arrak Nutan (battleship) NPC in **Contested Minmatar Legendary Stronghold** in Hraldar's Overrun Station pocket.

1. # Treasure Hunter - Hraldar's Sculpture (2 of 4)

Mission objective can be found in **Bazeri Palen** site in Hjoramold.

1. # Treasure Hunter - Telligman's Stone (3 of 4)

Mission objective is dropped when destroying the Bastion Museum structure, which is near the agent itself.

Bastion Museum is guarded by inactive (blue) NPCs. They remain dormant when attacking, destroying and looting the structure.
