# Stargate Trailblazers

The Stargate Trailblazers event begins on 25 May 2021 and is running until 3 June 2021. During this event, the Empires will be working to gather resources for the construction of new stargate routes around New Eden.

1. # Purpose and Background
The four empires are each planning expansions to the stargate network to advance their respective interests. These new stargates will be online in June 2021. The empires are putting their immense resources towards the completion of these new stargate connections, and they are more than capable of constructing the gates without capsuleer assistance if needed. However capsuleers are incredibly powerful and efficient resource harvesters so all four empires are offering to reward capsuleers who assist with collecting resources for these construction projects. The newly planned connections for each empire are:

  1. # New Gate Connections
Hykkota  (Highsec/The Forge/Caldari) ↔ Ahbazon  (Lowsec/Genesis/Amarr)

Saminer  (Lowsec/Tash-Murkon/Amarr) ↔ F7-ICZ  (Nullsec/Stain/Sansha)

Kenninck  (Lowsec/Placid/Gallente) ↔ Eggheron  (Highsec/Solitude/Gallente)

Irgrus  (Highsec/Metropolis/Minmatar) ↔ Pakhshi  (Highsec/Genesis/CONCORD)

1. # Agency Challenges
Only 3 challenges will be active at a time.

- A Boatload - 5 points - In any Mining Expedition, deposit 10,000 units of ore into an Orca.
- A Hauler's Cargo - 10 points - In any Mining Expedition, deposit 20,000 units of ore into an Orca.
- A Freighter's Frenzy - 15 points - In any Mining Expedition, deposit 30,000 units of ore into an Orca.
- Point Defense - 5 points - In any Mining Expedition, Destroy 10 Pirates.
- Set the charge - 10 points - In any Mining Expedition, Destroy 20 Pirates.
- No Man's Land - 15 points - In any Mining Expedition, Destroy 30 Pirates.
- Silver or Sweeter - 30 points - In any Mining Expedition, gain the rank of Silver or higher five times within the time limit.
- Go for Gold - 40 points - In any Mining Expedition, gain the rank of Gold three times within the time limit.

Once the construction of the stargates has been complete, the top ten Capsuleers and top five Corporations contributing at each construction site will be honored with a series of new monuments set to be built near the respective gates.
