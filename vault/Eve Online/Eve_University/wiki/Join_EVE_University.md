# Join EVE University

- EVE University** **[E-UNI]** is the oldest and largest in-game corporation specifically designed to train new players. We offer regular classes, fleets, and events as well as free ships and a variety of other services to help new players experience everything EVE has to offer. Our only mission is to help new players learn as much as possible, to prepare them for a successful career in one of EVE's many other player corporations.  You can read more about **what we offer here**.

If you are looking to join EVE University, please follow the **<u>full</u>** procedure below. This will get your application entered into our queue and ensure you have access to our Discord. Please complete all steps to avoid delays with your application, once your application has been processed by a volunteer you will receive an in-game invitation. There is no need to apply in-game, these applications will be ignored and/or rejected.

Please only apply with the main character you wish to join EVE University on. Information about adding alts to EVE University can be found at  section.

1. # The application process
# Open the [EVE University Alliance Auth](https://auth.eveuniversity.org) in your web browser and log in with EVE Online. You will need to enter an authorisation code for 2 Factor Authentication. Select the character you wish to apply on.
# Once your character is registered, open the [Character Audit](https://auth.eveuniversity.org/audit/r/). Click the "Add Character" button on the top right to register your character. Accept the list of scopes provided on the next page. Repeat this step for any alts you wish to join on.
# Go to the [Services tab](https://auth.eveuniversity.org/services/) to set up Discord. **Discord** is mandatory as it is the communications software we use. Ensure you have a Discord account, then click the Tick button under Discord to link your Discord account to your EVE character. You will be automatically added to the EVE University Discord server. Your nickname on the server will automatically be set to your EVE character name.
# Read through our **Rules page** to ensure these are all rules you are happy to follow.
# Go to the [Applications tab](https://auth.eveuniversity.org/hr/) to submit your application. Click Create Application and EVE University. Agree to the two statements and press Submit.
# Wait for the invitation to join EVE University in game. These are sent by volunteers, and it can sometimes take up to 48 hours to be processed, please be patient.

1. # Applying with an alt character
The process above is only for adding your first character to EVE University. Members are also able to add alt characters to EVE University, but the process is slightly different.  

# Open the [EVE University Alliance Auth](https://auth.eveuniversity.org) in your web browser and log in with EVE Online. You will need to enter an authorization code for 2 Factor Authentication. Select the your main character that is already a member of EVE University.
# Open the [Character Audit](https://auth.eveuniversity.org/audit/r/). Click the "Add Character" button on the top right followed by "New Character -> LOG IN with EVE Online" to register your new character. Select your new character and accept the list of scopes provided on the next page. Repeat this step for any alts you wish to join on.
# Open the [Alt Application Form](https://auth.eveuniversity.org/forms/form/1/) in the Forms section of Alliance Auth. Select your new character from the dropdown and press submit. If the character is not shown in the list repeat step 2 for all characters you want to add.
# Your application will be processed by our staff and you will receive an invite to the corporation after a short wait. These are sent by volunteers, and it can sometimes take up to 48 hours to be processed, please be patient. 

There is no limit on the number of characters you can have in the corp, but please carefully consider the space you are utilizing. We reserve the right to adjust limits by player or across the corp as we see fit to suit demand. 

We also have an Alt Alliance which is immune to war decs. Details for applying to that are on the page on **Ivy League Hall of Residence**.
