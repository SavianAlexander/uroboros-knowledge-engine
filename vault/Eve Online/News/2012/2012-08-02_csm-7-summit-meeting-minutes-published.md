The CSM 7 Meeting Minutes has just been published!

Read all about the CSM 7 summer meeting in Iceland from May 30 to June 1, 2012 and dive into a huge quantitify of information regarding EVE Online. The meeting minutes provide detailled information in over 20 articles regarding all sort of topics, ranging from industry and economy to nullsec warfare and pvp.

The meeting minutes are available [here](//community.eveonline.com/council/transcripts/2012/CSM_CCP_Meetings_May_June_2012.pdf), a devblog about the meeting minutes can be found [here](//community.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&nbid=73092) and the feedback thread for questions and discussions is available [here](//forums.eveonline.com/default.aspx?g=posts&t=139688).
