1. # Summary
The **Laelaps** is a special edition **Heavy Interdiction Cruiser**, and was introduced as one of the prize ships for **Alliance Tournament** XVII, alongside the **Raiju**. A total of 140 ships were given out, distributed among the top 16 teams of AT XVII.

Compared to the **Orthrus** upon which its based, the Laelaps gains a high slot, additional base hit points, cargo capacity, capacitor regeneration, and targeting range. It is slightly heavier than the Orthrus, however. Like other AT ships, it also gains tech II **natural resistances** and an additional 50 calibration points (from 350 to 400). In terms of traits, The Orthrus' warp disruption/scrambler range bonuses are shifted to the HIC-exclusive warp disruption field generators, and gains a unique reload speed bonus, offsetting the usual normal long reloads of rapid light missile launchers. Like the previously introduced HIC AT ship, the **Fiend**, it gains the HIC standard resist bonus, and an additional bonus negating signature bloom from warp disruption field generators.

1. # Notes

Though not explicitly stated, the Laelaps' scramble range bonus also applies to focused warp disruption scripted and unscripted warp disruption field generators.

In Greek mythology, Laelaps (*Λαῖλαψ*) was a semi-divine hunting dog which always caught its quarry.
