1. # Summary
While the **Bestower** provides the largest capacity for pilots who are willing to train a faction hauler skill to rank 3, the Mammoth holds that distinction for those who invest the time to get to rank 4. Also, similar to its little brother the **Wreathe**, the Mammoth is the fastest **T1 hauler** in its class while only having 4k less potential cargo capacity compared to the Bestower. With max skills, the Mammoth can align in 5.4 seconds when speed fit or 9.8 seconds when bulk hauling fit.
