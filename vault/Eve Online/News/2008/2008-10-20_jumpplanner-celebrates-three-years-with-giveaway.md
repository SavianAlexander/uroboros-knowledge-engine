Three years ago this week Jumpplanner, a wonderful tool designed to help capsuleers in their massive capital-class ships plot their jumps, was launched. In honor of Jumpplanner's anniversary, ICSC has announced the Jumpplanner Anniversary Giveaway.

Entering is quite simple. Either register a new account on the site, or log into your existing account. Doing so between 18 October and 1 November 2008 will automatically register you for the drawing. Prizes include game time, the EVE: The Empyrean Age novel, a t-shirt, and a mouse pad.

To learn more about the giveaway, point your browsers [here](//www.eve-icsc.com/press.php?id=2914).
