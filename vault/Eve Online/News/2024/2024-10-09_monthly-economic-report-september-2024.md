Economic Capsuleers!

The Monthly Economic Report for September 2024 is now available!

For September, the MER has received minor fixes to the way we categorize types for mining.

The economic summary shows the Ship Index continuing to drop, with a ~4.5% drop over the last 12 months. Conversely, the Mineral Price Index has reached a new record of 287.
