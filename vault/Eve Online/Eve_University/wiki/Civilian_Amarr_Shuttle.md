1. # Summary
The Civilian variants of shuttles are similar to their normal empire versions. They can be obtained from the **Making Mountains of Molehills (8 of 10)** mission by constructing the shuttle and then failing the mission. They can not be sold on the market in game and do not appear in the **Ship Tree**. While they used to be identical to other shuttles, they have missed some of the balance changes that other shuttles received and do not have a reduction in jump fatigue, and do not have passive nullification.
