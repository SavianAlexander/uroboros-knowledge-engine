The PLEX for Good: Japan drive has come to an end. During this drive players have donated a total of 2497 PLEX through 665 contracts and 19.6 billion ISK. In total, 2549 PLEX were collected, and 100% of the proceeds will be donated to the Red Cross in the name of "EVE Online Community." For more information read CCP Fallout's latest Dev Blog [here](//www.eveonline.com/devblog.asp?a=blog&bid=893).
