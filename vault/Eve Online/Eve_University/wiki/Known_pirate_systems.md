# Known pirate systems

This is a list of known locations for piracy. Pilots are well advised to use extreme caution when entering these systems, or even better, find a way to avoid them altogether.

Most pirate systems are located in systems with **low security status ratings** (0.1 - 0.4), and which are "choke points" along common traffic lines between trade hubs.  A good way to identify potential piracy systems is to open your **map** (F10 key), then in your Map Control Panel go to Stars by Color / Statistics / and select "Ships destroyed in last hour". You will see if your destination waypoints go through systems that had recent pirate activities.
