# Stillwater

<noinclude> </noinclude>

Accept the Mission remotely.

Ungated semi-deadspace pocket within 2-4 jumps of the previous mission. You can warp around freely.

  - Blitz:** kill Karkoti Rend, loot the Book Of St. Arzad and warp out.

Tips:
- Destroy the EWAR Angel Energy Neutralizer Sentry II first as it has 250 km range and drains 20 GJ/s
- Karkoti Rend's Battleship projects 500 DPS up to 150 km away (decreasing with distance) and approaches at 1000 m/s if over 40 km away. Damage split is ~ 28% EM / 11% Kinetic / 61% Explosive.
- Karkoti Rend has similar EHP to a Gist Seraphim (~45s to kill with a T1-ammo cruise Praxis), so it's best to focus him directly after the tackle frigates and neutralizer to quickly blitz the mission.
- When you kill Karkoti Rend, all other ships will warp out.
- Incoming damage will be quite variable depending on the ship types that predominantly spawn. EM and Explosive damage are the most prevalent overall, though.
