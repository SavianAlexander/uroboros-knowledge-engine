# Blood Raider Powergrid

- Blood Raider Powergrid** is an **Expedition** that can escalate from **Blood Annex**.

1. # Location One (Lowsec, Gateless)
Group is located ~65 km from warp in. Site is ungated.

|}

1. # Location Two (Lowsec)
Group is located 60-70 km from warp in. Site is ungated.

|}

1. # Location Three (Lowsec)
Group is located ~15 km from warp in. Site is ungated. The Newly Constructed Acceleration Gate is usable, but leads to an empty room.

|}

|}

1. # Location Four (Lowsec)
Site is gated. Initial wave is ~20 (may be 5) km from entry beacon.
