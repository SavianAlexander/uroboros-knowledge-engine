# Aakeo Oshaima

- Aakeo Oshaima** is a level 2 agent in **Gallente COSMOS**. They are located in Colelie at the Survey Station.

  - Major hint:** As many of the missions in this chain of agents revolve around Azure chasm, it would save a lot of time to check all agents and get all **items ahead of time** so you only have to go there once.

Aekeo offers the following missions:

1. # The Azure Canyon (1 of 4)

Retrieve the items from Balle VII - Moon 17 - Center for Advanced Studies School and return to the agent.

1. # The Azure Canyon (2 of 4)

Retrieve the items from Tendhyes VIII - Kaalakiota Corporation Factory and return to the agent.
