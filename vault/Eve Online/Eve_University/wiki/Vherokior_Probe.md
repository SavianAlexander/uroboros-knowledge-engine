1. # Summary
Vherokior Probe is a special edition of the **Probe** given out during the 2012 Christmas season similar to the **Sarum Magnate**, **Sukuuvestaa Heron**, and the **Inner Zone Shipping Imicus**. It appears to be functionally equivalent to the standard Probe but with a different skin and additional flavor text.

All models were converted to a regular Probe and a corresponding Vherokior **SKIN** with the Mosaic release in April 2015. Despite this, the model was given a unique quote with the release of the Revenant expansion in November 2024, over nine years after all copies of the ship were converted out of existence.
