# Equinox Expansion (Daily Login Campaign)

- Equinox Expansion** was a log in campaign in celebration of the release of the Equinox expansion. It lasted from 13 June to 20 June 2024, **downtime** to downtime. It was not mentioned in any promotion material prior to the launch.

The campaign offered up to 70k Skill Points for , and an additional 130,000 Skill Points for . Special boosters and materials for the newly introduced **Ship SKINR** system were also attainable.
