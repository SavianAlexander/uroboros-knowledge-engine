# Cleaning House

Mission is in deadspace, you will always be warping to the entry point(at range is allowed) and cannot warp to fleet members/bookmarks directly.

  - Blitz:**
- Blow up the Sansha Outpost, kill Wave 10.

  1. # Warp-in
Aggro on warp-in. Waves 1 to 7 are triggered by random kills in the initial frigates. Wave 8 and 9 trigger on random kills among first 7 waves. Wave 10 spawns at 90% shield on the Sansha Outpost. Each neutralizer hits for 40gj/s.
