# Foil The Military Preparations (Caldari)

- Blitz**: Wait for the Construction Freight ships to spawn, kill them (ignoring the rest of the spawn) and warp out.

Completing the mission changes your Caldary faction standing by -0.24.

  1. # Single Pocket
- Reinforcement is a timed spawn, about 3 minutes.
