# Intaki Liberation

- Intaki Liberation** is the eighth mission of the AIR epic arc **Vision of Greatness**

  - Supplies: Intaki Liberation** will be given after accepting the mission which includes (when opened):

- 1x Tristan
- 1x 75mm Gatling Rail I
- 1x 1MN Afterburner I
- 1x Remote Sensor Dampener I
- 5x Hobgoblin I
- 2,000x Antimatter Charge S

Tasks
- Destroy 6 hostile NPCs
- Remote Sensor Dampen the Executive Defense Cannon sensor
- Destroy as many Plundering Hauler as possible, allowing less than 2 of the 3 escape
