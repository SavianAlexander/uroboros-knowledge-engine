## Overview
Unleash your creativity with more options and freedom.

### Daring capsuleers,


The ongoing effort to create new career and specialization opportunities for budding SKINdustrialists  continues today with a new Equinox update. Several alterations were made in the **last update** on 25 July, including modifications to the cost and time requirements associated with SKINR, and a new skill that reduces Paragon Hub vendor fees. Today’s update continues that trajectory, adding the ability to try out any design elements in your creations and apply all nanocoatings to any slot, as well as several quality-of-life improvements. Furthermore, for the next twelve days, you can claim daily SKINR-themed rewards. At the same time, work continues on other important updates to Equinox, such as Skyhook raiding and more. 

**UPDATE:** In a happy little accident the DLI has been extended **until 27 August** and the total available skill points will be **425,000 for Omegas. **


## ENHANCED CREATIVE OPPORTUNITIES


Today’s update introduces the ability to try out any design element when crafting SKINs, whether you own them or not, enabling you to freely experiment with various elements to realize your creative vision, and then acquire them when you’re ready to sequence your designs. In addition, all nanocoatings can now be applied to any slot, opening more possibilities than ever before.  

Several quality-of-life improvements have also been added, including sorting nanocoatings by hue in the studio, streamlining the design process and speeding up the workflow. The interface now also links directly to the market or Paragon Hub if you need to quickly acquire the elements you’re missing for your masterpiece.  

In addition, for the **next twelve days**, you can claim daily SKINR-themed rewards, so be sure to log in every day to add to your collection of design elements and other components necessary to realize your artistic vision. Don’t miss stunning nanocoatings, sequencers, boosters, and up to 425,000 SP, perfect for training the recently added skills to further your design career. 


## SHARING IS CARING


Another update is coming very soon that introduces the ability to share your saved SKIN masterpieces through chat, whether you want to create an awesome design to fly with your friends, get specialized SKINdustrialists to sequence your creations, or just contribute to a vibrant SKIN ecosystem. 


## LET YOUR IMAGINATION RUN WILD


The ability to design SKINs using any elements regardless of ownership allows any capsuleer to freely explore the creative limits of SKIN design, contribute to the colorful tapestry of New Eden, and share your creative vision with the universe without needing to fund the sequencing or acquire all the necessary design elements.  

We look forward to seeing the latest SKINR creations flying in space.
