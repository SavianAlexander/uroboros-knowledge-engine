Are you planning to attend EVE Vegas 2016? Want to be part of the speaker program? Here's how you can be part of the presenter lineup this year!

EVE Vegas is designed for our players from the ground up, or the desert up, if you'd like to be technical, and there's a strong tradition of EVE players and community figureheads presenting together on one stage with EVE Developers.

We're happy to announce that we're now accepting proposals for player speakers for the event, so if you'd like to be a part of the lineup, you'll need to send your proposal to [evevegas@ccpgames.com](mailto:evevegas@ccpgames.com)

Please include the following information in your email:


- Your main character name
- Your full name and telephone number
- A brief outline of your talk proposal (Feel free to submit more than one proposal)
- The proposed duration of your talk (This can be anywhere from 10-40 minutes)


All player speakers will receive a complimentary speaker badge for EVE Vegas 2016, and a ticket to the Pub Crawl.

We have already started to work on the speaker schedule for the event, so please send us your proposal as soon as you have an outline ready.

The final deadline for proposals is Wednesday, July 27th 2016, so be sure to submit your proposal before then!

EVE Vegas takes place at the Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino from 28-30th October 2016. For more information on the event, head on over to the [EVE Vegas website](//vegas.eveonline.com/)!

To grab tickets for EVE Vegas 2016, be sure to visit the [EVE Vegas Eventbrite page](//www.eventbrite.com/e/eve-vegas-2016-tickets-24686308413).
