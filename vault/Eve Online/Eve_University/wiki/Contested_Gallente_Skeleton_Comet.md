  - Contested Gallente Skeleton Comet** is a COSMOS combat site located in Deltole. It is a static site that shows up as a warpable beacon in overview.

1. # Skeleton Comet

The first room contains periodically respawning rats, some structures and two acceleration gates. To access the items inside the Scorched Containers you will first have to destroy the group that sits on them. Rats don't aggro until approached/attacked.

1. # Serpentis Survey Site

This room contains two clusters of hackable containers protected by  a range of frigates, cruisers and battleships. The gate deeper is locked and requires Smuggler Knot Lock in your cargo hold. The key is not consumed on use.

Note that the frigates web and warp disrupt your ship while the cruisers and battleships deal a dangerous amount of damage. They will also frequently shoot drones as a group and deal damage in excess of 300-400 DPS Kin/Th.

|}

|}

1. # Ancient Ruins

The room contains rats spread out 30-100 km from warp-in. Central group will auto-aggro. The hacking containers' difficulty varies with the names. Ruined Monuments are very easy, Broken Monuments easy, Impaired Monuments medium, and Busted Monuments hard difficulty.

|}

|}

1. # Drone Infested Corridor

This pocket contains drones and Serpentis ships clustered in groups 50-200 km from warp-in. The drones will aggro all together if one of them if shot.

Though the acceleration gate is fairly close to warp-in, it is locked until all ships in the pocket are destroyed. Due to long travel distances to kill the NPCs, a small ship with MWD is recommended.
