  1. # 4th pocket - Think Tank
----

When you enter the room you are surrounded by 4 Serpentis Gate Sentries (Description : Siege Pulse Laser Sentry) and 2 Stasis Sentries. Gate Sentries deal heavy damage so you should have good overall resists (for EM/Thermal) and good buffer or active tank with repair capabilities. It is suggested to tank the incoming damage and destroy the webbing towers while moving away from the sentries which are hard to destroy. With sufficient distance (approx. 50km) sentries stop dealing damage so you can concentrate on pirate ships. Structure itself drop common loot and very hard to destroy so you can also skip destroying it and focus on hacking cans.
