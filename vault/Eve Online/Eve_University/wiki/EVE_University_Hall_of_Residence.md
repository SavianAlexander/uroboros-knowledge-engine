# EVE University Hall of Residence

The **EVE University Hall of Residence** [.HALL] corporation is the designated alt corporation of **EVE University**. 

- All members of EVE University Hall of Residence are bound to the **EVE University Rules** and **Communications Policy** and have to comply with them.
- Since EVE University Hall of Residence does not own any structures, other corporations can not declare war on it.
- The tax rate is set at zero.

  1. # Part of Ivy League Alt Alliance
EVE University Hall of Residence is part of the Ivy League Alt Alliance [IVY-A], an alliance of alt corporations owned by EVE University members. The Ivy League Alt Alliance is not eligible for war decs either.
The Ivy League Alt Alliance and its corporations, including EVE University Hall of Residence have mutual positive standings with **Ivy League** and therefore **EVE University**.

1. # Apply with your Alts or Alt corporation

If you are a current member of **EVE University** and would like to add an **Alt character** to EVE University Hall of Residence, please apply by using [this form](https://forms.gle/wMLowXQzQH3BwMYg6). The number alternate characters you can have within the Hall of Residence is not limited. Our HALL. Monitors process outstanding requests on the form regularly. If you have not heard back from them after one week, feel free to ping @HALL. Monitor in the #support channel on our Discord.

If you are applying to have your alt corp added to Ivy League Alt Alliance, please ensure that the corporation is not war eligible. 

To add your alt corp to the Ivy League Alt Alliance, please follow the following two steps.  You must have a Student title in order to apply with an alt corp.
# Apply using [this form](https://forms.gle/wMLowXQzQH3BwMYg6). 
# For alt corps, you must also apply in-game to the Ivy League Alt Alliance and transfer 24 million ISK to EVE University Hall of Residence. This amount covers the fees set by CCP for your corp to be part of the alliance.
