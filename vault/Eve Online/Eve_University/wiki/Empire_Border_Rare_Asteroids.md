# Empire Border Rare Asteroids

- Empire Border Rare Asteroids** is a **anomaly** that spawns in 0.5 **security level** systems on the border with lowsec (or, in rare cases, directly with nullsec).

It contains special "border ores" Ducinium, Eifyrium and Ytirium, which have high **reprocessing** value per. Moreover, they are a source of **minerals** otherwise not available in highsec.
