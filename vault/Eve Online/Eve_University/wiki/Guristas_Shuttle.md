1. # Summary
While all standard racial shuttles (Amarr, Caldari, Gallente, Minmatar) are functionally identical, the Guristas shuttle is neither standard nor identical. Its stats are, in fact, *worse* than the standard Caldari shuttle in every category where they differ; the armor, shields, and structure are all weaker, which means it's even easier to kill than a regular shuttle.

1. # Notes
The Guristas Shuttle can be received as a drop in **Contested Guristas Outrider Post** and one is required in a **Caldari COSMOS** mission. If you want to get one from there you will either leave your ship in site as you take the shuttle or a ship with large enough cargohold to take the shuttle in.
