## Overview
The Fanfest live stream returns with great hosts.

---

Capsuleers, 

With Fanfest drawing ever closer, ripples are spreading across New Eden. The live stream is returning for 2026, featuring hosts who know EVE Online like the back of their hand, ensuring those who can’t make it to Reykjavik still won’t miss a single moment. 

Whether you’re tuning in for the big reveals, deep dives by developers, or unforgettable community moments, this crew will guide you through every major beat of the festival. 

Erst, Lorumerth, Oz, and ThatGuyToxic will be live on site, bringing you sharp analysis, real-time reactions, and capsuleer banter straight from the heart of Fanfest. 

Each host brings their own style and strength to the table. Here’s a quick look at the voices shaping this year’s broadcast: 


### **Erst**


[*Twitch: Erst*](https://www.twitch.tv/erst)  

A long-time EVE creator known for sharp gameplay insights and relaxed atmosphere streams, Erst brings experience and a deep understanding of New Eden’s many layers. 


### **Lorumerth**


[*YouTube: Loru Gaming*](https://www.youtube.com/@lorumerthgaming)  

Lorumerth blends an engaging, high-energy on-screen presence with insight into a broad range of EVE topics. His analytical breakdowns and strong community focus make him a powerful voice for the Fanfest broadcast. 


### **Oz**


[*YouTube: OZ_Eve*](https://www.youtube.com/@OZeve)  

Market master, data-driven thinker, and one of the most recognizable educational creators in the EVE ecosystem, Oz adds economic expertise and sharp commentary to the mix. 


### **ThatGuyToxic**


[*Twitch: thatguytoxic*](https://www.twitch.tv/thatguytoxic)  

Known for dynamic streams and an authentic connection with the community, ThatGuyToxic brings energy, humor, and live-reaction commentary to the Fanfest stage. 

From economic impact to expansion speculation, from serious analysis to spontaneous stage moments, this lineup delivers experience, insight, and personality in equal measure. 

We look forward to working with them to make the live stream (almost) as much fun as attending Fanfest 2026 in person. 

See you at Fanfest and in Twitch chat!
