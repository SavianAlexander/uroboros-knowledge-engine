# TANF Cash Assistance Federal Regulations (45 CFR Part 260)

## 1. Statutory Objectives & Deprivation Criteria
Under Title IV-A of the Social Security Act, TANF provides temporary financial assistance to low-income families with dependent children:
- **Qualifying Minor Child**: Child under age 18 (or 19 if full-time student).
- **Parental Deprivation**: Continued absence, physical/mental incapacity, or unemployment of one or both parents.

---

## 2. Statutory Financial Tests & Disregards
- **Asset Limit**: **$2,500.00** liquid asset cap ($3,250 for households with elderly/disabled).
- **Initial Earned Income Disregard**: **$90.00** standard work incentive deduction.
- **Ongoing Work Incentive Disregard**: **30%** deduction applied to remaining earned income.

### Lifetime Benefit Limit:
- Federal statutory lifetime cap of **60 cumulative months** of TANF cash assistance receipt.
