# Guristas Strike - Defend the Civilian Convoy

<noinclude>  
  - Guristas Strike - Defend the Civilian Convoy** is mission 8 of the mission chain **Guristas Strike**. Unlike most storyline missions this mission gives no faction standing, but it does give agent standing. The faction standing is awarded for the final mission in the chain.
</noinclude>

Deadspace with gates. No hostiles at initial gate.

  - Blitz:** Kill group 3.

  1. # Single Pocket

Kill group 3 to complete the mission. The Storage Facility can drop general goods.
