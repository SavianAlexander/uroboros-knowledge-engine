# Angel Sanctum

The Angel Sanctum is a **combat anomaly** found in **Angel** infested regions. The site is moderate difficulty and can be run in a sniper battleship or advanced cruiser (Faction or T2) but probably won't be able to fight the possible capital spawn after the final wave.
1. # Walkthrough
Normal open deadspace pocket which you can choose warp distance for. There are 2 variants for this site, Ring and Station, that will decide the waves of the enemy.

  1. # Ring Spawn Variant
This variant will have 5 rings of explosion dampening arrays that you can use to orbit the waves.
