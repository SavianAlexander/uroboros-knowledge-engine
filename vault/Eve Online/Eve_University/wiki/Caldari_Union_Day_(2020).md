# Caldari Union Day (2020)

- Caldari Union Day** (alternatively **Union Day**) was a limited time event running between 4-7 September 2020, downtime to downtime. During this period, capsuleers could log in to earn fireworks, and SKINs for the **Caldari Shuttle** and **Badger**. Omega clones could also collect 35k **Skill Points** and Caldari-themed clothing. This event featured celebrations in select systems of **Caldari State** high security space, and overlapped with a 2v2 Caldari-themed Navy Frigate/Interceptor/Destroyer **Abyssal Proving Grounds** event.

1. # Parade

During the event, special event beacons with a Caldari fleet were spawned.

The systems where parades took place were:
- Airkio  (Wiyrkomi Corporation Parade - WPCS Tyunaul Seituoda - ~300 km off of Annaro VI - Moon 2 - Wiyrkomi Corporation Factory)
- Annaro  (Nugoeihuvi Corporation Parade - WPCS Istria Josameto - ~500km off of Josameto VIII - Nugoeihuvi Corporation Publisher)
- Josameto  (Lai Dai Corporation Parade - WPCS Saki Orluusa - ~200km off of Airkio VII - Moon 3 - Lai Dai Corporation Factory)
- Malkalen  (Ishukone Corporation Parade - IWS Otro Gariushi - ~200km off of Malkalen V - Moon 1 - Ishukone Corporation Factory)
- Muvolailen  (CBD Corporation Parade - SPS Laril Hyykoda - ~325 km off of Muvolailen X - Moon 3 - CBD Corporation Storage)
- Saisio  (Sukuuvesta Corporation Parade - POUS Tuvilo Kishbin - ~325 km off of Saisio VIII - Moon 20 - Sukuuvesta Corporation Warehouse
- Sobaseki  (Kalaakiota Corporation Parade - ~200km off of Sobaseki XI - Moon 1 - Home Guard Assembly Plant)
- Suroken  (Hiyosoda Corporation Parade - ~325 km off of Suroken VII - Moon 2 - Hyasoda Corporation Mineral Reserve)

On Saturday, 12 September, a task was given to all capsuleers to launch a firework at the titan of one of these fleets. Capsuleers were rewarded 5k SP for their efforts. Unlike previous events, this occurred one week after the login campaign.

Each titan had a description which provided background on their corporation. They can be accessed **here**.
