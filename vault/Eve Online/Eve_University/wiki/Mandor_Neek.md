# Mandor Neek

- Mandor Neek** is a level 4 agent in **Amarr COSMOS** located in Jakri on Garisas gate. This agent requires 8.5 faction standing with Amarr Empire.

1. # Improving Relations

Provide 30 x Sansha Silver Tag (3.0 m3) to agent.
