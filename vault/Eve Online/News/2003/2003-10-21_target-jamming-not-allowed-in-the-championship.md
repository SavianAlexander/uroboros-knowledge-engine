# Target Jamming not allowed in the Championship

- **Date**: 2003-10-21T21:16:48.000Z
- **Category**: news
- **Author**: Svarthol
- **Source**: https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/target-jamming-not-allowed-in-the-championship
- **Tags**: #news

## Overview
The Championship Council members have decided that target jamming will not be allowed in the tournament as it has never been favored by Amarrians and that the Championship is considered a fight of brave champions and not a competition in cowardice.
