# Yru Hatamei

- Yru Hatamei**  is a level 3 agent in **Caldari COSMOS** located in Ishisomo - Rusty Ridge Mine.

1. # Those Bastards (1 of 4)

1. # Got Milk? (2 of 4)

Cargo delivery destination is random and can be anywhere in [Okkelen](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/The_Forge/Okkelen#sec) or [Etsala](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/The_Forge/Etsala#sec) constellation, including in a low security system (like Ihakana).

1. # Ancient Weaponry (3 of 4)

Ancient Weapon drops from the Storage Silo in **Contested Caldari Lai Dai Refinery** - Contested Scordite field deadspace complex in the Airmia system

1. # Training Ground Destruction (4 of 4)

Guristas Outlaw Dogtag (0.5 m3) drop from Guristas rats in **Contested Caldari Lai Dai Refinery** deadspace complex in the Airmia system.
