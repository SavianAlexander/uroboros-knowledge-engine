# Neyan Khahsel

- Neyan Khahsel** is a level 4 **Blood Raider COSMOS** agent located in CX8-6K in [Delve](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Delve/CX8-6K). Neyan offers two missions, and requires a 6.0 standings with Blood Raiders to work with.

1. # Slaves To The Slaughter (1 of 2)

Mission flagged complete when you warp in, no looting required. Can does have 500 prisoners if you want to loot it. Mission reward is currently(When?, How?) bugged.
