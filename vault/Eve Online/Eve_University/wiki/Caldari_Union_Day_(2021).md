# Caldari Union Day (2021)

- Caldari Union Day** is a limited time event running between 1st and 8th September 2021, **downtime** to **downtime**. During this period, capsuleers can log in to earn fireworks, 10k **Skill Points**, and SKINs for the **Caldari Shuttle** and **Badger**. Omega clones can also collect an additional 15k **Skill Points** and Caldari-themed clothing. This event features celebrations in select systems of **Caldari State** high security space, and overlapps with a 2v2 Caldari Battlecruiser **Abyssal Proving Grounds** event.

1. # Parade

During the event, special event beacons with a Caldari fleet were spawned.

The systems where parades take place are:
- Airkio  (Wiyrkomi Corporation Parade - WPCS Tyunaul Seituoda - ~300 km off of Annaro VI - Moon 2 - Wiyrkomi Corporation Factory)
- Annaro  (Nugoeihuvi Corporation Parade - WPCS Istria Josameto - ~500km off of Josameto VIII - Nugoeihuvi Corporation Publisher)
- Josameto  (Lai Dai Corporation Parade - WPCS Saki Orluusa - ~200km off of Airkio VII - Moon 3 - Lai Dai Corporation Factory)
- Malkalen  (Ishukone Corporation Parade - IWS Otro Gariushi - ~200km off of Malkalen V - Moon 1 - Ishukone Corporation Factory)
- Muvolailen  (CBD Corporation Parade - SPS Laril Hyykoda - ~325 km off of Muvolailen X - Moon 3 - CBD Corporation Storage)
- Saisio  (Sukuuvesta Corporation Parade - POUS Tuvilo Kishbin - ~325 km off of Saisio VIII - Moon 20 - Sukuuvesta Corporation Warehouse
- Sobaseki  (Kalaakiota Corporation Parade - ~200km off of Sobaseki XI - Moon 1 - Home Guard Assembly Plant)
- Suroken  (Hiyosoda Corporation Parade - CPFS Kaal Osmon - 325 km off of Suroken VII - Moon 2 - Hyasoda Corporation Mineral Reserve)

Every day, the **capsuleers** are challenged to visit one of the Systems and fire a firework at the present **Titan** to gain 5,000 Skillpoints.

Each titan has a **description** that provides background on their corporation.

1. # Monuments
Two new lore monuments are being constructed in time for Caldari Union Day. One such is the Landfall Kutuoto Miru Orbital Center in New Caldari . You can also visit the Urlen II Provist Riots Memorial to pay your respects to the lives of State citizens lost at the hands of terrorists in YC110.

1. # Special Mechanics
During the event, a special Federation Day warp speed bonus of 2 AU/s is applied to all ships in **Caldari State** space.
