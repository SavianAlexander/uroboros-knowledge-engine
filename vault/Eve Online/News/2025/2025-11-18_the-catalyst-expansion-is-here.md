## Overview
Sparking a new resource rush in New Eden.

Capsuleers,

Mining is the foundation of New Eden, and with the Catalyst expansion, it has now become more dynamic, rewarding and contested. Catalyst introduces a new ore, new ships, the 2D map, a new epic arc, and much more. 

New phased fields and prismaticite, the most valuable ore ever seen, ignite a new resource rush across New Eden. Cooperation, mixed fleets, and a great deal of courage are required to claim the new riches. Race to claim them, but be alert, because every mining operation can turn into a battlefield. 

This expansion gives new pilots a strong start, with an easy path into mining through the Fractured Legacy epic arc, faster ways to earn and a new ship built to grow with you. Discovering adventures across New Eden has never been easier.

## From a Spark to an Inferno


Mysterious hyperspace fractures tear open across New Eden, revealing volatile asteroids, rich in prismaticite, an erratic ore that refines into different minerals.

Mining gets faster, more rewarding and dynamic, with critical mining hits, faster mining cycles, new in-space UI overlays, and many more mining enhancements. The new mining destroyer, the Pioneer, is a perfect middle ground between a mining frigate and mining barges and is available in three different variations. The new epic arc introduces the new updates and rewards you with your very own Pioneer!

Lead your fleet further afield and conquer uncharted territory with the Odysseus, a powerful new exploration command ship. Chart routes, locate resources and plan your expeditions with the new 2D map. Furthermore, a version of the New Eden map is now also available [online](https://eveonline.com/new-eden-map).

In addition, Catalyst introduces several other enhancements, including carrier updates, personal PLEX transaction logs, and much more. Check out the [Mining blog](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/mining-in-focus-new-ore-and-more), the [New Ships blog](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/catalyst-in-focus-new-ships-and-more), or the [**patch notes**](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/patch-notes-version-23-02) for **all the details** on what Catalyst has for you.

Until 27 November, all capsuleers can claim a special Omega offer from the New Eden Store: seven days of Omega for just 10 PLEX. Pilots with existing Omega status get a gift of 10 PLEX, which can be used to extend it by 7 days, or just to build your war chest.

This is the perfect boost to help you seize the moment, fly the new ships, and sharpen your edge, so don’t miss your chance.

## Expansion Packs and Offers


Don’t miss the new Catalyst packs in the EVE store, with one tailored more to newer players and the other for veterans. 

New pilots will enjoy a pack that includes Omega time, Skill Points, a cerebral accelerator, an expert system to acquire all the necessary skills to fully experience the Pioneer, and a stunning skin for their Pioneer.

Experienced capsuleers will appreciate a more advanced pack, including a wealth of PLEX and Skill Points, a cerebral accelerator, and a plethora of SKINR components, apparel, and Refined Resourcer SKINs.

The New Eden Store is also offering a fantastic deal on Multiple Character Training, including free Omega and SKINs.


## Catalyst has Just Begun


This is just the beginning of the Catalyst era, and more updates are just around the corner.

Freelance Jobs will get an update on 2 December, that introduces Access Lists allowing corporations to control who can access their jobs by black- or whitelisting specific characters, corporations, or alliances.

Stay tuned for more updates. The power to light the spark across New Eden is in your hands now.
