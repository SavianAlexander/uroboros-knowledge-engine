# Gallente COSMOS

- Gallente COSMOS** is a collection of Mission Agents, Deadspace Sites and unique asteroid **belt rat**s.

They are located in:
- High Security [Algintal](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Sinq_Laison/Algintal#const) constellation in Sinq Laison region
- Null Security [Assilot](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Cloud_Ring/Assilot#const) constellation in Cloud Ring region

1. # High Security Gallente COSMOS
COSMOS Agents are located in the Algintal constellation in the Sinq Laison region. They are close to the Dodixie Trade Hub.

  1. # Ship Suggestions
COSMOS Agents offer all types of Missions: Distribution, Mining and Security. 

You should bring:
- A fast hauling ship with at least 1,200 m3 Cargo Hold for **Croir Arghe** Missions: an Exploration ship, a hauler or even a **Sunesis**
- An **Instant align** ship for hauling low volume items through High and Low Security Systems
- A **Cruiser** or **Assault Frigate**
- Optionally, a **Venture** if you want to mine and haul the required ores

  1. # Agents
COSMOS Agents offer missions **only once**. Failing the mission or letting it expire will prevent you from doing it and the following missions in the arc.

 unless you are willing and capable to accept and complete their mission within 7 days.

COSMOS Agents will only give missions to those who have enough Faction Standing.

If you cannot see a COSMOS Agent on your **Overview**, enable "Beacon" and "Agents in Space" in the overview settings.
