## Overview
New navy destroyers, more rewards, more sites in highsec.

---

Capsuleers,   

The war between the empires is raging like never before. CONCORD safety measures have collapsed, and the conflict is casting an ever-expanding shadow across New Eden. The mayhem is about to ramp up, as even the new Warpath: Capital Mayday sites have reached highsec. 

The seasonal reward track for Capsuleer Day XXIII: Warpath has been extended, so you can keep going with more Skill Points, Warpath Operational Intel, a SKINR component, and Warpath Command Intel which you can trade for a brand-new navy destroyer BPC! 

This means that during the event, you can get your hands on these powerful new hulls, well in advance of the Cradle of War expansion, coming on 9 June, when they will be introduced to New Eden at large. 

After Capsuleer Day XXIII wraps up on 2 June, there will be no way to get these hulls until Cradle of War launches, so this is your chance! 

The regular reward track also offers SoCT ships, Skill Points, and much more, so if you haven’t finished it, you still have time. 


## Continue on the Warpath


The distribution of challenge sites is about to be expanded as well. The Warpath: Capital Mayday titan support site, which was previously only available in lowsec, is now appearing in highsec as well. It is a rare opportunity for capsuleers to witness a titan firing its doomsday weapon in highsec! The same goes for Warpath: War Grid combat sites for Tech II battleships and below, as well as the Warpath: Strategic Infiltration exploration sites, which have spread from lowsec to highsec. 

A new seasonal challenge chain has also been introduced for tackling the Capital Mayday site, so lend your support to defending an empire titan and get rewarded with ISK and seasonal points when your titan fires its doomsday. 

Capsuleer Day XXIII: Warpath offers activities across combat, exploration, industry, and mining, with challenges suited to both newer pilots and veterans hungry for greater risk. 

If you haven’t already, you should check out the updated Community Fittings, to help you crush your objectives in Capsuleer Day XXIII. 

And if you need more, there are plenty of amazing community guides to help you out.
