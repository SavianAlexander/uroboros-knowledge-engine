# Gaseous Nebula

- Gaseous Nebula** is a gas exploration site containing gas used in the production of **Medical boosters**.

This is a null sec gas site found through Exploration.

Minable Gas Clouds:

1 Golden Cytoserocin (9000 units in each of the gas clouds)

Golden Cytoserocin is used in the production of the Standard Crash Booster Booster.

This site is found only in the[09-4XW](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Tenal/09-4XW)constellation of the[Tenal](https://evemaps.dotlan.net/map/Tenal)region of space.
