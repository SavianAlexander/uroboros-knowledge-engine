# Contested Gallente Azure Canyon

- Contested Gallente Azure Canyon** is a **Gallente COSMOS** combat site in Colelie system in Algintal constellation. Up to T2 **Battlecruisers** can enter the site.

Video: [[Brutix](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d_GPYGL8E1A) 3 November 2018]

1. # Warpin
Taking the acceleration gate requires you to have Azure Canyon Tourist Pass in your cargo. The pass drops from Apte Donie and is not consumed on use.

|}

1. # The Tourist Resort

The battleship and Serpentis ships seem to have separate respawns.  Alarus Ekire does not aggress unless attacked.

|}

|}

1. # Azure Stone

This room contains a number of hostile rats. Frigates may aggro drones.

|}

|}

1. # The Black Market

To enter The Black Market you need to have "Black Market Entry Keycard" in your cargo. The key is not consumed on use. The black market room contains respawning rats and some structures. The prison Facility contains 18x Temko Mercenaries, the drug storage contains commodity drugs (blue pill), and the Serpentis Stronghold may drop a storyline module BPC. Rats will not aggro until attacked.

|}

|}

1. # The Ion Field

The Ion Field area houses agent **Ampsin Achippon**. A level 4 agent of **Combine Harvest** of **Gallente Federation**. The area also houses spider drones and FON sentries. They do not aggress unless attacked.
